# Koto acquires Stereo Creative to launch new global studio network

Source: [design_week](https://www.designweek.co.uk/koto-acquires-stereo-creative-to-launch-new-global-studio-network/)

Global creative company Koto has acquired international creative studio Stereo Creative, marking the first step in establishing a new global studio network focused on branding, digital, campaigns, and content production. The acquisition represents a significant expansion for Koto, increasing its capacity as clients seek integrated brand experiences. Stereo becomes the inaugural agency to join the newly created network.
Following investment from Westbridge secured in 2025, Koto set out to build a broader group structure in response to rising demand for integrated, creatively led services. The integration of Stereo brings a client portfolio across the sports, entertainment, technology and consumer sectors, including Nike, Arsenal, FIFA, Converse, EA Sports, Twitch and Bumble.
The expansion brings the newly formed Koto Group from 170 to 230 people across eight international hubs. The network now maintains studios in London, Berlin, Los Angeles, San Francisco, New York, Portland, Dubai and Sydney.
James Greenfield, chief executive and co-founder of Koto, said, ‘As Koto grows, welcoming Stereo is the first step in building a group of values-aligned, founder-led companies with the pursuit of excellence at their heart. We know how much our clients value the quality of our work, and this acquisition broadens the ways we can deliver it.’
Founded in London in 2015 by James Greenfield, Caroline Matthews and Jowey Roden, Koto quickly built a profile working with early clients including Coca-Cola, Hilton and PayPal. The agency subsequently guided Airbnb through its brand and digital transformation up to its IPO, reshaped Amazon’s global brand identity, became Microsoft’s branding agency of record, and created the identity for the LA28 Olympic and Paralympic Games. Most recently, Koto partnered with Meta to design Instagram’s new wordmark.
Stereo Creative brings almost two decades of experience, with existing bases in London, San Francisco, Los Angeles, Portland and Dubai. Together, the combined organisation will offer brand strategy, digital experiences, campaigns and ongoing content creation.
Andrew Duff, chief executive and co-founder of Stereo Creative, said, ‘Stereo and Koto have always felt like kindred spirits, sharing the same creative ethos and drive. Joining forces is a natural evolution for us and will ensure that both Stereo and Koto are better equipped to fight the good fight – standing up for true creativity and craft, always.’
The acquisition of Stereo is the first in a series of planned purchases intended to bring together independent, founder-led agencies under the Koto umbrella. The expansion strategy is designed to address changing client requirements while strengthening leadership capabilities across the group.
Jowey Roden, chief creative officer at Koto, said, ‘Partnering with Stereo deepens our commitment to creative excellence. We look forward to building something greater than the sum of its parts, and delivering exceptional product quality for the world’s most influential brands.’
Ed Minton, director at investor Westbridge, said, ‘We’re very pleased to see two brilliant businesses come together. Stereo is a strong addition to Koto’s growing studio network, and we’re excited to keep supporting the team as they continue to build an exceptional business of global scale.’
