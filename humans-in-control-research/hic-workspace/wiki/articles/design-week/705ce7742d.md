# Pentagram creates paired identities for new venues at Doha Design District

Source: [design_week](https://www.designweek.co.uk/pentagram-creates-paired-identities-for-new-venues-at-doha-design-district/)

Located in Msheireb Downtown, Doha Design District (DDD) appointed Pentagram to establish identities for two distinct venues situated opposite one another within the district. DDD director Dana Kazic tasked partner Samar Maakaroun and her team with branding the locations in a way that built individual identity while reinforcing the master brand equity of DDD.
The two venues serve contrasting functions within the district. Mada was conceived as a dedicated maker space focused on experimentation, artistic development, and learning. Directly opposite, Sada serves as an event and exhibition space created for public engagement, cultural events, and showcasing finished projects.
The team at Pentagram defined a flexible brand architecture that allows a cohesive visual language to flow between the sub-brands, across Arabic and English, and back into the wider district ecosystem.
Echo and reach
The studio explored linguistic and spatial concepts, seeking Arabic words with clear, sonorous pronunciations that functioned as two halves of a single narrative.
The resulting names, Sada and Mada, embody the full creative journey. Mada translates from Arabic as ‘range’, ‘reach’ or ‘possibility’, representing the site of artistic exploration and initial creation. Sada translates as ‘echo’, representing the platform where ideas generated in Mada are shared, amplified, and experienced by the wider community.
Drawn from local architecture
The team developed a shared visual DNA informed by the architecture of Msheireb Downtown and the established DDD identity. Custom letterforms extend from the lowercase letter ‘d’ contained within the primary DDD symbol, linking the sub-brands directly to the parent mark. Both identities act as sub-brands endorsed by the main district symbol, ensuring that every touchpoint supports the overarching destination.
Ali Al Kuwari, chief executive of Msheireb Properties, said, “Sada and Mada reflect the ambition at the heart of Doha Design District, to build a creative ecosystem that is authentically Qatari and genuinely world-class. Pentagram drew directly from the architecture of Msheireb Properties, and the result is a naming that honours both our heritage and our vision for what this district is becoming.”
Dana Kazic, director of Doha Design District, said, “Sada and Mada are something I am truly proud of – they are ours, conceived for our community, our makers, our stories. These spaces hold the full creative journey: what begins in Mada, in the making and the exploring, finds its voice in Sada, in the showing and the sharing. Pentagram created the names and brand identity, and in doing so, captured exactly what these spaces are and what we are building here.”
The project was carried out by Pentagram’s London studio under the direction of Samar Maakaroun, alongside project team members Giulia Saporito, Nadim Twal, Jacob Chung, Jonathan Nielsen and Caroline Fay Fright.
