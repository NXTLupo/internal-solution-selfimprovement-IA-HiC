# The Outline: Marie Kondo, Mr Jones Watches, and ten collage jackets – 24/08/2026

Source: [design_week](https://www.designweek.co.uk/the-outline-marie-kondo-mr-jones-watches-and-ten-collage-jackets-24-08-2026/)

Welcome to your weekly digest of noteworthy stories, announcements, and tidbits from across the design industry.
From recycled glass architecture and beautiful vandalism in packaging to festival shower boycotts and new creator strongholds in Shoreditch, here is what caught our attention across studios, streets, and sound booths over the past week.
Business News
VASO Global Opens Dumfries Manufacturing Plant
Building innovation firm VASO Global has launched its new 60,000-square-foot manufacturing facility at Irongray Park in Dumfries. Headed by experienced manufacturing manager Jim Patterson, the plant will produce high-performance structural composite panels manufactured from recycled glass, aiming to transform sustainable housing across the UK.
Supported by over £1m in funding from Scottish Enterprise, South of Scotland Enterprise, and PyON Ventures, the facility has created its first 15 local jobs. VASO Global plans to expand the Dumfries site to up to 70 roles over the next five years as additional production lines come online.
Adobe Firefly Expands with AI Audio Tools
Adobe has announced the general availability of commercial AI audio tools within its Firefly studio. The updated platform includes Generate Music, Generate Speech, and Generate Sound Effects, allowing creators to produce universally licensed soundtracks, natural voiceovers, and custom audio effects tuned directly to video length and mood.
Powered by the Firefly Music Model and integrated options like ElevenLabs and Gemini Omni Flash, the system removes licensing friction, enabling video creators and marketers to produce studio-quality sound seamlessly within a single workflow.
Marie Kondo to Headline Home, Life & You LIVE
Lifestyle event Home, Life & You LIVE is returning to ExCeL London on 17–18 October 2026 with Marie Kondo as headline speaker. Kondo will deliver the first talk on her new project, ‘The Art of Tokimeki’, focusing on discovering what sparks joy and aligning daily habits with personal well-being.
Across three stages – Home, Life, and You – the weekend brings together expert panels and over 100 brands to offer practical advice on decluttering, organisation, and self-development.
Qube Confirms October Opening for Shoreditch Creator Clubhouse
Creator economy platform Qube has announced the 1 October opening of its fifth London location, taking over the former Vice headquarters on Scrutton Street in Shoreditch. Spanning 34,000 square feet, the site marks Qube’s largest venue to date, bringing together 34 private office suites, podcast and video studios, music and DJ booths, meeting rooms, and a dedicated 80-capacity event space under one roof.
Co-founded by Amin Hamzianpour and Nicholas Sonuga to replace fragmented workflows for modern creative businesses, Qube Shoreditch pre-launches with 60% of its private office space already secured. It joins existing sites in Acton, Canary Wharf, Elephant & Castle, and Hackney Wick, expanding a network used by over 1,000 active members and brands like YouTube and Warner Music Group.
Appointments
Alanna Morgan Appointed at Arts University Plymouth
Arts University Plymouth has named Alanna Morgan as Senior Lecturer and Course Leader for its BA (Hons) Fashion Media & Marketing degree. Having taught on the degree since 2016, Morgan brings extensive commercial retail experience from previous roles as Area Creative Supervisor at Miss Selfridge and creative support at Topshop’s Knightsbridge flagship.
In her new role, she will lead a curriculum focused on emerging digital media, AI tools, visual storytelling, and live briefs with industry partners like Disney and Aston Martin Lagonda, helping students develop responsive portfolios and strong creative voices.
JP Cooper Names Kyle Wilkinson as Creative Director
Platinum-selling British singer-songwriter JP Cooper has appointed Kyle Wilkinson and his studio, Haus of Thrills, to lead the creative direction and visual identity for the next chapter of his career. Following his departure from Island Records to become an independent artist, Cooper enlisted Wilkinson to oversee album artwork, music videos, and overall visual narrative.
The collaboration marks Haus of Thrills’ expansion into music visual direction, bringing an art-led, immersive studio approach to craft an honest, cohesive aesthetic around Cooper’s independent releases.
Design Bits
OPEN Strategy & Design Crafts Identity for Black Poetry
Independent consultancy OPEN Strategy & Design has developed the brand positioning, visual identity, and packaging for Black Poetry’s entry into India’s emerging carbonated coffee market. Embracing the creative philosophy of “beautiful vandalism,” OPEN rejected conventional coffee design cues to build an irreverent visual world.
Treating the beverage can itself as the brand’s primary media channel, the vibrant packaging is crafted to disrupt expectations on crowded retail shelves, sparking curiosity and inviting consumers to experience a completely reimagined category.
ASOS and Barbour Partner with Jimmy Turrell
ASOS and Barbour have teamed up with British graphic artist Jimmy Turrell to launch a capsule collection of ten one-of-a-kind wearable art pieces. Sourced from Barbour’s Re-loved circularity program, classic Bedale wax jackets were re-proportioned and hand-embellished by the ASOS design team before receiving Turrell’s signature analogue collage, screen-print, and paint artwork.
Nine jackets will launch exclusively on ASOS, while the tenth will be auctioned via a Centrepoint charity raffle to support efforts to end youth homelessness.
Desk Curiosities
Unbound Launches “The Great Unwashed” Campaign for Rock Face
Independent agency Unbound has unveiled “The Great Unwashed,” a multichannel campaign for men’s deodorant brand Rock Face. Fronted by CEO James Wilkinson, the campaign humorously encourages festivalgoers at Reading and Leeds to skip campsite shower queues for three days and rely on Rock Face’s new 72-hour antiperspirant.
Backed by an open letter and a Change.org petition demanding better festival sanitation facilities, the cheeky campaign spans out-of-home advertising, social media, and influencer partnerships to transform an existing festival habit into a bold brand statement.
Mr Jones Watches Announces Young Designers Open Call
Independent watchmaker Mr Jones Watches has launched a global competition inviting young creatives aged 18 to 25 to submit original watch designs. Focused on individual expression and storytelling, the brand will select one winner to collaborate directly with its design team and turn their artwork into a limited-edition timepiece.
Entrants must use the official template and submit their concepts by 25 September 2025.
