# rbl rebrands University of East Anglia around ‘Original By Nature’ ethos

Source: [design_week](https://www.designweek.co.uk/rbl-rebrands-university-of-east-anglia-around-original-by-nature-ethos/)

Founded in 1963 to challenge conventional higher education, the University of East Anglia established the UK’s first creative writing degree and developed a prominent reputation in climate science research. Over time, however, successive brand updates created a fragmented visual presence that struggled to communicate the institution’s distinct character in a crowded market.
Strategic brand agency rbl was commissioned to create a cohesive strategic platform capable of supporting UEA’s national and international ambitions. To define the strategic direction, rbl consulted over 100 stakeholders and gathered insight from more than 800 members of the university community. The resulting platform, built around the organising idea ‘Original by nature’, aims to articulate UEA’s history of independent thinking alongside its physical location and environmental commitment.
rbl strategy director Rhiannon Lowe says, “UEA didn’t need another refresh. It needed to rediscover what made it different in the first place, and build a brand with the heart and conviction to carry that truth into the future. The story was already there. It just wasn’t being told.”
Visual elements and Norfolk influences
The updated visual identity system builds directly on the new strategic positioning. The primary logo has been refined to include its home location in the wordmark. Additionally, the university’s long-standing glint symbol has evolved into a curved beacon graphic intended to serve as a primary visual mark across print and digital touchpoints.
The colour palette draws directly from the surrounding Norfolk landscape, prioritising visual warmth and digital accessibility. Bulrush green serves as the lead brand colour, referencing the natural surroundings of the campus. Clean layouts are paired with the Inter typeface to maintain clear visual structure across various media.
Verbal identity and rollout
Alongside the visual system, rbl restructured UEA’s verbal identity around four defining traits: courageous optimism, confidently individual, compassionate heart and collaborative spirit. Prior to launch, testing conducted with students, staff and key stakeholders indicated that 95% of respondents viewed the new brand as original and confident, while 100% stated it reflected the university’s overarching strategy.
UEA brand guardian Charlene Katuwawala says, “rbl surpassed every expectation and delivered a brand that will carry us confidently and purposefully forward. Far from redesigning the identity, they helped us rediscover what makes UEA special in the first place. They listened deeply, challenged thoughtfully, and brought our community with them throughout the process. This has given us much more than a new brand. We now have a renewed sense of confidence, connection and direction.”
rbl executive creative director and co-owner Adam Concar says, “Brands that mean more, achieve more. This was about leaning into what makes UEA truly special and creating a genuinely different brand with the platform to compete with confidence, nationally and globally, without ever sounding like anyone else.”
The updated identity is currently being deployed across UEA’s recruitment campaigns, internal communications, signage and digital channels.
