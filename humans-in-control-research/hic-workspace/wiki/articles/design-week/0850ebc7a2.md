# OneMay’s surreal visual identity for Sam Kerr’s WAWA sparkling water

Source: [design_week](https://www.designweek.co.uk/onemays-surreal-visual-identity-for-sam-kerrs-wawa-sparkling-water/)

Working alongside creative venture company Spark&Ace, OneMay set out to challenge how healthy beverages present themselves on retail shelves. The core ambition driving the project was to position sparkling water as a deliberate choice rather than a fallback option.
While traditional brands in the sector typically base their visual positioning on restraint, focusing heavily on low calories, zero sugar, and functional benefits, WAWA was developed to inject energy, fun, and personality into the category without hiding its health credentials.
The resulting creative strategy, built around the core platform Water With Aura, establishes a flexible identity system designed to give each product variant its own distinct character.
Surreal collages and distinct moods
At the centre of the packaging system is a soft, expressive wordmark, crafted with rounded forms to ensure high visibility on can. Each flavour in the line-up, including Sunset Drive, Garden of Zen, Purple Haze, Palm Halo, and Zing of Life, is treated as an individual mood. The packaging pairs saturated colour palettes with surreal, collage-style artwork, combining elements such as koalas wearing 3D glasses, retro vehicles set against desert landscapes, and cosmic figures.
Lee Weaver, co-founder and creative director of OneMay, said: “Most sparkling waters are sold as a compromise. Less sugar. Less calories. Less guilt. Less fun. With WAWA, we didn’t want ‘better for you’ to mean boring. We wanted sparkling water to feel like something people choose, not the drink they settle for.” Weaver explained that the design had to trigger an emotional response before opening, adding that “the flavour is the product; the feeling is the brand.”
Extending across brand touchpoints
The identity system was engineered to scale seamlessly beyond physical cans into digital content, merchandise, and out-of-home campaigns. The surreal collage graphics translate directly onto wearable merchandise such as printed tees and tote bags, as well as billboard artwork featuring copy such as “Out of this world fizzy water.”
Martin Porter, co-founder of OneMay, said: “Sparkling water often leans into quiet, functional, health-led cues, but WAWA needed more energy than that. We wanted each can to feel like its own little world—something bright, strange and desirable, with a brand system that could move naturally from packaging into social, outdoor, merchandise and lifestyle content.”
