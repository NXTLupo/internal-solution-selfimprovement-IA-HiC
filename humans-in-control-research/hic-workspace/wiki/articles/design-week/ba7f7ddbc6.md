# Ragged Edge’s rebrand for financial app Tabby takes a playful approach

Source: [design_week](https://www.designweek.co.uk/ragged-edges-rebrand-for-financial-app-tabby-takes-a-playful-approach/)

Founded in 2019, Tabby originally operated as a flexible payment service allowing users to split purchases interest-free. The platform has since expanded to serve over 25 million customers across Saudi Arabia and the United Arab Emirates. The visual redesign coincides with the introduction of Tabby Cash, a dedicated spending account that enables users to hold, send and spend funds within a single interface.
The underlying creative concept built by Ragged Edge centres on helping users reclaim control over their personal finances. To establish this position, the visual system eschews traditional fintech conventions such as gradients, glossy 3D renders and generic vector art in favour of hand-crafted elements and tactile details.
Jugal Paryani, chief marketing officer at Tabby, said: ‘Most financial brands are built to look expensive. But it’s mostly surface level with rarely an idea underneath. We didn’t want to look expensive, we wanted to be on your side. That meant throwing out the gradients and glossy 3D icons and building something with actual character and meaning, a brand that treats people as the ones in charge.’
Hand-drawn characters and custom typography
Central to the updated identity is a suite of hand-drawn characters known as The Monions, featuring tactile, colourful forms. Created to personify financial actions such as sending, spending, earning and saving, the characters translate abstract digital transactions into recognisable visual anchors.
The design team also introduced a bespoke typeface engineered to function with equal confidence across both Arabic and Latin scripts. In addition, the photography direction spotlights real regional customers and merchants in natural settings, avoiding polished corporate staging in favour of grounded portraits.
Jessica Bong-Woon, creative director at Ragged Edge, said: ‘For too long, we’ve treated money like something to avoid rather than something to direct. So naturally, our creative idea is to help people Show Money Who’s Boss. Money isn’t here to run our lives – it’s just a small unit of resource, waiting for our guidance and control.’
Pete Sutupov, design director at Tabby, said: ‘We wanted Tabby to have a personality you’d actually want to spend time with. The colour, the Monions, the small moments of play – that’s what makes something as serious as money feel light and more fun. The best products are simple but magical, and that’s what we’re after.’
Physical and digital rollout
The brand’s dynamic elements draw direct inspiration from the character illustrations, creating an organic motion system designed to respond dynamically to user interactions within the application interface.
Following an initial rollout across the Tabby mobile application, website, and digital channels, the refreshed identity is now expanding into physical environments through a major out-of-home advertising campaign across the UAE.
