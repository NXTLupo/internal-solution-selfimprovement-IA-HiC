# Pixel Artworks adapts The Butterfly Trail experience for Bangkok

Source: [design_week](https://www.designweek.co.uk/pixel-artworks-adapts-the-butterfly-trail-experience-for-bangkok/)

Global experience design studio Pixel Artworks has launched its mixed-reality attraction, The Butterfly Trail, on south-east Asia’s largest indoor digital screen at Happitat Bangkok, marking the experience’s debut in Asia.
First launched at Outernet London in September 2023 as Pixel Artworks’ debut Originals feature, the installation has since toured locations across the UK and Greece.
The installation is situated on the Grand Stairs inside Happitat Bangkok, a new 200,000 sq m lifestyle destination and retail complex operated by Axtra Happitat Co Ltd. The experience transports visitors into the botanical workshop and glasshouse of fictional explorer Professor Peter Pelgrin.
Using their own smartphones without requiring a bespoke app or dedicated hardware, visitors can interact with and release digital butterflies directly within the physical architecture.
For its Asian debut, Pixel Artworks specially adapted the experience to reflect its local surroundings. The digital butterflies have been re-engineered to replicate native species found in Happitat’s physical butterfly garden, including the lime swallowtail and the plain tiger. This creates a direct thematic bridge between the venue’s living flora and its indoor digital screens.
Visitors can transform digital chrysalises into CGI butterflies that land in the palms of their hands, with responsive animations scaling according to user movement. The augmented reality mechanics were developed alongside specialist studio Vamplify utilising Snap’s Camera Kit AR platform.
Riaz Farooq, senior creative director at Pixel Artworks, said: “The Butterfly Trail was designed to inspire people of all ages and nationalities as it’s based on the wonder of nature. It’s amazing to see it evolve and connect with audiences in different places as it takes flight in Asia.”
For Bangkok, we wanted to do more than simply adapt it to a different venue. We’ve rooted the experience in Happitat’s own story and environment by recreating species found in its butterfly garden and renewing the way visitors interact with them.”
Orada Kerdhong, president of Axtra Happitat Co Ltd, said: “The butterfly holds a special meaning for Happitat. Our destination was inspired by the belief that when nature is given the space to flourish, life follows. Through our collaboration with Pixel Artworks, this beautiful connection has become part of the Happitat experience, bringing together nature, imagination and technology.”
In its initial year, the installation recorded over 1.36m digital butterfly releases and 3.94m nectar vials collected across 378,000 active sessions, generating over 28m combined views across Instagram and TikTok.
The Bangkok launch is a further example of Pixel Artworks’ strategy for IP development, creating experiences engineered to adapt to distinct cultural markets and architectural layouts. The Butterfly Trail at Happitat Bangkok is free to the public and will run under an 18-month licence.
