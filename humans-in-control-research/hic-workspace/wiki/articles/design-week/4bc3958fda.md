# Derek&Eric bring bright colours and funky typography to the launch of DRZZL

Source: [design_week](https://www.designweek.co.uk/derekeric-bring-bright-colours-and-funky-typography-to-the-launch-fo-drzzl/)

I’m not entirely sure what my Mum in Spain would think if I were to turn up and ask here what she thought of olive oil in a squeezy bottle.
Or she may just be happy to see that it’s a colourful, funky and easy way to dispense oil over a solid? She’d certainly be happy to see me. I don’t visit enough.
Either way, standing out in a market full of muted greens, golds and… lighter greens takes a little bit of imagination which Derek&Eric have brought to influencer Natalia Rudin’s new olive oil brand. Neon typography shouts confidently and tells you what to do with the oil within.
It’s an antidote to a decade of supermarket aisles drowned in Mediterranean Nostalgia.
The creator of the 1.6 million-follower Instagram account Nats Nourishments, has launched DRZZL – a single-source olive oil aimed at young home cooks who prefer practical utensils to culinary pretension.
Packaged in ergonomic squeeze bottles, the product drops traditional anti-design trends in favour of stacked lettering and an energetic palette of dark green, orange and yellow. Branding was handled by London agency Derek&Eric, with custom lettering from typography firm Studio Drama.
“Olive oil has become a seriously crowded category, with lots of brands using the same visual ingredients,” said creative partner Adam Swan. “We stripped everything back and let the simple pleasure of drizzling great olive oil inspire the entire identity.”
The pivot from dark glass to plastic squeeze bottles mirrors a shift across modern food branding. Utility and high-contrast visuals designed for phone screens are quietly replacing old-world heritage.
Founder Natalia Rudin said the project was about creating “something people genuinely connect with” while cutting through typical kitchen snobbery.
