Source: [arxiv_stat_ml](https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.06512v1)

Email messages have been an important mode of communication, not only for work, but also for social interactions and marketing. When messages have time sensitive information, it becomes relevant for the sender to know what is the expected time within which the email will be read by the recipient.
