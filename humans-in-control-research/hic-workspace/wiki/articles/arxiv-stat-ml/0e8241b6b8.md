# Correlated Dynamics in Marketing Sensitivities

Source: [arxiv_stat_ml](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.11702v2)

Understanding individual customers' sensitivities to prices, promotions, brands, and other marketing mix elements is fundamental to a wide swath of marketing problems. An important but understudied aspect of this problem is the dynamic nature of these sensitivities, which change over time and vary across individuals.
