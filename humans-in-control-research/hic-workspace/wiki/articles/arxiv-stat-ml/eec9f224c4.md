# Teaching Uncertainty Quantification in Machine Learning through Use Cases

Source: [arxiv_stat_ml](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.08712v1)

Uncertainty in machine learning is not generally taught as general knowledge in Machine Learning course curricula. In this paper we propose a short curriculum for a course about uncertainty in machine learning, and complement the course with a selection of use cases, aimed to trigger discussion and let students play with the concepts of uncertainty in a programming setting.
