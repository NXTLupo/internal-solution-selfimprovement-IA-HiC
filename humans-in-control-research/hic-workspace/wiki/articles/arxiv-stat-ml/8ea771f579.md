# Risk factor aggregation and stress testing

Source: [arxiv_stat_ml](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.04511v1)

Stress testing refers to the application of adverse financial or macroeconomic scenarios to a portfolio. For this purpose, financial or macroeconomic risk factors are linked with asset returns, typically via a factor model. We expand the range of risk factors by adapting dimension-reduction techniques from unsupervised learning, namely PCA and autoencoders.
