# Distribution-free Contextual Dynamic Pricing

Source: [arxiv_stat_ml](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.07340v1)

Contextual dynamic pricing aims to set personalized prices based on sequential interactions with customers. At each time period, a customer who is interested in purchasing a product comes to the platform. The customer's valuation for the product is a linear function of contexts, including product and customer features, plus some random market noise.
