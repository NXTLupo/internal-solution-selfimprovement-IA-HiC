# Designing Tools for Semi-Automated Detection of Machine Learning Biases: An Interview Study

Source: [arxiv_stat_ml](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.07680v2)

Machine learning models often make predictions that bias against certain subgroups of input data. When undetected, machine learning biases can constitute significant financial and ethical implications. Semi-automated tools that involve humans in the loop could facilitate bias detection. Yet, little is known about the considerations involved in their design.
