Source: [arxiv_stat_ml](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22560v1)

This study introduces SEGOMOE, a Bayesian optimization tool for optimizing complex, computationally expensive systems, especially in aeronautics. It efficiently handles mixed design variables (continuous, discrete, categorical, hierarchical) using adaptive Gaussian process models. SEGOMOE combines expert models to address nonlinearities in objectives and constraints, leveraging the open-source Surrogate Modeling Toolbox (SMT).
