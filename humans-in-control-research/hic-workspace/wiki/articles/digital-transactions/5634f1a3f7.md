# Accertify Gains HIPAA Compliance and other Digital Transactions News briefs from 8/25/26

Source: [digital_transactions](https://www.digitaltransactions.net/accertify-gains-hipaa-compliance-and-other-digital-transactions-news-briefs-from-8-25-26/)

- Fraud-detection provider Accertify said it has gained HIPAA compliance, allowing the company to offer more services to health-care organizations.
- Bluefin Payment Systems LLC said it now offers point-of-sale products for card-present payments integrated with Visa Acceptance Solutions.
- Centerbase, a software provider to law firms, said it has integrated the electronic billing technology BillSync from software provider Scan Logic. The e-billing capability adds to embedded payments and other services the company has been developing.
- Payment performance is making a difference in player spending on video game sessions, with 64% of gamers having abandoned a gaming transaction due to checkout problems, according to Paysafe’s “The Experience Economy Report 2026: Video Gaming.” The research spanned 5,000 game players in eight countries, including the U.S., who had conducted a game-related transaction in the past 12 months.
- In related news, spending on video gaming in the U.S. will almost triple by 2035, from $37.1 billion to $107.6 billion, according to projections from payment-technology provider Ebanx and World Data Lab.
- Cryptocurrency exchange Bybit said its users can buy any of 36 digital currencies using Apple Pay at no fee until Sept. 24. Bybit claims more than 80 million users globally.
- Nacha named Flagright as a Nacha Preferred Partner for ACH Compliance, Fraud Monitoring, and Risk and Fraud Prevention. Nacha is the rules-setting body for the automated clearing house network.
- Fideo Intelligence launched Fideo Lens, a real-time platform to aid in fraud investigations by detecting hidden links among identities, accounts, devices, and behavior patterns.
- Co-brand platform Imprint Payments Inc. announced $2 billion in new debt funding capacity created since April in a bid to boost the company’s growth prospects. The 6-year-old company counts Booking.com, H-E-B, and Shell among its clients.
- Payments platform Lightspeed Commerce Inc. appointed Rupal Hollenbeck to its board of directors, effective Oct. 1. A former Intel and Oracle executive, Hollenbeck is currently president and chief business officer at NextSilicon.
