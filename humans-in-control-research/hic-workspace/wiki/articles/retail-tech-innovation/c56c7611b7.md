# Christine Foster departs Kroger Precision Marketing to join The Home Depot’s retail media offshoot

Source: [retail_tech_innovation](https://retailtechinnovationhub.com/home/2026/8/25/christine-foster-departs-kroger-precision-marketing-to-join-the-home-depots-retail-media-offshoot)

Christine Foster departs Kroger Precision Marketing to join The Home Depot’s retail media offshoot
The Home Depot reports that Christine Foster will join the retailer as General Manager and Vice President at Orange Apron Media, its retail media arm, effective 8th September.
Foster brings with her 20 years of experience across retail media, digital marketing, content creation and media strategy. She joins from Kroger Precision Marketing (KPM), where she served as Group Vice President of Commercial Strategy and Operations.
“Christine’s deep experience, paired with a proven track record of building high performing teams and driving measurable growth, makes her the right leader to grow Orange Apron Media into an even more powerful engine for our brand partners. I know her vision and leadership will help us accelerate our strategy and unlock new value for the brands and customers we serve. We are thrilled to welcome her to The Home Depot,” says Molly Battin, Chief Marketing Officer at The Home Depot.
OAM connects brands with home improvement shoppers through advertising opportunities across in-store and online channels. Advertisers can work through managed service offerings or Orange Access, OAM’s self-service portal, to execute campaigns across DIY and pro audiences.
“The Home Depot is one of the most beloved brands in retail deeply rooted in serving both DIY and pro customers. OAM is a powerful retail media engine built on top of that strong foundation,” says Foster. “I look forward to continuing this momentum alongside the talented team, expanding our media capabilities, removing friction for our partners, and delivering impactful, highly relevant experiences for our shoppers.”
Battin says: “OAM continues to evolve as a powerful growth engine for our suppliers, partners and an expanding range of non-endemic brands, Christine is joining us at just the right time. Her leadership will be invaluable in helping us unlock new opportunities, strengthen our offerings and deliver even greater value for the brands we serve.”
