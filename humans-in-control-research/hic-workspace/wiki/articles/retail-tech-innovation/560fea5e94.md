# Barbour becomes the first to tap new modular retail display system designed to reduce store refits

Source: [retail_tech_innovation](https://retailtechinnovationhub.com/home/2026/8/25/barbour-becomes-the-first-to-tap-new-modular-retail-display-system-designed-to-reduce-store-refits)

Barbour becomes the first to tap new modular retail display system designed to reduce store refits
Barbour has become the first brand to use a new British designed modular retail display system that, according to those involved, enables retailers to refresh stores without replacing entire fixture schemes, helping cut the cost, disruption and waste associated with repeated refits.
The ReFrame system, designed, developed and manufactured by Ripple, has made its commercial debut at Blenheim Palace, where it has been used to create a dedicated Barbour retail destination within the visitor attraction's existing store.
David Wolfenden, Managing Director at Ripple, says: “Retailers are refreshing stores more frequently than ever before, but many are still replacing perfectly good fixtures every time layouts change. We developed ReFrame to offer a more flexible alternative.”
“By retaining the core framework and changing only the merchandising elements, retailers can adapt stores quickly, reduce waste, minimise disruption and protect their investment for the long-term. The first commercial use of ReFrame within the Barbour retail space at Blenheim Palace demonstrates how the system can deliver a premium branded retail environment that is designed to adapt over time.”
Judy Bendall, Head of Retail at Blenheim Palace, says: “We'd been looking for some time for new fixtures and fittings for our Barbour retail space, but we were struggling to find a solution that offered the quality and flexibility we needed. The installation process was incredibly smooth, with most of the units pre-assembled, so the whole display was installed and ready within just a few hours. The finished result has completely elevated the clothing area, creating a professional retail environment that both our staff and senior management team love.”
“Customers can now see the products much more clearly, making the space easier to shop. The flexibility of the system is a real advantage too. We can easily add or remove shelving, reposition display arms and adapt the layout as our stock changes. It’s a high quality solution that offers excellent value for money, and I’d happily recommend it to other retailers.”
