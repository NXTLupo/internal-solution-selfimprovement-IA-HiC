# OneView Commerce set to supply new EPoS System for the Post Office as part of Horizon replacement

Source: [retail_tech_innovation](https://retailtechinnovationhub.com/home/2026/8/25/oneview-commerce-lands-deal-to-supply-epos-system-for-post-office-as-part-of-horizon-replacement)

OneView Commerce set to supply new EPoS System for the Post Office as part of Horizon replacement
OneView Commerce has signed a contract with the Post Office to deliver a new electronic point of sale (EPoS) system across all its branches. The deal is valued at £141 million. It is part of the Post Office's wider programme to replace its legacy Horizon system and modernise technology across its branch network, a central pillar of its five-year transformation plan.
OneView will work alongside the Post Office and Accenture, which is separately leading the transition of day-to-day Horizon operations and back end development, to deliver the new EPoS platform.
Built on OneView's cloud native, API first architecture, the solution will enable EPoS, mobile services, customer engagement and clienteling and self-service kiosks, with the aim of giving postmasters modern, reliable tools to serve their communities.
"We bring a proven commercial solution built on the power of cloud native technology that will enable Post Office to achieve their EPoS objectives without requiring customisation," says OneView Chief Executive Officer Linda Palanza. "Our team's deep experience in both retail and complex, large scale digital transformation in postal allows us to deliver a modern EPoS platform that transforms daily operations, while ensuring services are delivered with the transparency and reliability postmasters need to serve their customers."
The programme is underway to establish plans, timelines and delivery arrangements for the new technology. The new EPoS system will be configured, tested and introduced in phases.
