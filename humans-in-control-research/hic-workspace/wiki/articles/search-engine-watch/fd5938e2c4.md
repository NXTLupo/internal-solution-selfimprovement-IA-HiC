# Is Google changing its homepage buttons again?

Source: [search_engine_watch](https://searchenginewatch.com/is-google-changing-its-homepage-buttons-again/)

Is Google changing its homepage buttons again?
Google appears to be making more changes to its homepage design. A couple of weeks ago, we covered Google adding new buttons below the search bar, and Google confirmed this test pointed users toward AI Mode. At the time, there were four buttons: “Create images,” “Ask about files,” “Brainstorm,” and “I’m feeling lucky.”
As a reminder, Google’s Robby Stein, VP of Product, confirmed this test on X, writing: “This is a small test on desktop to help people find new things they can do with Search. There is no impact to how the core search box works — just add your question and hit enter as usual (which is how most people search anyways!).”
