# Google changes call lead charging policy for Local Services Ads

Source: [search_engine_watch](https://searchenginewatch.com/google-changes-call-lead-charging-policy-for-local-services-ads/)

Google changes call lead charging policy for Local Services Ads
Google has informed Local Services Ads customers that the charging policy for call leads will change starting October 1, 2026.
The major change is that missed calls received during business hours can now also be considered charged leads.
Joe spotted this and shared it on X.
Here is what the screenshot shows:
“On October 1, 2026, we are updating how and when you are charged for call leads originating from Local Services Ads.
We remain committed to protecting the integrity of your leads through the following measures:
Customers turn to Local Services Ads every day with immediate needs, expecting to connect quickly with a trusted local professional. This policy update helps ensure our platform continues to meet these customer expectations while rewarding businesses that provide excellent responsiveness.”
