# The GEO conferences worth attending in 2026 and 2027

Source: [search_engine_watch](https://searchenginewatch.com/geo-conferences/)

The GEO conferences worth attending in 2026 and 2027
The first problem with finding a good GEO conference is deciding what the field is actually called, believe it or not.
Some organizers use generative engine optimization. Others prefer answer engine optimization, AI search, LLM SEO or AI visibility. The terms overlap, the definitions move around, and a few events appear to choose whichever acronym looks best on the ticket page that month.
Search demand gives us a clearer editorial answer. Public US snapshots show roughly 7,900 monthly searches for “generative engine optimization” compared with about 2,400 for “answer engine optimization.” The apparently larger volume attached to the bare acronym “AEO” is misleading: much of it belongs to American Eagle Outfitters and the Authorized Economic Operator customs programme, not search marketing.
That is why we are calling this our guide to the best GEO conferences, while still including serious AEO and AI-search events.
We checked the remaining 2026 calendar and every credible 2027 announcement we could find. We verified dates, prices, formats, access rules and published programmes against organizer pages. We also separated dedicated AI-search events from broader SEO and marketing conferences that happen to contain one GEO session.
If your interests extend beyond AI search, our separate guide to the best SEO conferences in 2026 and 2027 covers the wider calendar, including technical SEO, local search, publishing, ecommerce and international events.
There is no honest universal winner. A global brand operationalizing AI-search measurement needs a different room from a technical writer restructuring a documentation estate. Start here.
The strongest agenda on paper is not automatically the best purchase. GEO Conference has the cleanest specialist proposition in the US, but it costs $900 before travel and has no recordings.
The free online summit is much smaller, but its cost-adjusted value is difficult to argue with. Masters of Search may offer the best European operator density, but admission is curated.
The main calendar only includes events where AI-mediated discovery is the product, not a decorative track. “From” prices exclude tax, fees, travel and optional workshops unless stated otherwise.
September 22–24, 2026 | Online | Registration tiers vary
ConVEx IDEAS is the unusual entry in this list, and that is its strength. The Center for Information-Development Management is not staging a generic marketing summit. Its audience works on technical documentation, knowledge systems and structured information, and the 2026 theme explicitly pairs answer engine optimization with SEO.
That makes it relevant to a problem most marketing-led GEO agendas only touch in passing: how a large body of product, support or technical knowledge becomes retrievable, interpretable and useful to answer systems. If your organization owns thousands of help articles, manuals or developer pages, the documentation team may have more influence over AI answers than the social team, even if nobody has put “GEO” in its job titles yet.
The format is online across three days. The organizer’s public event listing exposed a very wide registration range when checked, so we have not reduced that to a misleading “from” price. Check the tier and inclusions directly.
Best for: technical writers, content-operations leads, knowledge managers and documentation-heavy software or manufacturing businesses.
Skip it if: your main goal is brand marketing, digital PR or a room full of acquisition leaders.
Question to ask before registering: how much of the final agenda is about retrieval and answer visibility, and how much assumes prior information-architecture knowledge?
October 1, 2026 | Berlin | German | €499 + VAT in-house; €899 + VAT agencies
GEO KNOW HOW is a dedicated German-language conference organized by Claneo. The main event runs from 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. at Am Flutgraben 2 in Berlin, with separate workshop options on September 30 and October 2.
The ticket structure tells you who the organizer wants in the room. Brands and in-house attendees pay €499 plus VAT; agencies and service providers pay €899 plus VAT. A conference-and-workshop package for brands and in-house teams was €1,499 plus VAT when checked. That is not a beginner price, and the language requirement narrows the audience further. For a German-speaking company building its first formal AI-search programme, however, that concentration may be more valuable than a larger English-language event where GEO competes with six other tracks.
The event positions itself as Germany’s first conference for generative engine optimization and focuses on visibility and citations across systems such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity. Treat the word “first” as organizer positioning, not a quality score. The better signal is whether the final sessions show methods, samples, failure cases and measurement limitations rather than renamed SEO checklists.
Best for: German-speaking heads of search, content and digital visibility, especially in-house.
Skip it if: you need an English programme, a low-cost introduction or broad paid-search coverage.
Budget note: compare the €1,499 workshop package with sending two people to the main conference. The better choice depends on whether you need hands-on design or cross-functional adoption.
October 7, 2026 | Online | 9–11 a.m. Mountain Time | Free
The AI SEO & GEO Online Summit is only two hours long, so it should not be compared with a two-day conference. It is nevertheless the lowest-risk way into the category: free registration, live YouTube delivery, five speakers, Q&A and recordings.
The announced programme has more specificity than many paid events. It includes a session drawing on SE Ranking research, a local-AEO presentation based on Duda’s study of more than 850,000 small-business sites, and sessions on turning content strategy into a repeatable operating system. The host also runs an AI SEO service and platform, so expect some agency context even though the page promises education rather than an end-of-session pitch.
This is a useful test before asking an employer to fund a larger event. Watch with a live project open. If the sessions change the prompt set, measurement design or content system you are building, you have evidence that a deeper specialist conference may be worth the spend.
Best for: global teams, freelancers, small businesses and anyone who wants an evidence-oriented introduction without travel.
Skip it if: networking is the main value you want or you need a full-day technical workshop.
Best use of the recording: assign each session to the person who owns that system, then reconvene for one hour to decide what is testable. “Everybody watch this when you have time” is where free conference value goes to die.
October 15–16, 2026 | Disneyland Paris | French | From €200 excl. VAT when checked
The SEO & GEO Summit calls itself France’s largest SEO and GEO event and moves to the convention centre at Disney Hotel New York – The Art of Marvel for 2026. It has also widened beyond search into SEA, social advertising, content, automation, CRM and acquisition.
That breadth is either the reason to go or the reason not to. A French digital director who needs search, paid media, content and AI discovery in one trip gets two days of cross-channel context. A specialist who wants every hour devoted to prompt sampling and citation measurement may find the programme too broad.
When checked, a one-day pass was €200 excluding VAT and a two-day pass €400 excluding VAT. The organizer’s published schedule showed those prices rising to €225 and €450 respectively from September 1, then €250 and €500 from October 1. The page’s formatting makes the sequence harder to parse than it should be, so confirm the amount shown at checkout.
The organizer has published many speakers and a substantial programme, but the event’s marketing language is exuberant. Judge individual sessions on whether they disclose data and implementation detail, not on promises of “secret” strategies.
Best for: French-speaking SEO, acquisition and digital leaders who want a large regional event.
Skip it if: you want a small English-language research room or a programme exclusively about generative discovery.
Travel decision: the venue makes this a destination event. Do not let the setting turn a one-day learning need into an expensive two-night trip unless the agenda and attendee mix justify it.
October 22, 27 and 29, 2026 | In person | Free by request
Profound’s Zero Click series is the most geographically useful AI-search circuit left in 2026: London on October 22, Sydney on October 27 and Singapore on October 29. Attendance is free, but it is not open in the ordinary “claim a ticket” sense. Space is limited and each person must request a place.
The promise is proprietary research, customers sharing real playbooks and networking with people working on AI search. The trade-off is product context. Profound sells enterprise AI-visibility software, and its customers and research naturally sit close to that worldview. Vendor ownership does not make the material useless; it means attendees should distinguish broadly applicable findings from conclusions that depend on a particular measurement platform.
These events are in-person only. The organizer says it will share recaps and recorded snippets, but there is no virtual ticket. Do not book non-refundable travel until the request is accepted.
Best for: in-house AI-search leaders and senior marketers in the UK or Asia-Pacific who can get admitted without building an entire trip around the event.
Skip it if: you need guaranteed entry, full recordings or vendor-neutral platform comparisons.
Question to take into the room: how does the research handle response volatility, personalization, geography, model version and prompt sampling? A dashboard can be precise and still measure an unrepresentative question set.
