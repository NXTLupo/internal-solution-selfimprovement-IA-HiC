# Why Tubi’s New Global Campaign &#8216;Feels&#8217; About Right

Source: [adweek](https://www.adweek.com/convergent-tv/tubi-global-campaign-feels-about-right/)

Tubi doesn’t want you to feel lonely. In fact, the FAST service wants to tap into all your feelings as it launches its new global brand campaign, “Find Any Feeling For Free,” banking on its humongous content library to find exactly what you are looking for regardless of your mood.
This new campaign, its largest to date by volume of creative assets and developed in conjunction with its creative agency of record, Mischief @ No Fixed Address, is built around the insight that people will go to ridiculous lengths to feel something. And if they can’t quite capture it, then they can simply turn to Tubi.
