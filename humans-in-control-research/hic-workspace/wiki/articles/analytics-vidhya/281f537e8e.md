# Top 7 Free AI Automation Courses with Certificates

Source: [analytics_vidhya](https://www.analyticsvidhya.com/blog/2026/08/free-ai-automation-courses-with-certificates/)

You don’t need to know anything about AI automation to get started. There are plenty of free courses that can take you from the basics to building your own automations, even if you’re starting from zero.
Some are completely beginner-friendly, while others are better once you’re comfortable with the basics. We’ve picked courses covering popular tools like n8n, Make, and Zapier, to help you build AI-powered workflows and agents.
Essentials: Your First Workflows – n8n | Gateway to AI Automation
This is a good starting point if you have never used n8n. The course covers workflows, credentials, triggers, data structures, transformations, expressions, and hands-on exercises. It is designed for learners with no prior n8n experience.
What makes this course special?
Best for: beginners who want to understand n8n properly before building AI workflows.
Automation to AI Agents: Foundation – Make | Agentic automation
Make’s free learning path moves from AI fundamentals and LLMs into AI automation and agents in Make. It contains six courses plus an assessment and takes roughly 2–3.5 hours. Completing it can earn you the AI Automation Explorer badge.
What makes this course special?
Best for: learners who want a structured introduction to Make-based AI automation.
n8n – A Complete Guide to Automation Tool – AV | Full guide of n8n
This course takes a more practical route. You learn n8n’s visual editor, triggers, integrations, APIs, multi-step workflows, and AI agents. It also includes hands-on projects such as an AI chat agent and content creator agent. It is currently free, takes about an hour, and is listed at intermediate level.
What makes this course special?
Best for: learners who want to learn n8n by building.
AI Builder Path – Zapier | Infrastructural automation
Zapier’s AI Builder Path takes you from basic Zaps to AI agents. The four-course path covers triggers, actions, app connections, AI utilities, decision logic, and building an AI agent. It takes about two hours and is free with a Zapier account.
What makes this course special?
Best for: beginners who want the easiest entry into workflow automation.
Build AI Automations with Make/Zapier – FreeAcademy | No-code automation
This short no-code course focuses on building automations visually with Make and Zapier, including AI workflows using ChatGPT and Claude. It covers email automation, social content, data pipelines, and multi-step workflows.
What makes this course special?
Best for: non-technical learners who want to start building immediately.
Automate and Extend Your Solutions with AI – Microsoft | Enterprise automation
Microsoft’s learning path focuses on combining traditional workflow automation with AI. You work with cloud flows, Dataverse, approvals, and generative AI through AI Builder. The path contains four modules and takes around 3.5 hours.
What makes this course special?
Best for: professionals working with Microsoft tools and business workflows.
Multi AI Agent Systems with crewAI – DeepLearning.AI | Technical Automation
This course teaches you how to create teams of AI agents for multi-step business processes. You build systems for research, customer support, outreach, event planning, financial analysis, and other workflows using crewAI.
What makes this course special?
Best for: learners who already know the basics and want to build more advanced agentic automations.
There is no single best AI automation course. Your choice mostly depends on what you want to build.
For n8n, start with the official n8n course or Analytics Vidhya’s practical guide. For no-code automation, Make, Zapier, and FreeAcademy are strong starting points. And for AI agents, UiPath, crewAI, and Nate Herk go deeper.
