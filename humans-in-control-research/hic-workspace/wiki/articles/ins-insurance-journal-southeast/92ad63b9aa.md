# Anderson Insurance Agency Launches in Coral Springs

Source: [ins_insurance_journal_southeast](https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/southeast/2026/06/29/875566.htm)

A south Florida businessman is stepping into the insurance agency with the launch of Anderson Insurance Solutions, based in Coral Springs.
Matthew E. Anderson, a licensed agent for life, health and property-casualty lines, said his agency will offer life, health, and, by the end of the year, homeowners’ insurance, through the independent agency.
He said last week that it’s too soon to say which property insurance carriers he may be appointed with.
Anderson has an extensive background in business development, corporate growth and partnerships in New York. He has been appointed with life insurance companies since 2024, the Florida Department of Financial Services records show.
“Before starting this agency, my time in business development and partnerships shaped how I work with clients today, focusing on making complex decisions feel simple and ensuring families and businesses feel well-informed when protecting their futures,” he said in a news release.
Anderson said he was ready to step away from the corporate world and come back to Florida.
“Florida is home, and given how unique the Insurance market is here, I saw an opportunity to be a trusted and guided resource for individuals, families, and businesses,” he said.
The agency may have plenty of insurance carriers to work with. At least 20 new property insurers have entered the Florida arena since state lawmakers in late 2022 approved major statutory changes that have been credited with resuscitating the once-distressed Florida market.
