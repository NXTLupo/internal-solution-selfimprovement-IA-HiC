Supply Chain Short Takes: Conductix-Wampfler - Improving uptime with dedicated service
Learn the importance of having a well-designed service strategy any time new production and automation systems are added.
Most modern distribution centers have a variety of automated systems to process orders more efficiently. Keeping these systems running is critical in order for the facility to meet its service level agreements and keep customers happy. In this short video discussion, Don Jones, director of service, Americas at Conductix-Wampfler, shares with DC Velocity's David Maloney why it is important to have a well-designed service strategy any time new production and automation systems are added.
