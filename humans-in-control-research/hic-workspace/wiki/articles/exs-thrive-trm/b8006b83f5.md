Executive Search Quarterly Report: Q2 2024 Midway through 2024, and it feels like—maybe—the market is finding its footing. Though executive search volume dropped 3% quarter-over-quarter, this can be seen as a win for a market used to taking two steps forward and one step back. The last time the executive search market seemed to be rebounding, the next quarter saw a 13% drop in search volume, erasing nearly all the momentum from the previous six months. Against that backdrop, Q2 feels like a positive development. Download Resource Executive Search Quarterly Report: Q1 2024 The start of 2024 felt a lot like the end of 2024: solid. Executive search volume grew 8% year-over-year in Q1 and 12% quarter-over-quarter—similar to Q4 2023. It has been almost three years since we have seen more than two consecutive quarters of consistent performance in the executive search market. While there is a general sense of optimism, it’s hard to ignore macro developments as indicators that there may be limits to the remaining rebound.
