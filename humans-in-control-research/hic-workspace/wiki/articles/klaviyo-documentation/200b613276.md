# Getting started with Canva for email

Source: [Klaviyo documentation](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/48509573327515-Getting-started-with-Canva-for-email)

Learn how to design an email in Canva, export it to Klaviyo as an HTML email template, and find it in Klaviyo to edit or use in a campaign or flow.
Note: This integration handles the export of HTML email templates from Canva to Klaviyo. It does not handle the importing or exporting of standalone images between the two platforms. For image syncing, see Getting started with Canva for images.
Before connecting Canva to Klaviyo for email:
Email designs must be created using Canva's Email template type. Standard presentation or social media designs cannot be exported as email templates.
Your Canva design environment is now connected to your Klaviyo account.
Note: Each Canva user can connect to 1 Klaviyo account at a time. You can connect 1 Canva team per Klaviyo account. If you manage multiple Klaviyo accounts, you will need to disconnect and reconnect each time you switch accounts.
Once your account is connected, you can export your Canva email design to Klaviyo as an HTML template.
To use your exported template in Klaviyo:
To send the email, select your exported template when creating a campaign or flow. Exporting from Canva saves the template in Klaviyo; it does not send an email.
Tip: Canva email templates are exported as HTML. You can edit the code and add Klaviyo personalization tags in the HTML editor. Drag-and-drop features, such as product blocks, require a hybrid template. See How to create a hybrid email template.
Before exporting, you can preview how your design will look on desktop and mobile.
After exporting, click Preview Email in Klaviyo’s HTML editor to check the content and personalization. Send test emails to check rendering in the inboxes you use; the on-screen preview does not verify rendering across email clients. Learn how to preview and send test emails in Klaviyo.
You've now connected Canva to Klaviyo for email, learned how to export a Canva email design as an HTML template, and know where to find and use your template inside Klaviyo.
