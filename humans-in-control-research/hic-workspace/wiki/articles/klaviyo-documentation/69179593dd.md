Learn how to make your emails and sign-up forms more accessible. When designing your emails and sign-up forms, you’ll want to make sure your designs are accessible to all of your recipients.
Make your subject lines and preview text descriptive, enticing, and short.

Klaviyo sign-up forms include keyboard and screen reader support, but you still need to check your form’s content and design for accessibility. Visitors can use Tab to move between fields and buttons and press Enter on a focused submit button. They can press Escape to close popup and flyout forms.
To make keyboard focus easier to see, select Edit styles in the form editor and choose a contrasting color under Focus outline.
Just like email design, you'll want to follow some basic guidelines to ensure that all of your recipients will get the information you want to share with them through your forms. When designing forms:
As you build your sign-up form, Klaviyo checks for common accessibility issues. Select Alerts in the form editor, then review Active alerts and follow each card’s instructions to fix the identified issues.
Automated checks do not catch every accessibility issue. Test your form with a keyboard and screen reader before publishing. To make your updates live on your site, select Publish or schedule, choose the Publish now option, and click Publish now.
