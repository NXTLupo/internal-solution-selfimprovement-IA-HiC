# Join Us for Framework by Figma: Our Global Design Systems Event | Figma Blog

Source: [Figma documentation](https://www.figma.com/blog/join-us-for-framework-by-figma-our-global-design-systems-event/)

Join us for Framework by Figma: Our global design systems event
We’re bringing together the design systems community on April 16 to share new features, best practices, and a peek into what’s coming next.
Share Join us for Framework by Figma: Our global design systems event
Design systems have come a long way in recent years, growing from simple style guides to become the foundation upon which any healthy product is built. At Figma, we believe in empowering design systems teams with the tools and knowledge to harness their full potential. That’s why we’re thrilled to announce Framework by Figma, a global design systems event happening April 16. Join us at the half-day event to be among the first to learn about our newest features and best practices for applying them to your work. We have so much in store for you, including:
- In-depth explorations of new features to supercharge your design systems
- Insights from industry leaders like Verizon on their design systems journeys
- Talks from our product teams on where we’re headed next
- Design to code roundtable with Bumble, GitHub, Hewlett Packard
- Expert Q&A where our team tackles your most pressing design systems questions
At Framework 2024, we announced Code Connect for developers, typography and gradient variables, and our Library Analytics API to help you drive design system adoption across your entire organization. Read more.
New features and best practices
Design systems have become a critical foundation for product design and development, but their implementation and management can be complex We talked to several industry experts about how they’re managing the future of design systems—from tooling to automation to accessibility.The future of design systems is complicated
As Sho Kuwamoto, Vice President of Product at Figma, writes: “We believe it’s our job to help you make changes to designs quickly, whether you have structured your designs ahead of time or not. When you look at Figma, almost every feature that helps you structure your file has a free-spirited cousin feature for the times when you want to keep things loose.” That guiding philosophy—balancing structure and freeform design—is one of the ways we try to future-proof Figma.
We’ll share more about new capabilities to make design systems more powerful and easier to adopt, as well as features to connect your system closer to code. Our product sessions will dive deep into strategies for structuring and maintaining your system.
Uniting designers and engineers
One of the key goals of Framework is to bring together designers and engineers working on design systems. We’ll have sessions tailored to these roles and their intersections, covering topics from design principles to technical implementation—including a new feature designed to bring design and development closer together, showcasing the critical role this cross-functional collaboration plays in building successful design systems.
A global stage for design systems
While the sessions are virtual, the event is a celebration of our global design systems community. Framework will be coming to our community in Asia virtually on April 18, and in-person (and virtually) in Tokyo on April 23. Stay tuned for more about in-person meetups happening across London and other cities to connect with local design systems makers and builders. No matter where you are, however, our virtual stream ensures you’ll have the best seat in the house for the main stage announcements and sessions.
Reserve your seat to be part of our virtual design systems event. Sign up now.
Save your spot, dim the lights, and get ready
Framework is just the beginning—what comes next is up to you. We can’t wait to share what we’ve been building and learn from all of you shaping the future of design systems. See you there!
