# HeaRT: A Hierarchical Circuit Reasoning Tree-Based Agentic Framework for AMS Design Optimization

Source: [arXiv CS.AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.19669v2)

Conventional AI-driven AMS design automation algorithms remain constrained by their reliance on high-quality datasets to capture underlying circuit behavior, coupled with poor transferability across architectures, and a lack of adaptive mechanisms. This work proposes HeaRT, a hierarchical circuit reasoning-based agentic framework for automation loops and a step toward adaptive, human-style design optimization.
