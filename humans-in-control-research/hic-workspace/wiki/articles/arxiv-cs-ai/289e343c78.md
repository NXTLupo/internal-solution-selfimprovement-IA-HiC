# Generative AI and Agency in Education: A Critical Scoping Review and Thematic Analysis

Source: [arXiv CS.AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.00631v2)

This scoping review examines the relationship between Generative AI (GenAI) and agency in education, analyzing the literature available through the lens of Critical Digital Pedagogy. Following PRISMA-ScR guidelines, we collected 10 studies from academic databases focusing on both learner and teacher agency in GenAI-enabled environments.
