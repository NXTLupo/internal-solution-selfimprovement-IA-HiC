# AI and the EU Digital Markets Act: Addressing the Risks of Bigness in Generative AI

Source: [arXiv CS.AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.02033v1)

As AI technology advances rapidly, concerns over the risks of bigness in digital markets are also growing. The EU's Digital Markets Act (DMA) aims to address these risks. Still, the current framework may not adequately cover generative AI systems that could become gateways for AI-based services. This paper argues for integrating certain AI software as core platform services and classifying certain developers as gatekeepers under the DMA.
