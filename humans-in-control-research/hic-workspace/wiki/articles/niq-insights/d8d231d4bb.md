- Are the insights global, regional, or market-specific?
- NIQ insights span all three levels: global, regional, and market‑specific. The NIQ platform publishes global perspectives (e.g., worldwide consumer trends), regional analyses (such as Europe, APAC, or the Americas), and highly granular, country‑ or category‑level reports tailored to specific markets and industries. 
- How can I use these insights for decision-making across marketing, sales, or category teams?
- NIQ insights are designed to support cross‑functional decision‑making by connecting consumer behavior, category performance, pricing, distribution, and innovation signals. Marketing teams can identify shifting shopper needs and brand equity drivers, sales teams can optimize go‑to‑market and customer strategies, and category teams can improve assortment, pricing, and shelving decisions using real, omnichannel purchase data.
