The next generation of creative talent: Introducing It’s Nice That’s Ones to Watch 2026
Meet 72 of the most exciting emerging graphic designers, illustrators, animators, artists, filmmakers and photographers from around the world, selected by It’s Nice That.

We are so excited to finally present It’s Nice That’s Ones to Watch for 2026! Our curated list of next generation creative talent has returned and it’s bigger than ever before, featuring a total of 72 creatives from 16 different countries. You can now discover an abundance of all of this delightful work via It’s Nice That’s dedicated Ones to Watch 2026 directory below.
When we came to close submissions on our search for the best new names in the industry this year we were astounded to discover that, in total, we had received double the amount of applications that had landed in our lap for Ones to Watch in 2025. The whole It’s Nice That team clubbed together to carefully pore over every application, and we were so impressed by the visual standard, original thinking, innovative ideation and keen creative eyes behind all of this year’s projects and portfolios – it was tough to whittle down to say the least!

This is a call-to-action for all you creative directors out there – consider this lot for your next commission or collaboration, you won’t regret it!
