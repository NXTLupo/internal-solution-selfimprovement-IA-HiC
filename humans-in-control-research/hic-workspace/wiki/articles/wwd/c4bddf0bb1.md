# EXCLUSIVE: Charlotte Tilbury Unveils Next Chapter of Airbrush Flawless With Jerry Hall, Grace Van Patten and More

Source: [wwd](https://wwd.com/beauty-industry-news/color-cosmetics/charlotte-tilbury-airbrush-flawless-campaign-1239146519/)

Charlotte Tilbury is ushering in a new era of its blockbuster franchise Airbrush Flawless with a star-studded campaign, featuring storied model Jerry Hall; “Tell Me Lies” lead Grace Van Patten; models Leni Klum, Noor Stars, Duckie Thot, and Song Wenyao, and music artists Lola Indigo and Louane.
Taking cues from ’90s matte minimalism, the campaign centers around two new innovations: the Airbrush Flawless Blur Loose Powder, $50, and the Airbrush Flawless Blush Blur, $43, both available exclusively on the Sephora app starting Friday and at charlottetilbury.com beginning Saturday.
Designed to blur and sculpt the face, the powder promises to deliver up to 24 hours of oil and shine control, while the blush is said to help achieve a smooth and natural flush of color. Existing products in the assortment include the brand’s bestselling Hollywood Flawless Filter, the Airbrush Flawless Finish Refillable Blurring and Setting Powder and the Airbrush Flawless Blurring and Matte Full Coverage Foundation.
“Airbrush Flawless has never just been about how you look, it’s about how you feel,” the namesake founder told WWD. “I always say that flawless is a feeling, and this campaign’s global cast of women brings that philosophy to life, celebrating beauty in all its forms across different ages, skin tones, styles and generations.
“With this campaign I wanted to create something that captured the confidence, empowerment and self-expression at the heart of Airbrush Flawless, inspired by the energy of iconic ’90s fashion and beauty editorials but reimagined for today,” she continued.
The corresponding film and imagery captures each star in what Tilbury identifies as their “need-state” — their beauty archetype, if you will — which is either Flawless Glam, Flawless Matte or Flawless Fresh. Flawless Glam is a heavily contoured and sculpted aesthetic; Flawless Matte is a no-shine look, and Flawless Fresh is modern skin-first makeup. Van Patten is the model Flawless Fresh face, while creator Noor Stars is the quintessential Flawless Matte girl. Hall, on the other hand, is the “epitome of timeless glamour,” added Tilbury.
In an exclusive statement sent to WWD, Hall expressed her admiration for the award-winning makeup artist and the creative behind the campaign. “Having spent much of my life in front of the camera, it’s taught me that beauty is deeply personal. It’s about expressing who you are and feeling like the best version of yourself. That’s why ‘Flawless Is a Feeling’, the message at the heart of the campaign, resonates so strongly with me. When you feel confident, comfortable and happy in your own skin, it shines through, and that’s something worth celebrating,” she said.
Van Patten shared a similar sentiment. “I’ve always loved Charlotte’s approach to beauty because it’s never about hiding who you are. It’s about enhancing what you already have and helping you feel confident. That’s why I’ve become a huge fan of the whole Airbrush Flawless range. Everything works together so seamlessly and the finish is beautiful. Your skin still looks like skin, just a little more polished. It’s makeup that makes you feel like the best version of yourself,” she said.
Named in WWD’s 2026 ranking of the 50 most powerful brands in beauty, Charlotte Tilbury has had quite the year thus far, expanding its retail footprint at home inside Boots and abroad by opening two stand-alone boutiques in India after naming actress Sobhita Dhulipala its inaugural beauty muse in late 2025. Meanwhile, the makeup tycoon was appointed global ambassador and co-chair of The King’s Trust in April. The announcement followed Tilbury’s 2022 appointment as the trust’s ambassador for enterprise and coincided with its 50th anniversary gala, held in New York.
