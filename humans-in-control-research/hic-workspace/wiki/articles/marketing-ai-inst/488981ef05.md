# 23 Marketing AI Platforms Marketers Should Know About

Source: [marketing_ai_inst](https://www.marketingaiinstitute.com/blog/ai-platforms-for-marketers-maicon-2026)

Name an AI use case, and there's probably a tool for it.
The AI marketing landscape is getting more crowded by the day, with platforms emerging for search and personalization, account intelligence, campaign optimization, you name it.
The opportunity isn't to adopt every shiny new tool. It's to understand what's possible and identify the technologies that have the potential to disrupt your day-to-day operations.
Below are 23 AI-powered platforms worth knowing about.
Disclaimer: We're featuring these platforms because they're all MAICON (Marketing AI Conference) sponsors. But we also chose them because they represent some of the interesting ways AI is changing what's possible in marketing. If any of these interest you, they will be onsite at MAICON in the expo hall to talk to you about your work, challenges, and opportunities.
- Acquia is a content platform that brings content, digital asset management, AI agents, and web governance into one workspace, all on an open, composable foundation built around Drupal.
What's Interesting: Acquia is ideal for organizations with a large, complex web presence (think multiple websites and brands spanning teams and territories). Its architecture is API-first and totally composable, meaning you can integrate it with the tools in your existing stack. And that goes beyond just your CMS; this tool can bring together SEO, governance, digital asset management, and more.
- ActiveCampaign uses AI agents to automate and optimize marketing campaigns based on customer behavior and business goals.
What's Interesting: ActiveCampaign is taking "AI can write that for you" to a whole new level. Its AI agents can build campaigns, suggest segments, create automations, personalize content, analyze performance, and recommend next steps.
- CallRail is an AI-powered platform that transcribes and analyzes calls, identifies high-quality leads and conversion signals, and gives marketers insight into which campaigns are driving not just leads, but meaningful customer conversations.
What's Interesting: CallRail just announced a major expansion of Voice Assist, adding real-time lead qualification and intelligent call transfers. Meaning AI can identify caller intent, qualify the lead, and route the call to the right person.
- Conductor is an enterprise AEO platform that covers the AEO lifecycle from visibility to site health.
What's Interesting: Ranking on Google isn't the whole search game anymore. Conductor helps brands understand whether they're showing up in AI-generated answers.
- Demandbase is AI-powered B2B account-based marketing software. It uses AI to analyze signals, accounts, and buying groups to identify where marketing and sales should invest time and energy.
What's Interesting: In April 2026, Demandbase launched Demandbase AI, repositioning the platform around an AI-first interface for orchestrating GTM execution. It added features like a Site Customization Agent, LLM integrations with ChatGPT and Claude, and new pipeline-influence capabilities.
- Fibr.ai turns static websites into adaptive experiences that use AI to personalize, test, and continuously optimize web pages for each visitor in real time.
What's Interesting: It uses AI to decide what's working and what's not, and dynamically change the experience itself based on visitor intent and context.
- Geo Vibe is OmniReach's AI-powered tool focused on Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), helping brands understand and improve how they appear in AI-generated search results and answers.
What's Interesting: GEO Vibe represents one of the biggest shifts happening in search right now: the goal is no longer just to rank on Google; it's to get mentioned, recommended, and cited by AI.
- Google Cloud is a broad cloud platform that gives businesses and developers the infrastructure, data tools, and AI capabilities needed to build and scale applications. Its AI ecosystem includes Gemini models, AI APIs, and the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform, which lets teams build and deploy AI agents and generative AI applications.
What's Interesting: Google Cloud is particularly interesting because it moves AI beyond the chatbot and into actual business infrastructure. Its platform can connect AI to a company's data, applications, and workflows, and its latest push toward AI agents is designed to let those agents handle increasingly complex, multi-step tasks.
- Hightouch connects directly to a company's existing data warehouse and uses AI to turn that customer data into personalized, real-time marketing actions across channels.
What's Interesting: Rather than requiring companies to build another centralized marketing database, Hightouch can work directly with data in their existing warehouse, such as Snowflake.
- KEN-E is building an autonomous AI marketing platform designed to coordinate specialized agents across a company's existing marketing platforms to plan, execute, optimize, and report on marketing work with minimal human intervention.
What's Interesting: The big distinction is execution across the whole marketing stack. Rather than being an AI assistant that helps you think or a point solution that handles one task, KEN-E is designed to sit above tools like Google, Meta, HubSpot, and Mailchimp, coordinating work across them with human review.
- LeanData uses AI and rules-based automation to route leads, buying signals, and AI-generated opportunities to the right sales rep at the right time, while keeping every decision governed and auditable.
What's Interesting: LeanData makes sure those leads are matched to the right accounts, assigned according to territory and business rules, and handed off without losing context.
- MementoPhoto.AI lets event attendees scan a QR code and opt into facial recognition to automatically receive the photos they appear in, all without downloading an app.
What's Interesting: Traditional event-photo platforms give everyone one giant gallery. Memento uses AI to identify attendees and create individual private galleries automatically, then adds engagement and sharing analytics for event organizers.
- Navu is an AI chat solution that turns your website into an intelligent, conversational experience.
What's Interesting: It's more than a chatbot. Navu answers visitor questions, guides them to the right content, captures intent, and can take actions to drive conversion.
- NinjaCat is an AI agent platform built for multi-account marketing teams, most often used by agencies, portfolio companies, and multi-location brands.
What's Interesting: Most marketing dashboards tell you what happened. NinjaCat's AI agents are designed to identify what needs attention, recommend or automate next steps, and help teams act across accounts.
- N.Rich uses AI and intent data to identify which B2B accounts are most likely to buy, orchestrate personalized ABM campaigns, and measure their impact on pipeline.
What's Interesting: Its AI layer can build custom dashboards, account lists, reports, and campaign workflows from plain-language prompts, so it's not just identifying target accounts; it's helping marketers act on the data.
- Optimizely is an AI platform for creating and optimizing digital experiences. It brings together content management, content marketing, experimentation, commerce, personalization, and analytics into a single platform.
What's Interesting: We like that the platform connects content, experimentation, personalization, analytics, and AI so marketers can actually determine what works, rather than simply producing more content.
- Pantheon is a WebOps platform that brings website infrastructure, workflow, and governance together for teams managing WordPress, Drupal, and Next.js sites.
What's Interesting: Pantheon recently introduced its vision for Agentic Fleet Management, with new and forthcoming capabilities designed to make an organization's collection of websites visible and actionable to AI agents — an interesting step toward AI not just creating website content, but helping manage and operate the web infrastructure itself.
- Progress provides enterprise software for data, application development, digital experiences, and AI. For marketers, its Sitefinity platform combines content management, personalization, analytics, and AI-powered capabilities to help teams create and manage digital experiences across channels.
What's Interesting: Progress is leaning heavily into agentic AI that works inside existing marketing workflows, rather than simply adding a chatbot to a CMS. Its new Sitefinity AI agents can help with content optimization, analysis, and editorial workflows.
- REHATCH developed ENSOR, an AI marketing operating system that brings creative production, landing-page creation, and data analysis into one platform, helping small teams accomplish more without adding headcount or relying on outsourcing.
What's Interesting: ENSOR goes beyond generating ad creative. It incorporates brand guidelines, advertising and CRM data, performance analysis, and a memory system that learns from past decisions and results, enabling it to reuse institutional knowledge and improve future creative and marketing decisions.
- Siteimprove uses AI to help organizations create digital content that is simultaneously accessible, discoverable, compliant, and optimized for performance.
What's Interesting: Siteimprove makes digital content accessible to people, discoverable by AI, and accountable to business results.
