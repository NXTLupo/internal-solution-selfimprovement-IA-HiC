WHAT WE DESIGNED
AGI provided Superior Ag with a comprehensive, high capacity fertilizer handling and blending system designed to support efficient receiving, storage, blending, and loadout operations. The system includes a robust barge receiving setup with a 15- X 15-foot hopper and dual delumpers, multiple 36- inch industrial conveyors, structural support towers, and a 600 TPH receiving capacity, along with a 300 TPH declining weight blending system featuring multi ton hoppers, micro ingredient systems, liquid impregnation, and precision control panels. Additional components such as bucket elevators, chain paddles, augers, weigh hoppers, loadout conveyors, and dust control systems ensure seamless material flow throughout the facility — delivering accurate blending, reliable throughput, and efficient handling from inbound product to final loadout.
