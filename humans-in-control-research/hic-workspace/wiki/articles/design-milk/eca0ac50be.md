# Valspar’s 2027 Color of the Year Makes a Case for Blue as the New Neutral

Source: [design_milk](https://design-milk.com/valspar-2027-color-of-the-year-cottage-door/)

Move over beige—there’s a new take on neutral. Valspar has named Cottage Door 8004-38E its 2027 Color of the Year, a mid-tone blue that balances warm and cool undertones, offering a little more personality without sacrificing versatility.
The selection taps into a growing desire for interiors that feel personal and lived-in rather than overly perfected. Valspar describes Cottage Door as a response to that desire for comfort and authenticity, drawing on soft minimalism, organic layering, and tactile materials.
“Cottage Door captures blue’s natural sense of security and trust while serving as a colorful new neutral that layers effortlessly into the home,” says Sue Kim, Valspar’s director of color marketing. “It creates spaces that feel balanced, comforting and inviting.”
That “colorful neutral” designation is perhaps the shade’s most interesting quality. Neither an airy pastel nor a dramatic navy, Cottage Door sits somewhere in between, giving it enough presence to define a room while remaining subdued enough to work across different materials and palettes.
Valspar pairs the blue with two supporting shades: Moon Shot 8005-2B, a warm gray, and Apricot Blush 8003-8A, a muted creamy pink. Light wood tones add another layer of warmth, creating a palette that feels soft without becoming overly sweet.
Valspar is making Cottage Door available across a range of interior and exterior paints, stains, and specialty finishes, allowing the blue to carry from cabinetry and furniture to exterior surfaces.
For 2027, at least, Valspar’s version of the neutral home isn’t devoid of color. It’s simply a little quieter about it.
