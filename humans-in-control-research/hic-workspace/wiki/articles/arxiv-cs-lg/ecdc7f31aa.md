# MEMe: An Accurate Maximum Entropy Method for Efficient Approximations in Large-Scale Machine Learning

Source: [arXiv CS.LG](https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.01101v1)

Efficient approximation lies at the heart of large-scale machine learning problems. In this paper, we propose a novel, robust maximum entropy algorithm, which is capable of dealing with hundreds of moments and allows for computationally efficient approximations.
