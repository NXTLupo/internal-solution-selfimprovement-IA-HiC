# LLMORPH: Automated Metamorphic Testing of Large Language Models

Source: [arXiv CS.LG](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.23611v1)

Automated testing is essential for evaluating and improving the reliability of Large Language Models (LLMs), yet the lack of automated oracles for verifying output correctness remains a key challenge. We present LLMORPH, an automated testing tool specifically designed for LLMs performing NLP tasks, which leverages Metamorphic Testing (MT) to uncover faulty behaviors without relying on human-labeled data.
