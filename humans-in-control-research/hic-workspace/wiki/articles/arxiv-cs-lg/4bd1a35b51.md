# Climate Adaptation with Reinforcement Learning: Experiments with Flooding and Transportation in Copenhagen

Source: [arXiv CS.LG](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.18574v2)

Due to climate change the frequency and intensity of extreme rainfall events, which contribute to urban flooding, are expected to increase in many places. These floods can damage transport infrastructure and disrupt mobility, highlighting the need for cities to adapt to escalating risks. Reinforcement learning (RL) serves as a powerful tool for uncovering optimal adaptation strategies, determining how and where to deploy adaptation measures effectively, even under significant uncertainty.
