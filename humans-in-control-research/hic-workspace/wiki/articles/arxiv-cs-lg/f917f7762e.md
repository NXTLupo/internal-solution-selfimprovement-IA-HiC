# Observation of high-energy neutrinos from the Galactic plane

Source: [arXiv CS.LG](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.04427v1)

The origin of high-energy cosmic rays, atomic nuclei that continuously impact Earth's atmosphere, has been a mystery for over a century. Due to deflection in interstellar magnetic fields, cosmic rays from the Milky Way arrive at Earth from random directions. However, near their sources and during propagation, cosmic rays interact with matter and produce high-energy neutrinos.
