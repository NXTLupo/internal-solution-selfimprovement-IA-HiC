# Foundation Model Engineering: Engineering Foundation Models Just as Engineering Software

Source: [arXiv CS.LG](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.08176v1)

By treating data and models as the source code, Foundation Models (FMs) become a new type of software. Mirroring the concept of software crisis, the increasing complexity of FMs making FM crisis a tangible concern in the coming decade, appealing for new theories and methodologies from the field of software engineering. In this paper, we outline our vision of introducing Foundation Model (FM) engineering, a strategic response to the anticipated FM crisis with principled engineering methodologies.
