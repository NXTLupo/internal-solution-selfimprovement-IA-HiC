Source: [arXiv CS.LG](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.08880v1)

Empirical results in software engineering have long started to show that findings are unlikely to be applicable to all software systems, or any domain: results need to be evaluated in specified contexts, and limited to the type of systems that they were extracted from.
