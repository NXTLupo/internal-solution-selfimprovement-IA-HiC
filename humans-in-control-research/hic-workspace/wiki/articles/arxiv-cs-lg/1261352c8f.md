# Factors other than climate change are currently more important in predicting how well fruit farms are doing financially

Source: [arXiv CS.LG](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.07685v1)

Machine learning and statistical modeling methods were used to analyze the impact of climate change on financial wellbeing of fruit farmers in Tunisia and Chile. The analysis was based on face to face interviews with 801 farmers. Three research questions were investigated. First, whether climate change impacts had an effect on how well the farm was doing financially.
