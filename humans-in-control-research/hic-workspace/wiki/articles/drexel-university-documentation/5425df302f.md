# Engineering Advising & Student Support | Drexel Engineering

Source: [Drexel University documentation](https://drexel.edu/engineering/academics/student-advising/advisor-bios)

Academic Advising is a partnership with students. By taking an active role in academic planning with an assigned advisor, students will make the most of their college experience. All of the college’s dedicated advisors guide students towards academic growth and success in their program, and with the larger Drexel Community.
Everything from plans of study, choosing or changing majors, adding minors, scheduling classes, transferring credits to Drexel, personal support, and explanations of university policy and procedures are some of the subjects to discuss with an advisor. For transfer and new first-year students, advisors play a key role in aiding with adjustment to the university, including providing instruction in the UNIV E101 course, which incoming students take during their first quarter on campus.
Students can find their specific advisor’s name and contact information in Drexel One. That portal is also used to schedule appointments, both in-person and virtual.
Students can visit the EngineerHub for timely opportunities and more college resources.
