# Rokos Group Tapped to Lead Search for Chief Human Resources Officer

Source: [exs_hunt_scanlon](https://huntscanlon.com/rokos-group-tapped-to-lead-search-for-chief-human-resources-officer/)

Rokos Group is honored to be partnering with Keck Medicine of USC to lead the search for their next Chief Human Resources Officer.
In this brief video, Rod Hanners, President and CEO of the USC Health System, shares insight into Keck Medicine’s mission, its continued growth and transformation, and the leadership qualities that will matter most in this role.
This opportunity will appeal to a strategic, relationship-driven healthcare human resources executive who values collaborative leadership, labor and employee relations, workforce transformation, HR technology, data-driven decision-making, and organizational excellence. The CHRO will serve as a trusted executive partner and lead the people strategy for a complex academic health system, with responsibility for workforce planning, talent acquisition, compensation, employee development, performance management, payroll, employee relations, and organized labor strategy.
