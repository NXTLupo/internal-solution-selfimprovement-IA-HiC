# Rokos Group Leading CFO Search at Mercy Flights

Source: [exs_hunt_scanlon](https://huntscanlon.com/rokos-group-leading-cfo-search-at-mercy-flights/)

Rokos Group is honored to be leading Mercy Flights Inc. on the search for its next Chief Financial Officer.
In this brief video, Sheila Clough, FACHE, Chief Executive Officer, shares insight into Mercy Flights’ mission, its commitment to growth, delivering vital integrated mobile healthcare, and the leadership qualities that will matter most in this role.
This opportunity will appeal to a growth-oriented, strategic, and hands-on healthcare finance leader who values mission-driven service, financial stewardship, revenue cycle excellence, payer strategy, operational improvement, team development, and meaningful community impact. Reporting to the CEO, the CFO will serve as a key member of the executive leadership team and provide oversight across Finance, Revenue Cycle, Information Technology, and Privacy.
