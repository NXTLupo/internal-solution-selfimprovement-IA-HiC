Soil Health Cost-Share Program in Lancaster County
The Alliance for the Chesapeake Bay (Alliance) is currently accepting applications for its pilot soil health cost-share program in Lancaster County. The Soil Health Equipment and Advising Program (SHEAP) is designed to ease the upfront financial risks of trying new soil health practices while providing the technical support needed to navigate challenges during the transition period.
Whether you manage cropland or pasture, many common soil health practices are eligible for funding. Funding for this program comes from a National Fish and Wildlife Foundation (NFWF) Innovative Nutrient and Sediment Reduction (INSR) grant, managed by Lancaster Clean Water Partners (LCWP) and the Alliance. For cropland, eligible practices include new acres of cover crops or multi-species cover crops with adaptive nitrogen management. For pasture management, this program supports comprehensive pasture renovation, including diversifying current forage, transitioning cropland to pasture, or developing prescribed grazing plans. For farms looking to boost biodiversity, funding is available to establish hedgerows or pollinator habitat in marginal areas on the farm.
