United States
Anwar Jibawi has mastered the art of physical comedy on YouTube. Known for his over-the-top reactions and exaggerated yet relatable daily struggle scenarios, Anwar's content keeps viewers laughing from the very first frame.
For the FIFA World Cup 2026™, Anwar is bringing his signature comedy straight to the stadiums, sharing highly-entertaining parodies and chaotic fan-vibes from the stands.
Mexico
Twin sisters from Mexico, Ara and Fer have taken YouTube by storm with their synchronized choreography, fun transitions, and viral dance trends.
For the World Cup, Ara y Fer have turned host-city stadiums into their own personal dance floors. Showing off their Mexican pride, the dynamic duo is creating viral Shorts featuring stadium vlogs, behind-the-scenes with players and mascots, and fun collaborations with fans and other creators gathering in Mexico.
United States
Donald De La Haye, better known as Deestroying, built a huge sports community. Showcasing performance by athletes from different regions and sports around the world, matchups that viewers wouldn’t be able to find anywhere else, and wild challenges, his channel bridges the gap between professional athletics and contemporary creator culture.
He’s tapping into the massive hype of the World Cup by engaging his audience in relevant debates ripe for hot takes and putting his own spin on fan culture, sparking passionate conversations and blending soccer history with the current tournament's energy.
United States
Ashley Alexander, known on YouTube as urmomashley, is a lifestyle, fashion, and travel creator known for her vlogs, family content, and sense of humor.
Ashley is giving the FIFA World Cup 2026™ a humorous and lifestyle-forward spin — offering her audience a guide to experiencing the tournament in style and with a laugh.
Kazakhstan
Kika Kim is an international sensation celebrated for her hilarious Shorts and lifestyle vlogs. Kika has established a massive global fanbase that tunes in to watch her bring trends to life.
Bringing her global appeal to the FIFA World Cup 2026™, Kika is capturing the vibrant, multicultural atmosphere of the tournament and documenting her journey to soccer fandom in the best way.
Indonesia
KYLECTRIX is a standout soccer (football!) creator from Indonesia, known for his immersive gameplay commentary and engaging fan discussions.
KYLECTRIX is bringing his infectious energy to the world stage as a primary driver of fan interaction. For the FIFA 2026 World Cup, he’s hanging out pitchside with legends of the game, and delivering some of the most exciting behind-the-scenes moments of the tournament, like the opening ceremony in Mexico City.
South Korea
Kwakyoongy is an Olympic short track speed skater and South Korean creator who’s built a community with his athletic prowess and hilarious behind-the-scenes vlogs documenting his adventures.
Kwankyoongy is translating his athletic insight into the ultimate FIFA World Cup 2026™ fan experience. He’s capturing the electric energy of South Korea’s match days and sharing behind-the-scenes experiences from the stadiums that will keep you watching.
United Kingdom
The Sidemen are titans of UK entertainment on YouTube. Best known for their massive real-life challenges, game shows, and their annual Charity Football Matches, this close-knit group has spent over a decade defining modern online creator culture.
For the FIFA World Cup 2026™, The Sidemen have been touring the USA, rooting for underdogs, while putting their famed Cheap vs. Expensive challenge to the test with their signature, laugh-out-loud style.
Mexico
Sonrixs is one of Mexico's most popular entertainment and comedy creators, beloved for his high-energy public pranks, viral street challenges, and hilarious couple videos. .
Sonrixs is capturing the vibrant, chaotic street culture surrounding the games, buddying up with fans from around the world, and capturing the hysteria of 80,000 Mexico fans cheering on their home team.
Brazil
Viniblogger is a prominent Brazilian lifestyle, travel, and comedy creator who’s built a passionate following through his vlogs and comedic takes on daily life.
Brazil’s FIFA World Cup 2026™ might have come to an end, but Viniblogger’s love for the game is as strong as ever, as he captures the drama of pivotal knock-out games, while having plenty of fun along the way.
