# Inside new football series ‘We Are Osos’ only on YouTube

Source: [youtube_creators](https://blog.youtube/culture-and-trends/we-are-osos-youtube-docuseries-mexico-football/)

What happens when NFL and NBA icons—including Ryan Kalil, Blake Griffin, Christian McCaffrey, George Kittle, Luke Kuechly, Sam Darnold, and Greg Olsen—buy a professional football team in Monterrey, Mexico?
You get We Are Osos, an eight-episode docuseries premiering tonight exclusively on YouTube.
Produced by the award-winning storytelling powerhouses at Supper Club (the creative minds behind Chef’s Table and FX's The Bear) and Mortal Media, the series follows the team's first season under new ownership as athletic ambition collides with the gritty reality of the Liga de Fútbol Americano Profesional (LFA).
