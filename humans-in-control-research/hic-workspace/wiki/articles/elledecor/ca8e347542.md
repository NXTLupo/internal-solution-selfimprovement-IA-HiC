# Le Creuset Just Unveiled Its Newest Color

Source: [elledecor](https://www.elledecor.com/shopping/a73513025/le-creuset-new-color-chateau-2026/)

Everyone has their own favorite color of Le Creuset’s enameled-cast iron cookware. A six-quart pot in the brand’s signature Flame hue—a fiery orange inspired by molten iron—was a fixture in Julia Child’s kitchen. Marilyn Monroe, meanwhile, preferred Elysée Yellow, a muted, almost buttery glaze that was released in 1955. Meghan, the Duchess of Sussex, seems to favor simple, classic white for her Dutch oven and braiser, as evidenced on Netflix’s With Love, Meghan.
Now, the beloved French brand is adding another distinctive shade to its glossy lineup. Developed in Le Creuset’s famed trend-forecasting Color Lab, Château is a sophisticated stony blue inspired by the rustic beauty of the French countryside. The design-forward neutral takes its cues from slate rooftops, weathered stone, and misty morning skies, lending it both a sense of history and a timeless quality. (Do we also detect a hint of lavender, a Provençal staple, in the mix?)
Like many of Le Creuset’s most enduring hues, such as Cerise and Riviera, Château is designed to subtly shift with its surroundings. Its softer blue undertones come forward in a bright, sunlit kitchen, giving it an almost silvery effect. In a darker, moodier setting, meanwhile, the color appears deeper and more atmospheric. That versatility and depth make it especially well suited to the collected, layered look that has become a hallmark of modern French Country interiors.
The shade also reflects the calming mood of many of the recent Colors of the Year, which have trended toward warm tones and earthy neutrals as of late.
Château is available exclusively at Crate & Barrel across an array of Le Creuset products, including the signature Dutch oven, stoneware mugs, tea kettle, rectangular roaster, and obsession-worthy mini cocotte.
