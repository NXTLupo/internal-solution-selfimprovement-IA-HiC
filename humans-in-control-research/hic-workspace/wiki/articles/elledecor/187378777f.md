# Behr Created a Brand-New Paint Color for Its 2027 Color of the Year

Source: [elledecor](https://www.elledecor.com/design-decorate/color/a73522874/behr-color-of-the-year-2027/)

At Behr’s reveal for its 2027 Color of the Year, there was an unofficial finding among attendees: People with green eyes looked especially good standing near it.
It is not exactly a scientific measure of a paint color’s success. But it does get at what makes Grounded—an olive green with mossy notes and a brown undertone—a bit more unique. The shade has enough warmth and depth to flatter both a room and, apparently, the people in it.
Behr developed the hue from scratch. “We created a completely new color that wasn’t in our collection before,” Kayla Kratz, Behr’s head of color, tells ELLE Decor. “With Grounded, we knew that we wanted a very specific shade of green.”
The team tested dozens of options before settling on the final version. “Some were more dark, some were more bright, some even had more of a chalky feel,” Kratz says. “We just kept on dialing it in.” The final pick has a brown cast rather than a gray one, which separates it from the sage greens that have been everywhere for the past few years.
The color may seem a tad familiar, though. It’s close to Behr’s 2026 Color of the Year, Hidden Gem. But instead of treating the color as a departure from last year’s smoky jade, Behr is presenting it as part of the same move toward deeper, earthier shades. Kratz says the company was not interested in picking a different color simply to check a box. “People tend to think about going around the rainbow,” she says. “And this year for us [it] is so important to talk about what’s really resonating with our consumer.”
In this case, that means green again, but a different version of it. Grounded has less blue than Hidden Gem and more brown, so it works more easily with the practical stuff already in a house: wood floors, cream upholstery, old brass, and the beige or gray sofa that has survived several rounds of redecorating.
“Grounded is that anchor in the space that brings those materials to life,” Kratz says. “Put Grounded behind it and see how that sofa gets a new life in the space.”
The bedroom is Kratz’s favorite place to use it. “What better space [than] the bedroom,” she says. “The ultimate place where you’re going to unwind from the day and you want that energy to settle.”
Grounded will be available in Behr Dynasty, Marquee, Ultra, and Premium Plus paint lines at The Home Depot.
