# The Best Urban Spas in the U.S., from New York to Los Angeles

Source: [elledecor](https://www.elledecor.com/life-culture/a73456248/best-urban-spas-us/)

There are spas that happen to be in cities, and then there are urban spas that make you forget the city exists at all. Step inside the right one, and the traffic and packed sidewalks give way to tranquil relaxation rooms, elaborate thermal circuits and tension melting massages.
The very best of these oases take that sense of escape even further. Design is as much a draw as what happens in the treatment room, with spaces ranging from jewel-box retreats to sprawling wellness playgrounds. Below, we’ve rounded up seven exceptional spas across the country that are worth escaping the city for—without actually leaving.
The Spa at Hotel Chelsea | New York
Housed on the penthouse level of Hotel Chelsea, the spa—much like the property itself—exudes character. Cate Mills of Brooklyn-based firm Ellison Mills Studio conceived of the space in collaboration with the hotel’s design team. Windows overlooking the city usher in natural light, while the vaulted brick ceilings and decor make the space feel like your cool friend’s downtown apartment, complete with antiques, richly colored wallpapers, and vintage lighting. We especially loved the landscaped terraces replete with medicinal plants and herbs, and on chilly days, there’s nothing better than the chairs by the fireplace in the lounge, where you can settle in with a mug of tea.
Services run the full gamut, but the Healing Arts menu especially caught our eye. Rooted in alternative practices such as Reiki, the six offerings include one called the Reader, which features a session with a tarot card reader.
Treatments start at $190.
The Spa Hotel Bel-Air | Los Angeles
With seven treatment rooms, the spa at this posh property is hidden amid lush gardens and channels 1940s Hollywood glamour. Soft pink and white tones, hand-painted floral motifs, and warm red-oak floors set the scene. Pre- and post-service, the relaxation lounge invites lingering with plush chaises and a glinting chandelier. The Enclave is a haven for couples with its two treatment beds, marble soaking tub, and private terrace.
We recommend the Ultimate Aromatherapy Experience, which pairs a selection of aromatic oils with a Swedish massage and lymphatic drainage. As an added bonus, the spa has a nail suite and hair salon, making it a one-stop spot for pampering from head to toe.
Treatments start at $275.
Tierra Santa Healing House at the Faena | New York
The 17,000-square-foot subterranean spa in New York’s newest luxury hot spot is a surrealist fever dream, awash in vivid color, texture, and visual drama. Argentine artist Juan Gatti created the three-dimensional lobby murals and intricate botanical wallpaper in the relaxation room, while Manuel Ameztoy’s suspended installation draws the eye to the soaring 12 foot ceilings.
At the heart of the spa is a thermal sanctuary with five pools—two warm, one hot, one salt, and one cold—along with dry and infrared saunas, a steam room, and a hammam. Treatments incorporate South American healing traditions and high-tech skin care. There are herbal body scrubs, therapeutic massages, LED light therapy, and facials featuring products from Augustinus Bader, Valmont, and 111SKIN.
Treatments start at $325.
The Spa at Acqualina Resort | Miami
This 20,000-square-foot oceanfront spa is spread over two levels. Among the highlights are an outdoor terrace with a cascading waterfall, heated jet pool, and stunning views of the Atlantic. Inside, guests will find multiple saunas, a crystal steam room, an arctic ice fountain, and coed and women’s relaxation lounges, each with a softly lit Himalayan pink salt wall.
Treatments include everything you’d expect from a luxury spa—deep-tissue massages, body scrubs, and the like—but the facials featuring products from prestigious Italian skin-care brand Seed to Skin Tuscany are distinctive. Especially notable is the 90-minute Age-Reversal Facial, which combines myofascial massage and Gua Sha techniques with a double mask and cryotherapy.
Treatments start at $260.
The Spa at Mandarin Oriental | Boston
The Mandarin Oriental’s Asian heritage meets New England elegance at this 16,000-square-foot oasis. The mood is warm and residential, with natural woods, creamy neutrals, stone finishes, and soft ambient lighting. Asian textiles add richness, including treatment-bed blankets patterned with gold thread that we’d happily take home. The expansive wet areas are another draw and include a crystal steam room and a vitality pool with hydrotherapy jets.
Sleep-focused treatments are what we’d book here. The Dream Catcher combines a light-to-medium-pressure body massage and scalp rub with an herbal-infused heat pack. We’re practically dozing off just thinking about it.
Treatments begin at $310.
The Spa at the Ritz-Carlton | Dallas
A Dallas institution where the city’s elite escape for the day, this spa got a major upgrade in 2022 lead by Dallas-based design firm ForrestPerkins. The revamped space is firmly rooted in the city famously known as the Big D, and its aesthetic takes cues from the green spaces throughout the sprawling metropolis. Greeting guests is a large-scale, hand-forged metal trellis sculpted by local artist Larry Whiteley that features a tangle of intricate vines, leaves, and flowers. Other noteworthy details include weathered bronze handles resembling tree branches and light fixtures fashioned from branches and crystals.
If you have the luxury of time, go for the Spa Sommelier, a three-hour experience personally customized by the in-house spa sommelier. Your prescription might include a massage, facial, and energy healing treatment or a different mix based on what you need most.
Treatments start at $210.
The Spa at Wynn | Las Vegas
Spanning 45,000 square feet with 44 treatment rooms, the spa at Wynn Las Vegas commands a sizable footprint, but its celestial-themed interior creates an atmosphere that feels intimate and serene. The relaxation lounge is especially striking, with dark navy walls, plush carpeting, and a ceiling adorned with a motif of Zodiac symbols. Equally impressive are the palatial locker rooms complete with hot and cold water circuits and experience showers.
The holistic treatments are the way to go here: The Good Luck Ritual draws on the five Chinese elements, weaving together massage, aromatherapy, and energy work. For something inspired by the landscape beyond the Strip, opt for the Desert Stone Therapy, which starts with a full-body sea salt exfoliation followed by a hot stone massage.
Treatments start at $205.
