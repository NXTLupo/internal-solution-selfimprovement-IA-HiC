# Victoria Hagan’s Guide to Nantucket

Source: [elledecor](https://www.elledecor.com/life-culture/travel/a73426650/victoria-hagan-guide-to-nantucket/)

Victoria Hagan, the ELLE Decor A-List Legend, has spent summers on Nantucket since she was a teenager. She eventually helped her parents design a house on the Massachusetts island and then built one of her own. She’s now been both visiting and working on Nantucket for decades. Today, she spends her days helping clients create their own dream Nantucket beach houses—along with spending plenty of time at the local hardware store.
Here, in addition to sharing her latest Nantucket project, she’s divulging all of her favorite places “on island.” Read on for her most helpful sourcing spots and her secret beaches.
Where to Eat
Hagan loves to watch the sunset over dinner at Galley Beach, a family-owned restaurant that sits right on the sand overlooking the Nantucket Sound. Her other favorite dining options include Topper’s, the restaurant at the Wauwinet Hotel, which “has a beautiful view of the sunset, too,” and, for a “lively and fun” feel, Cru at the harbor in town.
Over by her home in Sconset, she likes Claudette’s for their breakfast sandwiches and, Hagan says, “I think the Sconset Market has the best ice cream cones and the shortest lines in Nantucket.”
“I'm not a big one to wait on line. I'm not a line girl,” Hagan continues, but she does make an exception for Black Eyed Susan’s, the casual restaurant in town with no reservations. “You have to just get lucky,” she says.
And finally, she has a special place in her heart for the bar room at the Chanticleer, an old-school restaurant also in Sconset. “It's just very cozy,” she says. “I think it's the bee's knees.”
Where to Shop
Hagan has plenty of experience shopping on Nantucket, whether that’s for her clients or for her family and friends. One place she always returns to is Nantucket Looms, the homeware store founded in 1968 that has its own second-floor weaving studio. “They have beautiful handwoven throws,” Hagan says. “They have beautiful baby blankets, and they have really fun home things too.”
She also recommends Rafael Osona Auctions, a family-owned antiques and art auction house based on the island. “I've furnished many projects at Raphael Osona because they're always unexpected things at the auction house,” she says.
Another favorite is Murray’s Toggery Shop, best known for selling the Nantucket Red pants that the island made famous. But, they also have a variety of other options, from baseball caps to cotton sweaters. “I always think you can get the classic at Murray's,” says Hagan.
And finally, Hagan couldn’t share her list of Nantucket favorites without mentioning one slightly less glamorous spot: “There probably isn't a weekend that goes by that I'm not at the Marine Home Center,” she says. “It's a great hardware store, and they have a little home section too.”
Best Beaches
For beach time, Hagan heads to Sconset Beach or Quidnet Pond, which she can see from her house. Because the pond is adjacent to the ocean, it’s a calm place to sail or kayak.
But her favorite spot is Bunny’s Beach, which is somewhat hidden by Bunny Mellon’s old house in the Tom Nevers area. “What's special about Nantucket is that all the beaches are open. There are no private beaches,” she says. But, despite being open, Bunny’s Beach still feels a bit tucked away from it all.
“It's very quiet, and you can sneak in, and it’s beautiful.”
