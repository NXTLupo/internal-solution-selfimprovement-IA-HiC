# TFP’s Pine Walk launches Bermuda-based MGA focused on US SME casualty reinsurance

Source: [ins_reinsurance_news](https://www.reinsurancene.ws/tfps-pine-walk-launches-bermuda-based-mga-focused-on-us-sme-casualty-reinsurance/)

The Fidelis Partnership’s (TFP) specialist MGA platform Pine Walk has launched Adroit Underwriting Limited, a new Bermuda-based MGA focused on US small and medium enterprise (SME) casualty reinsurance.
The new business will be led by Jeff Markowski as CEO & CUO, who holds more than 20 years of US casualty treaty experience, most recently as Director of Underwriting at Peak Re, North America.
Markowski is joined by Derrick Holmes as CFO & COO, who brings over a decade of reinsurance finance and operational experience, including responsibility for agent operations with Peak Capital Ltd., and significant ILS market experience to support a “growing investor appetite” in casualty risk.
“Adroit will provide disciplined, data-driven reinsurance solutions across a broad suite of US casualty lines, including Medical Malpractice, Cyber Liability, General Liability, Construction (Architects & Engineers), Public Entity, D&O/E&O and Lawyers Legal Liability,” TFP explained.
Capacity will be provided through TFP’s Syndicates 3123 and 2126 alongside third-party carriers.
Michael Davern, UK CUO and Group Head of D&F Property and Energy at The Fidelis Partnership, commented, “Pine Walk is a key pillar of TFP’s differentiated distribution footprint and we are pleased to welcome Adroit as its 18th business.
“Jeff and Derrick bring an outstanding track record and deep market knowledge that make them exceptionally well placed to give brokers a differentiated proposition, rooted in experience, underpinned by a data-driven approach and enhanced by access to TFP’s broader platform to support cross-selling opportunities.
“This is exactly the kind of opportunity Pine Walk was built for, and we look forward to supporting them going forward.”
Jeff Markowski, CEO & CUO of Adroit Underwriting, added, “The US SME casualty reinsurance market is underserved by specialist underwriters with the depth of relationships and track record to move quickly and with conviction.
“Adroit has been built to fill that gap – bringing disciplined, actuarially-grounded underwriting to a market that needs it. Launching on the Pine Walk platform means we are backed by industry-leading infrastructure, capacity, and support to hit the ground running.”
