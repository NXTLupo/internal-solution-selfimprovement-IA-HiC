# Securing the agentic enterprise with data lineage

Data lineage is the new security perimeter, providing the visibility needed for governance, accountability, and trust, without impeding AI adoption at scale.

By 
*   [MIT Technology Review Insights archive page](https://www.technologyreview.com/author/mit-technology-review-insights/)

November 4, 2026

In partnership with[Cyberhaven](https://www.cyberhaven.com/)

[![Image 3](https://wp.technologyreview.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/cyberhaven_logo-e1792655542220.png)](https://www.cyberhaven.com/)

A sales representative asks an AI agent for customer pricing, and the agent pulls data from multiple sources: restricted pricing systems, internal communications, and archived quotes. The output looks accurate to the agent, so a second agent uses the data to draft the proposal for the sales rep. No human checks either handoff. When it’s discovered that the proposal includes unapproved, confidential, and sensitive information, no one can trace where the problematic data entered the workflow or how it spread across systems, files, and agents.

[![Image 4](https://wp.technologyreview.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/MITTR_Cyberhaven_V4_Cover.png?w=840)](https://www.cyberhaven.com/resources/research/mit-data-lineage-agentic-ai)

[DOWNLOAD THE REPORT](https://www.cyberhaven.com/resources/research/mit-data-lineage-agentic-ai)

This scenario illustrates why AI governance needs to evolve, rapidly and dynamically. As more enterprises begin to use agentic AI in daily workflows, they’re realizing that they must manage more than static files; they must manage moving and fragmented data that is continuously ingested, copied, summarized, transformed, reused, and redistributed in new forms. In short, AI security isn’t a tool problem; it’s a data visibility problem.

[![Image 5](https://wp.technologyreview.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/MITTR2026_CyberhavenV1DoshiSocialCards4.png)](https://www.cyberhaven.com/resources/research/mit-data-lineage-agentic-ai)
Consequently, traditional security approaches built solely around applications, systems, networks, or endpoints are no longer sufficient. Securing agentic AI at scale requires understanding how data moves. Teams, therefore, need to establish data lineage in order to effectively manage [governance, accountability, and trust](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework) across human, agentic, and hybrid workflows.
