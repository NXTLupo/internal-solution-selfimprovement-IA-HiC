Source: [agr_defra_uk](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/historical-national-statistics-notices-on-milk-utilisation-by-dairies-2026)

Historical national statistics notices on milk utilisation by dairies, 2026
Previously published copies of the monthly Accredited Official Statistics publication on the volume of milk used by dairies.
Documents
Details
This publication gives previously published copies of the Accredited Official Statistics publications on the volume of milk used by dairies in the UK in the production of drinking milk and milk products that showed figures for 2026. Each publication gives the figures available at that time. The figures are subject to revision each month as new information becomes available.
