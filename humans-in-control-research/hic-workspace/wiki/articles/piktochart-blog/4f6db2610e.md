# Canva Pricing 2026: What Every Plan Actually Costs (And Why Your Bill Might Have Changed)

Source: [piktochart_blog](https://piktochart.com/blog/canva-pricing-2026-costs-raised/)

If you’ve searched for Canva’s pricing recently and landed on a number that didn’t match what you saw at checkout, you’re not imagining things. Canva has changed its pricing structure more than once in the past two years, and a lot of guides online are still quoting outdated figures. Here’s what’s actually true as of 2026.
Canva’s Current Plans at a Glance
Worth noting, billing annually effectively lowers the Pro rate to $15/month and Business to just under $21/month, so the sticker shock mostly hits people paying month to month.
The Detail That Trips Most People Up: Pro Is a One-Person License
Pro is licensed to a single user. The moment a second person needs their own login, Canva moves your account to Business, which is a completely different pricing structure. This is where a lot of “wait, why did my bill change?” moments start.
Why Business Costs More Than People Expect
Business pricing is per seat, and it scales in a straight line with no volume discount built in. That means:
- 1 seat: ~$250/year
- 3 seats: ~$750/year
- 5 seats: ~$1,250/year
- 10 seats: ~$2,500/year
Ten seats really is ten times the cost of one. There’s no bulk buy break until you’re in Enterprise negotiation territory, so costs climb fast for growing teams.
How We Got Here: The 2024 Pricing Change
In September 2024, Canva made the most controversial pricing change in its history. The old flat rate Teams plan, $119.99/year covering up to five people, was replaced with per-seat pricing. For a five-person team, that meant a jump from roughly $120/year to $500/year, close to a 300% increase, though it’s worth noting the original $119.99 figure covered five people, not one, which is part of why the headline number looked more dramatic than the per-person math actually was.
Canva framed the change around its new Magic Studio AI features, positioning the new pricing as access to tools it valued at hundreds of dollars a year in value. The backlash was loud enough that Canva grandfathered pricing for many existing customers and published a public “Pricing Promise,” committing to at least 60 days’ notice before future price changes. It was a partial walk back rather than a full reversal, since new customers still pay the higher, per-seat rate.
The AI Credit Cap Most People Don’t Notice Until They Hit It
Magic Studio’s AI credits (50/month on Free, 500/month on Pro) are shared across every AI feature on the platform. Image generation, copywriting, background removal, and more all draw from the same pool. Heavy users burn through 500 credits faster than the number suggests, and there’s no way to buy more credits individually. If you run out, you either wait for the monthly reset or pay for a separate add-on.
If you’d rather not think about credit caps at all, Piktochart’s AI Design Generator and the wider Pikto AI Studio suite handle infographics, reports, presentations, images, and video from a single dashboard, with a straightforward monthly credit allowance instead of a shared pool spread across a dozen different tools.
Is Canva Still Worth It?
For casual, occasional use, Canva’s free plan and even Pro remain reasonably accessible. The real cost pressure shows up for teams. Per-seat Business pricing with no volume discount, plus a shared AI credit pool that can run out mid-month, adds up faster than the sticker price suggests once you’re budgeting for more than one or two people.
If you’re finding that the math doesn’t work for your team anymore, or you just want a platform built around straightforward pricing without the per-seat scaling shock, Piktochart is worth a look. You’ll find 800+ infographic templates alongside the broader template library for presentations, reports, and social graphics, plus a video editor and photo editing tools built into the same plan, without needing to price out each additional seat individually.
FAQ
Is Canva Pro worth $18 a month? It depends on how much you use the premium features. If you regularly need background removal, resizing, translation, and the larger template library, Pro can pay for itself for a single user.
It’s also worth thinking about this beyond today’s price tag. Canva has raised prices more than once in the past few years, most notably the 2024 change that moved Teams customers to per-seat Business pricing. That’s a pattern, not a one-off, and there’s no strong reason to assume $18/month is the last increase you’ll see. Before committing, it’s worth asking yourself two things: does the value you get today genuinely justify the cost, and are you comfortable with a platform that’s shown a willingness to raise prices again if a similar case can be made? If either answer gives you pause, it’s worth checking whether an alternative offers similar template quality, AI tools, and design capability at a lower, more predictable cost.
Why did Canva’s price go up so much? The most visible jump happened in September 2024, when the old flat rate Teams plan was replaced with per-seat Business pricing. Canva positioned the change around new AI features bundled into the plan, and later softened it somewhat with grandfathered pricing for existing customers and a public commitment to give more notice before future changes.
Is there a cheaper alternative to Canva for teams? Yes. Tools like Piktochart are built around simpler plan structures without the same per-seat scaling, and bundle templates, AI design tools, and video editing into one subscription rather than pricing each feature or seat separately.
What happens if I run out of AI credits on Canva? You either wait for the monthly reset or purchase the separate AI Pass add-on for more credits. Since credits are shared across every AI feature on the platform, heavy users of any single AI tool can burn through the monthly allowance quickly.
Does Canva offer a discount for larger teams? Not in the form of a published volume discount. Business pricing scales in a straight line per seat, so a 10-person team pays exactly ten times what a single Business seat costs. Meaningful negotiated pricing typically only shows up at the Enterprise tier.
Pricing figures above reflect Canva’s published pricing page as of mid-2026. Always verify directly on Canva’s site before making a purchasing decision, since plan pricing can change without much notice.
