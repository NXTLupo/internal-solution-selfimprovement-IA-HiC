# Product Update: Nano Banana Pro Now Available in Pikto AI

Source: [piktochart_blog](https://piktochart.com/blog/product-update-nano-banana-pro/)

Generating AI images with precise text, consistent subjects, and professional polish has been a challenge—until now. Pikto AI’s new Nano Banana Pro integration brings Google’s most advanced image model directly into your workflow, giving you unprecedented control over AI-generated visuals.
What is Nano Banana Pro?
Nano Banana Pro is Google’s latest AI image generation and modification model. Unlike our existing Nano Banana tool, this upgraded version delivers three key improvements: legible text generation, better subject consistency across reference images, and more professional, prompt-accurate results.
The standout feature is reference image support. Upload up to 14 images to guide your AI generations, or modify existing images using up to 13 additional references. This image-first approach means you can show the AI exactly what you want, rather than relying solely on text descriptions.
Powerful Use Cases Made Simple
Nano Banana Pro unlocks creative possibilities across multiple workflows:
- Brand Consistency: Upload your logo and brand assets as references to generate on-brand marketing materials, social graphics, or presentation visuals.
- Text-Heavy Designs: Create infographics, posters, or certificates with legible, properly formatted text—no more garbled letters or unreadable fonts.
- Style Matching: Reference existing designs to maintain visual consistency across campaigns, ensuring every piece matches your established aesthetic.
- Complex Compositions: Combine multiple visual elements from different sources into cohesive, professional designs.
How It Works
Using Nano Banana Pro is straightforward:
- Access the AI Image Generator or AI Image Modifier in Pikto AI
- Upload your reference images (up to 14 for generation, or 1 base image plus 13 references for modification)
- Enter your prompt describing the desired output
- Let Nano Banana Pro generate your professional-quality image
- Fine-tune results directly in Piktochart’s editor
Nano Banana Pro is available across multiple access points: the Piktonext editor, Pikto AI app, and Mini Image Editor, giving you flexibility to work wherever suits your workflow best.
Ready to Create Professional AI Images?
Nano Banana Pro is available now in Pikto AI. Whether you’re creating marketing materials, educational content, or internal communications, this powerful upgrade helps you generate professional visuals with the precision and consistency your projects demand.
Try Nano Banana Pro today and experience the difference reference-based AI image generation makes.
