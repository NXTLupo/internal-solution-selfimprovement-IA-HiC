# Beautiful.ai Pricing: Plans, Free Trial Terms, and a BETTER Alternative

Source: [piktochart_blog](https://piktochart.com/blog/beautiful-ai-alternative-pricing/)

Searching for Beautiful.ai’s pricing usually means one of two things: you’re about to start a trial and want to know what happens after day 14, or you’ve already hit a price wall and you’re checking whether there’s a cheaper way to get the same result. Beautiful.ai’s pricing page doesn’t make either question easy to answer at a glance, since the trial requires a card before you see anything, and the jump from solo to team pricing is steep.
This breaks down exactly what Beautiful.ai charges, what its free trial actually commits you to, and where Piktochart fits if you’re looking at alternatives that cover more than presentations.
Quick answer: Beautiful.ai has no permanent free plan, only a 14-day trial that requires a credit card and auto-charges if you don’t cancel. Pro costs $12/month (annual), and Team jumps to $40/user/month (annual), capped at 20 seats. Piktochart, one of the more complete alternatives, starts at $0 forever with no card required, and its team plan costs $17/member/month for up to 100 members while also covering infographics, reports, and video editing.
What Beautiful.ai actually costs
The individual Pro price looks reasonable on its own. The Team price is where it gets expensive: $40 per user per month, billed annually, works out to $400/month for a 10-person team, and that’s before you’re anywhere near the 20-seat ceiling that forces an Enterprise conversation.
The single-presentation plan at $45/month, billed monthly, is worth flagging too: it costs more per month than the annual Pro plan, so it only makes sense if you’re certain you need exactly one presentation and nothing else.
The free trial fine print
There’s no way to use Beautiful.ai without eventually entering payment details. The 14-day trial requires a credit card before you can start, and if you don’t cancel before the trial ends, you’re charged automatically for a full billing cycle. There’s no free-forever tier to fall back on if you decide it’s not for you.
That’s a different commitment than a genuine free plan. It works fine if you’re organized about cancellation dates. It’s worth knowing about if you’re not.
Beautiful.ai alternatives
If the pricing structure above is why you’re looking elsewhere, here’s what to weigh in an alternative:
- Does it have a real free plan, not just a trial that requires a card
- Does the per-seat price stay reasonable as your team grows, rather than jumping 3-4x between individual and team tiers
- Does it do anything beyond presentations, since most teams that need slides also need reports, infographics, or video at some point
Piktochart is the most direct alternative on all three counts. Its Free plan costs $0 forever with no card required and includes 60 AI credits a month. Its Business plan costs $17 per member per month (annual) and supports up to 100 members, less than half Beautiful.ai’s per-seat Team price for five times the seat capacity. And it doesn’t stop at presentations: the same subscription covers infographics, reports, posters, flyers, video editing, and ebooks.
Where Beautiful.ai and Piktochart diverge is scope. Beautiful.ai auto-designs slides using a library of 300+ Smart Slide layouts and nothing else. Piktochart spreads its AI generation across 14 dedicated generators, presentations being one of them, so the same credit pool covers a report one week and an infographic the next.
AI generation: one format vs. many
Beautiful.ai’s paid plans include unlimited AI generation, but it’s unlimited within the Smart Slide system only. There’s no usage cap, but there’s a content-type cap: it makes slides.
Piktochart’s AI credits work across infographics, reports, presentations, posters, flyers, banners, newsletters, timelines, and more. Free users get 60 credits a month, Pro users get 500, Business users get 1,000, none of it locked to one format.
Platform breadth
Beautiful.ai doesn’t build infographics, reports, posters, or video at any price. If your team needs those, you’re paying for a separate tool on top of Beautiful.ai regardless of which plan you’re on.
Piktochart is built to cover that in one subscription:
- Static visuals: infographics, reports, presentations, posters, flyers, and social graphics
- Video editing: transcript-based, no timeline required, auto-transcription in 60+ languages
- 14 AI generators spanning content types beyond slides
- Sector-specific templates for marketing, HR, education, nonprofits, healthcare, and finance
Exports
Piktochart exports natively to PNG, PDF, and PowerPoint. Beautiful.ai also exports to PowerPoint, but some users report needing to clean up formatting afterward, since Smart Slide layouts don’t always translate one-to-one into PowerPoint’s structure.
Who should use which tool
Stick with Beautiful.ai if: you exclusively build presentations, you’re fine with the per-seat jump to $40/user/month for a team, and you don’t need infographics, reports, or video anywhere in your workflow.
Look at Piktochart if: you want a real free plan before committing a card, you need more than presentations, or you’re scaling a team past a handful of seats and want the per-seat cost to stay reasonable.
Switching from Beautiful.ai
If you’re mid-trial or already paying and want to compare side by side: export your current decks to PowerPoint or PDF, sign up for Piktochart’s free plan (no card needed), and rebuild ongoing presentations using its AI Presentation Maker. Since Piktochart doesn’t require payment info to start, you can run both at once before your Beautiful.ai trial or billing cycle forces a decision.
