12 Presentation Apps and PowerPoint Alternatives for 2026

They can help you create and design beautiful presentations you’ll be proud to share with clients, prospects and other audience types.

How to Choose a Presentation App
The right presentation app or PowerPoint alternative for your team will depend on the combination of several aspects. The best way to figure it out is to run through these questions first before committing to any tool.
Take a tip from G2’s own content strategist, Soundarya Jayaraman:
“Some tools are crammed with features but feel like a chore to use, while others look sleek but lack the substance to back them up. So, when choosing the right software, focus on what really matters: features that save time, simplify the process, and help you connect with your audience.” She adds, “If a tool isn't user-friendly, it's out.”
What are you making?
Simple slides, an interactive experience, a pitch deck and an animated video each will point to different tools. For example, a sales pitch deck has different needs than a classroom lesson or a client-facing microsite. To check, open a tool's template gallery and filter by your use case or check the website and browse their features section. If your project type barely shows up, the tool might not cover that instance.
How much design control do you need?
Some apps snap elements into fixed layouts to help you move fast. Others hand you the full toolkit and let you place things wherever you want. If you're pulling together a slide deck for an internal update or a one-off social graphic, a fixed layout gets you there in minutes without any fuss. But for branded, client-facing work, where logo placement, spacing, and color need to match your brand guide exactly, full control matters more.
Here's a quick way to test it: open the editor you're considering and try dragging a single text box or image off the grid. If it won't budge, you're working inside the tool's rules, not your own.
Do you need AI to speed things up?
AI can draft slides, write copy and generate images from a prompt. But every tool offers different results. Check what each tool's AI actually does on their website and test it out. The "AI-powered" label can range from a single prompt-to-deck feature to a full editing suite. Run one real prompt during the free trial and see how much you'd still have to fix before it’s finished.
Who else touches the file?
Solo work and teamwork call for different features and workflow systems. If you plan to work collaboratively with colleagues, look for synchronous editing, comments, role assignment, and how brand assets are shared. Also check the approval flow and see if it matches your needs. To check, invite a colleague to one test file and watch how edits, comments and permissions behave.
What's your budget and ecosystem?
Free tools will surely cover the basics, but if you need professional features you’ll definitely have to get a paid subscription. Paid plans add elements like branding, analytics, export options and much more. If your team already runs on Google Workspace or Apple devices, a native tool may save money and setup time. Otherwise, you can start with a whole new platform. To see how your needs and budget align, list the two or three features you can't work without and confirm which plan tier includes them.
For Visme’s Growth Manager, this is one of the most important aspects to watch out for.
"Speed and accuracy are the two things every team needs when creating decks, internally or externally, and especially for leadership. If your tool makes you sacrifice either one, it isn't helping you scale or streamline. So, analyze whether the tool actually works with your existing system, or better yet, improves it." - Victoria Taylor, Organic Growth Manager, Visme

1. Visme
Visme is a cloud-based content creation software that allows designers and non-designers alike to easily create professional presentation slides, that you can present online or download as PDF, HTML5, PPTX or MP4
What sets Visme apart from other presentation apps and software is that it’s not just a presentation-making tool; it’s a full-scale content authoring platform.
Your Visme presentation editor comes with a wide variety of features for making many types of content, like infographics, documents, whiteboards, social graphics, and data visualizations, which can all be shared and tracked.
Templates and Design Elements
Visme offers a vast range of templates for presentations of any style: slideshow, video or interactive. You have the option to choose from pre-designed templates or themes with tons of slide layouts to combine as you wish.
The drag-and-drop editor has millions of design assets, including stock photography and video, animated vector icons, data visualization tools, hundreds of fonts, 3D elements, animated characters and preset color themes.
AI Capabilities
Visme splits its AI into two places. The AI Presentation Maker sits on your dashboard and builds a full presentation from a text prompt, generating the text, images and icons for a first draft you can edit.
Everything else lives in the AI Hub inside the editor:
- AI Writer writes, proofreads, summarizes and switches the tone of your slide copy.
- The AI image generator creates photos, illustrations, 3D graphics, icons and more from a description.
- AI TouchUp Tools unblur and upscale images, remove backgrounds, and erase or replace objects.
- AI Text-to-Speech turns slide text into a voiceover. Pick from 6 voices and set the accent, pace and mood, then drop the audio into a slide or hotspot.
- The Brand Wizard builds branded templates and keeps every slide aligned with your Brand Kit.
There's also Edit with AI. Found a template you like but the copy doesn't fit? Answer a few questions and Visme rewrites the template's content to match your topic, so you start from something close to finished.
Design Productivity
Creating a presentation with Visme is easy, thanks to several features that help minimize the time it takes to create slides. This is where the Visme vs PowerPoint difference really shows, with features like:
- Master Layout, which helps create a presentation that carries a base design across all slides.
- The ability to create and store content blocks and slides to use across other presentations.
- Dynamic fields to create personalized presentations according to the audience or prospect.
Interactivity and Animation
If you're looking for apps for creating immersive interactive experiences, Visme is a great option. There are various interactivity options, like:
- Hyperlink to other slides, projects or websites.
- Add hotspots to create new content areas and make slides more valuable.
- Embed videos from YouTube, Vimeo and more.
- Upload or record audio into your slides.
- Use Presenter studio to add a talking head recording of yourself.
- Beautiful animation and transition options that won’t overwhelm your audience.
Collaboration
Visme is a great presentation tool for teams to work together. With several collaboration tools, it’s easy to create content effectively with your colleagues.
Organize your work, share custom templates and ask for feedback from team members.
