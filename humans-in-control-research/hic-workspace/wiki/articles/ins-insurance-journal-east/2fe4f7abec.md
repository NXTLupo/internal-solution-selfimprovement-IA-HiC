# World Insurance Acquires Clarke Agency of New Jersey

Source: [ins_insurance_journal_east](https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/east/2026/07/01/875841.htm)

World Insurance Associates LLC reports that it acquired the business of Clarke Insurance Inc. of Mount Holly, New. Jersey on March 1, 2026.
Clarke Insurance was founded in 1979. The agency provides auto, home, business and employee benefits insurance throughout New Jersey. The agency has a dedicated bond department. Lawrence Clarke is the owner.
Terms of the transaction were not disclosed.
World Insurance, based in Iselin, New Jersey, is ranked #14 on Insurance Journal’s Top 100 Independent Property/Casualty Insurance Agencies list. The agency serves clients from more than 250 offices across the U.S. and U.K.
