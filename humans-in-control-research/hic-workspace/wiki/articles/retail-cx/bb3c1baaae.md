# Walmart launches Tap to Pay at Walmart stores, Sam’s Club locations

Source: [retail_cx](https://www.retailcustomerexperience.com/news/walmart-launches-tap-to-pay-at-walmart-stores-sams-club-locations/)

August 26, 2026
Walmart is launching Tap to Pay as a payment option at select Walmart stores and Sam's Club locations.
It plans to roll it out to all use U.S. stores and clubs by the end of the year and to fuel locations by mid 2027, according to a press release.
"We want customers and members to have choice in how they pay, so they can check out in the way that works best for them," stated the release
Customers and members can also add eligible Walmart, Sam's Club and OnePay cards to their digital wallets.
