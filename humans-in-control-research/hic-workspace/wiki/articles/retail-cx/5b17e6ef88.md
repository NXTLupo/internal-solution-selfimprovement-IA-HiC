# Wegmans testing revamped carts

Source: [retail_cx](https://www.retailcustomerexperience.com/news/wegmans-testing-revamped-carts/)

August 13, 2026
Wegmans is piloting redesigned shopping carts at two Pennsylvania locations.
The carts are lighter, quieter and include phone and cup holders, according to a Democrat and Chronicle report.
The redesigned carts are in pilot testing at the 3195 Monroe Ave., Pittsford, and 2157 Penfield Road, Penfield, store locations.
The carts offers greater flexibility for larger items, according to the news outlet. The grocer is adjusting the cart design given feedback from customers, according to the news outlet.
