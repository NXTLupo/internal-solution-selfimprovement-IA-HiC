# Northeast Dairy Producers Association Celebrates Dairy Month with NYS Department of Agriculture & Markets, Farmers, and Industry Partners

Source: [agr_morning_ag_clips](https://www.morningagclips.com/northeast-dairy-producers-association-celebrates-dairy-month-with-nys-department-of-agriculture-markets-farmers-and-industry-partners/)

ALBANY, N.Y. — The Northeast Dairy Producers Association (NEDPA), which represents New York’s progressive family dairy farms and industry partners, today announced highlights from June Dairy Month, including a Dairy Month Celebration in Jefferson County with the NYS Department of Agriculture & Markets, and a newly revamped NEDPA website: www.NEDPA.org.
“Every year, we gather in June to recognize our family farms and dairy partners who play an important role in getting wholesome, nutritious milk to consumers across this state and beyond,” said NEDPA Chair and Dairy Farmer AJ Wormuth. “During the roundtable discussion, farmers recognized the importance of science-based best management practices, the strides that have been made to improve farm efficiencies, and the continued support of Commissioner Ball, our champions in the State Legislature, and Gov. Hochul to ensure the viability of New York’s dairy industry. While much has been accomplished, we look forward to additional support for research that informs farm decision-making and other sustainable efforts to meet the increased demand for dairy across the state.”
For NEDPA’s annual Dairy Month Celebration, more than 30 farm and industry leaders representing New York dairy farm families, processors, allied businesses, and other organizations came together to celebrate National June Dairy Month. Farmers and industry leaders gathered at North Harbor Dairy in Sackets Harbor, Jefferson County, for a farm tour and roundtable discussion on how New York farms are working to remain competitive in a global market while continuing to strengthen industry collaborations and opportunities for the next generation.
Partners from across the state joined the celebration, along with leaders from the NYS Department of Agriculture & Markets, including Commissioner Richard Ball, Deputy Commissioner Katie Howard, Esq., and Empire State Development Director of Industry Development Allison Stark. Other industry partners included Cornell CALS Associate Dean Julie Suarez and PRO-DAIRY Associate Director Karl Czymmek, as well as representatives from NY Farm Bureau, Northeast Agribusiness and Feed Alliance, Northeast Dairy Foods Association, Jefferson County Economic Development, dairy processors, and other partners.
Farmers Recognize and Appreciate State Support
Farmers and industry leaders discussed the significant support of agriculture and dairy farming in the New York State Budget, including the extension of the Investment Tax Credit, funding for the Dairy Modernization Grant Program, and important funding for Cornell CALS, the NY Center for Agricultural Medicine and Health (NYCAMH), and Local Soil & Water Conservation Districts.

NEDPA Honors Assembly Agriculture Chair Donna Lupardo
Earlier this month, NEDPA joined Dairy Day at the New York State Capitol, where Board Chair and Dairy Farmer AJ Wormuth presented long-time Assemblymember and Agriculture Chair Donna Lupardo with the Friend of NEDPA Award. Lupardo was presented the award during Dairy Day in Albany on Tuesday, June 2, 2026, an annual event held in celebration of June Dairy Month that dates back to 1937.
The Northeast Dairy Producers Association, which represents progressive family dairy farms producing more than half of New York State’s fluid milk, is an organization of dairy producers and industry partners committed to an economically viable, consumer-conscious dairy industry dedicated to the care and well-being of our communities, our environment, our employees, and our cows. www.nedpa.org
— Northeast Dairy Producers Association
