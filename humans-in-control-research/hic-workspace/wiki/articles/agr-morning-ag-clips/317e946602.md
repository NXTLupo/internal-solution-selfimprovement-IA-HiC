# Christmas in July Workshop

Source: [agr_morning_ag_clips](https://www.morningagclips.com/christmas-in-july-workshop/)

WATERLOO, N.Y. — Transform your summer garden bounty or local farmers market finds into cherished holiday gifts! By preserving, drying, or infusing your produce right now, you can create thoughtful, cost-effective Christmas presents that friends and family will love all winter long. Join Cornell Cooperative Extension of Seneca County (CCE Seneca) on Thursday, July 9 from 6:00pm to 7:30pm at the Seneca Falls Community Center for our Christmas in July Workshop.
“Now is the time to begin to create holiday gifts with heartful meaning. Using local products whether you grow or purchase them, provides a connection to the land and a chance to revisit summer’s bounty!” says Christina Ehlers, CCE Seneca Nutrition/Human Ecology Educator. Through hands-on demonstrations led by CCE Seneca Master Food Preservers, participants will learn how to make jam, pickled vegetables, seasoning, cookies in a jar, and more!
The cost of the workshop is $15 per person. Registration is required. Space is limited. Register online at: www.senecacountycce.org. If you do not have access to the internet, or are in need of special accommodations, please contact the office at 315-539-9251.
CCE Seneca is a helpful resource for food safety information. Check out online resources for food preservation safety at http://senecacountycce.org/food-family/food-preservation or at https://nchfp.uga.edu/.
With over 100 years of serving the people of the County and named the 2023 Seneca County Chamber of Commerce Nonprofit Small Business of the Year, Cornell Cooperative Extension of Seneca County puts to practical use the scholarship and research of Cornell University and the national land grant system in the areas of agriculture and food systems sustainability; nutrition, food safety and security, and obesity prevention; 4-H youth development, and children, youth , and families; environment and natural resources, sustainable energy and climate change; and community and economic vitality. The office is located at 369 East Main Street in Waterloo. For more information about this and other programs, please visit our website: www.senecacountycce.org and our Facebook pages: @CCESeneca, @SenecaCounty4HNY, @CCESenecaF2S.
