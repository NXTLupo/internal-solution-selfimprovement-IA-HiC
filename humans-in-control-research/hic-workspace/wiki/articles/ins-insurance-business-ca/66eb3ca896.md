# Is your organization one of the insurance industry’s best workplaces?

Source: [ins_insurance_business_ca](https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/ca/news/breaking-news/is-your-organization-one-of-the-insurance-industrys-best-workplaces-580149.aspx)

The strongest insurance organizations know that great business starts with great people.
Insurance Business Canada has launched the eighth annual Top Insurance Employers, recognizing the companies creating exceptional workplaces through strong leadership, employee support, career development, and people-first culture.
This industry-wide recognition highlights organizations that are going beyond compensation — investing in wellbeing, engagement, flexibility, learning opportunities, and long-term employee success.
If your company is shaping a workplace people genuinely want to be part of, now is the time to gain the recognition it deserves.
