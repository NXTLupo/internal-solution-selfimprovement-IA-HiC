# Persona-Grounded Safety Evaluation of AI Companions in Multi-Turn Conversations

Source: [arXiv CS.CL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.00227v1)

There are growing concerns about the risks posed by AI companion applications designed for emotional engagement. Existing safety evaluations often rely on self-reported user data or interviews, offering limited insights into real-time dynamics. We present the first end-to-end scalable framework for controlled simulation and safety evaluation of multi-turn interactions with AI companion applications.
