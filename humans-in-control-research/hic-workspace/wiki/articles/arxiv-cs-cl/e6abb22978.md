# Towards trustworthy agentic AI: a comprehensive survey of safety, robustness, privacy, and system security

Source: [arXiv CS.CL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23989v1)

Agentic AI systems -- Large Language Models (LLMs) augmented with planning, tool use, memory, and long-horizon interactions -- can execute complex tasks autonomously, but their multi-step trajectories introduce new failure modes that challenge trustworthiness. This survey provides a focused examination of trustworthy agentic AI through two core dimensions that are critical for high-risk deployments: Safety and Robustness, and Privacy and System Security.
