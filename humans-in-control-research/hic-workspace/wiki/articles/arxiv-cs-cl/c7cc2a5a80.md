# An Approach for Auto Generation of Labeling Functions for Software Engineering Chatbots

Source: [arXiv CS.CL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.07094v2)

Software engineering (SE) chatbots are increasingly gaining attention for their role in enhancing development processes. At the core of chatbots are Natural Language Understanding platforms (NLUs), which enable them to comprehend user queries but require labeled data for training. However, acquiring such labeled data for SE chatbots is challenging due to the scarcity of high-quality datasets, as training requires specialized vocabulary and phrases not found in typical language datasets.
