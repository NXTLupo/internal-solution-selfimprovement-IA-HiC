Source: [arXiv CS.CL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.02707v2)

Risk assessment across industries is paramount for ensuring a robust and sustainable economy. While previous studies have relied heavily on official statistics for their accuracy, they often lag behind real-time developments.
