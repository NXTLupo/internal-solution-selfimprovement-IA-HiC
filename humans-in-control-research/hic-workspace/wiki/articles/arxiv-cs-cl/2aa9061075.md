# Methods for the Design and Evaluation of HCI+NLP Systems

Source: [arXiv CS.CL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.13461v1)

HCI and NLP traditionally focus on different evaluation methods. While HCI involves a small number of people directly and deeply, NLP traditionally relies on standardized benchmark evaluations that involve a larger number of people indirectly. We present five methodological proposals at the intersection of HCI and NLP and situate them in the context of ML-based NLP models.
