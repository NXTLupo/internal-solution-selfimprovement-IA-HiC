# NLP Service APIs and Models for Efficient Registration of New Clients

Source: [arXiv CS.CL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.01526v1)

State-of-the-art NLP inference uses enormous neural architectures and models trained for GPU-months, well beyond the reach of most consumers of NLP. This has led to one-size-fits-all public API-based NLP service models by major AI companies, serving large numbers of clients. Neither (hardware deficient) clients nor (heavily subscribed) servers can afford traditional fine tuning. Many clients own little or no labeled data.
