# How do Humans Process AI-generated Hallucination Contents: a Neuroimaging Study

Source: [arXiv CS.CL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16953v2)

While AI-generated hallucinations pose considerable risks, the underlying cognitive mechanisms by which humans can successfully recognize or be misled by these hallucinations remain unclear. To address this problem, this paper explores humans' neural dynamics to characterize how the brain processes hallucinated content.
