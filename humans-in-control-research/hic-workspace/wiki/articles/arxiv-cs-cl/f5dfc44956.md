# NLP meets psychotherapy: Using predicted client emotions and self-reported client emotions to measure emotional coherence

Source: [arXiv CS.CL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.12512v1)

Emotions are experienced and expressed through various response systems. Coherence between emotional experience and emotional expression is considered important to clients' well being. To date, emotional coherence (EC) has been studied at a single time point using lab-based tasks with relatively small datasets.
