# On the Effective Use of Pretraining for Natural Language Inference

Source: [arXiv CS.CL](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.02076v1)

Neural networks have excelled at many NLP tasks, but there remain open questions about the performance of pretrained distributed word representations and their interaction with weight initialization and other hyperparameters. We address these questions empirically using attention-based sequence-to-sequence models for natural language inference (NLI).
