# Neural at ArchEHR-QA 2026: One Method Fits All: Unified Prompt Optimization for Clinical QA over EHRs

Source: [arXiv CS.CL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10877v1)

Automated question answering (QA) over electronic health records (EHRs) demands precise evidence retrieval, faithful answer generation, and explicit grounding of answers in clinical notes. In this work, we present Neural1.5, our method for the ArchEHR-QA 2026 shared task at CL4Health@LREC 2026, which comprises four subtasks: question interpretation, evidence identification, answer generation, and evidence alignment.
