# 14 Dark Blue Paint Colors That Add Instant Drama to Any Room, According to Designers

Source: [veranda](https://www.veranda.com/decorating-ideas/color-ideas/a73494900/dark-blue-paint-colors/)

14 Dark Blue Paint Colors That Add Instant Drama to Any Room, According to Designers
From timeless navy to gutsy cobalt and everything in between.
Many homeowners tend to stray away from darker colors when it comes to painting their homes. Misconceptions lead us to believe that deep shades like aubergine, green, and even black will only make a space feel cold and cavernous. However, designers will tell you that there's nothing more chic than a room boldly and unapologetically painted in a rich hue. Thankfully, deep blues are one of the easiest color families to help you embrace the darker side.
It’s no secret that blue is one of the most valuable and versatile colors one can use in one’s home—but there’s something special about dark blue paint colors. They assert a timeless, yet enchanting quality that's downright addictive. A gutsy splash of cobalt on the cabinetry in a party closet or butler’s pantry turns the often forgotten-about spaces into design destinations of the home. Conversely, a deep teal may balance other vivacious shades in a sprawling living room and a blue-gray in a study may create a calming and comforting effect. 
With an array of shades and tones available, there's a dark blue paint color that will truly shine in your home—but it’s all about finding the right one. For inspiration, we’ve compiled a list of the 14 best dark blue paint colors that designers can’t resist. Whether you're drawn to a smoky teal or a chameleon cerulean shade, one of these blue paints is sure to be part of your next home project.
