# 28 of Dolly Parton’s Most Legendary Style Moments

Source: [veranda](https://www.veranda.com/luxury-lifestyle/luxury-fashion-jewelry/g73523664/dolly-parton-iconic-style/)

A Star Is Born
Michael Ochs Archives//Getty Images Prior to the release of her first number one hit, "Joshua," Parton gained notoriety for her songwriting, beautiful voice, and those amazing curls.
Becoming a Muse
Allan Tannenbaum//Getty Images Dolly Parton and Andy Warhol crossed paths several times throughout her career, and she most notably inspired his 1985 portrait of the singer. Here, at a Windows on the World event in New York City, she likely had the biggest hair in all of Manhattan.
Advertisement - Continue Reading Below
Bohemian Beauty
Michael Putland//Getty Images While performing at a Windows on the World in New York City in the ’70s, Parton is a vision in a flowing pink gown with sequin embellishments.
A Rising Star
Ron Galella//Getty Images In 1977, Dolly Parton had climbed the charts with hits like "Jolene," "I Will Always Love You," and "Coat of Many Colors." She was on the cusp of making the transition to pop-country crossovers as more leaders in the industry began to realize her star potential. Here, she sparkles at an RCA party with iconic agent Lee Solters.
Advertisement - Continue Reading Below
Dolly and the Pantsuit
GAB Archive//Getty Images Dolly Parton was rocking the pantsuit long before it was cool, and one of her first takes on the ensemble is classic Parton: bell sleeves, floral embellishments, and Barbie pink with a pair of oversize hoop earrings.
Lovely in Lavender
Richard E. Aaron//Getty Images Early on in Parton's career, the singer stuck mostly to pastels—unlike the vibrant colors we're used to seeing on her today. This breezy ensemble feels reminiscent of a simpler life back home in Tennessee, elevated with a bright purple belt and delicate beading.
Advertisement - Continue Reading Below
The Heartbreaker
Ed Caraeff/Morgan Media//Getty Images This now-iconic photo was taken for a cover session at an L.A. studio in 1978 and became the cover of her Heartbreaker album featuring the eponymous hit single. Layers of lace and pink chiffon give her a soft look.
London Calling
Pete Still//Getty Images Parton played at the Dominion Theatre in London all dressed up in colorful, beaded fringe. By this time in 1983, Parton had extended her fan base worldwide after collaborations with singers like Olivia Newton-John, Emmylou Harris, and Linda Ronstadt. However, her fame was cemented after starring alongside Jane Fonda and Lily Tomlin in 9 to 5.
Advertisement - Continue Reading Below
Country Barbie
Ron Galella//Getty Images Long before her release of Backwoods Barbie in 2008, Dolly Parton was the living embodiment of the term with her blonde hair, bright pink, and country flair. This 1977 outfit she wore at the Grammys introduced her new style era in the ’80s.
A Vision in White
Michael Putland//Getty Images Throughout the ’70s, Parton's style was relatively laid-back and, well, toned down. She always put a feminine touch on everything she wore, and this lacy blouse echoes her country roots in a chic way at an early ’80s press event in London.
Advertisement - Continue Reading Below
Lady in Yellow
Michael Ochs Archives//Getty Images While she was not known as much for wearing yellow in lieu of white or pink, this was a popular color choice for the singer over the years. It was during the ’80s that Parton began to develop her vibrant, larger-than-life style that matched her lovable persona.
Learning the Ropes
Ron Galella//Getty Images While Dolly Parton had released more than a dozen albums before 1980's 9 to 5, this was her first movie role. For the premiere, Parton hired costume designer Ann Roth to create a red carpet-worthy gown. Parton told People that this was the first time she truly felt fashionable after nearly 15 years in the limelight.
Advertisement - Continue Reading Below
Glamour Shot
ABC Photo Archives//Getty Images The over-the-top style trends of the 1980s helped fuel Parton's eccentric glamour, as she dug deeper into sequins, satin, and shoulder pads. Here, she posed for a photoshoot from her ABC variety show Dolly, which aired from 1987-1988.
Tasseled in Red
NBC//Getty Images Parton dazzled here in a brick-red dress embellished with a variety of golden accents for a performance on the Johnny Carson Show. She played up the country style of the 1970s in a more modern way for the ’80s.
Advertisement - Continue Reading Below
Country Strong
20th Century Fox//Getty Images This now-famous portrait was taken for Parton's film Rhinestone, where she starred alongside Sylvester Stallone. The pair earned stars on the Hollywood Walk of Fame following the film's release even though it was considered a flop.
Sophisticated Songstress
Ron Galella//Getty Images Parton looked cozy and chic in a fur coat at a premiere in Edinburgh, Scotland. The singer was most always found in voluptuous makeup and hoop earrings.
Advertisement - Continue Reading Below
All That Glitters
Ron Galella, Ltd.//Getty Images Parton and an all-star cast consisting of Shirley MacLaine, Daryl Hannah, Sally Fields, and Julia Roberts glowed at an event promoting Steel Magnolias. An all-white, beaded dress ensured Parton stands out like no one else.
Lovely in Leopard
Ron Galella, Ltd.//Getty Images Dolly Parton joined in on Fall Fashion Week fun in 1994 with legendary designer Calvin Klein.
Advertisement - Continue Reading Below
Business and Pleasure
Mike Coppola//Getty Images Here, at a 2011 event with country singer-songwriter Marty Stuart, Parton reimagined what the pantsuit could be with colorful beading in a botanical motif.
Back in Black
James Devaney//Getty Images Parton didn’t wear black often, but when she did, she made sure to do so with bedazzled flair. A fringe jacket, gold jewelry, and black pants with gold designs added distinct Dolly glamour.
