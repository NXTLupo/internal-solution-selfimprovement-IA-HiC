# 3 Fall Jewelry and Watch Trends We’re Coveting Right Now

Source: [veranda](https://www.veranda.com/luxury-lifestyle/luxury-fashion-jewelry/a73485356/fall-2026-jewelry-trends/)

As autumn gently comes rolling in, a new roster of dazzling jewelry and timepieces are about to earn their place in the spotlight. The upcoming season promises to be playful with a number of whimsical designs—from the return of charms to flirty fringed necklaces—hitting the market. And with every new creation comes an ode to the craftsmanship of years past. Here are the three jewelry and watch movements that I am most excited about this fall.
Playful, Personal Charms
Charms have long been used to show off one’s interests, travels, or mementos. Queen Victoria famously often wore a yellow gold bracelet featuring 16 oval- and heart-shaped charms. Some of those charms were set with jewels while others were engraved—yet all were meaningful to her.
Lately, jewelry designers have been infusing charms with a sense of whimsy and humor—like Marie Lichtenberg’s die charm (studded with diamonds, of course) and Circa 1700’s padlock that can actually be locked and unlocked with the accompanying key charm.
Celestial-Inspired Moon Phase Timepieces
The first object to track the phases of the moon was the Antikythera Mechanism. It essentially was an astronomical computer created in the second century B.C. by the Greeks. During the 17th century in England and Germany, apparatuses that could predict the moon’s phases began appearing in grandfather clocks, but it wasn’t until 1925 that Patek Philippe was able to include a moon phase complication on a wristwatch.
More than a century later, Patek Philippe has recently released Ref. 6105G-001, the Celestial Sunrise and Sunset timepiece. It takes its aesthetic inspiration from the heavens (specifically the night sky as seen from Geneva) and is able to display sunrise and sunset times, along with summer-to-winter time corrections.
Meanwhile, Van Cleef & Arpels’s Midnight Jour Nuit Phase de Lune watch boasts two complications, both as homages to the mysteries of the moon. One complication allows the moon to wax and wane and eventually give way to the sun, while the second powers the moon phase, which is visible upon demand.
Fabulous Fringed Jewelry
Fringed earrings, rings, and necklaces have been creeping their way back into the realm of jewelry, and they were in full force at Las Vegas’s JCK Market this summer. You’ll be seeing more and more of this glamorous, rock ’n’ roll-style pieces as the year goes on, so here’s your warning to snag your favorite one now. Earrings from iconic designer Robinson Pelham (at duster length, no less!) bring the drama without the weight, and necklaces like Cathy Waterman’s Arrow Fringe necklace are great for layering and adding movement without overwhelming.
