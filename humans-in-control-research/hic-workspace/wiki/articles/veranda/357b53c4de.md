# The 19 Best Furniture Brands Made in America, According to Interior Designers

Source: [veranda](https://www.veranda.com/shopping/furniture/a71774277/best-american-made-furniture-brands/)

Once upon a time, shopping for furniture meant investing in pieces that were handmade, high quality, and meant to last a lifetime. But over the years, fast furniture and mass production have taken center stage. For many homeowners, the influx of cheap furniture that doesn’t last has inspired a return to craftsmanship, longevity, and quality. That means interior designers are turning to American-made furniture brands for pieces that prioritize construction techniques designed to stand the test of time.
While aesthetics certainly matter, the best quality furniture is about so much more than appearances. From bench-made upholstery and handcrafted woodwork to sustainable sourcing and made-to-order production, so many American-made brands are creating pieces intended to be lived with for decades, not replaced every few years. We turned to interior designers to find out which made-in-America furniture brands they return to again and again for heirloom-quality pieces to add beauty and charm to your home.
Sherrill Furniture
Family-owned since 1945, Sherrill Furniture has long been recognized for its handcrafted upholstery and casegoods manufactured in North Carolina. The brand specializes in made-to-order pieces, offering a wide range of customization options including hundreds of fabrics, leathers, wood finishes, and trims. Its collections span everything from traditional silhouettes to more contemporary designs, all of which are of heirloom quality made to last a lifetime.
Coley Home
Founded in North Carolina, Coley Home is a relatively new brand that’s building a reputation for bringing custom, American-made furniture to a new generation of homeowners. The company prides itself on its wide range of made-to-order designs, including beds, sofas, dining chairs, and accent furniture at surprisingly accessible prices, courtesy of the brand’s direct-to-consumer model. Many of its pieces are designed to ship quickly, offering a speedy alternative to traditional custom furniture for shoppers who want high-quality handcrafted pieces without the typical extended lead time.
Kravet
Samuel Kravet moved to America from Russia in the early 1900s and began tailoring for New York City’s elite. In 1918, he started a business, called S.Kravet & Sons, selling fabric and trimmings to the interior design trade with his four sons. Though Kravet started as a small fabric house, the brand has expanded rapidly over the years—but still remains a fifth-generation family-owned business. To support its growing fabric collection—which now includes brands such as Kravet, Lee Jofa, GP & J Baker, Brunschwig & Fils, and Donghia—Kravet started selling upholstered furniture, which it manufactures in North Carolina, in 1992. Today, its furniture collections have expanded into several categories, including bedroom, dining, outdoor, and more.
Bernhardt
Established in 1889, Bernhardt is one of the oldest family-owned furniture manufacturers in the United States. While the company sources a number of its materials from around the world, much of its upholstery is handmade in North Carolina, where it continues to produce many of its signature collections. Known for blending traditional craftsmanship with modern and contemporary design, Bernhardt is a minimalist’s dream. The collection features a number of sleek and sculptural pieces, including everything from upholstered seating to dining furniture, and bedroom sets with an emphasis on quality construction and refined finishes.
Ebanista
Ebanista combines traditional furniture-making techniques with a distinctly modern sense of luxury. Founded by designer Fari Ferrini, the company produces artisan-made casegoods, dining tables, upholstered seating, beds, and lighting. The brand is known for layering classical influences with contemporary design, resulting in furniture that feels timeless and distinctive in equal measure. With an emphasis on exceptional materials, meticulous craftsmanship, and personalized service, Ebanista creates heirloom-quality pieces that bring lasting beauty and character to the home.
Stickley
Few names are as synonymous with American craftsmanship as Stickley. Founded by one of the leaders of the Arts and Crafts movement, the company has spent more than a century perfecting its approach to furniture.
“Their furniture has remarkable integrity, from the joinery to the wood selection, and the designs feel timeless rather than nostalgic,” says Jen Baxter, founder of Baxter Hill Interiors. Known for its solid wood construction and meticulous attention to detail, Stickley remains a favorite among designers and homeowners looking for furniture that feels both substantial and enduring.
Lee Industries
Lee Industries has become a go-to source for furniture that balances practicality and style. “For a family home that still needs to look like a real interior, Lee is usually my first call,” says Janelle Patton, owner of Dallas-based Lark Interiors. The company is particularly known for its slipcovered upholstery, sustainably sourced hardwood frames, and designs that feel both elegant and approachable.
Wren & Cooper
For those who appreciate the beauty of natural materials, Wren & Cooper is a standout. The brand specializes in solid wood furniture that highlights the character and individuality of each piece. “I love their emphasis on solid wood craftsmanship and exquisite details,” says Baxter. “Each piece has a warmth and individuality that’s increasingly rare.” Designed to develop a rich patina over time, Wren & Cooper’s furniture is intended to age gracefully, becoming more beautiful with use.
Medley
Sustainability and comfort rarely go hand in hand as seamlessly as they do at Medley. The California-based company has built a reputation for using nontoxic and environmentally conscious materials without sacrificing comfort. According to Baxter, “clean silhouettes, extensive fabric choices, and genuine customization” set the brand apart. Medley’s sofas, sectionals, and upholstered pieces have become particularly popular among homeowners on the hunt for healthier materials and personalized options.
Thayer Coggin
A pioneer of American modernism, Thayer Coggin remains one of the most respected names in contemporary furniture design. “The enduring Milo Baughman pieces still feel sophisticated and incredibly relevant today,” says Baxter. The North Carolina-based company is known for producing each piece from start to finish by a single craftsperson, a level of attention that designers say is increasingly uncommon in today’s furniture market.
Taylor King
Family-owned and based in North Carolina, Taylor King has earned a reputation among designers for both its heritage craftsmanship and innovation. “They pride themselves on heritage furniture that can be passed down for generations,” says Sarah Storms, founder and principal designer of Styled by Storms. “But this doesn’t mean it’s stuffy or uber traditional.” What sets the brand apart are its upholstery offerings, frame designs, and extensive fabric selections. The company also continues to invest in new technologies designed to improve comfort and everyday use.
Abner Henry
For those seeking truly one-of-a-kind furniture, Abner Henry is a go-to destination. “Abner Henry is a line our firm discovered years ago as a market find which has remained a firm favorite for all the right reasons,” says Amanda Sinistag, founder of Ellwood Interiors. Built in Ohio, the company allows designers and homeowners to customize nearly every aspect of a piece, from wood species and finishes to metal details and dimensions.
“The quality is unparalleled,” says Sinistag, noting the brand’s collaboration with Amish craftsmen and commitment to bespoke furniture making.
Verellen
Comfort and craftsmanship are the defining characteristics of Verellen’s furniture collections. “I especially appreciate their high-quality upholstery options, which feel both refined and deeply livable,” says Thecla Glueck, a San Francisco-based interior designer. Known for bench-made production and natural materials, Verellen has developed a devoted following among designers seeking understated, heirloom-quality pieces that are as beautiful as they are comfortable.
Lawson-Fenning
California-based Lawson-Fenning has become a leading voice in modern American furniture design. A favorite of Glueck’s, the brand’s work embodies a distinctly American interpretation of luxury, balancing warm woods, sculptural forms, and relaxed sophistication. While rooted in California modernism, Lawson-Fenning’s pieces are not without broad appeal, courtesy of their timeless silhouettes and emphasis on craftsmanship.
House of Leon
One of the newer names on the scene, House of Leon has quickly earned the attention of designers for its architectural approach to furniture. “I’m especially loving how they blend artistry, cultural influences, and a real enthusiasm for craftsmanship and quality materials,” says Glueck. The result is furniture that feels contemporary and distinctive while remaining approachable and functional enough for everyday living.
Cisco Home
For those drawn to relaxed, California-inspired interiors, Cisco Home is often a designer favorite. “As an L.A.-based designer, this local brand embodies the best of California casual styling with luxury USA-bred manufacturing,” says Sophia deDomenico, founder of Et Sal Interiors. The company is particularly well known for its upholstered pieces, including sofas, sectionals, and beds. DeDomenico notes that Cisco’s use of FSC-certified wood, hand-tied construction, and natural materials helps set it apart from many competitors.
Mayo Furniture
Though less well known outside of the design industry, Mayo Furniture has built a loyal following among professionals who consider durability a high priority. “They’ve been making upholstery in Sulphur Springs since 1977, and their durability standards are honestly some of the highest in the industry,” says Patton.
