# Playmates Toys Launches Collection Inspired by ‘Paris & Pups’

Source: [toy_book](https://toybook.com/playmates-toys-paris-pups-collection-launch/)

Inspired by Paris Hilton’s animated show Paris & Pups, Playmates Toys launched a Walmart-exclusive collection of playsets, trading cards, and plush. The collection is designed for kids ages 4 and up.
The affordable Sweetie Squad Charm Surprise Blind Box comes with a collectible trading card, bracelet, gold charm, and dog figure. There are more than 20 styles that kids can reveal. 
The Star DJ Doll & Bijou Puppy Playset includes a spinnable DJ table. The playset also includes a 5-inch Paris Hilton doll and a figure of her dog, Bijou. Kids can style Hilton and her dog with pink or purple headphones and a pink bow beret.

With the Paris Star DJ Deck Interactive Music Mixer, kids can play eight tracks from Paris & Pups. Kids can blend songs with the cross-fade slider, create remixes with the speed dial, or play tracks on repeat by activating looping. Additionally, 10 LED light effects, along with 35 SFX phrases and sounds, can be activated. 
The Paris & Pups Purse and Playset comes with a pink purse. When kids open the purse, it transforms into a playroom. The set includes an exclusive 2.5-inch figure of Hilton’s puppy Slivington, with a dog bed, sleeping mask, and gold-framed mirror.
The Slivington Interactive Plush Toy has more than 25 phrases and sound effects across two modes. When its collar is pushed, it lights up and runs around, wagging its tail.
	
Each set offers a surprise collectible gold charm and a trading card. The collection is available exclusively at Walmart. Learn more about Playmates Toys at playmatestoys.com.
