# Lululemon appoints new China and Apac leaders

Source: [inside_retail_asia](https://insideretail.asia/2026/08/24/lululemon-appoints-new-china-and-apac-leaders/)

Athleisure retailer Lululemon has reshaped its leadership structure across China and Asia Pacific, promoting San Yan Ng to regional president of China and Apac and appointing Jeffrey Hang as senior VP and GM of Apac.
The appointments bring Lululemon’s China and Apac operations under a shared leadership structure as the company continues to expand its presence across the region.
“As we continue to strategically increase our presence in these markets, we are pleased to promote San Yan to an expanded role and welcome Jeffrey to Lululemon,” said André Maestrini, interim co-CEO, president, and chief commercial officer, Lululemon.
“Together, their strong leadership and track records of success will help us to strengthen our local relevance in the region and grow our community of guests around the world.”
Ng has led Lululemon’s China business for the past eight years and will now oversee strategy across both China and Apac. She joined the company in January 2018 and has played a key role in building its presence in China, which has become one of Lululemon’s most important international markets.
Meanwhile, Hang brings more than two decades of experience leading premium consumer and retail businesses across Asia. Most recently, he served as MD for Bulgari across Southeast Asia, India, Australia and New Zealand. He was previously senior VP and CEO of Louis Vuitton China.
In his new role, Hang will lead Lululemon’s Apac teams, focusing on accelerating growth and strengthening the brand’s regional presence. He will report to Ng.
The new structure is designed to allow the retailer to better leverage market insights and capabilities across the two regions while retaining a local approach to their differing consumer needs and market dynamics.
