# Chanel scam: Luxury reseller calls out widely reused serial code

Source: [inside_retail_asia](https://insideretail.asia/2026/08/25/chanel-scam-luxury-reseller-calls-out-widely-reused-serial-code/)

Singapore-based luxury resale company Luxury Evermore has identified a Chanel serial code that has appeared 127 times across 36 countries in its authentication database.
The code, 10218184, has been recorded on multiple Chanel bag designs submitted through Luxury Evermore’s free authentication review service, raising concerns that it may be widely reused on counterfeit products.
According to the company, a genuine Chanel identification code should correspond to a single bag rather than appear across multiple products and designs worldwide.
Luxury Evermore said the finding emerged from data collected through its free Chanel Authentication Review service, which was launched to help consumers identify potentially counterfeit products in the growing secondhand luxury market.
“For us as resellers and authenticators, the serial number tag or microchip serves another important purpose: Helping us identify whether a product may be counterfeit,” Mingchuan Tian, founder of Luxury Evermore, shared with Inside Retail.
“There are specific patterns and details on these tags that are very difficult to replicate.”
Chanel’s product identification system has evolved over time. Bags produced before 2021 typically featured a serial-number tag and an authenticity card with the same code. Since 2021, the brand has moved to a microchip-based identification system, with the code engraved on a metal component.
However, counterfeit manufacturers also attempt to replicate these identifying features. Luxury Evermore says that because counterfeiters lack access to Chanel’s internal product records, they may reuse the same serial code across multiple bags and designs.
Luxury Evermore cautioned that a serial code alone is not enough to determine whether a bag is counterfeit. However, consumers considering a pre-owned Chanel bag carrying the code 10218184 should treat it as a warning sign and conduct further checks before making a purchase.
