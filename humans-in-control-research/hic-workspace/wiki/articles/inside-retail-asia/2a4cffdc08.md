# Italian luxury label Borsalino debuts in China

Source: [inside_retail_asia](https://insideretail.asia/2026/08/25/italian-luxury-label-borsalino-debuts-in-china/)

Italian luxury hatmaker Borsalino has opened its first permanent boutique in China at Plaza 66 in Shanghai.
The opening coincides with the brand’s 170th anniversary and forms part of its broader expansion strategy across Greater China and international markets.
The ribbon-cutting ceremony was attended by Borsalino MD Mauro Baglietto, Essence Group CEO Alec Hou, representatives of the Italian government and Plaza 66.
To mark the occasion, the brand unveiled a limited-edition jewellery collection featuring a Fedora adorned with a bespoke 18-carat gold logo set with rubies, sapphires and diamonds.
Alongside the boutique opening, Plaza 66 is hosting a Borsalino pop-up experience from 22 to 27 August, celebrating the maison’s heritage through a display of vintage hats, Italian craftsmanship and exclusive personalisation services.
Founded in Alessandria, Italy, in 1857 by Giuseppe Borsalino, Borsalino is the country’s oldest luxury hatmaker. Beyond its wholesale and retail business, the maison also operates through international distribution and has collaborated with luxury fashion brands and the film industry.
