The Brainfood
1. The History of HRTech
Only someone like John Sumser could write something like this - a concise and convincing history of HR Tech which goes from punch card technology in the 1900’s to GenAI in the 2020’s. The main point is HR is downstream of technology, and as tech changes, so will the human structures which evolved around their capability. Must read folks. John needs to give a talk on this, don’t you think?
TALENT OPERATIONS
2. Palantir - Institutional Sovereignty in the Age of AI
I'm finding hard to believe that this is the second consecutive newsletter where I’m platforming something from Palantir, but following on from CEO Alex Karp’s impassioned interview on CNBC last week, I think we should also read this manifesto on what Palantir called ‘Institutional Sovereignty’. I disagree (and object to!) Palantir on most things but this: companies will lose their coherency as discreet and sovereign institutions if they allow the frontier labs to ingest their cultural knowledge and rent it back to them on a subscription. Selfishly, I also think we’re going to do a lot more recruiting if more companies decide to self host, self maintain their own intelligence, as recommended here. Have a read - we have a stake in this, more than it might appear immediately obvious. H/T to brainfooder Srini Gundelli for the share in the online community
AI
3. I Want To Introduce You To Fake Stacy
Have you ever received an email from me which you had a suspicion was a fake? Judging from the number of messages I’m receiving daily from rightly concerned subscribers, I’m guessing a fair few of you might have. Brainfooders Stacy Donovan Zapar, Glen Cathey and others have been writing about this phenomenon and it’s worth us all having a read as to what they’re saying. The scam ultimately breadcrumbs its way to a CV rewriting service, a disappointingly lo-wattage fraud. Anyways, my primary concern is that scam is hugely corrosive to candidate experience - last thing job seekers need today is feel uncertainty when they are being approached for a job. For employers, this is an EB opportunity - can you fill the trust gap currently being widened by fraudsters?
EMPLOYER BRANDING
4. HR Automation Explorer
This tool maps out HR tasks and functions, scores them for automation risk and then recommendations actions for humans currently doing them. It’s pretty, and cool.
TALENT OPERATIONS
5. Why February New Hires are your Biggest Salary Compression Risk
New hire salary premium is a known phenomenon - companies need to offer better-than-average salary to entice people to move - leading to ripple effects on the existing members of the team who get disaffected by the newcomers higher salary. What we didn’t know is that this effect is not consistent across the year, and there seems to be noticeable spikes in February. Fascinating research from comp tech firm Ravio - with some intriguing implications as to when to time your hiring demand.
CULTURE
6. Global AI Confessions Report: CEO Edition 2026
Chances are there is no single biggest AI evangelist in your business than your CEO. Super interesting to see what they say about it in private. Obviously none of this can be verified, as we’re forced to trust me the aggregator, but the anonymised confessions are nevertheless a fascinating read.
CULTURE
7. Your Job Search is your first AI Project
The advice I’m giving to friends who are having a hard time looking for work right now is to turn the job search into an AI project, which I think will do three things for you: a) increase your effectiveness as a job seeker, b) practically upgrade your AI literacy with a real world project and c) re-establish a sense of control which we all need to maintain morale. Something like this approach seems accessible to anyone with a computer and internet. Worth a go? H/T to brainfooder Tim Sanchez for the share.
AI
8. The Chinese Voice Actor Forced to Prove He’s Human
That technology innovation outpaces the law is pretty much a law in of itself - and it’s finding expression first in the creator economy. Fascinating story of voice actor Shen Anyu who now spends most of his time tracking down unlicensed use of his voice and sending cease and desist letters. Who owns your voice, who owns your face, your gait, your expressions? Should be you of course, but how to stop anyone from using digital records that are already in the commons? Lots to think about here.
CREATOR ECONOMY
9. What is a Political Hire?
I know a few friends in the Civil Service, and I’ve always wondered how they can shift to execution only mode when carrying out policy from elected representatives they oppose in private. This is the essence of the ‘deep state’ narrative - the permanent civil service bureaucracy which has to keep the country running whilst democratically elected politicians cycle in / out in a perpetual routine of onboarding and off-boarding. How to assess for political neutrality? No perfect method but perhaps the ability to Steelman the arguments is as good a method as any.
ASSESSMENT
10. Catalyzing Competitiveness: Where Investment Happens and Why
We all know that greater investment is key to unlocking economic growth but do we really know how much ‘bang for our buck’ we’re actually getting? Fascinating research from McKinsey & Co on how much it costs to make things in US, EU, Mainland China and Rest of the World. Recruitment is downstream of industrial competitiveness, so if you’re interested in contextualising our direct lived experiences, this accessible interactive report s a great place to start.
