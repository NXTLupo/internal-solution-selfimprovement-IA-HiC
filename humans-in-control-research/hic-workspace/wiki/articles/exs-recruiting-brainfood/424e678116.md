The Brainfood 
1. LinkedIn Launches Creator Marketplace
This one caught me by surprise - a rather low wattage announcement that LinkedIn is going to formally create a marketplace where users can monetize their reach. Lots of things to think about here - the value of audience, decline of trust in traditional marketing channels, facebook / google both fumbling the ball when it comes to B2C social - and of course, how influencer marketing can be directly used as recruitment advertising.
CREATOR ECONOMY
2. A Little AI Survival Guide
There are a small number of us in the TA / HR community who are ‘frontier edge’ experimenters when it comes to AI & Automation. Brainfooder Guillaume Alexandre is one of those. He’s produced something here which I think everyone should read: a survival guide for HR, which covers not only the present but also the near future, especially on the requirement we all have to seriously investigate local, open source, on premise AI. Must read folks.
AI
3. The Age of the Solopreneur
One of the most underestimated ways which AI will impact employment will be to decouple capacity from labour; the aim of every CEO is now to establish ‘one person departments’ - Bersinian Superworkers who can do the work which previously might have required a full department. Yet this logic extends: if one person + ai agents can do all the work, why not step outside of the employment structure and collect more of the value? From the look of this excellent report from Stripe, it looks like its happening.
FUTURE OF WORK
4. Hybrid Working in 2026. The State of the UK and EU Tech Market
Where are we with hybrid working? Analysis of 100,000+ UK and EU tech job postings, to see how hybrid is at least being advertised, and how this correlates with candidate expectations and behaviour. Some particularly interesting insights: the collapse of tolerance of long commutes from 2 to 3 days is very real. Must read folks, so have at it here. H/T to brainfooder Nathan Connolly for the work and the share. Correlates with a teaser that brainfooder Adriano Herdman posted on LinkedIn last week, also worth a read (and stop teasing Adriano 🤣!)
REMOTE WORKING
5. The State of the AI Economy 2026
New analysis from the crew at Exponential View, who are becoming one of the leading independent researchers on the state of the AI economy. The 62 page slide deck is a triumph of concision + comprehensiveness; its quite technical economic analysis but useful for anyone here if you want to get a wide lens view of how the AI economy is structured, how the money flows through it, and how intelligence flows out of it. Must read folks.
AI
6. The World Cup: We Suck at Spotting Talent
We are terrible at identifying talent, so should accept this and create a system which allows multiple routers of re-entry, whilst focusing more on developing the talent we do have in pipeline, especially via informal, unstructured play. How much of this essay on scouting football players could be applied to corporate? On the humility part, quite a lot I’d say.
ASSESSMENT
7. LinkedIn UK Labour Market Report June 2026
Important report from LinkedIn on the state of the UK Labour market, with the key headline that employment rate remains below pre-pandemic levels. We’re suffering from a long period of anaemic economic growth, one which has already seen off multiple PM’s. Will Andy Burnham do any better? Going to take the sort of strategic endurance which UK current system of governance does not offer….H/T to brainder Jen Gronski for the share.
ECONOMY
8. Are You in the Weights?
A question that is irresistible to every narcissist out there 🤣. Nice website, not sure how accurate it is - nor how useful it might be - but a decent symbol of where we’re at in the SEO vs GEO space. Put your name in - which model gets you right?
EMPLOYER BRANDING
9. Where Salary Ranges Actually Help Job Seekers
Over 15 states in the US have pay transparency laws, but employers who have not spiritually bought into the idea, have a readily available workaround - massively wide salary ranges. This is useless for the job seeker, and it begs a question: how narrow does a salary range have to be in order to be useful? Interesting research from Corvi Careers, with breakdowns per metro and per sector
RECRUITMENT ADVERTISING
10. What We Learned from 1,604 Chinese AI Job Postings
As interest in Chinese open source AI is at a peak moment, with individuals and companies worldwide looking to hedge their bets against single vendor lock in in order to secure token efficiency, as well as business continuity, this might be an opportune moment to increase our knowledge of the Chinese AI eco-system. What can we infer from who they are looking to hire? Key point from this excellent piece of research: far less emphasis on experience vs US AI labs.
