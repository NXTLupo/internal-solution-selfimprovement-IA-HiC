Friends,
I’m writing this in Gyeongju - a wonderful small town in the south-east of Korea. The former capital of the Kingdom of Silla during the Three Kingdom’s period of Korea, it was purportedly once the 4th largest city in the world with 1 million people at peak. But Silla collapsed and the city retrenched leaving the outlying areas full of archaeological discoveries so today the place is known as the ‘museum without walls’. There is poignancy in decline and collapse - listen to Fall of Civilisation podcast if you want to immerse - but for a modern day visitor, former imperial capitals seem to be great places to visit.

The Brainfood
1. The 2026 AI Index Report
The HAL AI Index has rapidly become one of the must read annual. Some highlights from its release last week: performance of leading models is accelerating (no wall yet on LLM’s, even though LeCunian skeptics are confident there is a visible dead end), but increasingly concentrated on a few companies, especially OpenAI / Anthropic, potentially a emerging duopoly. Segments of particular interest to TA - the distribution of AI talent based on publications and patents, as well as a full segment on Responsible AI. Long report but set aside a couple hours and read it all. Download here.
AI
2. Mercer Global Talent Trends 2026
Interesting report in that it directly samples two different populations (CEO’s + CHRO’s) asking them the same questions and then comparing where we’re at. It’s uncomfortable to learn that C-level and HR are not only in dissonance on priorities, but also that only 8% of the former perceive the function as being strategically significant. The days of ‘asking for a seat at the table’ are over imo - that battle has been lost, and what is required today is no less than a total reinvention of identity and purpose. Going to start a Brainfood Live series on this, watch this space.
TALENT OPERATIONS
3. Turn Your Best AI Prompts into One-click Tools in Chrome
AI at the frontier might seem like a two way battle between OpenAI and Anthropic, but does anyone else get the feeling that Google / Microsoft might win out in the end, due to their unsurmountable distribution advantage? Small inkling here as to what Google might do if they managed to get all their ducks in a row - prompts turning into skills within Chrome by simply saving like a bookmark. Brainfooder Glen Cathey introduced to me with his post with his EB research use case, a great illustration of some base level productivity that is available to any Chrome user (about 60% of us). As Glen suggests, read Google’s own blog post about it and start using it.
SOURCING
4. I Submitted a GDPR Subject Access Request after an Interview Process that Ended in Silence.
One of the core principles of this era of data privacy is for the subject of the data processing to be able to request the data from the processor. Brainfooder Amanda-Jane Lamont - an experienced TA looking for work in Berlin - did just that after going deep into process with one employer but then seemingly being dropped in a classic ghosting outcome. Fascinating insight on the process and some interesting revelations when Amanda-Jane discovered the reason for the rejection…have a read folks.
CANDIDATE EXPERIENCE
5. Talent Intelligence Collective Market Scan
Talent Intelligence Collective - or TIC - is the No1 community for Talent Intelligence professionals. Ran by Toby Culshaw, who not only has the best CV in TI but also finds the time to knock up tools like this. I kind of need to get my act together don’t I?
TALENT INTELLIGENCE
6. That ‘70s Show? Oil Prices and Unemployment
Whilst the US-Israeli war on Iran will undoubtedly spur greater global commitment to new energy transition, the world still very much depends on fossil fuels, a significant percentage of which flows out of the middle east, through the Straits of Hormuz. Higher inflation means less headcount, means more gloom for us in the world of TA / HR. Julius Probst, Chief Economist at Appcast, comments with historical comparisons.
ECONOMY
7. 100 Hours Inside Kimi
There are no OKRs, no KPIs, no office politics, no manipulative managers, not even clock-in attendance. But if you are not AI-native, if you cannot generalise, if you cannot continuously reinforce and adapt, then you may struggle to find meaning for your existence here.
Super interesting translated article on the working culture at Moonshot AI, the company behind the increasingly popular Kimi model series. What’s fascinating to me is that every leading frontier model business seems to have distinct company cultures, you have to wonder how that then translates into the AI’s that they create. Great read.
CULTURE
8. The AI Layoff Trap 2026
We didn’t really need a 30 page research paper to tell us that the embedded imperatives of private sector firms in a capitalist economic system would mean a competitive race to use AI / Automation race to reduce payroll which no firm can afford to delay, but here we are. At least this paper seems to have reached some sort of traction and has got important people pondering modern heresies like worker equity share, corporation taxation calculated on AI displacement and the return of the ‘big state’ in wealth redistribution.
SOCIETY
9. When “Positive” Becomes Problematic: Rethinking Dysfunction in Organizations
When a team resists change, it may not be “stuck in the past,” but holding knowledge that has not been acknowledged in decision-making processes.
We often look at ‘negative’ reactions and behaviours are flaws which need to be corrected, but what it they are natural defence mechanism against bad ideas done too quickly? This article is a fascinating defence of the messiness of human social organisation - we’re an English country garden folks - and we might want to ensure we stay that way.
CULTURE
10. I Tracked Every Stage of My Job Search. The Results are a Damning Indictment of Senior Recruitment.
Last week’s post by Mark Dawkins really sparked an online conversation on ageism. My position on this is that it is both necessary to highlight the injustice but also necessary to advance the conversation to what to do about it - from both employers side, as well as from individuals side. I’m running a poll on the latter on LinkedIn, which I hope will be a starting gun for a different type of conversation on age-ism - love for you to chime in there with your vote and thoughts. In the meantime, another forensic post from an experienced job seeker logging the challenges of looking for work as someone ‘too senior’.

The Podcasts
11. Are Fossil Fuel Cars About to Have A Kodak Moment?
Increasing the cost of fossil fuels will inevitably accelerate the adoption of EV’s, and the moment may well have arrived for legacy auto makers who have almost all failed to respond to the risks and opportunities presented by new technology. We should remember that it was Nissan which launched the LEAF in 2009 before killing it off for being unprofitable, just as Kodak invented the digital camera but could not find a way to invest in a technology which would cannibalise their major money maker. Good chat on where we’re at today with EV’s. NB: incidentally, Kodak are still around, reinventing themselves as a lifestyle brand, as I discovered when I was in Seongsu…
