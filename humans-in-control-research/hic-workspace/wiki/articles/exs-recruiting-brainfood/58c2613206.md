The Brainfood
1. Deloitte 2026 Global Human Capital Trends
80% of leaders, managers, and workers are concerned their co-workers and teams are using AI to appear more productive than they are.
It’s one of the annual reports that you have to read, even though its survey data and rather annoying features the same visual across every segment as some sort of recurring motif. Some good bits though, especially the essay on cultural debt which employee use of AI may be incurring. Also check out the segment on the future of functions - the ‘run the business’ vs ‘grow the business’ framing is maybe the most useful mental model here. Download here
CULTURE
2. From Hierarchy to Intelligence
We underestimate the impact AI will have on jobs because we focus overly much on calculating the capacity it might displace individual workers, and not nearly enough on the coordination between workers it will may make redundant. Jack Dorsey might be one of the first CEO’s to explicitly explain the purpose of ‘bullshit jobs’ is to route information across a complex organisation, which requires a hierarchy, which then requires more hierarchy to support it. Not so if Block manage to diffuse intelligence across the business, create a ‘world model’ and replace hierarchy altogether. Fascinating essay, fascinating experiment, one to keep an eye on. PS: kind of related to this screed Collaboration is Bullshit, a sentiment familiar to anyone whose has too many meeting and wonders why…
TALENT OPERATIONS
3. The Enterprise AI Playbook: Lessons from 51 Successful Deployments
Outstanding piece of work from Stanford Digital Economy Lab, who conducted structured interviews on 51 large scale AI deployments and attempted to identify common causes for success / failure. Written in accessible language, with just-the-right-depth of case study, this document is going to be one of the most important ones we read this year. Download here. PS any number of lessons in this, including 61% of successful deployments had at least 1 x failed deployment preceding it…
CULTURE
4. Why Has the Hiring Rate Fallen?
We’re seeing a chart online which appears to depict the recovery of recruiter jobs but it doesn’t pass the sniff test for most of us in the game. Love my boy Amit from Trueup but as Jeff Bezos memorably said, if the anecdote and the data clash, check the data. Brainfooder Aaron Lintz with his thoughts, and I’m going to provide some alternative narratives painting a picture which feels more resonant with lived experience: this piece from Budget Lab tracking ‘flow rate’ of unemployed-to-employed, whilst this one from PIIE reminds us that research on AI impact on employment is still at its very early phases.
ECONOMY
5. LinkedIn is Searching Your Computer
LinkedIn doesn’t like unauthorised Chrome extensions and has been adding to its prohibition list every year - I think it is up to around 5000 by now? The overwhelming majority of these seem to related to LinkedIn’s core business which for me, is ‘fair enough’, but some others - religious / political extensions - also appear on the list and you wonder how safe such tracking might be, if we are able to ID religious and political affiliations thereby. An InfoSec issue or a DEIB one? Increasingly indistinguishable in these troubled times. H/T to brainfooder Jan Tegze for the share.

We’ve talked about ‘GEO’ - Generative Engine Optimisation - before in this newsletter, and I think EB pro’s are beginning to take the idea seriously. Brainfooder Glen Cathey with the provocation here, which is well worth experimenting on with your own EB. Good news is other community members are onto it, brainfooder Ben Philipps with his account on an AI visibility audit he’s currently conducting.

7. Most Recruiting Teams are One Good Agent Away from Getting their Hours Back
There is no question that OPC (One Person Companies) and / or small team operations are going to reap the AI dividend well ahead of folks in the Enterprise. Interesting breakdown on how one recruiter did it. Yeah compliance I know etc etc.
ASSESSMENT
8. The 2026 State of AI Traffic & Cyberthreat Benchmark Report
Well adjacent to compliance, is information security. Something which I suspect a lot of us recruiting are going to have to get well acquainted with. This is not going to be to everyone’s flavour so I expect a specialism to emerge with Talent Ops which deals specifically on this area. AI training crawlers - software which ingest the internet to feed training models - overtook human traffic last year. Feels like a milestone.
INFOSEC
9. Recruitment Rewired: an Update on the ICO’s Work on the Fair and Responsible Use of Automation in Recruitment
UK recruiters probably need to learn a new phrase - ADM - “Automated Decision Making”. Whilst outside of the EU, the UK is following GDPR provisions fairly closely, especially when it considers recruitment to be an AI use case requiring special attention. My reading of this update from the Information Commissioner’s Office is that its all about fair use + consent + right to dispute + evidence of testing for bias. Do-able stuff for operators, basically. Follow brainfooder Martyn Redstone if you want more nuanced commentary on this. H/T to brainfooder Zichuan Lim for the share

The Podcasts
11. Benedict Evans: OpenAI’s Moat Problem & the Future of Software
If you need an anti-dote to AI doomerism, then Ben Evans is your man. Reliably sanguine about the future of humanity, Evan’s main point is that tech revolutions are always exciting in the moment, but transformations means creation as well as destruction - and that humans will be ok. He’s a skeptic on the AI economy (bubble) and he’s a good listen to anyone who wants to understand this position.

12. EV Battery Manufacturing - Why Europe Can’t Keep Up
Donald Trump is an accidental accelerator for two of the big macro trends impacting Team Humanity at large - AI, and for New Energy Transition. The energy crisis in the Persian Gulf is only going to make coming off fossil fuels more attractive - and battery’s are the key to that. Super interesting documentary of the failure of one of Europe’s big hopes for this space - NorthVolt, a great of which can be placed on poor SWP & TA.
