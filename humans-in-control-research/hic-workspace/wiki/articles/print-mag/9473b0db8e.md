# What Matters to Debra McGuire

Source: [print_mag](https://www.printmag.com/fine-art/what-matters-to-debra-mcguire/)

Debra McGuire is an award-winning fine artist, fashion designer, and costume designer whose work spans film, television, and theater. Over nearly five decades, she has designed for some of the most recognizable projects in contemporary screen culture, including all ten seasons of Friends, her long-standing collaborations with David Mamet, including the Emmy-nominated Phil Spector, and projects with Judd Apatow, Jake Kasdan, Jay Chandrasekhar, Adam McKay, John Francis Daley, and Jonathan Goldstein. Her credits include Anchorman, The 40-Year-Old Virgin, Knocked Up, Superbad, Walk Hard: The Dewey Cox Story, Game Night, Sonic the Hedgehog, Sonic the Hedgehog 2, Murder Mystery 2, New Girl, Freaks and Geeks, Heroes, Crossing Jordan, The Morning Show, Super Troopers 2, and the forthcoming Super Troopers 3 and Mayday. A fine artist at heart, McGuire began as a painter before expanding into jewelry, accessories, fashion, couture, and costume design.
Pronouns for the post: she/her
What is the thing you like doing most in the world?
There is not one thing but many things: I love being with my family and friends; I love walking with my husband at dusk and marvelling at the sunset and the beauty of our trees that he cares for; I love travelling the globe and meeting new incredible people; I loved taking care of my parents; I love babies; I love working in my studio and getting lost in the work; I loved writing my book; I mostly love BEING in this world. I’m VERY blessed and grateful.
What is the first memory you have of being creative?
I do talk about this in my book, Dressing the Story: The Art of Costume Design, which just released. I was 7 when I sent drawings into Millie the Model Comic Book and they got published: Debbie Kay Fine, Designer. I always loved to draw and paint and took art classes at the Cleveland Museum of Art, starting when I was 10. My parents encouraged me. But I also liked to write (journals), sing and perform. I was focused on fashion, makeup and hair for as long as I can remember. When I was 4 my mom caught me propped on the bathroom sink trying to give myself a perm. It was the scent of chemicals that led her to me. She was hysterical but eventually told me that it was miraculous that I knew what to do!
What is your biggest regret?
It’s not really a regret but just the hand I was dealt…to work and leave my children everyday. Lily was born 3 days before FRIENDS started and off to work I went with her in a basket. I would give anything to have had those precious early years with them…but I get them now and what fabulous young adults they’ve become!
How have you gotten over heartbreak?
I’ve been thinking about this a lot as a parent. I’m very disciplined. I’ve meditated and exercised (swimming) every morning for 55 years. I think this is, and has been, my salvation. My big heartbreak was ending a relationship when I was 30, with someone I loved dearly but knew wasn’t the right person for me. I was catatonic for 8 months, working in my studio everyday. I moved in with friends who nurtured and cared for me. They helped restore my confidence and taught me about selfless love and true friendship.
What makes you cry?
Happy tears and sad tears come easily. I’m an empath and can cry for other people’s pain as well as the pain of a people or a country. Acts of kindness make me cry. I cry at every wedding and seeing a newborn child. I cry when I feel a beautiful, kind soul in my midst. My husband and children’s tears make me cry.
How long does the pride and joy of accomplishing something last for you?
I hold on as long as I can but real life and work take over pretty quickly.
Do you believe in an afterlife, and if so, what does that look like to you?
I believe in the kabbalistic journey of the soul and see, through reincarnation, how the soul completes its divine mission. Lets just say that the soul makes pit stops along the way to reincarnation and is believed to work across multiple lifetimes. I studied Buddhism in my 20’s and was a student of Chogyam Trungpa Rinpoche. This 10 year journey led me back to my Jewish ancestry and the study of Kabbalah for these last 30 years.
What do you hate most about yourself?
Ouch. That’s harsh. I’ve been working on myself for a long time. I would never use that word to describe something that I didn’t like about myself. I would like to be more patient and less anxious.
What do you love most about yourself?
I must have done something right to have the family and friends that surround me each day. My girlfriend hosted a book launch for me the other day and the atmosphere was filled with LOVE. LOVE everywhere. I would like to think that my energy and positivity are my best assets.
What is your absolute favorite meal?
Thin crust crispy pizza and a glass of very cold rose.
