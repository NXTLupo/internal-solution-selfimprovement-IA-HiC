# The Art of NYC Streets

Source: [print_mag](https://www.printmag.com/creative-voices/the-art-of-nyc-streets/)

<p>Creative craftsman Kristian “Sheps” Shepherd finds inspiration in the gritty, layered visual language of New York City’s streets, recreating its weathered signs, stickers, graffiti, and imperfections through handmade prop design. His experiment becomes a celebration of going analog—embracing tactile creativity, imperfection, and the simple pleasure of making things in an increasingly AI-focused creative world</p>
