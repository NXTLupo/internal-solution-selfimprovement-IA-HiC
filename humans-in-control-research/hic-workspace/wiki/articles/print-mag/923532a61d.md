# Instagram Refreshes Its Look for a New Era

Source: [print_mag](https://www.printmag.com/branding-identity-design/instagram-refreshes-its-look-for-a-new-era/)

Oh no, Instagram has refreshed—and, well, it has changed its logo. Meta announced today that the new design celebrates the brand’s creativity and community.
An evolution, rooted in heritage
First launched in 2010, Instagram is far from its original roots. The platform was focused on square moments, presented in chronological order and primarily shared among friends. Brands, influencers, videos, Stories—they weren’t really a thing yet. What a time to be alive! The logo was inspired by an old retro camera, and, well, sepia filters and weird borders were all perfectly acceptable.
By 2016, the platform was beginning to signal a whole new era, and a major visual refresh came into play. Now, almost 10 years later, we have a new wordmark, typography, gradients, and layouts—all inspired by the brand’s sense of community. (If by community we mean influencers, then I guess that’s who this design was for.)
In terms of the refresh, I think it addresses the typical proprietary needs of a brand at Instagram’s scale. Instagram Sans, the expanded typeface, and a more flexible system take into account all the needs the platform currently has for its “community,” with layouts that better serve those chronically posting across an ever-growing number of formats.
And, well, I’m not sure anyone will be up in arms over the new logo design. Personally, I find it to be a simple reinterpretation of the previous logo—an evolution rather than a reinvention.
