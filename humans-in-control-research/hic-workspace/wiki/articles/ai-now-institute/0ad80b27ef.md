# Episode 20: Resisting AI Data Centers, with Alli Finn and Matt Rodriguez

Source: [ai_now_institute](https://ainowinstitute.org/news/press/episode-20-resisting-ai-data-centers-with-alli-finn-and-matt-rodriguez)

On this episode of the Dreaming Against the Machine podcast, host Dr. Adam Becker talks with Alli Finn (AI Now Institute) and Matt Rodriguez (Athena Coalition) about a very specific kind of better future: one with way fewer AI data centers. Alli and Matt explain why we should fight AI data center construction, how to fight it, and what you can do if there’s already one in your city.
“If these things were actually beneficial to local communities, these billionaires would be building them where they live,” says Alli Finn. “And they’re not.”
