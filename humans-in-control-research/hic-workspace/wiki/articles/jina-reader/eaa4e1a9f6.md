# Welcome From Purdue President Mung Chiang

“With Excellence at Scale, Purdue University creates talents, jobs and innovation along America’s Hard Tech Corridor. Boilermaker students, faculty, staff and alumni take small steps each day that turn into giant leaps in the discovery and dissemination of knowledge.”

![Image 4: Purdue President Mung Chiang's signature.](https://www.purdue.edu/president/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/President-Mung-Chiang-signature.jpg)
Mung Chiang

President, Purdue University
