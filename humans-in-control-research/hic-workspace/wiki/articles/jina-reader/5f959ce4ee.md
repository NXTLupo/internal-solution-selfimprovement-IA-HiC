## Supporting Scholars

From the beginning, _Rising to the Challenge_ has been all about supporting the people of Johns Hopkins: our amazing students, faculty, and clinicians — who are the lifeblood of our institutions.

### Small Gift. Big Impact.

#### More than 878,000 gifts from 279,000 donors

##### The real story of this campaign lies behind these numbers; it is in the fueling of interdisciplinary and cross-divisional research, the investment in our people, the commitment to our home city of Baltimore, and our focus on daunting global and societal challenges.

## Fueling Discovery

As America’s first research university, we are fueling discovery across disciplines and delivering new knowledge for the benefit of humankind.

> A lot of science is solitary – you’re the only one who knows your question and what you’re looking for. Through Kavli, the collaboration has been outstanding.”
> 
> Audrey Branch Johns Hopkins Kavli Neuroscience Discovery Institute Fellow

### Small Gift. Big Impact.

#### 505,000

##### This is the total number of gifts we have received under $100, emphasizing that this has truly been a campaign of broad-based engagement throughout the Johns Hopkins family.

## Promoting & Protecting Health

We are proud of our legacy as a national and global leader in patient care and the fight against disease, and we reaffirm our commitment to creating a healthier future for all.

### Small Gift. Big Impact.

#### $5-$30,000 gifts

##### A scholarship created with gifts of $5 to $30,000 from more than 140 individuals has opened doors for a gifted Peabody student — and helped a family heal.

## Strengthening Communities

Mr. Johns Hopkins’ bequest was an extraordinary gift to students, faculty, and clinicians he would never meet, and patients he would never know. It was also a promise to the people of Baltimore that has only expanded over time.

> Baltimore is home. Baltimore will forever be home for me... I feel very strongly about keeping and cultivating great talent here, not just during college."
> 
> Brooke-Logann Williams A&S '15

### Small Gift. Big Impact.

#### 400 donors = $88,000 research fund

##### A research fund created by the family of Zach Sowers, which now totals $88,000 from 400 donors, has grown into a valuable resource for physician-scientists.

For more than 140 years, we’ve been rising to the challenges of our time. Will you join us?
