# Purdue launches 5-year, $4 billion campaign

![Image 5: Students walking and cycling under Gateway Arch](https://www.purdue.edu/newsroom/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/arch_students.jpg)
"Victories & Heroes: Your Campaign for Purdue" is the largest campaign in university history. (Purdue University photo)

February 17, 2025[Aaron Martin](https://www.purdue.edu/newsroom/author/aaron-martin/ "posts by Aaron Martin")

WEST LAFAYETTE, Ind. — On Saturday (Feb. 15), Purdue University President Mung Chiang announced the launch of “Victories & Heroes: Your Campaign for Purdue.” The goal of the fundraising campaign — the largest in the university’s history — is $4 billion raised by early 2030.

Chiang made the announcement during the President’s Council Annual Dinner at the Purdue for Life Foundation’s Annual Week in Naples, Florida.This year’s events were well attended by about 600 alumni, university leaders and trustees.

“From the Wabash River to the face of the moon, Boilermakers have made positive impact across generations. As American higher education goes through a transformative time, our university is uniquely positioned for excellence at scale,” Chiang said. “This comprehensive campaign, the third in Purdue’s history, will invest in the long-term future of this most consequential public university in the country. We are ever grateful for the philanthropic support from all our Boilermaker alumni and friends.”

![Image 6: Mung Chiang stands in front of banner reading $4,000,000,000](https://www.purdue.edu/newsroom/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/campaign_4billion_banner.jpg)

Purdue President Mung Chiang announces the launch of “Victories & Heroes: Your Campaign for Purdue” at the President’s Council Annual Dinner, held Saturday (Feb. 15) during the Purdue for Life Foundation’s Annual Week in Naples, Florida. (Purdue for Life Foundation photo)

This comprehensive campaign will accelerate the growth of Purdue’s endowment while focusing on the university’s four [strategic initiatives](https://www.purdue.edu/president/strategic-initiatives/) — its comprehensive urban expansion in Indianapolis, the Mitch Daniels School of Business, Purdue Computes and One Health — as well as Purdue Athletics and other key areas. The financial support will help further amplify successes across the university’s land-grant mission: learning, discovery and engagement. The philanthropic funds raised will support scholarships, professorships, research, facilities and university programs.

The “Victories & Heroes” theme is inspired by Purdue’s fight song, “Hail Purdue!” The lyrics, written in 1912 by student James Morrison, include a line celebrating “your vict’ries and your heroes.”

![Image 7: The lyrics for Purdue’s fight song, “Hail Purdue!” which inspired the Victories & Heroes campaign theme.](https://www.purdue.edu/newsroom/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/HailPurdue-lyrics.jpg)

The “Victories & Heroes” campaign theme originates from Purdue’s fight song, “Hail Purdue!” Written in 1912 by student James Morrison, the song has inspired generations of Boilermakers. (Purdue for Life Foundation photo)

“Victories & Heroes” will build on the success of two previous comprehensive campaigns, including “The Campaign for Purdue” and “[Ever True: The Campaign for Purdue University](https://www.purdue.edu/evertrue/).” “The Campaign for Purdue” was announced by President Martin Jischke in 2002, targeting $1.3 billion; it concluded in 2007 and raised $1.7 billion from 184,000 donors. “Ever True” was announced by President Mitch Daniels in 2015 and concluded in June 2019, exceeding its goal of $2.019 billion by generating more than $2.5 billion from more than 209,000 donors.

The campaign announcement launches amid ongoing momentum and record-breaking achievements at Purdue:

*   Purdue is ranked among the top 100 universities in the world, as the [No. 1 public university in Indiana](https://nam11.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.purdue.edu%2Fnewsroom%2F2024%2FQ3%2Ffor-second-year-in-a-row-purdue-ranked-among-top-50-undergraduate-colleges-in-america-by-u-s-news-world-report%2F&data=05%7C02%7CACMartin%40purdueforlife.org%7C0b4a8cf351374208304908dd30027a93%7Ce25b40edf9c844b99420fe1c86641854%7C0%7C0%7C638719509416112178%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=sd%2Bxt7eUlePvKBrK37kwXTVNUnRhzUak%2Bprp6AsUagc%3D&reserved=0) and as [No. 8 among U.S. public universities](https://nam11.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.purdue.edu%2Fnewsroom%2F2024%2FQ2%2Fpurdue-recognized-among-worlds-best-in-latest-qs-rankings-and-rises-to-no-8-among-u-s-public-universities%2F&data=05%7C02%7CACMartin%40purdueforlife.org%7C0b4a8cf351374208304908dd30027a93%7Ce25b40edf9c844b99420fe1c86641854%7C0%7C0%7C638719509416125893%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=d1MOj2rrQJuQGUfTgzwN7bjXKBHfStnBUAiTA0rGsc8%3D&reserved=0), with 31 undergraduate and graduate programs ranked among the nation’s top 10.
