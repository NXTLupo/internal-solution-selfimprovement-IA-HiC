## Institutional Data Analytics + Assessment

IDA+A performs research, statistical and predictive analysis, and reporting for campus leaders and decision-makers in support of evidence-based planning, evaluation, and assessment. We work with partners to transform institutional data into a rich body of strategic intelligence that is accessible, well-defined and useful for decision-makers throughout the institution.
