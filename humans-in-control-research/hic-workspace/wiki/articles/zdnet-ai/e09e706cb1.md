### ZDNET’s key takeaways

*   HR sees AI reskilling potential, but outside hiring outpaces internal moves.
*   Limited visibility into AI skills makes reskilling opportunities hard to spot.
*   Hiring externally often costs more and takes longer than reskilling.

* * *

HR leaders are confident that current employees can be trained for jobs created by artificial intelligence. Organizations are hiring externally instead.

That’s the finding in new research from [Careerminds](https://careerminds.com/?), a global provider of workforce solutions. The study, published in Careerminds’ white paper, [The AI Redeployment Gap: What Companies Lose by Hiring over Reskilling, reveals](https://careerminds.com/resources/redeployment-gap) that 92% of HR professionals and 88% of people managers believe employees within their organizations could be reskilled for AI-related roles.

**Also: [Nearly 70% of workers use AI regularly now – but many get no time to upskill](https://www.zdnet.com/innovation/workera-study-ai-skills-upskilling-2026/)**

More from ZDNET

Yet 51% of HR professionals and 47% of people managers report that their organizations are hiring externally for new AI-related positions, compared with just 42% in each group who report redeploying existing employees.

The research was conducted in partnership with [Censuswide](https://censuswide.com/) and surveyed 595 US HR professionals and 547 US people managers, ages 25–65, working at organizations with 500-10,000 or more employees. The research was conducted May 15–26, 2026.
