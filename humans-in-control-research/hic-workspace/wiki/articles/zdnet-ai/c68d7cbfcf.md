When is Labor Day?
This year, Labor Day falls on Monday, Sept. 7th. Many of the sales will kick off the week prior, with early sales starting now.
Are laptops really cheaper during Labor Day sales?
Yes, because Labor Day is the last chance for retailers to offload certain models before they are refreshed in fall lineups, and ahead of Black Friday/Cyber Monday sales, which kicks off the holiday shopping season.
