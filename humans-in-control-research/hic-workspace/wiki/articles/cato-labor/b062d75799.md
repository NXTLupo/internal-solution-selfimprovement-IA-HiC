From Red Tape to Real Choice: Reforming Childcare Policy

Childcare is expensive, and proposed policy solutions too often involve increased government spending without addressing the underlying costs. Alex J. Adams, assistant secretary for family support at the US Department of Health and Human Services, discusses the current state of childcare policy and his efforts to expand childcare choices for families, and economist Anna Claire Flowers discusses research on how reducing regulatory burdens can expand childcare options and lower costs for families.
