# Irish Furniture & Homewares Awards 2026 winners revealed

Source: [big_furniture_group](https://www.bigfurnituregroup.com/irish-furniture-homewares-awards-2026-winners-revealed/)

The Irish Furniture & Homewares Awards 2026 celebrated the very best of this year’s Irish Furniture & Homewares Show, recognising standout products, leading exhibitors and excellence in innovation, sustainability, presentation and sales.
Held on the evening of the show’s opening day, the awards brought exhibitors together after a busy first day of trading for an evening of recognition and celebration. Reflecting the quality, diversity and fresh thinking across this year’s exhibitor line-up, the awards showcased the strength of the products, brands and ideas on display.
Competition was strong across the categories, with Head Judge Amanda Bone assessing entries in the Global Product, European Product, Sustainability Initiative and Display & Exhibition Stand categories, alongside awards recognising supplier and sales excellence.
A Registered Architect and judge on RTÉ’s Home of the Year, Amanda brought extensive experience across architecture, interiors and residential design to the judging process. Her expert and discerning eye helped identify the entries that stood out for their design, quality, innovation and presentation, further reinforcing the significance of the Irish Furniture & Homewares Awards.
The awards provided a fitting close to the opening day, celebrating the achievements of this year’s winners while capturing the energy, ambition and innovation of the exhibiting community at the Irish Furniture & Homewares Show 2026.
Award Results
Best Global Product
Winner: Highgrove Beds – The Elements Collection
Highly Commended: Bluebone – Mammoth Dining Table
Shortlisted: Bluebone – Mammoth Dining Table, Highgrove Beds – The Elements Collection, Alcore Living – Sindhu
Best Sustainability Initiative
Winner: Cirtex
Highly Commended: Respa Beds
Shortlisted: Respa Beds, Bluebone, Cirtex
Best European Product
Winner: GlamMore – ECLIPSE
Highly Commended: Sealy – Select Medium Luxe
Shortlisted: GlamMore – ECLIPSE, Sealy – Select Medium Luxe, Adjust-a-Bed – Wallhugger Adjustable Base
Best Display & Exhibition Stand
Winner: Bluebone
Runner-Up: Welcome Furniture
Best Supplier
Winner: Respa Beds
Best Sales Representative
Winner: Conor Peacock from Faith & Ethan
The awards reflected the quality, creativity and ambition seen throughout the IFHS 2026 show floor. Highgrove Beds took home Best Global Product for The Elements Collection, while Cirtex was awarded Best Sustainability Initiative. GlamMore secured Best European Product with ECLIPSE, and Bluebone was recognised for Best Display & Exhibition Stand, with Welcome Furniture named runner-up.
Respa Beds was named Best Supplier, while Conor Peacock from Faith & Ethan received the award for Best Sales Representative. Highly Commended recognition also went to Bluebone for the Mammoth Dining Table, Respa Beds for its sustainability initiative, and Sealy for the Select Medium Luxe.
This year’s awards also provided an opportunity to recognise Lebus Upholstery, Bluebone and Signature Living for their long-standing support of the show, marking 10 years of exhibiting at IFHS. Their continued involvement reflects the strong relationships that have helped shape the event over the past decade.
The awards brought the opening day of IFHS 2026 to a fitting close, celebrating not only this year’s winners but also the commitment, creativity and continued support of the wider exhibiting community.
With the Irish Furniture & Homewares Show 2026 continuing over the coming days, trade visitors have the opportunity meet exhibitors, discover the latest furniture and homewares collections, and see many of this year’s award-winning products and stands for themselves.
