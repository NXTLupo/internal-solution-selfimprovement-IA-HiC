# RetailSystem launches Version 22.0 – built for the future of furniture retail

Source: [big_furniture_group](https://www.bigfurnituregroup.com/retailsystem-launches-version-22-0-built-for-the-future-of-furniture-retail/)

RetailSystem has announced the launch of Version 22.0, marking one of the most significant visual upgrades to the industry-leading POS and ERP platform in recent years.
Version 22.0, officially launched on 24 August 2026, introduces a completely refreshed, state-of-the-art interface, giving RetailSystem a cleaner, more modern look while retaining the familiar functionality, features and workflows its users rely on every day.
More importantly, the new format provides the foundation for the next generation of RetailSystem development, with a strong focus on AI-driven reporting, analytics and business intelligence.
David Hewitt, CEO of RetailSystem, commented: “This is a big step forward for RetailSystem. We wanted to modernise the entire experience without disrupting the processes our clients already know and love.
“Version 22.0 gives us the platform we need to introduce some incredibly exciting developments around AI, reporting and analytics in the future. It features a new look with the same power and is built for what comes next.”
