# Plumbs unveils groundbreaking furniture visualiser

Source: [big_furniture_group](https://www.bigfurnituregroup.com/plumbs-unveils-groundbreaking-furniture-visualiser/)

After more than 60 years of helping customers extend the life of the furniture they cherish, Plumbs has announced the launch of its most ambitious innovation to date: the Plumbs Furniture Visualiser.
Developed in partnership with The Drawing Room Creative, the new technology enables homeowners to upload a photograph of their existing furniture and instantly see it transformed in any one of Plumbs’ collection of more than 4,500 premium fabrics.
For decades, choosing new upholstery or furniture covers has relied on imagination. Today, that changes.
The visualiser bridges the gap between inspiration and reality, giving customers the confidence to explore colours, textures and styles in their own home before committing to a purchase.
Whether it’s a much-loved family sofa, an heirloom armchair or a complete living room refresh, customers can now visualise the transformation in minutes, save up to five design options and share them with family before booking a personalised in-home consultation with one of Plumbs’ expert advisers.
This launch represents one of the biggest technological investments in Plumbs’ 60-year history. The visualiser demonstrates how a heritage British brand is embracing new technology to enhance, rather than replace, its traditional service. By helping customers visualise their furniture before ordering, the experience removes uncertainty from one of the biggest decisions in the buying journey.
At a time when consumers are increasingly looking for sustainable alternatives to replacing furniture, the visualiser empowers homeowners to make informed decisions about restoring, reinventing and extending the life of existing pieces rather than sending them to landfill.
The launch reflects Plumbs’ continued commitment to combining traditional British craftsmanship with modern innovation, proving that heritage businesses can lead the way in creating digital experiences that genuinely improve the customer journey.
Sarah Page, Managing Director, said: “For over six decades we’ve been transforming furniture by hand. Today we’re transforming the customer experience too. This innovation gives people the confidence to see what’s possible before they make a decision, making it easier than ever to fall back in love with the furniture they already own. It’s a defining moment for Plumbs and one we’re incredibly proud to share.”
