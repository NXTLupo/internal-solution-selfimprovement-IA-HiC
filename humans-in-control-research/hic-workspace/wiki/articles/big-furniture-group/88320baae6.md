# Refined Design. Premium Comfort.

Source: [big_furniture_group](https://www.bigfurnituregroup.com/refined-design-premium-comfort/)

Beehive Furniture Wholesalers introduces two upholstery collections designed for customers seeking contemporary styling with premium comfort.
The Arizona Corner Sofa and Richmond 3+2+1 Recliner Suite each offer a distinct look while sharing the same focus on quality materials, refined detailing, and lasting comfort.
ARIZONA – CONTEMPORARY LUXURY IN A CORNER DESIGN
The Arizona corner sofa has been designed to create an impressive focal point without compromising on comfort. Its sweeping corner layout offers generous seating, making it equally suited to entertaining or everyday family living.
The high-back design provides excellent support, while the sculpted seat cushions and clean upholstery lines create a sophisticated appearance from every angle. Finished in a rich tan upholstery, Arizona combines warm contemporary tones with understated styling that complements both modern and classic interiors.
Rather than relying on unnecessary detailing, the design focuses on proportion, comfort, and craftsmanship, delivering a premium corner sofa with broad customer appeal.
Key Features
* Spacious corner configuration
* Supportive high-back seating
* Contemporary sculpted cushions
* Premium upholstery finish
* Clean, architectural styling
RICHMOND – MODERN RECLINING, REFINED FINISH
The Richmond collection brings together contemporary design and everyday comfort in a complete 3 seater, 2 seater and armchair suite.
Each piece features smooth electric reclining, allowing users to adjust their seating position effortlessly. Slim arms, raised metal-effect legs and precisely tailored upholstery create a lighter, more modern profile than traditional recliner furniture, making Richmond equally suited to contemporary homes and premium retail showrooms.
The structured back cushions provide excellent support, while the generous seat depth ensures lasting comfort without sacrificing the clean aesthetic of the collection.
Available as a coordinated suite, Richmond offers retailers a versatile range that appeals to customers looking for both comfort and a more design-led recliner.
Key Features
* Electric recliner functionality
* Available as a 3+2+1 suite
* Contemporary raised leg design
* Supportive high-back cushions
* Clean, tailored upholstery finish
