# Ag in Motion’s show director shares his top five picks for 2026

Source: [agr_western_producer](https://www.producer.com/saskatchewan/ag-in-motion-2026-top-five-picks/)

Ag in Motion’s show director shares his top five picks for 2026
From Ride & Drive to drone demos, here’s what to put on your show-day list for July 21 to 23 in Langham
13 hours ago
3 min read
Rob O’Connor has been show director since Ag in Motion opened in 2015, and he’s seen it all. To help you get the most out of the show, here’s his take on five things you will want to check out at Ag in Motion in 2026.
“Ag in Motion has grown into the largest agriculture trade show in Canada,” says O’Connor. “It’s widely recognized as Canada’s national farm show because Ag in Motion annually attracts dozens of product launches ranging from air seeding systems to new seed varieties. For farmers wanting to see agriculture’s future, this event is the first place they should come.”
Bigger and better equipment
Get inside the cab and take the latest farming equipment for a spin. This year’s Ride & Drive lets farmers test-drive sprayers, grain trucks and material handling equipment such as skid steers and small tractors. Move dirt, rocks and bales or test out acceleration on a sprayer. The Ride & Drive area lets you perform a wide variety of tasks from the comfort of the cab.
Drones are coming … are you ready?
Drone companies will be at the show to feature their agile machines to give you ideas about the many uses for drones on the farm. With drone spraying regulations in the news, here is your chance to see new developments in the field. Swing by the southeast corner along 7th Street.

Crop Management
Portable PathoScan device diagnoses crop diseases in the field
The plots thicken with growing agronomy onsite
See crop plots in their prime throughout the Ag in Motion site with dozens of different seed varieties and management practices. These crops demonstrate performance under real-world conditions; this is your chance to investigate your options up close.
AgTech innovations galore
Plan to visit the Conexus Credit Union booth along 5th Street and the AgTech Breakfast to find out about new innovations coming to the marketplace, or ideas that are still incubating. Fresh ideas for livestock and crop producers are all part of the Innovations Program at Ag in Motion, where you see what is new first!
Water management matters
We have water-themed exhibitors highlighting water management products and demonstrations. Get tons of ideas around irrigation, water management and drainage solutions for every farm operation. See it at 7th Street on BC Drive.
