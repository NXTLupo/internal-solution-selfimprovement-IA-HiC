What We Do
						The Executive Leadership Council is an independent non-profit founded in 1986 that opens channels of opportunity for the development of global executives to positively impact business and our communities. 					
					
				Membership
										The ELC is the preeminent member organization for the development of global business leaders. ELC members have access to a powerful network of business professionals around the globe and benefit from formal and informal networking and executive development opportunities.									
									
										Learn about Membership Criteria
										
									
								The Institute
										The ELC’s Institute for Leadership Development & Research seeks to strengthen the talent pipeline of executive leaders. It provides top-rate leadership programs that focus on individual growth at different career levels along with well-researched and documented understanding of the corporate experiences of managers and executives.
