# Exploring Feasible Design Spaces for Heterogeneous Constraints

Source: [arxiv_cs_ro](https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.01117v2)

We demonstrate an approach of exploring design spaces to simultaneously satisfy kinematics- and physics-based requirements. We present a classification of constraints and solvers to enable postponing optimization as far down the design workflow as possible. The solvers are organized into two broad classes of design space 'pruning' and 'exploration' by considering the types of constraints they can satisfy.
