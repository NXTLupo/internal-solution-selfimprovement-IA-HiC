In this report, we present our submission to the GOOSE 2D Fine-Grained Semantic Segmentation Challenge, organized as part of the Workshop on Field Robotics at ICRA 2026. The challenge combines data from the GOOSE and GOOSE-Ex datasets, which comprise more than 13k images captured from 4 distinct camera setups, annotated using a hierarchical taxonomy of 56 fine-grained classes and 11 broader categories.
