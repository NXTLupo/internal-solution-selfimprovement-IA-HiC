# Semantic Map Sharing and Capability-Aware Coverage Planning for AI-Native 6G Robotic Coordination

Source: [arxiv_cs_ro](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37666v1)

Search and Rescue (SAR) operations increasingly deploy heterogeneous teams of aerial and ground robots. However, conventional coverage methods typically do not translate perceived terrain into platform-specific reachability, while continuous image exchange imposes a high communication cost. We propose an edge-centric, semantic-aware coverage planning framework that integrates aerial terrain perception, robot-specific traversability reasoning, and payload-efficient semantic state sharing.
