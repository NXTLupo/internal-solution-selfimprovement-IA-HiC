# SPiralRoll: A Novel Adjustable-Stiffness Underactuated 3-DoF Joint with Torsion Springs for Rolling Robots

Source: [arxiv_cs_ro](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22443v1)

Compliant mechanisms are important in robotics because they can improve adaptability, safety, and energy efficiency while reducing hardware complexity. This paper presents SPiralRoll, a novel torsion-spring-based underactuated compliant mechanism for rolling robots and compliant robotic actuation.
