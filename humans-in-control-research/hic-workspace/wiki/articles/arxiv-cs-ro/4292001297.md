# Automated Generation of MDPs Using Logic Programming and LLMs for Robotic Applications

Source: [arxiv_cs_ro](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.23143v1)

We present a novel framework that integrates Large Language Models (LLMs) with automated planning and formal verification to streamline the creation and use of Markov Decision Processes (MDP). Our system leverages LLMs to extract structured knowledge in the form of a Prolog knowledge base from natural language (NL) descriptions.
