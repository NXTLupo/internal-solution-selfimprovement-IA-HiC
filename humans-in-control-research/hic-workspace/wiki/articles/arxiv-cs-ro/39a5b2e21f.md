# Concept: Dynamic Risk Assessment for AI-Controlled Robotic Systems

Source: [arxiv_cs_ro](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.14147v1)

AI-controlled robotic systems pose a risk to human workers and the environment. Classical risk assessment methods cannot adequately describe such black box systems. Therefore, new methods for a dynamic risk assessment of such AI-controlled systems are required. In this paper, we introduce the concept of a new dynamic risk assessment approach for AI-controlled robotic systems.
