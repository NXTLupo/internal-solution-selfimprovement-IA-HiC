Source: [arxiv_cs_ro](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.10755v3)

We introduce Infinigen-Articulated, a toolkit for generating realistic, procedurally generated articulated assets for robotics simulation. We include procedural generators for 18 common articulated object categories along with high-level utilities for use creating custom articulated assets in Blender.
