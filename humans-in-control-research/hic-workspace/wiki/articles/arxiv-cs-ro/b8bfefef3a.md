# Designing for Error Recovery in Human-Robot Interaction

Source: [arxiv_cs_ro](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.12473v1)

This position paper looks briefly at the way we attempt to program robotic AI systems. Many AI systems are based on the idea of trying to improve the performance of one individual system to beyond so-called human baselines. However, these systems often look at one shot and one-way decisions, whereas the real world is more continuous and interactive.
