Data scarcity is a fundamental challenge in developing AI and foundation models for agricultural robots. Existing open-source data platforms do not provide sufficient incentives for data providers so long-term data collection remains difficult. Furthermore, advances in generative AI have introduced a new challenge of verifying that collected data genuinely originates from real farm environments.
