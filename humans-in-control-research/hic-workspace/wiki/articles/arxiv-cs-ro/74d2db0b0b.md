# Leveraging the Template and Anchor Framework for Safe, Online Robotic Gait Design

Source: [arxiv_cs_ro](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.11125v1)

Online control design using a high-fidelity, full-order model for a bipedal robot can be challenging due to the size of the state space of the model. A commonly adopted solution to overcome this challenge is to approximate the full-order model (anchor) with a simplified, reduced-order model (template), while performing control synthesis.
