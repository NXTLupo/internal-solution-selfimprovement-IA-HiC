# Enabling the Deployment of Any-Scale Robotic Applications in Microservice Architectures through Automated Containerization

Source: [arxiv_cs_ro](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.06611v3)

In an increasingly automated world -- from warehouse robots to self-driving cars -- streamlining the development and deployment process and operations of robotic applications becomes ever more important. Automated DevOps processes and microservice architectures have already proven successful in other domains such as large-scale customer-oriented web services (e.g., Netflix). We recommend to employ similar microservice architectures for the deployment of small- to large-scale robotic applications
