# ExploreGen: Large Language Models for Envisioning the Uses and Risks of AI Technologies

Source: [arxiv_cs_hc](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.12454v1)

Responsible AI design is increasingly seen as an imperative by both AI developers and AI compliance experts. One of the key tasks is envisioning AI technology uses and risks. Recent studies on the model and data cards reveal that AI practitioners struggle with this task due to its inherently challenging nature. Here, we demonstrate that leveraging a Large Language Model (LLM) can support AI practitioners in this task by enabling reflexivity, brainstorming, and deliberation, especially in the ear
