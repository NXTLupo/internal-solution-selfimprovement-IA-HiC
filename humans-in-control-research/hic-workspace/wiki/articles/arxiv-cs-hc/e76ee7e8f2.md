# Generative AI Technologies, Techniques & Tensions: A Primer

Source: [arxiv_cs_hc](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.17497v1)

Generative AI systems have entered everyday academic, professional, and personal life with remarkable speed, yet most users encounter them as mysterious artifacts rather than intelligible systems. This chapter discusses large language models within a broader historical shift in computing paradigms and argues that many of the confusions surrounding their use arise from a mismatch between how these systems are built, how they behave, and how people expect computers to behave writ large.
