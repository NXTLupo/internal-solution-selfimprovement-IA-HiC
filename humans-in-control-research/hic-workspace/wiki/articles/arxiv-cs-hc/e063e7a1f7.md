Source: [arxiv_cs_hc](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.01126v1)

A methodology is proposed for design of templates of text communication messages that are based on best practices of experts in software marketing, ideas of marketing, communication theory, copywriting, media linguistics, semiotics. Description of the subject area is based on conceptual modeling and production systems. For the purposes of testing, the methodology was used as the basis of a software product.
