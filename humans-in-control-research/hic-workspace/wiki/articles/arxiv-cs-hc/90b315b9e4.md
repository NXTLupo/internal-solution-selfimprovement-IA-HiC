# AI prediction leads people to forgo guaranteed rewards

Source: [arxiv_cs_hc](articles/arxiv-cs-hc/8b5668e4ce.md)

Artificial intelligence (AI) is understood to affect the content of people's decisions. Here, using a behavioral implementation of the classic Newcomb's paradox in 1,305 participants, we show that AI can also change how people decide. In this paradigm, belief in predictive authority can lead individuals to constrain decision-making, forgoing a guaranteed reward. Over 40% of participants treated AI as such a predictive authority.
