# A short review and primer on using video for psychophysiological observations in human-computer interaction applications

Source: [arxiv_cs_hc](articles/arxiv-cs-hc/b70f2fc1a8.md)

The application of psychophysiological measures in human-computer interaction is a growing field with significant potential for future smart personalised systems. Working in this emerging field requires comprehension of an array of physiological signals and analysis techniques. An important aspect in measuring psychophysiological variables in real-world settings is the invasiveness of the measurement setup. Video is a signal which can be captured from a distance without interrupting the subject.
