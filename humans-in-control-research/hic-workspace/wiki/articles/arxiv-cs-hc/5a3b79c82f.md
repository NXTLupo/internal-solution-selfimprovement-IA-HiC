Image-generation AI systems increasingly support creative work by producing multiple design variations for users to evaluate and select. In such human-AI co-creation workflows, selection becomes a critical stage where human judgment guides AI-generated possibilities toward final outcomes. While presenting multiple alternatives is intended to encourage exploration, the simultaneous multi-option presentation may introduce systematic biases in human decision making.
