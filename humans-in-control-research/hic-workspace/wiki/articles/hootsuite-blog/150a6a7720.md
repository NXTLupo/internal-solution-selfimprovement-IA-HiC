# Best AI content creation tools for 2026: Tested picks

Source: [hootsuite_blog](https://blog.hootsuite.com/ai-content-creation-tools/)

Table of Contents
Key takeaways
- AI content creation tools help teams produce more across text, image, video, and audio without adding headcount or budget in proportion.
- The strongest 2026 tools go beyond basic generation. They offer brand voice controls, workflow integrations, and governance features that enterprise teams need.
- No single AI tool covers every content need. Most teams combine specialized tools, or work inside a connected system like Hootsuite Social OS, across the full workflow.
- AI speeds up drafting and production, but strategy, fact-checking, and brand judgment stay with people.
What are AI content creation tools?
AI content creation tools use artificial intelligence to generate written, visual, audio, or video content based on prompts, helping teams create content faster and at scale.
The category has widened considerably — generative AI is now a projected $394.66 billion market, and AI-driven content is among the defining social media trends for 2026. Early tools mostly wrote copy. In 2026, most are multi-modal and workflow-aware, meaning they draft, design, edit video, and plug into the systems where the work actually happens.
You will generally find them in five buckets: writing and copy, image generation, video and audio, presentation and design, and workflow platforms that connect the rest. Common examples include ChatGPT, Midjourney, Jasper, Canva, and Hootsuite’s AI writing tools, now part of Perch inside Hootsuite Social OS.

What are the benefits of using AI content creation tools?
AI content creation tools help marketers create, optimize, and scale content with less manual effort. They can assist with brainstorming, drafting, and managing everyday content tasks.
Here are four benefits worth building a workflow around.
Save time and boost productivity
The clearest win is time back. With 51% of content professionals using AI to accelerate production, it can take over smaller, more menial tasks, helping your team save time and focus on the bigger picture. Some use cases include:
- Proofreading: Spelling and grammar
- Batch content creation: Like generating product descriptions or summaries
- Research: Like summarizing reports or analyzing vast amounts of data
- Captioning: And other accessibility needs, like voiceovers
- Basic graphic design: Help
Chelse Hensley, Social Media Strategist at graphic design software Visme (a tool with its own AI-powered features), uses AI to streamline her workflow.
“I use AI to help me batch tweets from my company’s blog articles,” Hensley shares. “For a volume-based platform like X/Twitter or Threads, it’s extremely helpful to repurpose already-written (and approved by our team!) content into short-form tweets and threads.”
Formatting is another easy win. “I can input my existing content or ideas, and ask AI to formulate it into a bulleted list, or even a cleanly formatted table, which on my own might take hours (especially if you’re Excel challenged like me),” Hensley says. “Formatting is a great way for a SMM to leverage AI.”
Generate ideas and overcome creative blocks
Even the best brainstormers hit a wall. AI can jumpstart the process so your team doesn’t slow down.
You still bring the strategy and creativity. AI gives you a starting point, generating prompts, angles, or post ideas you can mold or build on. Sometimes that first batch of suggestions is all it takes to spark something you would not have landed on otherwise.
Scale content across platforms, formats, and regions
AI helps teams produce more content without dramatically increasing workload. Instead of starting from scratch every time, marketers can repurpose existing content into multiple formats. A single blog post can quickly become an X thread or a video script for Snapchat.
For enterprise teams, the bigger unlock is reach across markets. AI tools can translate content in minutes, making it easier to adapt campaigns for different languages and regions without a separate production cycle for each one.
Smaller teams feel it just as much. When asked how AI helps her do more as a team of one, Hensley says, “THIS is the right question. As so many social media managers are a one-person band, having AI to help draft and repurpose content is crucial.”
Hensley continues, “With the help of AI, I’m able to take tedious, time-consuming tasks and turn them into fast solutions.”
Optimize for social SEO and discoverability
Just like Google and other search engines, social media platforms come with their own SEO rules. If you are still learning how to optimize content across platforms, AI tools can help.
They can suggest keywords, hashtags, and caption structures that improve how social algorithms surface your content in feeds and search results. The result is posts that are easier for the right people to find.
What are the best AI content creation tools for 2026?
The best AI tools for content creation help social media marketers brainstorm ideas, generate copy, design visuals, and edit video in minutes. We have grouped them by category so you can jump to the one that matches your gap.
Best AI writing and copy tools
These tools handle drafting, editing, repurposing, and brand voice. They are the workhorses of most content workflows.
1. Hootsuite Social OS (Perch + Wisdom)
Hootsuite’s AI writing capabilities now live inside Perch, the content, planning, and publishing app in Hootsuite Social OS. You enter a prompt, and it turns that into social captions, post ideas, and content variations you can schedule without leaving the platform.
What separates it from a general chatbot is context. The AI is trained on Hootsuite’s social media playbook, which means it combines a strong language model with content formulas drawn from more than a decade of social marketing experience. Wisdom, the AI orchestration layer across Social OS, ties creation to the rest of your workflow so drafting, approvals, and scheduling stay connected.
Key features:
- Write a new social media caption in a specific tone of voice
- Write a post based on a link (i.e., a blog post or a product page)
- Generate post ideas based on a keyword or topic (and then write posts expanding on the idea you like best)
- Identify and repurpose your top-performing posts
- Create relevant captions for upcoming holidays
Pricing: Standard is $99/user/month, Advanced is $249/user/month, and Enterprise is custom priced. A free trial is available.
Best for: Social teams that want AI content creation connected to planning, publishing, analytics, and approvals in one place.
Limitations: Built for social content specifically, so it is not the tool for long-form blog drafting or technical documentation.
2. ChatGPT
ChatGPT is a conversational AI assistant that can produce human-style content, including social posts, blog articles, outlines, and scripts. Recent models also generate images and support custom GPTs, so teams can build a version primed with their own brand context.
Source: ChatGPT
It works best as a collaborator. ChatGPT can generate ideas and drafts quickly, but it still benefits from human editing, fact-checking, and strategic direction. Creativity, nuance, and brand voice need a human touch.
Key features:
- Idea generation and brainstorming for social posts, ad copy, and more
- Blog outlines and first drafts
- Custom GPTs loaded with your brand guidelines
- Image generation from text prompts
Pricing: Free tier available. Plus is $20/month, Business (formerly Team) is $20/user/month billed annually ($25/user/month billed monthly, 2-seat minimum).
Best for: General-purpose drafting, brainstorming, and research support.
Limitations: Full drafts from scratch tend to read generic, and factual or historical details always need verification against primary sources.
3. Claude
Claude is an AI assistant developed by Anthropic that helps users generate ideas and create content via conversational prompts. It runs on a system called Constitutional AI, which is designed to prioritize safer, more reliable responses.
According to its website, Claude can “process large amounts of information, brainstorm ideas, generate text and code, help you understand subjects, coach you through difficult situations, [and] help simplify your busywork.”
Source: Claude
Key features:
- Generating code snippets for your website
- Coming up with new high-quality content ideas
- Transcribing videos or podcast episodes
- Working through long documents and complex information
- Connecting to external tools, including Hootsuite, through MCP connectors
Pricing: Free tier available. Pro is $20/month, Team is $25/user/month billed annually.
Best for: Working with long documents, research synthesis, and technical content.
Limitations: Not built for end-to-end content production, and summaries still need a human read before anything ships.
4. Jasper
A popular ChatGPT alternative, Jasper specializes in content for articles, social media, scripts, and more. It has also expanded into AI image generation.
What sets Jasper apart is its focus on tone. The platform can generate or rewrite content that matches your brand voice, which makes it a common pick for teams producing high volumes across multiple writers.
