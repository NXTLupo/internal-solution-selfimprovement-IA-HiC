# 13 best social media scheduling tools for 2026

Source: [hootsuite_blog](https://blog.hootsuite.com/social-media-scheduling-tools/)

Table of Contents
Key takeaways
- Social media scheduling tools let you plan, create, and publish posts across every platform from one dashboard, keeping your content consistent without manual posting.
- When choosing a tool, prioritize multi-platform publishing, approval workflows, analytics, and AI-assisted content creation that match your team’s size and structure.
- AI is reshaping scheduling in 2026. Tools like Perch by Hootsuite use AI to recommend posting times, draft content, and connect publishing to performance data.
- Free native schedulers like Meta Business Suite handle the basics, but teams managing multiple platforms or brands need a third-party tool for cross-channel control and reporting.
Social media scheduling tools are software platforms that help you plan and automate your social media posts.
Instead of posting in real-time, these tools let you create and schedule content for automatic publication at the optimal time. They save time and help you stay consistent with your social media strategy.
Most social media platforms have some basic native scheduling and auto-publishing options. However, third-party social media scheduling software provides greater flexibility, functionality, and the ability to schedule content for multiple social media channels from one dashboard. Many of these platforms double as full social media management tools, adding analytics, inbox, and collaboration features on top of publishing.
Almost anyone posting to more than one social account regularly will get value from a scheduler, but what you need from it changes a lot depending on how you work. Here’s how the main groups break down:
- Solo creators: Need affordable, simple scheduling with a visual planner so they can batch content between other projects.
- Small businesses and freelancers: Need to stay visible on a few key channels without hiring help, so content recycling and easy onboarding matter most.
- In-house social teams: Need a shared content calendar, drafts, and approvals so two people never post the same thing twice.
- Mid-market marketing teams: Need scheduling tied to analytics and reporting so publishing decisions connect to performance.
- Enterprise and multi-brand organizations: Need governed workflows, role-based permissions, multi-region support, and audit trails alongside bulk publishing at scale.
If you fall into the last two groups, a single-purpose scheduler will likely run out of room fast.
The best scheduling tools do more than push posts out on a timer. Use this list as your evaluation checklist:
- Multi-platform publishing: Customize posts and cross-post to multiple social media accounts, all on one screen.
- Visual content calendar: Plan, curate, review, and edit an integrated schedule of social content across platforms and campaigns.
- Optimal timing recommendations: Make sure you post at the best time based on your own audience data, not generic industry averages.
- AI-powered content creation: Turn prompts, briefs, or existing assets into draft posts you can review and edit before scheduling.
- Approval workflows and permissions: Set custom permissions and review steps so nothing goes live before the right person signs off.
- Bulk scheduling: Upload and queue large batches of content at once instead of building posts one by one.
- Analytics and reporting: Tie published posts back to reach, engagement, and conversions in the same place you schedule them.
- Asset management: Store, organize, and reuse approved creative from a central media library.
- Integrations: Connect the tools your team already uses, from design software to ecommerce platforms and internal chat.
- Reliable customer support: Troubleshoot issues quickly and keep your publishing workflow running.
Weigh these against your team size. A solo creator can skip approvals; an enterprise team cannot.

We’ve grouped the tools below by what they’re built for, so you can jump straight to the category that fits your team.
All-in-one social media scheduling tools
These platforms combine scheduling with analytics, engagement, collaboration, and reporting, so publishing isn’t a standalone task.
1. Perch by Hootsuite
Best for: Large teams and enterprises managing multiple social media accounts.
Why it stands out: Perch is a planning, publishing, and analytics powerhouse. It’s built for social teams that need more than basic scheduling — think content planning, cross-channel publishing, approvals, asset management, reporting, and AI-powered content creation in one connected workflow.
Perch helps teams move from idea to approved post faster. You can plan campaigns in a visual calendar, customize content for each network from one place, schedule posts at recommended times, and track what’s working once content goes live.
Perch also connects with Wisdom, Hootsuite’s social-first AI, to turn prompts into ready-to-review posts. Because Perch sits inside Hootsuite Social OS, publishing also connects to Vigil for brand safety and governance, so approvals and permissions travel with your content instead of living in a separate spreadsheet.
Key scheduling features:
- Auto-posting and bulk scheduling
- Custom posting time recommendations based on your unique social media analytics
- A drag-and-drop visual content calendar for planning across accounts and campaigns
- The ability to customize one post for multiple platforms from a single screen
- Built-in collaboration tools, approval workflows, and role-based permissions
- A central media library to store, organize, and reuse approved assets
- AI-assisted content creation with Wisdom
- Post-performance tracking, benchmarking, and reporting in one dashboard
- App integrations, including Canva, Notion, Slack, Bluesky, Mailchimp, RSS Autopublisher, and more
Perch offers powerful content curation tools, including a simple drag-and-drop visual calendar that makes content planning across all your accounts easy.
If you sell online, Perch also connects to ecommerce platforms through the Hootsuite app directory, including Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, and Magento, so product content can flow into your calendar.
Where it shines:
- Scheduling, approvals, asset management, and reporting live in one workflow
- Best-time recommendations are based on your own account data
- Scales to multiple brands, regions, and users without breaking governance
Where it falls short:
- More platform than a solo creator posting to one channel needs
Platforms supported: Instagram (Posts, Stories, and Reels), Facebook, X (Twitter), Pinterest, LinkedIn, YouTube, TikTok, and Threads
Pricing: The Standard plan is $99 per user per month, Advanced is $249 per user per month, and Enterprise is custom priced.
2. Agorapulse
Best for: Mid-market teams that want scheduling and community management together.
Why it stands out: Agorapulse pairs a shared publishing calendar with a unified inbox, so the same team that schedules posts also handles the replies those posts generate. Content can move through internal approvals before it publishes, and reports can be exported for clients or leadership.
It’s a common pick for agencies and mid-sized in-house teams who have outgrown a basic scheduler but don’t need enterprise-level governance.
Key scheduling features:
- Shared content calendar with drafts and internal approvals
- Queue categories for recurring content types
- Unified inbox for comments and DMs
- Exportable reporting per profile or client
Where it shines:
- Publishing and engagement live side by side
- Clean client reporting without extra tools
Where it falls short:
- Per-user pricing adds up quickly for larger teams
- Fewer AI content features than AI-first platforms
Platforms supported: Facebook, Instagram, X (Twitter), LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube, Google Business Profile
Pricing: Paid plans start at $79 per user per month billed annually ($99 per user per month billed monthly).
3. Planable
Best for: Teams that live and die by content reviews and approvals.
Why it stands out: Planable is built around feedback. Posts appear exactly as they will on each network, and stakeholders can comment, suggest edits, and approve in line rather than over email. Multi-step approval flows mean a post can pass through a copy editor, a client, and a legal reviewer before it publishes.
You can also switch between calendar, feed, grid, and list views depending on whether you’re planning a campaign or reviewing a single week.
Key scheduling features:
- Multi-step approval workflows
- In-line comments and suggested edits
- Calendar, feed, grid, and list planning views
- Platform-accurate post previews
Where it shines:
- Fastest path from draft to sign-off for approval-heavy teams
- Clients and stakeholders can review without a full seat
Where it falls short:
- Analytics are lighter than all-in-one platforms
- No social listening
Platforms supported: Facebook, Instagram, X (Twitter), LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube, Google Business Profile
Pricing: Paid plans start at $33 per workspace per month, with a limited free plan available (50 posts total).
Social media scheduling tools for small teams
These tools trade advanced governance and reporting for speed, simplicity, and a lower price tag.
4. Buffer
Best for: Straightforward social media scheduling.
Why it stands out: Buffer‘s dashboard is simple and straightforward, offering great ease of use for social media managers and small teams who just want to get in, schedule, and get out. It’s the kind of tool you can pick up in minutes, with virtually no learning curve.
It allows you to schedule posts to Instagram, Facebook, X, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube, Mastodon, Bluesky, Pinterest, Threads, and Google Business Profile. You can also use it to reply to comments on Facebook Pages and Instagram business accounts.
