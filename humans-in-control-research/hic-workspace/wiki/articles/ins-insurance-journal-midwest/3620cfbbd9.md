# Westfield Specialty Acquires DOXA’s Arris Property Underwriters Program

Source: [ins_insurance_journal_midwest](https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/midwest/2026/06/24/875174.htm)

Westfield Specialty announced the acquisition of the renewal rights for DOXA’s Arris Property Underwriters Program. The E&S commercial property program provided custom solutions for real estate, hospitality, healthcare, education, municipalities, and manufacturing through wholesale distribution.
The renewal rights deal provides Westfield Specialty with an opportunity to continue to grow and diversify their property book.
Westfield Specialty currently offers customized shared and layered property, as well as middle-market property insurance solutions on a wholesale basis. The Company continues to have year-over-year profitable growth with the most recent financials reporting gross written premium (GWP) of $559 million for the first quarter of 2026, an increase of 31% year over year, while delivering a Combined Ratio (CR) of 95.7%.
