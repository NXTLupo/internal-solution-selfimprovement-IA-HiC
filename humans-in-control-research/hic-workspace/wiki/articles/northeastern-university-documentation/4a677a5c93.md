# Master your Northeastern email: 5 Outlook tips every student needs – IT Services

Source: [Northeastern University documentation](https://its.northeastern.edu/2025/09/09/master-your-northeastern-email-5-outlook-tips-every-student-needs/)

Your @northeastern.edu email is more than just an inbox—it’s your command center for academic success. Here are five game-changing Outlook features that will save you time and keep you organized.
1. Tame your inbox chaos
Focused Inbox automatically sorts important emails (like those from professors) from less urgent ones. Find it under View > Show Focused Inbox. You can train it by moving emails between tabs.
Quick tip: Create folders for each class (e.g., “ENGW 3302” or “CS 2500”) and set up rules to auto-sort emails. Learn how to create rules.
2. Never miss another deadline
Add all your assignment due dates and exams to Outlook Calendar. Here’s the secret:
- Color-code by class
- Set custom reminder times (example: 1 week for projects, 30 minutes for classes)
- Sync to your phone for notifications on the go
Pro move: Create categories for different deadline types—exams, papers, group projects—so you can see your workload at a glance. Learn how to use color categories.
3. Schedule group meetings without the back-and-forth
Use Scheduling Assistant to find times when everyone’s free:
- Create new meeting
- Select “Scheduling Assistant”
- Add your teammates
- Pick from the available time slots
Works perfectly for study groups and project teams.
4. Access everything on your phone
Download the Outlook mobile app to:
- Check emails between classes
- Get calendar alerts for assignments
- Swipe to organize emails quickly
- Work offline (perfect for the T or the subway)
5. Level up with AI features
Outlook’s AI can:
- Suggest quick replies to save time
- Highlight your most important emails
- Help schedule meetings automatically
Plus, use Delay Send to write emails at 2 a.m. but send them at reasonable hours. Your professors will appreciate it.
Want to dive deeper? Check out this ITS blog post on AI in Outlook for more advanced tips.
Bonus: Stay safe from phishing
No matter how strong the university’s email security features, scammers love targeting student emails. Before clicking any link or attachment:
- Check if the sender’s email looks suspicious (like “n0rtheastern.edu” instead of “northeastern.edu”)
- Hover over links to see where they really go
- If an email seems off, report it! Forward suspicious messages to phish@northeastern.edu
Visit the Phish Bowl to see real phishing attempts caught by other students.
Quick setup checklist
- Add your profile photo
- Create an email signature with your name and graduation year
- Set up Focused Inbox
- Download the mobile app
- Create class folders
- Import your course schedule to calendar
