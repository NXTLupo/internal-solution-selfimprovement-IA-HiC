# Build your AI foundations this February with ITS

Source: [Northeastern University documentation](https://its.northeastern.edu/2026/01/23/build-your-ai-foundations-this-february-with-its/)

Ready to build your tech skills and explore AI tools in 2026? Northeastern IT Services kicks off the new year with a comprehensive training lineup designed to boost your productivity and keep you ahead of the curve.
The early spring term programming highlighted below focuses on building confidence and competence with AI tools.
February: AI foundations
Microsoft’s AI integration takes center stage on February 4 with Introduction to Copilot, a session exploring how this AI companion enhances productivity across Microsoft 365 applications. Attendees will see practical demonstrations of Copilot’s ability to draft emails, summarize documents, analyze data in Excel, and streamline everyday tasks within the tools the Northeastern community already uses.
For those ready to elevate their prompt-writing skills, From Basic to Brilliant: Building AI Prompts with CARPS on February 11 introduces a structured framework for transforming vague requests into focused, effective instructions. The CARPS method—covering Context, Action, Result, Parameters, and Style—helps users consistently achieve exceptional AI results across any platform.
The foundations series concludes on February 18 with Building AI Fluency Skills, a session designed for those ready to advance beyond basic AI interactions. This training covers advanced prompting techniques, strategies for providing effective feedback to refine AI outputs, and methods for tackling more complex, multi-step tasks with confidence.
Communities of practice
Connect with the Northeastern community through monthly collaborative sessions focused on innovation, problem-solving, and sharing best practices.
Digital Community of Practice – On January 29 and February 26, exchange ideas about emerging technologies, troubleshoot challenges, and explore innovative solutions.
Digital Accessibility Community of Practice – Join ITS on January 27 and February 24 to share best practices and build a network of accessibility champions across Northeastern.
Technology office hours
Need quick help or guidance? Drop in for open conversations throughout the semester with ITS’ digital solutions engineers, available to assist with any technology questions in a relaxed, informal setting. Sessions are conducted online.
Get started today
Registration is now open for all spring sessions. Visit training.its.northeastern.edu to register and explore all available trainings.
