# New Enhancements to the Student Hub Centralize Academic Needs and Expand Personal Growth – IT Services

Source: [Northeastern University documentation](https://its.northeastern.edu/2021/09/08/new-enhancements-to-the-student-hub-centralize-academic-needs-and-expand-personal-growth/)

It’s true. The Northeastern student journey begins at the Student Hub. Just about any aspect of what a student needs to begin, to continue, and to complete both the academic path and the personal journey is now part of the enhanced Student Hub. Some of these new features are listed below:
Central Resources
All links once found in the myNortheastern portal are now centralized in the Hub’s Resources page.
Me
Students can create a personal profile that highlights their interests. By adding tags in their profiles, each student will create a much more personalized Northeastern community experience.
Discover
Interested in finding other students who share common interests? Connect with others and build out a Northeastern network, or search for clubs and other student organizations to follow or join.
Events
Find out what’s going on in and around Northeastern using calendar.northeastern.edu.
COVID-19 Testing
Stay up to date on COVID-19 testing, vaccination, and related university policies through the Wellness Assistant chatbot.
Click for more information about the Student Hub or students can visit The Hub itself.
