Source: [copyblogger](https://copyblogger.com/content-marketing-courses/)

They all offer video courses on roughly the same topics and provide some action items.
However, the content marketing courses that are most effective at helping you accelerate your career and generate the most revenue come down to a few differences, like the instructor’s experience, feedback opportunities, and the recency of the advice (e.g., does it account for the ever-changing content marketing landscape and AI?).
In this post, I’ll share a content marketing course evaluation criteria to help you choose the best content marketing course for your needs.
Want to learn how to create content that generates real leads and sales for your business?

2.) HubSpot Content Marketing Course
HubSpot’s Content Marketing Certification Course is a free, 8.5-hour program designed to help marketers develop and execute a comprehensive content marketing strategy.
The course includes 14 lessons, 54 videos, and 11 quizzes and it offers a certification that you can add on LinkedIn.
Instructor Credibility
Most of the courses are taught by Justin Champion, HubSpot’s Global Head of Surround Sound Growth (SEO + AEO), so you’re getting tips from a real expert with experience.
They also have guests like Andy Crestodina (author of Content Chemistry and co-founder of Orbit Media) and Matthew Howells-Barby (Director of Acquisition at HubSpot), both of whom are genuine content marketing experts.
Course Content
The content formats covered include:
- Storytelling and brand positioning
- Blog strategy and SEO frameworks (topic clusters, pillar pages)
- Video marketing strategy
- Guest blogging and repurposing content
- Content promotion and performance measurement
These topics are useful for brands, but the course doesn’t include email marketing or personal branding, both of which are essential for startup founders, side hustlers, and consultants/coaches who want to generate money through content.
Additionally, there isn’t any content related to AI and how to adjust your content strategy to adapt to the modern content marketing landscape.
Community
The course is self-paced and pre-recorded, with no one-on-one feedback or instructor interaction.
You’ll go through the videos and quizzes independently, so there isn’t much support if you get stuck along the way or have trouble executing the material.
In fact, the biggest drawback is the lack of focus on execution.
There is also no community or group for support and networking.
Pricing
The course is completely free and offers a digital certification upon completion.
3.) Grow and Convert’s Customers From Content Course & Community
Grow and Convert’s Customers from Content course promises to help you create content that actually drives conversions.
Created and taught by Benji Hyam and Devesh Khanal, this program is based on the same system they’ve used to grow traffic and leads for over 40 B2B and B2C brands, including Leadfeeder, Patreon, CrazyEgg, and CognitiveFX.
They’ve built their brand around the promise that their leads will actually drive conversions rather than just traffic. And, they teach you how to track ROI as well.
Instructor Credibility
Benji Hyam and Devesh Khanal are founders of the Grow and Convert agency, which is known for driving sales rather than just traffic.
They have a well documented system for executing these results, and in this content marketing course, they share each of those systems.
Course Content
The course is broken into five modules:
- Strategy: How to identify high-converting keywords and build a content strategy for B2B, B2C, SaaS, or service businesses.
- Writing: How to write blog content that ranks and resonates, with line-by-line breakdowns of strong vs. weak articles.
- Promotion: Tactical guides for paid and organic promotion, including Facebook, Twitter, influencer outreach, and link building.
- Conversions: How to measure ROI from content, track attribution in Google Analytics, and optimize CTAs for leads.
- Hiring: How to scale operations by finding and managing great writers.
Each lesson includes real client case studies, and Devesh and Benji frequently jump into the comments section to answer questions and help.
The content is also continuously updated and evolves as Benji and Devesh improve their own systems and processes. This is a key differentiator because it means their content updates and evolves with the current marketing landscape.
Community
The course is built directly inside a private discussion forum and members can:
- Ask questions directly to Benji and Devesh and receive detailed written or video feedback.
- Share keyword strategies, outlines, and drafts for review.
- Join monthly live Zoom calls for coaching and strategy teardown sessions.
It feels more like joining an agency apprenticeship than a typical online course.
Pricing
The course offers lifetime access for a one-time payment of $699, or six monthly payments of $135. You then have ongoing access to the community, monthly calls, and all future course updates without any subscription.
