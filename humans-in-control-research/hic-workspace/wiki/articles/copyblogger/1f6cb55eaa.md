# 50 LinkedIn Post Templates Based on Influencer’s Top Performing Posts

Source: [copyblogger](https://copyblogger.com/linkedin-post-templates/)

Most LinkedIn post templates offer frameworks, like:
- What’s a failure story and what did you learn?
- What’s your blueprint for (specific process)?
- What are your top three tips for (specific pain point)?
These frameworks are still effective, but I still find them limiting for a few reasons:
- They aren’t the only frameworks that work – and they often cater to a professional (and somewhat generic) tone of voice.
- The success of the post still depends on the topic of content you place in that framework.
- Frameworks are great, but you still have to come up with your own creative hook.
To solve these problems, we studied the top performing posts of 50 LinkedIn influencers and broke down each of their posts into LinkedIn post templates.
I’ll also explain why I think the LinkedIn post performed so well (e.g., was there something unique about the topic selected?) and what you can learn about that topic.
Therefore, these post templates not only give you very specific hook and copywriting ideas, but they also hopefully inspire you to look beyond the most commonly mentioned LinkedIn post frameworks.
