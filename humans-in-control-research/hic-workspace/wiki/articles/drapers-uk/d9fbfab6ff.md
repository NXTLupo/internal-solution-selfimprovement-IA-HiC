# In pictures: John Lewis unveils new restaurant concept

Source: [drapers_uk](https://www.drapersonline.com/news/in-pictures-john-lewis-unveils-new-restaurant-concept)

Platter will replace John Lewis restaurants in 32 stores by the end of 2027, as part of its ongoing £800m store transformation programme.
One of the “biggest restaurants on Oxford Street”, the in-store restaurant concept features a made-on-site deli, open kitchen serving pizza, a dessert parlour, a bar and terrace and houses seating for 300 customers.
Oxford Street’s Platter will also include an in-store roastery, offering both in-store and take home coffee, as well as a rotating pop-up concept – the first of which in partnership with patisserie outlet The Eclectic Collection.
John Lewis said the new concept reflects “how differently people shop now”, with hospitality accounting for more than 20% of the retailer’s in-store transactions to date.
Hospitality sales online have also grown by nearly 10% in the past 12 months, John Lewis said.

John Lewis director of services and hospitality Katie Papakonstantinou said: “This is a real investment in something customers told us mattered. Sitting down for something to eat and drink has become part of how people shop and Platter is built for that.
“Platter serves our own coffee – with beans roasted right here in Oxford Street – alongside an open kitchen making pizza, deli and patisserie on site. Whether stopping for coffee, a pizza with family, a treat from the dessert parlour or a drink at the bar, we’ve created a space that makes a shopping trip even more special.”
Platter joins John Lewis’s broader hospitality offer at its Oxford Street flagship, which also includes Benugo, Ori Caffè, Jamie Oliver Cafe, Huffkins tea rooms, Kamakura and the 1864 Rooftop Bar & Kitchen.
