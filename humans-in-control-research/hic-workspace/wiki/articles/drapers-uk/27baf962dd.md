# Topshop and Cara Delevingne collaborate for capsule collection

Source: [drapers_uk](https://www.drapersonline.com/news/topshop-and-cara-delevingne-collaborate-for-capsule-collection)

Topshop has co-designed the 16-piece collection with model and long-time collaborator Cara Delevingne.
It features denim jeans, skirts, dresses, jackets, tailoring and co-ords alongside ribbed vests, open-back tops and long-sleeved tees, designed to be mixed and layered.
Prices range from £26 to £95 with a size range offering UK 6 to UK 18. Cara Delevingne does not appear in the accompanying campaign, which was shot in London by photographer Magnus Lecher and is fronted by Miah Linardi.
Asos acquired Topshop and Topman in 2021 after owner Arcadia Group fell into administration in 2020. Asos sold a majority stake in Topshop and Topman for £135m to Heartland in September 2024. Heartland represents the Holch Povlsen family and the Bestseller business – it now owns 75% of Topshop, with the remaining 25% belonging to Asos.
Topshop.com was relaunched as an independent website in August 2025. The new site went live with a curated edit by ambassador Delevingne and she sat front row during Topshop’s catwalk in London’s Trafalgar Square in August 2025. Delevingne first worked with Topshop as an ambassador in 2014.
Advertisement
Drapers exclusively revealed that Liberty was Topshop’s first physical retail stockist last August. The brand landed in select John Lewis stores before Christmas and continues to be stocked at the retailer. International Topshop stockists include Irish department stores McElhinneys and Shaws, France’s Printemps and Denmark’s Magasin du Nord.
Cara Delevingne said: “Topshop has been part of my style story for as long as I can remember, so co-designing this collection felt really personal. I’ve always loved the way the brand captures London style that feels distinctive yet effortless. These pieces are a celebration of that feeling in a way that feels true to me.”
Michelle Wilson, managing director of Topshop said: “Cara has always been synonymous with the confidence and creativity at the heart of Topshop. As a global style icon with deep London roots, she brings a distinctive point of view that resonates with our community around the world.”
The collection will be available at Topshop.com and at Asos.com from 25 August.
