# Skims names Supreme exec as its first chief design officer

Source: [drapers_uk](https://www.drapersonline.com/news/skims-names-supreme-exec-as-its-first-chief-design-officer)

Magee served as chief creative officer at Supreme for more than 22 years, according to her LinkedIn profile, joining in August 2004 when the brand was 10 years old.
She oversaw design, product development and product strategy across clothing, accessories, collaborations and special projects.
At Skims, Magee will lead design and creative product direction as the brand continues to expand its global footprint across categories and markets.
Last week it announced it will open its second standalone UK location at Westfield London early next year.
Kim Kardashian, chief creative cfficer and co-founder of Skims, said: “I’ve admired Erin’s work for years – the way she approaches design with such a strong point of view while staying completely connected to her audience is something I deeply respect.

“She understands what it means to build something that truly resonates and I’m so excited to welcome her to Skims.”
Jens Grede, CEO and co-founder of Skims, added: “Erin brings an unparalleled creative vision and a proven ability to build brands that transcend fashion and become cultural touchstones.
“Her eye for design, her deep understanding of product and her instinct for what resonates make her exactly the right person to lead Skims into its next era. We’re incredibly excited to welcome her to the team.”
Magee said: “ Skims has done something rare: built a brand that is both deeply personal and universal. The commitment to design, innovation and solutions for every body is genuinely inspiring, and I’m excited to help shape what comes next.
“I look forward to working alongside this talented team to push the creative boundaries of the brand and create products that people truly connect with.”
