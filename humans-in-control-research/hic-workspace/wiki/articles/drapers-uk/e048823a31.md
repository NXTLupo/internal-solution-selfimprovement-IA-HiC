# Frasers calls for Accent Group chair to resign

Source: [drapers_uk](https://www.drapersonline.com/companies/directory/frasers-group/frasers-calls-for-accent-group-chair-to-resign)

Frasers Group‘s Wootton issued a letter to Myers calling for him to resign following Accent Group’s FY26 trading update last week (21 August).
The letter reads: “In my view, your staggering ‘head in the sand’ approach to capital allocation and the company’s recent underwhelming FY26 results has proven beyond doubt that your position as chairman cannot rightly continue.
“You continue to ask shareholders to place their faith in the board-approved 2030 strategic growth plan, which your own management team provided little conviction over in last week’s earnings call. Accent and its shareholders deserve better.”
Wootton continued by stating: “In my opinion, you have failed to steer the company through its most recent difficulties; failed to take decisive action to address a staggering fall in the company’s share price; and failed to engage with your biggest shareholder in any meaningful way.”
For the 52 weeks to 28 June, Accent Group’s EBITDA dipped 3.4% to $278.9m AUD (£146.9m), while total sales climbed 1.2% to $1.64bn AUD (£864m).

Drapers has contacted Frasers and Accent Group for comment.
This comes after Frasers made an all-cash takeover bid for Accent Group at a price of $0.65 AUD (34p) per share on 15 June.
The offer period for its takeover bid was later extended until close of trading on 30 September.
