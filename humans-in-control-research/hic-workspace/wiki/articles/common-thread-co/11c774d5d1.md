# Google Ads August 18, 2026: The Branded Search Metric That Finally Connects Your YouTube Spend to Brand Demand

Source: [common_thread_co](https://commonthreadco.com/blogs/coachs-corner/google-branded-searches-conversion-measurement-august-2026)

On August 18, 2026, Google updated its Branded Searches conversion type, rolling out a refined measurement system that tracks when users search for your brand name on Google or YouTube after viewing an ad. For ecommerce brands running YouTube and Demand Gen campaigns, this means you now have a direct attribution line between your upper-funnel spend and the brand demand it generates, with a new 7-day default conversion window replacing the previous 30-day view-through model.
Branded Searches now function as a reporting-only conversion action, appearing in the "Results" and "All Conversions" columns rather than the standard Conversions column, so they cannot be used as a direct bidding goal. YouTube and Demand Gen campaigns are the eligible campaign types, with Performance Max notably absent from the current list. The update formally categorizes Branded Searches under the "Consideration" goal in Google Ads, positioning it as the go-to metric for proving that awareness investment is turning into brand demand.
The core challenge for 7-9 figure ecommerce brands has always been justifying upper-funnel spend when last-click attribution makes YouTube look inefficient. Google's Branded Searches metric solves this by connecting the dots between an ad impression and the moment a user types your brand name into the search bar, giving brands a concrete proof point that brand campaigns drive actual demand.
The previous version of Branded Searches had a 30-day view-through window, which overstated brand lift for most product categories and made the data hard to trust. The new 7-day default window is more conservative and therefore more credible. Advertisers can adjust this window anywhere from 1 to 30 days depending on their category and purchase cycle.
For brands running Google's AI analytics tools launched earlier this month, Branded Searches integrates naturally into the measurement stack, giving your team one more signal that brand-building investment is working before customers hit the bottom of the funnel. Combine it with our full Google Ads 2026 update tracker to see how each platform change fits into the bigger picture.
Brand Lift studies require a minimum spend threshold and a controlled holdout group to run, making them inaccessible for most ecommerce brands outside the enterprise tier. Branded Searches is always-on and available at no additional cost. It captures intent at a more concrete moment than ad recall, measuring actual search behavior rather than a surveyed response.
This makes it particularly valuable for brands building brand equity on YouTube, where the direct conversion path is long and click-through rates are inherently lower than search. If your YouTube campaigns consistently drive branded search volume, that is the signal your media mix needs more of it, and Branded Searches gives you the data to prove it to leadership and finance.
For brands working with a Google Ads partner to manage full-funnel performance, this metric becomes part of the conversation about how brand investment translates to demand, especially heading into Q4 when upper-funnel spend decisions are being made.
The Branded Searches metric requires correct brand mapping in your Google Ads account. Without it, Google cannot accurately attribute branded search queries to your campaigns. Brand mapping tells Google which search terms represent your brand name and closely related terms, ensuring the attribution is specific to your brand rather than diluted by category searches.
Once brand mapping is configured, you activate the Branded Searches conversion action and set your preferred conversion window. Google surfaces this data in the "Results" and "All Conversions" columns across eligible YouTube and Demand Gen campaigns. Because it is a reporting-only action, no bidding adjustments are needed, but the data directly informs manual budget decisions and media mix allocation for Q4.
For brands also tracking the September language targeting changes coming to Search campaigns, now is an ideal time to audit your full Google Ads account settings before Q4 planning begins.
