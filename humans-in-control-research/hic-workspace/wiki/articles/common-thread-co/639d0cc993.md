# This Week in Ecommerce: TikTok Shop's New Image Rules Are Suspending Accounts, and Snapchat's DPA Numbers Demand Attention

Source: [common_thread_co](https://commonthreadco.com/blogs/coachs-corner/this-week-in-ecommerce-tiktok-shops-new-image-rules-are-suspending-accounts-and-snapchats-dpa-numbers-demand-attention)

On August 1, 2026, TikTok Shop activated automated enforcement of its updated product image requirements, with non-compliant listings now facing immediate sales suspension. For ecommerce brands selling on TikTok Shop, this means 5 specific violation types can trigger automatic delisting: watermarks on main images, non-square aspect ratios, promotional text overlays, multi-angle collages, and AI-generated imagery without disclosure labels.
TikTok's automated systems are scanning every product listing and triggering enforcement without manual review. Listings with violations are being suppressed in search, removed from algorithmic recommendations, or delisted from the marketplace entirely. If your TikTok Shop main images contain watermarks, wrong aspect ratios, promotional text, collages, or undisclosed AI-generated imagery, your listings are actively at risk.
The timing matters. TikTok Shop is entering Q4 with accelerating commerce volume, and brands that clean up their listings now will be positioned to capture holiday traffic. Brands that do not may find their products invisible when purchase intent peaks.

Snapchat released Q2 2026 results that include some of the strongest ecommerce performance data the platform has ever reported. Dynamic Product Ad revenue grew 43% year-over-year. App purchase volume rose 128% year-over-year. Cost per purchase fell 18% year-over-year. Total ecommerce conversions across the platform increased 56%. These are not brand awareness metrics. These are lower-funnel signals from a platform that most ecommerce brands treat as supplemental reach.
The improvement is structural, not seasonal. Snapchat has invested heavily in its Conversions API infrastructure and goal-based bidding system, with app advertising revenue through goal-based bidding growing 27% year-over-year in Q2. The DPA format is also maturing: better signal matching through CAPI means Snapchat's machine learning is working with more accurate purchase data, which improves delivery targeting and reduces wasted spend. A CPP drop of 18% year-over-year on a platform that was simultaneously growing conversions 56% suggests the system is getting materially better at finding buyers, not just reaching more people.
Snapchat now reaches 493 million daily active users globally, with a growing share of its audience aging into higher-value purchase categories including home goods, automotive, financial services, and health and wellness. For brands targeting 18 to 35 year olds, Snapchat's Q2 direct response results make it a credible lower-funnel channel alongside Meta and Google, not just a supplemental reach vehicle. The 43% DPA growth and 56% conversion lift outpaced the year-over-year gains reported by most major platforms in the same period, and the 18% CPP reduction means efficiency improved alongside volume growth.
For brands tracking blended ROAS across channels, Snapchat's improving signal quality makes it worth rerunning in budget allocation models that previously excluded it or capped it as awareness-only. TikTok Shop's automated enforcement system can reduce your product's visibility in search and browse, suppress it from algorithmic recommendations, or delist it from the marketplace entirely. In August 2026, brands reported sales suspensions triggered within 48 hours of a non-compliant image going live. Repeat violations increase the risk of full account-level action.
The August 2026 enforcement focuses on main product images, which are the first image shoppers see in TikTok Shop search and browse. Additional product gallery images have more flexibility, but all imagery is subject to TikTok Shop's broader content guidelines. The immediate risk of delisting is tied to main image violations.
Snapchat's Q2 2026 DPA gains reflect structural improvements in its ad delivery and signal infrastructure, not a seasonal spike. The 43% DPA revenue growth and 56% conversion lift were driven by improved CAPI integration across the advertiser base, maturation of its goal-based bidding system, and a growing addressable ecommerce audience as Snapchat's user base ages into higher-value purchase categories. Brands with active CAPI setups are reporting the most consistent improvement.
