# Google Is Auto-Resizing Your Performance Max Videos with AI: What Ecommerce Brands Must Decide Before September 4

Source: [common_thread_co](https://commonthreadco.com/blogs/coachs-corner/google-pmax-generative-ai-video-resize-september-2026-ecommerce)

On August 17, 2026, Google announced it will automatically apply generative AI to resize existing Performance Max video assets into every missing aspect ratio across Google's full placement inventory. For ecommerce brands running Performance Max campaigns, this means your existing video creative will be modified and served in AI-generated formats by default, with one concrete action required before September 4, 2026: decide whether to opt out.
What Does Google's Generative AI Video Resize Mean for Your Performance Max Campaigns?
Google's new asset optimization upgrade scans your active PMax campaigns, identifies any video assets missing certain aspect ratios, and uses generative AI to build those missing versions automatically. This is on by default for all Performance Max advertisers. Your window to opt out closes September 4, 2026.
How Does Google's Automatic Video Resizing Actually Work?
The upgrade builds directly on existing Google video enhancement features that already convert horizontal videos into vertical and square formats. The new generative AI layer extends that capability further, applying more advanced resizing to fill additional missing aspect ratios across all available placements. If your PMax campaign has a standard 16:9 horizontal video but no 9:16 vertical or 1:1 square versions, Google's AI will generate those missing formats and begin serving them in placements that favor them, such as YouTube Shorts and Display.
Google stated the goal is to maximize video ad reach while improving the viewing experience. The change operates at the asset level, meaning Google reads your existing creative and generates new output from it rather than creating entirely new ads from scratch. Advertisers who want to adjust their video settings can do so at any time within their Google Ads account, and those who want to opt out entirely can contact their account management team or submit an opt-out form by September 4.
Why Is Google Expanding AI Control Over Your Creative Right Now?
This announcement fits a pattern that has accelerated throughout 2026. In July, Google began auto-generating Shopping ad descriptions using AI. On September 1, Google will begin auto-upgrading Search campaigns to AI Max for accounts using Campaign-level Broad Match. Now Google is auto-generating video aspect ratio variants from existing creative in PMax campaigns. The consistent direction is that Google is assuming more creative and optimization control by default, and requiring advertisers to actively opt out if they want to keep manual control over their assets.
For ecommerce brands, Performance Max campaigns typically carry significant revenue volume. The creative running in those campaigns has been tested, approved, and tuned. Introducing AI-generated aspect ratio variants adds a new variable to performance data and potentially to how your brand appears across placements, making this change worth a deliberate decision rather than passive acceptance.
Google is taking more creative control across every campaign type by default. The question is no longer whether AI will touch your ads, it is how much control you choose to keep.
Should You Opt Out or Let Google Resize Your Performance Max Videos?
This depends on your creative standards and how much variance you can absorb in your PMax performance. There are valid reasons to act in both directions.
Ecommerce brands that should consider opting out include those with tightly controlled brand visual guidelines, high-production creative where AI resizing risks distorting key product imagery or on-screen text, regulated product categories where precise framing and disclosures matter, or brands that have already produced native assets in every major aspect ratio. If you have done the creative work to cover all formats, there is nothing for the AI to add.
Brands that may benefit from staying opted in include those running direct-response video creative, user-generated content, or testimonial-style ads where broader reach across additional formats outweighs the risk of AI-modified outputs. If your creative can flex visually and you have limited production bandwidth, the AI-generated variants may extend your reach at no additional cost.
The most effective way to neutralize this change without opting out is to upload native video assets in every major aspect ratio. Google's AI only fills gaps, so eliminating the gaps removes the variable.
What Should You Do Now About Google's Performance Max AI Video Optimization?
- Audit your active PMax campaigns this week. Pull a list of all video assets currently running in Performance Max. Identify which aspect ratios are present (16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:5) and which are missing. If your campaigns already have native assets in every major ratio, the AI resizing has minimal impact and you may not need to take action.
- Decide your opt-out position before September 4. If your brand has strict creative standards or you want full control over how your video assets appear in market, contact your Google account management team or submit the opt-out form before the deadline. After September 4, you can still adjust video settings directly within Google Ads at any time, but the proactive opt-out window will have closed.
- Upload native aspect ratio versions now if you have production capacity. Brief your creative team on cutting 9:16 and 1:1 versions of your top PMax video assets. Google's AI only generates missing formats, so providing native versions in all major ratios eliminates the gap the AI would otherwise fill and gives the algorithm the best possible assets to optimize against.
- Segment by creative sensitivity if a blanket opt-out does not fit. If you want AI-generated resizing for some creative but not others, consider restructuring your PMax asset groups so high-sensitivity creative is managed separately. This avoids a blanket opt-out while still protecting specific assets where brand control matters most.
- Monitor your PMax asset performance report after September 4. Whether you opt in or out, watch for changes in impression volume by format, view-through rates, and conversion rates by video variant. AI-resized assets appear in your asset performance report and can be identified and paused individually if they underperform your benchmarks.
