# Meta Is Rewriting Your Ad Images by Default: What the Advantage+ Creative Text Update Means for Ecommerce Brands

Source: [common_thread_co](https://commonthreadco.com/blogs/coachs-corner/meta-advantage-plus-creative-image-text-rewriting-ecommerce-2026)

On July 27, 2026, Meta activated a default-on feature inside Advantage+ Creative that automatically rewrites headline text embedded in your uploaded ad images. For ecommerce brands running Advantage+ campaigns, this means Meta's AI is now generating up to 8 headline variants from every image containing text, maintaining your original font, colors, and layout while substituting the copy, without requiring your approval before those variants run.
Any ad image you upload that contains visible text is now eligible for AI-generated text rewrites, and these variants are tested automatically against your original creative. Because the feature is on by default across Advantage+ placements, every ecommerce brand running catalog ads, dynamic creative, or standard Advantage+ campaigns is affected. The risk is that an AI-generated headline could misrepresent a product, contradict a live promotion, or conflict with the copy on your landing page.
Meta's system ingests your uploaded ad image, identifies any text within it, and generates up to 8 alternate headline variants. Each variant preserves your original font, banner, color scheme, and overall layout. Only the text content changes. Meta's AI attempts to maintain brand tone and optimize for click-through performance, but it does not have access to your landing page copy, your promotional calendar, or your approved brand guidelines unless you explicitly configure them in the Branding section of Ads Manager.
The variants are tested algorithmically, and the version Meta predicts will perform best is served more frequently over time. If a rewritten headline outperforms your original, you may never see the original version run again unless you actively review the creative report. This is a meaningful shift in who controls the final message your customers see.
This change continues the trend CTC has been tracking throughout 2026: Meta is moving toward a model where advertisers supply the inputs and AI controls the outputs. Brands that are not actively managing their Advantage+ Creative settings are ceding significant creative control to the algorithm. Keep up with every Meta change in our full Meta Ads 2026 update tracker.
Meta's goal is to improve ad performance at scale by removing the bottleneck of manual creative testing. For brands with large catalogs and limited creative resources, AI-generated variants can meaningfully expand test surface area. The challenge is that AI-generated text does not carry the same brand-safety constraints as copy reviewed by a human marketing team.
The recent update to Meta's exclusion-only audience targeting gave brands more control over who does not see their ads. The image text rewriting feature moves in the opposite direction, reducing control over what those audiences see. The two changes together make it more important than ever to audit your Advantage+ settings as a unified system.
For brands preparing holiday creative, the timing is particularly critical. With Q4 approaching, any mismatch between your promoted offers in ad images and the landing page the customer reaches creates a trust and conversion problem that no amount of budget can fix.
Meta added a Branding section in Ads Manager to give advertisers control over how AI uses their brand assets. Logos, fonts, colors, and a restricted words list can all be configured here. Restricted words are the most important lever for brand safety, as they tell Meta's AI which terms cannot appear in generated text under any circumstances.
Brands running campaigns informed by the Meta 2026 Holiday Insights Center recommendations should review their Branding settings before launching Q4 campaigns. Any promotional language in your images, such as sale percentages or free shipping language, is at risk of being rewritten to something generic unless restricted words are set. Advertisers can also opt out of the image text rewriting feature entirely on a per-campaign basis within Advantage+ Creative settings.
