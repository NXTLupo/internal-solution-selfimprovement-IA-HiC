# Digital Insurer Honeycomb Insurance Expands into Nevada

Source: [ins_insurance_journal_west](https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/west/2026/06/25/875189.htm)

Digital insurer Honeycomb Insurance is expanding into Nevada roughly one month after receiving a $40 million funding round.
The company’s recent launches include operations in Kansas and Missouri.
Honeycomb’s digital platform is designed to enable agents to quote and bind policies quickly using only a property address for initial eligibility. Agents will have access to Honeycomb’s admitted and non-admitted habitational products, as well as excess liability coverage.
Honeycomb is headquartered in Chicago, with offices in the U.S. and Israel. The company specializes in landlord and condominium associations and now offers coverage in 24 states.
