# People Moves: Ward Joins Alliant Insurance Services Benefits Team as SVP; Delos Appoints Cammet Chief Product Officer

Source: [ins_insurance_journal_west](https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/west/2026/06/18/874167.htm)

Ward Joins Alliant Insurance Services Benefits Team as SVP
Brandon Ward joined Alliant Insurance Services as senior vice president within its employee benefits group.
Ward has a background working with technology companies, venture-backed businesses and growth-stage organizations. His experience includes human capital management, employee benefits, total rewards consulting, executive and board-level advisory services, and talent attraction and retention planning. Prior to joining Alliant, Ward was a business consultant with an employee benefits consulting firm.
Alliant is headquartered in Irvine, California.
Delos Insurance Solutions Appoints Cammet Chief Product Officer
Delos Insurance Solutions appointed Wendy Cammet to a newly created role of chief product officer.
Cammet is an insurance executive with experience in property/casualty insurance, product strategy and risk management. She has held several senior leadership roles, most recently as president of Foremost Insurance (a Farmers Insurance company).
Delos is headquartered in San Francisco, California.
