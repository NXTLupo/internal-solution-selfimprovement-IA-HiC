# Grocery Tech Trends Study: Where grocers are investing in tech

Source: [canadian_grocer](https://canadiangrocer.com/grocery-tech-trends-study)

Despite the heavy economic pressures weighing on them, grocers surveyed for our study aren’t pulling back on tech spending in 2026.
    Technology has always helped shape the business of selling groceries—from the introduction of barcode scanners and refrigeration sensors to electronic shelf tags, inventory tracking robots and AI-powered personalization. But as the pace of innovation accelerates, where are grocery retailers prioritizing their technology investments today? What solutions are gaining traction? And what are the obstacles facing grocers trying to future-proof their business? To answer these questions and more, we surveyed grocery retailers across Canada for our inaugural 2026 Grocery Tech Trends Study. Read on for some highlights from the study.
