# Tre Stelle awakens ‘sleepy shoppers’ with new campaign

Source: [canadian_grocer](https://canadiangrocer.com/tre-selle-awakens-sleepy-shoppers-new-campaign)

The campaign features Tre Stelle Original Cream Cheese and Tre Stelle's latest release, Lactose-Free Garlic & Chives.
    A new campaign from Tre Stelle asks Canadians to rethink their grocery shopping habits.
On Thursday morning (Aug. 13), commuters in Toronto’s Union Station, Vancouver’s Commercial–Broadway Station and Montreal’s Gare Centrale may have spotted “sleepy shoppers” sporting bathrobes and pushing grocery carts as part of the cheese brand’s new “Sleep Shopper”platform.
The campaign encourages Canadians to “wake up” from grocery shopping on autopilot.
