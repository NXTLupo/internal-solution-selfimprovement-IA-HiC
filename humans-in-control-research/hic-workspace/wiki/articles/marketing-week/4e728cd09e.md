Source: [marketing_week](https://www.marketingweek.com/body-shop-turnaround-cmo/)

‘Affordable indulgence’: The Body Shop’s CMO on getting back to the brand’s ‘DNA’
Following a tricky few years, The Body Shop is looking to future-proof its brand by refining its ethical and affordable positioning for a new generation.
2024 was a tumultuous year for The Body Shop. Once a high-street staple, the retailer found itself in administration after a period of tough trading, including swinging to a £71m annual loss in December 2022.
However, within its first 100 days post-acquisition in September 2024 by investment fund Auréa, led by British cosmetics entrepreneur Mike Jatania, The Body Shop had already generated £2m in profit. Today the brand and marketing department is in turnaround mode.
