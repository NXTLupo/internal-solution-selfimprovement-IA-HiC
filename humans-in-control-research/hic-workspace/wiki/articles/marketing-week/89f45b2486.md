# From ‘better for you’ to ‘better than anything’: How Proper Snacks is growing ahead of the category

Source: [marketing_week](https://www.marketingweek.com/proper-snacks-balance-joy-health/)

From ‘better for you’ to ‘better than anything’: How Proper Snacks is growing ahead of the category
With Proper Snacks, which owns the Proper and Eat Real brands, consumers don’t have to trade-off between health and joy, says CMO Sandie Dilger, something that is helping it outpace category growth.
The snack category is faced with a consumer who thinks more carefully than ever about what they eat. It’s also a sector with plenty of big brands, with big budgets and long histories of being in the snacking category.
