Changing tactics, consistent fundamentals: New effectiveness principles revealed
Amid a rapidly changing marketing environment, Ipsos and Effie UK have aimed to create effectiveness principles that brands can “keep coming back to”.
Marketing has become increasingly preoccupied with what comes next. AI, new platforms and changing consumer behaviours are creating pressure to adapt, but research from Ipsos and Effie UK suggests some of the principles underpinning effective marketing are considerably less new.
