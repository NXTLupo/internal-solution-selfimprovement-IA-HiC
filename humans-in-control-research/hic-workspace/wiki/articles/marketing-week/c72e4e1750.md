# Klarna CMO to depart after nine years

Source: [marketing_week](https://www.marketingweek.com/klarna-cmo-depart-business/)

Klarna CMO to depart after nine years
David Sandstrom is credited with building the Swedish fintech into “one of the most recognised brands in global finance”.
Klarna’s chief marketing officer David Sandstrom is to depart after more than nine years as the finance brand’s top marketer.
Sandstrom took on the CMO role at the Swedish buy-now-pay-later (BNPL) brand in April 2017. Over the subsequent nine years the company massively expanded its global user base, adding new products and becoming a publicly listed company in 2025.
Klarna describes Sandstrom’s departure as a ‘planned transition’ set to take place in early 2027, which will also see CFO of six years Niclas Neglén depart. Sandstrom will continue to lead his function through this transition and “prepare the brand and the team for the next phase of Klarna’s development”, the company states.
Sandstrom is praised by CEO and co-founder Sebastian Siemiatkowski for leaving Klarna “a stronger company than the one [he] joined”.
“David has given Klarna a voice; he took a Nordic payments company and has built one of the most recognised brands in global finance,” Siemiatkowski says.
Klarna CMO: I love marketing, I don’t love advertising
When Sandstrom first joined Klarna it was a brand focused on its BNPL proposition. Since then, the business has added products including savings accounts, Klarna debit and financing cards, and peer-to-peer money transfer. The brand also completed its initial public offering on the US stock exchange in September last year.
Whereas back in 2017 the fintech firm had 20 million users, Klarna now boasts 120 million. In its most recent quarter, the brand drove impressive growth, with revenue up 27%. As it has expanded its product portfolio, Klarna has also sought to deepen engagement with its users. Revenue per active consumer grew 24% year-on-year during the second quarter.
Commenting on his departure, Sandstrom described his time at Klarna as the “defining chapter” of his working life.
“Building this brand with an extraordinary team has been a privilege and I am proud of what we built to get here,” he says.
Prior to joining the fintech brand, Sandstrom worked agency side. He was CEO at DDB Nordics between 2012 and 2016, having first joined DDB’s Stockholm office in 2009.
