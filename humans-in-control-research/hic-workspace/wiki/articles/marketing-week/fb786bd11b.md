‘Missions that matter’: Boots on why its new brand platform is a ‘rallying cry’
Almost a year into “the best job in marketing”, CMO Charlotte Lock is looking to the future with a new brand platform designed to give customers a burst of confidence.
Boots is launching into autumn with a new brand platform, as the retail giant embarks on a mission to honour its 180-year history while looking ahead to the next 180.
The idea behind ‘Give it Some Boots’ is to put the retailer’s expertise at the forefront, celebrating how Boots helps consumers celebrate life’s beautiful and brutal moments.
