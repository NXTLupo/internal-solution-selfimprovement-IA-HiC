# Why treating LLMs like ‘VIP customers’ helped one brand fuel visibility

Source: [marketing_week](https://www.marketingweek.com/b2b-akamai-llms-ai-search/)

Why treating LLMs like ‘VIP customers’ helped one brand fuel visibility
By overhauling its search strategy to appeal to LLMs, B2B cybersecurity firm Akamai delivered a 133% increase in ChatGPT visibility versus rivals.
Showing up in AI search has quickly become a key metric for brands and marketers as the consumer journey changes before their eyes. It is particularly important in B2B, where buying decisions can be shaped and altered by the recommendations of a large language model (LLM) before marketing has even had a chance to get its message across.
For cybersecurity B2B firm Akamai, the change prompted a complete rethink of how it shows up in search.
