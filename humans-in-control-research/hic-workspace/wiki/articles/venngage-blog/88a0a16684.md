Part of my job as a content strategist at Venngage is to test our product against competing AI design tools. I’ve created infographics with just about every AI infographic generator worth trying, including ChatGPT, Claude and dedicated infographic software.
Most tools can generate an infographic from a text prompt in minutes. But still many of them produce designs with poor layouts, weak visual hierarchy or inaccurate content that requires extensive manual editing before publishing. That defeats the purpose of using AI to speed up the design process.
In this guide, I’ve shortlisted the 11 best AI infographic generators worth trying, based on what they actually do well, so you can find the right tool for your workflow.
What is an AI infographic generator?
An AI infographic generator is a design tool that creates infographics from a text prompt. Instead of designing from scratch or editing an infographic template manually, you add a text prompt explaining your idea and the AI generates a polished design with proper layout, icons, colors, and copy.
Some tools even allow you to upload PDFs, docs, and CSV files and automatically create an infographic from the content.
What to look for in an AI infographic generator?
Here are the some of the most important features you must look for in an AI infographic generator beyond text–to-image generation:
- AI-powered layout generation: AI should organize your content into a balanced, easy-to-follow layout with a clear visual hierarchy instead of simply filling a template.
- AI-assisted editing: The best tools let you refine layouts, rewrite copy, replace visuals and reorganize sections using AI after the first draft is generated.
- AI chart generation: If you’re creating statistical or business infographics, look for tools that can generate editable charts, recommend better chart types and visualize your data automatically.
- Document-to-infographic conversion: Choose a tool that can transform PDFs, Word documents, presentations, spreadsheets or blog posts into editable infographic drafts.
- Relevant visual recommendations: AI should recommend icons, illustrations, images and color palettes that match your content instead of using generic visuals.
- Automatic brand application: Look for a tool that can automatically apply your brand colors, fonts and logos across the entire infographic to maintain a consistent visual identity.
- Flexible customization: AI should save time, not limit creativity. Make sure you can edit every part of the AI-generated infographic design , including the layout, charts, icons, colors and copy.
Common limitations of AI infographic design tools
AI infographic generators can save hours of work, but they’re not perfect. Here are a few limitations to keep in mind before choosing one.
- AI can get the content wrong: It may generate incorrect facts, statistics or citations. Always verify the information before publishing.
- The first draft is rarely final: Most AI-generated infographics still need manual edits to the layout, copy, colors or visual hierarchy.
- Long content doesn’t always translate well: AI can struggle to organize lengthy reports or complex topics, leading to overcrowded or uneven layouts.
- Customization varies by tool: Some platforms only generate the initial infographic, while others let you refine layouts, charts, icons and copy with AI.
- Free plans come with restrictions: Many tools limit exports, AI credits, premium templates or branding features unless you upgrade.
Methodology: How I evaluated the best AI infographic generators
To compare these AI infographic generators fairly, I tested each tool using the same four criteria.
AI infographic generation
I looked at how well each tool turned a prompt into a complete infographic. This included the quality of the layout, content organization, AI-generated visuals and how much manual editing was needed after the first draft.
Data visualization
Since infographics rely heavily on data, I evaluated how accurately each tool created charts, graphs and diagrams. I also tested how easy it was to import data using PDF/CSV and edit the charts.
Customization and branding
I assessed how much creative control each tool offered. This included editing the layout, changing colors and fonts, applying brand assets, using templates and customizing the final design.
Ease of use
Finally, I looked at the overall workflow to understand how easily someone without design experience can create, edit and publish an AI-generated infographic.
