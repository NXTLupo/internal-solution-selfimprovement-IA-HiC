# Gopuff adds to private label brands

Source: [supermarket_news](https://www.supermarketnews.com/private-label/gopuff-adds-to-private-label-brands)

Gopuff adds to private label brands
The company used shopper ordering data to come up with products in demand
August 19, 2026
Gopuff on Tuesday unveiled new additions to its private label brands, Crave Shoppe and Basically. For the first time, the assortment includes seasonal, limited-time offerings.
The expansion comes from what customers are ordering most on Gopuff. Sales of fall-flavored products rose 19.4% year-over-year last fall, signaling strong demand for seasonal favorites. In response, Gopuff developed a broader assortment of high-quality, affordable products featuring the flavors customers demand. The lineup includes:
- Fall LTOs: Introducing seasonal products available for a limited-time, including Crave Shoppe Iced Pumpkin Cake, Pumpkin Donut Holes, Cheese Danish, Mini Apple Fritters and Bourbon Salted Caramel Fudge as well as a new Basically Apple Cider 
- Sliced cakes: Baked in small batches and pre-sliced, customers can order Crave Shoppe’s new Iced Lemon Cake and Banana Nut Cake 
- Edible and bakeable cookie dough: Crave Shoppe Chocolate Chip Cookie Dough and Brownie Batter are plant-based, gluten-free and safe to eat raw or baked 
- Coffee pods: Basically Coffee Pods are available in 12-packs of Donut Shop and Vanilla flavors 
- Individually wrapped treats: Big Chocolate Chip Cookie, Big Candy Cookie and Big Chocolate Brownie 
- Toaster pastries: Crave Shoppe Toaster Pastries are available in Strawberry and Cookies & Creme flavors 
- Fudge: Crave Shoppe Chocolate Fudge and Crave Shoppe Chocolate Peanut Butter Fudge 
- Cookie thins: Basically Cookie Thins are available in Chocolate Chip and Brownie flavors 
- Snack mixes: Basically’s three new Snack Mix flavors: Sweet Heat, Dill Pickle and Sour Cream and Onion Pepitas 
“Fall flavors are some of the most anticipated of the year, and we know our customers want more ways to enjoy them,” said Jillian Jensen, general manager of Private Label Merchandising at Gopuff. “By tapping into our own ordering and search data, we were able to identify the flavors and products our customers were craving most and turn those insights into a private label assortment designed specifically around their tastes.”
This latest expansion builds on Gopuff’s private label portfolio which has grown 20% year-to-date in 2026, with private label products now included in nearly 1 in 5 Gopuff orders.
