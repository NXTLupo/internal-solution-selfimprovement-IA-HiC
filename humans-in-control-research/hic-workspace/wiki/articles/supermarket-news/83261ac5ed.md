# Walmart rolls out tap-to-pay checkout

Source: [supermarket_news](https://www.supermarketnews.com/grocery-technology/walmart-rolls-out-tap-to-pay-checkout)

Walmart rolls out tap-to-pay checkout
Company is one of last major retailers to accept the ubiquitous technology
August 24, 2026
Walmart on Friday said it plans to install “tap-to-pay” technology at the checkout stations of all U.S. Walmart and Sam’s Club stores by year end.
The rollout, which began at select locations on Monday, will be expanded to all fuel centers by the end of next year, the company said.
The technology will allow customers to check out using “Google Pay, Apple Pay and other” contactless payment methods, a spokesperson for Walmart told Supermarket News. Samsung Pay will also be accepted, according to a report on SamMobile.com, an online site for fans of Samsung technology.
The Walmart spokesperson declined to disclose details about the specific technology involved in the rollout or the cost of installation.
Some observers said Walmart’s rollout of contactless payment was long overdue, given that the technology has been available for more than a decade.
“It's wild that it's taken so long for Walmart to start embracing tap-to-pay,” said one report on the engadget technology website, noting that Google Wallet, the predecessor to Google Pay, was introduced in 2011, followed by Apple Pay in 2014.
Walmart had previously tried to launch a contactless payment service called CurrentC. That platform, which relied on QR codes, was shut down in 2016. The new contactless payment technology uses near-field communication (NFC), which allows electronic devices to share data when placed very close to each other.
In the meantime, Walmart in 2016 rolled out its own digital payment technology called Walmart Pay, which also uses QR codes.
“We keep our technology simple and use tested hardware that’s already widely in place,” Walmart says on its website promoting Walmart Pay. “Walmart Pay doesn’t use near-field communication.”
Walmart Pay allows customers to use the Walmart app to pay for purchases, view receipts and access Walmart+ fuel discounts. Walmart Pay, which can be loaded with customers’ credit cards or Walmart gift cards, will continue to be available to customers.
Meanwhile Sam’s Club members have embraced the use of that banner’s Scan & Go technology to scan and pay as they shop rather than go through the traditional checkout lanes, the company said in recent earnings calls. Scan & Go also will continue to be available to Sam’s Club members who shop in-store, the company said.
