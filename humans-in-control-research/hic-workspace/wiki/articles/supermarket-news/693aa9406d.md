# Walmart brings commerce intelligence platform to Sam’s Club

Source: [supermarket_news](https://www.supermarketnews.com/grocery-technology/walmart-brings-commerce-intelligence-platform-to-sam-s-club)

Walmart brings commerce intelligence platform to Sam’s Club
Walmart brings commerce intelligence platform to Sam’s Club
August 21, 2026
Walmart announced plans on Thursday to bring Scintilla, its first-party commerce intelligence platform, to Sam’s Club in 2027. The expansion reflects Walmart’s continued investment in enterprise capabilities that transform trusted first-party insights into actionable commerce intelligence, helping merchants and suppliers better understand members, improve execution and deliver an even better member experience.
Scintilla connects insights across members, omnichannel performance and business outcomes in a single platform, giving Sam’s Club merchants and suppliers the information they need to anticipate member needs, strengthen collaboration and make more confident, data-driven decisions. The result is stronger assortment, improved availability and an even better shopping experience for members.
“Everything starts with the member and understanding what they need, what they love and what they’re looking for next,” said Myron Frazier, Chief Merchant, Sam’s Club. “Scintilla will help our merchants and suppliers listen better and act faster. It gives us a clearer view of what matters to our members so we can bring them the right items, create more moments of discovery, and keep making Sam’s Club a place they are excited to come back to.”
Walmart continued to gain U.S. market share in the second quarter as grocery sales and growing use of its ecommerce services helped the retailer increase sales and transactions.
Walmart U.S. reported net sales of $125.2 billion for the fiscal second quarter ended July 31, up 3.5% from $120.9 billion a year earlier. Comparable sales excluding fuel increased 2.6%, compared with 4.6% growth during the same quarter last year. Transactions rose 1.5%, while the average ticket increased 1.1%.
For the grocery industry, one of the more notable results came from Sam’s Club, where comparable sales excluding fuel increased 4.4%. The company said the increase was driven by higher transaction counts and unit volumes, with strength in grocery as well as general merchandise.
Transactions at Sam’s Club jumped 7%, although the average ticket fell 2.5%. Net sales increased 8.8% to $25.7 billion, while sales excluding fuel rose 4.5% to $22.1 billion.
