# Target grocery is all about trends and good health

Source: [supermarket_news](https://www.supermarketnews.com/grocery-operations/target-grocery-is-all-about-trends-and-good-health)

Target grocery is all about trends and good health
The retailer is focusing on items not offered at competing stores
Target believes it has found a winning grocery strategy, and by all accounts, the retailer appears to be hitting the mark.
During its second-quarter earnings call Wednesday, the retailer credited strong sales in food and beverage for another successful quarter in which comparable sales rose 3.8%.
Target is completely overhauling its grocery aisles this year, expanding the section by 20% and as much as 50% in some locations. The retailer is giving fresh produce more space while pulling back on pantry staples that other stores offer.
The strategy involves trendier and health-conscious offerings, Bloomberg reports. Collagen-infused strawberry matcha powder, mushroom coffee brands, gluten-free pumpkin spice cereal and sour melon candy are just a few examples ushering in the change. Asian food is also getting a boost, according to Bloomberg.
Target knows that more than half of its shoppers buy groceries when they shop at the store, and the department is one of the company’s fastest-growing.
The retailer has added about 4,600 new food items, Bloomberg reports, and introduced more than 60 brands in the second quarter.
While most competitors have worked on lowering food prices, Target has decided to focus on consumer trends, reflecting evolving tastes, interests and wellness needs. The retailer hopes shoppers will pay a little more for the experience.
Here are more examples of Target’s grocery exclusivity, according to the Bloomberg article:
Clean-label potato chip brand Snackish is only available at Target stores and premiered in June. Scoot only offers its frozen lemonade pouches at Target, and Sweet Loren’s, which makes refrigerated cookie dough, introduced a snickerdoodle flavor that can only be purchased at the bullseye store.
