# Supermarket News names finalists for first-ever Best New Products Contest

Source: [supermarket_news](https://www.supermarketnews.com/grocery-categories/supermarket-news-names-finalists-for-first-ever-best-new-products-contest)

Retailers can vote for their favorites now
August 25, 2026
Supermarket News has named the finalists and opened the voting in its first-ever Retailer Choice Best New Products Contest (BNPC). The BNPC honors today’s top product innovations and tomorrow’s solutions in the supermarket industry, focusing on the most successful product launches from the past year.
SN editors identified all finalists in 20 categories from products nominated by CPG manufacturers.
Retailers can vote now through Sept. 7. Winners will be announced in late September and will be featured in the October print issue of SN and online. Access the ballot here.
