# Raley’s plans to close more stores

Source: [supermarket_news](https://www.supermarketnews.com/store-closings/raley-s-plans-to-close-more-stores)

Raley’s plans to close more stores
Four slated to close in coming months after three were shuttered earlier this year
August 24, 2026
Raley’s plans to shutter three locations in California and one in Nevada in the coming months, the company confirmed on Monday.
The closings will bring to seven the total number of Raley’s set for closure in 2026 and early 2027. The West Sacramento, Calif.-based company previously closed a Nob Hill Foods store in Mountain View, a Raley’s in Roseville and another Raley’s in Antioch.
Another Raley’s in Elko, Nev., is slated for closure in December, and a Nob Hill Foods location in Los Gatos is scheduled to close by next June, the reports said. The other two Raley’s that are scheduled to close in the coming months are in Brentwood and Petaluma, Calif.
“This is not part of a larger downsizing effort,” a spokesperson for Raley’s told Supermarket News. “We are evaluating stores on their performance continually.”
The company attributed the closures to local market conditions and a lack of “long-term financial sustainability,” among other factors, the spokesperson said, noting that Raley’s also plans to open a new store in Madera, Calif., in March of next year.
“These decisions are part of the same disciplined approach that guides investments across our business, including our planned Madera store opening in March 2027,” the spokesperson said. “This approach ensures Raley’s remains strong and well-positioned to serve customers and communities for generations to come.”
The closures come amid a wave of traditional supermarket downsizing, including several Kroger- and Albertsons-owned stores that are part of an ongoing streamlining effort at those companies. Kroger announced last June that it plans to close 60 stores by the end of this year while Albertsons reduced its store count by a net of 26 locations last year and has continued to close stores in 2026.
In addition, Grocery Outlet, which is based in Emeryville, Calif., also unveiled plans to shut down 36 stores earlier this year, including nine in Southern and Central California. In addition, Save Mart recently closed two Lucky Supermarket locations in Danville and San Francisco.
O’Brien’s Market, a two-unit independent operator in Modesto, Calif., also recently announced its plans to shutter both locations. In a statement on its website, the retailer, which had been in business for 49 years, attributed some of its challenges to the impact of the Covid pandemic, from which it “never fully recovered.”
Raley’s, which is a third-generation, family-owned company, is part of The Raley’s Cos., which includes the Bashas’ Family of Stores, operating primarily in Arizona. Together Raley’s and Bashas’ have 235 locations across six states.
