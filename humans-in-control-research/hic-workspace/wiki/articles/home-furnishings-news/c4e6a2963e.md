# Kohl’s launches Martha Stewart appliances and expands Bobby Flay cookware lineup

Source: [home_furnishings_news](https://www.hfndigital.com/housewares/kohls-launches-martha-stewart-appliances-and-expands-bobby-flay-cookware-lineup/)

Allison Zisko// Editor in Chief//August 25, 2026
Allison Zisko// Editor in Chief//August 25, 2026
Martha Stewart’s kitchen electric collection, exclusive to Amazon since April, has now expanded to Kohl’s.
The collection encompasses a range of countertop appliances, including a 5-quart stand mixer, family-sized griddle, 8-quart slow cooker, digital tea kettle and colorful toasters, and is made by The Cookware Company.
In the fall, Kohl’s will introduce an exclusive pomegranate colorway across select products, including the hand mixer, stand mixer and slow cooker, “arriving just in time for the holidays,” the company said in a release.
Some of the products in the line will be available in the retailer‘s brick-and-mortars stores, while the full collection will be available on its website.
The Martha Stewart lineup is also expanding into home fragrance with a new collection of candles, according to Kohl’s.
The retailer is also expanding its Bobby Flay collection of cookware to include multi-piece stainless-steel and ceramic cookware sets as well as individual fry pans.
