# Caskata launches bakeware line

Source: [home_furnishings_news](https://www.hfndigital.com/tabletop-gifts/caskata-launches-bakeware-line/)

HFN Staff// News & Commentary//August 18, 2026
HFN Staff// News & Commentary//August 18, 2026
Caskata is taking its first step into bakeware this fall, bringing the distinctive patterns and playful point of view the brand is known for to a collection designed for cooking, serving and leaving right on the table.
The new collection includes 15 forms in high-fired porcelain, from loaf and mini loaf pans to ramekins and round, square, and rectangular bakers. True to Caskata’s approach to pattern, the artwork doesn’t simply sit on the surface: it travels across the sides, slips over rims, and turns up unexpectedly inside and along the bottoms of the pieces.
The debut assortment brings together some of the brand’s most recognizable designs, including bestselling Lucy the Octopus, School of Fish, Crabs & Nets, and Sardines, the newest pattern to quickly become a customer favorite.
Every piece is dishwasher-, microwave-, and oven-safe to 450°F, and meant to move easily from oven to table.
The collection arrives this fall and will be on display in the Zrike Brands Showroom during this October’s Tabletop Show at 41 Madison. Caskata was acquired by Zrike Brands in July.
