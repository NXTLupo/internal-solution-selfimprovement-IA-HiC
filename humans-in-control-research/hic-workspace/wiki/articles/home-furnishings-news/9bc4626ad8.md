# Cookware & Bakeware Alliance to deliver ‘packed’ speaker lineup for fall meeting

Source: [home_furnishings_news](https://www.hfndigital.com/housewares/cookware-bakeware-alliance-offers-packed-speaker-lineup-for-fall-meeting/)

Allison Zisko// Editor in Chief//August 19, 2026
Allison Zisko// Editor in Chief//August 19, 2026
The Cookware & Bakeware Alliance’s fall member meeting returns next month with an expanded three-day format, a packed speaker lineup and new networking opportunities, the group said.
The meeting runs Wednesday, Sept. 30 through Friday, Oct. 2 at the Sheridan Suites Chicago O’Hare, with programming built around “Committees in Action,” industry trend forecasting, and policy and trade updates.
Managing Director Fran Attilio said this year’s event will require three days to “pack it with networking opportunities, speakers and engagement.” Day 1 begins with Committees in Action sessions on Wednesday afternoon alongside a board meeting, then moves into full-member programming Thursday and Friday. Members who attend in person are also welcome to join the Committees in Action meetings, even if they do not currently serve on a committee, the CBA said.
A new offsite networking event is scheduled for Thursday night at Kings in Rosemont, which Attilio characterized as “bowling, billiards and bars.”
Guest speakers on Thursday will discuss trends, retail, legislation and tariffs.
Vanessa Peterson of paint company PPG returns with a Color of the Year presentation that connects trend direction to business strategies.
That session will be followed by one on legislation and compliance. The CBA’s long-time partners Tom Lee (Latham & Watkins LLP) and Steve Burns of The Cookware Sustainability Alliance will lead a live, Q&A-friendly legislation update focused on what’s coming and what’s already in motion. The update is expected to cover PFAS and lead, along with topics members are tracking closely, including EPR, AI and microplastics.
“[T]hese issues aren’t going away, and they keep evolving,” Attilio said in a release. “The goal is to make sure members have the information they need as requirements shift.”
Friday’s agenda leans into commercial strategy. Circana returns with Joe Derochowski and the Circana team to present “Cooking Up Growth: Unlocking Opportunities in Cookware & Bakeware.”
Circana presented at the Spring Member Meeting and was the highest-rated presentation of the event, the CBA said. “The fall program is positioned to go beyond ‘latest trends’ into what’s truly changing consumer behavior and the retail environment around meal prep, shopping journeys and category growth,” it added.
Then retail journalist Warren Shoulberg will present “The Stores of the Future in Housewares and Home are Already Here,” a deep dive into how retailers are reinventing the home category. The session looks at changes in formats, merchandising, omnichannel expectations, and community-building strategies that are already affecting how products are discovered and purchased.
Following that, Milissa Rick and Samantha Kraemer of Regal Holdings will present “Building the Foundation: Positioning Legacy Organizations for Future Growth.” Their session focuses on how established brands modernize without losing what makes them trusted, using stakeholder engagement, consumer insights, brand architecture, and digital experience strategy.
Finally, the meeting closes with a timely look at global trade. Kevin Koch of C.H. Robinson delivers a trade briefing focused on tariffs and government updates.
Registration for the meeting closes Sept. 8. You can register here.
