# Creative Co-Op inks extension for Vegas showroom

Source: [home_furnishings_news](https://www.hfndigital.com/industry-news/creative-co-op-inks-extension-for-vegas-showroom/)

Amanda Erd// Managing Editor, Gifts & Decorative Accessories//August 19, 2026
Creative Co-Op president says company is "proud of the role Las Vegas has played" in growth thus far.
Amanda Erd// Managing Editor, Gifts & Decorative Accessories//August 19, 2026
Creative Co-Op, one of the longest standing tenants at World Market Center Las Vegas, has decided it’s not leaving anytime soon and has renewed its lease at the trade center with Andmore.
News of the renewal comes as Andmore noted a “wave of positive momentum” coming on the heels of a successful summer Las Vegas Market. According to the news release, securing Creative Co-Op as a continued tenant “reaffirms confidence” in the west coast market.
“Having a tenant of Creative Co-Op’s caliber recommit to World Market Center Las Vegas speaks volumes,” said Amory Wooden, chief revenue officer at Andmore. “They’ve been with us since the beginning, and their continued investment in this market reflects the momentum we’re seeing across the board. Las Vegas is growing, and partners like Creative Co-Op are a big part of why.”
Creative Co-Op has operated showroom space at World Market Center Las Vegas since the campus got its start. Today, the home décor company works out of a 27,000-square-foot showroom. It launches new product lines twice a year during both the January and July markets.
“Every trip to Las Vegas is a different experience with Creative Co-Op,” said Adam Schrier, president of Creative Co-Op. “Buyers know that when they walk into our showroom, they’re going to see something new in both January and July, and that kind of energy is exactly what keeps this market appealing for our West Coast and Midwest buyers. We’re proud of the role Las Vegas has played in our growth, and we’re excited for what’s ahead.”
