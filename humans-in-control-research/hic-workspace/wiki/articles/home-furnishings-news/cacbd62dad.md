# French tabletop brands line up collections for Paris Design Week

Source: [home_furnishings_news](https://www.hfndigital.com/tabletop-gifts/french-tabletop-brands-line-up-collections-for-paris-design-week/)

Allison Zisko// Editor in Chief//August 19, 2026
Allison Zisko// Editor in Chief//August 19, 2026
Some of France’s leading tabletop brands will celebrate their craft during Paris Design Week in September.
Running alongside the Maison & Objet trade fair, Paris Design Week transforms the capital into a vast showcase dedicated to design, where galleries, workshops, showrooms and design houses unveil their newest collections.
French tabletop brands will offer their interpretation of the art of hospitality in their Paris showrooms, according to Francéclat, an export trade office that promotes French watch, jewelry and tableware brands. Some will revisit iconic interiors, while others will highlight new materials, collaborate with designers or artists, or create collections that respond to new ways of living and socializing.
Atelier Monochrome, Christofle, Ercuis et Raynaud, Gien, Haviland, Jars, Marie Daâge and Robert Haviland & C. Parlon will participate in Paris Design Week, which is scheduled for Sept. 10-14, 2026.
Nearly two dozen French tabletop brands will also exhibit at the Maison & Objet show, which takes place at the same time in Villepinte, in Halls 4, 5 and 6. A highlight of this year’s fair is Les Rendez-vous Cook&Share by La filière France, a shared space that brings together trade organizations, federations, partners, manufacturers, distributors, brands and lifestyle experts to relax, exchange ideas, listen and discuss practical business topics.
Four new brands will participate in Cook & Share: Volca plates, Delissea mugs, L’Atelier Rosie knives and Lepage knives.
