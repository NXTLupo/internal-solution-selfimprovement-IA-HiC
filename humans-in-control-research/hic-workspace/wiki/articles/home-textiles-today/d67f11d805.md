# Dallas Market Center named finalist for 2026 Good Impact Award

Source: [home_textiles_today](https://www.hometextilestoday.com/markets-amp-tradeshows/dallas-market-center-named-finalist-for-2026-good-impact-award/)

Allison Zisko// Editor in Chief//August 24, 2026
Dallas – Dallas Market Center has been named a finalist for Trade Show Executive magazine’s 2026 Good Impact Award, DMC said.
It is one of four finalists in the Good Impact Award category for its Stand With Main Street initiative on behalf of independent retailers. Winners will be announced in September.
DMC launched Stand With Main Street in April 2025 in response to sweeping tariff policies that affected independent retailers.
The campaign mobilized more than 2,200 customers to sign a petition delivered directly to Commerce Secretary Lutnick, Treasury Secretary Bessent, and U.S. Trade Representative Greer, and brought DMC customers — including the president of Hallmark Cards — into a briefing with Kansas Senator Jerry Moran’s staff, DMC said.
DMC also joined a broad coalition that filed amicus briefs with the Court of International Trade and the U.S. Supreme Court, arguing that the tariffs exceeded presidential authority under the International Emergency Economic Powers Act (IEEPA).
“Dallas Market Center is a family-owned company and protecting the small and independent retailers we serve has always been part of who we are,” said President and CEO Cindy Morris.
