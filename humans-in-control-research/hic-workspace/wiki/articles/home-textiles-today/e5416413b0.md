# 3 fabric execs weigh in on their strategy for remainder of 2026

Source: [home_textiles_today](https://www.hometextilestoday.com/manufacturers/3-fabric-execs-weigh-in-on-their-strategy-for-remainder-of-2026/)

Cindy W. Hodnett// Executive Editor of Brand Development//August 24, 2026
High Point, N.C. — Domestic manufacturing in the textile category has fabric suppliers are navigating disruptions due to tariffs, logistics and fickle consumer sentiment.
HTT sister publication Furniture Today queried fabric suppliers about a range of issues challenging business, and three executives outlined how they plan to support their customers in months to come.
Our story for the remainder of 2026 is clear: The industry doesn’t need another fabric supplier; it needs a strategic partner who can help build stronger brands, create differentiated collections and execute flawlessly from concept to delivery.
As we head into High Point Market and Interwoven, we’re showcasing new performance-driven solutions within our upholstery line led by LiveSmart, our premier performance brand, along with expanded offerings in pet-friendly, washable and solution-dyed fabrics.

Today’s consumers expect products that are beautiful, durable and easy to live with, making performance no longer a premium feature, but an expectation. Our focus is on delivering innovation that helps our customers create meaningful differentiation at retail.
Through our experienced design, merchandising and product development capabilities, we help translate consumer insights into market-right assortments that resonate with shoppers, accelerate speed to market and create competitive advantage. We believe our role extends well beyond supplying fabric; we’re helping our customers shape the next generation of successful home furnishings products.
Supporting all of this is a transformed global operating platform. Today, our dedicated management team leads manufacturing operations across five countries, with North Carolina serving as the foundation of our global network. This structure gives our customers the flexibility, reliability and responsiveness they need in an ever-changing marketplace.
Our story for retailers is helping them deliver lasting value to today’s more intentional consumer.
In a challenging market, consumers are taking more time to make decisions and want confidence that their investment will look great and perform for years to come. Sunbrella helps retailers answer that need with a combination of globally inspired designs, long-lasting performance, and the brand consumers trust and prefer most.
We’re continuing to bring excitement to the market through new ready-to-ship textures and design-forward collections that create excitement on the retail floor. At the same time, every product carrying the Sunbrella name is backed by decades of proven durability, easy care, and consumer trust, giving retailers a compelling reason to close the sale and helping consumers feel good about investing in quality.
That lasting value extends beyond the fabric itself. With vertically integrated manufacturing, a strong global footprint that includes five U.S. plants and a customer-focused service model that extends beyond the sale, Sunbrella helps provide the consistency, responsiveness and operational stability retailers need in an uncertain market.
Our big story for the remainder of 2026 is the Crypton Toolkit and the opportunity it creates for retailers to make performance easier to sell.
The Toolkit brings together 25 of Crypton’s best-selling textures, patterns and colorways products that have already proven themselves in leading retail showrooms. The assortment spans linens, velvets, bouclé’s and chenille’s, all powered by Crypton performance, and will be available across participating furniture manufacturer brands.
We believe this gives retailers something very practical in a challenging market: a curated assortment of commercially proven fabrics rather than an overwhelming number of choices. These are products that combine the design aesthetic consumers want with the performance they increasingly expect.
We’re also extending the program beyond the product itself. We want to be a true partner to our retail customers, providing Crypton education, in-store lunch-and-learns, events and sales support to help associates understand and confidently communicate the value of performance to the consumer.
At the end of the day, retailers don’t need more complexity. They need products that give consumers a reason to buy and partners who can help them sell.
