# Everything up for Williams-Sonoma in second quarter

Source: [home_textiles_today](https://www.hometextilestoday.com/financial-results/everything-up-for-williams-sonoma-in-second-quarter-q2/)

Thomas Lester// Retail Editor//August 26, 2026
San Francisco — Powered by its home furnishings brands, Williams-Sonoma Inc. posted gains across its key metrics for the second quarter of FY2026.
“We delivered a very strong second quarter. In Q2, our comp came in at 6.2%, with total revenue growth of 6.7%, and we drove an operating margin of 17.3% with earnings per share of $2.10. Every brand delivered again in the quarter, driven by strong execution across our brands, our channels, and our team,” said Laura Alber, president and CEO.
For the three months ended Aug. 2, the company, which owns and operates Pottery Barn and West Elm, reported net revenues of $1.96 billion, up 6.7% compared with $1.84 billion in the same quarter of 2025. Additionally, gross profit increased from 47.1% to 51.6%.
By category, Pottery Barn totaled $770.8 million in the quarter while West Elm added $496.3 million to the total. Between them, those brands accounted for almost two thirds of the net revenues.
Net earnings for the period came in at $338.1 million, or $2.84 per diluted share, an increase of 36.58% against $247.6 million, or $2 per diluted share.
Regarding IEEPA tariff refunds, Williams-Sonoma reported that substantially all of its initial refund claim of $197.8 million has been collected as of Aug. 2, with a remaining tariff refund receivable of $3.2 million.
Looking ahead, the retailer is raising its fiscal 2026 guidance to reflect its strong performance year-to-date, with the assumptions that all tariffs currently in place remain, oil prices remain elevated and no additional benefits come from tariff refunds or related interest. In fiscal 2026, Williams-Sonoma now expects annual net revenues in the range of +4.7% to +7.2%, with comps in the range of +4% to +6.5%; and an operating margin, on a non-GAAP basis, between 17.8% to 18.2%.
“Our strategies continue to gain momentum, and our results reflect the power of our execution. We gained market share, continued to outperform the industry, and raised our annual outlook on both the top and bottom lines,” Alber said. “We are delivering compounding results despite the housing market and other macroeconomic events, and we remain confident in our priorities and plans for the remainder of 2026 and beyond.”
