# Heritage brand Bombay on the hunt for home licensees

Source: [home_textiles_today](https://www.hometextilestoday.com/lighting/earthbound-brands-license-bombay-home-products/)

Jennifer Marks// Editor in Chief//August 25, 2026
New York – Earthbound Brands has been named the exclusive licensing agency to bring Bombay into new home and lifestyle categories.
The home furnishings brand was founded in New Orleans in 1978 as a mail order business. Its aesthetic focused on classic, globally inspired designs at accessible prices. In partnership with Earthbound, Bombay will tap into its heritage to create refreshed designs while remaining rooted in craftsmanship.
Bombay’s licensing program will include soft home, home textiles, furniture, accent furniture, lighting, rugs, flooring, candles, pet products and luggage.
“Bombay has spent decades helping consumers create timeless, thoughtfully curated spaces,” said Jeff Cohen, co-founder and CEO of Earthbound Brands. “We’re excited to partner with their team to build on that foundation by creating a cohesive collection of products that bring the brand into every corner of the home.”
The partnership will build on the brand’s current retail presence and will also focus on expanding into new retail channels.
“The brand has been in people’s homes for over forty years, and there’s a lot more ahead of it,” said Jack Saadeh, president of GHP Group, which owns the Bombay brand.
