# Early bird pricing for HTT/HFPA Foundation market gala ends Aug. 31

Source: [home_textiles_today](https://www.hometextilestoday.com/industry-news/early-bird-pricing-for-htt-hfpa-foundation-market-gala-ends-aug-31/)

Jennifer Marks// Editor in Chief//August 24, 2026
New York – Time is running out to nab early bird pricing on tickets for the 2026 HTT/HFPA’s Home Excellence Awards party, which raises funds for the HFPA Foundation’s scholarship program.
The event returns to the Harvard Club of New York on Sept. 16 from 5:30-7:30 p.m. Ticket availability is limited. Early Bird prices of $250 will rise to $275 effective Sept. 1 for non-HFPA members. Ticket prices for HFPA members and donors to the Foundation are set at $200.
For more information or to register, click here.
The September market cocktail gala – co-presented by the HFPA Foundation and HTT – will bestow 5 honors during the event:
The gala event will be a lively two-hour evening affair featuring cocktails, hors d’oeuvres, an introduction of students from scholarship’s beneficiary institutes, and the presentation of the 2026 Home Excellence award to five honorees – including retailers and suppliers.
The HFPA Foundation, established in 1996, has provided scholarships to more than 250 students since its inception. Currently, the Foundation awards scholarships to students enrolled in surface design or home product marketing courses of study at the Fashion Institute of Technology (FIT) and Thomas Jefferson University.
Fuze is the headline sponsor of the HFPA fundraiser. Supima is the Platinum sponsor of the Home Excellence Awards. Cottonworks by Cotton Incorporated and Taram Textiles are the Gold sponsor of the Home Excellence Awards. Lichtenberg is the Pearl sponsor for the scholarship fund; Indo Count, JLL, Loftex and Welspun are Gold sponsors; 1888 Mills, Ciclo and Mohawk are Silver Sponsors.
All proceeds from ticket sales will benefit the HFPA Foundation’s scholarship fund. For more information on the HFPA Foundation see Foundation & Scholarships – HFPA.
