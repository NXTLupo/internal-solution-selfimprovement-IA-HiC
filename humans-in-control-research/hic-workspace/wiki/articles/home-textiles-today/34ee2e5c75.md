# Citi Trends boosts guidance after strong Q2

Source: [home_textiles_today](https://www.hometextilestoday.com/financial-results/citi-trends-boosts-guidance-after-strong-q2/)

Jennifer Marks// Editor in Chief//August 25, 2026
Savannah, Ga. – Off-pricer Citi Trends Inc. marked its eighth consecutive quarter of comp store sales growth during the second quarter.
Sales climbed 10.9% to $211.6 million during the quarter ended Aug. 1. Comps rose 10.5%, boosted by growth in both average basket and transaction count.
Gross margin increased 60 basis points to 40.6%. The company credited improved merchandise margin and investments to reduce shrink, slightly offset by higher freight due to increased fuel surcharges.
On the bottom line, adjusted net income improved to $400,000 (or $0.05 per share) and adjusted EBITDA reached $5.5 million. However, because last year’s second quarter benefited from the $11.0 million the company took in on the sale of its Savannah office building, Citi Trends reported a net loss of $931,000 (or $0.11 per share).
Citi Trends ended the quarter with 594 stores after opened three net new locations during the period. The company is now paring down this year’s store opening plan, expecting to opened 20 new stores rather than 25.
Instead, the chain will invest in an additional 10 to 15 remodels on exiting stores. So far, Citi Trends has completed 51 remodels this year. It originally planned to revamp 50 stores for the full year.
“We remain focused on consistent execution, strong sales flow-through to profit, and disciplined growth,” said Ken Seipel, chairman and CEO.
Following the strong Q2 results, Citi Trends raised its full-year guidance:
Comp store sales: Raised to a growth range of 9% to 11%, up from the previous 8% to 10%.
Total sales: Raised to a range of 10% to 12%, up from the prior range of 9% to 11%
Adjusted EBITDA: Raised to a range of $38 million to $42 million, up from the prior range of $35 million to $40 million.
“With continued momentum in our merchandise strategy, launch of our new Insiders Club customer relationship management platform, a growing new-store pipeline, and a strong, debt-free balance sheet, we believe Citi Trends is increasingly well positioned to accelerate profitable growth and create meaningful long-term shareholder value,” said Seipel.
