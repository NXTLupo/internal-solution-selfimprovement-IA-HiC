# Strada Education Foundation Research

Source: [strada_research](https://stradaeducation.org/research/)

Actionable research
Together with our partners, we are asking the hard questions and sharing promising solutions.
Strada’s research is human-centered and action-oriented. Understanding what learners need to realize their career goals and what employers need to identify and develop talent are the first steps in making education-to-employment pathways more equitable.
Where we focus
Strada’s five focus areas each represent an important dimension of the support, collaboration, and alignment needed to connect education to opportunity. Our research in these five areas supports efforts to:
- Lift up new approaches to career coaching and work-based learning.
- Strengthen data systems that enable students to make informed choices about where and what to study.
- Engage with employers to ensure alignment with education providers.
- Bring stakeholders to the table to make postsecondary education more affordable.
Our research reports
The goal of Strada research is to help answer hard questions about postsecondary success and economic opportunity. We seek a better understanding of what students need to realize their career goals, and what employers need to build a robust talent pipeline.
Institute for the Future of Work
Research from Strada Institute for the Future of Work provides actionable insights on how to strengthen the educational pathways that lead to economic opportunity for individuals and respond to the talent needs of employers. Its research focuses on the future of work, the future of postsecondary education and training, and talent-driven economic development.
How we use our research
Strada’s research is at the heart of our work. We test and provide evidence about promising solutions and support the work of our education and employer partners. The research we do and support is helping us:
- Inform our strategic priorities and guide our mission-aligned investments and grantmaking.
- Advocate for policy solutions that are nonpartisan and evidence-based.
- Build evidence for education and employment pathways that are better connected and affordable for all current and future students.
State Opportunity Index
Designed to guide state progress in five focus areas, the State Opportunity Index helps states assess how well they are leveraging education pathways — including degrees, certificates, and other credentials — to strengthen workforce competitiveness and pathways to opportunity.
Talent Disrupted
Using a combination of online career histories of tens of millions of graduates, as well as census microdata for millions of graduates, the report offers a comprehensive picture of how college graduates fare in the job market over their first decade of employment after college.
Building Better Internships
Strada’s report, Building Better Internships, looks at the latest findings from the National Survey of College Internships (NSCI), a survey developed by the University of Wisconsin-Madison’s Center for Research on College-Workforce Transitions.
