# Monevium Ltd enters special administration

Source: [ins_fca_uk](https://www.fca.org.uk/news/news-stories/monevium-ltd-enters-special-administration)

On 18 June 2026, Monevium Ltd (Monevium) entered special administration. Adam Henry Stephens and Christopher Allen of S&W Partners LLP (S&W) were appointed as special administrators.
Monevium is authorised by the FCA to provide payment services.
On 28 February 2024, Monevium agreed to a voluntary undertaking, which restricted the activities it can carry out.
Who to contact
The special administrators are responsible for managing customer claims against the firm and returning funds to customers where possible.
If you have any questions about the administration process, please contact the special administrators:
- Email: [email protected]
- Telephone: +44 (0) 20 4617 5500
- Website: https://www.swgroup.com/services/restructuring-services/monevium/
As the firm continues to be authorised by us, we will engage with the special administrators and seek the best outcome for customers.
