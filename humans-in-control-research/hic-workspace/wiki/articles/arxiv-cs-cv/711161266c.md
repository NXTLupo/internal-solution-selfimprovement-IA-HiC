# What Does TERRA-REF's High Resolution, Multi Sensor Plant Sensing Public Domain Data Offer the Computer Vision Community?

Source: [arxiv_cs_cv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.14072v2)

A core objective of the TERRA-REF project was to generate an open-access reference dataset for the evaluation of sensing technologies to study plants under field conditions. The TERRA-REF program deployed a suite of high-resolution, cutting edge technology sensors on a gantry system with the aim of scanning 1 hectare (10$^4$) at around 1 mm$^2$ spatial resolution multiple times per week.
