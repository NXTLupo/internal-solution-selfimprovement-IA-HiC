# Machine-based Multimodal Pain Assessment Tool for Infants: A Review

Source: [arxiv_cs_cv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1607.00331v3)

Bedside caregivers assess infants' pain at constant intervals by observing specific behavioral and physiological signs of pain. This standard has two main limitations. The first limitation is the intermittent assessment of pain, which might lead to missing pain when the infants are left unattended. Second, it is inconsistent since it depends on the observer's subjective judgment and differs between observers.
