Source: [arxiv_cs_cv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18176v1)

This report presents MARS, short for Multimodal Agentic Reasoning with Source selection, our system for the CASTLE Challenge at EgoVis 2026. Participants must answer 185 closed-form questions over the CASTLE 2024 dataset.
