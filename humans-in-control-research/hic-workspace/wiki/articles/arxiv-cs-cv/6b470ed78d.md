# WiCV 2019: The Sixth Women In Computer Vision Workshop

Source: [arxiv_cs_cv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.10225v1)

In this paper we present the Women in Computer Vision Workshop - WiCV 2019, organized in conjunction with CVPR 2019. This event is meant for increasing the visibility and inclusion of women researchers in the computer vision field. Computer vision and machine learning have made incredible progress over the past years, but the number of female researchers is still low both in academia and in industry. WiCV is organized especially for the following reason: to raise visibility of female researchers
