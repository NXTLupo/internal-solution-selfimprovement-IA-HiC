# The Eleventh NTIRE 2026 Efficient Super-Resolution Challenge Report

Source: [arxiv_cs_cv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.03198v1)

This paper reviews the NTIRE 2026 challenge on efficient single-image super-resolution with a focus on the proposed solutions and results. The aim of this challenge is to devise a network that reduces one or several aspects, such as runtime, parameters, and FLOPs, while maintaining PSNR of around 26.90 dB on the DIV2K_LSDIR_valid dataset, and 26.99 dB on the DIV2K_LSDIR_test dataset.
