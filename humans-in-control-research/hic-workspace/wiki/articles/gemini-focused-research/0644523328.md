# AI Research (gemini): Medforth higher education AI enrollment workflows

Source: [gemini (focused research)](deepresearch://1efc3bd501099a)

Based on a review of institutional evidence, executive recruitment data, and system integrations, here is an analysis of Medforth Global Education’s (MGE) approach to higher education AI enrollment workflows. 

---

### 1. Key Findings Organized by Theme

#### Theme A: Strategic Mandate for AI Integration & Agentic Solutions
* **Creation of a Marketing Capabilities Center of Excellence (CoE):** MGE—the parent platform for St. George’s University (SGU) and Rocky Vista University (RVU)—is restructuring its marketing and enrollment operations. The company has initiated recruitment for a **Vice President of Marketing Capabilities & Transformation**. This executive is strategically tasked with building and leading a CoE dedicated to marketing technology, digital platforms, and AI-enabled engagement across MGE's multi-billion-dollar portfolio.
* **Shift toward Agentic AI Workflows:** A primary operational priority for MGE is **deploying AI and agentic solutions** designed to proactively guide prospective students from inquiry to application. 
* **Reducing Call Center and App Reliance:** The strategic business objective of these agentic workflows is explicitly defined: to enhance the student journey **while reducing MGE's overall reliance on traditional digital enrollment applications and physical call center models**.
* **Governance and Process Redesign:** The leadership model positions the CoE as the "executive sponsor" for AI transformation, establishing clear utilization roadmaps, compliance protocols, vendor evaluations, and systematic workflow redesigns. 

#### Theme B: Core Technology Stack & Systems Integration
* **Salesforce CRM Ecosystem:** Salesforce is the foundational system of record for all Medforth enrollment operations. The CRM is used to track applicant progress, record touchpoints, and document enrollment-step progression.
* **Core ERP and Document Systems:** Salesforce is integrated with major higher-education enterprise systems, notably **Ellucian Banner** (student information system) and **OnBase** (for document imaging and management). 
* **Data Integration for Full-Funnel Visibility:** MGE has historically integrated **Salesforce Marketing Cloud, Datorama, and Tableau** with its core CRM and data warehouse. This stack provides comprehensive, cross-brand data visualizations of the prospective student lifecycle.
* **Administrative and Finance AI Workflows:** To eliminate friction in background enrollment and financial operations, MGE utilizes **AppZen's Finance AI**. Across higher-education deployments, this specific integration has been shown to deliver **50% to 80% straight-through processing** on routine administrative documents and **30% to 70% faster processing cycle times**.

#### Theme C: Human-AI Collaboration & the "Pod" Model
* **The "Pod" Enrollment Structure:** Human admissions and enrollment operations at MGE are structured around collaborative, cross-functional "Pods". Each Pod operates as a cohesive unit to transition students down the funnel and consists of:
  1. An Admissions Officer
  2. An Admissions Coordinator
  3. A Recruiter
  4. A Financial Aid / Institutional Student Financial Assistance Specialist
* **Operational Flow Steps:**
  * *Data Intake & Validation:* Managed by the *Admissions Data & Processing Coordinator*, who acts as the first line of validation. This role manages duplicate record checks in Salesforce, indexes digital documents, and cleans data before forwarding files to the Pod.
  * *Enrollment Step Progression:* The *Admissions Coordinator* monitors file progression, guides deposited students through critical onboarding tasks (financial aid, housing, travel, registration, orientation), and lifts holds.
* **Strict Performance SLAs:** The human-AI workflows are bound by rigid response times, with established service standards requiring the team to respond to prospective student inquiries within **24 to 48 hours**. Automated workflows are strategically utilized to relieve staff of manual data-entry tasks to ensure this threshold is met.
