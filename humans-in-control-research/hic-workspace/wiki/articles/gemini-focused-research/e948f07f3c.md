# AI Research (gemini): NXT Humans Intelligence Augmentation CX deployment

Source: [gemini (focused research)](deepresearch://16c418aa6f037c)

### Executive Summary
NXT Humans is an AI-native advisory and creative agency co-founded in August 2024 in Philadelphia. The firm is built on the principle of "Intelligence Augmentation" (IA)—using AI systems to enhance rather than replace human capabilities. 

By bridging the gap between high-level strategy and technical execution, NXT Humans helps mid-market and enterprise clients implement generative AI and autonomous agents into customer experience (CX) workflows. Their deployment strategy leverages proprietary platforms—such as the **GenAI Co-Lab** and a **Synthetic-Customer Simulator**—and prioritizes human-centered design through partnerships with firms like Think Company.

---

### Key Findings by Theme

#### 1. Firm Profile & Strategic Positioning
*   **Organizational Scale:** Headquartered in Philadelphia, NXT Humans is a boutique agency comprised of approximately 10 to 20 consultants, designers, data scientists, and engineers. As of 2026, the unfunded startup operates with an estimated annual revenue of $1,283,325 and an estimated valuation of $4.2 million.
*   **Leadership Team:** The agency was co-founded by industry veterans Andrew Fegley, Robert Koch, and Jonathan Lupo, who collectively bring over 25 years of experience in digital strategy, CX, and innovation. The technical pipeline is overseen by CTO Shane Brennan, with creative execution led by AI Creative Director Delaney Gibson Moon.
*   **Market Position:** NXT Humans positions itself as the practical bridge between "strategy houses that stop at slideware and software studios that lack boardroom fluency". Rather than billing by hours, they operate on outcome-priced "growth sprints" and build agent prototypes that clients retain ownership of.

#### 2. Proprietary IA & CX Technology Stack
To support human-centric customer experience design, NXT Humans deploys two main proprietary AI structures:
*   **GenAI Co-Lab Platform:** A secured Microsoft Azure workspace housing fine-tuned Large Language Models (LLMs), proprietary CX datasets, and reusable prompt libraries. For every client engagement, NXT Humans spawns a dedicated Co-Lab instance to collaborate on real-time data analysis, concept drafts, and prototype builds alongside client stakeholders.
*   **Synthetic-Customer Simulator:** A multi-agent simulation environment that generates thousands of persona-level journeys and "what-if" consumer behaviors. Consultants and client teams query these simulated cohorts to stress-test messaging, landing pages, marketing funnels, and service blueprints before deploying them in the real world.

#### 3. Real-World CX and Decision-Support Deployments
NXT Humans has applied its intelligence augmentation model across healthcare, financial services, and higher education:
*   **Critical Care Bedside Copilot (Healthcare/Clinical CX):** On July 29, 2025, NXT Humans announced a strategic partnership with Moberg Analytics to deploy bedside generative AI for brain-injured patients. Driven by prior funding from the Department of Defense (DoD), this partnership integrates NXT Humans' generative AI with Moberg’s advanced multimodal brain monitoring platform. The resulting *Critical Care Copilot* translates massive volumes of complex neurophysiological data into real-time decision-support alerts, shifting ICU clinical teams from reactive monitoring to proactive, lifesaving patient management.
*   **U.S. Bank Simulator (Financial Services CX):** NXT Humans deployed its *Synthetic-Customer Simulator* for a major U.S. bank. By building and querying thousands of persona-based financial journeys, the agency mapped customer friction points and optimized the bank's marketing funnels, localized communication strategies, and digital onboarding experience.
*   **AI-Driven Student Experience (Higher Education CX):** NXT Humans led strategic research and product definition for an AI-enabled digital student experience at a leading New England university. Using rapid, AI-driven prototyping tools (such as NotebookLM, Claude, and Replit), they established a continuous feedback loop between university leaders, academic experts, and students to build a personalized, multimodal campus ecosystem.
*   **Direct-to-Patient Primary Care (DPC Platform):** Led by NXT Humans' design team, the agency developed branding and prototyped AI-augmented patient and provider experiences for an emerging DPC healthcare platform, optimizing the virtual patient-onboarding journey.
*   **Agentic Knowledge Hub (Life Sciences CX):** The agency deployed an agentic knowledge hub for a life sciences enterprise. This internal deployment was designed to translate complex data structures into an actionable knowledge repository, reducing the administrative burden on employees and improving internal user experience.

#### 4. Strategic Partnerships for Delivery
*   **Think Company Alliance:** Launched on August 8, 2024, this partnership merges Think Company’s two decades of digital experience design, product design, and CX expertise with NXT Humans' technical advisory, AI, and data analysis talent. The collaboration focuses on ensuring complex enterprise AI systems remain human-friendly, highly accessible, and aligned with user-centered design paradigms.
