# Capital Projects Dashboard - Citywide Budget and Schedule

Source: [nyc_open_data](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/City-Government/Capital-Projects-Dashboard-Citywide-Budget-and-Sch/fb86-vt7u)

All major capital infrastructure projects with a committed budget. Financial information and agency schedule details for each project are joined via FMS ID. Only projects in the design phase or later have project schedules displayed. This dataset is part of the <a href="https://data.cityofnewyork.us/browse?Data-Collection_Data-Collection=Capital+Projects+Dashboard">Capital Projects Dashboard</a>.
