# Capital Projects Dashboard - Citywide Budget and Spend by Fiscal Year

Source: [nyc_open_data](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/City-Government/Capital-Projects-Dashboard-Citywide-Budget-and-Spe/gyhf-rsr3)

All major capital infrastructure projects with a committed budget and spend by fiscal year. Financial information and agency schedule details for each project are joined via FMS ID. Only projects in the design phase or later have project schedules displayed. This dataset is part of the <a href="https://data.cityofnewyork.us/browse?Data-Collection_Data-Collection=Capital+Projects+Dashboard">Capital Projects Dashboard</a>.

Note: Each row is uniquely identified by its Financial Management Servic
