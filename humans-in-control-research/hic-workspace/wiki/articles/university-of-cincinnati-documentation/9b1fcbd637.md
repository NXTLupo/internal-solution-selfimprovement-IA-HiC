# Student Teaching - Campus Life | University of Cincinnati

Source: [University of Cincinnati documentation](https://www.uc.edu/campus-life/career-co-op-support/gain-experience/student-teaching.html)

Student Teaching
In student teaching, students who are preparing to become teachers practice their emerging skills in a K-12 classroom under the guidance of a mentor teacher.
Student teaching is a long-established practice in undergraduate education programs.
At UC, student teaching experiences and assessment are managed by the home college.
