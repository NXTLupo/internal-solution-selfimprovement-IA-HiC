# 100 Days

Source: [logo_design_love](https://www.logodesignlove.com/100-days)

Each year, the students of SVA’s Masters in Branding program spend 100 days documenting their process with a chosen creative endeavour.
This year, Print Mag has been showcasing each student. Here are a few 100 Days projects that stood out from a logo perspective.
Kaylin Ingram’s fun Brand Castles is a hint at the ephemeral nature of business, like castles in the sand. Follow on Instagram.
Marisa Goldberg takes a craft approach with The Brand Blanket, knitting 100 logos. Almost finished on Instagram.
Brand What Ifs is a series by Deane Cruz who wonders what well-known brands would look like if they branched into new markets. Also updating through Instagram.
More projects from the Masters in Branding students on Print Magazine’s SVA 100 Days page.
