# Reducing Development Time with AI Tools: Enhancing Human-AI Collaboration in Software Development

Source: [Crossref (150M DOIs)]({'URL': 'https://lorojournals.com/index.php/emsj/article/download/1424/1382', 'content-type': 'application/pdf', 'content-version': 'vor', 'intended-application': 'text-mining'}, {'URL': 'https://lorojournals.com/index.php/emsj/article/download/1424/1382', 'content-type': 'unspecified', 'content-version': 'vor', 'intended-application': 'similarity-checking'})

<jats:p>Software development teams are under an increased amount of pressure in the era of rapid software development, where there is more and more pressure to complete complicated software projects within increasingly shorter timelines. Through this holistic investigation, the article is going to explore how the use of artificial intelligence tools is changing old development methods in all stages of the software development process. Whether it is mockup drawings or ultimate deployment, these intelligent companions generate a new way through which programmers imagine, develop, and support the applications. The glaring benefits of using these digital collaborators have been evidenced by the high quality and the rate at which teams harness their development. In addition to pointing out technical benefits, this article addresses the practical challenges faced by an organization when implementing it, e.g., the complexities of integrating a new system and the inherent human propensity to be less likely to adopt new practices to change old ones. The article presents practical ideas on how to overcome such challenges, noting that it is advisable to adopt them gradually, have clear limits, allow for appropriate training, and ensure it is measured meaningfully. In the future, new technologies are predicted to offer a more advanced level of support thanks to further context awareness, more automation of tasks, and the cooperation model that draws on human creativity and machine precision to make software that cannot be produced separately.</jats:p>
