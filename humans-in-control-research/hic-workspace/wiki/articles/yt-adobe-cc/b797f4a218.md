# Make festival promo assets with Adam Wright using Adobe Illustrator! #shorts

Source: [yt_adobe_cc](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/PVXs_kyGu8g)

Hey, I'm Adam Wright and I'm a brand identity designer and illustrator. I'm creating a promotional poster for an epic pop-up local festival, and I'm working to a deadline. So I need several sizes and variations of that design fast. Normally that means extending edges, fixing those awkward gaps, trying to keep everything looking consistent. And that's a really repetitive part of my workflow. And it takes away from refining the finished design. With generative expand in Adobe Illustrator, I can extend the artwork itself, not just the artboard, while keeping the style and composition intact. Then with Turntable, I can change perspective inside the poster and quickly create new compositions and brand assets from the same illustration, which is important because every asset needs to feel consistent across the festival campaign. Drop in the comments how you use Adobe Illustrator to take one design through to the finished campaign.
