# Watch Candyce Richardson bring her shelf design to life in Adobe Illustrator!

Source: [yt_adobe_cc](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/549qsmqtpz8)

Hi, I'm Candice and I create whimsical displays that help small businesses showcase their products. For this shopping bag shelf, I started with a quick hand sketch before bringing it into Adobe Illustrator using Sketch to Vector and notated what I envisioned. I refined the curves with the direct selection tool, adjusted the colors and details, and explored a few variations until I had a clear concept to build from. Having an editable vector made it easy to fine-tune the design before bringing it to life. If you have an idea waiting in your sketchbook, try Adobe Illustrator today.
