# People Are Putting Tiny Skirts on Their Wine Glasses — and I’m Charmed

Source: [apartment_therapy](https://www.apartmenttherapy.com/skirted-wine-glasses-trend-instagram-37686911?utm_source=RSS&utm_medium=feed&utm_campaign=Category%2FChannel%3A+main)

People Are Putting Tiny Skirts on Their Wine Glasses — and I’m Completely Charmed
If you love hosting dinner parties or cocktail hours, you may have already hopped on the wine glass trend that involves cutesy stem charms. These little decorations help your guests keep track of their glasses and limit accidental swapping. But one Instagram user came up with a new way to dress up wine glasses that not only helps guests keep track of them, but also makes them so beautiful and totally whimsical (in a way that truly leans full cottagecore!).
“Even the wine glasses dressed up,” Dehanne Knee wrote in a caption of a recent post she shared, showcasing an ingenious DIY decor hack. “This paper doily wine glass skirt takes less than a minute to make, but it’s the kind of little detail that makes a table feel extra special. Save this idea for your next dinner party, bridal shower, or celebration.”
How to Jump on This Skirted Glass Trend at Home
This little decor twist for your next happy hour soirée is so easy, I think I honestly could do it with just a few minutes to spare before guests arrive. It all revolves around the classic paper doily, which can be sourced in a small pack, like this one from Michaels. They should be about the same size as the base of your wine glasses, but it’s OK if they’re a bit larger — this will just add to the dramatic effect!
From there, you’ll need to do the following:
- Cut a slit into the center of each doily using scissors.
- Then cut a small hole in the center of the doily for the stem of your wine glass. Don’t worry about being too exact with this.
- Punch two holes into the doily on each side of the slit you cut.
- Write your guest’s name on the doily. You can get creative with your penmanship or use fun markers or pencils to add a pop of color!
- Slip the doily onto your wine glass and use a decorative ribbon or length of twine to secure it in place using the holes you punched.
There Are Countless Ways to Bring the Skirted Decor Trend into Your Kitchen
You can decorate your doilies with markers, pencils, or even watercolor paints before attaching them to your wine glass. And you can further customize the skirt for each guest by using a variety of different ribbons or yarns as the skirt ties. Or, stamp your guest’s name with alphabet letter stamps rather than writing. There are so many ways to branch off and make this DIY unique to you. And if you’re careful (no spills!), you can save the skirts to use again at the next function.
A little DIY can go a long way toward giving your guests a memorable experience and creating a tablescape that’s unforgettable. Give this doily wine glass skirt DIY a try, and get ready to receive lots of oohs and aahs from your friends and family.
