# Elevate, the campaign for Northern Arizona University

Source: [YouTube: NAU Advancement | Foundation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UVWLJvwjbVE)

In a state where nature is harsh and water is scarce, research happens. In a landscape defined by transformation, change happens. In a place filled with history, learning happens. Atop the Colorado Plateau, beneath a sacred mountain, in a pine forest surrounded by desert, is Flagstaff and Northern Arizona University. NAU is perfectly located at the forefront of education and opportunity. Our mission is clear. We aim to be the nation's preeminent engine of opportunity, vehicle of economic mobility, and driver of social impact, positioning our graduates well for careers of consequence and lives of purpose. I'm first generation. I come from a place where those resources were limited. Scholarships are the main reason why I'm even here to begin with. It helped me finally be able to reach my dream of attending NAU. Every time I visit NAU, I feel this sense of energy and Lumberjack spirit that is truly unique. Here we are giving students the tools to understand, adapt, and improve the way that we interact with our environment. What we're looking at is to prepare teachers for our schools, to make sure that we have quality teachers in all classrooms. Coming to the Sanguine College of Engineering, there's a natural place for social mobility and expanding access. I see all the opportunities and I see all the great things that this university will be. What we are really doing here at NAU is targeting opportunities through collaboration to strengthen and leverage opportunities for Native American students and their career pathways. We are experiencing a national shortage in nursing, so we are excited to be able to be part of the solution in addressing that shortage. We advance public educational programs and scholarly endeavors based on understanding our past and through intercultural conversations. The work being done here impacts the world. It changes the lives of students and their families and shapes our future. Whether you give your time, talent, or testimony, you can help build a future where every student has the opportunity to succeed. It's important to help underrepresented individuals to better themselves because you never know what potential they might have. Together, we help advance this knowledge for the benefit of our Southwestern community. This increases academic achievement of Native American students broadly. Students feel like they can do anything. They can come to an institution like this. They can learn something that is of great value to our society and they can be successful and off to make a difference in the world. Because of your commitment to supporting our students, we are building a brighter future for our students, strengthening Arizona's workforce, and laying the foundation for lasting impact in our communities. Thank you. For elevating. For elevating excellence. At NAU. Together, we elevate Northern Arizona University.
