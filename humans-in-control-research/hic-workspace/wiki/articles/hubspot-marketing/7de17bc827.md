# HubSpot AEO vs. Ahrefs Brand Radar: Features compared [2026]

Source: [hubspot_marketing](https://blog.hubspot.com/marketing/hubspot-vs-ahrefs-aeo)

As more buyers skip search entirely and go straight to ChatGPT, Gemini, or Perplexity for recommendations, marketers are realizing they need a new kind of tool — one that shows them how their brand appears in AI answers and what to do about it. That’s what both HubSpot AEO and Ahrefs Brand Radar are built for. But they take meaningfully different approaches, and the right choice depends almost entirely on what you need to do after you get the data.
In this post, we’ll break down how each tool works, compare them across the features that matter most to buyers, and give you a clear framework for choosing between them.
We’ll also flag one common source of confusion upfront: HubSpot’s free AEO Grader is a separate diagnostic tool, not the product we’re comparing here. HubSpot AEO is the full platform, and this post covers it.

HubSpot AEO vs. Ahrefs Brand Radar at a Glance
Quick verdict: HubSpot AEO wins on workflow integration and actionability. Ahrefs Brand Radar covers platform breadth and prompt volume. If you’re an Ahrefs shop that just needs visibility monitoring bolted on, Brand Radar is a good choice. If you need to actually move the needle on your AI presence and can’t afford the gap between “insight” and “shipped content,” HubSpot AEO is the stronger fit.
What HubSpot AEO and Ahrefs Brand Radar Actually Do
HubSpot AEO tracks your brand’s visibility across ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity, then connects that data directly to the content tools you need to act on it. The key differentiator is the workflow: when you spot a visibility gap, you don’t export a CSV and open a new tab. You act on the recommendation inside HubSpot. That could mean writing a blog post, publishing a LinkedIn update, or engaging on Reddit, all from the same platform.
The CRM connection is meaningful, too. Instead of manually entering prompts to track, HubSpot suggests them based on what it already knows about your business, customers, and competitors. That type of connectivity is a real time-saver, and it means you’re tracking the right questions from day one.
Ahrefs Brand Radar approaches AI visibility as an extension of everything Ahrefs already does well: massive data, broad coverage, and a monitoring-first philosophy. It tracks brand mentions across seven AI platforms (more than any other tool we looked at), and its prompt database of 406 million monthly queries is built from real user searches rather than synthetic data. If you want to know which platforms are mentioning you, how you stack up against competitors, and what sources are driving AI citations, Brand Radar covers that thoroughly.
It also tracks visibility on YouTube, Reddit, and TikTok (free while in beta), which is a nice bonus for brands that care about presence beyond pure AI answer engines. And for teams that want to pull that data into their AI workflows, it has an MCP server that lets you query Ahrefs data directly inside Claude or ChatGPT.
HubSpot AEO vs. Ahrefs Brand Radar: Features Compared
Which AI Engines and Prompts Are Tracked
This is probably the starkest difference between the two tools.
HubSpot AEO tracks ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity. Those three cover the majority of AI search volume right now, but if your category requires coverage of Microsoft Copilot, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, or Grok, you’ll hit a ceiling.
Ahrefs Brand Radar tracks seven platforms: AI Overviews, AI Mode, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot, Gemini, and Grok. If breadth of engine coverage is a hard requirement, Brand Radar has the advantage.
On the prompt side, HubSpot uses your CRM data to suggest which prompts to track. You’re not starting from a blank slate, and the suggestions improve as your business data evolves. Ahrefs has a database of 406 million monthly prompts derived from real search queries, which is an impressive dataset. You can also add custom prompts in both tools, though Brand Radar’s custom prompt limits vary significantly by plan (from 5 prompts on Lite up to 2,500+ on Enterprise standalone).
Citations, Sources, and Sentiment
Both tools offer citation analysis and sentiment tracking, but they surface the data differently.
HubSpot AEO’s citation analysis shows you which domains, URLs, and content types are driving AI mentions, broken down by source type (owned content, competitor content, third-party, social, affiliate) and content type (blog, news, product, social). Sentiment is scored on a scale of -100 to +100, so you’re not just seeing whether you’re mentioned but whether those mentions are doing you any good.
Ahrefs Brand Radar tracks citations across its full set of AI platforms and also pulls in coverage from YouTube, Reddit, and TikTok. That social layer gives brand teams a more complete picture of where they’re being referenced.
For teams that need to understand the full ecosystem of content influencing their AI presence, Brand Radar’s multi-surface tracking is a meaningful addition.
Historical Trends and Reporting
HubSpot AEO shows how your brand visibility score trends over time across each engine, and ties that visibility data directly to your campaign metrics. So when your AEO score improves after a content push, you can actually connect those dots inside the same platform rather than correlating across tools.
Ahrefs Brand Radar provides historical trend data across its engine set. Because it’s integrated with the broader Ahrefs suite, teams on full Ahrefs plans can layer AI visibility trends alongside their traditional SEO metrics (organic traffic, backlinks, rankings) for a unified view of search performance.
Which one turns insights into shipped content faster?
This is where the two tools genuinely diverge, and it’s the question that should drive most buying decisions.
From Visibility Gap to Brief
In HubSpot AEO, when your data shows a visibility gap (say, a competitor is consistently appearing for “best [your category] for small teams,” and you’re not), the tool surfaces a prioritized recommendation. That recommendation might say: write a blog post that targets this prompt, update this existing page, or publish a LinkedIn post on this topic. It’s not just data — it’s a next step.
In Ahrefs Brand Radar, you get the same visibility gap data, but the path from insight to action is your responsibility. You’ll need to incorporate that information into your content workflow, create the brief, assign the work, and track progress in whatever system you use.
From Draft to Publish to Measure
Because HubSpot AEO lives inside Marketing Hub, you can act on a recommendation without switching tabs. Blog posts, social posts, and content updates are all native to the platform. Once you publish, the AEO data updates as AI mentions shift, and your campaign analytics are right there alongside it.
With Ahrefs Brand Radar, publishing happens wherever your team already publishes. The measurement loop comes back to Brand Radar for the AI visibility side, but you’d need to pull in other data sources to see the full picture of impact.
For teams that already have a well-oiled content workflow and just need better monitoring data to feed it, this isn’t a dealbreaker. For teams that are still building that workflow or that want fewer tools in the stack, HubSpot’s integrated approach saves meaningful time.
HubSpot AEO vs. Ahrefs Brand Radar: Pricing
HubSpot AEO Pricing
HubSpot AEO starts at $50/month (or $45/month if you pay annually). It includes ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity coverage, brand visibility and sentiment analysis, prompt tracking with CRM-powered suggestions, competitor and citation analysis, and prioritized recommendations.
There’s a free 28-day trial with 25 prompts across all three engines, no credit card required. That trial is a low-friction way to see real data before you commit.
If you want AEO built into your full marketing stack, HubSpot for Marketers (Marketing Hub + Content Hub) starts at $900/month and includes AEO alongside the full campaign, content, and social toolkit.
For current pricing, see: hubspot.com/products/aeo
Ahrefs Brand Radar Pricing
Ahrefs doesn’t offer free trials or discounts, so you’re committing upfront.
Standalone Brand Radar (includes an Ahrefs Free account):
- Base entry: $199/month
- Select Platforms: $398/month (pick specific AI engines, plus YouTube, TikTok, Reddit)
- All Platforms: $699/month (full access to the 406M prompt database, plus 2,500 custom prompt checks/month)
- Custom Prompt Only packages: $50/month (2,500 checks), $100/month (7,000 checks), $250/month (25,000 checks)
Bundled in full Ahrefs plans (Brand Radar included):
- Starter: $29/month
- Lite: $129/month ($108/month annually) — 5 custom prompts, 150 checks/month
- Standard: $249/month ($208/month annually) — 10 custom prompts, 300 checks/month
- Advanced: $449/month ($374/month annually) — 20 custom prompts, 600 checks/month
- Enterprise: starts at $1,499/month — custom limits, uncapped API, SSO
The pricing story in plain terms: If you’re already on Ahrefs, adding Brand Radar is part of your existing plan, and the incremental cost is low. If you’re coming in cold and want standalone Brand Radar with full platform coverage, you’re looking at $699/month.
