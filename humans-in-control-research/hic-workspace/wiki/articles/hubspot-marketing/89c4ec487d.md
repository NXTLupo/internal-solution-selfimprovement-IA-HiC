# HubSpot AEO vs. Rank Prompt: Which AI visibility tool should you choose?

Source: [hubspot_marketing](https://blog.hubspot.com/marketing/hubspot-vs-rank-prompt)

AI search is no longer a niche experiment. According to HubSpot’s own research, 42% of buyers now use AI search as part of their evaluation process — and it’s the top predictor of purchase intent. Brands that show up in those answers earn the conversation before a prospect even clicks.
That’s exactly what Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) tools do. They track how your brand appears in AI-generated answers, identify gaps, and give you a clear path to fix them.
Two tools generating real buzz right now: HubSpot AEO and Rank Prompt. Both are purpose-built for AEO. Both track prompt-level visibility. But they serve different teams in different ways.
This guide breaks down the key differences so you can choose the right fit — and get started fast.

What are HubSpot AEO and Rank Prompt?
HubSpot AEO
HubSpot AEO is HubSpot’s native answer engine optimization product, launched in Spring 2026. It monitors your brand association and how it appears across AI tools and agents,
ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity connect those insights directly to your CRM.
Because it lives inside the HubSpot platform, it knows your industries, competitors, and customer segments from day one; no manual setup required.
HubSpot AEO is also available as a standalone product for $50/month (with a free 28-day trial) and is included in the Marketing Hub Pro and Enterprise plans for teams who want it bundled with their full marketing stack.
HubSpot AEO is also part of a larger AEO ecosystem: HubSpot’s free AI Search Grader provides a quick baseline audit, while Breeze AI and Content Hub power content creation and optimization.
Rank Prompt
Rank Prompt is an all-in-one AI search visibility platform covering the complete AEO loop: monitor, improve, and operate. Founded by marketing agency founders who built it as an internal tool before opening it to the public, the platform is now trusted by major brands like Mastercard, P&G, and 7-Eleven.
It tracks six AI platforms (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude, Grok, and Google AI Mode) from a single dashboard.
Rank Prompt combines AI visibility monitoring with a highly actionable execution suite, including:
- AEO Content Studio: Generates 8 distinct types of SEO-optimized articles, including Comparison Articles and Ranked Lists, designed specifically to earn AI citations.
- Citation Outreach: Uses a proprietary Rank Prompt Score (RP Score) to analyze how “reachable” a citation source is, helping you target sites that actually accept contributor content (rather than wasting time pitching unreachable sites like Wikipedia).
- Technical SEO Audits: Run comprehensive checks featuring Google Lighthouse and Core Web Vitals to measure 100+ on-page data points, building on the crawlability foundations covered in technical SEO best practices.
- Agent Mode: An AI-powered copilot that lives in a persistent dock, allowing you to ask visibility questions in plain English, edit drafts in a live canvas, and execute real platform actions.
Both HubSpot AEO and Rank Prompt are purpose-built for AEO — not SEO tools trying to bolt on an AI module. This approach makes them meaningfully better at prompt-level tracking than traditional SEO platforms.

HubSpot AEO vs. Rank Prompt: Pricing and Plans
Not only should a tool fit into your workflow, but it should also fit into your budget. After all, if you’re spending more for a tool than you’re getting back from it, it’s not a wise investment.
HubSpot AEO and Rank Prompt both offer fairly low entry-point pricing, but differ in their offerings. Let’s break them down.
HubSpot AEO Pricing
HubSpot AEO starts at $50/month (or $45/month billed annually), but a free 28-day trial is available with 25 ChatGPT prompts and no credit card required.
For teams already on Marketing Hub Pro or Enterprise, AEO is included — making it essentially free if you’re already paying for the platform.
Rank Prompt Pricing
Rank Prompt’s pricing is a little more complicated.
Rank Prompt uses a flexible credit-based pricing model, where one credit equals one prompt scanned across all six AI platforms simultaneously, though credits can also be spent on AI-generated articles (10 credits), Brand Audits (10 credits), or Lighthouse SEO audits (1 credit).
Pricing starts at $49/month on the Starter plan (or $39.17/month if billed annually), which includes 150 monthly credits and a $5/month Agent Mode budget. Every paid plan comes with a 7-day free trial and 50 free prompts to test the waters.
This credit model is flexible, allowing you to scale up or down without locking into unused capacity, but if you are worried about budgeting for prompt volume fluctuations month to month, Rank Prompt offers one-time credit top-ups (starting at $18 for 50 credits) that never expire and stack with your monthly allocation.
TLDR: Budget-conscious teams and agencies will appreciate Rank Prompt’s flexible credit model and top-up system. HubSpot AEO wins on simplicity and value for teams already invested in the HubSpot ecosystem.
— Pricing and features verified as of July 2026. Always confirm current pricing at hubspot.com/products/aeo and rankprompt.com/pricing before purchasing.
HubSpot AEO vs. Rank Prompt: Integrations and Workflow Fit
HubSpot AEO: Built for the HubSpot Ecosystem
HubSpot AEO’s biggest advantage is what it connects to natively:
- Smart CRM: Prompt suggestions are automatically informed by your contact records, buyer personas, and sales pipeline.
- Marketing Hub: AEO insights feed directly into campaign planning, email, and social workflows.
- Content Hub + Breeze AI: Generate, structure, and publish AEO-optimized content without leaving the platform. Breeze Content Assistant helps draft answer-first content aligned with your brand’s AEO gaps.
- Reporting: Tie AI visibility directly to pipeline stages and revenue for closed-loop attribution.
For a practical overview of how HubSpot’s AEO workflow connects these pieces, see Optimize content and improve brand visibility for AI in the HubSpot Knowledge Base.
Rank Prompt: Built for Stack-agnostic Teams
Rank Prompt integrates with the core tools most marketing teams already use, while adding a few unique connections for AI-specific workflows. For instance:
- GA4 + Google Search Console: Detects and classifies traffic from over 20 AI platforms (including ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Claude) while pulling in GSC queries, clicks, and impressions.
- WordPress & Hosted Blogs: Offers one-click publishing of AI-ready articles with SEO metadata to WordPress, or you can use their fully Hosted Blog (with SSL, CDN, and SEO defaults included).
- Google Business Profile: Manages local AI visibility by offering AI profile optimization, AI-generated review replies in your brand’s voice, and bulk post scheduling.
- ChatGPT Ads / OpenAI Ads: Allows you to connect your OpenAI Ads account to sync campaigns, track conversions, and generate ad creatives with AI.
- Developer API + MCP Server: Connect Rank Prompt to custom tools via the REST API, or use the MCP Server to query live visibility data inside Claude Code, Cursor, or Claude Desktop.
What Rank Prompt doesn’t offer is a CRM integration. If you need to tie AI visibility data to contacts, pipeline, or revenue, you’ll need to build that connection yourself using their API or use a separate platform.
Fortunately, Rank Prompt is building fast. Its MCP Server integration enables technical teams to query live visibility data directly from Claude Code or Cursor, which is useful for engineering-led marketing orgs.
