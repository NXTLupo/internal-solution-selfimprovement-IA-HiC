# AEO for content marketers: How to capture awareness and drive revenue with content

Source: [hubspot_marketing](https://blog.hubspot.com/marketing/aeo-guide-for-content-marketers)

Content marketers are used to publishing consistently, earning trust, and measuring how content contributes to demand. But now, buyers also do their research in AI assistants and answer engines, sometimes without clicking through to a website.
That changes what content teams need to measure, since it’s no longer enough to know whether a page ranks. Content teams also need to know if their brand appears in AI-generated answers, which sources those answers cite, and what happens when AI-referred visitors reach their site.
For content marketers, answer engine optimization (AEO) builds on familiar work: understand what your audience is asking, create useful content that answers those questions, and measure the results.
Start by tracking where your brand appears in AI results.
Start with a baseline that answers three questions:
- Which answer engines mention your brand for prompts that are relevant to your category?
- How do those answers describe your brand?
- Where do competitors appear when your brand doesn’t?
Without that baseline, it’s hard to tell which topics need attention or whether your changes are improving visibility.
HubSpot AEO gives you a brand visibility score across ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity, along with prompt-level tracking and competitor comparisons. Track prompts that reflect questions your target audience might ask, review the responses, and see where competitors appear instead.
Use AEO recommendations to prioritize content opportunities.
Strong AEO content starts with familiar fundamentals: Answer a real question clearly, make the information easy to find, and keep it current. AEO adds a feedback loop by showing which sources answer engines are citing for your tracked prompts.
The recommendations tab in HubSpot AEO uses citation patterns across tracked prompts to identify and prioritize content opportunities. With Marketing Hub Professional and Enterprise, you can act on AEO recommendations directly inside HubSpot’s content tools without switching platforms.
According to HubSpot’s data, customers actively optimizing for AI search generate 170% more marketing qualified leads than comparable customers that aren’t.
The next step is measuring whether AI visibility and AI-referred traffic contribute to demand.
Measure AI referral traffic alongside pipeline.
AI visibility and AI referral traffic aren’t the same things. Suppose a buyer sees your brand in an AI answer but doesn’t click; that exposure is harder to attribute directly. When a buyer does click through from an AI assistant, HubSpot can classify that traffic under AI Referrals.
HubSpot’s Original and Latest Traffic Source properties can show AI Referrals for contacts, and associated deals can mirror those source properties. For teams using AEO in Marketing Hub Professional or Enterprise, AEO data also sits alongside campaign metrics, making it easier to evaluate visibility work with downstream demand.
For content marketers, that creates a practical measurement loop: Track where your brand appears, use citation data to decide what to create or update, and monitor AI-referred traffic alongside the rest of your marketing performance.
