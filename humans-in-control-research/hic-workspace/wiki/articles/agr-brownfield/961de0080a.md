# Heat stress could impact cattle gains as summer temperatures rise

Source: [agr_brownfield](https://www.brownfieldagnews.com/news/heat-stress-could-impact-cattle-gains-as-summer-temperatures-rise/)

News
Heat stress could impact cattle gains as summer temperatures rise
A beef cattle specialist at Ohio State University Extension says high temperatures this summer could create challenges for cattle producers.
Garth Ruff says heavier cattle are more vulnerable to heat stress.
“This could be our heavy feedlot cattle or cattle that have high energy diets,” he says. “For our younger calves, I think the concern is feed intake. We’re likely going to lose a little bit of production during this time of high heat.”
He tells Brownfield proactive management could provide temporary relief.
“We want to provide shade whenever possible,” he says. “We want to make sure we have plenty of clean, fresh water. That water intake can as much as double. Feed at cooler times of the day, early in the morning, late in the evening, and really avoid handling and transporting cattle in the peak of the heat during the afternoon if possible.”
Ruff says crowding near water sources and standing for longer periods of time are often signs of heat stress.
The National Weather Service has issued an extreme heat warning for Illinois, Indiana, Ohio, Kentucky, Michigan, Wisconsin, Missouri, Wisconsin, and Iowa is in effect through Thursday evening.
AUDIO: Garth Ruff, Ohio State Extension
