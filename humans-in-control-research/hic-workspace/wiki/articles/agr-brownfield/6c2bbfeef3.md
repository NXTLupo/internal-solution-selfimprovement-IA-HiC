# SW Minnesota agronomist gives crops an ‘A’

News
SW Minnesota agronomist gives crops an ‘A’
An agronomist in southwest Minnesota is giving crops high marks.
Jacob Foley is with DEKALB/Asgrow.
“I’d give this year so far, I’d give it an ‘A’ I think.”
He says crop development is ahead of normal.
“We’ve had excess heat (and) enough moisture, that’s maybe the one area where things have been maybe a little short in some spots. But just recently we’ve kind of got caught up on that I think.”
Foley recently traveled through Iowa and Illinois and tells Brownfield some of those crops have had too much rain.
“And not enough nitrogen because they’ve leached it out or denitrified it. So I think the southern part of Minnesota is fairly lucky now this year with how our weather has progressed, we’re sitting in a good spot.”
He says there’s a lot of potential with this year’s crop and encourages growers to take steps to preserve yield.
