# Falling farm income pressuring crop sector

Source: [agr_brownfield](https://www.brownfieldagnews.com/news/falling-farm-income-pressuring-crop-sector/)

News
Falling farm income pressuring crop sector
An ag economist says declining farm income trends continue to raise concerns across the farm economy.
Scott Irwin, professor and chair of agricultural marketing at the University of Illinois, says the downward movement has occurred in just a few years.
“The crop sector is doing poorly and the livestock sector is doing much better,” he said. “They don’t completely offset so overall we see a trend down in farm income, particularly from the highs we reached in 2021, 2022, and 2023.”
He says it’s been a grind for the crop sector since 2024.
“In essence, the sector is now surviving on various forms of federal support.”
Irwin says the federal assistance has allowed the sector to stay afloat.
“And not eat too far into liquidity reserves and into longer term assets and balance sheet matters.”
He made his comments during a recent webinar hosted by the Plant Based Products Council.
