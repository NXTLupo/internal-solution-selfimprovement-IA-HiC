# Tar spot concerns grow as humid conditions persist across Indiana

Source: [agr_brownfield](https://www.brownfieldagnews.com/news/tar-spot-concerns-grow-as-humid-conditions-persist-across-indiana/)

News
Tar spot concerns grow as humid conditions persist across Indiana
Purdue University Extension’s corn agronomist says recent high humidity and saturated soils have created optimal conditions for disease development.
Dan Quinn says tar spot has already been detected in Indiana, and he expects more cases to pop up as the season continues.
“It’s just kind of been found in small, localized areas,” he says. “When you have periods of wetness, but also some of the cooler temps we’ve had outside of this week with relatively cooler temperatures, tar spot kind of thrives in those environments.”
He tells Brownfield producers should continue to scout their fields.
“Look at the lower leaves, walk through some of your crop,” he says. “Especially in areas where you’ve had it in the past and where you might expect it to show up. A lot of areas are getting close to tassel.”
According to the Crop Protection Network, tar spot has been detected in Indiana, Illinois, Missouri, Iowa, Nebraska, and Kansas.
