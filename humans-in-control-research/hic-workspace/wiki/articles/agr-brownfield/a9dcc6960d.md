# USDA launches new $500 million program to expand U.S. fertilizer production

Source: [agr_brownfield](https://www.brownfieldagnews.com/news/usda-launches-new-500-million-program-to-expand-u-s-fertilizer-production/)

USDA launches new $500 million program to expand U.S. fertilizer production
The USDA is investing $500 million to increase domestic fertilizer production.
“We want fertilizer plants built in America,” she says.
Ag Secretary Brooke Rollins says the Fertilizer Investment and Expansion for Long-Term Domestic Supply (FIELDS) Program will help finance new and expand existing U.S. fertilizer facilities.
“It will prioritize project readiness,” she says. “It will prioritize financial viability. We will be looking at private investment that is already in a project or is already coming into the project with measurable outlines.”
Deputy Secretary Stephen Vaden says expanding production could give farmers more buying options and help lower prices.
“For some of these fertilizer markets, there are two companies that control 90 percent of the market,” he says. “What we’re seeking to do is provide money to the people that are going to come in and compete aggressively.”
Rollins says the USDA has also restarted stalled projects that are part of the Fertilizer Production Expansion Program.
The agency says grants will range from $15 million to $150 million and require a 50 percent private match to be eligible for funding. The applications will be open for 45 days.
