# May soybean crush, corn for ethanol use both up on year

News
May soybean crush, corn for ethanol use both up on year
The domestic soybean crush continues to hang around some lofty levels.
The USDA says 213 million bushels of beans were used for meal and oil production in May, down 5 million on the month, but up 9 million on the year, with producers trying to respond to demand while maintaining margins.
That faster-than-last-year production has led to an increase in soybean product supplies, with meal and oil stocks both above a year ago.
Margins are driving U.S. ethanol production.
The USDA says 471.784 million bushels of corn were used for fuel ethanol production in May 2026, rising 10% from April and 6% from May 2025.
Margins for the average Iowa producer spent the month of May in positive territory, with that rate of return well above last year’s levels.
Production of distillers dried grains with solubles was 1,774,035 tons, 11% more than the previous month, but 1% less than a year ago.
The USDA’s updated soybean crush and corn for ethanol use projections covering the 2025/26 marketing year is out July 10th.
