# Michigan young farmers say high lamb prices are creating new growth opportunities

Michigan young farmers say high lamb prices are creating new growth opportunities
A significant increase in lamb prices is helping young farmers expand into the industry.
Sam and Chase Bos work as agriscience teachers in West Michigan while supporting the family’s commercial ewe operation.
“The lamb market right now, tremendous prices at the moment, and it’s really exciting,” Sam shares. “It feels like we can make money this year, right? That’s a great feeling.”
Chase tells Brownfield raising sheep can be a lower cost of entry for beginning farmers, but land prices are not.
“Looking at young people, including ourselves, who are trying to get into the industry, maybe have land of their own, try and start some of these farms, the cost is just a very prohibitive factor,” he says.
According to the American Sheep Industry, prices for the end of June were at least 50 percent higher than last year.
Sam says spillover demand for beef and a robust Islamic holiday calendar have helped elevate prices during the first half of the year.
The Ottawa County couple was named this year’s Michigan Farm Bureau Young Farmers Excellence in Agriculture Award winners for their leadership efforts.
