# Farm bill delay would prolong uncertainty for farmers, Olson says

Source: [agr_brownfield](https://www.brownfieldagnews.com/news/farm-bill-delay-would-prolong-uncertainty-for-farmers-olson-says/)

News
Farm bill delay would prolong uncertainty for farmers, Olson says
The vice president of Minnesota Farm Bureau is concerned the farm bill could get held up in the Senate.
Carolyn Olson says the Federation is pleased Senate Ag Committee chair John Boozman released a draft text.
“And (I’m) a little concerned about how the conversations will go. Farmers would really like to have a farm bill done, we’ve been working on this for so long.”
She tells Brownfield farmers need policy certainty.
“That just makes life easier, it makes things less stressful when it comes to planning. And getting that over the finish line would be a big deal.”
Boozman says his Agricultural Act of 2026 would expand mental healthcare access, double funding for trade programs, strengthen foreign ownership disclosure requirements, and support local and regional meat and poultry processing.
