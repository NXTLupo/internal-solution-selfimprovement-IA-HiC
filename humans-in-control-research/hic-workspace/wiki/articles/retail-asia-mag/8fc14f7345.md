# GNC products will be distributed through DFI’s Guardian stores in Singapore.

Source: [retail_asia_mag](https://retailasia.com/news/dfi-retail-named-gncs-exclusive-wholesaler-in-singapore-hk-macau)

DFI Retail named GNC’s exclusive wholesaler in Singapore, HK, Macau
GNC products will be distributed through DFI’s Guardian stores in Singapore.
DFI Retail Group is expanding its partnership with GNC into Singapore, becoming the health and wellness brand’s exclusive wholesaler, distributor and franchisee across Hong Kong, Macau and Singapore.
Under the multi-year partnership, DFI will oversee GNC’s sales, marketing, distribution and logistics, with products distributed through its Guardian Singapore stores.
The partnership follows a milestone for GNC in Singapore after the Singapore Court of Appeal in May 2026 upheld and enforced GNC’s rights to assume former GNC store leases in full. GNC is now proceeding with the assignment of the relevant leases.
GNC has operated in Singapore since 1997, building its presence through its science-backed products and in-store health and wellness guidance.
DFI’s Guardian, which has more than five decades of experience in Singapore, provides preventive and personalised health and wellness solutions.
The companies said their combined expertise will support a differentiated health and wellness offering for consumers in Singapore.
