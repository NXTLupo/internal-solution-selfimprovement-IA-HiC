# Nine Korean brands signed strategic agreements with JD.com.

Source: [retail_asia_mag](https://retailasia.com/jdcom-boosts-korean-brand-sourcing-china)

JD.com boosts Korean brand sourcing for China
Nine Korean brands signed strategic agreements with JD.com.
JD.com is expanding its direct sourcing operations in South Korea to help Korean brands reach Chinese consumers.
JINGDONG Cross-Border, JD.com’s cross-border import platform, signed strategic cooperation agreements with nine Korean brands across fashion, lifestyle and beauty, including E-Land Group’s LLOYD and RECLOW.
Under the agreements, JINGDONG Cross-Border will directly source selected RECLOW products and launch a JD-operated flagship store for the brand. LLOYD and other participating brands will sell to Chinese consumers through official flagship stores on JD.com.
JD.com’s new sourcing team in Seoul will provide Korean-speaking support and work directly with brands to identify products suited to Chinese consumer demand.
The direct sourcing model allows JD.com to purchase products directly from brands and connect them with its retail, logistics and supply chain network, reducing the complexity of entering the Chinese market.
RECLOW CEO Kim Sung-jun said the model can reduce logistics and settlement burdens for small and medium-sized companies entering China.
JD.com’s sourcing expansion builds on its existing logistics operations in South Korea. JINGDONG Logistics provides import and export services, bonded warehousing and shipping solutions in the country.
The company launched its “10 Billion GigaGrowth Plan” in July 2025, targeting 1,000 new international brands and RMB 10 billion in cumulative sales growth over three years.
JD.com said it will continue expanding direct sourcing in South Korea and partnerships with Korean brands to bring more products and exclusive offerings to Chinese consumers.
