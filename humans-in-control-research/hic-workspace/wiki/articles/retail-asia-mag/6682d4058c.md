# Smartphone revenue reached $6.3b (RMB42.1b).

Source: [retail_asia_mag](https://retailasia.com/news/xiaomi-leans-premiumisation-drive-growth)

Xiaomi leans on premiumisation to drive growth
Smartphone revenue reached $6.3b (RMB42.1b).
Xiaomi’s second-quarter revenue rose 9.9% quarter-over-quarter to $16.2b (RMB108.9b), supported by smartphone premiumisation and growth in IoT and lifestyle products despite higher component costs and intensifying competition.
Revenue from Xiaomi’s smartphone × AIoT segment reached $12.5b (RMB84b).
Smartphone revenue stood at $6.3b (RMB42.1b), whilst IoT and lifestyle products generated $4.7b (RMB31.3b), up 26.7% quarter-over-quarter, supported by rapid overseas revenue growth.
The company’s smartphone premiumisation strategy also lifted sales value. Average selling price rose 25.9% year-over-year to a record $201 (RMB1,351), whilst global smartphone shipments ranked amongst the top three for the 24th consecutive quarter.
