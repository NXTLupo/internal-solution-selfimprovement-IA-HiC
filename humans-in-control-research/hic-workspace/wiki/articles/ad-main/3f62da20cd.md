FAQs
What Is the Best Material for a Luxury Shower Curtain?
Through our testing, we’ve determined that lightweight, breathable fabrics like cotton, linen, and canvas are durable materials that wick away moisture significantly better than polyester shower curtains. However, you need to pair it with a plastic liner that’s mold- and mildew-resistant, water repellent, and ideally, PVC-free. We highly recommend pairing your shower curtain with an EVA plastic liner, a phthalate- and chlorine-free alternative to non-recyclable PVC. For a lower-maintenance set, some brands also have snap-in liners to pair with their fabric curtains.
