# 11 Best Swivel Armchairs for Your Seat Rotation (2026)

Source: [ad_main](https://www.architecturaldigest.com/story/best-swivel-chairs)

Functional, fashionable, and fully rotating; the best swivel armchairs add an element of flexibility to your seating arrangements. Valued for their versatility and convenience, swivels work in any open-concept layout, at a bedroom vanity table, or pulled up to your work from home set up—really anywhere the ability to rotate a full 360 degrees comes in handy. In our search for the cream of the spinning crop, we prioritized picks that were thoughtful with design details like high-end upholstery and innovative frame shape. Our list includes a Fritz Hansen icon, luxurious velvet finds, and printed picks that don’t look dated. Whether you’re looking to curate more flexible seating arrangements to keep the conversation flowing or your goal is enhanced mobility, these seats are worth taking for a spin.
Our Top Picks for Swivel Armchairs
Best Customizable Swivel Chair
Another discreet design, this low-profile armchair with clean lines and subtle curves is the perfect seat to support book club discussions or strap into a solo movie night. Like most of West Elm’s furniture, you have lots of upholstery options (37 to be exact), but whatever you choose, there’s a handsome strip of wood grain peeking out from the base to create some textured contrast.
A Classic Barrel Chair
This curvaceous design is distinguished by its circular base, which brings in ash hardwood to enable a smooth 360-degree seat turn. Like all Quince furniture, it’s priced competitively with similarly designed chairs in today’s market, and you can select from three jewel-tone performance velvet upholsteries that look luxurious and are stain-resistant if you’re in the market for nice dining chairs.
Best Boucle Swivel Chair
Castlery’s tricolor bouclé Solari chair has our vote for one of the most fashionable pieces on this list—the woven upholstery brings a stylish warmth and earthiness while being resilient enough to resist spillage and constant use. The oversized armrests, soft curves, and swivel function culminate in a piece that makes even casual lounging look like an Instagrammable moment.
For the Traditionalist
Arhaus’s Savannah design feels like something we ripped from the set of Moulin Rouge! For minimalists looking to expand their horizons, solid colored upholstery helps ground the piece in some neutrality, while the floor-sweeping fringe brings the wow factor.
An Iconic Silhouette
Over the years, we’ve seen quite a few swivel-enabled Arne Jacobsen-designed Egg chairs in AD-featured homes. They’re a bit chameleon-like; while leopard-printed iterations can encapsulate a maximalist’s aesthetic, handsome leather-upholstered Eggs (like the one spotted in the home of Jay McInerney and Anne Hearst) evoke quiet luxury. “The Egg has got poise, from its soft, organic lines that are easy on the eye to its oversized wingspan,” contributor Collier Sutter reported in her review of the design icon. “The chair quickly proved its style adaptability in my Memphis-style apartment in Brooklyn—it was a grounding force in my room, which is clad in wacky primary colors.”
A Striped Seat
While technically designed for outside, Studio Tolvanen’s Terassi Swivel Lounge Chair feels nice enough to welcome indoors. Its softened silhouette evokes casual elegance, and the monochromatic striped upholstery (sure, there’s solid colors too, but what’s the fun in that?) feels like a cheeky nod to classic cabana furniture.
Best for Bold Colorways
A distinctly modern swivel chair, Sarah Sherman Samuel’s Fern chair is an overstuffed, almost bun-like seat. The low-profile back isn’t designed for head-to-toe support, but the slight recline and high arms still offer you plenty to lean against. An ideal reading nook chair or a side chair option for a media room, this velvet pick is streamlined and easy to decorate around.
Designer-Approved
Shopping market editor Kate McGregor adores the stationary version of this accent chair, part of Pierce & Ward’s West Elm collaboration. The swiveling option, new as of last month, is a more structured seat that rotates a full 360 degrees on a wooden base, ideal for formal living rooms and spaces designed more for conversation than lounging. “The arms are a bit too low to lean against, but I find that they’re the perfect height to rest my cocktail while chatting in the evenings,” she says. “The mid-height back is supportive and hits right at my shoulder blades (great for my lackluster posture), and I don’t find myself needing to adjust my position every few minutes.”
A Glamorous Pick
The Salon chair, designed by Laura Harrier and Tiffany Howell for Crate & Barrel, offers a dramatic alternative to traditional barrel swivel chairs. The abstract shape isn’t quite a wingback, but boasts a similarly cocooning silhouette. Velvet upholstery and a subtle burl wood base add to the glamour, particularly in the porcelain colorway.
An Outdoor Option
With its solid wood frame and adjustable back cushion, Article’s Pilou outdoor swivel chair is your patio’s missing piece. Turn to face the game inside, the pool, or the grilling station with one smooth motion. The 360-degree swivel makes it easy to follow the action, while its outdoor-friendly construction is durable enough to be left outside all season long.
