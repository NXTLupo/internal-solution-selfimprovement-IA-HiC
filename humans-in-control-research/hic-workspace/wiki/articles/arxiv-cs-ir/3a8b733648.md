# Causal Micro-Narratives

Source: [arxiv_cs_ir](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.05252v1)

We present a novel approach to classify causal micro-narratives from text. These narratives are sentence-level explanations of the cause(s) and/or effect(s) of a target subject. The approach requires only a subject-specific ontology of causes and effects, and we demonstrate it with an application to inflation narratives.
