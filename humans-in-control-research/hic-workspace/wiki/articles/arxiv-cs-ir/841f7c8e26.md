# Analysing the Influence of Macroeconomic Factors on Credit Risk in the UK Banking Sector

Source: [arxiv_cs_ir](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.14943v1)

Macroeconomic factors have a critical impact on banking credit risk, which cannot be directly controlled by banks, and therefore, there is a need for an early credit risk warning system based on the macroeconomy. By comparing different predictive models (traditional statistical and machine learning algorithms), this study aims to examine the macroeconomic determinants impact on the UK banking credit risk and assess the most accurate credit risk estimate using predictive analytics.
