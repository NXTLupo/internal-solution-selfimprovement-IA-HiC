# A Semantic Approach for User-Brand Targeting in On-Line Social Networks

Source: [arxiv_cs_ir](https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.01326v1)

We propose a general framework for the recommendation of possible customers (users) to advertisers (e.g., brands) based on the comparison between On-line Social Network profiles. In particular, we represent both user and brand profiles as trees where nodes correspond to categories and sub-categories in the associated On-line Social Network.
