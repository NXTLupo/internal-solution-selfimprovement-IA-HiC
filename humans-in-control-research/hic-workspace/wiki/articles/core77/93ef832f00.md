# A Slick, Minimalist Height-Adjustable Side Table

Source: [core77](https://www.core77.com/posts/145139/A-Slick-Minimalist-Height-Adjustable-Side-Table)

This elegant Hopper side table is by Swedish designer Mattias Stenberg. It features an offset leg, as it's meant to be cantilevered over a chair or sofa. The circular black things you see on the feet aren't wheels, but low-friction glides that allow you to smoothly move the table or pivot it into position.
On the top you see this flush button.
That allows you to change the table's height, thanks to a lift mechanism concealed within the leg.
The table is available with tops made of glass, wood or powder-coated metal. It's in production by Swedish furniture brand Materia.
