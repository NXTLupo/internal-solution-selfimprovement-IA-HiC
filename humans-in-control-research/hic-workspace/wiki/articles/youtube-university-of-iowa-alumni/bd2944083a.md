# University of Iowa Sport Development Funds | One Day for Iowa

Source: [YouTube: University of Iowa Alumni](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f2IjkEzfRfA)

There are dozens of meaningful ways for supporters to make an impact on Iowa Athletics, whether that's through academics and scholarships, revenue sharing, facility campaigns, or by investing directly in sports they care most about. That's where our development funds come in. Every Hawkeye program has its own sport development fund. These funds are used at the discretion of the head coach and support operational needs they determine as most essential for their student-athletes. This can include facility enhancements, uniforms and equipment, technology, analytics tools, team travel, and nutrition resources, or any other priority that elevates the student-athlete experience. Sport development funds give coaches the flexibility to address changing needs and fill gaps other budgets may not cover. By supporting these funds, donors empower coaches to provide a high-quality, competitive, and holistic experience for Hawkeye student-athletes. That's why our One Day for Iowa Sport Giving Challenge is so important. Rallying support for each team's development funds gives every support the boost it needs to stay competitive and continue achieving excellence. Go One Day for Iowa, and Go Hawks! We're pumped for One Day for Iowa!
