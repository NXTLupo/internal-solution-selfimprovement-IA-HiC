Current Employment Statistics - CES (National)

The Current Employment Statistics (CES) program produces detailed industry estimates of nonfarm employment, hours, and earnings of workers on payrolls. CES National Estimates produces data for the nation, and CES State and Metro Area produces estimates for all 50 States, the District of Columbia, Puerto Rico, the Virgin Islands, and about 450 metropolitan areas and divisions.

Each month, CES surveys approximately 119,000 businesses and government agencies, representing approximately 622,000 individual worksites.

THE ECONOMICS DAILY
What’s driving school and employee bus transportation employment?

With schools back in session, let’s check out the driving forces behind employment trends in the school and employee bus transportation industry. Each June and July, as the school year comes to an end, employees in school and employee bus transportation fall off payrolls. Employment trends in August can vary from year to year, but a large employment buildup often follows in September, as school resumes. For instance, before seasonal adjustment, 23,000 and 52,000 workers fell off payrolls in June 2025 and July 2025, respectively. In September 2025, school and employee bus transportation employment increased by 70,000, largely offsetting the earlier layoffs. In 2026, 29,000 employees fell off payrolls in June, followed by another 52,000 in July. read more »

Economic trends: home and community-based care versus institution-based care
