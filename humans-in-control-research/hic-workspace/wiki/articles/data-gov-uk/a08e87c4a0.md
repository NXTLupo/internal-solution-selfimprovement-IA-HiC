# Child Maintenance Debt Management

Source: [data_gov_uk](https://data.gov.uk/dataset/child-maintenance-debt-management)

These  are systems in which support the collection of child maintenance arrears, debt enforcement activity and/or offences against Child Maintenance legislation.  
Investigations  - where an offence has been suspected of having occurred which is against Child Maintenance Law.  This can be relating to a legal action taken against a Person or External Organisation, the goal of which is not directly the recovery of debt but punishment for non-compliance or illegal behaviour (e.g. fraud, violence).
