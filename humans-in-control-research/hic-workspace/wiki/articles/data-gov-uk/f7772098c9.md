# 5 year Flood and Coastal risk management (FCRM) asset maintenance programme

Source: [data_gov_uk](https://data.gov.uk/dataset/5-year-flood-and-coastal-risk-management-fcrm-asset-maintenance-programme)

The five year Flood and Coastal risk management (FCRM) asset maintenance programme shows the planned work for frequent and intermittent maintenance of watercourses and assets, such as channels, raised defences, structures and reservoirs carried out by the Environment Agency to reduce flooding in England.
