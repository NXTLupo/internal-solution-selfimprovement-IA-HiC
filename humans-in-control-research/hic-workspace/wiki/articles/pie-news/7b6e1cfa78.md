# Canada extends 150,000 international student arrivals target to 2029

 Canada will maintain its target of 150,000 new international student arrivals annually through 2029, extending the target set out in last year’s immigration plan.

The Canadian government has announced it will maintain its target of welcoming 150,000 international students annually in 2027, 2028 and now also in 2029, following the release of its [Immigration Levels Plan 2027-2029](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/news/2026/11/building-a-stronger-canada-through-a-well-managed-predictable-immigration-system.html).

This extension, revealed on November 5, is part of the Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) department’s wider goal of welcoming 370,000 new temporary resident arrivals to Canada in each of the next three years.
