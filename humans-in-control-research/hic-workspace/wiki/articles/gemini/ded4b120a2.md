Markdown Content:
Claude Cowork, ChatGPT Work, Microsoft 365 Copilot, and Google's new Gemini agent can all take on work that used to eat up days of your week. Each platform has real strengths and gaps. The teams getting results share one thing: the AI platform is connected to the tools they use most and can see the data behind them.

Without your CRM, board materials, or last quarter's forecast, you get generic answers – fine for polishing emails, useless for executive work.

Here's how the four AI work OS platforms compare, and how to pick one for you and your team.

## The 4 AI work OS platforms at a glance

|  | [Claude Cowork](https://claude.com/product/cowork) | [ChatGPT Work](https://chatgpt.com/work/) | [Microsoft 365 Copilot](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot) | [Google Gemini Enterprise](https://cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **What it is** | Agent that works across your files and apps to finished deliverables | Agent mode inside ChatGPT | Copilot Chat + Cowork agents + Agent 365 governance | Gemini in Workspace apps + the new Gemini agent |
| **Agent capabilities** | Works in the folders and apps you choose, shows each step, runs scheduled tasks with your laptop closed | Breaks long projects into steps, uses computer use across apps, runs scheduled and event-triggered tasks | Cowork runs multi-step work in the background across M365 apps and data; build custom agents in Copilot Studio | One agent for chat, long-running tasks, and code, with sub-agents and persistent coworker agents |
| **Strongest at** | Deep work on files, skills your team writes | Breadth of connectors, fastest rollout | Governance inside a Microsoft tenant | Google-native companies, data and analytics |
| **Biggest gap** | Cowork activity not yet in the Compliance API | Governance lighter than Microsoft or Google | Hard-to-predict usage bills | Agent isn't GA, pricing for coworker agents unannounced |

## Pros and cons of Claude Cowork, ChatGPT Work, Copilot, and Gemini

### **Claude Cowork**

Claude Cowork carries multi-step tasks through to finished work. Think Claude Code without the terminal, built for people who don't write code.

*   **Does** – documents, spreadsheets, decks, and analyses with citations back to source files
*   **Connects to** – Slack, Google Drive, Gmail, Microsoft 365, and local folders through the desktop app
*   **Runs** – in Anthropic's cloud for new Pro and Max tasks since [October 6](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/15520349-use-claude-cowork-on-web-desktop-and-mobile), in beta for Team, and admin-enabled on Enterprise

**Pros**

*   ✅ Best at deep work on real files – finished deliverables, not chat answers
*   ✅ Skills let teams encode their own processes – Brainlabs wrote ~400 in four weeks
*   ✅ Team is self-serve with a flat, predictable bill

**Cons**

*   ❌ Cowork activity isn't in the Compliance API yet
*   ❌ Cloud-by-default means files now leave the device
*   ❌ Usage limits hit fast for heavy users
*   ❌ It sits beside your productivity suite, so it's one more tool to govern

Pick it for the depth of work it finishes, not for how it fits your existing stack.

### **ChatGPT Work**

ChatGPT Work is an agent mode inside the ChatGPT seats many companies already pay for.

*   **Does** – documents, spreadsheets, presentations, and dashboards, with a plan mode that shows its approach before it acts
*   **Connects to** – 1,400+ apps
*   **Runs** – shared [recurring tasks](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11391654-chatgpt-business-release-notes) on a schedule or trigger, plus @ChatGPT inside Slack and Microsoft Teams

**Pros**

*   ✅ Deepest connector library of the four
*   ✅ Likely already in the building, so rollout is an email
*   ✅ Meets people inside Slack and Teams

**Cons**

*   ❌ Governance is lighter than Microsoft's or Google's control planes
*   ❌ Fast release cadence means admins chase new toggles every month
*   ❌ Like Claude, it's a separate tool on top of your suite

It's the fastest path to broad usage, with the least control over what that usage does.

### **Microsoft 365 Copilot**

Microsoft 365 Copilot is now a family of five products. Since a [September 25 relaunch](https://futurumgroup.com/insights/microsoft-splits-the-copilot-pricing-model-into-everyday-and-advanced-ai/), it includes Home, Chat, Cowork, Code, and Autopilot.

*   **Copilot Chat** – drafting and research
*   **Copilot Cowork** – background agents that complete tasks, running on Claude models
*   **Agent 365** – registers, secures, and monitors every agent in your tenant

**Pros**

*   ✅ Strongest governance for E3/E5 shops already running Purview and Entra
*   ✅ Agent 365 is the most mature answer to agent sprawl
*   ✅ Anthropic's models inside Microsoft's controls

**Cons**

*   ❌ $30 seat plus metered credits makes budgets hard to forecast
*   ❌ Five products under one name – buyers struggle to know what they're paying for
*   ❌ Publishes contracted seats, not usage – KPMG's 276,000-person rollout came with no productivity figures

Charles Lamanna, Microsoft's president of business applications and agents, summed up the split: "Copilot Chat already makes it easy to research topics and think through ideas, and Copilot Cowork allows you to take action and complete activities in the background."

### **Google Gemini Enterprise**

Google Gemini Enterprise works on two layers, and the new one isn't GA yet.

*   **Gemini in Workspace** – built into Gmail, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Drive, Meet, and Chat on Business Standard and up, with Workspace Studio agents on every plan
*   **Gemini agent** – [announced October 8](https://9to5google.com/2026/10/08/gemini-agent-google-cloud/), one agent for chat, long-running tasks, and code that also runs inside Microsoft 365 and Slack
*   **Coworker agents** – persistent agents with their own Workspace accounts and @agents.company.com email addresses

**Pros**

*   ✅ Gemini is already in Workspace Business Standard and up, so the incremental cost can be near zero
*   ✅ Model choice – Gemini and Claude today, more to come
*   ✅ Agent identity and per-project spend caps that pause an agent when hit
*   ✅ Strongest data story if you run BigQuery or a lakehouse

**Cons**

*   ❌ The Gemini agent is in private preview, and coworker agent pricing is unannounced
*   ❌ Most customer metrics come from earlier Gemini Enterprise deployments, [not the new agent](https://futurumgroup.com/insights/google-collapses-enterprise-ai-into-a-single-gemini-agent-at-gemini-at-work-2026/)
*   ❌ No independent benchmarks yet on how reliably it finishes long, multi-app tasks

Executives told analysts to expect[general availability around late October or early November](https://www.implicator.ai/google-launches-gemini-agent-that-gives-ai-coworkers-their-own-workspace-email/). Google Cloud CEO Thomas Kurian's pitch: "You give it objectives, not just instructions."

The short version: Anthropic and OpenAI sell an agent. Microsoft sells a control plane with agents attached. Google sells the agent, the productivity suite, and the data platform underneath as one bundle.

## What each AI work OS costs

Seat prices look similar. The bill doesn't, because three of the four now meter agent work on top of the seat.

|  | Seat price | How agent usage is billed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Claude Team** | $20 Standard / $100 Premium per seat per month (annual) | Flat – weekly usage limits per seat, optional prepaid credits |
| **Claude Enterprise** | $20/seat/month (annual) | Every token at API rates, capped by org, group, or user spend limits |
| **ChatGPT Business** | $20 Standard / $100 Premium per seat per month (annual) | Shared usage allowance, Enterprise spend controls in admin console |
| **Microsoft 365 Copilot** | $30/user/month on E3 or E5; E7 bundle at $99 | Cowork, Code, and Autopilot billed in Copilot Credits at $0.01 each |
| **Google Workspace + Gemini** | ~14BusinessStandard/~22 Business Plus per user per month | Gemini agent included for Gemini Enterprise customers; coworker agent pricing TBA |

A few things the table doesn't show:

*   **Cowork burns usage faster than chat** – on Claude Team, plan for a tier above what you'd guess
*   **Microsoft's meter is off by default** – Cowork needs an admin spending policy, and partners estimate 1–3 for a light task and $7+ for a research brief
*   **Google is cheapest on paper and least defined** – it [hasn't said](https://futurumgroup.com/insights/google-collapses-enterprise-ai-into-a-single-gemini-agent-at-gemini-at-work-2026/) how it'll bill coworker agents, which look a lot like seats

## Claude Team vs Claude Enterprise for an AI work OS rollout

If Claude is on your shortlist, the plan matters as much as the product. We covered this in depth in [how to choose Claude Team vs Claude Enterprise](https://codingscape.com/blog/how-to-choose-claude-team-vs-claude-enterprise).

The short version:

|  | Claude Team | Claude Enterprise |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Seats** | 5–150 | 20+ self-serve, 50+ sales-assisted, no cap |
| **Billing** | Flat per seat | Seat fee + usage at API rates |
| **SSO and domain capture** | Yes | Yes |
| **SCIM, audit logs, Compliance API** | No | Yes |
| **HIPAA-readiness and BAA** | No | Yes |
| **Cowork rollout control** | All-or-nothing across the org | Per team, via groups and custom roles |
