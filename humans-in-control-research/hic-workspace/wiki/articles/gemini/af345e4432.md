## ChatGPT usage

ChatGPT is the reference point for the whole category. Its growth curve from 2025 into 2026 is one of the steepest in software history.

*   01 ChatGPT reached **1.2 billion weekly active users**, OpenAI said at its DevDay event in September 2026.

Source: [OpenAI DevDay, via Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2271985/openai-dev-day-live-blog-chatgpt-news/), 2026 
*   02 ChatGPT’s weekly active users grew from **400 million in February 2025 to 800 million by October 2025, 900 million by February 2026, and 1.2 billion by September 2026**.

Source: [TechCrunch, citing OpenAI](https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/27/chatgpt-reaches-900m-weekly-active-users)&[OpenAI DevDay](https://www.engadget.com/2271985/openai-dev-day-live-blog-chatgpt-news/), 2026 
*   03 OpenAI reported more than **50 million paying consumer subscribers** and over **9 million paying business users**.

Source: [OpenAI, via Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/chatgpt-900-million-weekly-active-users-470492), 2026 
*   04 By July 2025, **700 million people were sending 18 billion ChatGPT messages each week**: roughly 10% of the global adult population.

Source: [OpenAI economic research](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/a253471f-8260-40c6-a2cc-aa93fe9f142e/economic-research-chatgpt-usage-paper.pdf), 2025 
*   05 ChatGPT became the **fastest app ever to reach one billion downloads** across iOS and Google Play.

Source: [Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/report/q2-2025-market-index), 2025 
*   06 ChatGPT ranked **#2 globally by app downloads in 2025**, with a 426% surge in total time spent in the app.

Source: [Sensor Tower State of Mobile 2026](https://technologychecker.io/blog/chatgpt-statistics) 
*   07 ChatGPT recorded approximately **5.51 billion monthly website visits** in April 2026.

Source: [DemandSage](https://www.demandsage.com/chatgpt-statistics), 2026 
*   08 The most common ChatGPT use cases are **practical guidance (28.3%), writing and editing (28.1%), and seeking information (21.3%)**.

Source: [Thunderbit, citing OpenAI](https://thunderbit.com/blog/chatgpt-statistics-user-growth), 2026

Source: [Meta, via DemandSage](https://www.demandsage.com/meta-ai-users/), 2025 
*   03 Microsoft Copilot reached about **218 million active users** across Windows, app and website, with over **30 million paid Microsoft 365 Copilot seats** as of Q4 FY2026.

Source: [Business of Apps](https://www.businessofapps.com/data/microsoft-copilot-statistics/)&[Microsoft Q4 FY26 earnings](https://news.microsoft.com/source/2026/07/29/microsoft-cloud-and-ai-strength-fuels-fourth-quarter-results-4/), 2026

Source: [Reuters, via Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/anthropic-says-revenue-jumped-to-nearly-17b-last-quarter-reports-2), 2026

Source: [Business of Apps, citing Anthropic](https://www.businessofapps.com/data/claude-statistics/), 2025 
*   06 Perplexity AI reached over **45 million monthly active users** by mid-2026, more than doubling from about 22 million at the start of 2025, and draws roughly 170 million monthly visitors.

Source: [DemandSage](https://www.demandsage.com/perplexity-ai-statistics/), 2026 
*   07 DeepSeek reached about **130 million active users** by the end of 2025, up from roughly 33 million in January 2025.

Source: [SpaceX Q1 2026 IPO filing, via DemandSage](https://www.demandsage.com/grok-ai-statistics/), 2026

## How people use AI to find information

This is the shift that matters most for brands. AI is becoming a front door to information, and a filter on which brands people see and trust.

*   01**37%** of consumers say they begin their searches with AI tools rather than traditional search engines.

Source: [Eight Oh Two, via Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/consumers-start-searches-ai-not-google-study-467159), 2026 
*   02**60%** of consumers say AI delivers better, clearer answers than traditional search; only 6% say it performs worse.

Source: [Eight Oh Two, via Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/consumers-start-searches-ai-not-google-study-467159), 2026 
*   03**85%** of consumers still double-check AI answers somewhere else before acting on them.

Source: [Eight Oh Two, via Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/consumers-start-searches-ai-not-google-study-467159), 2026 
*   04**47%** of consumers say AI influences which brands they trust.

Source: [Eight Oh Two, via Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/consumers-start-searches-ai-not-google-study-467159), 2026 
*   05**56%** of US consumers mostly or always default to a search engine, while **16%** mostly or always default to an AI chatbot.

Source: [Bain & Company](https://www.bain.com/insights/why-consumers-choose-an-ai-chatbot-over-a-search-engine-snap-chart), 2025 
*   06 Only **42%** of millennials and Gen Z default to a search engine, versus 76% of baby boomers.

Source: [Bain & Company](https://www.bain.com/insights/why-consumers-choose-an-ai-chatbot-over-a-search-engine-snap-chart), 2025 
*   07 Google’s Gemini-powered AI Overviews reach more than **2.5 billion users monthly** across 200+ countries, with Google’s AI Mode itself passing 1 billion monthly users.

Source: [Google I/O 2026 keynote](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/sundar-pichai-io-2026/), May 2026

## Consumer adoption of generative AI

Adoption is broad but uneven. It skews younger, and a large share of the public remains cautious about AI in daily life.

*   01**44%** of US adults have used ChatGPT as of June 2026 — up from 34% a year earlier and more than double the share in 2023.

Source: [Pew Research Center](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2026/06/17/americans-and-ai-2026-chatbots-smart-devices-and-views-on-impact/), 2026 
*   02**61%** of US adults under 30 have used ChatGPT, versus 44% of all adults.

Source: [Pew Research Center](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2026/06/17/how-opinions-and-use-of-ai-differ-by-age/), 2026 
*   03**54.6%** of the US population aged 18–64 had adopted generative AI by August 2025, up from 44.6% a year earlier.

Source: [Harvard Project on Workforce](https://pw.hks.harvard.edu/post/the-generative-ai-adoption-tracker), 2025
