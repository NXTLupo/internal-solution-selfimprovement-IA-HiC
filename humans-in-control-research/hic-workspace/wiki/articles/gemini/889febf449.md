Search for free AI tools for students and you'll find lists that mix two different things. Some companies give verified college students a paid plan at no cost, usually for a limited time and often with a card on file that is charged when the free period ends. Most also have a free tier that anyone can use, with lower limits and no deadline.

Here's what is actually free right now. US college students can get 12 months of Google AI Pro if they redeem by December 31, 2026, and four months of ChatGPT Plus if they claim by October 31, 2026. Students verified by GitHub get Copilot free for as long as they stay verified. ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Gemini Notebook (the new name for NotebookLM) and Perplexity all have free tiers worth using, and your school account may already include more.

We checked every offer on the company's own page on September 30, 2026. Offers change without much notice, so each one below links to the page that decides.

## Student offers: paid AI plans you can get free right now

The two subscription offers below ask for a payment method at sign-up and bill you automatically when the free period ends, unless you cancel. The coding offers need proof that you're a student, not a paid plan.

### Google AI Pro: 12 months free for US college students

On August 19, 2026, Google announced [a free year of Google AI Pro for eligible US college students](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/student-offer-google-ai/), a plan it values at $19.99 a month. The offer gives you four times the Gemini usage limits of a free account, Gemini in Gmail and Google Docs, and 5 TB of storage. Students at colleges in more than 140 other markets get a year of Google AI Plus instead; Canada, Albania, Bolivia, Hong Kong, Macau and Tunisia are left out.

The [offer terms](https://one.google.com/offer/studentoffer8), last updated August 19, 2026, hold the rules that trip people up:

*   **Age and verification.** You must be 18 or older, enrolled at a college, and verified through SheerID, the third-party service Google uses to check student status.
*   **Personal account only.** The offer can't go on your school-issued Google account. Members of a Google family group and supervised accounts are excluded too, and so is anyone who already gets Google One through a third party such as Google Fi.
*   **Payment method at sign-up.** The 12 months start on the day you redeem. When they end, Google charges its standard monthly price unless you cancel first.
*   **Your college's location decides the plan.** The terms tie it to the country or region of your school.
*   **Cancelling early.** Google says that if you cancel during the free year, your membership may stay active until the year is up.
*   **Storage after the year.** If you've gone over the free storage limit and then cancel, you can't store anything new until you delete files or pay.
*   **Limits can differ.** Usage limits on the offer may not match a regular paid plan, and Google can change them.

[Google's US student page](https://gemini.google/us/students/) settles one more question: new users qualify, and so do students whose 2025 AI Pro trial has already expired. If you're still inside last year's trial, check the offer page again once it ends.

For coursework, one concrete difference is in Gemini Notebook. The free tier holds up to 50 sources per notebook; Google AI Pro raises that to 300 and gives four times the usage, according to [Google's Gemini Notebook plans](https://notebook.google/plans). That's room for a whole course's readings, slides and notes in one notebook.

### ChatGPT Plus: four months free for US college students

OpenAI's [2026 Back to School offer](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001493-chatgpt-back-to-school-offer-for-students) gives current full- and part-time college and graduate students at eligible US degree-granting schools four free monthly billing periods of ChatGPT Plus. You have to claim it by October 31, 2026, and you can redeem it once. After the four periods, it renews at $20 a month unless you cancel.

Before you claim:

*   **Verification runs through SheerID.** Depending on your school, SheerID checks enrollment records, sends you through your school's single sign-on, or asks for a document. Your ChatGPT account doesn't have to use your school email.
*   **The offer attaches to whichever account is signed in.** OpenAI can't move it to another account later, so claim it on the account you plan to keep.
*   **Current subscribers can claim it** if they pay monthly, directly through ChatGPT. A Plus subscription billed through the App Store or Google Play doesn't qualify while it stays store-billed.
*   **An earlier student promotion doesn't rule you out.** OpenAI says past participation doesn't by itself block this one.
*   **Cancelling early costs you months.** If you cancel, Plus lasts to the end of that billing month and the free months you hadn't reached are forfeited. If you don't want to pay, cancel during the fourth month.

Most of what Plus adds over the free plan is headroom: more messages and uploads, expanded deep research and more memory, plus OpenAI's advanced reasoning models, according to [OpenAI's plan comparison](https://chatgpt.com/pricing/). If you only open ChatGPT a few times a week, the free plan may be enough, and there's nothing to remember to cancel.

### GitHub Copilot Student and Codex credits, for coding classes

[GitHub gives verified students free access to Copilot's features](https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/how-tos/copilot-on-github/set-up-copilot/enable-copilot/set-up-for-students) through GitHub Education. GitHub's docs list no end date; instead, GitHub re-checks your eligibility every month. Apply from the education benefits page in your GitHub settings, then activate Copilot Student. Approval and activation are separate steps, and the benefit can take several days to appear. If you're shown only paid options in the meantime, GitHub's advice is not to buy anything: wait a few days and try again.

OpenAI also gives university students in the US and Canada [$100 in Codex credits](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001147-codex-credits-for-students-terms-of-service), which shows up as 2,500 credits for Codex, its coding tool. You verify through SheerID with your university email, and you have to live in the US or Canada when you claim. The credits work on a free ChatGPT account, stretch Codex past your plan's limits and expire 12 months after they're granted. It's one offer per student.

Check your course policy before you use either on a programming assignment.
