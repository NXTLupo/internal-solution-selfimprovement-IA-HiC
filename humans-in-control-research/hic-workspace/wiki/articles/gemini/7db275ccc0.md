This report tracks how usage is divided across the leading AI platforms each month. We define a “generative AI chatbot” as any LLM-powered web or mobile application used by the public to get answers or produce content. All figures are estimates of monthly active users across U.S. web and mobile platforms, expressed as a share of the category total, with the latest readings covering usage measured through October 26, 2026.

ChatGPT added 0.2 points in October to reach 52.3%, its third consecutive monthly gain, in the month GPT-6 Sol became the default chat model for paid ChatGPT plans. Claude posted the largest gain of any platform, up 0.6 points to 11.2%, while Gemini gave back 0.6 points to 26.3%, its second straight decline since its August high. Grok and Microsoft Copilot held flat at 3.4% and 1.3%, and Perplexity slipped 0.1 points to 1.8%.

Many users now keep two or three chatbots in rotation instead of switching outright, limiting how far any single platform can climb or fall. Our projections indicate that ChatGPT is likely to hold in the low 50s through the remainder of 2026, with Gemini and Claude splitting most of the remaining growth.
