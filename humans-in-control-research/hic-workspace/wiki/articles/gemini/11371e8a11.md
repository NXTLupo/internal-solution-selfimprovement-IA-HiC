I went through every offer page and every set of terms myself over the last two days, because the coupon sites covering this cluster are recycling 2025 numbers and one of them still lists a Grok promotion that xAI's pricing page no longer mentions at all. One is a free year with an auto-renew at the end, and OpenAI's free months, the other big one, closed on October 31. Two vendors publish nothing for students, and one of them says so in writing. ## Claude Student Discount: There Isn't One, and Anthropic Says So in Writing

If you got here looking for a Claude AI student discount, an Anthropic student discount, or a student rate on Claude Pro, this is your section, and it comes first because it is the most searched question in the whole cluster. It also has the cleanest answer of any, straight from Anthropic's help centre article on the Pro plan:

> "We don't offer standard discounted pricing for individual plans (Pro, Max, and Ultra)."

The same article adds that Anthropic occasionally runs limited-time promotions but has "no standing discounts available upon request," and that its support team cannot issue one-off discounts. Claude Pro is $20/month in the U.S. and it is $20/month if you are a student, which is the same figure in [what Claude costs](https://www.izzedo.chat/blog/how-much-does-claude-cost) at every other tier too. There is no code, no `.edu` flow, no SheerID step. Anyone still telling you otherwise is describing 2025.

That leaves two honest routes to cheaper Claude.

The first is the free tier, which Anthropic points to in the same breath. It is genuinely capable for coursework, and if you are pairing it with another model for second opinions you may never hit the ceiling.

The second is institutional. Claude for Education is a university-wide plan covering students, faculty and staff, and the pricing page describes it as "comprehensive access for all university members at discounted rates" alongside dedicated API credits for student learning. There is no number attached anywhere on Anthropic's site, because the school negotiates it. So this is a thing your university buys, not a thing you buy. Again: ask IT.

A note on the related search for a student rate on Claude Code, since it comes up often enough to answer directly. Claude Code is included in the Pro plan rather than sold separately, and the help article quoted above covers every individual plan, so there is no separate student rate for it either. Same $20.

## Google Gives Students the Most, and Almost Nobody Reads the Terms

Google's offer is the largest one on the board and the most misreported, because the plan you receive depends on where you study.

The U.S. student page advertises a free Google AI Pro plan for one year, with four times the usual usage limits and 5 TB of storage. It renews at $19.99/month. That makes the offer worth roughly $240, three times what OpenAI's four free months were worth before they closed.

Load the same page outside the U.S. and the offer changes. You get Google AI Plus instead: two times the usage limits, 400 GB of storage, renewing at $4.99/month. Same twelve months, same verification, a materially smaller plan. Google's terms explain why in one line, and it is worth quoting because no roundup I found mentions it. "The subscription plan included in your Offer depends on the country or region of your eligible higher education institution."

The terms page, last updated 19 August 2026, is where the rest of the useful detail lives:

*   You must redeem by **31 December 2026**, and be **18 or over**.
*   Verification runs through SheerID.
*   A payment method is required at sign-up.
*   Six countries and regions are excluded outright: Bolivia, Albania, **Canada**, Hong Kong, Macau and Tunisia.
*   You do not qualify if you are in a family group, an enterprise purchaser, a discounted Pixel bundle subscriber, on a supervised account, or subscribed to Google One through a third party such as Google Fi.
*   The offer does not work on a school-issued Workspace for Education account. It has to be a personal Google account.

Canada being on the exclusion list next to Bolivia and Macau is the kind of thing you only find by opening the terms. If you are a Canadian student, the largest offer in this post simply does not exist for you, and the marketing page will not tell you that.

The personal-account rule is not unique to the offer, either. Google's help article for Gemini's learning tools carries the same shape on the features themselves: checked again on 29 September 2026, it says the practice-question and OpenStax features are "only available in English and in the United States" and "not available to ... users signed in with a work or school account". So the account your university issued you is locked out of both the free year and the practice-question tools. I went through what that costs you day to day in [Claude vs Grok vs Gemini for school](https://www.izzedo.chat/blog/claude-vs-grok-vs-gemini-for-school).

One more line from those terms that I have not seen quoted anywhere: "As part of the Offer, you may experience different usage limits than Google One members on a paid plan." Google is telling you in advance that the free year is not identical to the paid product. Fair enough for something free, but if you build your whole term around it and hit a wall in November, now you know why. Undisclosed allowances that move with demand are the norm across the industry now, which I went through in detail for [ChatGPT's limits](https://www.izzedo.chat/blog/chatgpt-limits).

## OpenAI's Four Free Months Closed on October 31

OpenAI's 2026 Back to School offer gave current full-time and part-time students at eligible **U.S.** degree-granting institutions four free monthly billing periods of ChatGPT Plus, which its student page called an "$80 value". The claim deadline was October 31, 2026. When I re-read OpenAI's help article on 3 November, it still gave that date, along with a note telling students whose claim failed before the deadline to contact Support. There is no extension and no replacement Plus offer.

If you claimed in time, three rules from OpenAI's own FAQ still apply.

**The four months run from the day you claimed.** After that, Plus renews at $20 a month on the card you added to activate it.

**Cancelling early forfeits the rest.** Your access continues to the end of the current billing period, and in OpenAI's words, "Any remaining promotional months are forfeited when the cancellation takes effect." If you don't want to pay, cancel during the fourth month, not the first.
