# Creating Listening and Learning Institutions 

Despite having funded the creation of the Internet, the public sector lagged in its adoption. Now the public sector has the opportunity to lead on the use of artificial intelligence for public good. AI has the potential to make government more conversational and transform how the public and government interact because of its power to sort, organize, and summarize vast amounts of data. 4Most government information is trapped behind hard-to-navigate websites and inscrutable PDFs that are hard for the public and public servants to find, let alone understand. At the same time, public knowledge, which is widely distributed, is equally hard to make sense of.
