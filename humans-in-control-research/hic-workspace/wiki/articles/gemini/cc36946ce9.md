### Google AI Pro (Gemini)

Google's most generous active offer. Verified US college students get up to 12 months of AI Pro free, including Gemini 3.1 Pro level access, NotebookLM premium features, and 2 TB of Google One storage. Verification runs through SheerID. Outside the US, the offer has wound down; some country-specific telco partnerships in places like Japan and India have offered extended access. Worth claiming first if you're eligible.

### Notion Plus (and 50% off Notion AI)

Notion gives the full Pro plan free to individual students who verify with a school email. Notion AI is a separate add-on, but verified students pay 50% off. If you're already organizing notes in Notion, this is the cheapest way to add AI writing assistance.

### GitHub Copilot Pro (via Student Developer Pack)

Computer science students get Copilot Pro completely free as part of the GitHub Student Developer Pack, alongside dozens of partner offers (Codespaces hours, JetBrains, Datadog credits). Sign up at github.com/education with your school email or transcript.

### Canva Education and Figma Education

K - 12 teachers and students get Canva Pro features free; Figma's Education plan unlocks Professional features free for students and educators with verification. Useful for any course that involves slide decks, posters, or design work.

### Perplexity Pro for Education - $10/month

Half off the $20 standard price for verified students and faculty. Pro includes unlimited Pro searches, file upload, and access to the strongest reasoning models. Some students have reported a full 12-month free trial through education verification - worth checking your eligibility.

### Adobe Creative Cloud - first-year discount

Students and teachers pay roughly $20/month for the All Apps plan in year one, against the standard $60/month. Renewal pricing rises in year two, so set a calendar reminder.

### Grammarly Premium - student-discount marketplaces

Grammarly itself doesn't sell a public student plan, but UNiDAYS and Student Beans run 20-25% off promotions periodically. Many universities also have institutional licenses - check your writing center first before paying.

### Otter.ai Pro - 20% off

With a verified .edu email, Pro drops to about $13.59/month or $79.99/year. Useful if you record lectures.

### Microsoft Azure for Students - $100/year credit

Not AI-specific, but pairs well with OpenAI API or Hugging Face inference if you're building projects. No credit card required at signup; renewable each year you stay enrolled.

### ChatGPT Plus

OpenAI offered eligible U.S. college and graduate students four free monthly billing periods through its 2026 Back to School promotion. The claim deadline was October 31, 2026, and the offer renews at $20 per month unless canceled. This was a temporary exception, not a standing student discount. See the [official offer terms](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001493-chatgpt-back-to-school-offer-for-students) and our [ChatGPT Plus student discount guide](https://krater.ai/blog/chatgpt-plus-student-discount).

Anthropic has no individual student discount. Claude for Education exists but is sold to universities. Most students do not have institutional access. Details in our [Claude Pro student discount guide](https://krater.ai/blog/claude-pro-student-discount).
