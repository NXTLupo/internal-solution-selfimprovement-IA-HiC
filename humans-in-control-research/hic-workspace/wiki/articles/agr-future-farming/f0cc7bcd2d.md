# Elbit Systems acquires Bluewhite: a new chapter for autonomous farming

Source: [agr_future_farming](https://www.futurefarming.com/tech-in-focus/autonomous-semi-autosteering-systems/elbit-systems-acquires-bluewhite-a-new-chapter-for-autonomous-farming/)

Israeli defence and technology group Elbit Systems has completed the acquisition of Blue White Robotics, better known as Bluewhite, through its subsidiary FUSE. Founded in 2017 and headquartered in Tel Aviv, the company has established itself as one of the most promising players in autonomous vehicle technology for agriculture.
With the acquisition by Elbit Systems’ FUSE, Bluewhite gains access to considerably greater resources, technological expertise and a global network. Bluewhite CEO Ben Alfi described the deal as an exciting new chapter. He believes the combination of Bluewhite’s proven autonomy expertise and Elbit Systems’ advanced technologies and extensive operational experience will accelerate the deployment of intelligent autonomous systems in some of the world’s most demanding environments.
For the agricultural sector, this means the technology Bluewhite has developed can be further refined and rolled out at greater scale. Integration with IoT devices and cloud computing will expand its capabilities further still — enabling real-time decision-making, predictive analytics and an adaptability that helps farmers navigate the growing unpredictability of climate and markets.
The acquisition of Bluewhite by Elbit Systems reflects a broader shift taking place across the agricultural sector. Autonomous machines are no longer experimental. With a proven platform, hundreds of thousands of operational hours and now the backing of one of Israel’s largest technology companies, Bluewhite is well positioned to expand its role further.
What sets Bluewhite apart from many other technology companies in the agricultural sector is its depth of real-world experience. The company has now accumulated hundreds of thousands of cumulative autonomous operating hours across agricultural and defence applications. It is a proven system deployed daily under real conditions, in orchards, vineyards and arable fields.
Bluewhite focused from the outset on permanent crops such as fruit trees and grapevines – a segment where precision and consistency are critical. Autonomous machines navigate narrow row spacings, carry out tasks such as spraying and crop monitoring, and do so with a level of accuracy that human operators struggle to maintain over long working days.
2 years ago, Bluewhite announced a multi-phase partnership with New Holland, a brand of CNH, to collaborate on distribution, manufacturing and integration of Bluewhite’s autonomous solutions for New Holland tractors in North America. This partnership enables New Holland tractors to operate fully autonomously in orchards, vineyards and other specialty crop operations.
Bluewhite was founded with a clear mission: to bring practical autonomy to environments where there are no paved roads. Agriculture proved to be the ideal starting point. Farmers worldwide are grappling with a growing shortage of labour, rising costs and mounting pressure to produce more sustainably. Bluewhite saw an opportunity and developed a platform that converts existing farm vehicles into fully autonomous machines – without requiring farmers to replace their trusted tractors.
The platform centres on two products. Pathfinder is an autonomous retrofit kit that transforms conventional vehicles into self-driving machines. The system is OEM-agnostic, meaning it works regardless of the brand or type of vehicle. Compass is the accompanying cloud-based software that allows farmers to manage, monitor and control their autonomous fleet remotely. Together, they form a complete ecosystem for autonomous farming.
