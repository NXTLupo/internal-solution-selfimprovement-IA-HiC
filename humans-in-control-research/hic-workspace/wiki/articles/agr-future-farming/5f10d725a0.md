# Former Volkswagen CEO Herbert Diess develops electric tractor

Source: [agr_future_farming](https://www.futurefarming.com/smart-farming/former-volkswagen-ceo-herbert-diess-develops-electric-tractor/)

Former Volkswagen (VW) chief executive Herbert Diess is developing an electric mid-range tractor through his new company, DIESS E-Agrartechnik. According to the company, the machine is scheduled to enter the market in 2027 and is intended for both agricultural and municipal applications.
The tractor will feature a battery swapping system, allowing depleted battery packs to be replaced quickly. In a LinkedIn post, Herbert Diess also highlighted a reversible operator’s station and compatibility with a wide range of implements, including mowers, front loaders, snow ploughs and spreaders. With these features, DIESS E-Agrartechnik is targeting a versatile machine suitable for both agricultural operations and municipal work.
For the time being, electric tractors remain largely confined to compact models and specialist applications. However, development continues across the sector. In the Netherlands, for example, Holland-Utrecht previously unveiled an electric tractor equipped with a 400 kWh battery pack. By focusing on a mid-range tractor designed for everyday use, DIESS E-Agrartechnik is clearly targeting this growing market segment.
The former Volkswagen chief executive claims that, from the first deliveries in 2027, the electric tractor will be able to compete directly with comparable diesel-powered models. According to Diess, operating costs could be up to 50% lower.
For now, however, technical specifications remain limited. Details such as power output, battery capacity and machine weight have not yet been disclosed.
