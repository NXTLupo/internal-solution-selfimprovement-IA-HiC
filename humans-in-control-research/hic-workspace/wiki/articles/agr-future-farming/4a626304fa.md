# ePropelled launches new integrated propulsion unit for agricultural drones

Source: [agr_future_farming](https://www.futurefarming.com/tech-in-focus/drones/epropelled-launches-new-integrated-propulsion-unit-for-agricultural-drones/)

Demand for agricultural UAVs is growing at a fast pace and advancing their technology is key to capture significant market share. A new unit incorporating propulsion and power technology has been launched onto the market to improve drone performance.
A new integrated propulsion and power unit specifically designed to improve spraying precision by agricultural drones has been launched by US company ePropelled. With a manufacturing plant based in New Hampshire, USA, ePropelled also has an innovation centre based in Coventry, UK, and a further engineering facility in Chennai, India.
The company is experienced in developing electric propulsion and intelligent power units for uncrewed vehicles on land, air and sea, but its latest innovation is specifically targeting agricultural drones.
With a global market projected to exceed $4 billion by 2026, the agricultural drone sector is growing at between 20% and 30% per year. That growth is driven by demand for precision agriculture, maximising labour efficiency and adhering to government legislation on reducing chemical use. In order to do this, agricultural drones are becoming more precise in arable farming by enabling more targeted spraying, crop monitoring, and automated field operations.
Therefore, tapping more into this market, ePropelled has come up with a new integrated system that combines high-efficiency brushless motors, advanced electronic speed controllers, and intelligent power management into a unified propulsion architecture designed to meet the unique demands of agricultural drone applications.
Knowing the market, ePropelled says agricultural UAVs require propulsion systems capable of delivering sustained high thrust, precise control, and reliable performance under challenging operating conditions, including exposure to dust, chemicals, and variable temperatures. The company says its system has been engineered to address these challenges by delivering high thrust density to support heavy payload spraying and spreading missions.
The new unit delivers improved energy efficiency to extend flight time and reduce operating costs and has advanced thermal management for sustained performance in harsh environments. The integrated motor and ESC control offers more precise and consistent spray application.
Overall, the result is a propulsion platform optimised for both light monitoring drones and heavy lift systems operating at scale across large agricultural environments.
Dean Marcarelli, Chief Commercial Officer at ePropelled said: “With agricultural drones becoming a core part of farm operations globally, propulsion is no longer just a component decision, it is a mission critical enabler.” “OEMs and operators are under pressure to deliver higher productivity, reduce input costs, and operate more sustainably. By integrating propulsion, control, and intelligent power into a single optimised system, we are enabling a new generation of UAVs that can operate longer, carry more, and perform more reliably in real-world conditions.”
ePropelled recognises the UAV industry is shifting more towards fully integrated propulsion systems, where motors, control electronics, and power management are engineered together to maximise efficiency, reliability, and ease of integration. While initially developed for agricultural UAV applications, the same propulsion and power architecture can also be applied to a wider class of uncrewed systems, including ground-based robotic platforms used in precision agriculture and autonomous field operations.
Dean Marcarelli added: “Over time, this same architecture will also support broader adoption of autonomous systems across both aerial and ground based platforms.”
By delivering a unified motor, ESC, and intelligent power architecture, ePropelled has a number of benefits for OEMs.
