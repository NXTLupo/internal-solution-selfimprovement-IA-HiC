# Farm-ING now offers a choice of 3 different smart weeders

Source: [agr_future_farming](https://www.futurefarming.com/crop-solutions/weed-pest-control/farm-ing-now-offers-a-choice-of-3-different-smart-weeders/)

Austrian smart farming equipment specialist Farm-ING has added 2 additional smart weeder options to its line-up. Farmers and contractors can now also spot-spray and laser-weed with Farm-ING.
With a total of 3 different options for smart weeding, Farm-ING now offers the most extensive and varied portfolio of specialised AI-supported machines for selective weeding. The young Austrian manufacturer initially developed its InRowING in-row hoeing machine. Launched at the 2023 Agritechnica trade show in Hanover, Germany, the InRowING hoe is now available with working widths up to 7.5 metres. Prices start at €65,000.
Since 2026, the company has also been delivering its SpotSprayING spotsprayer that uses the same algorithms as the in-row hoeing machine to distinguish plants from weeds. The spotsprayer is available with 5 different working widths ranging from 2.75 to 12 metres and from prices starting at €75,000. With the 12-metre-wide working width, the manufacturer is setting new standards when it comes to mounted 3-point linkage spotsprayers. In a solo configuration, both the spotsprayer and the tanks are combined in one unit behind the tractor. With a split configuration, the standard 400-litre tank moves to the front.
Laser weeding is currently an extremely hot topic with farmers, growers and contractors across the world, and Farm-ING is joining the companies offering laser weeding solutions. A first prototype of the mounted laser weeder was shown at the 2025 Agritechnica trade show in Hanover, Germany. The Farm-ING LaserING with working widths of 2 or 3 metres is expected to become available in 2026 for prices starting at €300,000.
