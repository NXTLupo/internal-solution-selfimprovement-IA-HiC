# Precision Farming Dealer's Best of the Web: July 1, 2026

Farmer Identifies Technology He’s Most Excited About
Corey Hillebo says one of the technologies he’s most excited about is See & Spray. “This has potential to be a giant gamechanger for profitability,” he says. Hillebo describes his experience with the technology and how it’s paying off on his farm in this LinkedIn video.
The Precision Farming Playbook for 600-Bushel Corn
David Hula, who holds the world record for highest corn yield, describes the technologies he’s using to break the 600-bushel corn barrier in Charles City, Va. “My late grandad and my late dad were always innovators, always wanting to try something new,” Hula tells Tyne Morgan in this Ag Web feature.
New Report Details How Farmers Are Using AI
Morgan Myers surveyed farmers, ranchers and dealers about whether and how they are using AI, where they see value, where improvements are needed and whether they trust the results. CropLife compiled the findings into a new report that offers a closer look at how agriculture is approaching AI today and where it may be headed next.
Transforming Older Equipment into ‘Autonomous Workhorses’
Monte Bottens goes 1-on-1 with Tyler Niday of Bonsai Robotics for a conversation about practical autonomous farming solutions in the latest episode of the AgEmerge Podcast.
Planting & Seeding an Entire Crop with a Driverless Tractor
Check out the lead story from our sister publication No-Till Farmer’s Conservation Ag Update. Kentucky farmer Quint Pottinger explains how he autonomously planted his entire crop in 2026.
