# [Podcast] Autonomy Takes the Wheel: Farmer Plants Entire Crop with Driverless Tractor

Source: [agr_precision_farming_dealer](https://www.precisionfarmingdealer.com/articles/7164-podcast-autonomy-takes-the-wheel-farmer-plants-entire-crop-with-driverless-tractor)

On this episode of the Precision Farming Dealer podcast, brought to you by Ag Express, Quint Pottinger explains how he was able to plant his entire corn crop this spring with a driverless tractor.
Pottinger became the first farmer in Kentucky to plant all his crops with a driverless tractor, using Sabanto, Panorama and Starlink to get the job done in 2026.
The young farmer explains the steps he took to implement autonomy in his operation, including significant changes to his equipment arsenal, challenges faced along the way and the big bucks he’s saving on costs.
