# Precision Farming Dealer's Best of the Web: June 24, 2026

Source: [agr_precision_farming_dealer](https://www.precisionfarmingdealer.com/articles/7175-precision-farming-dealers-best-of-the-web-june-24-2026)

Precision Farming Dealer editors encounter a variety of articles, social media posts, podcasts and videos that offer a unique look at various aspects of our great agricultural industry. Here is our favorite content from the past week:

- Farmers Get Up-Close Look at Cutting-Edge Sprayer Tech
- How California Farmers Use Pressure Bombs to Save Water & Money
- Exploring the Future of AI in Ag: Turning Data into Action
- Discussing CNH’s New Management Structure for Case IH & New Holland
Workshop Provides Hands-On Precision Experience to Students
Kansas State’s 2026 Smart Farm Challenge gave high school and college students the chance to gain hands-on experience with drones, sensors, farm machinery and other precision technologies. Professor Gaurav Jha shared this recap on LinkedIn.
Farmers Get Up-Close Look at Cutting-Edge Sprayer Tech
Verdant Robotics shared this footage of its SharpShooter in action at Grand Farm in Fargo, N.D. last week. The SharpShooter is an AI-powered machine that targets and sprays weeds with millimeter precision.
How California Farmers Use Pressure Bombs to Save Water & Money
Farmers facing tight water regulations in California’s Central Valley are finding new ways to relieve the pressure. CBS News has the scoop on a new tool from Precision Ag Services that’s essentially a “blood pressure monitor for crops.”
Exploring the Future of AI in Ag: Turning Data into Action
Jeremy Groeteke, global head of IT and digital strategy for Syngenta, talks all things AI in the latest episode of The Scoop Podcast. Groeteke explores the 3 buckets of AI, the future of autonomous farming, how dealers can overcome “data fatigue” and more.
Discussing CNH’s New Management Structure for Case IH & New Holland
In this episode of Tractor Therapy, the hosts tackle the news of CNH’s new management structure, looking back at the Case and IH merger, and when Case IH and New Holland came together. After tackling some other topics in the news, they get into this topic around 6:50. Give it a listen.
