# 1.618 Golden Ratio Paperclip Clever Product Design

Source: [abduzeedo](https://abduzeedo.com/1618-golden-ratio-paperclip-clever-product-design/)

1.618 Golden Ratio Paperclip Clever Product Design
The 1.618 Golden Ratio Paperclip is a clever product design project by Yuan Design Studio turning mathematical geometry into desk tools.
The project takes a common desk item and rebuilds it with pure math rules. At the core of the idea sits the golden ratio number phi. Creative team Yuan Design Studio cut fine lines into spring steel to trace the spiral. Instead of simple bent wire loops, this product design uses nested geometric frames that grip sheets of paper with balanced spring force. The matte black metal gives each piece a clean industrial feel. It works both as a paperclip and as a small visual viewfinder for daily work.
Mathematical Proportions and Clever Product Design
Every line in the metal cutout serves a clear goal in daily use. The curved spiral wire flexes out to hold stacks of pages without leaving deep marks. When clipped onto an open book page, the open grid frames words and photos like a lens. The design team made matching sets based on golden triangles and nested golden circles. Clean laser cuts keep every edge smooth to the touch. This clever product design resists wear from daily desk use. Strong steel spring memory ensures the clip keeps its grip over time.
The project shows how smart product design turns abstract ideas into a tactile tool. By linking classical math with everyday office tools, the studio created a clever item for creative desks. It offers great ideas for designers who want to rethink simple objects with fresh purpose. The minimal metal frame proves that basic tools can carry deep meaning.
