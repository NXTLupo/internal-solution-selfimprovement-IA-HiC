VAYA Vakantiepark Visual Identity: Lakeside Branding
VAYA Vakantiepark lakeside branding system combines custom linework marks, A-frame cabin collateral, and warm natural color palettes.
Designed by Alex Maikoe, VAYA forms a calm nature retreat identity. The core mark joins four clear shapes. A rising sun, A-frame cabin, lake wave, and green leaf fill the logo. Early pencil sketches in the designer sketchbook show how these forms came to life. Clean line drawings, geometric patterns, and paper cards build the brand base. Warm wood tones and deep lake blue set a clean shop tone. Each visual part builds brand identity across sign posts, print books, and web screens. All brand assets look good on store walls and outdoor signs.
Modular Linework and Nature Concepts in Branding
System tools anchor this branding project. Linear patterns, custom menu typography, and outdoor flags show how branding links natural surroundings to guest comfort. The visual system turns peaceful lake views into clean design details. A clear grid layout balances the fluid mark across print menus, key cards, and phone apps. Physical product details show clear craft. Canvas flags, paper food menus, and wooden room keys fill the set.
