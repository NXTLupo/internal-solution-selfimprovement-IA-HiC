# Bosh & Gray Visual Branding and Identity System

Source: [abduzeedo](https://abduzeedo.com/bosh-gray-visual-branding-and-identity-system/)

Bosh & Gray Visual Branding and Identity System
Bosh & Gray branding identity system sets a quiet visual tone for a modern design project. Created by Jonas Bombardelli, the work relies on strong type choices and clean paper texture.
The wordmark acts as a firm anchor on every printed item. Bold serif titles contrast with small sans serif details. Each print asset uses clear grid lines and solid paper weight. The branding identity feels calm, direct, and closely aligned with real physical space. It gives the brand a clear voice across all main touch points.
The branding visual language extends across name cards, specimen sheets, and folder wraps. The studio avoids extra visual noise to focus on clean grid order. High contrast type sets a steady pace across cards, labels, and paper covers. Deep debossing adds subtle surface texture without extra ink or color. Each layout respects margin space and grid lines. This simple branding design path lets natural paper stock and strong type speak with quiet confidence. Every item feels intentional and well made.
Minimal Typography and Systemic Branding
Custom paper stock and exact grid layouts form the core of the branding asset suite. Envelopes, project cards, and signage units share matching margin ratios and grid rules. Sharp serif letterforms bring clear focus to top headlines. Clean sans serif text organizes small details. The design shows how the studio shapes real space with simple forms, raw materials, and balanced light. Each piece fits into a single visual framework.
By keeping every design choice simple, the branding system forms a clear link between print items and real space.
