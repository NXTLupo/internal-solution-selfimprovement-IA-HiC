# Mission Space Visual Identity by SANDS STUDIO

Source: [abduzeedo](https://abduzeedo.com/mission-space-visual-identity-sands-studio/)

Mission Space Visual Identity by SANDS STUDIO
Explore how the new visual identity for Mission Space by SANDS STUDIO merges high-tech telemetry UI with physical branding assets.
SANDS STUDIO designed a system that challenges the conservative style of the aerospace industry. The visual identity relies on a high-contrast palette of deep space black, electric green, and cold white. A custom logomark represents orbital paths and weather vectors, forming a dynamic geometric glyph. The logo functions as a modular symbol, adapting to both digital interfaces and tactile hardware components. By utilizing these strong visual pairs, the studio establishes a clear narrative of weather monitoring. The icons use sharp lines and consistent grid proportions to represent different satellites and sensors.
Telemetry grid and visual identity design
The telemetry grid architecture organizes data across complex dashboard screens. SANDS STUDIO built a layout using monospaced typography, precise rules, and pixel-aligned UI grids. This structure ensures weather readings remain legible under varying conditions. The typographic scaling prioritizes numerical telemetry, using large sans-serif stats offset by smaller label text. Every information panel fits into a modular card layout. This grid controls the visual identity, helping operators process space weather alerts. The digital layout balances dense information with clear zones of negative space, allowing for quick scanning during emergency events.
Physical artifacts extend the digital telemetry into the real world. Business cards use extra-thick black paper stock with silver foil stamping to replicate metallic satellite surfaces. Custom physical badges use matte plastic and laser-etched telemetry coordinates. SANDS STUDIO shows how a modern visual identity bridges the gap between hardware and software. The physical collateral matches the high-contrast digital screens, using raw textures and precise geometric lines. This material discipline ensures the brand feels unified, from orbital calculations to physical stationery. The final packaging for hardware components uses heavy cardboard structures with minimalist typography.
