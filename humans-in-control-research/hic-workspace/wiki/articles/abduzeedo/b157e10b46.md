Brand Identity: RELIANCE DESIGN + BUILD by • TRÜF •
Explore the geometric brand identity and architectural collateral created by TRÜF for Los Angeles-based Reliance Design + Build.
Designing for architects and builders requires a visual system that reflects structural integrity. TRÜF answered this need for Los Angeles-based Reliance with a brand identity that strips away excess. The primary emblem is an abstract lowercase "r" constructed from thick black beams and a single, perfect circle representing the rising sun. Rather than relying on standard commercial visual clichés, the studio selected a color palette of deep forest green, crisp cream, and dark charcoal. The green represents organic growth, while the cream and charcoal provide a high-contrast foundation that juxtaposes cleanly with professional architectural photography.
A Structural Approach to Brand Identity
A strict grid controls the layout hierarchy across all digital and print mediums. Grotesque letterforms anchor the brand identity with typographic weight, ensuring clear readability at any scale. The bold lines of the geometric mark extend into decorative graphic patterns, framing case studies and dividing content on the custom website. By utilizing a simple grid system, TRÜF maintains visual tension across editorial spreads and digital screen layouts. Large typographic headings pair with generous negative space, allowing the architectural imagery to occupy center stage without unnecessary decoration.
The brand identity extends naturally into physical construction environments. Tactile business cards, branded hard-hat graphics, and large-scale site signage carry the same typographic discipline. Rather than simulating materials in a digital workspace, the physical collateral uses matte finishes and heavy paper stocks to mirror the tactile nature of real-world home building. This dedication to clean, physical execution ensures that the branding feels as solid and precise as the custom homes the company builds, grounding the digital design in structural reality. The design translates across print media and on-site signage with absolute consistency.
