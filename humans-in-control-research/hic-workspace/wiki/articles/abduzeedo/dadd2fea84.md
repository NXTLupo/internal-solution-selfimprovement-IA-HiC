# Brand Identity: Turkish Towels by Naci Mert

Source: [abduzeedo](https://abduzeedo.com/brand-identity-turkish-towels-naci-mert/)

Brand Identity: Turkish Towels by Naci Mert
Naci Mert designs a geometric brand identity for Turkish Towels, combining Anatolian cotton motifs with Denizli's warm regional tones.
For this regional initiative supported by DENIB, Naci Mert developed a visual system that avoids typical nostalgic tropes. The design is grounded in geometric minimalism, featuring clean vectors and structural line weights. The natural color palette draws directly from the landscape of the Denizli region, pairing dry terracotta, soft sand, and deep cobalt blue. These natural tones are applied consistently across business stationery, custom fabric labels, and cotton packaging boxes. The traditional Anatolian cotton bud is abstractly stylized into a precise geometric symbol that serves as the central logomark, bringing structural order and cultural heritage to the brand identity. Every graphic element is stripped of unnecessary decoration to focus on the raw quality of the textile craftsmanship.
Refining the Brand Identity Through Geometric Architecture
The typographic system is built on a rigorous grid architecture. Clean sans-serif headlines are set at a large scale, contrasted against small, technical metadata footnotes aligned to a tight vertical grid. This typographic hierarchy defines the brand identity. On catalog spreads, layout tension is achieved through extensive margins and off-center text columns, allowing the high-fidelity photography of the woven textures to dominate the page. The typographic alignment remains crisp across all corporate documents, ensuring that the communication elements feel structured, precise, and highly legible for an international audience.
Tactile print execution is central to the project. The business cards and stationery sheets utilize premium textured paper stocks, while the product boxes feature clean cardboard with blind debossed marks and white screen-printed branding. Fabric clothing labels are woven from raw Anatolian cotton, carrying the geometric motif. This material discipline ensures that the brand identity translates from digital media to the physical touchpoints of the global export market, reinforcing the centuries-old weaving tradition with modern design principles.
