# Nomēa Studio Visual Identity and Branding by Schickeria Studio

Source: [abduzeedo](https://abduzeedo.com/nomea-studio-visual-identity-and-branding-schickeria-studio/)

Nomēa Studio Visual Identity and Branding by Schickeria Studio
Schickeria Studio created a Mediterranean-inspired visual identity and branding system for Nomēa Studio with copper foil and custom type.
Designed for a Vienna-based creative agency specialized in hospitality and tourism, the branding balances playful Mediterranean warmth with rigorous Austrian editorial structure. Schickeria Studio anchors the visual identity with an oversized grotesque display wordmark paired with high-contrast serif italic subtitles and letter-spaced mono utility tags. A deliberate chromatic palette combining soft lavender (#F0DCF8), burnt orange (#FF4600), deep olive green (#006B44), and lemon yellow (#E6FA24) gives every brand application an unmistakable sun-drenched presence without relying on generic travel tropes, stock resort photography, or predictable coastal motifs.
Tactile Print Finishes and Editorial Web Branding
The visual system moves with purpose between physical stationery and digital interfaces. For print collateral, the branding employs tactile lavender paper stock embellished with metallic copper foil stamping and deep blind debossing. Business cards feature oversized blind-debossed letterforms that catch ambient side light, transforming simple contact details into sculptural paper objects. Die-cut stickers—ranging from acid-yellow starbursts and green diamond lozenges to scalloped wave cards—inject spontaneous energy across envelopes, promotional packs, and bespoke guest collateral.
In digital execution, the branding translates into a clean, responsive web layout built for hospitality storytelling and destination showcases. Editorial photo tiles, Super 8 film captures, and full-bleed mountain vistas are organized inside modular responsive grid cells alongside crisp typography. A custom circular compass monogram (`NOS`) reinforces the studio's role as a creative guide for destinations across the Mediterranean, Alpine regions, and international retreats.
By pairing high-character typography with refined physical print finishes and disciplined digital grids, Schickeria Studio establishes an elevated branding benchmark for boutique creative agencies. The branding system delivers authentic Mediterranean character while maintaining pristine typographic hierarchy and spatial restraint across every print and digital medium.
