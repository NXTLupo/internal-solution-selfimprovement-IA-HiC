# Design Week Mexico 2026 Opens with a Bold New Identity

Design Week Mexico 2026 Opens with a Bold New Identity
Design Week Mexico 2026 opens today in Mexico City, and its new graphic identity leads with a giant grotesque DWM26 set in off-white on black.
The whole campaign hangs on that one move: the wordmark IS the image. DWM26 fills the frame edge to edge at a weight where the letterforms stop reading as text and start reading as architecture. The palette stays near-monochrome, bone off-white on deep black, and color is rationed like a precious material. It appears only in the thin inauguration bars that code each program opening, one flat hue per event, floating like labels pasted over the type. Nothing else competes. The restraint is what makes the system feel confident rather than loud.
The Design Week Mexico 2026 identity system
The typography is a workhorse grotesque set tight and huge, with a smaller caps lockup beneath it carrying the full name, the dates, and the city. On the program poster the same voice drops down to a strict information grid: event names in caps, date ranges in parentheses, venues on the line below, and the colored inauguration bar as the only chromatic event on the page. The mark crops hard at the frame edge, the footer locks "18 AÑOS / SEMANA DEL DISEÑO" against the discipline list, and the registered mark rides along without apology. It is a system built to survive every format from a phone screen to a street banner.
In application the identity flips between two colorways, black and light, without losing its nerve, and the countdown campaign keeps the same typographic punch at every size. The 18th edition runs October 5 to 11 across Mexico City with Chile as guest country of honor and the France residencies continuing, while PRINT, the graphic design platform, opens October 7 at the White Box of G.56 Hub Creativo under the curatorial line "Perspectivas del Sur Global". A festival identity that treats its own name as the main event is exactly the kind of confidence graphic designers should study.
