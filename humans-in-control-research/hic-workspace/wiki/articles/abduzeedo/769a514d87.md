# Kampeki Sushi Brand Identity Branding by Blank Design Studio

Source: [abduzeedo](https://abduzeedo.com/kampeki-sushi-brand-identity-branding-blank-design-studio/)

Kampeki Sushi Brand Identity Branding by Blank Design Studio
Blank Design Studio creates the Kampeki Sushi branding identity with elegant typography, warm paper stocks, and motion video clips.
Blank Design Studio created a refined visual identity for Japanese dining concept Kampeki Sushi. Standard restaurant branding often relies on generic wave patterns or heavy red stamps. Here, the creative team builds a clean branding language centered on minimal letters and warm paper stock. Subtle type pairs with deep black fields and natural beige card stock. The full branding system turns food craft into clean visual stories. High-contrast ink density keeps each printed menu looking sharp in low dining light. Clear line spacing gives header text a clear stance on entry doors.
Culinary Craft and Restaurant Branding System
The visual system extends across food boxes, menu cards, chopstick sleeves, and video clips. Each paper item uses heavy card stock with gold foil details that stand out at dining tables. The Kampeki mark acts as a repeated stamp across takeout bags and glass doors. By making quiet craft a core part of the branding language, the designers create a strong physical feel. Motion video clips show key dining moments and kitchen prep with smooth pacing. Deep dark fields and white space keep menu items easy to scan. Simple grid lines guide price text for clear reading. Soft paper textures feel good in hand during dining.
The project balances raw food craft with clean visual structure. Neat layout grids organize dish names, ingredient facts, and drink pairings without clutter. The print items give the dining room a solid stance. The work shows how targeted restaurant branding strengthens dining identity without extra art. Simple type choices make menu cards easy to read on screen and paper. To explore full identity specs, motion videos, and print items, explore Blank Design Studio's project portfolio.
