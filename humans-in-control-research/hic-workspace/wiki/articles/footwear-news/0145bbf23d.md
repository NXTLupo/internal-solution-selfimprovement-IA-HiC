# As Sydney McLaughlin-Levrone Launches Third New Balance Collection, Inside the Design Process and How Her ‘Super Creative’ Husband Plays a Role

Source: [footwear_news](https://wwd.com/footwear-news/sneaker-news/sydney-mclaughlin-levrone-new-balance-collection-details-1239164222/)

Track star and new mom Sydney McLaughlin-Levrone has another big arrival coming soon: Her third signature collection for New Balance.
The line, set for a Sept. 8 release, features evolved footwear models that include a race-day cleat with a lightweight plate, a tried-and-true training silhouette with the brand’s FuelCell cushioning and one of New Balance’s most beloved lifestyle models.
The collection emphasizes elevated fabrications, including French terry, stretch knits, and multipurpose lifestyle offerings. A fitted tank, high-rise legging, pant, crewneck shirt and dress compose the apparel collection. Footwear includes the SuperComp MD-X v3 x Sydney McLaughlin-Levrone track spike, the FuelCell Rebel v5 training shoe and the 9060 lifestyle shoe.
The athlete talked about the evolution of her collections in In a recent interview for FN’s June “Women Who Rock” cover.

“In 2021, we were trying to figure out our stride in terms of my style, how I wanted to represent myself. It probably wasn’t my best work, to be honest,” said the four-time Olympic gold medalist. “But I was really excited about the one in 2024. All of the neutral tones speak to me and my style. It’s quiet and elevated. It’s always fun to get in the lab and create what we’re going for. I’m a refiner. My husband is also super creative. He’s more of an original idea person. I love to talk to him through the process and create together,” she said.
McLaughlin-Levrone described her overall style as “minimalist” and “sophisticated.” “I’m not super loud. I’m quality in a sleek way,” she said.
	She also discussed her loyalty to the FuelCell Rebel training shoe. “I want something that’s going to help me feel fast and light but also take the pounding of daily training,” she said. “The Rebel is a consistent source of that. I’ve loved giving feedback to figure out how we can make it better. It’s been cool to see all the innovations over the years and how it’s evolved and changed with time.”
