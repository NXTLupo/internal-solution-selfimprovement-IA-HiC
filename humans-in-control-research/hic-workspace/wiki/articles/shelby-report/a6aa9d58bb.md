# Fareway Opens New Meat Market in KC’s Waldo Neighborhood

Source: [shelby_report](https://theshelbyreport.com/2026/08/25/fareway-opens-new-meat-market-in-kcs-waldo-neighborhood/)

Johnston, Iowa-based Fareway Stores Inc. has opened a new Fareway Meat Market at 7921 Ward Parkway in Kansas City, Missouri, marking a new chapter in the company’s continued investment in the Waldo neighborhood.
The opening follows a ribbon-cutting ceremony Aug. 24, where community leaders, neighborhood representatives, customers and Fareway employees gathered to mark the completion of the new facility.
The store replaces the existing Fareway Meat Market at 79th Street and Ward Parkway. That location has served the Waldo neighborhood since Fareway acquired the historic McGonigle’s Market in 2020.
The 11,660-square-foot store is designed to provide an enhanced shopping experience while maintaining personalized service. It offers expanded grocery selections, including additional specialty items, fresh produce, an expanded deli counter, smoked meats and a full-service butcher counter. The store also features a walk-up window offering ready-to-order meals.
“This investment reflects our long-term commitment to Kansas City and in particular, area residents,” said Reynolds W. Cramer, CEO of Fareway Stores. “The new store and expanded offerings allow us to better serve our customers, support our employees, and create an experience that reflects the quality and service standards of Fareway.”
The development represents the first phase of a broader redevelopment plan for the property. The next phase will involve removing the old facility, leaving a commercial lot for future retail development. Fareway worked with city officials and neighborhood stakeholders throughout the planning and development process.
Family-owned Fareway operates nearly 140 stores in a seven-state region – Iowa, Illinois, Kansas, Minnesota, Missouri, Nebraska and South Dakota. Founded in 1938 in Boone, Iowa, the company, is among the largest employers in Iowa with employs more than 13,000 workers.
