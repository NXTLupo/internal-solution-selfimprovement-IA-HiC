# Natural Grocers Plans One-Day Anniversary Afterparty Aug. 27

Source: [shelby_report](https://theshelbyreport.com/2026/08/24/natural-grocers-plans-one-day-anniversary-afterparty-aug-27/)

Natural Grocers will extend its 71st anniversary celebration with a one-day Anniversary Afterparty on Aug. 27. On that day, {N}power members can save up to 52 percent off hundreds of items throughout the store, and all customers will have the chance to win free groceries for a year, a $6,000 value, by submitting an entry form available at all Natural Grocers locations that day.
Featured afterparty deals include Simple Mills Almond Flour Crackers at two for $5, select 32-ounce Ancient Nutrition Multi Collagen Protein in vanilla or unflavored at $29.99 each, C2O Coconut Waters at $19.99 for a 12-pack, Truly Grass-Fed Naturally Creamy Butters at $3.25 for 8 ounces and Caboo Tree-Free Bath Tissue at $7.79 for 12 count.
{N}power members also will receive a free limited-edition reusable bag with purchase and one free 17.5-ounce can of C2O Coconut Water, with no minimum purchase required and a limit of one per account. Members must load the offer to their account from the Natural Grocers mobile app, {N}power email or online dashboard before checkout.
Members who purchase one dozen Contented Hen eggs or one dozen Natural Grocers brand eggs in any variety will receive a second dozen of lesser or equal value free.
{N}power is Natural Grocers’ free rewards program, offering members access to discounts, digital offers and promotions.
Founded in 1955, Natural Grocers by Vitamin Cottage Inc. is a specialty retailer of natural and organic groceries, body care products and dietary supplements. The grocery products sold by Natural Grocers must meet quality guidelines and may not contain artificial flavors, preservatives or sweeteners, as defined by its standards, synthetic colors or partially hydrogenated or hydrogenated oils. The company sells only USDA-certified organic produce and pasture-raised, non-confinement dairy products and free-range eggs.
The company’s smaller-store format allows it to operate in a clean and convenient retail environment. Natural Grocers also provides free science-based nutrition education programs to help customers and crew make informed health and nutrition choices. The company is committed to its Five Founding Principles, including its “Commitment to Community” and “Commitment to Crew.”
In fiscal year 2025, Natural Grocers invested more than $16 million in incremental compensation and discretionary payments for crew. Headquartered in Lakewood, Colorado, Natural Grocers has 174 stores in 22 states.
