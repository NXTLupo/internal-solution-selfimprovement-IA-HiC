# EFF Launches Privacy Badger for Safari on macOS

Source: [eff_ai](https://www.eff.org/press/releases/eff-launches-privacy-badger-safari-macos)

Title: EFF Launches Privacy Badger for Safari on macOS

URL Source: https://www.eff.org/press/releases/eff-launches-privacy-badger-safari-macos

Published Time: 2026-10-19T09:00:25-07:00

Markdown Content:
SAN FRANCISCO—Privacy Badger, the [award-winning](https://blog.mozilla.org/addons/2025/12/17/presenting-2025-firefox-extension-developer-award-recipients/) tracker blocker made by the member-supported digital rights nonprofit [Electronic Frontier Foundation](https://www.eff.org/) (EFF), is now available for Safari on macOS.

Safari users can [download the free browser extension](https://privacybadger.org/) today and join the more than 4 million people using Privacy Badger to take a stand against data brokers and online surveillance. Users will need Safari version 26.5 or higher to use it.

Ranked as one of the best ad tracker blockers by [The Wirecutter](https://www.nytimes.com/wirecutter/reviews/our-favorite-ad-blockers-and-browser-extensions-to-protect-privacy/), [Consumer Reports](https://www.consumerreports.org/electronics/digital-security/to-protect-against-websites-that-spy-on-you-get-an-adblocker-a1003441908/), [PC Mag](https://www.pcmag.com/picks/best-ad-blockers) and more, Privacy Badger is different from other blocking extensions. Created by EFF to stop companies from spying on you online, Privacy Badger [automatically learns](https://privacybadger.org/#How-is-Privacy-Badger-different-from-Disconnect%2c-Adblock-Plus%2c-Ghostery%2c-and-other-blocking-extensions) to block hidden trackers on the web, along with additional privacy-protecting features.

"Most websites contain invisible tracking code that lets companies collect, share, and profit from your personal data," said Lena Cohen, Staff Technologist at EFF. "Creepy ads are just the tip of the iceberg when it comes to the harms of online tracking: Once your information [enters the data broker ecosystem](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2025/03/online-tracking-out-control-privacy-badger-can-help-you-fight-back), it’s nearly impossible to know who buys it or what they’re doing with it. Safari support has long been one of Privacy Badger’s most requested features and EFF is excited to be able to make this important tool available to Safari users to fight back for a better web."

On Safari, Privacy Badger goes beyond the browser’s [built-in privacy features](https://webkit.org/blog/15697/private-browsing-2-0/) to provide additional protection against online tracking. In its default browsing mode, Safari limits the information that trackers can access, but it still allows them to load. Privacy Badger blocks online trackers from loading in the first place, preventing them from tracking you using [techniques](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2021/04/after-cookies-ad-tech-wants-use-your-email-track-you-everywhere) that bypass Safari’s protections.

With today's launch, Privacy Badger [is now available](https://privacybadger.org/) on all major desktop browsers.

As the leading nonprofit defending online civil liberties, EFF is committed to fighting mass surveillance in the courts, through activism, and by making free, privacy-protecting tools like Privacy Badger. Now, even more people can join the movement against online tracking, by installing Privacy Badger themselves, telling their friends and family who use Safari about it, or [by supporting Privacy Badger today](https://supporters.eff.org/donate/support-privacy-badger).
