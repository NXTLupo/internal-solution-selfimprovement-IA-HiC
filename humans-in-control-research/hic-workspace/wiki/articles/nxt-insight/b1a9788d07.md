A pre-built modular email component system beats regenerating layouts per campaign

Practitioner and agency voices converge on locking a fixed set of reusable blocks (typography, spacing, CTA hierarchy, product and editorial modules) before letting AI assemble a send. Raw AI generators produce basic, off-brand layouts and matching a brand aesthetic from scratch each time is unreliable. This directly supports the reusable-component (MJML) approach: constrain agents to iterate within a locked design system rather than regenerate aesthetics per email.

we generally try to establish a small email design system first: typography, spacing, image treatment, CTA hierarchy, product modules, editorial modules, promotional modules [social-intelligence/reddit-deep/emailmarketing/1voakip.md#c1]
