Context Portability via Model Context Protocol Enables Personal AI Layer

Students and technical users are adopting user-owned context layers that persist across multiple foundation models rather than relying on institutional silos. By deploying local context managers and Model Context Protocol servers, users export communication styles and past histories between Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini. This decouples student identity from university-provided software.
