No-code automation hype is collapsing back into strict software engineering principles

The initial marketing wave that positioned low-code or no-code workflows as easy, permanent business systems has broken against operational realities. Clients do not care about the underlying low-code stack; they demand results, scalability, and long-term reliability. Without standard software engineering principles like human-in-the-loop validation, documentation, and error handling, no-code automations decay quickly, transforming no-code agencies back into specialized development firms.

We’re basically a development agency. Clients don’t care if it’s n8n, Zapier, Make, Node, Python, whatever. They care about: results, reliability, time-to-market, price [articles/reddit-deep-r-startups/95107422da.md#c0]
