AI adoption in insurers is outpacing the AI-ready data infrastructure that governs it

EY research cited in an asset-management study found 83% of senior business leaders said AI adoption would be faster with stronger data infrastructure, and 67% admit lack of infrastructure is actively holding back AI adoption [articles/vertexaisearch-cloud-google-com/6e2d771327.md#c3]. Legacy architecture, data silos, and data errors (54% of firms) throttle the analytics that underwriting increasingly depends on. As farm-facing carriers deploy LLM tools, this data bottleneck determines whether AI-enabled agricultural underwriting delivers or stalls.
