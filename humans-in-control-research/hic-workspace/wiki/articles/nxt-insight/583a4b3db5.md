Ag tech only creates value when it drives field-level decisions, not at the point of invention

The AgriBusiness Global Ag Tech Talk series frames a consistent finding: technology adoption stalls unless it converts into practical action across the value chain. GROWMARK's co-op deployment centers on integration over disruption, reducing workflow friction for crop specialists rather than deploying tech for its own sake. This reframes the adopter divide as a decision-utility gap, not merely a funding gap.

Technology only creates value when it drives practical decisions in the field and across the ag value chain.
