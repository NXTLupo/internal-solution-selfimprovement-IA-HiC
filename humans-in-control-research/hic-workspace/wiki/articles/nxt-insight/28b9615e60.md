Automated adjudication overrides clinician judgment without medical review

Consumers repeatedly describe denials issued by algorithmic or non-clinician review that overrule a treating specialist. A BCBS Dupixent denial is framed as 'practicing medicine with an Excel spreadsheet,' and an MRI ordered as medically necessary was denied by a doctor who never saw the patient. This is the central AI-adoption tension for payers: automated cost-containment is experienced as care rationing, eroding the trust any payer AI deployment depends on.
