Automated denials reversed on appeal reveal AI's accuracy gap as the core provider barrier

A claims-experienced commenter notes that 'a lot of claims are denied by automation' and that provider appeals with submitted records 'frequently' overturn the denial. This documents the false-positive denial pattern of current automated adjudication: AI speeds the no, but humans must re-do the yes. For providers, this is the operational barrier (rework, appeal labor) and for payers the accuracy/governance liability that constrains expanded AI use.

A lot of claims are denied by automation, and frequently once the provider appeal is filed and they submit necessary records, the denial is overturned
