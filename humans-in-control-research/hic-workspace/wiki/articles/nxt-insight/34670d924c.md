Portal and data-handling failures undermine trust in payer digital systems

A ClearShare/Clearwater member documented 25-plus claims with inconsistent statuses and a portal where 'uploads lacked clear tracking, duplicate receipts were rejected, appeal/document uploads did not work.' An Anthem member reports digital appeals 'lost' or crossed with unrelated visits, and Aetna took six months to update an address. These data-integrity failures are the foundation any payer AI must sit atop; broken systems-of-record are a structural barrier preventing reliable AI adoption.
