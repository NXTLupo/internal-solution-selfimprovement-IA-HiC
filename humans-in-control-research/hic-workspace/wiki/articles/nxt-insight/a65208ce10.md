Locked templates let marketers riff while brand designers protect design integrity

Figma Buzz gives brand designers and marketers a shared space where designers publish team templates and lock parts of any template to give marketers a focused editing experience that protects design integrity. Marketers can edit unlocked text or image layers like filling out a form, and can remove brand guidelines to edit freely at their own risk. This is the direct model for preserving creative flexibility while enforcing brand compliance in Deciem's email production.

By locking parts of any given template, designers are able to give marketers a focused editing experience that allows the team to riff while protecting design integrity. [articles/figma-blog/4387992c1a.md#c2]
