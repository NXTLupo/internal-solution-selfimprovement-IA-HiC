Prior-authorization timing rules trap elective surgeries, a structural barrier AI scheduling could ease

Surest/UnitedHealthcare required an authorization code 3-30 days before elective procedures but would not release it until five days prior, and told members to obtain it from HR who had no such authority. Patients received provider-fee denials up to ten months post-surgery for missing authorization codes. These brittle, time-boxed payer rules illustrate the integration reality AI builders confront across EHR and billing systems.
