Automated claim adjudication overrides human coding, generating disputed denials

Insurer back-end systems re-tier provider-submitted claims without human reconciliation, the operational failure mode any deployed healthcare automation must avoid. In one case Anthem overrode a hospital that coded a visit strictly as Urgent Care and adjudicated it as an Emergency Room visit, raising a $60 copay to over $500 [social-intelligence/reddit-deep/healthinsurance/1u3r32d.md#c0]. Denials cite algorithmic medical-necessity criteria such as MCG M-555 and InterQual that members and providers cannot inspect [social-intelligence/reddit-deep/healthinsurance/1tl5w3r.md#c2]. This is the live, practitioner-observed shape of automated adjudication friction relevant to any 2026 healthcare-AI operational use case.

Anthem's internal systems apparently arbitrarily overrode the hospital's coding and bumped it up to an ER tier, seemingly just because the clinic physically sits inside a hospital complex.
