Institutional monitoring ambiguity creates student anxiety over AI interaction privacy

Students express substantial concern regarding university visibility into their personal AI interactions and side projects. While enterprise access (such as Northeastern's Claude Premium partnership) grants advanced model tiers, students worry administrators retain full logging rights or could claim IP rights over commercial products developed on institutional credentials.

If you try to make money off of these side projects, that probably counts as some whole other thing. [social-intelligence/reddit-deep/neu/1w1vvey.md#c2]
