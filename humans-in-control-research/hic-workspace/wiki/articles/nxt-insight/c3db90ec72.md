Agents need a code repo or middleware or they cannot reconstruct designs reliably

A senior practitioner states the hard precondition: 'If you don't have a DS documented in code and some sort of middleware (an mcp, primitives in a repo, etc) AI is not going to work well trying to reconstruct designs from images or even Figma' [social-intelligence/reddit-deep/uxdesign/1uke7pb.md#c3]. Another reports that abandoning Figma entirely and getting 'access to your Frontend repo' for the coding agent lets you 'do pretty much anything you normally would do in figma but without touching any 3rd party design tools' [social-intelligence/reddit-deep/uxdesign/1uke7pb.md#c6]. This repo-and-middleware dependency is a structural extensibility requirement, not an optional optimization.
