Real-world developer efficiency trials expose a stark productivity perception gap

While many corporate announcements assert massive productivity multiples from AI tools, randomized controlled trials reveal a significant perception gap. Experienced open-source developers working on real issues in their own repositories often run slower when allowed to use AI, due to repetitive feedback loops and unhandled edge cases. Despite this measured slowdown, developers still consistently believe the AI made them faster.

Doesn't mean AI never helps, but it's a real measured counterexample to the productivity claims, not just vibes. [social-intelligence/reddit-deep/chatgptcoding/1wn6o5d.md#c2]
