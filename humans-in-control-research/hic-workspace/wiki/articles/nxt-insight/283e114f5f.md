Step therapy forces patients through ineffective drugs before approval

Payer formulary logic compels patients to fail cheaper drugs first. A migraine sufferer with 25+ headache days a month is restricted to triptans she is allergic to until she tries all of them; a Veozah patient is told to take the hormones her breast-cancer gene contraindicates. A commenter notes step therapy and pre-auth are add-ons sold to employer plans to guarantee cost savings. These rules-based gates are exactly the logic AI both enforces and could humanize.
