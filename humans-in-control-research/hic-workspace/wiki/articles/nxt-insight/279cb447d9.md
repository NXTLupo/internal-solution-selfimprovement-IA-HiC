Customer acquisition costs exceeding $300 force standalone robo-advisors to pivot to white-label API embedding

Standalone robo-advisor models have become financially unsustainable, with direct-to-consumer acquisition costs surpassing $300 per account. To bypass these expenses, major market players are abandoning direct software licensing in favor of white-label API infrastructure. JPMorgan exemplifies this pivot by deploying modular platforms like WealthOS to embed fractional-share engines directly into their existing mobile banking ecosystem. This allows asset managers to onboard massive client cohorts from checking relationships without incurring direct marketing overhead.

Direct-to-consumer client acquisition costs for standalone robo-advisors frequently exceed $300 per account.
