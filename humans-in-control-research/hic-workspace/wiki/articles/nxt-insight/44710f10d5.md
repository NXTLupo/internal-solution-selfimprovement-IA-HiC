Medicare Advantage authorization friction outpaces traditional Medicare for caregivers

A nurse and former home-health scheduler documents that Medicare Advantage 'always' required an authorization process with 'much more rejections' and narrower networks than traditional Medicare, which delivered more RN/PT visits and 'never' rejected referrals. Another commenter warns the administration is pushing MA plans toward for-profit care decisions. As payers deploy AI in MA utilization management, this cohort-specific friction defines both the elderly-consumer barrier and the caregiver-navigation burden.

There was always an authorization process... Generally it was a longer process that resulted in much more rejections than traditional Medicare
