A 50% Technology Deficit Chokes the $12 Billion Alternative Asset Expansion

High-net-worth demand pushes alternative asset allocations aggressively toward $12 billion averages per advisor. However, half of all wealth management firms lack the third-party infrastructure to track and report on illiquid investments. This operational strain forces manual workarounds and creates a hard cap on alternative asset distribution scalability.

Only 50% of wealth management firms use third-party technology systems to oversee, report on, and automate illiquid alternative assets.
