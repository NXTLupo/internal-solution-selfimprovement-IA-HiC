Family-deductible reprocessing lag shows claims systems lack automated reconciliation

Post-birth billing reveals systems that 'don't automatically reprocess on their own': a member's OB global billing applied individual deductible before a newborn was added, and commenters confirm reprocessing 'takes months or even a year, sometimes it's missed and no one realizes.' This manual-reconciliation dependency is the operational inefficiency AI-driven claims orchestration targets, and its current state burdens both consumers and the payer back office.

All the claims systems I've used don't automatically reprocess on their own. Someone has to realize there is claims that should be reprocessed
