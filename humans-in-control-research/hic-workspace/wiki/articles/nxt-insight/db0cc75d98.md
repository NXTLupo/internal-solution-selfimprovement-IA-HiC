Gen Z builds loyalty emotionally and socially, not through frictionless transactions

Pion's Richard Jackson argues Gen Z doesn't want less engagement, they want less friction between inspiration and action, and they build loyalty emotionally, socially and culturally rather than transactionally [articles/internet-retailing-uk/b132992c91.md#c1]. The real risk is not friction but forgettability: brands mistaking visibility for loyalty are sleepwalking into irrelevance. For email design this reframes the template as an environment to participate in and a source of emotional clarity, not just a conversion machine.

the easier commerce becomes, the easier brands become to replace... Gen Z doesn't build loyalty transactionally. They build it emotionally, socially and culturally. [articles/internet-retailing-uk/b132992c91.md#c1]
