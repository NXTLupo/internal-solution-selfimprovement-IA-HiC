Western wildfire underinsurance gap now runs into six figures per home as rebuild costs rose 47 percent since 2020

The Cotality Wildfire Risk Report 2025 identified nearly 2.6 million homes at moderate or greater wildfire risk across the western US, representing $1.3 trillion in reconstruction value, with California alone accounting for 1.26 million at-risk properties worth roughly $796.1 billion. APCIA reports rebuild costs rose 47 percent since 2020, producing an 'underinsurance shock' where dwelling limits lag true reconstruction costs by six figures. California's FAIR Plan enrollment jumped 43 percent between September 2024 and December 2025, and the state approved a 17 percent emergency rate hike for State Farm. Agricultural structures and ranch dwellings in the wildland-urban interface face the same coverage-adequacy collapse.

Rebuild costs have risen 47% since 2020, according to APCIA. Many homeowners hold policy limits that no longer match current reconstruction costs.
