Fiscal Pressures Drive Higher Education Service Consolidation

Universities face mounting external headwinds including federal funding constraints, tax law changes on endowments, and a shrinking high school demographic pipeline. These financial pressures force smaller institutions into acquisitions or closures, expanding network models like Northeastern University. Institutions must innovate service delivery and career readiness partnerships to remain viable.

At least 40 colleges have been acquired by other schools since 2020, including Marymount Manhattan College and Mills College, which are now part of Northeastern University’s global network. [articles/the-conversation-rss/4a1210f144.md#c1]
