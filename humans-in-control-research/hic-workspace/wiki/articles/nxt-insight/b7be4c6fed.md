Credit access and technology adoption are one coupled lever, not two independent ones

Finance is the prerequisite for climate-smart adoption: without credit, farmers cannot buy the improved inputs, irrigation, storage, or processing that reduce the underlying production risks insurers price [articles/nxt-insight/db7decbcb5.md#c0]. This means underwriting viability and lending capacity are structurally intertwined rather than sequential. Irish evidence shows the same coupling in reverse: farmers were 'sold on the wisdom of solar' but lack the money to invest due to low dairy and beef prices [articles/agr-agriland-ie/6dc9a09ffe.md#c1], leaving risk-reducing capex stranded.

finance is equally critical to enable farmers and agri-SMEs to adopt climate-smart technologies and infrastructure, such as improved inputs, irrigation, storage or processing [articles/nxt-insight/db7decbcb5.md#c0]
