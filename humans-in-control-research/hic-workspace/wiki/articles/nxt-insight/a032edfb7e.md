Provider billing and coding errors generate six-figure exposure that automated claim-integrity tools could intercept

Patients describe catastrophic billing failures: a hospital filed a corrected claim eight months late and then billed the patient nearly $50,000 after the claim denied for timely filing [articles/reddit-deep-r-healthinsurance/e6be9df62e.md#c0]. Another faced ~$350,000 after a provider billed the wrong insurer [articles/reddit-deep-r-healthinsurance/ac60a2bef4.md#c0]. These coding and timely-filing failures are exactly the structured, rules-based tasks AI claim-scrubbing tools are positioned to automate.
