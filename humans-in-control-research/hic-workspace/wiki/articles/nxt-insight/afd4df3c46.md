Configuring in a sandbox before production is the step teams skip and most regret

HubSpot's deployment guidance stresses always configuring in a sandbox before touching production, calling it the step most commonly skipped when timelines get tight and the one you'll regret skipping most. Webflow's agent playbook likewise launches the agent in a sandbox version of the site with a simple, trackable directive before going live. This defines the staged-rollout requirement for validating the email agents against real Figma libraries and a test send before any live campaign.
