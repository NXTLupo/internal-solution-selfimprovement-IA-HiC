Payers deny established treatments as experimental, leaving consumers without an appeal path

Consumers report payers reclassifying long-covered treatments as 'experimental' after a plan switch, then blocking the appeal mechanism itself. A Cigna member receiving occipital nerve blocks for two years was told the treatment was experimental as of 2023 and that no one would hear an appeal, with the insurer also refusing a hard-copy denial. This denial-then-stonewall pattern is the exact friction AI claims-automation and appeals tooling is positioned to address for the payer cohort.

She told me that Cigna won't let me appeal it. She also told me that they won't send me a hard copy of the denial, which I think is illegal.
