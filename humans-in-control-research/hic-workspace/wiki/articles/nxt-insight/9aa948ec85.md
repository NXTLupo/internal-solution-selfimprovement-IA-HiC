Modern agentic systems are ensembles of specialized models, not one generalist agent

NVIDIA frames modern always-on agents as systems of models where a frontier reasoning model plans and orchestrates a workflow while smaller specialized models perform targeted tasks such as code review and tool use. This validates the multi-agent decomposition the objective calls for: an orchestrator plus distinct design-extraction, component-assembly, personalization, and QA agents rather than a monolithic email generator.
