Insurance gains rarely reach the farmer

Despite risk-mitigation benefits, interviews across markets show these gains are rarely passed to borrowers. Lenders do not expand risk appetite or reduce loan interest rates; to cover premium costs, pricing may actually increase when insurance is bundled with loans, worsening affordability under high-rate conditions.
