Read-Only Data Integrations Win Consumer Trust Over Automated Write-Back Actions

In sensitive verticals like personal finance and healthcare, consumers demonstrate a clear preference for read-only context aggregation over automated system write-backs. OpenAI's Epic EHR integration and Anthropic's Claude Personal Finance tool explicitly restrict AI models to fetching and summarizing authorized data while deferring major decisions and record edits to human professionals. Consumers actively manage privacy settings to prevent unwanted data leakage, signaling that university AI architectures should prioritize read-only access to academic and co-op records rather than autonomous write-backs.
