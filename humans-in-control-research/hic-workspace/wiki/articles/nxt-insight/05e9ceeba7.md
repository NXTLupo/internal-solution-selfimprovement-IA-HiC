Students Demand Portable User-Owned Context Layers Over Institutional Destinations

Technological adoption patterns reveal a strong preference for personal, persistent AI tools that travel across platforms rather than isolated university destinations. Frameworks like open-context use Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers to export conversation history, factual memory, and communication preferences between Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini [articles/hacker-news-algolia/372a44f025.md#c5]. This decouples execution logic from static institutional infrastructure [articles/nxt-insight/9dc5a1b16a.md#c0].
