The insurer-retreat doom loop strands undercapitalized farms in uninsurable, unbankable segments

Without parallel investment in climate-smart technologies such as improved inputs, irrigation, storage and processing, insurers retreat from the most exposed segments, further constraining finance and reinforcing a cycle of underinvestment and vulnerability [articles/hic-theme-synthesis/0649f90dec.md#c8]. This threatens to bifurcate the market, stranding undercapitalized farmers while consolidating production among well-capitalized operators who can afford risk-mitigating infrastructure, a structural driver of the persona divide the objective requires.
