Hybrid Specialist-Wholesaler Models Arise for Alternative Assets

The rapid expansion into alternative investments exposes a critical gap in advisor expertise and sales enablement, threatening distribution volume. To bridge this knowledge gap, 79% of Tier-I asset managers are deploying specialized advisor-facing product experts. These specialists operate alongside generalist wholesalers to successfully distribute complex, illiquid alternatives. This dual approach directly counters the fact that 75% of asset managers view advisor education as their single largest obstacle in the private markets.

Fully 75% of asset managers identify advisor education as their single largest obstacle in distributing private market products.
