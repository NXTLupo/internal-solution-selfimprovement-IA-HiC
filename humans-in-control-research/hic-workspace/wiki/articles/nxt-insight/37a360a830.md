Governance and access controls form the primary constraint for production feasibility

Transitioning an intelligence prototype to production requires explicit ITS agreements on role-based access, data export policies, and PII protection [articles/client-uploads/0fec271931.md#c3][articles/client-uploads/009c1b4b5b.md#c10]. Enterprise delivery frameworks emphasize structured governance dependencies and strict review turnarounds to prevent schedule slippage [articles/client-uploads/009c1b4b5b.md#c12][articles/client-uploads/009c1b4b5b.md#c24].

Use authenticated, role-based access; approved exports; logged activity; and no donor PII in unapproved model prompts.
