Farmer-level behavioral barriers to uptake

Low financial literacy, limited trust, cultural preference for traditional risk-sharing, and poor experience with past products suppress demand. In Ethiopia farmers are not fully aware of crop-insurance benefits and question the timeliness and adequacy of compensation.
