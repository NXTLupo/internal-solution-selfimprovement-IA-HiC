Screening questions and reference checks keep failing to surface poor executive fits

HR practitioners report that reference checks and screening questions failed to surface poor executive fits, and swap strategies for reducing costly bad hires. This exposes a screening-logic gap that AI-assisted signal detection and structured reference synthesis could target directly for search firms whose core promise is de-risking executive hires.
