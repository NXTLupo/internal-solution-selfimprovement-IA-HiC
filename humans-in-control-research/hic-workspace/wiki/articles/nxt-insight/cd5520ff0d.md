Curated newsletters outrank video and blogs as the preferred learning format

Convince & Convert's annual survey of marketing leaders found newsletters are the preferred way to stay informed, 'almost twice as likely to be ranked first than any other medium' ahead of short-form video, webinars, blogs, and podcasts. The insight: readers crave well-curated smart summaries that do the thinking, not lists of links. For a Gen Z skincare brand this argues the email is a curation and point-of-view vehicle (routine guidance, ingredient explainers) rather than a promotion dump, and that the format itself still carries weight.
