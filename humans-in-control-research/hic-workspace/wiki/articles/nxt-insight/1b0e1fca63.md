The 2026 shift is from infinite content to infinite context in every interaction

Mitch Joel argues the opportunity of AI is not infinite content but infinite context, enabling a one-to-X world where a brand communicates at scale while each interaction feels individual. This reframes email design away from volume toward contextual relevance. For a Gen Z organic skincare brand, the template should be engineered for dynamic, context-aware content blocks (skin concern, purchase history, season) rather than higher send frequency.
