AI adoption in the job search is real but still minority behavior in 2026

Resume Genius reports 31% of job seekers use AI to support their search, 38% have used AI tools to help with applications, and among AI users 59% write resumes and 48% write cover letters with it. [articles/resume-genius-blog/5c06c567f2.md#c2] Indeed Hiring Lab data shows only 4% of job postings mentioned AI or LLM technologies by December 2025. [articles/resume-genius-blog/5c06c567f2.md#c1] The finding calibrates DSG expectations: candidate-side AI is common, but employer-posting AI language remains early.
