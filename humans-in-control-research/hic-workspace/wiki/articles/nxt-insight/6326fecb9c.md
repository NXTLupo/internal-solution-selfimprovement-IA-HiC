Sequential Interactive Nudging Replaces Overwhelming Passive Advice Feeds

Users report friction with overwhelming text advice, preferring structured, sequential interactive prompts that ask one question at a time. Behavior transformation strategies succeed when AI assistants pace interactions dynamically based on user context rather than delivering static advice dumps. Pacing interventions prevents cognitive overload and encourages active user reflection.

You will ask me 3 crucial questions, ONE AT A TIME. Wait for my complete answer before asking the next question.
