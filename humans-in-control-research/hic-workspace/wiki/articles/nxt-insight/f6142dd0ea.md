Plausible design ships when other functions can imitate design's visible outputs

Smashing Magazine argues AI hands product and engineering enough design capability to bypass design: a PM who can generate a decent flow, copy, and prototype may not involve design early, and the danger is plausible design that looks coherent, uses the right components, and ships because nobody objects. In large orgs the cut to design roles could be 50% or more. This is the stakeholder-risk context that Figma critics and approval gates exist to counter.

Plausible design is dangerous. It looks coherent in a product review. It uses the right components. The spacing is fine... So it ships.
