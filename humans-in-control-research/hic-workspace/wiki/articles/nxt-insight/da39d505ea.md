Continuous measurement closes an improvement loop from spotting a gap to judging the result

Webflow defines an agentic platform by its ability to carry an opportunity through a full cycle: real-time signal monitoring flags a declining click-through rate, autonomous recommendations propose a feasible response, integrated workflows route approvals, website execution deploys, and continuous measurement tests the change against the objective and feeds evidence into the next decision. This monitor-recommend-approve-execute-measure loop is the operating model the white paper should map Figma capabilities onto.

Once the change goes live, the platform should test and compare it against the original objective, then automatically feed that evidence into the next decision. This creates an improvement loop
