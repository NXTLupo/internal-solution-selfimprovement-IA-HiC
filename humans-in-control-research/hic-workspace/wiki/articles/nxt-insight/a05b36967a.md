Resume Tailoring Automation Replaces Repetitive Application Tasks

Job seekers and early-career candidates are moving beyond simple prompting toward specialized workflow tools that tailor master resumes in seconds. Manual customization causes severe application burnout during job searches. Candidates actively normalize bringing workspace AI tools directly into timed interview evaluations.

I built LandThisJob instead to build a master resume once, and automatically tailor it to any job description later in ~60 seconds. [articles/reddit-deep-r-careerguidance/91d8ac49e8.md#c0]
