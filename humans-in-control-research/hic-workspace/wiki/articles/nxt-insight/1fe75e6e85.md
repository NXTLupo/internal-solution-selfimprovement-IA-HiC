Autonomous actions require explicit allowlists and mandatory human signoff

Consumers welcome proactive AI agents for research and drafting but enforce strict boundaries against autonomous execution. Operating guides for personal assistants like Meta's Muse mandate explicit user confirmation before sending external messages, placing orders, or altering settings [articles/reddit-deep-r-museagent/874d1a19c7.md#c0]. Practitioners emphasize that irreversible actions must hit a deny-by-default allowlist rather than relying on unreliable internal model confidence scores [social-intelligence/reddit-deep/artificial/1x1aro3.md#c2].
