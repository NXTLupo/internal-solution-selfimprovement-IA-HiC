CAP's new age-40 young-farmer definition codifies the generational shift underwriters must model

German MEP Norbert Lins' post-2027 CAP report defines a young farmer as 40 years old or younger and the head of the holding, introducing a sub-article the Commission text omitted [articles/agr-agriland-ie/e2c301121f.md#c1]. A parallel push seeks 10% of CAP funding ring-fenced for young farmers [articles/agr-agriland-ie/2cd1a5f69f.md#c0]. These definitions formalize a generational transfer of land and management, directly shaping the persona segmentation, subsidy exposure, and successor-risk questions underwriters and lenders must answer.
