Clear Separation Between AI Pre-Session Drafting and Essential Human Advisor Authority

Students view AI as an excellent assistant for low-stakes preparation; such as structuring resumes, organizing questions, and summarizing degree requirements; but strongly prefer human experts for high-stakes academic, career, and personal decisions. Attempts to replace advisor interactions with unmediated AI lead to student distrust, while AI tools that augment advisor preparation deliver high value.
