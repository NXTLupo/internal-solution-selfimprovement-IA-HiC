Only 42.5M farmers are insured globally and 88% sit in one Indian scheme, exposing a systemic protection gap

Agriculture microinsurance currently reaches 42.5 million people, 37.5 million (88 percent) of whom are covered through one national scheme in India. In India around 63 percent of farmers still remain uninsured even under a mandated integrated credit-insurance product, and Ethiopia's market stays nascent after over 60 pilots across two decades. This concentration reveals how thin the underwriting safety net is against the climate and macro shocks the 2026 report evaluates.
