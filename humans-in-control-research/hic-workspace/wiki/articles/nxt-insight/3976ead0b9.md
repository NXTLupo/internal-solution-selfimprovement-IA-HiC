Crop insurance payouts cut past-due farm loans and lower banks' risk-weighted asset ratios

The FDIC finding cited by ISF shows a one-standard-deviation increase in crop insurance payouts reduces past-due farm loans by roughly 60 basis points, about 20 percent of typical delinquency fluctuations. By lowering effective risk weights on agricultural loans, insurance improves lenders' capital adequacy even under covariant shocks. This is a direct mechanism linking underwriting viability to farm-portfolio solvency for the 2026 report.
