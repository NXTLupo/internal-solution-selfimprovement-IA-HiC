Alternative Asset Distribution Demands Hybrid Specialist-Wholesaler Models

The rapid expansion into alternative investments exposes a critical gap in advisor expertise and sales enablement. Fully 75% of asset managers identify advisor education as their single largest obstacle in distributing private market products. To bridge this knowledge gap, 79% of Tier-I asset managers are deploying specialized advisor-facing product experts. These specialists operate alongside generalist wholesalers to successfully distribute complex, illiquid alternatives.
