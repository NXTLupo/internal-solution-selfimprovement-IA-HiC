Designers shift from making the first version to selecting, correcting, and directing agent output

A r/productdesign practitioner reports AI has shifted work from making the first version to selecting, correcting, and directing, letting one designer generate, critique, and revise ten plausible directions before the first interview is scheduled. A r/uxdesign poster with two years of experience describes Claude outputting an entire UX strategy in about a minute. This human-in-the-loop selection role is precisely what Figma's approval and critic surfaces must support rather than automate away.
