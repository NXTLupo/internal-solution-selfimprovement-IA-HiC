Non-engineers ship production software from specs alone when a credential portal removes setup friction

AWS reports Peloton's Bureau let six product managers independently build and deploy working prototypes from PRD descriptions alone in one month, recovering an estimated 18 engineer-days, backed by a credential portal wrapping Bedrock with SSO and auto-refreshing short-lived tokens. This shows Deciem's marketing and design teams could operate the email pipeline without engineering sprints, provided a credential and access layer separates human and agent tokens and eliminates static keys.
