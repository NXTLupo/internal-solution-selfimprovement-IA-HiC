Agents ship safely only inside a staged Draft-Validate-Execute-Log pipeline

Marketing-ops practitioners converge on a staged pipeline that keeps execution privileges separate from drafting: Draft, then Validate, then Execute, then Log. Every editable change is emitted as a proposed diff rather than a direct edit, and every run writes a full audit log of inputs, outputs, and actions taken. This is the operational spine for an email pipeline: the assembly agent drafts, a validation agent checks, and only a gated step sends, with everything logged.

Use a staged pipeline: Draft, Validate, Execute, Log. Keep execution privileges separate from drafting. [articles/reddit-deep-r-marketingautomation/877c6ad6be.md#c1]
