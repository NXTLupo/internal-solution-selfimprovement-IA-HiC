Academic Research Workflows Require Task-Specific AI Selection and Verification

Students and researchers deploy different specialized AI tools for distinct stages of academic work rather than relying on a single platform. General chatbots like ChatGPT serve initial conceptual summarization, while specialized search platforms (Scite, Primo, Consensus) ground literature reviews in peer-reviewed data. Because general LLMs exhibit high error rates on factual lookups, validation protocols remain essential.

AI tools – be it ExLibris’ Primo Research Assistant, Research Solutions’ Scite, Anthropic’s Claude or OpenAI’s ChatGPT – are trained with various datasets and use different algorithms to generate results. They should not be used interchangeably. [articles/the-conversation-rss/2fecc0d0c2.md#c1]
