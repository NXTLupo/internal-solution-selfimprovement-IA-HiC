Physician-built AI tools emerge to draft prior-authorization appeals patients cannot write alone

A self-identified physician is testing a free tool to help patients fight prior-authorization denials, offering to draft appeal letters citing the insurer's own medical-policy criteria. Patients repeatedly report they lack the medical knowledge to win appeals and that providers must do the fighting. This is a concrete provider/consumer AI use case driven by administrative-burden pain rather than clinical novelty.
