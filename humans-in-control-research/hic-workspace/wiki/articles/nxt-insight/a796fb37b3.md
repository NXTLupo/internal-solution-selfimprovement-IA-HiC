AI-generated imagery is a quality-control liability that can repel buyers

Creative Bloq documents backlash against AI-generated product and food imagery, with consumers treating it as a signal of poor attention to detail and vowing to avoid brands that use it. For an organic skincare brand where authenticity and clean-ingredient trust are central, AI-generated hero images in emails risk undermining the exact credibility the brand needs; real product photography remains the safer conversion choice.
