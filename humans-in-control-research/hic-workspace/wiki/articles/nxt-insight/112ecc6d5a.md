Authentic, human tone is displacing polished corporate messaging

Convince & Convert's 2025 outlook reports brands moving away from overly corporate messaging toward a human, approachable, storytelling tone that builds emotional connection, mirroring B2C relatability. This trend supports a clean-beauty template copy voice that is conversational and values-forward rather than salesy, aligning with the same Gen Z audience that distrusts vague marketing claims.
