Deterministic static-analysis gates, not AI, validate agent output against declared standards

A r/designsystems practitioner argues most design-system enforcement is already computable at the static-analysis level: you do not need AI to validate AI, only a schema and a deterministic check comparing what the AI produced against what you declared valid. Composition rules (a date picker must be wrapped in a form-field organism) encode as dependency rules the gate catches. This is the exact mechanism the white paper's deterministic-hooks section requires: the gate scales because it does comparison, not inference.

You do not need AI to validate AI. You just need a schema and a deterministic check. The gate is simply comparing what the AI produced against what you declared as valid.
