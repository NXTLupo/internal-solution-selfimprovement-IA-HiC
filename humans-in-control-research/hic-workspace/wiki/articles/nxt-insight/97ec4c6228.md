Design-to-code handoff historically fragments across tools, which agents are positioned to collapse

eBay found their design-system documentation lived in scattered Figma files where designers had one version, developers maintained separate documentation, and accessibility guidelines lived somewhere else entirely, creating friction at every step. Sketch and Webflow describe developer handoff, QA reviews, and in-context commenting as separate collaboration surfaces. An MCP-connected agent pipeline unifies these fragmented handoffs into one governed flow from Figma source to validated HTML.
