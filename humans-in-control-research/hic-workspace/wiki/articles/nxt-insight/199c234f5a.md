Provider directories list ghost and out-of-network doctors, undermining trust in payer data systems

Consumers and ex-payer staff describe directories listing providers who are fake (residential addresses, disconnected phones) or shown in-network then flipped to out-of-network mid-claim. A member was mailed three cards with the wrong doctor before the directory error was resolved. Data-integrity failures in payer systems set the baseline accuracy problem any AI-driven directory or matching tool must overcome for both consumer and payer cohorts.

I am talking about fake providers that have residential addresses and phone numbers that are disconnected. These appear to be deliberately faked up
