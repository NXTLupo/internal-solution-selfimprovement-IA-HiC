Human Mentors Retain Domain Monopoly Over Soft Power and Organizational Nuance

While students rely on AI for initial task execution, resume generation, and technical preparation, they explicitly reserve high-stakes career decisions and organizational navigation for human experts. AI tools cannot interpret office politics, manage interpersonal dynamics, or evaluate manager temperaments.
