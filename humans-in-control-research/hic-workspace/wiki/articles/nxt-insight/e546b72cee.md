One clear goal, one focal point, one CTA drives scannable conversion

Most flyers get ignored because people scan rather than read, and a strong design grabs attention, communicates one clear message, and tells readers exactly what to do [articles/venngage-blog/428ece5234.md#c1]. The rule is one focal point, one message, one next step, with 100 to 200 words plenty for most layouts [articles/venngage-blog/428ece5234.md#c3]. Asking readers to do too many things at once is a common mistake; pick one primary action and build around it [articles/venngage-blog/428ece5234.md#c4]. Email templates should carry a single benefit-driven headline and one unambiguous CTA.
