Provider directory inaccuracy defeats network-matching tools before AI can help

A pregnant patient called over 20 'in-network' OBs from a 52-provider list and none accepted her marketplace plan; WellPoint asked her to report the inaccurate directory entries herself. A separate patient saw 'again 0 providers for 98 miles' on a Florida Blue portal. Directory data this corrupt makes any AI care-navigation or provider-matching tool only as good as the underlying ground truth, a data-quality barrier that affects the consumer cohort directly.

I have now called 20+ providers. Most said they do NOT take my plan at all... None accepted my specific marketplace plan.
