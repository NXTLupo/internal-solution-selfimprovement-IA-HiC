Figma opens the canvas to AI agents via use_figma and generate_figma_design

As of the beta, Figma lets AI agents design directly on the canvas through two complementary tools: generate_figma_design translates live app/website HTML into editable Figma layers, and use_figma lets agents edit or create assets using a team's own components and variables. Figma frames these as a step forward for agent workflows, currently free during beta and slated to become usage-based paid. This is the foundational capability any extensibility white paper must anchor to.

Starting today, you can use AI agents to design directly on the Figma canvas. And with skills, you can guide agents with context about your team's decisions and intent.
