Undisclosed AI evaluation is outpacing transparency in candidate assessment

Resume Genius reports only 35% of hiring managers say their company always discloses when AI is used to evaluate candidates, while 20% say AI is used without any disclosure. Meanwhile 82% are concerned about candidates using AI in their applications. This two-sided transparency gap creates a compliance and trust exposure that any search firm deploying AI evaluation, including DSG, must manage in its client and candidate protocols.
