Executive Function Support Depends on Step Reduction Rather Than Instant Answers

Students with ADHD and executive function challenges reject AI tools that instantly generate finished answers. Value is created when assistants act as cognitive scaffolds, decomposing multi-week assignments into five-minute micro-tasks and enforcing structured pacing without replacing original human thought.
