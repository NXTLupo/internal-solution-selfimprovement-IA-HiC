Publishing skills to the team turns one designer's creative direction into a shared rule set

Figma lets designers publish skills so their thinking becomes part of how the whole team works; a design philosophy, a signature aesthetic, or a feedback lens turned into something any teammate can implement, learn from, and build on. Agent conversations are visible to file collaborators by default, so the iteration history becomes a shared resource, though it can be made private.
