Cross-state and out-of-network lab rules trap patients in billing logic

Adjudication rules tied to provider geography produce denials no consumer can anticipate. An Anthem PPO with a national BlueCard suitcase logo billed in-network labs out-of-network solely because the ordering doctor was in another state, an obscure rule not in the policy documents. The same logic recurs when a provider is acquired and silently changes network status. These hidden rule layers are the brittle base any payer AI either codifies or untangles.
