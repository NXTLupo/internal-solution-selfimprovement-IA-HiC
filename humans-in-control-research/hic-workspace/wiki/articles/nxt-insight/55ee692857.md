Staged campaign calendars beat one-shot sends by building anticipation before the offer

The November BFCM schedule sequences a soft-launch touchpoint, VIP early-access announcement, sneak peek, and hype email over days, warming the list before the hard sell. A well-executed Q4 can add 40% to annual revenue on this cadence. A Gen Z skincare brand should design its email templates as a modular sequence for product drops, not standalone one-offs.
