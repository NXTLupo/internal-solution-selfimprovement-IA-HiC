Human-Agent Alignment Boundaries Reserve Sovereign Authority for Critical Decisions

Users establish a explicit division of labor: AI handle preparation, administrative drafting, and scenario modeling, but humans retain absolute sovereignty over high-stakes career, financial, medical, and relational commitments. Empirical frameworks highlight agency and autonomy alignment as core requirements for successful human-agent collaboration.

It should not make irreversible medical, legal, financial or relational commitments without explicit authority. Its purpose is not maximum autonomous activity. Its purpose is useful continuity while the human remains sovereign.
