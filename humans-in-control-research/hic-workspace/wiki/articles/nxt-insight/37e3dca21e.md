Formulary and PBM churn destabilizes consumer drug access faster than members can track

Consumers report pharmacy benefits changing every four months without notice, GLP-1 formulary exclusions (CVS Caremark dropping Zepbound for Wegovy), and long-therapeutic drugs reclassified as complementary and dropped. A retired federal worker was told a years-effective therapy would no longer be covered. This rapid, opaque formulary volatility is a consumer-cohort barrier and a use case for AI formulary-monitoring and alerting tools.
