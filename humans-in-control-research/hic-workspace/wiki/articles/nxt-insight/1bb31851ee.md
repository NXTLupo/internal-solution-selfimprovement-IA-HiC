Crop insurance measurably buffers farm loan delinquency and lender balance sheets

FDIC analysis found a one-standard-deviation increase in crop insurance payouts reduces past-due farm loans by around 60 basis points, roughly 20% of typical delinquency fluctuations, while lowering banks' risk-weighted asset ratios. This is direct evidence that underwriting integration protects both individual farm solvency and agricultural credit institutions, meaning any contraction in underwriting viability under escalating climate risk would cascade into lender defaults.
