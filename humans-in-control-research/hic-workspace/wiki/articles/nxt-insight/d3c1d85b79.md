White space and restrained typography carry the premium signal

White space helps readers focus on the headline, offer and CTA and makes content easier to scan; if something feels crowded, remove content before shrinking text [articles/venngage-blog/428ece5234.md#c4]. Fonts and colors should make a layout easier to read, sticking to two or three fonts and prioritizing readability [articles/venngage-blog/428ece5234.md#c3]. This aligns with the earthy-minimalist skincare register of generous margins and typographic hierarchy over decoration [articles/nxt-insight/f439cbec48.md#c0]. Email templates should over-invest in negative space.
