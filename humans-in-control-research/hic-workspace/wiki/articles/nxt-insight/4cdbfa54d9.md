Provider-side claims work offshoring degrades quality the same way naive AI would

An insurance-industry insider states 'many claims are handled in Manila because it is cheaper' and first-level IT in India 'sort of fixes things but usually has to get escalated to North American support.' Another worker rejects offshore customer service 'for anything medical' citing poor coding skills. This cost-driven quality-degradation pattern is the precedent payers will follow with AI claims-handling, and the documented escalation failures are the operational risk AI deployment must avoid repeating for the payer cohort.

I do not agree with using offshore customer service for anything medical... when I explain why the claim denied it is clear they do not have great coding skills.
