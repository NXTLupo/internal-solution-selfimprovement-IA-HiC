Precision-ag adoption splits growers who can fund technology from those merely surviving the season

A precision-ag dealer roundtable identified a growing divide between growers who adopt as soon as technology is available and those who cannot justify investment in the current economy, where 2026 producers face fertilizer shortages, fuel uncertainty and persistently low commodity prices [articles/agr-precision-farming-dealer/1f2ba3fc2f.md#c2]. Dealers deploy enhanced trade-ins, refurbishment programs, rentals and try-before-you-buy to reduce perceived risk, and reframe the decision as adopt now versus adopt later [articles/agr-precision-farming-dealer/1f2ba3fc2f.md#c3]. Recent research suggesting precision-ag financial returns have been overstated compounds reluctance among strained operations.

There are those who adopt new technologies as soon as they become available and those who simply cannot justify additional investment in the current economic environment.
