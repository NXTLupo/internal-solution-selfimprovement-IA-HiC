Administrative agile frameworks are getting bypassed by hyper-lean delivery pairings

Heavy Scrum frameworks and administrative agile ceremonies are increasingly viewed as unnecessary overhead for small, AI-powered engineering teams. Micro-teams that pair one Product Manager and one Software Engineer in direct tandem can bypass complex backlogs and multi-week sprints. These hyper-lean teams define requirements quickly and iterate on-the-fly, establishing a massive velocity gap compared to traditional heavy-agency operations.
