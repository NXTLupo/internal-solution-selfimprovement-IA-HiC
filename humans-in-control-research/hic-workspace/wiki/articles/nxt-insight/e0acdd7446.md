Lack of harness engineering turns AI outputs into silent liabilities

Deploying generative tools without strong guardrails and automated quality gates turns rapid builds into silent engineering liabilities. Unverified outputs create massive codebases that lack structural coherence. Successful AI engineering requires a deep investment in testing harnesses and exit assertion rules.
