Independent reviews and Reddit, not brand pages, feed the AI recommendation engine

The same Marketing Week AI Visibility Audit logged 5,772 citations across ten CPG categories: the single most-cited source was the NHS website, Reddit turned up in all ten categories (the only source that did), and Instagram, Facebook and TikTok together managed 53 citations, under one percent. Just over half of everything cited came from independent authorities; under a quarter came from brand-owned pages and retailer listings. Backlinko shows how repeated consistent phrasing (UnderFit's 'stays tucked all day') gets a brand surfaced. Email campaigns for a skincare brand should drive verified reviews and consistent benefit language into the wider web.
