Algorithmic Claim Denials Outpace Clinical AI Adoption

Payers prioritize operational algorithms to automate claim denials, shifting the financial burden to patients. Insurance companies deploy proprietary algorithms that routinely overrule board-certified specialists during the prior authorization process. Furthermore, carriers integrate third-party pricing engines like Data iSight to algorithmically suppress reimbursement rates below standard contracted expectations. This adversarial use of automation defines the current operational integration reality.

But sure, let’s pretend an insurance algorithm knows more than a board-certified specialist who’s actually seen the patient.
