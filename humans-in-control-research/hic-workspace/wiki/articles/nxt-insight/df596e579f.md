Agency valuations suffer steep discounts from extreme founder dependency

Small agencies struggle to scale or secure high M&A valuation multiples due to a heavy reliance on individual founder relationships. When project details and operating logic remain undocumented, the firm becomes a liability rather than an asset. Acquirers heavily discount businesses that cannot operate independently.
