New World screwworm re-emergence is an existential threat to cattle and dairy despite 1966 eradication

UC ANR reports that the New World screwworm poses an existential threat to cattle farming and dairy production; the USDA officially declared the parasite eradicated from the US in 1966, but several notable outbreaks have occurred since, with one happening now. This is a live biosecurity tail risk for livestock underwriting that a 60-year-old eradication record had lulled the industry into discounting.
