A pre-built modular email design system beats per-campaign AI generation

Agency and practitioner voices in r/emailmarketing converge on establishing a small fixed set of reusable blocks (typography, spacing, CTA hierarchy, product modules) before letting AI assemble campaigns. Raw AI generators produce basic, off-brand layouts, and matching brand aesthetic from scratch each send is unreliable. The Branded Agency explicitly builds an email design system first, then constrains AI to iterate within it. For a template redesign this argues for locking modular blocks rather than regenerating aesthetics per email.
