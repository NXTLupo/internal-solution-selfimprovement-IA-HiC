Deepfakes and AI in applications are eroding trust in the candidate signal

Resume Genius reports 81% of hiring managers have encountered candidate AI use during recruitment, most often in resumes and cover letters (58%), and 17% report encountering deepfakes in a video interview. A detection gap appears by age, with one-third of Baby Boomers saying they never noticed candidate AI use. For executive search, this degrades the reliability of the raw candidate signal and raises the value of verified, relationship-sourced candidates and AI-assisted authenticity checks.

Deepfake use remains less common but is no longer marginal: 17% of hiring managers report encountering it during a video interview. [articles/resume-genius-blog/44849ef1e9.md#c2]
