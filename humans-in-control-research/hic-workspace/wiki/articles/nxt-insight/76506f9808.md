Plain-text thank-you emails out-earn every designed campaign for the year

A repeat email operator claims the plain-text gratitude email sent post-purchase outperforms every designed BFCM campaign, calling it the most lucrative send after roughly 100 uses. This challenges the assumption that a template redesign must be visually elaborate; for a Gen Z clean-beauty brand it argues for a stripped, sincere post-purchase touch alongside the branded flow. Community reaction was split, with some calling it a genuine loyalty move and others recycled advice.

I've sent this email nearly 100 times, and it almost always outperforms every email that was sent out during the ENTIRE YEAR.
