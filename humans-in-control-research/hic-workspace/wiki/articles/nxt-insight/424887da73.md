Wealthtech shifts to white-label APIs to bypass $300 client acquisition costs

Major institutions are opting to rent modular API platforms like WealthOS to embed fractional-share and complex rebalancing engines directly into existing mobile banking apps [articles/gemini-focused-research/33aec05c8b.md#c2].
