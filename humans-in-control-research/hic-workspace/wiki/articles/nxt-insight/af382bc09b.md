North American skew in digital CAC data obscures non-US distribution benchmarks

Global digital acquisition strategies are hindered by a structural lack of distribution benchmarks outside the US and UK. Highly granular statistics regarding TAMP adoption and digital CAC are heavily skewed toward North American markets. Consequently, alternative asset distribution platforms in Latin American and Asia-Pacific regions operate without baseline performance metrics. Firms expanding globally must build their own localized CAC models from scratch.
