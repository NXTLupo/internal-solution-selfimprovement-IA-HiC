Agriculture is the hardest sector to underwrite

Identifying, quantifying, pricing and managing risk is one of the most challenging aspects of smallholder agrifinance because diversity, volatility and limited data leave risks interconnected. In rural, data-poor environments lenders cannot isolate individual drivers of loss, undermining accurate underwriting.

Quantifying risk in agriculture is inherently difficult because of diversity, volatility and limited data availability in the sector
