Policy fragmentation splits agrifinance and insurance across separate ministries, stalling integrated coverage

In most countries agricultural insurance and agrifinance are governed by separate ministries or regulatory bodies, producing fragmented policy ownership and limited coordination. Agricultural insurance rarely receives the public funding lending programmes get, insurers remain underfunded with weak data systems, and few countries have frameworks that attract private participation at scale. Ethiopia's NAFIR roadmap and India's mandated integrated credit-insurance product are early counter-moves, but most initiatives are far from scale.
