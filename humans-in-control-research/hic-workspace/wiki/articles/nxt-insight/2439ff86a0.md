Outsourced and offshore claim handling deepens consumer distrust

Insurers route claims and first-level IT to lower-cost geographies, which members experience as opaque and unreachable. A worker explains claims are handled in Manila and first-level IT in India because it is cheaper, while a TPA system outage lasting two months left members showing as expired to their doctors. Crucially, one commenter notes employees are not the actual customers - employers are - so member happiness is irrelevant. This dynamic frames who 2026 payer AI is actually built to serve.
