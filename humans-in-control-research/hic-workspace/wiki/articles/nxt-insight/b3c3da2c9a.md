Inaccurate provider directories push patients to invoke 'directory reliance' as a coverage defense

Members repeatedly report directories listing nonexistent or impossibly distant in-network providers: a Florida Blue search surfaced only one physical therapist 98.6 miles away, and an Anthem member was billed after relying on the Sydney app's facility listing. Commenters now coach the 'directory reliance' and 'inaccurate provider directory' appeal language. Directory accuracy is a structured-data problem AI is positioned to maintain, and its current failure directly harms the consumer and caregiver cohorts seeking care.
