Embedded insurance bundles coverage by default, eroding consumer agency

Embedded finance auto-bundles insurance at the point of purchase, accepting terms in a tap without context, explanation, or alternatives. In India, first-time users encounter insurance bundled by default through agri-input apps and delivery platforms, with crop insurance auto-triggered on failure and health/accident cover embedded in gig apps. The critique is that inclusion without agency leaves users bearing risks they did not consciously choose. This reshapes coverage structure and perception as distribution shifts to embedded rails.
