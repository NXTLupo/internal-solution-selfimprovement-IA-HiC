Even government-guaranteed instruments carry residual default-risk nuance

A CFA ethics discussion clarifies that 'guaranteed' framing is only defensible for capital covered by explicit institutional or sovereign guarantees, and that even government bonds are only virtually - not absolutely - default-free, a reminder that no financial guarantee fully eliminates tail risk relevant to how farm-credit guarantees are marketed.
