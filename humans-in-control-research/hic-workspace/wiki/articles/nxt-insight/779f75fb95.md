Clear layer naming and locked aspect ratios are the preconditions for reliable template reuse

Figma's asset-template guidance shows that unnamed layers like Text 45 or Rectangle 12 leave editors unsure what they are updating and produce misplaced content, so layers must be renamed to labels like Event Title or CTA Button Text. It also directs locking aspect ratio on images and vectors so logos and illustrations do not distort when a template is adapted across formats. These are the low-level hygiene rules that let a component-mapping agent bind Figma layers to email tokens deterministically.

If your teammate sees a box like Text 45 or Rectangle 12, they won't know what they're updating, and you'll likely end up with misplaced content. [articles/figma-blog/0c58f20247.md#c2]
