Lack of advisor education forces 79% of Tier-I asset managers to deploy specialized product wholesaling teams

The complexity of distributing alternative assets has created an educational bottleneck for generalist advisors. Fully 75% of asset managers rank advisor education as their single largest obstacle in scaling alternatives distribution. To overcome this, 79% of Tier-I asset managers are restructuring their distribution models to include dedicated product specialists. These specialists provide the deep technical support needed to close complex private market allocations that generalist wholesalers cannot handle alone.
