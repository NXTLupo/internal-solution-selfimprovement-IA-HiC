Figma's defenders argue an AI prompt cannot be the versioned source of truth

Across two r/figmadesign threads (227 upvotes on one), designers argue Figma was never just a design tool but collaboration infrastructure: the single versioned source of truth where specs, handoff, tokens, and component libraries live. A prompt-generated UI has no versioning, no component system, and no shared context, so it solves speed of first draft, not truth of final output. This frames why Figma's agentic role is defensible even as one-prompt tools proliferate.

A prompt-generated UI has no versioning, no component system, no shared context between a designer and a developer. It's a screenshot with good lighting.
