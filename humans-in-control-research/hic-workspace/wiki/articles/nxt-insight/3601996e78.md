Platform optimization is treated as an ongoing discipline, not a one-time configuration project

Clockwork's operations guidance stresses that optimization is not a one-time project: firms getting the most value constantly refine workflows, audit platform usage, and identify gaps between process and configuration, because the team, clients, and process all evolve. Even small consistent changes meaningfully improve adoption, data quality, efficiency, and client delivery. This reframes AI adoption at DSG as continuous configuration and data-hygiene discipline rather than a single deployment event.
