Without parallel investment in climate-smart tech, insurers retreat from the most exposed farms

UNDP warns that finance is needed to enable farmers to adopt climate-smart technologies such as improved inputs, irrigation, storage and processing that reduce the underlying production risks insurers price. Without parallel investment in these underlying technologies, insurers are likely to retreat from serving the most exposed segments, further constraining access to finance and reinforcing a cycle of underinvestment and vulnerability. This is the reinforcing doom-loop at the heart of farm-solvency risk.
