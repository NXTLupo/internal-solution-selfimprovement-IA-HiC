Clean minimal earthy visual identity is now table stakes, not differentiation

A luxury skincare identity concept (Velora) on r/graphic_design leaned on an editorial aesthetic inspired by Rhode and Jacquemus, an earthy palette of Olive Leaf, Papaya Whip and Blush Rose, clean typography and minimal wordmark. The top comment warned it is 'well executed' but 'not breaking any new ground,' a solid identity that 'would work well for a brand targeting Sephora, Target.' The clean-organic look Gen Z skincare brands favor is saturated; email templates must earn distinction through voice and content, not minimalism alone.

I don't think you're breaking any new ground, but it's a solid brand identity that would work well for a brand targeting Sephora, Target. [articles/reddit-deep-r-graphic-design/a89e9298a4.md#c0]
