Orchestration agents triggered by one prompt sequence sub-agents into complete monthly workflows

Social Media Examiner describes Keith's orchestration agent Leo, triggered at the start of each month with one prompt, which knows which sub-agents to activate and in what order, creating asks in ClickUp, drafting emails, and starting projects while the human just reviews output, cutting two weeks to one hour. This is the concrete orchestration model for Deciem: a top-level agent that sequences design-extraction, assembly, personalization, and QA sub-agents on a single campaign trigger.

He gives Leo one prompt, and Leo knows exactly what to do, which sub-agents to activate, and in what order. [articles/social-media-examiner/9f8b1b0462.md#c1]
