Segmenting engaged subscribers protects deliverability more than blasting the full list

Operators warn against sending to an entire list at once, especially after irregular sending, because it lands mail in spam; the fix is segmenting and prioritizing engaged subscribers. A DTC founder separately notes emailing the same group repeatedly stops working without new list entrants. A template redesign only converts if it reaches the inbox, so segmentation logic is a design constraint, not an afterthought.

Don't blast out emails to your entire list at once ... Segment and prioritize your engaged subscribers to improve your chances of landing in the inbox.
