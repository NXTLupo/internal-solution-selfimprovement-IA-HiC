Production companion launches depend on synchronized institutional change management and UAT windows

Technical capability alone does not guarantee a successful production launch; institutional alignment and rigorous testing gates are critical dependencies. Northeastern's deployment protocol requires completing user acceptance testing (UAT) and delivering consolidated written feedback within the Stage 3 window [articles/client-uploads/5b7f886799.md#c11].
