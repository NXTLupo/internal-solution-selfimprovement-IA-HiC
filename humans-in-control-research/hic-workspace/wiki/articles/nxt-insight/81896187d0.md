Gamified, high-visibility CTAs and value cues drive opt-in conversion for beauty

A 2025 email benchmark study of 875 widgets across 214 sites and 14.7M impressions found gamification mechanics like Spin-to-Win, strong value communication, and centered high-visibility CTAs consistently outperform generic newsletter sign-ups, with seasonality amplifying uplift [social-intelligence/reddit-deep/emailmarketing/1ojjx7y.md#c0]. Beauty leads all categories in conversion. For the redesign, opt-in and CTA design, not just email body layout, is a measurable conversion lever.
