A dated BFCM email cadence, not a single blast, drives Q4 conversion

The r/emailmarketing BFCM guide, drawn from managing email for hundreds of brands, prescribes a day-by-day November schedule: teaser buildup, VIP early access, a no-pitch Thanksgiving gratitude email, then staggered launch, mid-push, and last-call sends with countdown urgency. A well-executed Q4 can add 40% to annual revenue. The template redesign must support this sequence architecture (VIP variants, plain-text gratitude, urgency modules), not just a one-off promo layout.
