Dumping unreviewed AI deliverables erodes professional trust and shifts liability

Practitioners are increasingly utilizing generative models to compile large technical guides and playbooks in minutes. Passing these documents directly to clients without intensive internal review, however, shifts immense operational risk and quality ownership onto the client. When migration or development efforts fail due to unverified AI errors, professional credibility is destroyed, and teams face immediate contractual liability.
