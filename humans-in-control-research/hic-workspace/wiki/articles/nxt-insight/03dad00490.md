Excessive multi-round hiring processes undercut the same firms claiming talent shortages

Veteran talent-acquisition practitioners argue that firms lamenting talent shortages sabotage themselves with excessive, poorly-run hiring processes and six-round interview loops. This sits against JLL's finding that for the first time in 15 years of Future of Work research, talent constraints surpassed financial constraints as the leading barrier to transformation. The tension frames process-efficiency AI (scheduling, interview synthesis, structured evaluation) as a way to shorten cycles without lowering the bar.
