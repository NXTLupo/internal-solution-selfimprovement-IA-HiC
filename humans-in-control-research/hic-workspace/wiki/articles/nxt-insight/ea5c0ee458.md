Students use AI as interactive conceptual tutors rather than answer generators

Students derive genuine learning value when using AI to explain complex concepts or critique draft work [articles/reddit-deep-r-chatgpt/bf78c8fa2d.md#c1]. Conversely, copy-pasting raw AI outputs leads to passive retention loss [articles/reddit-deep-r-chatgpt/bf78c8fa2d.md#c1]. Students prefer guided learning modes that challenge their thinking over tools that provide immediate answers [articles/reddit-deep-r-chatgpt/bf78c8fa2d.md#c1].

If I paste my draft and ask it to point out gaps or explain stuff I missed, I actually remember more; if I just copy answers, it goes in one ear out the other [articles/reddit-deep-r-chatgpt/bf78c8fa2d.md#c1]
