Transition from Disposable Chat Prompts to Persistent Sovereign Counterparts

Users are evolving beyond single-session prompting toward persistent personal assistants with auditable memory, private cognitive graphs, and non-vendor-locked memory storage. Rather than relying on ephemerally summarized context windows, users adopt local or private databases to maintain lifelong personal continuity. This shift transforms AI from a transactional tool into a long-term personal counterpart.

A bounded AI counterpart that maintains a structured model of one human over time and remains accountable to that human.
