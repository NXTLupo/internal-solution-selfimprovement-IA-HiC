A generational AI-adoption gap splits how deeply hiring managers embed AI in decisions

Resume Genius data shows resume-screening AI adoption ranges from 68% of Gen Z hiring managers to 45% of Baby Boomers, with AI-assisted video interviews used by 40% of Gen Z versus 10% of Boomers. 20% of Baby Boomers and 17% of Gen X use no AI in recruitment at all. For a search firm, this generational split shapes both change-management inside the firm and how client hiring teams will receive AI-augmented deliverables.
