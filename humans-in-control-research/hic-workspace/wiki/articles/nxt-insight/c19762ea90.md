Rigid product roadmaps fail to survive high-frequency AI iterations

Traditional 12-month product roadmaps fail because rapid prototyping tools enable companies to launch features in days rather than quarters. Sticking to static plans prevents teams from reacting to high-frequency beta user feedback. Product leadership must adopt an off-road mindset that prioritizes real-time adaptation.

But when we follow a roadmap prescriptively, we run the risk of holding too tightly to a specific direction—even if it’s no longer the right path forward. [articles/figma-blog/bb4e73728c.md#c1]
