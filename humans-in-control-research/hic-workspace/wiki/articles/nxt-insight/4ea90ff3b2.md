Only 50% of wealth management firms maintain sufficient technological infrastructure to automate alternative asset repor

Despite the massive influx of capital into private credit and private equity, the operational backbone supporting these investments remains critically deficient. Exactly half of wealth management firms rely on manual, error-prone processes because they lack third-party technology systems capable of overseeing and reporting on illiquid alternatives. Upgrading this data infrastructure represents the most urgent operational benchmark for firms seeking to capture HNW market share.

Only 50% of wealth management firms use third-party technology systems to oversee, report on, and automate illiquid alternative assets.
