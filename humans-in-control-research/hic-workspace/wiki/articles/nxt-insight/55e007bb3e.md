Collaborative canvas workspaces are dissolving linear design-to-code workflows

The division between visual design and software implementation is dissolving as modern visual tools support code layers directly on the canvas. With AI agents parameterizing assets, developers and designers can iterate on live code, interactive shaders, and logic in real-time, side-by-side. This shifts product creation from sequential handoffs to non-linear, collaborative canvas experimentation.

The canvas is more than where your work lives. It’s also where everything connects. [articles/figma-blog/11dc555e29.md#c1]
