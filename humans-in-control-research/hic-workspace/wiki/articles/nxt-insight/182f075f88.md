Short-form video is the awareness engine Gen Z email must reference and repurpose

Wyzowl's 2026 research found 93% of video marketers say video increased brand awareness, and Hootsuite flags short-form video as the clearest current strategy example. Semrush and UXmatters describe repurposing video into bite-sized cross-channel content. For a Gen Z skincare brand, email templates should embed or link short-form video (tutorials, routines) rather than relying on static hero images alone.
