Traditional black box delivery models sour client trust

Traditional agencies rely on slow, opaque delivery where clients wait weeks to see drafts that ultimately miss requirements. This black-box method is being replaced by real-time co-design and rapid weekly feedback sessions. Modern consultants build trust by embedding directly in customer workstreams.

They are done with the “black box” method: getting grilled with questions, having to wait a week for a simple build, only for it to miss the mark on what was truly needed. [articles/reddit-salesforce/fe14ca7d38.md#c1]
