Data Sovereignty Concerns and Granular Permission Expectations for Personal Data

Users express anxiety over how public AI platforms handle uploaded personal, financial, and behavioral data. While consumers willingly input data to achieve immediate utility, they demand clear deletion rights, local processing guarantees, and robust safeguards against institutional surveillance or unexpected model training.

I think the key question isn’t whether AI exists, but whether companies are transparent about data use and give users real control.
