Academic Evaluation Value Shifts from Answer Generation to Inquiry Quality

As generative models make basic answer synthesis instant and cheap, educators and admissions evaluators are re-centering assessment around student inquiry skills. Grading focuses on a student's ability to formulate deep questions, reason through complexity, and demonstrate authentic personal voice.

In a world in which answers are increasingly easy to generate, the more important skill is knowing what to ask. [articles/hechinger-report-rss/8f301402df.md#c4]
