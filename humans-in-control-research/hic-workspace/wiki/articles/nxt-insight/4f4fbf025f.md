Executive search software is shifting from static databases to lifecycle workflow control

Clockwork frames executive-search performance around search speed, placement rate, client experience, team alignment, and long-term data value, and separates workflow-driven platforms from database-driven systems. [articles/exs-clockwork/8ad772998f.md#c1] Recruiterflow adds that larger firms need reporting on recruiter productivity, pipeline conversion, search performance, business development activity, and placements. [articles/exs-recruiterflow/db18516ca2.md#c1] This lifecycle-workflow framing is where AI use cases attach for DSG: business development through kickoff to final placement.
