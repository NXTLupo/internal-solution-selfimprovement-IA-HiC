Gen Z trusts influencer content over ads, rerouting the discovery-to-purchase path

Sprout Social's influence report finds 81% of younger consumers made an influencer-inspired purchase in the last year and 49% of Gen Z prefer influencer content over traditional ads versus 15% preferring ads. For a Gen Z clean-beauty brand, this means email templates should feature creator/UGC modules and social proof rather than polished ad-style hero shots, since influencers act as both discovery engine and decision driver.

49% of Gen Z say they prefer influencer content over traditional ads, with only 15% preferring the latter.
