Retention flows, packaging and rewards drive repeat DTC purchases more than one-time conversion

The topic-cluster of DTC operators converges on post-purchase flows, packaging and rewards as the real drivers of repeat purchase. A snack-brand founder plateaued at ~$25k/month with Klaviyo flows and Meta ads and is told return-customer rate and retention are the salvageable levers. For a clean-beauty redesign, transactional and post-purchase email templates (order confirmation, replenishment, rewards) deserve as much design investment as the promo campaign.

Email is great for retention ... Otherwise you risk keep sending the same emails to the same group of people, which will stop working one day.
