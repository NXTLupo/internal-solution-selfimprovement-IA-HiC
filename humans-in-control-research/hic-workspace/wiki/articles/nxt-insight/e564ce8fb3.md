Manual re-entry recurs when intake systems fail to coordinate front-desk and downstream records

Across the corpus the dominant operational failure pattern is data not flowing between an intake point and the system that finally acts on it, forcing a human to re-key or chase information. A self-pay patient was repeatedly told a bill would be mailed and never received it; a recent grad describes co-op work doing manual data and healthcare-process entry. The same coordination gap maps directly onto admissions-to-enrollment handoffs an AI capability would automate. This friction is the operational consequence any shared-service deployment is meant to remove.

Every single time I go to the clinic, I go up to the front desk. Tell them I don't have insurance and that I'm self pay.
