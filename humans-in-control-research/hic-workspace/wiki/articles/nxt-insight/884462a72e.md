AI-generated design reads as inauthentic and taxes brand trust

Content perceived as AI-generated is judged less authentic and less trustworthy than identical human-made content, per Venngage's analysis of the 'AI slop' backlash. The Salty Otter café faced customer questions about its values after revealing an AI logo. For a clean, organic skincare brand courting Gen Z, this trust tax is a direct design constraint: templates that look machine-made undercut the 'natural and honest' positioning the brand sells on.
