Brand foundation (palette, fonts, logo) must precede AI-assisted design or output fractures

Social Media Examiner reports that before opening any AI tool the foundational work is defining brand meaning, audience and core visual elements (color palettes, fonts, logos); without that grounding AI output doesn't hold together across pieces, as a fashion brand with only scattered assets discovered. A Scandinavian cosmetic student CD project models this discipline with a single typeface (Work Sans) and a constrained blue/white/black palette. A clean-beauty template redesign should lock tokens first so every AI-generated variant stays on-brand.

Without that grounding, AI tools produce output that doesn't hold together across pieces.
