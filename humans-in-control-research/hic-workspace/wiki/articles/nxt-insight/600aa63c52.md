Students demand Bring-Your-Own-AI context layers over institution-locked tools

Evidence reveals a clear shift away from static institutional AI destinations toward user-owned context layers that persist across applications. Students leverage tools like open-context and Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers to maintain persistent memory, communication preferences, and personal history across Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini. This decoupled model treats AI as a personal tech layer traveling with the user across institutions.
