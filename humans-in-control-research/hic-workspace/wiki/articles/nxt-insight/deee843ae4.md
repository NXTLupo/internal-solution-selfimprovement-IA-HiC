Countdown timers and scarcity language convert when the deadline is real

Practitioners embed countdown timers (via tools like Sendtric) and FOMO language with clear start and end dates, sending reminders as the window closes, to drive time-sensitive checkout. The BFCM schedule stages this across sneak-peek, hype, and reminder emails. For a Gen Z skincare launch or drop, real scarcity mechanics belong in the template's hero, but only when the deadline is genuine, avoiding trust erosion.
