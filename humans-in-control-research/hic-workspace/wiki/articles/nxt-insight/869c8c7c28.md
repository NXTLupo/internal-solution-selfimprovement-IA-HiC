Plain-text gratitude emails outperform every polished sales blast of the year

An experienced email operator on r/emailmarketing reports the plain-text Thanksgiving/thank-you email, sent with no sale and no pitch, 'almost always outperforms every email that was sent out during the ENTIRE YEAR' across nearly 100 sends. A parallel r/marketing case saw an authentic 'Oops!' apology email generate over $20,000, the brand's biggest single-email revenue. Both point to humility and authenticity beating heavily designed promotional templates, a direct signal for how much visual polish a Gen Z skincare template actually needs.

This is your chance to send out a plain text email and simply express gratitude... it almost always outperforms every email that was sent out during the ENTIRE YEAR. [articles/reddit-deep-r-emailmarketing/2bedfdff01.md#c3]
