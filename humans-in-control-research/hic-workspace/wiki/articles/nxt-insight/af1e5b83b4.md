Proactive Autonomous Execution Triggers Backlash Without Explicit Escalation Paths

Market feedback around fully autonomous AI agents reveals that minimal human intervention is primarily an operations and safety challenge rather than a model feature. Unchecked proactive behavior risks automating errors, sending unauthorized messages, or spending budgets when tool calls fail or credentials expire. Systems operating effectively rely on explicit spending limits, durable event logging, idempotent execution, and automated escalation paths to human operators. For higher-ed advising, proactive nudges must be tightly bounded to prevent unwanted surveillance perceptions.
