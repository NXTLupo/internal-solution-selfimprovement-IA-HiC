Job Seekers Replace Mass Automation with Targeted AI Workflow Systems

Early-career job seekers report that full application automation creates spam and low-quality submissions. Successful job hunting strategies combine structured application tracking with specialized AI prep for technical and behavioral interviews. Job seekers rely on AI to build reusable bullet banks and run timed SQL or case drills rather than spray-and-pray applications.

I’d be careful with full application automation, because a lot of it just creates more noise and lower-quality applications. [articles/reddit-deep-r-jobs/b52b8d1014.md#c0]
