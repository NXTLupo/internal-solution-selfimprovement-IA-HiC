Enterprise and Institutional AI Policy Demands Governance at the Data Layer

As AI agents perform autonomous actions, organizational governance must move into underlying data platforms rather than relying on prompt instructions. Security leaders require digital leashes, explicit role-based scopes, and real-time intervention capability to halt out-of-policy calls before execution. Carrying federated user identity across gateways ensures auditability and prevents unauthorized system modifications.

When agents act on their own, governance has to live in the data layer [articles/venturebeat-ai/25983c28eb.md#c3]
