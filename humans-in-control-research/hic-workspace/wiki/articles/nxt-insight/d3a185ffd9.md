Multi-agent supervisor-worker architecture splits the analytical chain into specialized single-step agents

AWS's supply-chain implementation with a global footwear and apparel retailer used a Supervisor Agent to interpret intent and orchestrate specialized Worker Agents, each handling one step, because splitting responsibilities was essential for accuracy. Salesforce's Siemens deployment similarly broke lead qualification into a coordinated multi-agent workflow where one agent engages and a second gathers and routes data. This directly models how an email pipeline should decompose into design-extraction, component-assembly, personalization, and QA agents rather than one monolithic agent.
