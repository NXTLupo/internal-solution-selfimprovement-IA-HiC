Patients must self-advocate and document everything as defensive practice

Across cases, the consensus survival tactic is relentless documentation: screenshot the in-network directory, record call dates and rep names, file Department of Insurance complaints, and escalate to legislators and CEOs. One compliance-veteran commenter says change only happens when people consistently appeal and involve regulators. This is the consumer-cohort behavior pattern AI tools could either automate (claim navigation assistants) or be weaponized against.
