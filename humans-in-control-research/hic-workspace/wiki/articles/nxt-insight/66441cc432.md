Designers report AI improves craft even as it destabilizes the workflow

Figma's State of the Designer 2026, an 906-designer NewtonX survey, reports 91% say new AI tools improve their designs, 89% say they work faster, and 80% collaborate better, with AI-leaning designers 25% more likely to be satisfied at work. This optimism sits alongside the practitioner frustration about token drift, framing the demand side: designers want agentic help that raises craft, which the approval and critic extension points must preserve rather than dilute.
