Students Trade AI Shortcut Efficiency for Deep Cognitive Learning

Students widely adopt AI for note synthesis, research overviews, and draft generation, but struggle with cognitive dilution. Educators and practitioners observe that using AI to generate study materials skips the struggling process necessary for memory retention and idea formulation. Consequently, students and advisors seek frameworks that position AI as a thinking sparring partner rather than a task bypass.
