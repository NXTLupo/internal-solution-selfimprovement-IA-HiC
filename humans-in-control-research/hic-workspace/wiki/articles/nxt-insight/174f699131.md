Students Demand AI Coaching for Task Breakdown while Preserving Human Judgment

Students and neurodivergent learners rely on AI tools as cognitive partners to overcome executive dysfunction and break complex assignments into manageable steps. However, learners reject tools that generate completed work without requiring critical thinking. Students prefer AI to act as a structured coach while leaving analysis, synthesis, and final decisions to human judgment.

They could use AI as a coach, but they would still need to analyze the information, explain their reasoning, check their claims, and make the final decision themselves.
