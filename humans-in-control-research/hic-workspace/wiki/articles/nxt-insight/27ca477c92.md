For DTC, the ESP's brand kit is the enforcement layer for template consistency

A r/emailmarketing agency respondent separates the decision into platform versus design system, recommending Klaviyo for ecommerce/DTC because it pairs automation with reusable brand styles for logos, fonts, colors, and buttons, or Mailchimp's Brand Kit which auto-applies visual identity across templates. Beefree is named as the design layer for creative flexibility. The redesign choice is not just visual, it must live inside an ESP that enforces the brand system automatically.
