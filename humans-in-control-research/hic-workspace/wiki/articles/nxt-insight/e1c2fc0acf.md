Failure attribution in multi-agent systems is unsolved, so trace logs must be designed in

PSU, Duke, Google DeepMind, and collaborators show that multi-agent systems commonly fail at a task despite a flurry of activity, leaving developers unable to say which agent at what point was responsible. They describe sifting interaction logs as finding a needle in a haystack that halts iteration and optimization. An email pipeline of Figma-reader, component-mapper, MJML-composer, and QA agents needs deliberate attribution and audit logging to be debuggable at all.
