Mobile rendering of custom blocks is the hidden cost of a modular template

A practitioner in r/emailmarketing warns that getting the initial custom blocks to render correctly on mobile takes longer than writing the campaigns themselves, but once set the system is repeatable. This is the operational friction a Gen Z-facing brand must budget for, since Gen Z consumes email predominantly on phones. The finding tempers the modular-system enthusiasm: the payoff is real but the upfront mobile QA is the bottleneck.
