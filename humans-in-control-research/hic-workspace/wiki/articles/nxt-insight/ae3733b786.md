Automated first-pass denials force applicants into manual appeal loops

Across r/HealthInsurance threads, members describe insurers auto-denying claims on first submission, then requiring repeated human appeals to reverse. Commenter Alisha_Nat notes carriers 'use an automated process to automatically deny many claims when they are first submitted.' This pattern of automation-without-resolution is the inverse of the research objective's goal: AI that accelerates rather than obstructs a long approval cycle. It is adjacent context for designing enrollment automation that does not replicate denial-loop friction.

Often insurance companies use an automated process to automatically deny many claims when they are first submitted, esp for emergencies.
