Human fingerprints outsignal technical polish in a feed flooded with AI output

A Sidebar designer essay argues that in a world buried in 'competent' frictionless AI output, results that signal human love and idiosyncrasy 'will get louder' because scarcity and authenticity do that, the reason a hand-lettered sign stops people when a printed one does not. This reframes the skincare email redesign around evidence of a human hand, hand-drawn marks, founder voice, deliberate imperfection, as the conversion lever for a Gen Z audience already trained to filter synthetic content.

In a world flooded with AI output, results that signal human love and fingerprints will get louder. Scarcity does that. So does authenticity. [articles/sidebar-io/852fd667f6.md#c1]
