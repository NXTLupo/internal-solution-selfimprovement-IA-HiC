Satellite, IoT and AI modeling as fix and barrier

Remote sensing, IoT soil-moisture and crop-health sensors, blockchain and AI weather forecasting are pitched to improve risk assessment and reduce basis risk. Yet these presuppose connectivity, data infrastructure and farmer digital literacy the corpus flags as scarce, making technological adoption itself a barrier rather than a given.

The integration of insurance with IoT devices, such as soil moisture sensors and crop health monitors, can provide real-time data for more precise insurance coverage, and reduce basis risk
