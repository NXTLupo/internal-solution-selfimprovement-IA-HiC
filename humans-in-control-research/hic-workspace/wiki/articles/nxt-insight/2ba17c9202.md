Unchecked AI tool reliance induces 'prompt exhaustion' and analytical overfitting

Heavy day-to-day engagement with generative AI tools is introducing cognitive friction, specifically characterized as prompt exhaustion. Furthermore, younger analysts allowed open access to AI models show a distinct pattern of overfitting on raw data catalogs. This manifests as a tendency to populate reports with immense volumes of unverified, overconfident metrics that lack contextual interpretation or critical human review.
