Executive search platforms are optimized for outbound relationship work, not high-volume inbound recruiting

Clockwork separates contingent ATS platforms (built for high-volume inbound) from executive-search platforms (built for outbound, research-driven, relationship-based execution). [articles/exs-clockwork/8ad772998f.md#c1] This distinction is load-bearing for DSG's AI strategy: proven high-volume recruiting AI does not transfer cleanly to retained search. Bersin separately calls talent-acquisition AI a mature market citing Eightfold, Paradox, and Maki, underscoring the gap between commodity recruiting AI and retained-search needs.
