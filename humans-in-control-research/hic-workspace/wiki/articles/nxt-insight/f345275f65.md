The inbox must feel like a seamless continuation of the website and brand

Practitioners warn that AI-assisted email automation gradually turns every campaign into a different-looking brand unless the inbox experience feels like a continuation of the website and wider brand [social-intelligence/reddit-deep/emailmarketing/1voakip.md#c2]. Marketers reach for pre-made template systems (Stripo, Klaviyo, Emailify inside Figma) to keep visual consistency [social-intelligence/reddit-deep/emailmarketing/1voakip.md#c3]. For the redesign, email and web must share tokens so the template reads as one coherent brand across touchpoints.
