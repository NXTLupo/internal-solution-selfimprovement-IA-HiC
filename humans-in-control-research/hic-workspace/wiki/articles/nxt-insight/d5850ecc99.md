Black-Box Delivery Model Exhausts Clients and Drives Churn

Clients are increasingly exhausted by traditional agency approaches that keep delivery inside a black box. Getting grilled with questions and waiting a week for simple builds often results in deliverables that miss the mark [articles/reddit-salesforce/fe14ca7d38.md#c1]. To solve this, providers are shifting toward embedded delivery with regular work sessions and real-time iteration [articles/reddit-salesforce/fe14ca7d38.md#c1].
