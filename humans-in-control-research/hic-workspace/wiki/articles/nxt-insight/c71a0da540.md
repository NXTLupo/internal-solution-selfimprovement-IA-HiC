Governance documents and prompt-template libraries are the building blocks of an agentic workflow

Webflow enumerates the building blocks of an agentic workflow: tools and integrations, an agent core running a thought-action-observation loop, governance documents (policy and SOPs uploaded to keep the model inside company guidelines), prompt-template libraries of potential actions, and memory systems retaining lessons from every interaction. These map one-to-one onto the components the white paper must specify for Figma skills, critics, and hooks.

Governance documents: Resources, like policy documents and standard operating procedures, that you upload to teach the machine learning model how to stay within company guidelines.
