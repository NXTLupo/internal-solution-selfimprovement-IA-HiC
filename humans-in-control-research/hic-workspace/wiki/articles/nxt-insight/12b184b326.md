Gen Z detects and scrolls past AI-generated imagery, cutting conversion

A consumer trust report cited on r/marketing found 78% of Gen Z can identify AI-generated images and will scroll past them. An apparel brand targeting 18-25 year-olds saw CTR drop 40% after switching to AI lifestyle shots, then recover when it returned to real product photos. For a clean organic skincare brand, this argues for authentic, real product and human photography in email hero blocks rather than synthetic renders. Coca-Cola's repeated AI Christmas ads drew the same 'slop' backlash.
