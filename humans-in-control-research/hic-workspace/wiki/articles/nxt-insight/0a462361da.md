Inflation and elevated interest rates are absorbed by cash-strapped policyholders stripping coverage they later regret

Synthesis of r/insurance practitioner evidence shows macroeconomic strain reshaping coverage selection downward: households strip add-ons, double deductibles to save marginal dollars, and absorb escrow-driven premium hikes. Cash-strapped policyholders present a heightened risk profile through deferred property maintenance and desperation-driven claims. For farmers facing the same inflation and rate environment, this predicts a high-risk pool of under-insured operations, directly threatening underwriting viability and solvency.
