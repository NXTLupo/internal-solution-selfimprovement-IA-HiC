Brands now optimize for two audiences at once: human shoppers and the AI that recommends them

Total Retail frames the strategic shift as e-commerce teams optimizing simultaneously for human shoppers and the AI systems shaping what those shoppers see, replacing the question 'how do we get more visitors' with 'would AI confidently recommend us'. Backlinko and Semrush reinforce that consistent, well-sourced brand mentions feed both search and LLM citations. Skincare email content and product copy must be structured, attribute-rich, and quotable for AI systems.
