Clean, mobile-first templates with one CTA outperform busy promotional layouts

Practitioners in r/emailmarketing anchor high-converting design to restraint: branded, easy-to-read, mobile-friendly layouts that funnel to a single call-to-action. The advice treats template cleanliness and flow as the conversion mechanism, not decoration. This directly informs a Gen Z clean-beauty redesign where the brand aesthetic and minimalism are the product story.

Make sure your emails are branded, easy to read, and mobile friendly ... stick to one clear CTA (Call to Action).
