Reinsurance capital abundance is temporarily softening property rates despite structural risk

After no hurricanes struck Florida in 2025, reinsurers posted strong profits and capital providers want money fully deployed, putting downward pressure on prices, evidenced by 15% to 25% reductions many Florida insurers achieved at June 1 renewals. US property catastrophe reinsurance rates peaked in 2023 at more than double their 2017 trough and have retraced only a portion of post-2017 increases. Legal reforms and improving reinsurance conditions are moderating Florida premiums, but the executive notes risks could reverse the trend. This creates a dangerous window: pricing softens on quiet-year capital dynamics while the underlying climate loss trend keeps building, a mismatch that matters for multi-year agricultural underwriting.

That trend is evident in the average 15% to 25% reductions many Florida insurers achieved at June 1 renewals.
