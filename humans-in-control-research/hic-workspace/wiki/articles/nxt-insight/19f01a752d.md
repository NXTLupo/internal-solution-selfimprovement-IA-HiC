Subject lines win by choosing either blunt directness or personalized curiosity

The BFCM guide from an operator managing hundreds of brands defines exactly two working subject-line modes: extreme directness ('Our Black Friday Sale Starts NOW') or manufactured curiosity ('We're giving away gifts to people in {{Customers_City}}'), with personalization tokens boosting open rate. This is a concrete, testable design input for a Gen Z template's preview-and-subject system rather than vague 'compelling copy' advice.
