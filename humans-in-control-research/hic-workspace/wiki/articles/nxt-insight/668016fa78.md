Plan-exclusion legalese forces patients to self-adjudicate SPD language against denials

Members are increasingly parsing Summary Plan Descriptions themselves: a Cigna TMJ MRI denial turns on whether CPT 70336 imaging is excluded when the SPD only excludes TMJ 'treatment,' and a gynecomastia denial hinges on 'activities of daily living' definitions. Commenters cite specific page numbers and ADL definitions. This document-interpretation burden is a leading consumer-facing AI use case (plan-language Q&A), and its absence today leaves patients doing manual ERISA interpretation.

The SPD does NOT say... Imaging of TMJ is excluded... It excludes treatment.
