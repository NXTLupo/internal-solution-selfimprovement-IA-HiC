Consumers cannot get a price before care, the transparency gap AI assistants are positioned to close

Payers refuse pre-service cost estimates to non-members as policy, and even members cannot get reliable estimates because billed-versus-allowed amounts vary by provider, location, and contract. One consumer compared it to a restaurant refusing to state a price until after payment. This information-asymmetry is a core consumer-cohort friction that AI cost-estimation and benefits-navigation tools claim to solve.
