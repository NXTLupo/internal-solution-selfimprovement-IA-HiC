Surging Client Acquisition Costs Force Shift to White-Label API Platforms

Direct-to-consumer client acquisition costs for standalone robo-advisors frequently exceed $300 per account, eroding profitability. To circumvent these surging costs, the market size for white-label API platforms is projected to surpass direct software licensing. Major players are opting to rent rather than build, embedding fractional-share and rebalancing engines directly into established mobile banking applications to capture users without direct marketing spend.
