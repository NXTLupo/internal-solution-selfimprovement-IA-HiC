Prior-authorization delay becomes a clinical risk for time-sensitive care

Patients document prior-auth bottlenecks that threaten life. A Cigna cancer surgery was cancelled the night before because no nurse reviewer was even assigned 48 hours past a 72-hour deadline, and voiding the authorization would restart a 5-10 business-day clock. A Myasthenia Gravis patient faces hospitalization while a denied daily injection sits in 30-60 day appeal. For payers, any AI that accelerates or further automates prior-auth must be measured against the harm slow human-plus-automated review already causes.
