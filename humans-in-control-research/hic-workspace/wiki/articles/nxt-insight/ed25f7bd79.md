Real-Time Value Alignment Maintains User Trust in High-Risk Guidance Scenarios

Human trust in autonomous AI systems depends on dynamic value alignment, particularly when task risk is elevated. Adaptive strategies using inverse reinforcement learning match AI goals to user priorities in real time, sustaining perceived performance. In high-stakes institutional or career contexts, static prior assumptions fail to maintain trust without active personalization.

Results from the simulation study show that alignment of values is important for trust when the overall risk level of the task is high. [articles/arxiv-cs-ro/620658f3c8.md#c0]
