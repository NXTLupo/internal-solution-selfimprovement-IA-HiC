Broad-based annual giving relies on optimizing retention movement rather than isolated gift acquisition

Sustainable fundraising growth requires analyzing donor movement categories; including renewed, lapsed, reactivated, upgraded, maintained, and downgraded donors; rather than evaluating single-transaction gift totals [articles/client-uploads/5114fee1d5.md#c2][articles/client-uploads/0fec271931.md#c2]. Broad-based annual giving teams targeting roughly 400,000 alumni require scalable cohort segmentation to improve retention and long-term donor lifetime value [articles/client-uploads/0fec271931.md#c0][articles/client-uploads/0fec271931.md#c1].

This shows movement, not just a single rate: who renewed, lapsed, reactivated, upgraded, maintained, or downgraded, and in which reliable cohorts.
