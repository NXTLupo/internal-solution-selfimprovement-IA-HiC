Post-enrolment neglect and delayed payouts drive farmer churn, making retention not sign-up the real battle

The CGIAR brief stresses that efforts should not cease after sign-up: many farmers feel neglected post-enrolment, leading to dissatisfaction, and delayed payout processes force long waits for compensation that damage trust. It calls for constant during- and after-sales engagement through digital channels, extension visits and bundled agro-advisory, plus localized communication via native-language radio. This reframes agricultural insurance viability around lifecycle engagement and education rather than one-time acquisition.
