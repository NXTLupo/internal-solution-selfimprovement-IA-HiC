Plan-language ambiguity becomes the payer's adjudication lever against consumers

Consumers document payers exploiting interpretive ambiguity: a TMJ exclusion limited to treatment used to deny diagnostic MRI explicitly listed as covered, and a member uncertain whether a specialist copay applied before deductible. These disputes turn on plan-document interpretation, the exact natural-language understanding task AI plan-navigation and adjudication tools are marketed to perform for both payer and consumer cohorts.
