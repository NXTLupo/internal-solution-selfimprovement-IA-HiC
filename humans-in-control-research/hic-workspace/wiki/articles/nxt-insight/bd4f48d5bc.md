IoT and AI underwriting tools presuppose rural connectivity and digital literacy that the field lacks

Insurers pitch soil-moisture sensors, crop-health monitors, and AI weather forecasting to reduce basis risk, but these presuppose connectivity, data infrastructure, and farmer digital literacy the corpus flags as scarce [articles/hic-theme-synthesis/0649f90dec.md#c3]. The result is that the technological fix becomes itself an adoption barrier for lower-capacity personas. Precision underwriting stalls not on actuarial sophistication but on physical rural infrastructure [articles/hic-theme-synthesis/0649f90dec.md#c3]. Reddit farmer sentiment reinforces that large-scale operators plan inputs far ahead while smaller growers are exposed [social-intelligence/reddit-deep/stocks/1s92kzr.md#c8].
