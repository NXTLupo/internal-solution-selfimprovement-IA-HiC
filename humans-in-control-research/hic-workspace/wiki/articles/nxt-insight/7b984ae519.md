Private equity monopolizes TAMP distribution toll booths

Private equity firms have aggressively acquired major TAMPs, treating them as critical distribution toll booths. Bain Capital's $4.5 billion purchase of Envestnet and GTCR's $2.7 billion privatization of AssetMark highlight this shift. Giant platforms like SEI and Orion now control over $100 billion in assets each. This consolidation creates insurmountable barriers for asset managers relying solely on legacy wholesaler models.
