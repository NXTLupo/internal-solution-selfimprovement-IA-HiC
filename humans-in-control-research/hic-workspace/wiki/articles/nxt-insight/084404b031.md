Engagement-based segmentation and personalization beat generic mass sends

A Dubai email agency segments lists into whales, potential whales, loyal customers, and lapsed cohorts, sending up to eight emails per day each to a unique segment [articles/practical-ecommerce/9338e2ed7d.md#c1]. NXT-Insight cites Nike and Apple segmenting into narrow lifestyle groups, with McKinsey data on personalization expectations [articles/nxt-insight/8baf3db7c5.md#c0]. For a Gen Z skincare template this argues for dynamic, skin-concern-segmented blocks rather than one send to everyone.

you can split up your customer or subscriber list into many different segments. There's no overlap. It's one email per person.
