Claims adjudication failures force patients into multi-call manual rework

Insured patients repeatedly describe spending dozens of phone calls and hours on hold to correct mis-adjudicated claims, with one BCBS IL member spending 50 minutes on hold before a rep reversed a denial that a later rep re-imposed. Another patient reports it 'takes an average of three phone calls per claim' to resolve recurring denials of an in-network provider. This operational breakdown is the human-side condition that AI administrative tooling is positioned to address, but the evidence here is the failure state, not the AI fix.

It takes an average of three phone calls per claim. Do that with a newborn. Insurance is broken. (social-intelligence/reddit-deep/healthinsurance/1lycqor.md#c3)
