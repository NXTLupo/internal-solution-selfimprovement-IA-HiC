Precision-ag adoption hinges on proof of field results, not the technology itself, revealing a trust-not-capability barr

Across AgriBusiness Global's Ag Tech Talk series the recurring finding is that 'technology only creates value when it drives practical decisions in the field,' with adoption driven by data-driven insights, proven field results, and effective communication rather than novelty. GROWMARK's co-op case frames the focus as 'practical integration' that reduces workflow friction and builds trust across a federated network, not disruption. At the Dutch Arable Farming Day, farmers who admired a precision tine harrow still 'admitted they would still find it nerve-racking to use it in their own sugar beet crops,' evidencing that the adoption barrier is confidence and demonstrated ROI, not access.

Technology only creates value when it drives practical decisions in the field and across the ag value chain.
