Intelligence Augmentation is positioned to enhance, not replace, human CX capability

NXT Humans, founded August 2024 in Philadelphia by Andrew Fegley, Robert Koch, and Jonathan Lupo, frames its entire CX practice on Intelligence Augmentation (IA): AI that enhances rather than replaces human capability. This founding philosophy maps directly onto organic practitioner sentiment on r/customerexperience, where operators reject pure bot deflection in favor of supportive AI. The alignment signals IA is a response to market demand, not just a proprietary slogan. The marketing report must lead with this value proposition.
