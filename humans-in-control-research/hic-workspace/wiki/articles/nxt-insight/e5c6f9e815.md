Automating creative production removes the repetitive work that trains junior designers, risking a skills gap

D&AD's report warns that layouts, first drafts, mockups, and iteration build the instincts that matter later and that repeating that work is how juniors become seniors, so AI doing it lessens entry-level opportunity. IPA data cited shows the share of employees aged 25 and under at UK agencies fell 19.2% year-on-year. This is the stakeholder-risk counterweight to email automation: Deciem must preserve creative-flexibility touchpoints and human review roles so the pipeline augments rather than hollows out the design team.
