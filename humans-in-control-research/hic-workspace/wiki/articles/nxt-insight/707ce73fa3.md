Crop insurance payouts cut past-due farm loans by ~60 bps, proving insurance stabilizes solvency

The FDIC found that a one standard deviation increase in crop insurance payouts reduces past-due farm loans by around 60 basis points, roughly 20 percent of typical delinquency fluctuations. UNDP adds that agricultural insurance lowers the effective risk weight of loans, reducing lenders' risk-weighted asset ratios and improving capital adequacy even under covariant shocks. This is direct evidence that underwriting integration protects farm solvency and lender balance sheets.
