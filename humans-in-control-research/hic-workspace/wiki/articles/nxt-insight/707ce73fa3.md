Crop insurance payouts cut past-due farm loans by ~60 bps, proving insurance stabilizes solvency

The FDIC found a one standard deviation increase in crop insurance payouts reduces past-due farm loans by around 60 basis points, roughly 20 percent of typical delinquency fluctuations, and lowers banks' risk-weighted asset ratios [articles/nxt-insight/707ce73fa3.md#c0]. UNDP adds that agricultural insurance lowers the effective risk weight of loans, improving capital adequacy even under covariant shocks. This is direct evidence that underwriting integration protects farm solvency and lender balance sheets, the core of the 2026 objective.
