Self-funded ERISA plans strip away state regulatory recourse

Because most employer coverage is self-insured under ERISA, state insurance departments disclaim jurisdiction, leaving consumers without local recourse. A Kentucky member was told the Department of Insurance cannot help because the employer is self-insured, noting close to 70 percent of Kentucky employer coverage is self-insured. Commenters redirect victims to ERISA advocates and the federal Department of Labor. This jurisdictional vacuum shapes where AI-driven member-protection and appeals tools must operate.
