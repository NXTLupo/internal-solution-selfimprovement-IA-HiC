Structured threat-scoring (probability x severity) is how farms are being told to self-assess property risk

Nationwide's Farm Risk Ready Plan Builder instructs operators to enumerate facility, natural, operational, personnel, social and technology threats, score each on probability (1-5) and severity (1-5), and prioritize scores of 10-25 for mitigation. Technology threats such as data corruption, network failure or hack are explicitly listed alongside fires, floods and disease. This client-side methodology shows how insurers are pushing structured, AI-and-tech-inclusive risk identification onto farmers as an underwriting and education tool.
