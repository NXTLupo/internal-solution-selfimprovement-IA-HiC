Patients are forced into contract-interpretation expertise just to obtain covered care

Members repeatedly describe healthcare access as requiring fluency in EOBs, SPDs, CPT/place-of-service codes, and appeal procedures. One commenter asks why a system requires people to 'become experts in contract interpretation and navigating phone trees' [social-intelligence/reddit-deep/healthinsurance/1lycqor.md#c5]. A TMJ MRI denial hinged on whether 'diagnostic imaging' counts as 'treatment' under ERISA exclusion language [social-intelligence/reddit-deep/healthinsurance/1rb3f3b.md#c1]. This literacy gap is the core consumer-facing AI navigation use case.

Why not instead have a system that doesn't require people to become experts in contract interpretation and navigating phone trees in order to get healthcare?
