A nominal 99-cent intro offer retains subscribers far better than free

Recharge data across 132.9M subscriptions shows first orders priced at $0 reach a sixth order 8.7% of the time versus 32.8% for orders priced between one cent and 99 cents [articles/recharge-blog/8898b209c5.md#c1]. The gap opens at the second order: 25.6% of free subscriptions rebill against 63.6% of nominal-paid [articles/recharge-blog/8898b209c5.md#c4]. For a Gen Z skincare intro-offer email, a small paid sample filters tire-kickers and builds a real commitment.

Subscriptions whose first order was priced between one cent and 99 cents reached a sixth order 32.8% of the time, a 70% improvement.
