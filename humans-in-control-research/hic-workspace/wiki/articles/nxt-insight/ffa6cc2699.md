Candidate-side AI use is common but employer-posting AI language remains early

Resume Genius reports 31% of job seekers use AI to support their search, 38% have used AI tools for applications, and among AI users 59% write resumes and 48% write cover letters with it [articles/nxt-insight/5774b1fd89.md#c0]. Indeed Hiring Lab data shows only 4% of job postings mentioned AI or LLM technologies by December 2025 [articles/nxt-insight/5774b1fd89.md#c0]. This asymmetry calibrates DSG expectations: candidate-side AI is already widespread, so evaluation methods must adapt, while employer-side AI signaling is still nascent.

The share of job postings mentioning AI or LLM technologies reached 4% in December 2025 (Indeed Hiring Lab, 2026) [articles/resume-genius-blog/5c06c567f2.md#c1]
