Disconnected AI tools fail without direct access to live proprietary CRM data

Firms utilizing generic LLMs suffer from hallucinated reports because the models lack access to proprietary search intelligence. Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers resolve this by allowing AI to directly query live candidate status, relationships, and project notes without data duplication. This transforms AI from a basic reasoning engine into an integrated delivery infrastructure.

A.I. does the reasoning. An MCP server does the connecting. Your executive search CRM stays the central system of record
