Scandinavian minimalism uses font-weight hierarchy, not decoration, to build clean structure

A cosmetic-brand corporate design exercise (Snögg, inspired by Nivea and luxury Swedish brands) builds hierarchy purely through Work Sans weight variation (Bold, Medium, Regular) on the website while keeping Instagram at a single consistent weight for simplicity. This is a directly transferable email-template system: establish visual order through type weight and whitespace rather than color blocks or ornament, matching Gen Z's preference for uncluttered layouts.

The website additionally uses Bold, Medium, and Regular weights to establish visual hierarchy [articles/reddit-deep-r-graphic-design/f78f1fcdac.md#c0]
