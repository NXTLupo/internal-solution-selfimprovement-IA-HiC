Bundling insurance with credit, inputs and agro-advisory is the proven lever for sustained farmer uptake

Case evidence shows bundling drives adoption: Kenya's Family Bank and Digifarm distribute risk-contingent credit bundling weather-index insurance via a digital platform to reach more farmers at reduced cost; Hollard and Blue Marble bundled insurance with seeds in Mozambique; India combines insurance with credit and advisory services. CGIAR recommends bundling with inputs, extension and health coverage plus during- and after-sales engagement to counter the post-enrolment neglect that damages retention. This informs the product and education strategy across personas.

In Kenya, Family bank and Digifarm have come together to distribute Risk contingent credit which bundles credit with Weather Index Insurance (WII), through a digital platform [articles/vertexaisearch-cloud-google-com/e634f5d039.md#c6]
