Opaque Black-Box Consultancies Face Client Exhaustion and Co-Ownership Demand

Traditional agencies isolate execution inside opaque workflows, triggering severe client fatigue. Independent operators bypass this friction by embedding clients into the design loop via homework and regular co-ownership sessions. This shift mitigates standard agency decline by transitioning delivery from black-box handoffs to rapid, iterative co-creation.
