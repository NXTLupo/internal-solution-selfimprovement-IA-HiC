Parametric cover is gaining traction as traditional property-cat rates fall in emerging agricultural markets

Howden Re reports property catastrophe rates fell 15-20 percent in Latin America at renewals while parametrics gain traction, a structure that pays on a measured trigger (rainfall, wind speed) rather than assessed loss. For agriculture, parametric design aligns naturally with the satellite and weather-index data now available, offering a route around slow, contested crop-loss adjustment. The convergence of falling traditional rates and rising parametric appetite marks a structural shift in how climate-exposed farm risk can be transferred.
