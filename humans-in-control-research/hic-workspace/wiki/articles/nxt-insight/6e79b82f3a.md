Practitioners rate agent-generated hi-fi design as junior-with-no-taste slop

A recurring practitioner verdict caps the design ceiling: agents are useful for quick wireframes and 2-3 UI variations to avoid a blank page, but full design specs land 'to the level of an inconsistent junior designer with no taste.' Attempts to have Claude critique designs with essential context felt too generic. One designer stopped fixing hi-fi output entirely because the result was 'THAT SLOP.' This is the honest limitation boundary against which the objective's critic and skill surfaces must be scoped; the value is in ideation and process, not finished specs.

For fully realised design specs, it’s still way off. It can do it for sure, but to the level of an inconsistent junior designer with no taste.
