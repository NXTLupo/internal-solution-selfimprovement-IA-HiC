The 2026 email delivers value inside the inbox, not behind a click

Convince & Convert rebuilt its newsletter, The Trendline, to prove worth inside the email itself after finding readers ignore emails that force a click-through [articles/nxt-insight/2c79697e42.md#c0]. It abandoned the list-of-links format entirely [articles/nxt-insight/d20d80cbe1.md#c0]. For a Gen Z skincare template this reframes the layout around self-contained routine tips and product education with a clear hierarchy, rewarding the open directly rather than treating email as a link farm to the shop.

We needed to prove our value inside the email itself, not just on the other side of a new browser tab [articles/convince-convert/3639b3d4fc.md#c2]
