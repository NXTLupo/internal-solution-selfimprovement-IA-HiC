No single tool handles email design end-to-end, forcing a multi-tool chain that compounds cost

An r/emailmarketing practitioner using Mailmodo says a template generator is good for direction but that no tool is particularly good at handling the entire design process end-to-end, so teams stitch together Claude, an ESP, and Canva while pricing adds up. A second commenter recommends separating the decision into the email marketing platform and the design system you build inside it. This validates the multi-agent decomposition premise: the workflow is a chain of specialized steps, not one monolith.

if you're looking for something that can handle the entire design process end-to-end, I don't think there's any tool particularly good right atm. [articles/reddit-deep-r-emailmarketing/0f0adc56ff.md#c0]
