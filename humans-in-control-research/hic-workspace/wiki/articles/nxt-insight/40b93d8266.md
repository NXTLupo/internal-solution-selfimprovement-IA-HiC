Uncontrolled automation loops are a distinct failure class requiring circuit breakers

AWS identifies uncontrolled automation loops as one of three categories of risk every retail agent faces, illustrated by an agent that retried a declined card six times in rapid succession and triggered the bank's fraud detection. For an email pipeline, retry logic on failed Figma pulls, failed renders, or failed sends must carry backoff and hard limits. Machine Learning Mastery's guidance to build retry logic inside individual agent coroutines before exceptions reach the group reinforces the same control.
