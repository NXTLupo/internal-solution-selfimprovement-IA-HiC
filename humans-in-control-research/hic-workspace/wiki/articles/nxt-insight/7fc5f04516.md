BYOAI Context Portability over Proprietary Destination Interfaces

Students and technical power users increasingly view AI as a personal, persistent tech layer that travels with them across services. Using open context tools and local Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers, users manage conversation history, persona preferences, and background memory locally, exporting them seamlessly across Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini. Rather than adopting locked-in institutional AI destinations, users demand headless interoperability where services plug directly into their chosen assistant.

"open-context is a tool for keeping your AI context portable and persistent. It lets you bring your full conversation history when switching AI assistants, and gives Claude a persistent memory through an MCP server." [articles/hacker-news-algolia/372a44f025.md#c5]
