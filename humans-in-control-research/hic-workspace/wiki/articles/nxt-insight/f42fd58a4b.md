Dual-Vector Preference Architectures Maintain Long-Term User Memory

To prevent session memory loss without massive fine-tuning costs, systems utilize dual-vector preference spaces. Combining persistent long-term preference vectors with dynamic short-term vectors allows personal assistants to maintain communication alignment across long-term interactions while adapting to session-specific context.
