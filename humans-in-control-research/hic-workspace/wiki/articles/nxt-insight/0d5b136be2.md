Unverified AI Grading Degradation Forces Source-Attributed Verification Checklists

Prolonged persona-based feedback degenerates into fictional criteria over extended interactions. Students counter hallucinated grading and unhelpful feedback by converting general persona prompts into explicit rubric checklists that require the AI to tag every critique with a source reference. Users actively audit model output against trusted materials.

I think the issue is asking it to “grade like your professor” turns into professor fanfiction after a while. I’d make it less of a persona and more of a checklist [social-intelligence/reddit-deep/chatgpt/1v05yom.md#c3]
