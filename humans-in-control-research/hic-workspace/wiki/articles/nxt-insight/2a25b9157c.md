A semantic layer mapping business terms to code is what sets an agent's accuracy ceiling

The AWS retail team found the most common source of agent errors was not syntax but misunderstanding business terms, and fixed it by injecting a mapping from business terms to code expressions directly into the agent's prompt, calling this semantic layer the structured codification of enterprise knowledge that sets the ceiling for accuracy [articles/aws-retail-blog/b7ad106ca4.md#c3]. For Deciem, the equivalent is a codified mapping of brand terms (a named component, a token, a spacing rule) so a design-parsing agent interprets Figma intent consistently rather than guessing.

This semantic layer is the structured codification of enterprise business knowledge, and its quality sets the ceiling for the system's accuracy [articles/aws-retail-blog/b7ad106ca4.md#c3]
