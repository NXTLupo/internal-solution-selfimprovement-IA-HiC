Human review bottlenecks in agentic pipelines

AI systems push development velocity beyond the capacity of human review processes. Human developers often commit codebase modifications without reviewing them, leading to unverified codebases that legacy teams struggle to maintain [articles/reddit-deep-r-productmanagement/1cf3ecafb4.md#c0]. Passing off massive, unverified outputs to colleagues right before product deadlines results in high technical debt [articles/reddit-deep-r-consulting/92cd351544.md#c0].

"Our CTO's counter is that a team can only safely absorb code as fast as it can read it." [articles/reddit-deep-r-chatgptcoding/d10098214d.md#c0]
