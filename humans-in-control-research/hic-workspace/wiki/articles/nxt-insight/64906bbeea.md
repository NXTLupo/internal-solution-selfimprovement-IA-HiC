The aggressive advisor pivot into private markets exposes critical deficits in alternative asset reporting technology

Advisors are rapidly escalating their utilization of private equity and private credit, pushing average alternative allocations from $7.5 billion in 2022 to $12 billion in 2025. Projections indicate advisors managing over $500 million will allocate 5.7% of portfolios to illiquid assets by 2027. However, technological infrastructure lags severely behind this asset shift, with only 50% of wealth management firms currently employing third-party systems to automate and report on illiquid alternatives.

Only 50% of wealth management firms use third-party technology systems to oversee, report on, and automate illiquid alternative assets.
