A single source of truth for tokens and naming stops agents from hallucinating conventions

Posts warn that without a documented taxonomy 'the agent hallucinates conventions.' They mandate a written runbook of definitions, naming conventions, and rules the agent must cite. For Deciem this maps to brand guidelines and the Figma variable collections as the authoritative token source the generation agent binds to, rather than inventing hex values or spacing, preserving brand compliance.

No single source of truth for naming/taxonomy; the agent hallucinates conventions. [social-intelligence/reddit-deep/marketingautomation/1s2gl28.md#c2]
