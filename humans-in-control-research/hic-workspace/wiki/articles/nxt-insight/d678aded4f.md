Payer algorithmic decisioning already governs care and seeds consumer AI distrust

A caregiver describes Medicare Advantage care decisions as 'governed by an AI algorithm to determine if it's financially expedient for them,' citing skilled-nursing approvals capped near 20 days regardless of need. PCPs separately report Medicare Advantage 'questions my decisions even if medically sound' with multiple authorization hoops. This is the only direct AI reference traceable in the corpus, and it is negative: existing payer algorithmic decisioning frames AI adoption as adversarial to patients and clinicians, a trust barrier for the payer and caregiver cohorts.

every aspect of your care is governed by an AI algorithm to determine if it's financially expedient for them. There is a huge conflict of interest. It's not about your needs, it's about their bottom line.
