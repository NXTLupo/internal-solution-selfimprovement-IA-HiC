Four risk-management strategies contest insurance demand

The Nationwide framework classifies strategies into avoidance, acceptance, transfer (insurance or contractual) and control/mitigation, the last including loss prevention like wind rings around grain bins and diversification such as backup combines. Underwriting demand is contested by farmers who can self-insure or mitigate rather than buy coverage.

Risk transfer. Transfer through purchasing insurance or contractual means. Risk control / mitigation. Change the likelihood (loss prevention) e.g., add wind rings around grain bins
