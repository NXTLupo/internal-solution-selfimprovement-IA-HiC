AI now generates parallel design variations, shifting the question from how to make to which to expand

Figma and Venngage converge that AI collapses the cost of producing alternatives: Figma Make turns prompts into working prototypes and Copy design brings them to the canvas, shifting the conversation from how to make something to which version to expand on. Venngage's AI Designer generates multiple variations in minutes, keeping the same message while varying headlines, CTAs, layouts, and colors. For email this supports an agent that proposes several on-brand variants for human selection rather than one deterministic output.

This shifts the design conversation from how to make something to which version to expand on. [articles/figma-blog/4f255726fb.md#c2]
