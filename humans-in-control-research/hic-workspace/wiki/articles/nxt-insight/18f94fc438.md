The Shadow Co-op Market via External Conversion

When students fail to secure placements through official university channels, they frequently source their own opportunities externally. Students use platforms like LinkedIn or personal connections to find internships, which they then convert into official co-ops by having the employer complete university paperwork. This indicates a gap in the university's inventory of desirable roles.
