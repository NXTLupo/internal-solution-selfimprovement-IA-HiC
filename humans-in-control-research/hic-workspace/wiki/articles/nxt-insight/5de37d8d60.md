Rapid over-reliance on AI guidance creates cognitive skill degradation

Multi-university studies reveal severe performance crashes when users are stripped of AI assistance after brief reliance, a phenomenon labeled the 'boiling frog' effect [social-intelligence/reddit-deep/artificial/1sqcz1m.md#c7]. When individuals rely on automated outputs without understanding underlying reasoning, their ability to execute independent problem solving diminishes rapidly. Students note that passive AI note generation similarly stunts information retention compared to active mental synthesis [articles/reddit-deep-r-chatgpt/24e253bd70.md#c1].

Performance crashed below the control group and people stopped trying. UCLA, MIT, Oxford, and Carnegie Mellon call it the 'boiling frog' effect. [social-intelligence/reddit-deep/artificial/1sqcz1m.md#c7]
