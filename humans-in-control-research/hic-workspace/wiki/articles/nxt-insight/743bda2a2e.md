Legacy RPA metrics fail to capture the true value of exception handling

Classic ROI models designed for RPA count minutes and multiply by loaded labor rates, failing to price complex agentic exceptions [articles/aws-ml-blog/fe8edf14f1.md#c1]. Most of the value of agentic automation is centered on exceptions handling and workflow redesign rather than speed [articles/aws-ml-blog/fe8edf14f1.md#c1]. Agencies must educate buyers on outcomes over simple hours saved.
