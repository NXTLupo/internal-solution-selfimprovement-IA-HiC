Design context becomes the primary UX deliverable when AI does the building

Nielsen Norman argues that if AI does more of the building, AI becomes the consumer of research and design deliverables, and what an AI consumes is context [articles/nielsen-norman/010ac25d6d.md#c2]. UX-context design is the practice of curating what an organization knows into the context that guides everything its AI tools generate, then testing that context's efficacy with the actual models in use [articles/nielsen-norman/010ac25d6d.md#c2]. Since product managers and engineers now also prompt design tools, the goal is ensuring everything AI generates is informed by organizational standards [articles/nielsen-norman/010ac25d6d.md#c1]. For Deciem this reframes brand guidelines as machine-consumable context, not human-only PDFs.
