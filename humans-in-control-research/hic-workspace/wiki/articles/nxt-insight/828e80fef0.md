InterQual and clinical-criteria engines downgrade inpatient stays to observation

Payers apply automated clinical-criteria engines (InterQual) to retro-deny inpatient level of care. A patient admitted for abnormal uterine bleeding and anemia who received a transfusion was told the stay did not meet InterQual criteria because documentation did not support hemolytic anemia, so the care should be reimbursed at observation level. This is a concrete payer AI/criteria-engine use case whose accuracy directly determines patient financial exposure.
