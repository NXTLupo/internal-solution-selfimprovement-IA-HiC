On-Device Local Processing Guards Sensitive Personal and Behavioral Data

Users express strong reluctance to transfer intimate behavioral logs or personal communications to central institutional or vendor clouds. On-device machine learning models run local inferences without auto-reporting metadata to external servers. This privacy-first architecture guarantees user sovereignty and protects against administrative surveillance.

No message content leaves the device for classification or is auto-reported to WhatsApp, Meta, or anyone else.
