Existing advisor workflows require exportable student artifacts over portal builds

To preserve advisor centrality without building custom advisor portals in SOW012, the experience relies on lightweight integration with existing tools. Design provisions mandate exportable and shareable student-generated plans and summaries that flow into existing advisor workflows [articles/client-uploads/b8e5bd5ac5.md#c9]. This maintains human oversight while avoiding out-of-scope advisor portal development [articles/client-uploads/b8e5bd5ac5.md#c11].
