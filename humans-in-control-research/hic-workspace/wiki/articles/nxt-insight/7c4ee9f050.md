Stratified sampling and threshold alerts give agentic pipelines SRE-like observability

Zepto's Databricks-built evaluation framework shows how critic layers and notifications scale for agentic execution: stratified sampling on high-value customers, changed flows, and high-risk interactions yields an 18-20% evaluation sample (~14,400 traces/day), capturing 45-60% of edge cases and detecting issues within 4-6 minutes at 86% lower review cost per issue. Critical alerts run every 5 minutes for intent-accuracy drops, groundedness violations, and latency breaches. This observability-and-alerting model is directly transferable to how Figma-agent critics and notifications should be architected.
