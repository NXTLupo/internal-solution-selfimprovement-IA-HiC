Coverage determination opacity blocks any AI tool patients are told to self-serve

Consumers report being unable to determine in-the-moment whether a service requires prior authorization, with member portals and Summary of Benefits documents giving 'generic things like some imaging may require PA which is useless.' One patient was told to obtain the CPT code from the provider and call insurance to verify benefits before each service. This information asymmetry is the precondition any consumer-facing AI benefits navigator must solve, and the same opacity that frustrates human callers will defeat naive chatbots.

Are pts seriously expected to ask their providers for a CPT code and then call insurance to verify benefits? The SBC sometimes will say generic things like some imaging may require PA which is useless.
