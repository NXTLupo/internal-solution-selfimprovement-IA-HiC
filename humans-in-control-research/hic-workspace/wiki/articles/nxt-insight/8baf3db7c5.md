Hyper-personalized micro-targeting now outperforms generic campaigns across email and social

Nike and Apple are cited for segmenting audiences into narrow lifestyle groups (trail runners vs gym-goers, creative professionals) and delivering tailored ads, emails, and social content, producing higher engagement and loyalty. McKinsey data shows 71% of consumers expect personalized experiences and 76% are frustrated without them. For a Gen Z skincare template, this argues for dynamic, skin-concern-segmented email content over a single mass send.
