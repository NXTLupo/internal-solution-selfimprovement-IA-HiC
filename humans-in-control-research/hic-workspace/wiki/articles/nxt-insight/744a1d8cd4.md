Manufacturer copay and patient-assistance programs backfill payer denials

Patients lean on drug-manufacturer copay cards and patient-assistance programs to bridge coverage gaps while appeals drag, but these stopgaps increasingly fail. One member's manufacturer card would not process without the prior authorization; another reports insurance will no longer accept the manufacturer-provided drug. Commenters point patients to Pfizer access coordinators and third-appeal persistence. This parallel financing layer is a financial-cohort dynamic any AI benefits-navigation tool must model.
