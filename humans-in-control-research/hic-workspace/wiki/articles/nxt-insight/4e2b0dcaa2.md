Growing Bifurcation Between Tool-Leveraged Reasoning and Conceptual Atrophy

AI adoption in higher education is creating a widening gap between two distinct student groups. Advanced students use AI as a force multiplier to streamline boilerplate tasks while sharpening high-level engineering reasoning. Conversely, novice or disengaged students over-rely on raw AI generations without acquiring fundamental concepts, exposing themselves to catastrophic errors when AI logic fails.

"There are those who have STEM backgrounds, understand how to think, reason, and proven solve. These individuals work WITH the tools rather than having the tools work FOR them. These are the people who will succeed." [social-intelligence/reddit-deep/engineeringstudents/1r0s1nj.md#c3]
