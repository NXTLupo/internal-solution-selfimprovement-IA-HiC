Coding mismatches and system glitches auto-deny covered claims

Automated adjudication fails on trivial data errors. A national lab's platform drops the period in diagnosis codes, so every test is deemed not covered until the period is re-added, with the patient suspecting deliberate cash-flow padding. An Anthem app misrepresented an urgent-care clinic's tier and the system arbitrarily overrode the hospital's urgent-care coding to bill an ER tier. These show the operational fragility of rule-based and AI adjudication that any 2026 payer modernization must fix rather than amplify.
