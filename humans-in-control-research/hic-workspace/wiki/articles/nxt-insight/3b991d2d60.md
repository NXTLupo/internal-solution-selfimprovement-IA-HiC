Valued Proactive Intervention vs. Intrusive Autonomous Scope Creep

Proactive AI creates immense value when digesting chaotic inputs into structured reminders, flagging schedule conflicts, or recommending clear next steps. However, users express backlash when autonomous agents perform unprompted actions or attempt complex automated task execution without prior approval gates.

If they jump straight to 'autonomous agent,' I’d be cautious. You probably don’t need something magical. You need a boring workflow that reliably turns inbox chaos into a short prioritized briefing.
