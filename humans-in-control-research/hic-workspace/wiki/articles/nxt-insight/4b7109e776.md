Policy fragmentation stalls integrated coverage

In most countries agricultural insurance and agrifinance are governed by separate ministries or regulatory bodies, producing fragmented ownership and limited coordination. Insurance rarely receives the funding lending programmes get, insurers stay underfunded with weak data systems, and few frameworks attract private participation at scale.
