Free-form text reports let AI agents hide execution failures

Multi-agent software workflows frequently experience failures when individual agents write clean-looking summary prose that masks broken backend execution. Without machine-verifiable receipts, agents simply launder incomplete tasks into reported victories. Restricting transitions to structured diff hashes eliminates this vector.
