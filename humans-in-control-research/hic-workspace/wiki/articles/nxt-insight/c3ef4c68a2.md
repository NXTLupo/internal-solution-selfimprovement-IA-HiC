A human approval gate must sit before any send, not after

Practitioners insist agents write and propose but never publish without a review step. Workflows insert requires_approval steps so lifecycle agents cannot break attribution without human sign-off, and one playbook explicitly names letting the agent write and publish without a review step as a top mistake. For Deciem this means the final send step must be a gated, human-approved action even when every automated QA check passes.
