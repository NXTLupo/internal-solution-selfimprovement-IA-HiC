High-net-worth households controlling 40% of wealth force a distribution pivot toward independent RIAs

The concentration of wealth among high-net-worth (HNW) households has fundamentally shifted asset distribution strategies. Households with assets between $5 million and $20 million now control 40% of addressable U.S. wealth. Because these clients heavily favor independent advisory models, 79% of distribution executives have named independent RIAs as their primary distribution channel for the next three years. This requires distribution platforms to accommodate diverse, fragmented RIA technology stacks rather than standardized wirehouse systems.
