Price opacity pushes consumers toward cash-based and medical-tourism alternatives

Members increasingly conclude that insured prices are higher and less transparent than cash prices, eroding the value proposition of coverage itself. Commenters describe paying $5/month for generics via Amazon, using GoodRx, and one expat reports $70,000 in 2019 US out-of-pocket versus roughly $360/year abroad, driving repeated medical tourism. This price-transparency demand is the consumer-facing opportunity AI shopping and cost-estimation tools target.

When the cash price for care is lower than the insured price, something in the system is fundamentally broken [social-intelligence/reddit-deep/healthinsurance/1pn9f11.md#c5]
