Equipment dealers, not manufacturers, gate technology adoption through absorption-rate economics

Dealership-of-the-year data shows Messick's on $648,000 with a 107% absorption rate and King Ranch Ag & Turf at $190 million revenue, 72% large-ag market share, and a 69% absorption rate [articles/agr-tractor-supply-note/90f0861439.md#c2]. Parts-and-service absorption, not machine sales, defines dealer survival, meaning the channel that installs, services, and finances precision tech is consolidating around a few high-share players. Whether a grower can adopt and maintain advanced equipment depends heavily on this dealer network's local reach.
