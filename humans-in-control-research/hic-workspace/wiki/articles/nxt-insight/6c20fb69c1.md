A locked modular email design system beats regenerating aesthetics per campaign with AI

Practitioners in r/emailmarketing converge on building a small fixed set of reusable blocks before letting AI assemble campaigns, because raw AI generators produce basic off-brand layouts. The recommended system fixes typography, spacing, image treatment, CTA hierarchy, and product, editorial, and promotional modules first, so the inbox feels like a continuation of the brand rather than a collection of one-off templates. For Deciem this means locking modular blocks and constraining AI to iterate inside them.
