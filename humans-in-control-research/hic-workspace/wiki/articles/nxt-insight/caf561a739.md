Large-scale operators pre-secure inputs while small farms bear the volatility, a persona divide underwriters must price

A self-identified farmer on Reddit operating in the USA, South America and Ukraine states that 'this years inputs have been secured for months' and that 'most large scale farmers are not idiots when it comes to inputs and had a grow schedule prior to Feb/Mar,' with fertilizer drawn from barns holding 'a couple of seasons stored.' The same thread notes the risk 'becomes very real very fast' if disruption lasts a full grow season. This exposes a sharp persona divide: capitalized operators hedge input risk in advance while small-scale farmers face acute exposure, warranting differentiated underwriting.
