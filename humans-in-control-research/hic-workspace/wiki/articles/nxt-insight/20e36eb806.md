Toxic culture, not pay, is the strongest predictor of the attrition search firms are hired to prevent

MIT Sloan analysis of over one million Glassdoor reviews found toxic company culture was the number one predictor of attrition, vastly outranking pay, and that innovative companies often see higher turnover than traditional peers in the same industry. Since executive search is ultimately hired to place leaders who reduce this attrition, cultural-fit assessment becomes a defensible, judgment-heavy layer that resists commoditization and where AI plays a support, not decision, role.

Corporate culture is a much more reliable predictor of industry-adjusted attrition than how employees assess their compensation. [articles/workable-blog/1c40a13d2d.md#c2]
