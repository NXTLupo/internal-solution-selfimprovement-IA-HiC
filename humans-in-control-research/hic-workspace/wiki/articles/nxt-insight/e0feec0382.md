Newsletter conversion improves when hierarchy is curated by purpose, not by content type

Convince & Convert rebuilt its newsletter (The Trendline) around purpose-driven sections with strict word budgets: a 25-50 word intro, one key number, 75-150 word strategic takes, replacing the old medium-first format that made readers work to reach strategic thinking. For a Gen Z clean-beauty email, this argues for a curated modular hierarchy with tight copy budgets per block rather than a wall of product tiles.

The old format ... made the reader work more to get to strategic thinking ... each section would have a clear purpose to match the importance of the curated content.
