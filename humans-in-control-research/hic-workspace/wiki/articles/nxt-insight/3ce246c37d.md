Software implementation is rapidly commoditizing, shifting the value bottleneck to design

With code-generation models rapidly automating basic execution and prototyping, standard software engineering is on a trajectory toward commoditization. Enterprise value has transitioned from pure technical syntax delivery to defining product requirements, managing complex systems, and ensuring UX/design quality. This forces agencies to compete on customer experience and context curation over sheer lines of code.
