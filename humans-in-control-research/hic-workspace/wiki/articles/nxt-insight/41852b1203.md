Buyer shift from static blogs to micro-tools

Corporate buyers increasingly ignore static, manual marketing write-ups and promotional documentation in favor of interactive micro-tools and custom databases. Engineering teams utilize AI capabilities to generate functional, structured mini-features that establish immediate utility [articles/reddit-deep-r-startups/bb635c3313.md#c2]. This changes software marketing from static content creation into active programmatic development [articles/reddit-deep-r-startups/bb635c3313.md#c2].
