Non-Technical Users Leverage LLMs for Self-Correction and Cognitive Untangling

Beyond generating content, office workers and students increasingly use AI assistants as sounding boards to untangle complex priorities and stress-test ideas before human interaction. Non-technical employees report using AI to play devil's advocate, rephrase tense communication, and categorize competing tasks by urgency. This cognitive untangling allows individuals to enter human-to-human meetings with clarified thoughts and structured proposals, reinforcing the role of AI as an individual preparation partner that enhances rather than replaces expert collaboration.
