AI value in farming lives in field decisions, not the technology, a trust-not-tool barrier

Across five Ag Tech Talk episodes the recurring finding is that technology only creates value when it drives practical decisions in the field, with GROWMARK framing AI as practical integration rather than disruption inside a federated co-op [articles/agr-agribusiness-global/6008a062b6.md#c1]. Field-proven results and effective communication, not the tools themselves, gain traction. This confirms the adoption barrier is trust and demonstrated ROI, which shapes both the educational needs of farmers and how underwriters should evaluate tech-enabled operations.
