Transition from Simple Prompting to Specialized Agent Workflows with Human Safety Checks

College job seekers and early-career professionals have moved beyond basic chat prompting toward multi-step automated workflows for resume tailoring, document ingestion, and interview preparation. However, users explicitly construct controlled loops that require human review and approval before submitting applications or sending communications. Fully autonomous agents that take action without consent are rejected as risky and unsafe.

"Keep a human approval step before anything gets sent or archived. The main thing is permissions and privacy. Be careful who you give mailbox access to, and make sure the system is read-only at first." [articles/reddit-deep-r-artificial/e9b8d38dcc.md#c1]
