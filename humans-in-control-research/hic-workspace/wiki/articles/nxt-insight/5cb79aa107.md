Fragmented Data Interoperability Blocks Operational AI Deployment

Fundamental data silos prevent seamless operational automation across the healthcare sector. Provider portals routinely fail to synchronize with hospital billing systems, causing claims to route to incorrect or outdated payer profiles. Additionally, delayed credentialing database updates and lost prior authorization requests highlight severe infrastructure friction. These basic interoperability failures must be resolved before advanced operational AI can be integrated at scale.
