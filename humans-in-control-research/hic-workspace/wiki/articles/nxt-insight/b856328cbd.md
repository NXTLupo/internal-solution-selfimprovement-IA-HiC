Self-funded vs fully-insured plan distinction silently controls who can override a denial

Patients cannot tell whether their employer plan is self-funded (employer pays claims, can grant exceptions) or fully-insured (insurer bound to contract, cannot). Insurance professionals in-thread explain the override path runs through the employer's HR/benefits team, not the carrier [social-intelligence/reddit-deep/healthinsurance/1m7ebd0.md#c8]. A self-funded ERISA plan defers final say to the SPD, not the carrier rep [social-intelligence/reddit-deep/healthinsurance/1nto4x3.md#c3]. AI triage that routes a member to the correct override authority addresses a hidden structural barrier.

Your employer is your plan and they are the ones who set the coverage rules and they are the ones who have the power to override the coverage.
