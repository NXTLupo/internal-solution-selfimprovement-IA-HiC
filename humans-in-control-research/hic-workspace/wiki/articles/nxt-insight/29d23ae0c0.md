A low-hire, low-fire labor market makes each executive placement higher-stakes

Indeed and the Conference Board report a low-hire, low-fire market: job openings near 7.4-7.53 million, quits at 2%, layoffs at 1.1%, and hiring near levels seen 11 years ago despite a labor force 13 million larger. The ETI rose to 107.71 in July 2026 but sits only 0.6% above a year prior. In this environment, workers cling to jobs and each senior hire carries more weight, raising the premium on search precision and the cost of a bad executive placement.

Workers are clinging to their jobs in this environment, and the quits rate has been at or below 2% for almost a year. [articles/exs-indeed-hiring-lab/691e65ce3e.md#c2]
