Preview and permission scoping controls what agents and reviewers can inspect or export

Sketch's preview model demonstrates the notification-and-approval granularity the objective implies: share a specific slice — a few pages, a prototype flow, a single route — with a password or expiration, and decide whether people can inspect the design or export assets. Developers get a clear layer list; admins get a workspace-wide overview of everything shared, reviewable or removable in one click. This scoped-share pattern is the review-and-handoff analog Figma agent approvals must match, keeping the workspace limited to actual collaborators.

When you set one up, you can choose whether people can inspect the design or export assets—ideal for engineers or anyone who needs the finer details.
