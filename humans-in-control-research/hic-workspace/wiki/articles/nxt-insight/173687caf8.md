Insurers brand established treatments "experimental" to deny coverage, forcing patients to self-litigate

Cigna deemed an occipital nerve block "experimental" in 2023 and refused to let the patient appeal in 2026, despite two physicians attesting it is standard care. The patient could not obtain a denial ID or hard copy. This payer behavior is the precise friction that AI prior-authorization-appeal tools are being built to automate, with one commenter explicitly a physician building such a tool.

They called back and said Cigna denied it and deemed it “experimental.” A very basic Google search will indicate that it is NOT experimental.
