Balance billing and allowed-amount math erase the coverage consumers believe they bought

Consumers discover headline benefits are illusory: an Anthem PPO advertising 70% out-of-network coverage actually reimbursed roughly 44% of a $200 bill after allowed-amount reductions and balance billing. Cash-pay prices are repeatedly cited as far lower than insured rates. This gap between perceived and actual coverage is a consumer-cohort trust barrier that AI benefits-modeling tools claim to expose pre-service.

Insurance covers: ~$87 -> ~44% of the original bill!!! ... This 'headline benefit' is essentially a white lie.
