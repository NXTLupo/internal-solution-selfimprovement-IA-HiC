Interactive and UGC-driven content outperforms polished posts for Gen Z engagement

Glossier uses Stories polls to let followers vote on new product colors, creating two-way conversations, and Warby Parker showcases UGC to strengthen social proof and loyalty [articles/socialpilot-blog/57cced5f92.md#c1]. Gen Z and Millennial audiences heavily engage with short videos, relatable memes, brand transparency and real customer stories [articles/socialpilot-blog/57cced5f92.md#c2]. Email can borrow this by embedding poll-style CTAs, customer review carousels and behind-the-scenes moments rather than static promotional imagery.

Glossier frequently uses polls in Stories to let followers vote on new product colors or campaign ideas, creating two-way conversations. [articles/socialpilot-blog/57cced5f92.md#c1]
