Europe's 91%-concentrated soy dependency is an insurable strategic supply vulnerability

The Protein Project report found the EU sources 91% of its soy protein from just three countries and warns that without imported high-protein meal the EU could lose an estimated 15% of beef and dairy, 40% of pork, and 60% of poultry and egg production [articles/agr-agriland-ie/c983ee2773.md#c1]. Framing soy as the protein backbone of European livestock farming that deserves the same seriousness as energy and defence recasts input concentration as a solvency-threatening exposure across the livestock value chain.
