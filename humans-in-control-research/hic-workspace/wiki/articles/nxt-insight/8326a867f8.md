Candidates penalize vague, AI-generated job descriptions, raising the bar on employer-side content quality

Resume Genius 2026 data shows 72% of job seekers are less likely to apply when they see typos, errors, or AI-generated job descriptions, and 80% of hiring managers say they can often tell when a resume has been written by AI. [articles/resume-genius-blog/5c06c567f2.md#c2] This two-sided AI-detection dynamic sets a quality floor: AI can draft, but unedited AI output damages both candidate and employer credibility. For DSG it argues for AI-assisted, human-finished content.
