Asset managers deploy specialist-wholesaler hybrids to close knowledge gaps

Managing alternative investments requires extensive advisor education, which 75% of asset managers identify as their single largest obstacle in private market distribution [articles/gemini-focused-research/33aec05c8b.md#c3]. To successfully distribute complex, illiquid alternatives, 79% of Tier-I asset managers are now employing specialized advisor-facing product experts [articles/nxt-insight/e7d1fc790d.md#c0]. These specialists operate alongside generalist wholesalers to bridge the knowledge gap and capture assets within independent environments [articles/nxt-insight/0182d6dae4.md#c0].
