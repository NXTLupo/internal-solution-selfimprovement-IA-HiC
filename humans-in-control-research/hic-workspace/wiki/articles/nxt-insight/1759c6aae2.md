Rising deductibles turn insurance into deferred risk, eroding consumer trust that AI tools must overcome

A commenter reports deductibles rising from $1,500 twenty years ago to as high as $10k in-network and triple out-of-network, with 80-85% of families living paycheck to paycheck unable to absorb that before coverage activates. People delay care as a result. This eroded trust in the payer relationship is the adoption headwind any consumer-facing payer AI must confront.

That is not cost sharing. It is financial roulette.
