Sequential workflow digitization is mandatory before deploying automated AI assistants

Automating complex institutional processes requires prior manual execution and structured prompt testing. Workflows evolve along a defined continuum from 100% human execution to automated assistant generation reviewed by human operators [articles/marketing-ai-inst/3d133d608d.md#c1]. Attempting to deploy automated tools without establishing manual baselines leads to brittle execution [articles/marketing-ai-inst/3d133d608d.md#c1]. For Northeastern, deploying the Student AI Companion directly into the Co-op Dashboard grounds the conversational assistant in established experiential learning workflows [articles/client-uploads/5b7f886799.md#c11].

Modern marketing workflows evolve in a specific sequence from 100% done-by-human-hands to 100% done by assistants … and then reviewed by humans. [articles/marketing-ai-inst/3d133d608d.md#c1]
