Skills can be published and shared so one person's creative direction scales to a team

Skills turn recurring prompts into reusable slash commands, and publishing a skill makes one person's thinking part of how the whole team works - a design philosophy or feedback lens any teammate can implement [articles/figma-blog/e372681f45.md#c3]. Anyone can author a skill without building a plugin or writing code, and several launch skills came from community practitioners defining what great design looks like in practice [articles/figma-blog/213ea4dcb6.md#c2]. This shareability lets Deciem's brand designers codify email standards once and have every agent apply them.
