Insurance risk-reduction gains are rarely passed to farmers, so premiums rise instead of loan rates falling

Despite agricultural insurance's risk-mitigation benefits, interviews across markets show these gains are rarely passed on to borrowers. Lenders often do not expand risk appetite or reduce loan interest rates; to cover premium costs, pricing may actually increase when insurance is bundled with loans. Reasons include delayed recognition of risk reduction, already low-risk MFI group lending, and a lack of robust evidence demonstrating benefits to lenders. This directly bears on farm affordability under high-rate conditions.
