AI agents produce screens that look 80% right but sit 100% off-system

An enterprise DevOps design lead reports that agents using Claude Code and Cursor reinvent existing components, hardcode values that have tokens, and produce screens that look 80% right and were 100% not in the system. The fix that worked was making AI-readability a first-class requirement, same tier as accessibility. This is the central limitation the white paper's critics and hooks must address: model capability alone does not guarantee on-system output.
