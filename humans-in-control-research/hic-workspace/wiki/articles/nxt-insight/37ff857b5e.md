Self-funded employers hold an override lever automated coverage rules ignore

Coverage decisions that look like fixed payer policy are frequently overridable by the self-funded employer who actually bears claim risk, a governance reality any deployed payer-side AI must encode. Commenters note self-funded employers can request medication exceptions because they pay the claims directly [social-intelligence/reddit-deep/healthinsurance/1m7ebd0.md#c8], and that roughly 67% of covered U.S. workers are in self-funded plans [social-intelligence/reddit-deep/healthinsurance/1st94fp.md#c4]. This decision-authority layering is essential context for who governs healthcare automation outcomes in 2026.

If the employer has a self-funded plan, they MAY be able to request an exception. Self-funded means the employer is paying all the claims.
