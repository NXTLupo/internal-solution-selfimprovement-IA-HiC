Approval flags before send are the gate that keeps lifecycle agents from breaking attribution

Practitioner playbooks converge on inserting requires_approval steps so lifecycle agents cannot break attribution or expand reach without human sign-off. One r/marketingautomation contributor draws a hard boundary between an agent that can draft and validate and a human who alone approves changes to segment logic and suppression rules. This defines the human-in-the-loop control point that qualifies automated email output as sendable rather than auto-shipped.
