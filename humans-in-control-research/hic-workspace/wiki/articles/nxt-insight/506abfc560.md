Clash between slow timelines and rapid pocs

Enterprise clients demand functional software pilots in half the time compared to historic delivery models. Boutique MVP studios bypass traditional agency discovery phases to deploy investor-ready codebases within weeks [articles/gemini-focused-research/5ba150df2f.md#c2]. This shift forces a transition away from upfront planning fees toward ongoing, recurring subscription models that prioritize continuous iterations [articles/webflow-blog/3fd94dbdcc.md#c1].
