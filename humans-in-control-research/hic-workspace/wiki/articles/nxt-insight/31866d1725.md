NXT Humans positions Intelligence Augmentation as enhancing, not replacing, human capability

NXT Humans frames its entire CX practice on 'Intelligence Augmentation' (IA), defined as using AI to enhance rather than replace human capabilities. The firm, co-founded August 2024 in Philadelphia by Andrew Fegley, Robert Koch, and Jonathan Lupo, bridges high-level strategy and technical execution for mid-market and enterprise clients. This positioning maps directly onto external practitioner sentiment that the goal is to augment humans rather than swap them for bots. The IA framing is the core value proposition the marketing report must lead with.
