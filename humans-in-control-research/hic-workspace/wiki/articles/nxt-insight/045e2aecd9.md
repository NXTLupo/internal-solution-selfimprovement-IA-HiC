Caregivers act as proxy navigators fighting denials on a patient's behalf

When patients are too sick to advocate, spouses and partners take over the system fight. A husband ran his wife's cancer-surgery escalation through the employer broker, the governor's office, and Cigna's CEO email; a partner managed a Myasthenia Gravis denial while his significant other was in a flare and had to file a representative authorization form. This caregiver-as-navigator role is a distinct cohort whose needs AI assistants and authorization workflows must explicitly serve.
