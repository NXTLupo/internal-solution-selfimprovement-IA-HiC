Retroactive post-payment audits claw back approved claims years later

Payers run post-payment integrity programs that reverse already-approved, already-paid claims. A UHC surgeon's bill approved and paid June 29, 2025 was retroactively denied December 20, 2025 by an outside auditor (EXL) with a seven-year lookback window. A commenter who runs these post-pay recovery queries daily confirms the practice is routine. This is a high-impact AI/analytics use case for payers - automated overbilling detection - that simultaneously generates acute consumer distrust.
