Autonomous Task Execution Backlashes Unless Human Choice Remains Preserved

Proactive AI agents provoke strong backlash when autonomous background actions execute without direct user confirmation. While background automation streamlines complex navigation, consumers require explicit separation between automated execution and human decision-making. Preserving mandatory confirmation prompts prevents unwanted automated influence and maintains user trust.

Nothing that pays or books happens without a clear final yes/no screen.
