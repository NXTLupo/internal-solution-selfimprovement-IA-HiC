Support Gaps Drive Adoption of AI as an Always-Available Proxy Advisor

Severe structural access barriers in traditional human advisory services; such as long wait times and insurance denials; drive individuals toward AI platforms for immediate support. Users appreciate that AI maintains consistent availability, lacks unexamined interpersonal biases, and operates without administrative friction. However, practitioners highlight significant risks when AI acts as an unmonitored substitute for expert human judgment.
