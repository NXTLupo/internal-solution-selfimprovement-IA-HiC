Advisors managing over $500M increase illiquid alternative allocations to a projected 5.7% by 2027

High-performing advisors are steadily shifting portfolio compositions away from traditional public equities in favor of private markets. Advisors overseeing more than $500 million in AUM currently allocate 4.4% of client portfolios to illiquid alternatives, with projections indicating a rise to 5.7% by 2027. This continuous growth demands that distribution platforms build API pipelines capable of surfacing transparent, compliant data for complex illiquid products.
