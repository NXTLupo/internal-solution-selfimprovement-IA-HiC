Standard hourly retainers are collapsing as AI compresses production time

As AI accelerators drastically lower implementation effort and speed up delivery, traditional hourly billing models are suffering severe margin degradation. Clients are increasingly aware of these technological efficiencies and are refusing to pay premium hourly fees for repetitive tasks. Agencies are being forced to transition away from headcount-scaling toward value-based retainers, performance metrics, and asset-based models.
