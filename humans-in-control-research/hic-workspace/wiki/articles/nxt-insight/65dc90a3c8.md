AI-driven development demands precise specification as the new bottleneck

Generative AI increases developer throughput to a degree that planning and scoping become the primary developmental bottleneck. An average planning specification of 5/10 previously succeeded because human developers compensated during builds. In an agentic environment, specifications must exceed 8/10 to avoid compounding errors.
