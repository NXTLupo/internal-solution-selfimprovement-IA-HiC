Beauty discovery has migrated from brand messaging to creators and live commerce

The 2026 Glossy Pop Awards show beauty brands winning through creator partnerships, TikTok Shop and live experiences rather than product-led messaging. MAC won Best Use of TikTok and Best Brand Activation; its TikTok Shop campaign combined creators, live commerce and curated products, showing social platforms 'have evolved from awareness channels into destinations for discovery and purchase.' Glow Recipe and Beautyblender won for a co-branded Watermelon Glow collaboration. Email for a Gen Z skincare brand must therefore route to and echo creator/UGC content, not stand alone.
