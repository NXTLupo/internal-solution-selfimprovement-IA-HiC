Gen Z reads clean-beauty claims as greenwashing unless certifications back them

A skincareaddiction poster researching clean beauty concludes most brands greenwash, packaging in new plastic and hiding supply-chain and carbon data, and only trusts NSF organic certification. This is decisive for template copy: a clean, organic brand targeting Gen Z should surface verifiable certifications and transparency in-email rather than vague 'clean' language that this audience actively distrusts.

They tout 'clean ingredients' and 'natural formulas' but then package everything in new plastic ... NSF organic kept coming up as one of the few with real requirements behind it.
