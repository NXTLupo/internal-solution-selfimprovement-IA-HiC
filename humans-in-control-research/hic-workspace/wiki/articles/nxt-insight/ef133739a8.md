Gen Z penalizes visibly AI-generated marketing content

Gartner found in June that 49% of U.S. consumers think AI has made content quality worse, rising to 57% among Gen Z and millennials [articles/digiday/4cf91768fb.md#c1]. REI drew backlash for an AI-generated ad of a woman with a bicycle that had two sets of handlebars, and Quip was called out merely for a surrealist campaign [articles/digiday/4cf91768fb.md#c1]. For a Gen Z email redesign, obviously AI-looking hero imagery risks the exact backlash the target cohort punishes hardest.

Brands risk backlash from this cohort if they use AI too obviously, or incorrectly [articles/digiday/4cf91768fb.md#c1]
