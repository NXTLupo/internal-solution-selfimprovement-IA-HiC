Subscription costs and premium model tiers fragment student AI access parity

Disparities in financial access create tiered usage patterns between students reliant on default free models and those leveraging paid enterprise or premium subscriptions ($125+/seat or custom university allocations). Model routing architectures (e.g., NeMo Switchyard) emerge to balance high-capability frontier models with budget-constrained local models.
