Inaccurate provider directories strand patients, exposing a high-value AI network-accuracy use case

A pregnant patient on Christus Texas found every in-network OBGYN had stopped accepting her insurance or left practice, while another relied on Anthem's own Sydney Health app that listed a facility as in-network before Anthem reclassified it. Directory inaccuracy is a recurring payer data-quality failure that AI directory-validation and network-adequacy tooling directly addresses.
