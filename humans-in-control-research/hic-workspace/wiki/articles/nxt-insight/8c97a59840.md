Clients reject low-value AI throughput and demand senior-led strategic judgment

The rapid proliferation of automated pipelines has saturated the market with unverified, lower-quality work. Clients are pushing back on agencies that utilize AI to cut delivery corners, expressing deep skepticism toward unreviewed outputs that fail in production. Consequently, strategic value has migrated back to senior advisors who utilize AI to improve quality internally while emphasizing hands-on accountability.
