Arithmetic Preference Control and Multi-Objective Alignment in Personal AI Guidance

Consumers expect personal AI assistants to offer explicit, arithmetic control over preference trade-offs—such as balancing helpfulness against verbosity or technical depth against accessibility. Rather than accepting opaque model defaults, power users and students demand granular configuration over how AI balances competing objectives.

"In comparison with the scalar-reward RLHF, DPA offers users intuitive control over LLM generation: they can arithmetically specify their desired trade-offs (e.g., more helpfulness with less verbosity)." [articles/arxiv-stat-ml/2c8a7a339a.md#c0]
