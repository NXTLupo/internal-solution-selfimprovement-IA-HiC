User Demand for Portable Context Layers Over Locked Institutional Interfaces

College students and early-career workers increasingly view AI context as a sovereign personal layer that must travel across services rather than remaining isolated inside university destinations. Tools using Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers allow users to maintain communication preferences, background knowledge, and persistent memories across ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. This validates a headless institutional direction where services expose endpoints to student-chosen AI engines.

Technological patterns show a decisive shift toward user-owned context layers that persist across multiple AI providers and applications.
