Unreliable constituent denominators destroy confidence in participation metrics

Incomplete or miscoded class-year rosters for cohorts such as 2025 and 2026 generate misleading participation and retention rates if unvalidated [articles/client-uploads/5114fee1d5.md#c1][articles/client-uploads/0fec271931.md#c0]. Product design requirements mandate that application interfaces suppress unsafe rates and visibly present coverage indicators rather than projecting false certainty [articles/client-uploads/5114fee1d5.md#c1][articles/client-uploads/0fec271931.md#c2]. Product specifications must maintain clear communication on data boundaries to ensure team alignment [articles/reddit-deep-r-productmanagement/dee215959f.md#c3].

If the Class of 2025 or 2026 denominator is incomplete, the product will say so and suppress the rate.
