AI models function effectively as interpretive assistants rather than autonomous truth engines

Operational guidelines specify that AI capabilities must serve exclusively as assistive tools for content generation and pattern interpretation, relying entirely on governed deterministic metrics [articles/client-uploads/5114fee1d5.md#c1][articles/client-uploads/0fec271931.md#c2]. Autonomous campaign execution and generative data fabrication remain strictly out of scope to preserve institutional data integrity [articles/client-uploads/5114fee1d5.md#c1][articles/client-uploads/0fec271931.md#c3]. Intelligence platforms require corpus-grounded workflows to ensure consulting-grade delivery without autonomous risk [articles/client-uploads/2731f7f1f5.md#c0].

AI helps the team interpret and act; it does not replace the data model, governance, or human approval.
