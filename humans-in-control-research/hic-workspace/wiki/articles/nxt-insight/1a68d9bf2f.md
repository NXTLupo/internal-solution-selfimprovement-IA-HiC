Actuarial AI outpaces carrier pricing infrastructure, and the laggards are mispricing farm risk

Milliman principal actuary Peggy Brinkman maps a trajectory from univariate techniques through generalized linear models and gradient boosted machines to explainable boosting machines, warning that carriers falling behind are mispricing risk as a result [articles/ins-insurance-business-au/c6c93226d1.md#c1]. Compute used to train leading models has doubled roughly every six months since 2012, outpacing Moore's Law, with RAND projecting fourfold annual growth in frontier training compute through 2030 [articles/ins-insurance-business-au/c6c93226d1.md#c1]. AIG has deployed Palantir Foundry and LLM tools giving underwriters access to more than four million records [articles/ins-insurance-business-au/bc350bdb23.md#c1]. AI-enabled agricultural underwriting is becoming table stakes.

Actuarial AI is advancing faster than most carriers' pricing infrastructure can keep up - and the carriers falling behind are mispricing risk as a result.
