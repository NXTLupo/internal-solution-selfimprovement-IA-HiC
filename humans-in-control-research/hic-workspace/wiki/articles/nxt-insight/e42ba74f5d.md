Users measure Figma's design agent against Framer, Claude Design, and Google Stitch and find it wanting

Practitioners evaluating the newly announced Figma design agent compare it unfavorably to one-prompt rivals; Framer, Google Stitch, Claude Design; and question RTL support. A common complaint: Figma Make was the chance to build from a design system but doesn't, doesn't read libraries correctly, and output isn't developer-usable. Claude Design, by contrast, starts from a design system, exports to Claude Code, and supports comment-on-element edits. Yet other users counter that Claude and Codex 'sux at designing anything' even given a completed mockup, so the competitive verdict is contested.

I tried Claude design this weekend and suggest everyone do the same. They START from a design system. You can export to Claude code, and you can even comment on specific elements.
