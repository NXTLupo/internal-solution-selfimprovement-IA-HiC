Cheapest-coverage spiral under macro strain

Macroeconomic strain reshapes coverage selection downward: households strip add-ons, double deductibles for marginal relief and absorb escrow-driven hikes. Cash-strapped policyholders present a heightened risk profile through deferred maintenance and desperation-driven claims, predicting a high-risk pool of under-insured operations if the same behavior reaches farms.

If you are looking for the cheapest price, you will get the cheapest coverage! It's as simple as that
