Subpar portfolio management technology creates acute churn risk and blocks institutional asset allocations

Digital capability is a primary driver of client retention and institutional asset flow. Approximately 29% of retail investors report readiness to switch financial advisors specifically due to subpar technology capabilities. Simultaneously, over 66% of institutional investors now prefer allocating capital to managers utilizing cutting-edge technological infrastructure, forcing half of all wealth firms to modernize their core platforms.

Approximately 29% of investors state they expect to switch financial advisors, with subpar technology capabilities cited as a primary driver.
