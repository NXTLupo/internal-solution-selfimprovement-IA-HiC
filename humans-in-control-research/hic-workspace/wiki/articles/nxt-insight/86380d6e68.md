Asset managers deploy specialized product teams to bridge the severe advisor education gap in illiquid alternatives

Distributing private market assets introduces severe education friction, which 75% of asset managers identify as their single largest obstacle to alternative asset distribution. Generalist wholesalers lack the technical depth required to independently sell these complex vehicles to RIAs. Consequently, 79% of Tier-I asset managers are restructuring their sales motions by pairing specialized advisor-facing product experts with their traditional wholesale distribution teams.

79% of Tier-I asset managers are employing advisor-facing product specialists to support generalist wholesalers in distributing complex alternatives.
