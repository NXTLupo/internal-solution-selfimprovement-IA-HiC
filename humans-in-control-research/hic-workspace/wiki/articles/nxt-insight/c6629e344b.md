Consumers already suspect payers use AI to deny claims and invoke the NAIC model bulletin

A r/healthinsurance commenter advising a member on a denied specialty drug (Rinvoq) explicitly told them to file a state complaint asking whether the insurer is 'now using AI to process claims' and whether it follows the NAIC AI model Bulletin. This is the clearest signal in the corpus that the consumer cohort perceives payer AI adoption as an adversarial, denial-accelerating force, framing the trust barrier any patient-facing AI must overcome.

File a complaint with your states dept of insurance and be sure to include language about are they now using AI to process claims
