El Nino losses cascade sequentially from crops to livestock to total livelihood collapse

FAO's Alvar-Beltran describes a cascade in which a farmer might first lose crops, then livestock, and with that their entire livelihood. In Southern Africa the most recent cycle brought the worst drought in over a century, leaving 61 million people in need and pushing 8 million into food insecurity. In Haiti, 2015-16 harvests collapsed up to 70 percent. This sequencing means single-peril crop cover understates correlated multi-asset exposure.
