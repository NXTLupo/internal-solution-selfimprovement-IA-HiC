Dry-spring moisture stress splits 2026 UK yields by soil type and region, not by average rainfall

The final AHDB Crop Development Report found only 64% of winter wheat rated good or excellent after prolonged dry April-May conditions, with recovery variable, particularly on lighter soils and in parts of eastern England [articles/agr-agriland-ie/2fb526b55c.md#c1]. Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland benefited from more favorable moisture. This regional and soil-type divergence means climate risk cannot be priced at a national average, it must be geolocated to the field, echoing the correlated-loss concentration in cached drought themes.
