Coverage adjudication complexity exceeds patient comprehension, fueling demand for AI navigation

Patients cannot decode "allowed amount," balance billing, individual-versus-family deductible application, or why an Anthem PPO covering "70% OON" actually pays ~44% of a bill. A commenter explains "allowed amount = insurance contracted rate" because members do not understand it. The cognitive load of EOB interpretation is the core consumer pain that AI benefits-navigation assistants target.

Insurance covers: ~$87 → ~44% of the original bill!!! ... This 'headline benefit' is essentially a white lie.
