Raw AI email generators produce basic layouts, pushing teams toward modular blocks and raw-HTML export

In r/emailmarketing, a team trying to keep brand aesthetic reports that matching brand with raw AI generators is tough because most spit out basic layouts, and that many teams export raw HTML into their sender. Commenters favor a modular template setup with a small set of custom blocks, while noting the initial block build and getting them to render on mobile takes longer than writing the campaigns. This is direct practitioner evidence for the reusable-component (MJML-style) approach over prompt-only generation.
