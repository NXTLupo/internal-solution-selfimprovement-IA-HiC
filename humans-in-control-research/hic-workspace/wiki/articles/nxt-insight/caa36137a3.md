Bundling is the proven adoption lever

Case evidence shows bundling drives adoption: Kenya's Family Bank and Digifarm bundle weather-index insurance with credit via a digital platform, Hollard and Blue Marble bundled insurance with seeds in Mozambique, and India combines insurance with credit and advisory. Bundling with inputs, extension and health coverage counters post-enrolment neglect.

In Kenya, Family bank and Digifarm have come together to distribute Risk contingent credit which bundles credit with Weather Index Insurance (WII), through a digital platform
