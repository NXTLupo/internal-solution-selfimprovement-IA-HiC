Structured Life-Coaching Prompts Convert AI into 24/7 Accountability Partners

Students and young professionals actively configure AI models as structured, non-judgmental life coaches and language tutors. By deploying step-by-step sequential prompts rather than open-ended queries, users create ongoing accountability systems for goal setting, skill development, and executive function support.
