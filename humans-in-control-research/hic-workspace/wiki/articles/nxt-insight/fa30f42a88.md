Human-in-the-loop approval is inserted as an explicit requires_approval step

The corpus clusters strongly around approval flags: workflows insert a requires_approval step so lifecycle agents cannot break attribution without human sign-off, and one-click approve/deny logs who approved and what changed [articles/reddit-deep-r-marketingautomation/8dbb56dac9.md#c1]. The agent-workflow template names a human approver as a required field [social-intelligence/reddit-deep/marketingautomation/1q8a7ov.md#c1]. Figma reinforces the same collaborative-review stance: the AI produces the first 80% and a human review pass swaps images and edits copy before anything ships [articles/figma-blog/8f7ed15f3a.md#c2]. This is the governance layer that preserves brand compliance and creative control.
