Prior-authorization and appeals labor is the workflow AI automation must displace

Providers describe prior authorization, denial handling, and appeals as a dedicated multi-person cost center. One provider notes hiring 'ten people just to verify insurance, handle prior authorizations, deal with denials and appeals' renders the $80 per-visit insurance reimbursement financially infeasible. Another provider states they 'do all the appeals and coverage issues for our patients and patients rarely ever have to touch anything.' This is the highest-volume administrative task AI claims-automation and authorization-prediction tools are positioned to absorb for the provider and payer cohorts.

When we have to hire 10 people just to verify people's insurance, handle prior authorizations, deal with denials and appeals, etc., the $80 insurance may give us per visit is no longer financially feasible.
