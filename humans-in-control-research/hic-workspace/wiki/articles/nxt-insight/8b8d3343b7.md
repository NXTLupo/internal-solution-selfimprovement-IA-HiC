Component variants and auto layout let templates flex without breaking brand

Figma Buzz templates are now built from component properties so marketers browse and switch flexible layouts without sifting hundreds of variants [articles/figma-blog/56342e97ed.md#c1]. Brand designers set up editing guardrails: auto layout keeps spacing consistent when content changes, separated text layers map to CSV fields for bulk creation, and component variants let a logo or color swap without exposing too much manual-edit surface [articles/figma-blog/0c58f20247.md#c1]. This flex-but-don't-break template model is the reusable-component approach the objective calls for, and it preserves the creative flexibility Deciem needs.

To create self-serve templates that bend—but don’t break—brand designers need to consider work through a marketing lens [articles/figma-blog/56342e97ed.md#c1]
