A super El Nino threatens a mini-Dust-Bowl that would stress crop yields for years

AccuWeather forecasters put the probability of a super El Nino at 70%, bringing extreme Western wildfire risk, drought, and a possible 'mini-Dust Bowl' across the Great Plains. Colorado, Nebraska, Oklahoma and Wyoming already show extreme drought versus virtually none a year prior. NOAA confirms El Nino conditions are present and expected to strengthen into winter 2026-27. Verisk's Tory Farney flags increased wildfire likelihood across the Northwest, Northern California, and Four Corners, concentrating correlated property-risk exposure exactly where drought is worsening.

If the long-term drought is as bad as it could be, it raises the possibility of a 'mini-Dust Bowl,' stressing crop yields that could last months or years ahead
