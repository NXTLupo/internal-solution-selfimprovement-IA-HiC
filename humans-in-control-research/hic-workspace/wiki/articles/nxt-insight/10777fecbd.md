Figma's own critique ritual defines the goals an automated critic must inherit

Figma's design team documents six critique methods and codifies the goals of critique: unblocking problems and generating ideas, elevating quality, encouraging consistency, and sharing context. Critically, they explicitly exclude 'Making major product decisions' or 'Determining product roadmaps' from critique scope. They also diagnosed real failure modes: feedback too shallow, not actionable, groupthink and '+1s,' and people being too nice rather than honest. Any AI critic built on Figma inherits both the target goals and these documented failure modes to avoid.

Noticeably missing from this is: "Making major product decisions" or "Determining product roadmaps." [articles/figma-blog/28b0812a51.md#c2]
