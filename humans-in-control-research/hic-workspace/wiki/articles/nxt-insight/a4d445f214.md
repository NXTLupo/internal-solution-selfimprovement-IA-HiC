Over a quarter of user interactions with personal AI assistants center on professional, career, and relationship advice. Users expect objective, non-sycophantic guidance that respectfully challenges assumptions rather than offering empty praise. Model providers are specifically training frontier models to reduce excessive validation in personal advisory scenarios.
