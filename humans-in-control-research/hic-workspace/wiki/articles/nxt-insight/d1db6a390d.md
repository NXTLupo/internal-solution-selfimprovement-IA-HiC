Autonomous cross-application agents require granular human consent gates

Users embrace cross-tool agents that coordinate tasks across email, calendar, and messaging platforms, but react strongly against unverified autonomous modifications. Practical adoption depends on explicit human-in-the-loop validation modes, such as 'ask before edit,' to prevent accidental modifications and prompt injection vulnerabilities.
