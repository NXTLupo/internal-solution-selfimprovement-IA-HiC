Emergency care triggers retroactive medical-necessity denials, the highest-stakes payer friction

Consumers face denials after emergency presentations: a member admitted for abnormal uterine bleeding and anemia denied because InterQual criteria deemed it observation-level, and an ER chest workup denied as a mental-health panic claim. Payers argue care could have been delivered at a lower level. These post-hoc necessity determinations are where AI clinical-criteria automation most directly shapes consumer and provider outcomes.

The request did not meet InterQual criteria because there was no documentation supporting the presence of hemolytic anemia... These services can be rendered and reimbursed at an observation level of care.
