Provider-payer billing handoffs fail at the integration seam, not the algorithm

Repeated cases show claims breaking at data-exchange boundaries between provider billing systems and payer adjudication, the same interoperability seam the corpus clusters flag as the gating barrier to EHR-connected AI (SMART on FHIR, EHR switching). A lab claim processed out-of-network purely because the prescriber and lab sat in different states' Blue Cross plans [social-intelligence/reddit-deep/healthinsurance/1qkiipv.md#c4]. A provider transposed insurance info incorrectly, then took a year to refile [social-intelligence/reddit-deep/healthinsurance/1rdy3v7.md#c7]. Integration friction, not model quality, is the recurring operational failure.
