Job seekers construct custom AI agents to automate targeted resume alignment

Early-career applicants deploy specialized application agents that manage pre-approved libraries of resume bullet points. By matching job descriptions against local truth files rather than generating text from scratch, candidates save token costs, prevent hallucinations, and optimize LinkedIn skill profiles for recruiter screening algorithms.

When applying to a new job I gave it the job description, then had it selected the highest match skills, job and bullets [articles/reddit-deep-r-jobs/f1bb86494c.md#c2]
