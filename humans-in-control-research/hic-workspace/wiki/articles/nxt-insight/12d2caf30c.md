Information arbitrage in low-code AI setup is rapidly collapsing

Low barrier to entry in simple AI consulting has flooded the market with generalists who repackage off-the-shelf tools like Zapier. As SaaS tools build native AI capabilities, this knowledge arbitrage is vanishing. Sophisticated clients now bypass expensive mediators to build internal pipelines.

We've created a market for expensive mediation between clients and tools they could use directly. [articles/reddit-deep-r-consulting/990be33b24.md#c0]
