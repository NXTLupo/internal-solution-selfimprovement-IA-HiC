Measuring the workflow before automating it is the missing baseline that kills pilots

Sandy Carter argues that half the companies running AI in production cannot measure its ROI because they skipped capturing a baseline, and prescribes picking one weekly workflow, writing down what it costs today, defining better as a number, and naming a sign-off owner. She warns that saved hours are not a result unless redeployed into something you can point to. For Deciem this sets the pre-automation baseline discipline for the email production workflow before the multi-agent system replaces it.

Pick one workflow you run every week, write down what it costs today in hours or dollars, define better as a number, and name the person who signs off. [articles/marketing-ai-inst/3e68697629.md#c1]
