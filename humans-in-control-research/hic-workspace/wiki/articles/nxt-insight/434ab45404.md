Repeat purchase, not first conversion, is where DTC skincare revenue compounds

Recharge platform data shows subscription categories with frequent reorder cycles behave like annuities, and DTC operators converge on post-purchase flows, packaging, and rewards as the real drivers of repeat purchase. An ecommerce operator in r/ecommerce notes most sales come from paid traffic and email and worries about creative fatigue bidding the same audience pools. For skincare, a consumable, the email template must carry replenishment and rewards logic, not only acquisition promos.
