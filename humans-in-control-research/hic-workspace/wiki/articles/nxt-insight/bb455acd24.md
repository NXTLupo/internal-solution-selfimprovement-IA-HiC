Alternative asset allocations hitting $12B by 2025 severely strain existing third-party technology systems

The rapid migration toward private equity and private credit has exposed severe limitations in wealth management infrastructure. Average advisor allocations to alternatives expanded from $7.5 billion in 2022 to $12 billion in 2025. This surge is causing significant operational friction, as legacy platforms were not designed to handle the illiquidity, capital calls, and complex reporting required by private assets. Asset managers are scrambling to upgrade tech systems to facilitate smooth data flow for these instruments.
