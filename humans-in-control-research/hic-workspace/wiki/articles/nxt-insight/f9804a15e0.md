Defensible agency IP is shifting from human experience to structured code assets

Historically, design and technical consultancy value was locked inside the heads of senior leads, internal runbooks, and manual processes. In the AI-native landscape, standard checklists are quickly commoditized. To remain valuable, agencies must translate their proprietary methods and delivery expertise into programmatic assets, reusable multi-agent workflows, and specialized technical tools that clients cannot easily replicate.

The IP used to be locked in the experience of a few technical leads, institutional knowledge, run books, and yes some real software IP. Current AI dev tools have just changed the equation. Now, all your IP needs to be software. [articles/reddit-deep-r-agency/a4c0258e76.md#c2]
