Digital health tools and backend systems routinely mistranslate or override provider coding, leading to severe financial consequences. Health apps misrepresent facility tiers, while lab systems drop decimal points in diagnosis codes. These integration and legacy system failures represent the baseline operational barriers that future AI deployments must resolve.
