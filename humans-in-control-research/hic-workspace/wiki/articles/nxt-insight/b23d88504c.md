A third buyer, the LLM, now gatekeeps which brands get recommended

Marketing Week argues an 'Invisible Buyer', the LLM, now sits between brand and consumer: being found (GEO) is not the same as being chosen. In a 220-answer sports-nutrition audit, Optimum Nutrition appeared in 65 answers and Myprotein in 59, but when the model named one winner it chose Optimum Nutrition 16 times and Myprotein once. Over half of all AI citations came from independent authorities and Reddit appeared in all ten categories; brand-owned pages supplied under a quarter. A skincare brand's email and off-site reviews now feed what AI recommends, so email should actively solicit reviews and UGC.

GEO will make your brand present and retrieved. It will not make it the brand the machine recommends. [articles/marketing-week/9ce13d9b7b.md#c2]
