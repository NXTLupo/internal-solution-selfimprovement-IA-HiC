The first rebill, not the discounted first order, decides whether a subscriber sticks

Recharge measured first-renewal rate across 29.8 million new subscriptions from 20,000+ CPG subscription brands and found the sweet spot for a first-order discount sits under 25%, with value falling off a cliff past 75%. Nearly half (48%) of new subscribers are acquired with a discount, and nine times out of ten the second order steps back to full price. For a skincare brand building replenishment email flows, this reframes the welcome email around post-purchase education and unboxing, not deeper discounts.

a subscriber you acquire at up to ~25% off is worth as much over 12 months as that store's own typical subscriber. Past 75% off? The value falls off a cliff. [articles/recharge-blog/47a8d6b269.md#c1]
