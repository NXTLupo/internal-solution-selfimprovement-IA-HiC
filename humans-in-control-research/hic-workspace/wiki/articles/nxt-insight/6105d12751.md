Practitioners build persona critics as Skills that critique designs over MCP

A r/UXDesign practitioner describes creating a Skill that consumed selected user-interview notes and encodes five personas 'based on real quotes and opinions from five real users,' then points that Skill at Figma designs via MCP to have 'them' critique from an informed perspective. When compared against real feedback from the same users on the same prototype, the persona critique overlapped with the real feedback. This is the clearest field evidence of the objective's critic-and-skill pattern working in practice, and it names the exact wiring: Skill plus MCP.

I can point this skill at my Figma designs (via MCP) and have "them" critique from a bit more of an informed perspective. [social-intelligence/reddit-deep/uxdesign/1uke7pb.md#c5]
