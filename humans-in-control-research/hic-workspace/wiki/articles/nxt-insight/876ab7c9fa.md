Multi-Agent Interoperability Across Heterogeneous Personal Assistant Ecosystems

Early adopters of personal assistants like Meta Muse are encountering silos where individual agents cannot coordinate across family or peer groups. Emerging solutions create intermediary shared context layers to link disparate agents (e.g., Muse, Grok, ChatGPT) without requiring users to standardize on a single provider platform. This highlights the necessity for institutional APIs to support cross-agent communication protocols.

The other thing I'm excited about is that everyone doesn't have to use the same AI. I can use Muse, my partner can use Grok, and Smitty keeps us working from the same family information.
