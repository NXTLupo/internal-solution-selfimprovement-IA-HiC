Figma's agent draws unfavorable comparison to Framer, Paper, and one-prompt rivals

Users evaluating Figma's newly announced design agent question its capabilities and RTL support and compare it unfavorably to Framer, Google Stitch, and Paper. One argues Figma is a vector-graphics tool whose auto-layout only mimics flexbox and needs a translation layer, whereas Paper is code where what you see is what can be implemented, so agents have a simpler job. This competitive pressure defines the limitations band of the white paper.

Figma is dated at this point, it's a vector graphics tool that often does things that appear to be the same as code, but they are definitely not and require a translation layer.
