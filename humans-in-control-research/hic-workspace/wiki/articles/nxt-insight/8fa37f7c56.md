Opaque clinical guideline enforcement drives widespread operational exhaustion

The manual burden of contesting medical necessity denials is exhausting both patients and provider billing departments. Payers routinely deny procedures that meet clinical guidelines or demand authorizations for emergency interventions. This severe friction points to a primary administrative use case for AI: automating prior authorization and defending clinical necessity.
