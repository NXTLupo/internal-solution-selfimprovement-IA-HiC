Candidates who ace interviews and cognitive tests often fail at the actual work

Hiring leaders report that candidates who ace interviews and assessments like the CCAT often fail at the actual work, questioning current evaluation methods. One TA manager describes a top interview candidate scoring in the 38th percentile on CCAT, splitting the team on whether to trust the test or the interview. This predictive-validity gap between assessment performance and on-the-job performance is exactly the evaluation problem AI-assisted competency signals aim to narrow.
