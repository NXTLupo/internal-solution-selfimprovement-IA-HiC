Bounded scope definitions protect core delivery timelines from feature creep

Successful product implementations strictly distinguish between immediate scope deliverables and future capabilities like predictive modeling or autonomous sending [articles/client-uploads/0fec271931.md#c3][articles/client-uploads/5114fee1d5.md#c1]. Documenting non-goals and multi-stage delivery timelines prevents resource fragmentation and maintains team focus on high-priority outcomes [social-intelligence/reddit-deep/saas/1m6h008.md#c2][articles/client-uploads/d832673667.md#c9].

The demo prioritizes aggregate cohort decisions for Annual Giving; individual CRM depth is not day-one scope.
