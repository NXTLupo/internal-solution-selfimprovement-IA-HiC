Prior-authorization gating on drugs and tests delays caregiver-managed care

Step-therapy and PA hurdles dominate medication and testing access: Blue Shield CA requires six months of documented diet and exercise for Wegovy/Zepbound, BRCA genetic-testing requires pedigree plus in-network lab routing, and a formulary change forced a Rinvoq patient from $25 to $300 monthly. Commenters note providers can clear PA faster than members. PA automation is the most-cited payer AI use case, and its current friction falls hardest on caregivers coordinating chronic and specialty care.

prior authorization requires proof of a calorie deficit, exercise, and what looks like 6 months of documented lifestyle changes
