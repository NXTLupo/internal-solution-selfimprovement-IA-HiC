Front-line payer reps cannot answer coverage questions, exposing a chatbot-augmentation gap

Patients repeatedly report customer-service reps read outdated portal information, give the run-around, and disclaim knowledge of whether a procedure is covered. One patient was told reps would not pre-estimate cost because she was "not a customer." This service-quality vacuum is the leading consumer-facing AI deployment target, but the same threads show why trust in opaque payer AI is fragile.
