Deterministic linters are the hook practitioners trust over the agent

Rather than trusting the agent, practitioners build deterministic linter hooks tied to their build pipeline. One recommends 'Just make a linter for your design system' then having the agent 'make a linter that governs it and tie it to my npm processes' so if it sneaks in a color or spacing unit that doesn't exist 'it bounces and remembers to fix it' [articles/reddit-deep-r-designsystems/8acc3a5632.md#c3]. Another vibe-coded a Figma plugin that 'audits my designs to make sure I've been using tokens rather than raw hex or px values' and checks for deprecated tokens [social-intelligence/reddit-deep/uxdesign/1uke7pb.md#c6]. This deterministic-guardrail pattern is the exact hooks mechanism the white paper's deterministic-hooks section requires.
