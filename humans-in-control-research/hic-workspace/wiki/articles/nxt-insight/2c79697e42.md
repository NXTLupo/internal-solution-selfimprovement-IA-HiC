The 2026 email itself becomes the destination, delivering the debrief without the click

Convince & Convert rebuilt its newsletter, The Trendline, around delivering strategic value inside the email so readers don't have to click through, with a deliberate hierarchy: brief intro, prioritized stories with takeaways, then links and a poll. This inverts the classic teaser-and-click model. A Gen Z skincare template can borrow this self-contained, hierarchy-driven structure to respect short attention and reward opens directly.
