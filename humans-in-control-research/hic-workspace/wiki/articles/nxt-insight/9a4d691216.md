Prior-authorization friction is the operational chokepoint a clinical-AI agenda will be measured against

The corpus is saturated with prior-authorization failures: an approved pre-auth still denied as 'not medically necessary' a year later [social-intelligence/reddit-deep/healthinsurance/1p5zwgc.md#c0], step-therapy forcing patients onto cheaper drugs against physician orders, and annual re-authorization treated as bureaucratic waste [social-intelligence/reddit-deep/healthinsurance/1pn9f11.md#c7]. One developer-adjacent question even asks whether provider portals auto-flag pre-auth needs inside Epic or require manual checking [social-intelligence/reddit-deep/healthinsurance/1rymum8.md#c3]. This is the highest-volume operational use case for automation in the corpus and the one with the clearest member demand for fix.

If a physician orders say a head CT in Epic, will they then have to manually check for benefits coverage with insurance company X's provider portal? Or is it more integrated?
