Identity and Session Propagation Bottlenecks across Multi-Platform Ecosystems

As students attempt to connect their personal AI tools across learning management systems, career portals, and cloud backends, distributed auth models create severe usability friction. Without a unified identity bridge, users encounter constant re-authentication prompts, uncoordinated token refreshes, and security risks associated with raw token forwarding.
