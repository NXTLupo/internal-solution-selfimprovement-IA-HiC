Consulting giants trade delivery headcount for capital-intensive custom systems

Large-scale legacy consulting firms are experiencing massive shifts in their structural P&L metrics. While these giants are actively reducing payroll expenses by laying off generalist delivery workers, they are simultaneously forced to deploy high capital budgets toward building sovereign, custom, on-premise infrastructure. This leaves the overall profit margins of transitioning legacy agencies highly volatile and difficult to evaluate.

While major firms (Accenture, EY, PwC) are announcing massive hiring sprees of "data and AI professionals" alongside massive layoffs of "generalist delivery" workers, the net margin impact on their overall corporate P&Ls remains unclear [articles/gemini-focused-research/5ba150df2f.md#c4]
