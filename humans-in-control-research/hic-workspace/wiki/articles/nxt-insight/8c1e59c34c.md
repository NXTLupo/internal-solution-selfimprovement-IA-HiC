# AI co-pilots compress the analyst research cycle

Across the healthcare corpus, analysts report that AI assistants now originate the first research pass and surface evidence faster than manual review. This shifts the bottleneck from collection to judgment. The pattern recurs in FDA-regulated workflows where review time, not data access, governs throughput.

This is a synthesized insight grounded in collected evidence for cross-run reuse.
