Prior authorization consumes the majority of front-desk capacity, defining AI's clearest operational target

A practicing physician reports front-desk staff spend roughly 75% of their time on prior authorizations, and that even authorized care is later clawed back by insurers despite documentation [social-intelligence/reddit-deep/healthinsurance/1oh36hl.md#c0]. This administrative load is the operational use case healthcare AI most directly addresses heading into 2026. The friction is corroborated across patient-side accounts of urgent prior-authorization assignment delays at Cigna [social-intelligence/reddit-deep/healthinsurance/1qwrg35.md#c0]. The volume and repetitiveness of these workflows make them the densest automation surface in the sector.
