Provider billing errors become patient liability and credit threats

When providers miscode, bill the wrong payer, or miss timely-filing windows, the consequence lands on the patient as collections and credit risk. A hospital took eight months to file a corrected claim, the claim denied for timely filing, and the patient was billed nearly $50,000 then sent to collections. A Mayo billing department refuses to file appeals under $2,000. This documentation/coding fragility is a direct provider-cohort use case for AI claims-integrity tooling.
