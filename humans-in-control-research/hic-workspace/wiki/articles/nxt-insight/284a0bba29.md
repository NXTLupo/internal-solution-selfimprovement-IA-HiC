A large share of placements come from candidates already sitting in the firm's own database

Recruiterflow's Economics of Recruiting research found a significant share of placements comes from candidates already known to the agency, and its AIRA Matchmaker surfaces matching candidates already inside the database. For a retained search firm like DSG, this identifies database-intelligence and re-surfacing as one of the highest-yield, lowest-risk AI use cases, since the data already exists and the task is retrieval rather than new sourcing.
