Segmentation errors punish batch-and-blast, rewarding an owned engaged base

The $20,000 'Oops' email began as a segmentation failure, a thank-you sent to everyone including non-purchasers, triggering a flood of confused replies. Practical Ecommerce reframes deliverability around this: send primarily to subscribers who show interest, using base segments like signed up in 30 days, opened in 45, clicked in 90. Apple Mail Privacy Protection inflates opens into a 'vanity metric,' so click behavior, not opens, should drive which Gen Z subscribers receive polished versus plain templates.
