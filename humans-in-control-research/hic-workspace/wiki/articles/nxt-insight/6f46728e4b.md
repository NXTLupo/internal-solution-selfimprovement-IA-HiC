Prior-authorization codes payers refuse to issue block emergency surgery coverage

A Surest/UnitedHealthcare member was denied surgeon and PA fees for an emergency cholecystectomy for missing an 'authorization code' that Surest itself would not provide, redirecting the member to HR, which lacked authority to issue it. Another family needing a nephrectomy was forced to wait until five days before surgery for a code. This authorization-issuance bottleneck is the highest-volume payer workflow targeted for AI automation, and the cohort experiencing it (consumers and providers) bears the financial risk.
