Confidential client data is the binding constraint on AI adoption in advisory firms

Boutique consultants handling PE deal data describe refraining from uploading confidential client data into AI tools but no longer being able to afford that, and debate ChatGPT Enterprise, Gemini, or self-hosted models. One commenter insists on a secure, approved pipeline before adding any tool. For DSG, whose search engagements carry sensitive executive and client information, data-governance architecture is a prerequisite gate on every AI use case, not an afterthought.

So far I have refrained from uploading confidential client data into AI tools, but I feel like we can't afford that anymore. [articles/reddit-deep-r-consulting/72e3e55510.md#c0]
