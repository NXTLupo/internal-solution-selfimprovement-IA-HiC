Opaque administrative gatekeeping forces individuals into manual multi-party escalation

The corpus repeatedly shows enrollees and patients trapped between an institution, a third-party administrator, and a provider, each disclaiming authority. A Surest/UHC member was told to obtain an authorization code from an employer HR group that had no power to issue one. A teacher was told only the employer, not the insurer, could change a plan. This pattern of fragmented ownership and manual phone-chain escalation mirrors the cross-system handoff problem an AI shared-service model would target, though the corpus situates it in healthcare, not enrollment operations.

You need an authorization code for a cholecystectomy, but we can't provide you with the authorization code. You need to call your employer benefits group
