The model-harness interface, not the model, is where agent execution fails

Amazon Science argues that as models improve, model-specific optimizations become brittle, so teams should target invariant components at the model-harness interface, the boundary where model outputs are interpreted and executed. Two questions define the alignment problem: does the harness understand what the model intends, and is the model clear about how the harness interpreted its actions. For Figma, this frames the harnessed-execution extension point: the MCP server is the harness translating agent intent into canvas mutations.
