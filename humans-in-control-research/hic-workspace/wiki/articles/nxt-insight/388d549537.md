A locked modular email design system beats regenerating aesthetics per campaign with AI

Practitioners in r/emailmarketing converge on building a small fixed set of reusable blocks before letting AI assemble campaigns, because raw AI generators produce basic off-brand layouts [articles/nxt-insight/59b39797ea.md#c0]. The recommended system fixes typography, spacing, image treatment, CTA hierarchy, and product/editorial/promotional modules first, so the inbox feels like a continuation of the brand rather than a collection of one-off templates [social-intelligence/reddit-deep/emailmarketing/1voakip.md#c2]. For a redesign this means locking modular blocks, then constraining AI to iterate inside them.
