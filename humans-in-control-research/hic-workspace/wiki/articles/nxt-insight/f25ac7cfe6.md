Admissions intake flows are pre-built but require sandbox validation before deployment

A Salesforce practitioner describes sandboxing three education-cloud admissions inquiry and contact-request screen flows and confirming they work as intended, evidencing that repeatable admissions-workflow components exist as configurable platform artifacts rather than bespoke builds. This is the closest the corpus comes to the 'build once, scale across' admissions mechanism central to the research objective. It shows the validation step a portfolio operator would standardize before pushing a capability to multiple schools. The evidence is from a single practitioner thread, limiting confidence.

I have sandboxed the three edu admissions inquiry/contact request flows and they work great with the intended screen flows.
