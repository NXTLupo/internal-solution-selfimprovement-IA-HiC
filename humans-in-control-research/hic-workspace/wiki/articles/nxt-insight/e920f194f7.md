Risk transfer is only one of four strategies farms weigh against avoidance, acceptance and mitigation

The Nationwide framework classifies risk-management strategies into avoidance, acceptance, transfer (insurance or contractual) and control/mitigation, the last including loss prevention like wind rings around grain bins, loss reduction like fire detection, separation, and diversification such as backup combines or generators. This shows underwriting viability is contested by farmers who can self-insure or mitigate rather than buy coverage, a substitution dynamic that shapes demand for agricultural insurance products.
