Visual clutter kills the message when every element competes for attention

Venngage's flyer example crams photos, illustrations, icons, badges, checklists, multiple service categories, testimonials, guarantees, and several CTAs into one design, so each element competes and the text becomes hard to follow. A polished-but-empty AI poster looked great yet 'conveyed nothing.' For an email redesign, this argues for restraint and a single dominant CTA rather than stacking offers, badges, and social proof into one crowded frame.
