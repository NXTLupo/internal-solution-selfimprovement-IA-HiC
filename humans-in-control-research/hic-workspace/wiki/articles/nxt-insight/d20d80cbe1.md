Email value must land inside the inbox, not behind a click

Convince & Convert's newsletter refresh abandoned the list-of-links format because readers ignore emails that force a click-through to find value. They rebuilt to prove worth inside the email itself, treating curation and distilled insight as the deliverable rather than a reading assignment. For a skincare brand this reframes the template around self-contained value (routine tips, product education) instead of a link farm to the shop.

We needed to prove our value inside the email itself, not just on the other side of a new browser tab [articles/convince-convert/3639b3d4fc.md#c2]
