Payers outsource claims and service offshore, eroding member confidence and access

A thread explicitly asks why insurers contract overseas vendors for customer service and claim reviews, with members reporting opacity and difficulty reaching resolution. Commenters frame it as cost-control: insurers reduce overhead because medical costs rise faster than inflation [social-intelligence/reddit-deep/healthinsurance/1ms43t8.md#c6]. One notes members 'aren't their customers, we're the product' [social-intelligence/reddit-deep/healthinsurance/1ms43t8.md#c6]. AI deployment here risks deepening the same trust deficit unless paired with resolution authority.

Insurance companies don't care about confidence in plan members. We aren't their customers we're the product. Employers are their customers
