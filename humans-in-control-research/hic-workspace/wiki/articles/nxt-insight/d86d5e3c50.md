Behavioral demand suppression outweighs pure economics

Farmer insurance uptake is suppressed as much by risk perception, trust, and cultural preference for traditional risk-sharing as by price. This reframes underwriting viability: expanding coverage requires behavioral and educational intervention, not only premium subsidy. Directly informs the educational-needs dimension of the 2026 objective.

there is a general lack of risk perception and awareness, particularly evident in Ethiopia where farmers are not fully aware of the benefits of crop insurance
