Selective Cognitive Augmentation Over Blind Content Generation in Academia

Students actively differentiate between using AI as a cognitive partner (pointing out gaps, outlining, tutoring) versus copy-pasting raw output. Verbatim student testimonies confirm that automated copy-pasting degrades retention ('in one ear out the other'), while using AI to critique student drafts or structure arguments strengthens core skills. Academic guidance models must foster interactive critique rather than passive generation.

If I paste my draft and ask it to point out gaps or explain stuff I missed, I actually remember more; if I just copy answers, it goes in one ear out the other lol.
