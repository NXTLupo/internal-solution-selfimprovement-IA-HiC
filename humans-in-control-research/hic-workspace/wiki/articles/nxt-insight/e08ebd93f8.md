Search firms want native AI across the ATS/CRM workflow, not bolt-on point tools

Recruiterflow's platform analysis reports recruitment technology is shifting from individual AI features toward AI that works across the entire recruiting workflow, with firms wanting intelligence built into their ATS and CRM rather than added as a separate layer. Its AIRA layer spans candidate matching, meeting capture, research, database updates, sourcing, and job-change monitoring. This validates a single-workflow AI architecture as the industry-preferred implementation path for firms like DSG, over stitching disconnected assistants together.

Rather than offering AI as a standalone assistant, Recruiterflow's AIRA works as an intelligence layer across recruiting workflows including candidate matching, meeting capture, research, database updates [articles/exs-recruiterflow/db18516ca2.md#c2]
