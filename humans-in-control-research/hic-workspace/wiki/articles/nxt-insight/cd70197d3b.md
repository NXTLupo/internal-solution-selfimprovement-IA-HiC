Leaders demand human-in-the-loop checkpoints and source-citing transparency before shipping agents

Figma's Sightlines newsletter distills senior-leader guidance on agentic products: trust is p0, and users are more likely to trust an agent when it shows its work and cites its sources. Leaders should define governance checkpoints early; aligning on what routine work the agent handles alone versus what always keeps a human in the loop, like anything involving sensitive health information. Accor's Thomas Vidal frames the goal as creating rules for consistent system behavior, and Expedia's Rachel Been stresses rigor around testing and launching. This is the approvals-and-governance axis of the objective.
