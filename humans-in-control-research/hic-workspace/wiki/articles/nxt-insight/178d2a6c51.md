Manual, repeatedly-rebuilt content assets break down at scale without a single source of truth

Visme documents that high-volume deliverables are rebuilt repeatedly because content is hard to find, checks on what goes out are insufficient so a deck reaches a client with an old logo, and data is moved by hand so every manually entered value can be typed incorrectly. This is the current-state pain a reusable-component email pipeline solves: templated, tokenized components eliminate re-keying and enforce the current brand asset, directly addressing why manual email production fails as Deciem scales.
