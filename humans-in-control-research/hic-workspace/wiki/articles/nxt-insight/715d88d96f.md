2026 crop insurance revenue guarantees are locked below break-even production costs

The USDA RMA finalized spring discovery crop insurance prices at $4.62/bu for corn and $11.09/bu for soybeans in March 2026, cementing revenue guarantees below national average break-even costs of ~$5.00/bu corn and $12.27/bu soybeans. Projected 2026 marketing average prices ($4.20/bu corn, $10.30/bu soybeans) sit even lower. This structural gap between insured revenue floors and cost of production directly threatens farm solvency and reshapes the underwriting margin an insurer can price against.
