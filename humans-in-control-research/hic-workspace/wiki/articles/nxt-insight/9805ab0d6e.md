Human approval flags gate agent actions before irreversible steps like sending execute

AWS requires a confidence threshold before any price-changing action, routing below-threshold actions to a human approver as an orchestration-layer gate, not an LLM judgment call. The deterministic clusters converge on requires_approval steps and approval flags so lifecycle agents cannot break attribution without human sign-off, and on approvals, audits, and guardrails as prerequisites for trusting agents in lifecycle marketing ops. For Deciem's send step, an explicit human-approval gate is the control that preserves creative and brand oversight.

Before executing any price-changing action, you want to require that the agent has a confidence threshold. Below that threshold, the action routes to a human approver. [articles/aws-retail-blog/259d295d8d.md#c3]
