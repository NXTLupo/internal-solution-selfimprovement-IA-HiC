Out-of-state lab and site-of-service rules trigger surprise out-of-network reprocessing

Hidden network rules generate surprise denials: a national BCBS PPO processed an in-network MA lab as out-of-network solely because the prescriber was in California, a rule 'NOT in the actual policy documents.' Hospital-owned PCPs route routine bloodwork to in-house labs at multiples of Quest's cash price. These coding/routing rules are deterministic logic AI could surface pre-service, and their opacity is a top consumer-cohort financial-harm driver.

if the lab and the prescriber are in different states, even if they are both in network with a national PPO, the lab claim will still be processed OON
