Offshore payer call centers fail on coding-literate queries, exposing the service-automation opportunity

Payer employees state offshore customer service is used because three to seven offshore staff cost what one US agent does, and that these centers lack coding skills needed for medical claims. Consumers report reps contradicting each other call-to-call and spending more on a three-hour dispute than the claim was worth. This high-volume, education-heavy, low-accuracy service load is the explicit target for AI agent automation in the payer cohort.

i do not agree with using offshore customer service for anything medical... when i explain why the claim denied it is clear they do not have great coding skills.
