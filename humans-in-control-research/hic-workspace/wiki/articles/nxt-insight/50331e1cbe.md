Typography precision separates a credible template from an 'uncanny' one

Venngage identifies typography as the fastest tell of low-quality design: inconsistent kerning, uneven baselines, shifting stroke weights, and disproportionate heading sizes create an 'uncanny valley' effect a Reddit discussion flagged. Percentage signs rendered differently across stats and cramped, hard-to-scan sections read as careless. For a skincare brand's email templates, disciplined, consistent type is not decoration but a baseline credibility requirement.
