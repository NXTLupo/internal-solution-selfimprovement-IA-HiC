Open-enrollment time pressure drives wrong plan selection under unclear options

Across multiple r/HealthInsurance threads, enrollees report selecting the wrong coverage tier because they faced compressed deadlines and indistinguishable options. A preschool teacher received paper forms a week before the deadline and lost two-thirds of a $40k paycheck to a misselected premium. Another poster scripted an HR escalation framing the choice as an administrative error. This evidences how short enrollment windows plus low-clarity option sets produce costly, regret-laden decisions, the adjacent decision-friction mechanic the objective targets in long enrollment cycles.

I saw four options in the application and just picked one. They all looked extremely similar in criteria listed so I couldn't tell which one was the cheapest premium.
