High-net-worth wealth concentration forces asset distribution executives to prioritize independent RIA channels

Capital control is shifting upmarket, with high-net-worth households possessing $5 million to $20 million now controlling 40% of addressable wealth in the United States. This represents a massive surge from 18% in 2013. In response to this expanding asset pool, 79% of distribution executives explicitly identify independent RIAs as their primary distribution channel target for the next 36 months.

79% of distribution executives identify independent RIAs as their primary distribution channel for the next three years.
