AI-driven discovery splits the website into an AI-readable feed and a high-intent conversion engine

Total Retail argues the website is bifurcating: product pages, reviews, and metadata become raw material AI systems trust and recommend, while human visitors arrive later with higher intent and less patience. Semrush adds that beauty is one of only a few industries whose organic traffic grew in 2025 despite AI search. For a 2026 skincare email program, this means templates must continue the AI-recommendation context, not restart the shopping journey.
