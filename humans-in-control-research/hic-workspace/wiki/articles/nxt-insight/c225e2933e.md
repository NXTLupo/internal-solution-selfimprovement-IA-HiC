A 40-year-stagnant H-2A visa program leaves farm workforce stability, and thus solvency, structurally exposed

Rep. Glenn Thompson's Securing Agriculture's Workforce Act of 2026 (H.R. 9535) would be the first statutory H-2A reform in 40 years, backed by AFBF, NMPF, NASDA and over 400 groups. AFBF reports only 182 domestic applications were submitted for nearly 415,000 advertised farm positions in 2025, and H-2A issuance grew 2766 percent between 1996 and 2024. NMPF states 'workforce stability underpins animal care, milk quality, and overall farm viability.' Labor fragility is a direct solvency risk: without a reliable workforce, insured operations cannot maintain the production that services their debt.
