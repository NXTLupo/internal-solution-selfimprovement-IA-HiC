Legacy Infrastructure Throttles High-Net-Worth Alternative Asset Growth

Wealth management firms face severe operational bottlenecks as clients demand private equity and credit. Advisor allocations to alternatives surged from $7.5 billion in 2022 to $12 billion in 2025. Despite this influx, only 50% of wealth firms possess third-party technology capable of overseeing illiquid assets. This infrastructure deficit traps firms in manual workflows that threaten high-net-worth market capture.

Only 50% of wealth management firms use third-party technology systems to oversee, report on, and automate illiquid alternative assets.
