Conversational AI releases require explicit scope boundaries to prevent deployment creep

Deploying student-facing AI companions into production environments requires hard technical and conversational guardrails. In Stage 3 deployment within the Northeastern Student Hub Co-op Dashboard, defect resolution is strictly limited to approved conversational foundations and guardrail definitions [articles/client-uploads/5b7f886799.md#c11]. Any expansion into new knowledge domains, personalized student data, or novel channel integrations is governed via formal Change Orders to maintain deployment schedules [articles/client-uploads/5b7f886799.md#c11]. This structural boundary reflects broader industry experience indicating that complex AI assistants cannot be built in a single unconstrained iteration [articles/marketing-ai-inst/3d133d608d.md#c1].

Defect resolution in Stage 3 covers defects against the approved conversational foundation and guardrail definitions; new knowledge domains, personalized student-data use cases, channel integrations, or feature requests will be handled through the Change Orders clause [articles/client-uploads/5b7f886799.md#c11]
