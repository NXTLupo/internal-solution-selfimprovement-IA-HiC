Patients are forced to become their own claims adjudicators, signaling unmet consumer navigation demand

Consumers express that no one should need to become an insurance specialist to get a bill paid, contrasting health insurance with auto and homeowner claims that are handled for them. One member with a decade of industry experience still spent three-plus hours fighting a routine urgent-care claim. This explicit demand for someone to navigate on the consumer's behalf is the adoption driver for AI benefits-navigation assistants in the consumer cohort.

When do we realize the NO one should have to become an insurance specialist to have insurance pay a bill!?!? ... What do we pay THEM FOR??
