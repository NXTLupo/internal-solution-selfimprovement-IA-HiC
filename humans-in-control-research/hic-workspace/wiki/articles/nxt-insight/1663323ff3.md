Machine-readable spec files, not mock-up decoding, drive accurate AI generation

Smashing Magazine and Nielsen Norman converge that design decisions should be treated as infrastructure written into text spec files the AI reads every time it generates, because text files are more cost-effective and accurate than making AI decode patterns from mock-ups. A DESIGN.md holds machine-readable exact values plus human-readable dos and donts, letting tools know exactly what a color is for and check against accessibility contrast. This is the brand-guidelines substrate an MCP configuration would expose to the email agents.
