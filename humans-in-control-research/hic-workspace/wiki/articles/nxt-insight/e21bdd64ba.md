Deep first-order discounts recruit worse subscribers than shallow ones

Recharge's same-store analysis of 350 CPG subscription stores found the discount sweet spot is at or below 25% off, where a subscriber is worth as much as a full-price one; subscribers acquired with 75%-plus discounts are worth $30-38 less over 12 months and renew 5-12 points worse. For a Gen Z organic skincare brand, this reframes email promotional design away from headline slashes toward shallow, brand-first offers embedded in the template.
