Gen Z trusts peer and comment-section recommendations over brand ads

YPulse data shows 81% of 13-17s and 73% of 18-24s agree a recommendation from a regular person is more authentic than an ad from a brand [articles/nxt-insight/4e3a846f93.md#c0]. 70% of teens and 75% of young adults say the comments section has the best recommendations, and 71% of 18-24s skip posts marked #ads [articles/nxt-insight/4e3a846f93.md#c0]. In Southeast Asia two-thirds of consumers, 67%, purchased because a creator recommended it, ahead of online reviews and brand claims [articles/retail-asia-mag/9360072180.md#c1]. Email should surface verified reviews and real customer language, not polished brand copy.
