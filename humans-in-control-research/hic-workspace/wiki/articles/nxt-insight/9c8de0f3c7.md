Provider-side billing and place-of-service coding errors trigger denials patients cannot fix

Members repeatedly learn that denials stem from how the provider coded the visit, not the care itself. An urgent care billed an ER place-of-service code, applying a deductible the member should not have owed; commenters note only a corrected resubmission from the clinic fixes it [social-intelligence/reddit-deep/healthinsurance/1ohh6i5.md#c8]. A newborn billing case turned on a Colorado birthday-rule mandate [articles/reddit-deep-r-healthinsurance/3071514321.md#c0]. AI coding-validation at the provider point of submission targets this upstream error source.

it is not uncommon for urgent care centers to code an urgent care visit as ER. that part is unfortunately, on them.
