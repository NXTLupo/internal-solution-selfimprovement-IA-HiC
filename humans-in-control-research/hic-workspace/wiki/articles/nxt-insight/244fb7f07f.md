Automated screening forces candidate optimization while undermining signal quality

Automated ATS screening drives candidates to paste job description keywords directly into resumes using AI tools [articles/reddit-deep-r-jobs/0a10b0683d.md#c0]. Applicants tailor materials specifically to clear AI filters, creating high volume but low qualitative signal [articles/reddit-deep-r-jobs/0a10b0683d.md#c0]. Tailoring software like Jobscan provides concrete keyword match scoring to bypass automated rejections [articles/reddit-deep-r-jobs/bf879e2104.md#c1].
