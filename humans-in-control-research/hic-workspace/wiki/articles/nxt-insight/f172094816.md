Technical Job Preparation Shifts Evaluation from Syntax to Live Reasoning Defense

Employers have restructured hiring assessments in response to ubiquitous AI usage among candidates. Over 25% of U.S. employers now explicitly allow AI tools in technical evaluation rounds, shifting focus from raw code generation to how candidates inspect, debug, and justify AI output. Meanwhile, proctored no-tools rounds emphasize fundamental logic over syntax recall. Job seekers report success using focused application systems and STAR story banks rather than generic auto-apply bots, proving that personal judgment and defense remain key differentiators in AI-mediated job markets.

The evaluation flips: interviewers assume you can get working code and watch how you check it, fix it, and explain it. [articles/kdnuggets/55625fbcf3.md#c2]
