Fractional CRM operators stall at generalist overload before reaching specialization

Solo CIOs and Salesforce architects seek paths out of generalist overload toward RevOps and Higher Ed Cloud specialization. This pattern is relevant to the shared-service-model dimension of the objective: it is the human counter-argument to centralized AI capability. Where individual operators drown in generalist work, a holding company's centralized AI shared service is precisely the structure that lets one specialized capability serve many portfolio companies instead of each hiring its own overloaded generalist.
