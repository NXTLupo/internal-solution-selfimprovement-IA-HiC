Retroactive claim reversals expose payer adjudication as a process AI could stabilize

United Healthcare retroactively denied an approved, paid surgical claim six months later via an outside auditor (EXL) that its own customer service had no knowledge of, with a stated seven-year lookback window. The member held the original processed EOB, but it had been removed from the portal. This pattern of opaque, after-the-fact reversal is precisely the adjudication consistency problem payers cite as an AI use case, and the same lack of internal data visibility that frustrates members.
