Technology capability deficits actively drive 29% of investors to switch financial advisors

Subpar digital experiences are causing measurable client attrition across the wealth management sector. Approximately 29% of investors explicitly cite technology gaps as their primary reason for seeking a new financial advisor. This shifts technological capability from an operational efficiency lever to a core client retention mechanism. Asset managers are forced to prioritize digital integration simply to maintain existing AUM, rather than just using tech for operational scaling.

Clients are actively demanding digital integration. Approximately 29% of investors state they expect to switch financial advisors
