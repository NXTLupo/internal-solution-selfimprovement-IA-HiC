Urgency and scarcity mechanics (countdown timers, deadlines) drive email checkout

The same email-marketing playbook builds conversion around FOMO: embedded countdown timers, clear start and end dates, low-stock language, and escalating reminders. For a Gen Z clean-beauty template, timers and deadline blocks are reusable modules, but the greenwashing sentiment elsewhere in the corpus cautions against overusing manufactured scarcity with a values-driven audience.

Include urgency with countdown timers (Sendtric makes it easy to embed timers) ... Use language that creates FOMO.
