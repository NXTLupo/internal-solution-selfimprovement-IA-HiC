Figma's design agent ships as open beta with code, plugins, and web-search on canvas

As of the Figma blog announcement, the Figma design agent entered open beta for Full seat users on Professional, Organization, and Enterprise plans, free during beta, with Collab, Dev, and View seats able to use it in drafts. The agent brings code, plugins, shaders, and external context onto the canvas rather than approximating from a flat image. It can search the web live for real data without leaving Figma, pulling current UX patterns and up-to-date content into mockups.

The Figma design agent is available in open beta for Full seat users on Professional, Organization, and Enterprise plans. Collab, Dev, and View seats can use the agent in their drafts.
