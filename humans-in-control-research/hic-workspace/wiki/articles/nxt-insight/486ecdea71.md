Claim adjudication errors persist because no system auto-reprocesses corrected coverage

Payer-side workers describe a structural automation gap: 'all the claims systems I've used don't automatically reprocess on their own. Someone has to realize there are claims that should be reprocessed and then actually do it.' Errors from retroactive coverage changes (newborn additions, deductible tier shifts) sit uncorrected for months or a year. This manual-reconciliation gap is a concrete, high-volume payer AI use case where the workflow failure is documented from inside the industry.
