Bulk create maps CSV fields to layers, the seam where personalization tokens enter

Figma Buzz's Bulk create generates many assets at once by importing content from a CSV or XLSX file, where each data field maps to a layer in the design [articles/figma-blog/0c58f20247.md#c1]. This requires separated, clearly named text layers, because combining multiple styles in one layer breaks formatting and makes updates harder [articles/figma-blog/0c58f20247.md#c1]. eBay's team pulls real-time component status from library metadata and lints and validates exports from Figma without touching a CMS manually [articles/figma-blog/fa5dca9c3a.md#c2]. This field-to-layer mapping is the natural integration point for personalization tokens in an automated email flow.
