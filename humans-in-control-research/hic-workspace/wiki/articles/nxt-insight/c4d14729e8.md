Semantic tokens in Figma are treated as the contract between designers, devs, and agents

A Brazilian designer building a DS for humans and AI keeps semantic tokens in Figma and in documentation, then uses MCP to have the AI generate components against a documented flow, splitting docs into 'spec' (intent) and 'stack' (how to code) with a 70/30 AI-to-human ratio [articles/reddit-deep-r-designsystems/c1b6016a0a.md#c0]. UXDSL frames the same idea: design intent trapped in Figma should compile to plain CSS via tokens [articles/reddit-deep-r-designsystems/4a66d4c76c.md#c0]. Semantic tokens are the design-system-integration substrate a Figma white paper must specify.

I have a structure of semantic tokens in Figma as well as in documentation form [articles/reddit-deep-r-designsystems/c1b6016a0a.md#c0]
