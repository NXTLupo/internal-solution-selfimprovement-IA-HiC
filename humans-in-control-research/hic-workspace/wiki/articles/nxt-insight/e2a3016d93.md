Agriculture is the hardest sector to price because risks are interconnected in data-poor environments

The UNDP deep-dive names identifying, quantifying, pricing and managing risk as one of the most challenging aspects of smallholder agrifinance. Agriculture spans diverse crops, geographies and production systems with distinct risk profiles, is exposed to market fluctuations beyond farmers' control, and sits in rural, data-poor environments where reliable information is scarce and costly. Because risks are interconnected, lenders cannot isolate individual drivers of loss, undermining accurate underwriting.

Quantifying risk in agriculture is inherently difficult because of diversity, volatility and limited data availability in the sector [articles/vertexaisearch-cloud-google-com/90018ada56.md#c4]
