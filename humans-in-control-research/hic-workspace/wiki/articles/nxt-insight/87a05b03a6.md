Backlash Against Mandatory Institution-Forced AI Destinations

Users resist locked platform models where enterprise software vendors or educational institutions mandate non-negotiable AI integrations without user opt-outs. Discontent arises when platforms force embedded AI search or closed assistants, creating demand for modular architectures that let users bring their own preferred AI tools.

Many will argue that Google should let schools access web search without chatbots. However, that’s just not something Google wants to support at any level
