The retained-search process breaks into eight discrete stages AI can target unevenly

Clockwork frames retained search as eight stages and notes practitioners are divided on whether AI can intelligently source and assess finalist candidates, while believing AI will have little impact on the final stages of a search and will supplement most marketing efforts. This staged view lets DSG sequence AI investment where feasibility is highest (sourcing, research, marketing, reporting) and preserve human judgment where the industry itself doubts AI (finalist assessment, close).
