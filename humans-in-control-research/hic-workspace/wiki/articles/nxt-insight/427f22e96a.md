Satellite, IoT and AI risk-modeling are pitched as the fix but remain adoption barriers for data-poor farms

Both source documents position remote sensing, IoT soil-moisture and crop-health sensors, blockchain, and AI-driven weather forecasting as the mechanism to improve risk assessment, reduce basis risk and price coverage more precisely. Private insurers such as PULA, ACRE Africa and Lemonade deploy blockchain and satellite data at scale. Yet these same technologies presuppose connectivity, data infrastructure and farmer digital literacy that the corpus repeatedly flags as scarce, making technological adoption itself a barrier rather than a given.
