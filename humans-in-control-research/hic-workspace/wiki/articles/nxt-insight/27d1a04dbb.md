Centralized agent instructions turn scattered brand and legal context into an enforced operating system

Webflow shipped Agent Instructions to give agents a unified source of context because for most teams that context is fragmented across multiple tools and resources. Teams point agents at the design system, CMS content, legal docs, and voice-and-tone guidelines, then define skills and rules for how tasks get done. One practitioner describes build-time rules becoming the site's own operating system, shipping the same voice, brand, and compliance guardrails through the MCP. This models how Deciem's brand guidelines should be codified as agent-consumable instructions.
