Excessive multi-round interview processes contradict employers' own talent-shortage claims

Veteran talent-acquisition practitioners argue firms that lament talent shortages sabotage themselves with excessive, poorly-run hiring processes such as six-round interviews (Cluster 6). This process-friction finding is an operational-efficiency signal: search cycle time is inflated by the client's own process, not just sourcing. It bounds where AI efficiency gains are realistic for DSG versus where the constraint is client behavior.
