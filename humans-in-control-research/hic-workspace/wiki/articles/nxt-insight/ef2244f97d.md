Skills work as an agentic workflow layer above lower-level SDKs, adding config and verification

NVIDIA's Jetson video skills illustrate the general skill-layer pattern the objective probes: an agentic workflow layer above the Video Codec SDK that discovers capabilities, generates recipes, benchmarks performance, and validates the workflow — returning configuration, results, warnings, and evidence required to reproduce the outcome. The skills add device-aware configuration and verification the SDK lacks, distinguishing advertised capabilities from operations that actually succeed. This validation-and-evidence discipline maps onto how Figma skills could wrap and verify design-system operations deterministically.

The SDKs provide accelerated video primitives; the skills add device-aware configuration and verification... preserve recipes, inputs, outputs, and measurements for reproducibility.
