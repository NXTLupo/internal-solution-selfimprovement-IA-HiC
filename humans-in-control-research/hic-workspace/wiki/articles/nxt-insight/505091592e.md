Plain-text post-purchase emails out-earn designed campaigns

A repeat email operator claims a plain-text gratitude email sent after purchase outperformed every designed campaign for the year across roughly 100 uses [articles/nxt-insight/76506f9808.md#c0]. This directly challenges the premise that a redesign must be visually elaborate. For a Gen Z clean-beauty brand it argues for a stripped, sincere post-purchase touch alongside the branded flow, though community reaction was split between genuine loyalty move and recycled advice.

I've sent this email nearly 100 times, and it almost always outperforms every email that was sent out during the ENTIRE YEAR.
