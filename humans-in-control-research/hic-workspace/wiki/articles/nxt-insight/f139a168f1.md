Creative fatigue erodes performance when brands reuse the same angles for a year

Ecommerce practitioners diagnose stalled revenue as creative fatigue, not spend, urging fresh hooks and new angles rather than more of the same. This maps onto template design: a Gen Z clean-beauty brand needs a modular system that lets it rotate angles and hero products fast, since a static template accelerates the very fatigue that flattens conversion.

creative fatigue is real if you've been running the same angles for a year. Not 'post more organic content,' more like fresh ad creative and new hooks.
