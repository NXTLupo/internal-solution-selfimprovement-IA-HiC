Herbicide-resistant super-weeds now defeat up to nine sites of action, forcing chemistry rotation as a solvency issue

Bayer's Convintro EPA approval targets Palmer amaranth and waterhemp, weeds that show resistance to up to 9 different herbicide sites of action. The Group 12 chemistry (diflufenican) is introduced specifically because resistant weeds have conquered existing modes of action. Escalating resistance threatens both productivity and long-term sustainability, per US Crop Protection Lead Kenin Eubank, raising the operational cost floor for row-crop growers.
