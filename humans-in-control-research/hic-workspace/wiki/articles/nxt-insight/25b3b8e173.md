Authentic product photography is the highest-leverage conversion asset for stores buyers cannot touch

Better Lemonade Stand argues product photography is by far one of the most important things for an ecommerce store because visitors cannot see, touch, or smell products in person, and honest imagery lets customers know what they are getting. Contrasted with AI-imagery backlash, this positions real, well-lit product shots as the conversion backbone of a Gen Z skincare email template's hero and grid.
