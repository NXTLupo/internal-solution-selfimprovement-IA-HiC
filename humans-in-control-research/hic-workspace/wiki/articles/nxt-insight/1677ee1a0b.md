Prior-authorization breaks at the point of care, exposing the workflow AI is pitched to automate

Consumers and providers describe prior authorization as a system that fails in real time: requests never submitted, denials issued weeks after emergency surgery, and peer-to-peer reviews providers refuse to conduct because they are time-consuming and usually unsuccessful. One member's $148,257 inpatient claim was denied via a preauthorization rejected a week after discharge. Real-time PA verification at point of service is limited, defining the provider-payer use case for AI auto-submission.

Real-time confirmation at the exact point of service is limited unless the provider's system can instantly verify or auto-submit with the payer.
