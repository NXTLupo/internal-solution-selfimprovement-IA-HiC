Transition from retainers to subscription waas

Squeezed agency operators abandon traditional hourly retainers for productized monthly subscriptions to preserve profit margins. These agencies reuse structural design platforms and standardized foundations across different accounts to scale delivery limits [articles/webflow-blog/3fd94dbdcc.md#c1]. This commercial shift allows boutique shops to serve smaller budgets while minimizing administrative overhead [articles/social-intelligence/reddit-deep/agency/1wznugn.md#c5].
