Disconnected Advancement and Student Resource Systems Cause Workflow Fragmentation

Northeastern University faces operational friction because guidance, data, and resources reside in multiple disparate platforms. Northeastern Information Technology Services engaged NXT Humans to consolidate readiness tools into a single student destination because existing resources are currently spread across multiple systems [articles/client-uploads/b8e5bd5ac5.md#c8]. In parallel advancement initiatives, initial prototype specifications mandate validating data schemas, extracts, and quality scorecards to overcome legacy data fragmentation prior to production architecture deployment [articles/client-uploads/0fec271931.md#c5].

Today, co-op resources and guidance are spread across multiple systems, and students lack a single student-facing destination for co-op readiness.
