An operational contract defining what an agent can and cannot do is the entry point to safe autonomy

Webflow's implementation guidance for agentic workflows starts with a one-page operational contract: broad directives like 'contact and nurture leads' plus explicit limitations like 'cannot delete or move customer data,' erring toward caution for sensitive information. This scoping-first pattern mirrors Figma leaders' call to define governance checkpoints early and VentureBeat's insistence on scoped authority per agent. For Figma-agent implementers, the operational contract is the deterministic boundary that precedes any skill, hook, or approval flow.

You’ll first need a one-page document: an operational contract that outlines what your AI agent will and won’t do... describe the limitations you want the agent to stay well within.
