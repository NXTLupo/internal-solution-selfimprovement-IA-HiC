Prior-authorization bottlenecks delay medically necessary care and medication

Multiple members describe prior-authorization and step-therapy loops that deny medication as 'not medically necessary' despite identical prior approvals, with one neurology patient cycling through repeated denials while running out of medication. Another commenter notes urgent PAs can be resubmitted for a 72-hour turnaround. This is the administrative decision workflow that clinical-AI and automated-PA vendors target, but the corpus documents only the friction, not any AI deployment against it.

this OTHER Anthem Plan somehow views the Same Patient with the Same Doctor and the Same Diagnosis needing the Same Medication as 'Not Medically Necessary'. (social-intelligence/reddit-deep/healthinsurance/1mfx7if.md#c1)
