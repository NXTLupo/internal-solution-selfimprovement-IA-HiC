Sharing rich project context to the agent shapes what it makes, not just how well it performs

Figma's design-agent guidance states good context does not just inform the agent, it shapes what it makes, and lets users attach files like a user interview, UX copy doc, or data report directly into the agent. Nielsen Norman echoes that a research finding steering generation toward denser or simpler screens is how context steers AI output. For Deciem this means brand guidelines, prior campaigns, and design patterns must be supplied as first-class agent context, not as after-the-fact review criteria.
