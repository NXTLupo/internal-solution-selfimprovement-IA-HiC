AI adoption in hiring is concentrating in evaluation, not candidate relationship-building

Resume Genius survey data shows resume screening rose to 46% in 2026 and is the top AI use case across every age group, while AI-driven candidate outreach and communication stayed flat at roughly 27%. Adoption is concentrating more heavily in evaluation than relationship building. This tells DSG where the industry is placing AI bets and where a differentiated relationship-led search firm could either follow the evaluation curve or protect the human outreach layer.
