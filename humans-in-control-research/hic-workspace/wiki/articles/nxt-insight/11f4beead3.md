Agents now act on six skills on average, up from two, extending to nine at peak demand

Salesforce reports the average agent can act on six skills, up from two at the beginning of 2025, and during peak retail season acts on nine, a 350 percent increase, taking on multistep needs and actions across different cloud domains. It ties this multi-skill, cross-domain capability to a headless architecture that decouples agent logic from front-end interfaces. This supports assigning each email-pipeline agent a bounded but growing skill set rather than one monolithic capability.
