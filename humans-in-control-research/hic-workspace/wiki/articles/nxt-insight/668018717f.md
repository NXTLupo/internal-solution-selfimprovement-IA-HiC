Unregulated quasi-insurance products leave members holding catastrophic bills

Health-share and broker-sold quasi-insurance products are misrepresented as comprehensive coverage and then deny responsibility. A ClearShare member found the product's own materials say it is not insurance and members remain personally responsible; Washington fined it and Oregon issued a cease-and-desist. A LifeX/BHPI member is staring at a >$27K bill questioning whether the product is legitimate insurance at all. This regulatory gray zone is the consumer-protection backdrop for any AI-driven plan-shopping tools.
