Regulated, complex industries deploy the most sophisticated agent networks, not the tech front-runners

Salesforce's Sophistication Index maps agent actions across five tiers and finds manufacturing, financial services, and healthcare build more advanced agent networks than technology and retail, with financial services representing 10% of total monthly agent output. This reframes readiness: sophisticated multi-agent deployment is proven in operationally complex, compliance-heavy settings, which supports Deciem building a compliance-gated multi-agent pipeline rather than treating it as bleeding-edge.
