Splitting the workflow into Planner, Analyst, Executor roles beats one omni-agent

A recurring role decomposition appears across posts: a Planner creates the step plan, an Analyst checks data and logic, and an Executor drafts changes but never publishes. Others warn against 'mixing multiple jobs into one agent (triage + copy + segmentation).' This is the direct evidence for Deciem's multi-agent role split rather than a monolithic email agent, improving debuggability and auditability.

Split the workflow into 3 roles: 1) Planner 2) Analyst 3) Executor (drafts changes, never publishes) [articles/reddit-deep-r-marketingautomation/b439817f55.md#c1]
