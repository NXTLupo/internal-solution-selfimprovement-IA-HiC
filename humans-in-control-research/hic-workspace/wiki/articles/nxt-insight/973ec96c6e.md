Design critique is a Figma-owned ritual agents have not yet absorbed

Figma's own design team publishes how it runs a design critique as a structured human ritual [articles/figma-blog/28b0812a51.md#c0]. No corpus evidence shows this critique practice encoded as an agent skill or critic, exposing the gap between Figma's human critique culture and its automated critics. Practitioners already use AI 'for generating naming ideas, documenting components, or suggesting edge cases I might miss' but do not trust it 'to create the system itself' [articles/reddit-deep-r-designsystems/68f8c75f24.md#c0]. The white paper's critics section can codify Figma's own critique method into an agent-runnable critic skill.
