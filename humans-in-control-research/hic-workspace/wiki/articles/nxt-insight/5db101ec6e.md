Critics should audit against user goals, not grade designs on a fixed rubric

A r/DesignSystems commenter draws a sharp distinction that governs any Figma critic implementation: a grade is a test with pass/fail benchmarks, while an audit 'simply reveals what's there and what's not,' leaving the participant to judge. The warning is that grading a design without knowing the user's specific goal is 'an overbearing assumption.' The tool author concedes the assessment is meant to surface ambiguity for an AI consumer, not label a system good or bad. This is the design principle the objective's critic use case must encode: audit, don't grade.
