Intelligence Augmentation is the deck's opening value proposition, not automation

The capabilities deck must open on Intelligence Augmentation as the differentiating line: the machine handles collection, synthesis, design, and quality control while the person sets direction and approves. This separates NXT Humans from unsupervised AI tooling and anchors the first positioning slide. Observation: the practice is framed entirely on IA. Implication: opening on automation would collapse the differentiation. Recommendation: lead slide one with the machine-does-labor / human-sets-direction division.
