El Nino drought impacts concentrate in low-coping-capacity regions, skewing risk to the upside

FAO analysis drawing on 41 years of satellite imagery finds more than 80 percent of drought impacts on agriculture are projected to hit low- and middle-income countries, with the most recent El Nino bringing Southern Africa's worst drought in a century, leaving 61 million people needing assistance [articles/agr-agribusiness-global/4d193293c1.md#c2]. Risks are now skewed to the upside as climate extremes collide with conflict and economic stress. This concentration of loss where coping capacity is thinnest raises correlated, hard-to-diversify underwriting exposure.
