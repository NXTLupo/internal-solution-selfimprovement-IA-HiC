Directory and system-of-record errors propagate downstream and land on the end user to fix

A repeated structural failure across the corpus is that authoritative directories and records are wrong, yet the burden of correction falls on the individual rather than the institution: a pregnant member called 20-plus 'in-network' providers from a 52-name directory and none accepted her plan, and members are advised to screenshot portals because directories aren't updated. For an enrollment-operations AI scaled across portfolio schools, the same lesson holds, automating on a stale system of record amplifies error at scale. Governance over data accuracy is the precondition for safe deployment.

They emailed me a 14-page list with 52 providers within 20 miles. I started calling down the list and got EIGHT (8) pages in. None accepted my specific marketplace plan.
