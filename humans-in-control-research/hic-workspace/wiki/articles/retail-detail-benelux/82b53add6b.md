# Kardashian’s Skims brings in design chief from Supreme

Source: [retail_detail_benelux](https://www.retaildetail.eu/news/fashion/kardashians-skims-brings-in-top-designer-from-supreme/)

Skims continues to professionalize, now with the appointment of two new chief executives. Kim Kardashian’s lingerie and fashion brand has promoted Kim Schraub to chief brand officer and brought on Erin Magee, formerly of streetwear brand Supreme, as chief design officer.
From the very beginning
Schraub has served as creative director for Skims since 2019 and was thus one of the label’s founding members. Previously, she held creative and design roles at Yeezy ( the shoe line of Kardashian’s ex, Kanye West), Victoria’s Secret, and Abercrombie & Fitch. In her new role, she will be responsible for how Skims presents itself in stores, campaigns, collaborations, and brand narratives—both commercially and creatively.
