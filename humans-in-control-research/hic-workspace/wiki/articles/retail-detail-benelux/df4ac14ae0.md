# KAAI opens pop-up store at Bijenkorf in Amsterdam

Source: [retail_detail_benelux](https://www.retaildetail.eu/news/fashion/kaai-is-getting-its-own-pop-up-store-at-the-bijenkorf-in-amsterdam/)

The Belgian handbag brand KAAI opens its own pop-up store today at de Bijenkorf in Amsterdam. The temporary store will remain open through 27 September and is part of the Antwerp-based brand’s international expansion.
International growth
KAAI has been selling its products through De Bijenkorf for some time, but this is the first time it has its own dedicated space at the Dam location. Visitors can explore the entire collection there, from business handbags to more recent styles. The Netherlands is the brand’s second-largest market after Belgium.
