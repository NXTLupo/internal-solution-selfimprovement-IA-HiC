# Pandora appoints new CEO for Southern Europe

Source: [retail_detail_benelux](https://www.retaildetail.eu/news/fashion/pandora-appoints-new-ceo-for-southern-europe/)

Pandora restructures its organization in Europe: the Danish jewelry chain has appointed Eric Lejeune as managing director for France, Spain, and Portugal. He is tasked with driving the brand’s continued growth.
“Unique position”
“Protecting, serving, and growing strong brands has always been my primary motivation. Pandora has built a unique position, firmly rooted in self-expression and sustainability, and has become a global leader in its category,” says the new executive. “I am extremely excited and honored to be part of the next phase of growth for this pioneering company.”
