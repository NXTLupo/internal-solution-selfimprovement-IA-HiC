# Bolia continues European expansion with entry into the British market

Source: [retail_detail_benelux](https://www.retaildetail.eu/news/home/bolia-continues-its-european-expansion-with-its-entry-into-the-british-market/)

The Danish design brand Bolia is launching on the British market with the opening of two flagship stores in prime locations in London. At the same time, the retailer is unveiling a new store concept, developed in collaboration with marketing expert Martin Lindstrøm.
New milestone
On September 9, Bolia will open a flagship store on Tottenham Court Road. The store spans 332 m² and is located at a prominent corner site with an elegant glass facade. A day later, the retailer will also open a store at Portman Square in the exclusive Marylebone neighborhood. That store spans 300 m² spread across two floors of a beautifully restored historic building.
