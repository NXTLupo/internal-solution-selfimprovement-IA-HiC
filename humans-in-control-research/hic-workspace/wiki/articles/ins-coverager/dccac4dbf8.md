# World Insurance Associates acquires ML Ruberton Agency and MLR Risk Management

Source: [ins_coverager](https://coverager.com/world-insurance-associates-acquires-ml-ruberton-agency-and-mlr-risk-management/)

World Insurance Associates acquires ML Ruberton Agency and MLR Risk Management
World Insurance Associates has acquired the business of ML Ruberton Agency and MLR Risk Management, both based in Hammonton, New Jersey. The transaction closed on March 1, 2026, and financial terms were not disclosed.
MLR provides personal and commercial property insurance, life and health insurance, as well as consulting services.
“MLR has a steadfast commitment to exceptional service, and a focus on building relationships, providing personalized protection solutions, and remaining actively engaged in the community,” said PJ Pullia, owner of MLR. “Customer service is at the heart of everything we do. It’s great to join World and expand the products and services we provide to our customers.”
“I’d like to extend a warm welcome to MLR as they join the World family,” said Rich Eknoian, executive chairman and founder of World. “MLR has a strong presence in the Hammonton community, and an experienced team. I know they will continue to grow as part of World.”
