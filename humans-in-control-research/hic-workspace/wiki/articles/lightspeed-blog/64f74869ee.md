# 8 Ways Lightspeed Can Help You Close The Season Strong

Source: [lightspeed_blog](https://www.lightspeedhq.com/blog/8-ways-lightspeed-can-help-you-close-the-season-strong/)

The 2026 golf season is shaping up to be another strong one, with National Golf Foundation (NGF) data showing U.S. play up about 4% compared to this time in 2025. But high demand across the industry doesn’t mean it’s time to coast. As peak season winds down, every unbooked tee time, missed F&B order or slow process is a missed opportunity to boost your bottom line.
Finishing the year strong comes down to three main priorities:
- Filling your tee sheet and eliminating gaps in play
- Increasing food and beverage revenue
- Protecting your margins
To help you hit these targets, our product teams have spent the past year building new tools designed to streamline your operations and elevate the golfer experience. Below, we outline eight features released over the last year—ranging from quick settings you can toggle on today to larger updates—that will help you maximize your end-of-season revenue and start 2027 ahead of the game.
Understand what’s working (and what’s not)
Which priority you should focus on first depends entirely on your course. Last year, we launched a brand-new Business Intelligence (BI) tool to simplify your reporting and help you spot new revenue opportunities. Since then, we’ve been continuously rolling out new reports—and there are plenty more on the way.
To figure out where to focus your efforts first, assess your:
- Tee sheet gaps: Check your rounds, occupancy and promotion dashboards to analyze total bookings and pinpoint which channels are performing best
- Food & beverage spend: Leverage our comprehensive restaurant reporting and insight tools to get a clear picture of your food and beverage performance
- Payments processes: Ask your staff and account team if your current payment solution is working for them. Since they handle transactions daily, they’ll quickly tell you if this needs immediate attention
To give courses total visibility, we also introduced consolidated sales reports this year, making it effortless to see your entire facility’s revenue in one place. Easy access to data is key, which is why we’re also excited about new developments in Lightspeed Restaurant (K-Series)—including powerful new AI tools designed to uncover actionable insights.
Spotlight: A new way to engage with F&B data via Lightspeed AI
We know that you and your team don’t always have time to dig through complex reports. Now, you can get answers directly from Lightspeed AI.
Embedded directly into Lightspeed Restaurant (K-Series), Lightspeed AI acts as your built-in, always-on data analyst. You’ll spend less time analyzing raw data and more time making smart business decisions. Just ask questions like:
- “How did sales look last week compared to this week?”
- “Which menu items are our top performers?”
- “Why did orders drop on Thursday?”
- “Can you analyze our staff productivity?”
The answers to your biggest operational questions are now right at your fingertips—all you have to do is ask.
1. Fill your tee sheet and eliminate gaps
Automate reminders to your golfers
No-shows leave costly gaps on your tee sheet and put revenue at risk. Our new Tee Time Reminders tool automatically alerts golfers about upcoming bookings, making it easy for them to confirm or modify reservations in just a few clicks.
- Timely alerts: Automated emails are sent 48 hours before play, helping to reduce no-shows
- Self-service editing: Golfers can confirm or update their bookings directly (where permitted)
- Visual clarity: Easily see which groups confirmed their attendance with clear confirmation indicators directly on your tee sheet
- Better staffing: Plan labor and operations around accurate, real-time occupancy data
Boost reservations with “Book on Google”
Google is the dominant search engine and the place for your course to be found. In June 2026, Google accounted for ~85% of the search market in the United States. Golfers rely on Google to find local courses and information about them. While this drives search traffic, it also means golfers have to leave Google and navigate over to your website to book.
Our seamless integration with Google eliminates that extra step:
- Streamlined booking: Add a direct “Book” button to your Google Business Profile so golfers can reserve times immediately
- Trackable growth: Monitor your Google bookings and evaluate performance inside our Business Intelligence tool
Pro Tip: With Lightspeed Restaurant, you can also link your restaurant reservations directly to your Google My Business profile. Consolidating your booking links on Google makes reserving a table or a tee time effortless for guests.
Keep your tee sheets clean with Block Notes
To keep tee sheets accurate, courses often create placeholder bookings to block off times for events or maintenance, which skews reporting data.
With Block Notes, you can hold tee times without corrupting your analytics:
- Accurate data: Block off course time while keeping your utilization metrics 100% accurate
- Clear staff communication: Add context directly to the block (e.g., Tournament, Private Event or Scheduled Maintenance) so your staff always knows what’s happening on the course
2. Increase food & beverage revenue
Raise average order value with Lightspeed Order Anywhere
F&B revenue isn’t limited to the clubhouse dining room. Order Anywhere allows golfers to order seamlessly from the course or the patio. We’ve added new features designed to drive higher spend per order:
- Smart upsells: Automatically suggest relevant add-ons during checkout such as high-margin snacks, side items or daily specials
- Targeted promotions: Launch percentage-based discounts or “Buy One, Get One” deals across your menu in a few clicks to boost direct ordering
These easy-to-use tools help you adapt fast if you want to launch a great deal and give a boost with same-day results.
Streamline table bookings with Lightspeed Reservations
Managing an on-premise restaurant comes with unique booking demands. Lightspeed Reservations connects every step of your dining service into one unified platform:
- Fast setup: Go live and accept online table reservations in minutes across your website, Google listing and social channels
- Centralized management: View and update bookings from a single dashboard or directly on your POS floor plan for smoother service
- Automated communication: Send instant confirmations and automated reminders to reduce dining no-shows
- Personalized service: Let guests add custom notes (e.g., dietary preferences, allergies or special celebrations) before they arrive
Setting up Lightspeed Reservations can be done in just minutes, so you can start accepting restaurant bookings right away. Simply add your restaurant reservations to your Google listing, website and other online channels and start filling seats.
3. Protect your margins
Offset fees with House & Family Account Surcharging
Members love the convenience of charging payments to their House or Family accounts using credit cards, but processing fees can quickly erode your margins.
Our new Automated Surcharging feature for Lightspeed Payments helps you protect your bottom line effortlessly:
- Automatic fee collection: Pass on credit card processing costs automatically without manually calculating rates or raising menu/membership prices
- Custom rates: Tailor your surcharge percentage, maxed at your payment processing rate
- Clean reporting: Surcharges are automatically tracked, ensuring your financial reports remain balanced and up to date
*Note: Credit card surcharging is subject to applicable laws, card network rules and processor requirements, and may not be available in all jurisdictions.
