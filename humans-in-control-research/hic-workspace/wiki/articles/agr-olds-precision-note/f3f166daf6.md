# MBody AI expands service robotics operations to eleven states and Canada

Source: [agr_olds_precision_note](https://www.therobotreport.com/mbody-ai-expands-service-robotics-operations-eleven-states-canada/)

MBody AI Corp., which is developing an operating system for enterprise service robots, is expanding its North American operations, said Check-Cap Ltd., which is planning to merge with the company.
“Reaching eleven states and entering Canada shows how the Orchestrator platform scales across new markets,” stated John Fowler, CEO of MBody AI. “The value of Orchestrator compounds with each deployment. As the fleet grows, we generate and assess more data each day.”
Founded in 2024, MBody AI said it is “building the autonomous workforce of the future” with embodied AI. The company‘s proprietary, hardware-agnostic Orchestrator platform delivers real-time data intelligence and AI-driven performance insights for robotic fleets.
MBody AI added that its capabilities include deployment optimization, predictive maintenance, and full-fleet coordination. The Las Vegas-based company said it serves blue-chip customers across multiple industry verticals.
MBody AI Orchestrator handles multi-robot fleets
The North American service robotics market is experiencing strong growth, reaching about $16 billion in 2026 and projected to grow to $29 billion by 2031, according to Mordor Intelligence. In Canada, the sector could approach $1.5 billion by 2029, said Statista.
The MBody AI Orchestrator is designed to coordinate and scale autonomous robotic operations across diverse environments. The company said it acts as a command layer, integrating AI and real-time data insights to manage multi-robot deployments.
Orchestrator enables customers to remotely monitor robotic fleets, assign tasks, and use AI to turn telemetry data into improved operational decision making. Its goal is to optimize the performance of robotic fleets, said MBody AI.
In March, MBody said it had expanded an autonomous floor care deployment across a Fortune 500 gaming and hospitality operator in properties across the U.S.
“We deploy. We do the work. We add value. The data improves operations and creates the justification to expand. This is our flywheel in action,” said Fowler at the time. “This operator expanded based on what they measured in their own properties — not what we projected.”
Check-Cap merger advances
Check-Cap is transforming itself from a clinical stage medical diagnostics company into an embodied AI company with its planned merger with MBody AI. Upon completion of the transaction, Check-Cap said it expects to become a publicly traded provider of enterprise-grade AI orchestration for robots across hospitality, gaming, commercial real estate, healthcare, and data center operations.
“MBody AI is developing as a critical robotic solutions provider for industries seeking to address labor shortages and enhance service commitments in North America,” said David Lontini, chairman and interim CEO of Check-Cap. “We remain highly focused on completing the transaction.”
MBody’s and Isfiya, Israel-based Check-Cap’s shareholders have approved the transaction. Nasdaq completed its initial review of the listing application in April.
