The Ultimate Liquid Fertilizer for Strip-Tillage: NACHURS K-Fuse®
Maximize your strip-tillage efficiency with NACHURS K-Fuse®, a premium liquid fertilizer engineered specifically for deep subsurface banding.
Striking the perfect balance for high-yield systems, K-Fuse blends seamlessly with liquid nitrogen (UAN) or ammonium polyphosphate (10-34-0), allowing growers to deliver a highly concentrated dual-placement band of potassium and sulfur in a single pass.
K-Fuse works exceptionally well for strip-tillage because it bypasses the "nutrient tie-up" common in broadcast applications, placing concentrated fertility directly where developing roots can intercept it.
Powered by advanced NACHURS Bio-K® technology (potassium acetate), K-Fuse features an ultra-low salt index and superior solubility. This ensures that when injected deep into the cool, high-residue soil of your strips, it safely accelerates early-season root elongation and improves nitrogen use efficiency without causing equipment clogging or root burn.

Upgrade to Germinator Steel Closing Wheels by Farm Shop Mfg®
The Germinator® Steel Closing Wheel is a game-changer for strip-tillage because it directly solves the method's biggest challenge: maintaining a perfectly sealed seed trench in highly variable soil conditions.
When planting into a freshly prepared strip-till zone, the soil can easily dry out, causing traditional rubber, smooth cast or traditionally spiked wheels to let the seed furrow crack back open. The Germinator’s heavy-duty, 50-grade steel design features a specialized "inner shoulder" that packs soil firmly right around the seed for maximum seed-to-soil contact. At the same time, its aggressive steel teeth fracture crusting and slice through heavy residue paths without plugging.
By eliminating air pockets and preventing trench re-opening, the Germinator ensures uniform moisture retention and consistent soil temperature. This results in fast, even seedling emergence across the entire field—protecting your yield potential in conservation tillage environments.
Kinze and Yetter Combine to Create The Falcon SF700
The Falcon SF700 combines the Kinze 5700 toolbar platform with Yetter 2984 Strip Freshener CC row units to create the ideal seedbed which maximizes planter performance. A true Kinze and Yetter partnership, the SF700 helps growers improve planter-to-strip alignment, manage residue, condition the seedbed, and easily place nutrients in a single pass. Available in 20", 22", and 30" row spacings and on 40' and 60' toolbar configurations, the SF700 leverages the Kinze 5700’s durable frame, weight transfer system, and terrain-following design, while the Yetter 2984 Strip Freshener CC delivers cab-controlled adjustments, excellent soil conditioning, and high-speed, low horsepower strip-till performance.

Boost Productivity with the KUHN Krause VT-PRO Strip-Till Row Unit™
KUHN Krause introduces the VT-PRO™ strip-till row unit for Gladiator® toolbars, engineered to deliver a high-quality seedbed with ultimate speed and precision. Operating at 7–10 mph, the VT-PRO creates wide, uniform strips while precisely placing fertilizer at 2–5 inches to promote early root development.
Equipped with an intuitive touchscreen display, operators can make on-the-go adjustments to row cleaners and downforce, ensuring consistent strip formation across varying field conditions. The unit features maintenance-free sealed bearings, greaseless bushings, and tool-free depth adjustments. When paired with Liqui-PRO, it enhances nutrient management by applying liquid fertilizer at rates up to 80 GPM.
Designed for durability in challenging spring conditions, the VT-PRO requires just 12–17 HP per row. It is available for 30-inch Gladiator toolbars in 4-to-16-row 3-point models and 12-or-16-row pull-type configurations.
