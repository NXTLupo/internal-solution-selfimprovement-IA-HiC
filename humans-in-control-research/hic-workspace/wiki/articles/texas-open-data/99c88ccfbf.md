# Pharmacy Medical Billing Services (SV4) Header Information - Historical

Source: [texas_open_data](https://data.texas.gov/dataset/Pharmacy-Medical-Billing-Services-SV4-Header-Infor/jkpg-wdht)

The Texas Department of Insurance, Division of Workers’ Compensation (DWC) maintains a database of pharmacy medical billing services (SV4). It contains charges, payments, and prescriptions billed on a DWC Form-066, Statement of Pharmacy Services by pharmacies with dates of service more than five years old going back to 2010.
