# Digital Transformation Evidence

This page will be populated as source documents are ingested into the research
workspace. It serves as the central evidence repository for claims related to
digital transformation: success/failure rates, adoption patterns, change
management effectiveness, technology ROI, and organizational factors that
predict transformation outcomes.

Currently, the benchmark data in [[benchmarks/technology-adoption]] is marked
HYPOTHESIS. As primary sources (McKinsey transformation studies, Gartner
surveys, Deloitte/MIT digital maturity research, client case studies) are
ingested, verified evidence entries will be added here and the corresponding
benchmark entries will be upgraded to PARTIALLY EVIDENCED or EVIDENCED.

---

## Evidence Entry Format

Each evidence entry in this page should follow this structure:

```markdown
### [Claim/Finding]
- **Data**: [Specific number, percentage, or finding]
- **Source**: [Document name], [section/page], [date]
- **Confidence**: EVIDENCED | PARTIALLY EVIDENCED | HYPOTHESIS
- **Cross-refs**: [[related-wiki-page]], [[another-page]]
- **Last verified**: YYYY-MM-DD
```

Confidence definitions:
- **EVIDENCED**: Supported by 2+ convergent sources with consistent findings
- **PARTIALLY EVIDENCED**: Supported by 1 source or by indirect/analogous evidence
- **HYPOTHESIS**: No source evidence; flagged for validation

## Pending Evidence Categories

The following categories are awaiting source ingestion:

1. **Transformation success/failure rates** -- The widely cited 70% failure
   rate (McKinsey) needs primary source verification and contextual nuance

2. **Technology adoption curves** -- MarTech utilization (Gartner 33%), AI
   adoption rates, CDP penetration
3. **Change management effectiveness** -- Which change management approaches
   correlate with successful transformation?
4. **Culture as predictor** -- Evidence for culture being the primary barrier
   vs. technology, budget, or talent
5. **ROI realization timelines** -- How long do transformations take to deliver
   measurable financial returns?
6. **Cloud migration outcomes** -- Cost overruns, timeline slippage, and
   realized vs. projected benefits
7. **AI scaling barriers** -- Why do 85-90% of AI pilots fail to reach
   production scale?
