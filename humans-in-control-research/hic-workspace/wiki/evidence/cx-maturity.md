# CX Maturity Evidence

This page will be populated as source documents are ingested into the research
workspace. It serves as the central evidence repository for claims related to
CX maturity: organizational readiness, maturity assessment findings, progression
patterns, and maturity-to-outcome correlations.

Currently, the benchmark data in [[benchmarks/maturity-models]] is marked
HYPOTHESIS. As primary sources (Gartner reports, Forrester CX Index data,
client assessments) are ingested, verified evidence entries will be added here
and the corresponding benchmark entries will be upgraded to PARTIALLY EVIDENCED
or EVIDENCED.

---

## Evidence Entry Format

Each evidence entry in this page should follow this structure:

```markdown
### [Claim/Finding]
- **Data**: [Specific number, percentage, or finding]
- **Source**: [Document name], [section/page], [date]
- **Confidence**: EVIDENCED | PARTIALLY EVIDENCED | HYPOTHESIS
- **Cross-refs**: [[related-wiki-page]], [[another-page]]
- **Last verified**: YYYY-MM-DD
```

Confidence definitions:
- **EVIDENCED**: Supported by 2+ convergent sources with consistent findings
- **PARTIALLY EVIDENCED**: Supported by 1 source or by indirect/analogous evidence
- **HYPOTHESIS**: No source evidence; flagged for validation

## Pending Evidence Categories

The following categories are awaiting source ingestion:

1. **Maturity distribution data** -- What percentage of organizations fall at
   each maturity level? (Gartner 5-stage, Forrester 4-stage)
2. **Progression benchmarks** -- How long does it take to advance one level?
   Where do organizations stall?
3. **Maturity-to-revenue correlation** -- What is the measurable revenue
   difference between maturity levels?
4. **Governance structures** -- What organizational structures correlate with
   higher maturity?
5. **VoC program maturity** -- How do listening programs evolve across maturity
   stages?
6. **Employee experience correlation** -- How does CX maturity correlate with
   employee engagement and retention?
