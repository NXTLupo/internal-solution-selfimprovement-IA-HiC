## What this exemplar teaches

Niva is a conversational AI assistant for households. Its design solves four problems that recur in every HiC AI prototype brief: (1) **multi-identity context** — the assistant must reason for Mom, Dad, Kira, and the dog without making the user re-state who they are; (2) **gentle reasoning surfaces** — confidence and rationale appear as soft inline cards, not as modal popovers; (3) **schedule-as-object** — calendar items are rendered as composable cards the user can drag, defer, or hand off; (4) **assistant-as-presence** — the orb breathes, listens, and acknowledges without consuming screen real estate.

The interaction primitives are the lesson, not the visual style. Any HiC prototype that needs an AI to negotiate across multiple users and surface its reasoning gently should study Niva's information architecture, not its color palette.

## Verbalized framings — how #NXT extends to this pattern

Five candidate translations of Niva's interaction language into the NXT Humans design system. Probabilities indicate typicality — lower-p framings escape the modal "soft AI assistant" register.

### α · Typography-led extension (p = 0.32)

Niva relies on a rounded humanist sans and ambient illustration to feel warm. NXT replaces the soft type stack with **Bebas Neue ALL-CAPS** for action labels (CHECK SCHEDULE, ADD TO FAMILY ROUTINE), **Archivo 400** for body copy, and **JetBrains Mono 500** for the assistant's structured output — times, names, calendar slots. The orb is preserved but its label shifts from sentence case to tight 12px / 4px-tracking ALL-CAPS subhead. Visual density doubles through type hierarchy alone. The result reads less like a friend and more like a quiet operator who knows your calendar better than you do.

### β · Motion-led extension (p = 0.24)

Niva's identity lives in the breathing orb. NXT keeps the breathing but halves its amplitude and forbids the chromatic gradient. The orb pulses between #0D0D0D and #1A1A1A with a **1px chartreuse (#B8B800) ring** that appears only when the assistant is actively listening. Response messages cascade in via 80ms stagger, never longer. Spinners are banned — replaced by a thin chartreuse progress line at the top of the active reasoning card. Cyan (#00B7C3) flashes briefly when the assistant cross-references another family member's schedule. Every motion event has a reason.

### γ · Density-led extension (p = 0.18)

Niva's mobile layout uses 16px corner radii and generous whitespace. NXT inverts this: **drop radius to 2px**, cut card padding by 40%, pack three context cards into the screen Niva uses for one. The visual rhythm becomes editorial — front page of a Saturday FT print weekend. Family member names become **JetBrains Mono 11px labels**, not heroes — the data they generate is the hero. The orb shrinks to 32×32px and moves to the top-left, behaving like a status indicator rather than a character.

### δ · Color-restraint extension (p = 0.16)

Niva uses warm pastels (peach, sage, dusty blue) to signal approachability. NXT renders the same screen in **95% achromatic plus one accent**: pure black on near-white (#F5F5F0), with chartreuse appearing ONLY on the active assistant state, rust (#CC6644) ONLY on schedule conflicts, and cyan (#00B7C3) ONLY on cross-references to shared resources. Teal (#115054) becomes the family/household identity color. This forces the assistant's intelligence to become the visual centerpiece — not the chrome around it.

### ε · Radical-departure extension (p = 0.10)

NXT subverts the assistant-as-orb metaphor entirely. The assistant has **no avatar, no chrome, no name**. It is text on a black page in JetBrains Mono 14px with a single thin chartreuse cursor that moves as it works. Family member context appears as **inline footnotes beneath relevant lines**, not as side panels. The whole interface looks like a terminal that happens to be helping a family — confident, intellectual, irreverent. Niva's warmth is reproduced through clever copy and verbal restraint, not through soft colors and breathing shapes. This is the framing that would make Linear's design team smile.

## Verbalized framings — how #NU extends to this pattern

Five candidate translations into the Northeastern University design system. NU is institutional: near-black (#000000 to #1B1B1B), Huskies red (#C8102E), Mercury Sans / Inter / Source Sans editorial type, conservative chrome.

### α · Editorial-institutional extension (p = 0.34)

Niva's family identity strip becomes a **cohort identity strip** — Mom/Dad/Kira translate to Mentor, Advisor, Cohort 2028, Co-op Sponsor. The assistant is named generically ("Husky Assistant" or simply "Compass") and presented in **Mercury Sans Bold 18px** with a thin red 2px underline. Schedule cards inherit a serif headline style for course names ("CS 2510 · Fundamentals of Computer Science II"). The orb is replaced with a small **N monogram** in red that pulses on activity. Reads as Northeastern's voice — measured, credentialed, advisorial — not as a consumer product.

### β · Motion-led NU extension (p = 0.24)

Motion is austere. The N monogram fills with red on listen, drains to outline on idle. No bouncy springs. **120ms linear easing** across every state transition (NU brand convention). When the assistant retrieves a recommendation, the red accent strip slides in from the left over 200ms — confident, deliberate, librarian-paced. Co-op match cards flip with a Y-axis rotation at 240ms when the user requests "show me why this match." No glow, no shimmer, no gradient.

### γ · Density-led NU extension (p = 0.18)

NU's institutional voice rewards density. Niva's three-card-per-screen budget expands to **six**, each card scoped to a single sentence + one data row. The advisor view collapses to a left rail with red dots indicating new vs. read items — pure information architecture, zero ornament. Coursework, co-op opportunities, cohort events, faculty office hours stack in a single vertical stream sorted by relevance score. The assistant says less and shows more.

### δ · Color-restraint NU extension (p = 0.14)

Even within NU's already-restrained palette, this framing tightens further: **black, white, red, and one neutral gray (#525252)**. No teal, no secondary accents. Red is reserved exclusively for: (1) active assistant state, (2) deadline urgency on a course or co-op application, (3) cohort identity badges. Every other state is grayscale. The intelligence has nowhere to hide behind chrome — the recommendation must carry itself on the strength of its reasoning.

### ε · Civic-archive radical extension (p = 0.10)

NU has a 125-year history as an archive of co-op pedagogy. This framing leans into that: the assistant interface is rendered in the typographic register of **Northeastern University Library catalog cards**. Each recommendation appears as a stamped index card with serial number, faculty endorsement line, retrieval date. Animations are quiet — a thin red line scribes under a recommendation as the assistant "writes" it. The metaphor is **research librarian, not chatbot**. Reads as scholarship-grade — the polar opposite of a SaaS Copilot panel.

## How to use this exemplar in a brief

When a HiC prototype brief includes any of:
- `interaction_pattern: "multi-identity-reasoning"` or `"household-coordination"` or `"schedule-negotiation"`
- `aesthetic_keywords` including warmth, family, household, cohort, multi-user
- `committed_frame: radical` with NXT brand → load framing ε (radical-departure)
- `committed_frame: convention-honoring` with NXT brand → load framing α or δ
- NU brand engagement (Khoury, College of Arts and Media, Mills, co-op platform) → load NU α or γ

The retrieval contract: `brief.visual_direction.exemplar_refs` should cite this exemplar by `exemplar_id`, the chosen framing letter, and a one-sentence rationale (e.g., `"behance-241915081 / NXT-ε — terminal-grade assistant restraint matches the dean-of-students authority register"`).
