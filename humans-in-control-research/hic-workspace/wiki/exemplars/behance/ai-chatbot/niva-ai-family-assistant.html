How #NXT extends this pattern

α TYPOGRAPHY-LED p = 0.32

Replace soft humanist sans with Bebas Neue ALL-CAPS action labels, Archivo body, JetBrains Mono for assistant data. The orb stays; the label below it goes from sentence case to a tight 12px tracking-4px subhead. Visual density doubles via type hierarchy alone.

β MOTION-LED p = 0.24

Keep the breathing orb but halve its amplitude. No chromatic gradient — pulses between black and near-black with a 1px chartreuse ring on listen. Responses cascade in via 80ms stagger. Spinners are banned, replaced by a thin progress line on the active card.

γ DENSITY-LED p = 0.18

Drop card radius to 2px. Cut padding by 40%. Pack three context cards into the screen Niva uses for one. Family member names become JetBrains Mono 11px labels, never heroes. The orb shrinks to 12×12px and behaves like a status indicator — front-page-of-the-FT rhythm.

δ COLOR-RESTRAINT p = 0.16

95% achromatic plus one accent. Accent appears ONLY on the active assistant state, rust ONLY on conflicts, teal as family identity color. No warm pastels. The intelligence becomes the visual centerpiece — chrome cannot carry weight it shouldn't be carrying.

ε RADICAL · TERMINAL p = 0.10

Subvert the orb metaphor entirely. No avatar, no chrome, no name. The assistant is JetBrains Mono on black with a single chartreuse cursor. Family members appear as inline footnotes beneath relevant lines, not as side panels. Warmth comes from clever copy and verbal restraint — not from soft shapes.
