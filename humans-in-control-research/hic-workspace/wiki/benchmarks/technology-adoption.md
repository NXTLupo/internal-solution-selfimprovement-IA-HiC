# Technology Adoption Benchmarks

This page collects quantitative benchmarks for technology adoption across
MarTech, AI, CRM, and digital transformation initiatives. All data points
originate from AI training knowledge and have NOT been verified against primary
sources. Each entry is marked HYPOTHESIS until a corresponding source document
is ingested and cross-referenced.

## Validation Notes

All 20 data points in this file are marked HYPOTHESIS. They represent commonly
cited industry benchmarks drawn from AI training knowledge.

**Priority validation targets**: The Gartner 33% MarTech utilization figure and
the McKinsey 70% digital transformation failure rate are the two most heavily
cited statistics in this domain. Both have been challenged in recent years and
should be verified against the most current editions of their respective reports.
The McKinsey 70% figure in particular has been attributed to multiple different
original sources and may represent a composite rather than a single study finding.
