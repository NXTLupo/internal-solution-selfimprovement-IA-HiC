# Financial Impact Benchmarks

initiatives to financial outcomes: ROI, cost avoidance, revenue uplift, and
loss from poor CX. All data points originate from AI training knowledge and have
NOT been verified against primary sources. Each entry is marked HYPOTHESIS until
a corresponding source document is ingested and cross-referenced.

## Validation Notes

All 15 data points in this file are marked HYPOTHESIS. Financial impact data is
the most scrutinized category in consulting deliverables because it directly
informs business case construction.

**Priority validation targets**:
1. The Bain 5-25x acquisition-vs-retention ratio is the single most cited CX
   statistic and has been challenged as overly broad. Verify the original study
   parameters and any industry-specific refinements.
2. The Qualtrics $3.7T cost-of-bad-CX figure is recent (2024) and should be
   traceable to a specific methodology and sample.
3. The Forrester $3-$5 CX ROI ratio needs the specific study, sample size, and
   industries covered to be credible in a business case.

**Analytical caution**: Many of these figures originate from vendor-sponsored
research (Qualtrics, Salesforce, Gainsight). While directionally sound, they may
carry selection bias. When possible, triangulate vendor figures against
independent academic or analyst research for EVIDENCED confidence.
