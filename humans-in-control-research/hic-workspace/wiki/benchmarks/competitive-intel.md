# Competitive Intelligence Benchmarks

This page collects quantitative benchmarks for competitive intelligence (CI)
programs: adoption rates, effectiveness metrics, win/loss analysis, and
intelligence-to-action conversion. All data points originate from AI training
knowledge and have NOT been verified against primary sources. Each entry is
marked HYPOTHESIS until a corresponding source document is ingested and
cross-referenced.

---

## CI Program Adoption & Maturity

### Formal CI Program Adoption Rate
- **Data**: 56% of B2B organizations report having a dedicated competitive intelligence function; however, only 24% rate their CI program as "mature" (systematic collection, regular analysis, integrated dissemination)
- **Source**: Crayon, State of Competitive Intelligence Report, 2024
- **Confidence**: HYPOTHESIS - verify with current data
- **Cross-refs**: [[benchmarks/maturity-models]], [[frameworks/strategy]]
- **Last verified**: 2026-04-10

### CI Budget as Percentage of Revenue
- **Data**: Organizations with formal CI programs allocate 0.1-0.5% of revenue to competitive intelligence activities; market research budgets (broader category) average 0.5-1.5%
- **Source**: SCIP (Strategic and Competitive Intelligence Professionals), CI Benchmarking Survey, 2023
- **Confidence**: HYPOTHESIS - verify with current data
- **Cross-refs**: [[benchmarks/financial-impact]], [[frameworks/gap-analysis]]
- **Last verified**: 2026-04-10

## Win/Loss Analysis

### Win/Loss Analysis Adoption
- **Data**: 71% of B2B organizations conduct some form of win/loss analysis, but only 30% do so systematically (on every deal or a statistically valid sample)
- **Source**: Anova Consulting Group, Win/Loss Survey, 2023
- **Confidence**: HYPOTHESIS - verify with current data
- **Cross-refs**: [[benchmarks/financial-impact]], [[frameworks/gap-analysis]]
- **Last verified**: 2026-04-10

### Win Rate Improvement from Formal Win/Loss Programs
- **Data**: Organizations with formal, third-party win/loss programs report 15-30% improvement in competitive win rates within 12-18 months of implementation
- **Source**: Clozd / Primary Intelligence, Win/Loss Effectiveness Report, 2024
- **Confidence**: HYPOTHESIS - verify with current data
- **Cross-refs**: [[benchmarks/financial-impact]], [[evidence/cx-maturity]]
- **Last verified**: 2026-04-10

### Top Reasons for Competitive Losses
- **Data**: Most commonly cited reasons for competitive losses: product capability gaps (34%), price/value perception (28%), relationship/trust (19%), implementation/support concerns (12%), other (7%)
- **Source**: Primary Intelligence, Win/Loss Trends Report, 2024
- **Confidence**: HYPOTHESIS - verify with current data
- **Cross-refs**: [[benchmarks/cx-metrics]], [[frameworks/strategy]]
- **Last verified**: 2026-04-10

## Validation Notes

All 10 data points in this file are marked HYPOTHESIS. Competitive intelligence
benchmarks are among the hardest to verify because the CI profession is
relatively niche, and much of the benchmarking data comes from CI tool vendors
(Crayon, Klue, Clozd) with inherent commercial interest.

**Priority validation targets**:
1. The Crayon State of CI Report is published annually and is the most
   comprehensive publicly available benchmark. Verify the 2024 edition for
   current figures.
2. Win/loss improvement claims (15-30%) come primarily from win/loss service
   providers. Cross-reference with independent academic research or SCIP
   publications.
3. ESOMAR global market research spend figures are the gold standard for
   market research investment data and should be verifiable.

**Analytical caution**: The CI field lacks the depth of independent benchmarking
found in CX or digital transformation. Many figures come from small sample
surveys (200-500 respondents) with self-selection bias. Treat these as
directional indicators rather than precise measurements until validated.
