## Concept
=== APPROVED PRODUCT BRIEF (the user picked this from a 5-brief set; build EXACTLY this) ===

Frame: convention-honoring (probability 0.4)
Title: PortfolioPulse
Thesis: A unified console where gift officers see AI-prioritized donor portfolios, next-best actions, and draft outreach in one daily workspace.
Why this frame: This is the schema-congruent advancement CRM-overlay every competent designer would build: a prospect-management cockpit augmented with AI scoring and drafting. It's the safest, most defensible read of 'engagement console.'

PRIMARY PERSONA:
  Name: Marcus Bellamy
  Role: Major Gifts Officer, Northeastern University Advancement (Northeast region)
  Context: Manages a portfolio of ~150 prospects across Boston, New York, and the broader Northeast corridor. Splits time between travel, donor meetings, and grinding through CRM hygiene. His annual metric is $4.2M raised plus a visit/qualification quota.
  Emotional state: Capable but perpetually behind—overwhelmed by a stale CRM, second-guessing who deserves his next hour, anxious that a warm prospect went cold while he chased a colder one.
  Pain points:
    - Salesforce/Blackbaud is a data graveyard—knowing who to call today requires manual scanning across dozens of records
    - Wealth events, gift anniversaries, and engagement signals surface late or never, so he misses windows of warmth
    - Personalizing outreach at scale means he either sends generic templates or spends 40 minutes per donor crafting one note
    - Meeting prep means reconstructing a relationship from scattered notes, gift history, and LinkedIn the night before a flight
    - He can't see whether his portfolio pacing is on track for his goal until quarterly reviews, when it's too late to course-correct
  JTBD: When I start my day, I want to instantly know which 5-7 prospects deserve my attention now and have outreach ready to go, so I can spend my energy on relationships instead of triage.
SECONDARY ACTORS:
  - Director of Development: Reviews team pacing dashboards, reallocates portfolios, coaches officers on stalled prospects, signs off on solicitation strategy
  - Prospect Research Analyst: Validates AI wealth signals, enriches Donor 360 profiles, flags capacity ratings and philanthropic interests
  - Annual Giving / Leadership Annual Giving Officer: Hands off upgraded donors to major gifts when propensity crosses a threshold; receives downgraded prospects back
  - Advancement Operations / CRM Admin: Manages data sync, field mappings, gift coding integrity, and compliance with donor data governance
  - Dean / Faculty Partner: Brought into cultivation as a 'closer' for principal gifts; needs concise briefing packets before donor interactions

DEMONSTRATED MOMENT: Monday 8:15am portfolio review—Marcus opens PortfolioPulse to find his 150 prospects already ranked by today's opportunity score, the top three surfaced with the reason they rose (a wealth event, a gift anniversary, a re-engaged email), and three tailored outreach drafts waiting in his voice. He edits one, sends two, and books a meeting—all before his coffee cools.

USE CASES (11) - the prototype MUST demonstrate at least 5 of these as live flows:
  1. Morning portfolio triage
     Trigger: Marcus opens the console at the start of his day and sees his full portfolio re-ranked overnight by an opportunity score.
     Flow: Console loads the Daily Prospect Queue → top 7 prospects sit above the fold with score, reason chips, and a one-line 'why now' → Marcus expands a card to see the signal stack → he accepts, snoozes, or reassigns each → accepted prospects flow into his action plan for the day.
     AI role: Re-scores every prospect overnight using engagement, wealth, recency, and pipeline-stage signals; generates the 'why now' rationale string in plain language.
     Outcome: Marcus goes from a cold, undifferentiated list of 150 to a prioritized call list of 7 in under three minutes, with confidence that nobody warm slipped through.
  2. AI propensity-to-give scoring
     Trigger: Any time a donor's record changes—new gift, event RSVP, email open, wealth signal—the propensity engine recalculates.
     Flow: Signal ingested → feature vector updated → propensity model scores 0-100 with capacity band → score delta surfaces on the Donor 360 with an arrow → if the delta crosses a threshold, a notification fires.
     AI role: Computes propensity-to-give and capacity-to-give as separate dimensions, explains the top three contributing factors, and flags confidence level.
     Outcome: Marcus trusts the ranking because every score is decomposed into human-readable drivers—he can see WHY someone is an 84 and not just THAT they are.
  3. Suggested next-best-action
     Trigger: Marcus clicks into a prospect, or accepts a queue card, and asks 'what should I do with them?'
     Flow: Console reads the prospect's stage, last touch, and signals → recommends a specific action (call, send brief, invite to event, request meeting) → shows expected impact and effort → Marcus accepts to auto-stage the action or requests an alternative.
     AI role: Reasons over pipeline stage and relationship history to recommend the single highest-leverage next move, with a fallback ranked list of three.
     Outcome: Marcus never stares at a record wondering what to do—he gets a confident, contextual recommendation he can act on or override in one click.
  4. Draft personalized donor outreach
     Trigger: Marcus accepts an outreach action, or clicks 'draft a note' from any prospect card.
     Flow: Console pulls relationship history, recent signals, and donor interests → drafts an email/letter in Marcus's established voice → renders side-by-side with the source facts it used → Marcus edits inline, regenerates a paragraph, or sends.
     AI role: Generates personalized, factually-grounded outreach matched to the donor's relationship stage and tone, citing the specific facts it referenced.
     Outcome: A 40-minute personalization task becomes a 3-minute review-and-send, with quality that feels handwritten because it references real shared history.
  5. Meeting prep brief generation
     Trigger: Marcus has a meeting on his calendar within 48 hours, or clicks 'prep me' on a prospect.
     Flow: Console assembles a one-page brief → relationship timeline, last interactions, giving history, capacity, talking points, and three suggested asks → Marcus reviews on mobile en route → can request a deeper dive on any section.
     AI role: Summarizes the entire relationship into a scannable brief and proposes conversation strategy and ask amounts grounded in capacity data.
     Outcome: Marcus walks into every meeting fully briefed in five minutes instead of reconstructing the relationship over an hour the night before.
  6. Pipeline stage progression tracking
     Trigger: Marcus logs an interaction or the AI detects a milestone (e.g., first qualifying visit completed).
     Flow: Interaction logged → AI suggests advancing the prospect to the next stage → Marcus confirms or holds → the kanban updates and pacing recalculates → stalled prospects (no movement in 60 days) get flagged.
     AI role: Detects stage-progression signals, suggests moves, and identifies stalls before they become silent losses.
     Outcome: Marcus and his director always see a true, current pipeline—no more inflated stages or zombie prospects clogging the funnel.
  7. Wealth-event alerting
     Trigger: A prospect experiences a liquidity event—IPO, company sale, board appointment, real estate transaction, public gift elsewhere.
     Flow: External wealth signal ingested → matched to a portfolio prospect → capacity band re-rated → an alert with the source and recommended timing surfaces in the queue → Marcus acts within the warmth window.
     AI role: Detects and disambiguates wealth events from noisy external data, scores their relevance, and recommends an outreach window.
     Outcome: Marcus catches the moment a prospect's capacity jumps and reaches out while it's top of mind—turning luck into a repeatable advantage.
  8. Quarterly goal pacing dashboard
     Trigger: Marcus opens the pacing view, or it's surfaced automatically at the start of a new month.
     Flow: Console aggregates committed, pledged, and projected gifts → compares against goal and time elapsed → shows ahead/behind with a projected year-end → highlights which prospects could close the gap → suggests reallocation.
     AI role: Projects year-end attainment from current pipeline velocity and recommends which prospects to accelerate to hit goal.
     Outcome: Marcus sees he's $600K behind pace in March instead of discovering it in June, with a concrete list of prospects who can close the gap.
  9. Re-engagement of lapsed prospects
     Trigger: A previously-active prospect goes dark for 90+ days, or a lapsed donor re-opens an email.
     Flow: Console detects dormancy or a re-engagement signal → surfaces the prospect with a 'reconnect' play → drafts a warm, low-pressure check-in → Marcus sends or schedules.
     AI role: Identifies cooling relationships and re-warming signals, and drafts non-transactional re-engagement outreach.
     Outcome: Marcus rescues relationships that would have quietly died, recovering pipeline he'd otherwise have written off.
  10. Activity logging via natural language
     Trigger: After a call or meeting, Marcus dictates or types a quick note.
     Flow: Marcus types 'Great call with Patricia, interested in scholarship endowment, follow up with naming options in 2 weeks' → AI structures it into a logged interaction, extracts the follow-up, sets a reminder, and tags the gift interest.
     AI role: Parses free-text notes into structured interaction records, extracts action items, and auto-schedules follow-ups.
     Outcome: CRM hygiene becomes a 15-second voice note instead of a dreaded end-of-day form—so the data actually stays current.
  11. Portfolio health review with director
     Trigger: Marcus's director opens the team view, or a 1:1 review is scheduled.
     Flow: Director sees portfolio coverage, stall counts, pacing, and AI-flagged risks → drills into Marcus's queue → leaves a coaching note on a specific prospect → Marcus sees the note in context.
     AI role: Surfaces portfolio-level risk patterns (over-concentration, stalls, ignored high-propensity prospects) for coaching conversations.
     Outcome: Reviews become forward-looking strategy sessions grounded in shared, current data instead of accusatory hygiene audits.

KEY SCREENS (10) - every screen is a real view, not a placeholder:
  1. Daily Prospect Queue - purpose: The home base—Marcus's prioritized list of who to engage today, with reasons and ready actions.
     Primary element: A vertically-stacked feed of prospect cards, top 7 above the fold, each showing opportunity score, reason chips, and a 'why now' line, with accept/snooze/draft controls.
     Motion character: Cards settle into rank order on load with a subtle staggered rise; accepting a card slides it gracefully into the day plan with a confirming check.
  2. Donor 360 Profile - purpose: The single source of truth for one relationship—history, giving, capacity, signals, and AI insights.
     Primary element: A three-column layout: relationship timeline center, giving/capacity dossier left, AI insight panel right with decomposed propensity score.
     Motion character: Score ring animates to its value on entry; timeline events fade in chronologically; the insight panel breathes with gentle live updates.
  3. AI Action Recommendations Panel - purpose: Shows the next-best-action for a prospect with rationale and alternatives.
     Primary element: A primary recommendation card with expected impact/effort meters and an expandable 'other options' list of three ranked alternatives.
     Motion character: The recommendation slides in with quiet authority; expanding alternatives unfolds smoothly; accepting pulses a confirmation.
  4. Outreach Composer - purpose: Where AI-drafted personalized outreach is reviewed, edited, and sent.
     Primary element: A split view: editable draft on the left, source-fact citations on the right showing what the draft referenced, with regenerate-paragraph controls.
     Motion character: Text types in with a streaming cadence on first draft; regenerated paragraphs cross-fade; citation chips highlight on hover over the related sentence.
  5. Meeting Prep Brief - purpose: A one-page, mobile-friendly briefing for an upcoming donor interaction.
     Primary element: A scannable brief: header with capacity/propensity, relationship summary, last-three-interactions, talking points, and three suggested asks with rationale.
     Motion character: Sections snap into place as a clean print-like document; suggested asks reveal their reasoning on tap with a smooth accordion.
  6. Pipeline Kanban - purpose: Visual stage management of the full portfolio funnel.
     Primary element: Horizontal columns (Identification → Qualification → Cultivation → Solicitation → Stewardship) with draggable prospect cards and per-column value totals.
     Motion character: Cards drag with a lift shadow and snap into columns; stalled cards carry a subtle amber pulse; column totals tick up on drop.
  7. Goal Pacing Dashboard - purpose: Real-time view of attainment against goal with projection and gap-closing prospects.
     Primary element: A pacing chart (committed/pledged/projected vs. goal-to-date line) above a 'close the gap' prospect strip.
     Motion character: The pacing line draws left-to-right on load; the projection cone shades in; gap-closing prospects flip up like cards being dealt.
  8. Activity Logging Timeline - purpose: Fast, natural-language capture and review of all prospect interactions.
     Primary element: A unified timeline with an always-ready quick-capture input that parses free text into structured entries.
     Motion character: Typed notes resolve into structured cards with a satisfying snap; extracted follow-ups bloom as task chips beneath.
  9. Team Portfolio Health View - purpose: The director's overview of team coverage, pacing, stalls, and risk patterns.
     Primary element: A grid of officer cards with coverage/pacing/stall metrics, expandable into individual queues with a coaching-note affordance.
     Motion character: Officer cards arrange in a calm grid; risk flags glow gently; drilling into an officer expands with a confident zoom.
  10. Wealth Signal Alert Stream - purpose: A dedicated feed of incoming wealth and engagement events matched to portfolio prospects.
     Primary element: A reverse-chronological alert feed, each item with the signal source, matched prospect, capacity delta, and a recommended action.
     Motion character: New alerts drop in from the top with a soft chime-equivalent visual; acting on one slides it to a resolved state.

AI CAPABILITIES (8) - every one wires to a real /api/ai/* endpoint via the multi-provider fallback helper:
  1. Opportunity scoring & overnight re-ranking (HEADLINE)
     User action: Marcus simply opens the console in the morning—no action required.

     Input: Each prospect's engagement events, gift recency/frequency/magnitude, pipeline stage, last-touch recency, wealth signals, and event/email interactions from the last 90 days.
     Output: A 0-100 opportunity score per prospect, a ranked queue, and a plain-language 'why now' rationale string naming the top driving signals.
     User benefit: Marcus's entire portfolio is triaged before he arrives—he starts the day knowing exactly who deserves his attention and why, with zero manual scanning.
  2. Propensity & capacity decomposition
     User action: Marcus views a Donor 360 profile or hovers the score on a queue card.
     Model: Gemini 3.1 Pro
     Input: Historical giving, wealth indicators, philanthropic interests, engagement trajectory, and peer benchmarks.
     Output: Separate propensity-to-give (0-100) and capacity band (e.g., $50K-$100K), each broken into the top three contributing factors with directional arrows.
     User benefit: Marcus trusts and can defend the ranking because every score is explainable down to its drivers—no black box.
  3. Next-best-action recommender
     User action: Marcus accepts a queue card or opens the recommendations panel on a prospect.
     Model: Gemini 3.1 Pro
     Input: Pipeline stage, last action and its outcome, days since last touch, active signals, and relationship temperature.
     Output: One primary recommended action with expected impact and effort meters, plus three ranked alternatives.
     User benefit: Marcus always knows the single highest-leverage move for any prospect, eliminating decision paralysis.
  4. Donor-voice outreach drafting
     User action: Marcus accepts an outreach action or clicks 'draft a note.'
     Model: Claude Opus 4.7
     Input: Relationship history, recent signals, donor interests and communication preferences, and a sample of Marcus's prior sent messages to learn his voice.
     Output: A personalized, factually-grounded email or letter draft in Marcus's tone, with inline citations to the source facts used.
     User benefit: Outreach that feels genuinely handwritten in a third of the time, because it references real shared history and sounds like him.
  5. Meeting-prep summarization
     User action: Marcus clicks 'prep me' on a prospect or has a meeting within 48 hours.
     Model: Claude Opus 4.7
     Input: Full interaction timeline, giving history, capacity rating, prior notes, and stated philanthropic interests.
     Output: A one-page brief: relationship summary, last-three interactions, talking points, and three suggested asks with rationale and amounts.
     User benefit: Marcus walks into every meeting fully prepared in five minutes instead of an hour of frantic reconstruction.
  6. Wealth-signal detection & matching
     User action: Passive—runs continuously; surfaces alerts when relevant.
     Model: Wiki RAG
     Input: External news, SEC filings, public records, and philanthropic disclosures, cross-referenced against portfolio prospect identities.
     Output: Disambiguated, relevance-scored wealth event alerts with source, capacity delta, and recommended outreach window.
     User benefit: Marcus catches liquidity moments while they're fresh, turning serendipity into a systematic advantage.
  7. Natural-language activity capture
     User action: Marcus types or dictates a quick note after an interaction.
     Model: Gemini 3.1 Pro
     Input: Free-text note plus prospect context.
     Output: A structured interaction record with extracted follow-up tasks, gift-interest tags, and an auto-scheduled reminder.
     User benefit: CRM hygiene drops from a dreaded form to a 15-second voice note, so data actually stays current.
  8. Pacing projection & gap-closing
     User action: Marcus opens the goal pacing dashboard.
     Model: Gemini 3.1 Pro
     Input: Committed/pledged/projected gifts, historical close rates by stage, time elapsed against goal, and pipeline velocity.
     Output: A projected year-end attainment figure plus a ranked list of prospects whose acceleration would close the gap.
     User benefit: Marcus spots a shortfall months early with a concrete action plan, instead of discovering it too late to fix.
