## Concept
=== APPROVED PRODUCT BRIEF (the user picked this from a 5-brief set; build EXACTLY this) ===

Frame: convention-honoring (probability 0.4)
Title: Ascend Funnel Console
Thesis: An executive-grade command console that reads a gift officer's weekend portfolio like a morning paper, routing every insight one click to the work.
Why this frame: This is the canonical advancement console the most competent fundraising-tech designer would build — digest, funnel, roster, profile, action queue — tightened so AI calls degrade gracefully and never break the experience.

PRIMARY PERSONA:
  Name: Marcus Chen
  Role: Major Gift Officer, Northeastern University Advancement
  Context: Manages a 150-prospect portfolio spanning $25K to $5M capacity across six funnel stages; reports to a Senior Director of Development; accountable to the Experiential Learning Initiative campaign goal.
  Emotional state: Monday-morning overwhelm laced with quiet dread — too many prospects, too little signal, fear that the one warm donor goes cold while he reads stale spreadsheets.
  Pain points:
    - Cannot tell at a glance which of 150 prospects moved over the weekend or why it matters
    - Spends the first 90 minutes of Monday reconstructing portfolio state from CRM exports instead of acting
    - Distrusts auto-suggested actions because data confidence is invisible — he doesn't know which records are safe to act on
    - Stalled prospects rot silently; by the time he notices a 96-day stall the relationship is cold
    - Ask amounts are guesswork — capacity says $2.5M but he has no defensible recommended ask tied to readiness
  JTBD: When I open Monday to a portfolio I can't fully hold in my head, help me find the three prospects that matter most and act on the first one before my coffee cools, without ever acting on data I can't trust.
SECONDARY ACTORS:
  - Senior Director of Development (Marcus's manager): Reviews campaign forecasts and pipeline health; receives handoff continuity briefs; arbitrates ask-amount disagreements.
  - Prospect Research Analyst: Receives 'route to research' requests for low-confidence fields; supplies verified capacity, affiliation, and wealth-screening data.
  - Annual Giving Officer: Originates handoffs of upgraded donors into Major Gifts; sends continuity context at the handoff boundary.
  - Gift Planning Officer: Receives planned-giving-ready prospects handed off from Major Gifts; reviews stewardship history before accepting.
  - Advancement Operations / Data Steward: Owns the audit log write-back integrity and the synced Ascend seed-data pipeline that AI flows fall back to.

DEMONSTRATED MOMENT: Monday 8am triage — Marcus opens the Digest, sees 'Three things that matter, out of 150,' the urgent Eleanor Whitfield follow-up is already drafted with a cleared confidence gate, and he sends and logs it with a single keystroke before reading the rest.

USE CASES (12) - the prototype MUST demonstrate at least 5 of these as live flows:
  1. Monday briefing triage
     Trigger: Marcus opens the console Monday at 8am; the Digest screen loads with the weekend's portfolio delta already computed.
     Flow: Console boots → weekly_digest_summary fires against weekend funnel movements, prospect count, and priority actions → briefing paragraph renders under the headline 'Three things that matter, out of 150' → three ranked priority actions appear as cards (Eleanor P95, Park P90, Okafor P88) → Marcus clicks the top action to jump straight into the work.
     AI role: weekly_digest_summary composes a calm executive paragraph, three focus items, and a single risk note from the weekend delta; if the call fails, synced Ascend counts and seeded priority actions render identically so the screen never looks broken.
     Outcome: Marcus has triaged a 150-prospect portfolio in under 60 seconds and knows exactly where to spend his first hour.
  2. Weekend funnel movement review
     Trigger: Marcus taps the Funnel tab to understand the directional health behind the digest headline.
     Flow: Funnel screen renders Advanced/Stalled/Regressed stat cards → six stage columns (Discovery through Renewal) show counts, total capacity, and net movement → Eleanor Whitfield's Cultivation→Solicitation jump is highlighted in red → Marcus hovers a stage card to see prospect chips and AI scoring provenance.
     AI role: conversion_likelihood_scoring annotates each moved prospect with a likelihood and a one-line reason; provenance label shows provider/model so Marcus trusts the ranking.
     Outcome: Marcus sees the portfolio as a directional flow, not a list, and confirms the gala-driven advancement that anchors his week.
  3. Re-score a segment by conversion likelihood
     Trigger: Marcus selects the Cultivation stage and wants the roster re-ranked by who's most likely to convert this quarter.
     Flow: Roster screen shows '[Cultivation] roster' → Marcus clicks 'Re-score segment' → conversion_likelihood_scoring runs across the segment → rows re-sort by likelihood with updated confidence badges and sparklines → movable-only filter narrows to actionable prospects.
     AI role: conversion_likelihood_scoring returns an array of {id, likelihood, reason}; on failure the seeded conversion_likelihood values render with a compact 'using synced Ascend data' note and a retry button.
     Outcome: Marcus has a defensible, model-backed priority order for an entire segment in one click.
  4. Confidence-gated bulk action
     Trigger: Marcus multi-selects eight movable prospects and wants to queue a batch of stewardship touches.
     Flow: Marcus checks rows → bulk panel partitions selection into 'safe to act' (record_confidence ≥ 70) and 'verify first' (< 70) → he reviews the partition → confirms the safe cohort to the action queue and routes the verify-first cohort to research.
     AI role: data_gap_inference and the seeded record_confidence drive the partition; AI never lets Marcus bulk-act on records below the 70 confidence threshold.
     Outcome: Marcus moves a batch forward without ever risking an action on untrustworthy data — the gate enforces discipline silently.
  5. Next-best-action draft and send
     Trigger: Marcus opens the top priority action — a follow-up to Eleanor Whitfield after the President's Gala.
     Flow: Action Queue shows 'What to do next' → animated priority count-up settles on the queue → next_best_action_engine drafts the follow-up artifact → Marcus reads 'why this / why now,' edits the textarea inline → confidence gate is cleared → he presses S to Send & log.
     AI role: next_best_action_engine rewrites a tailored draft with rationale and a confidence gate; if it fails, the seeded drafted_artifact appears so Marcus always has something to send or edit.
     Outcome: The most urgent relationship touch is sent and logged in one keystroke before Marcus's coffee cools — the headline aha.
  6. Stall root-cause diagnosis
     Trigger: Marcus visits the Stalls tab to understand why the Okafor Family Foundation has sat in Cultivation for 96 days.
     Flow: Stalls screen groups stalled prospects by severity → Marcus expands the Okafor diagnosis → stall_detection returns a root-cause hypothesis and re-engagement approach → he chooses 'Stage re-engagement,' 'Mark intentional pacing,' or 'Route to research.'
     AI role: stall_detection produces a per-prospect root-cause hypothesis; the deterministic fallback applies 'cold contact, not disinterest' logic so the diagnosis is never empty.
     Outcome: Marcus distinguishes a genuinely cold prospect from intentional pacing and acts on the difference instead of guessing.
  7. Ask reconciliation against capacity
     Trigger: On Eleanor's profile, Marcus needs a defensible ask amount that reconciles her $2.5M capacity with her 82 conversion likelihood and Solicitation stage.
     Flow: Profile shows the Capacity vs Readiness card → Marcus opens the Ask Reconciliation Advisor → ask_reconciliation_advisor returns recommended_ask, a risk_flag (aligned/under/over/unset), and rationale → Marcus adopts the recommendation into the solicitation.
     AI role: ask_reconciliation_advisor weighs capacity, likelihood, working ask, and stage; the fallback computes roughly 40% of capacity rounded to the nearest $10K with an 'unset' or 'aligned' flag.
     Outcome: Marcus enters a solicitation with a model-defensible ask he can justify to his director, not a round-number guess.
  8. Campaign forecast with confidence interval
     Trigger: Marcus checks the Campaigns tab to see whether the Experiential Learning Initiative will close on goal.
     Flow: Campaigns screen shows total goal, committed, open pipeline, projected close → each campaign card runs campaign_forecast → projected close with a confidence interval and a risk label (on-track/at-risk/trending-below) renders → Marcus drills into at-risk campaigns.
     AI role: campaign_forecast projects close from goal, committed, and pipeline with a low/high CI; the fallback uses seeded projected_close, ci_low, and ci_high so the forecast is always present.
     Outcome: Marcus and his director see campaign trajectory with honest uncertainty bounds, not a single misleading point estimate.
  9. Field-level data gap request
     Trigger: On a profile, Marcus spots a stale or missing field (e.g. Okafor's verified capacity) flagged low-confidence.
     Flow: Profile field confidence grid shows each field with a confidence label and provenance → Marcus clicks 'Request research' on the stale field → REQUEST_RESEARCH writes to the audit log → the field is marked pending verification.
     AI role: data_gap_inference labels each field's confidence, reason, and source; the fallback uses seeded confidence and dataGap records.
     Outcome: Marcus closes the loop on bad data with one click and a clean audit trail, instead of acting on a stale number.
  10. Handoff continuity and accept
     Trigger: An Annual Giving officer proposes handing an upgraded donor into Marcus's Major Gifts portfolio.
     Flow: Handoffs screen shows Proposed / In Review / Accepted columns → Marcus opens the Annual Giving→Major Gifts proposal → a continuity brief is generated summarizing relationship history → Marcus accepts → ACCEPT_HANDOFF writes a back-traceable audit log entry.
     AI role: A continuity-brief generation flow (built on weekly_digest_summary-style summarization) distills the prior relationship into a handoff brief; deterministic fallback summarizes seeded timeline events.
     Outcome: The donor relationship transfers with full context and zero dropped continuity — no warm donor goes cold at the handoff boundary.
  11. Keyboard-driven queue triage
     Trigger: Marcus wants to clear his action queue at speed without touching the mouse.
     Flow: Action Queue exposes keyboard hints (S send, K skip, R route) → Marcus reads each draft → presses S to send and log, K to skip, or R to route to research → the queue progress rail advances → the next action surfaces.
     AI role: next_best_action_engine has pre-drafted each artifact and set each confidence ribbon so Marcus only judges, never composes from scratch.
     Outcome: Marcus processes a full queue of actions in minutes with cleared confidence gates and a complete audit log.
  12. Stewardship follow-through on a closed gift
     Trigger: Margaret & Sun-Hee Park's $500K gift closed and they entered Stewardship over the weekend.
     Flow: Digest surfaces the Steward action (P90) → Marcus opens it → next_best_action_engine drafts a personalized stewardship touch → he edits and sends → the Park record advances cleanly in the Stewardship stage.
     AI role: next_best_action_engine drafts a warm, donor-specific stewardship artifact tied to the closed gift; fallback uses the seeded drafted_artifact.
     Outcome: A major closed gift is stewarded within hours of closing, protecting the next-gift relationship.
