## Concept
=== APPROVED PRODUCT BRIEF (the user picked this from a 5-brief set; build EXACTLY this) ===

Frame: convention-honoring (probability 0.4)
Title: Portfolio Pulse
Thesis: A unified console giving gift officers a prioritized, AI-ranked view of their donor portfolio with next-best-action prompts.
Why this frame: This is what a competent advancement-tech designer builds first: the canonical major-gifts officer workbench layered with predictive scoring, schema-congruent with Salesforce and Blackbaud conventions.

PRIMARY PERSONA:
  Name: Marcus Chen
  Role: Senior Major Gifts Officer, Northeastern University Advancement
  Context: Manages a 120-prospect portfolio across the College of Engineering and entrepreneurship alumni. Plans his week Monday mornings, runs 8-12 donor visits per month, and is accountable for a $4.5M annual fundraising goal.
  Emotional state: Time-starved and quietly anxious about which relationships are slipping; confident in person but overwhelmed by CRM data scattered across systems.
  Pain points:
    - No single view tells him who is most worth contacting this week versus who can wait
    - Wealth-screening data lives in one system, giving history in another, event attendance in a third
    - Preparing for a donor visit takes 45-60 minutes of manual record digging across tabs
    - He second-guesses ask amounts and often anchors too low, leaving money on the table
  JTBD: When I sit down to plan my week, I want to instantly know which donors deserve my attention today and exactly why, so I can spend my limited hours on the relationships that move the pipeline.
SECONDARY ACTORS:
  - Director of Major Gifts (Marcus's manager): Reviews pipeline forecasts, reallocates portfolios, coaches on stalled prospects via the manager rollup view.
  - Prospect Research Analyst: Validates AI capacity estimates, enriches profiles with wealth-screening sources, flags data quality issues.
  - Advancement Services / Gift Processing: Source of giving-history truth; AI activity logging writes back to the gift accounting system of record.
  - Donor (alumnus or parent): Subject of the portfolio; their engagement signals (events, emails, pledges) feed the scoring engine.
  - Annual Giving & Leadership Annual Giving team: Hands off rising mid-level donors flagged by the propensity model for major-gift cultivation.

DEMONSTRATED MOMENT: Monday 8:15am: Marcus opens Portfolio Pulse with coffee, sees his ranked queue of five donors to act on today, and within four minutes has a generated visit brief for his 11am lunch with a $2M-capacity engineering alum.

USE CASES (12) - the prototype MUST demonstrate at least 5 of these as live flows:
  1. Daily prioritized portfolio queue
     Trigger: Marcus opens the console to start his week or his day.
     Flow: Console loads → AI ranks all 120 prospects by an urgency composite → top five surface as action cards with rationale → Marcus reviews each card's 'why now' chip → he accepts, snoozes, or reassigns each.
     AI role: Ranks the full portfolio by blending propensity score, days-since-contact, pipeline stage velocity, and time-sensitive signals (recent gift, event RSVP, birthday).
     Outcome: Marcus knows his five highest-leverage actions in under 60 seconds instead of guessing across spreadsheets.
  2. AI wealth & capacity scoring
     Trigger: A prospect is added or refreshed, or Marcus opens a Prospect 360 profile.
     Flow: Profile opens → capacity model pulls real-estate, business affiliation, prior-giving, and peer-benchmark signals → renders an estimated giving capacity band with confidence → Marcus can drill into contributing factors.
     AI role: Estimates a capacity range ($ band) and propensity-to-give percentile with explainable factor attribution.
     Outcome: Marcus trusts a defensible capacity figure instead of relying on stale wealth-screen ratings.
  3. Next-best-action recommendations
     Trigger: Marcus reviews a queue card or opens a profile.
     Flow: AI evaluates relationship state → proposes one concrete action (call, hand-written note, event invite, solicitation) → shows expected impact and timing → Marcus accepts to auto-create a task or edits.
     AI role: Recommends the single highest-value next move per prospect with reasoning and suggested channel and timing.
     Outcome: Marcus stops deliberating about what to do and just executes the recommended move.
  4. Moves-management stage tracking
     Trigger: An interaction is logged or an AI signal suggests a stage change.
     Flow: Activity recorded → AI evaluates whether the prospect should advance (Identification → Qualification → Cultivation → Solicitation → Stewardship) → prompts Marcus to confirm a stage move → pipeline metrics recalculate.
     AI role: Detects stage-advancement signals and flags stalled prospects who have been in a stage too long.
     Outcome: Marcus's moves-management discipline stays current without manual stage hygiene.
  5. Visit prep briefing generation
     Trigger: Marcus has an upcoming meeting on his calendar or clicks 'Prep brief' on a profile.
     Flow: Marcus opens a prospect with a scheduled visit → clicks Generate brief → AI synthesizes giving history, last 5 interactions, interests, family, and open asks → produces a one-page briefing → Marcus reviews and adds notes.
     AI role: Summarizes the entire relationship into a structured, scannable one-page briefing with talking points and a recommended ask.
     Outcome: Marcus walks into a lunch fully briefed in 4 minutes instead of an hour of tab-hopping.
  6. Ask-amount modeling
     Trigger: Marcus is planning a solicitation and opens the Ask Calculator.
     Flow: Marcus selects a prospect and a fund/initiative → model proposes a recommended ask, a stretch ask, and a floor → shows comparable peer gifts and capacity rationale → Marcus sets the target into the proposal record.
     AI role: Optimizes ask amount using capacity, giving history, peer benchmarks, and engagement to suggest a calibrated three-tier ask.
     Outcome: Marcus anchors asks confidently and stops under-asking high-capacity donors.
  7. Pipeline forecast dashboard
     Trigger: Marcus or his director reviews progress toward goal.
     Flow: Open forecast board → expected value rolls up each prospect (ask × probability) by stage and expected close date → AI projects quarter-end against the $4.5M goal → highlights gaps and at-risk asks.
     AI role: Probability-weights each open opportunity and forecasts the portfolio's expected fundraising total with confidence bands.
     Outcome: Marcus and his director see a realistic forecast and where the gap is, before the quarter ends.
  8. Activity logging assist
     Trigger: Marcus finishes a call, visit, or email exchange.
     Flow: Marcus dictates or pastes raw notes → AI structures them into a contact report (type, sentiment, next steps, ask status) → suggests stage change and next-best-action → Marcus confirms and it writes back to the CRM.
     AI role: Transforms unstructured notes into a clean contact report and extracts follow-up tasks automatically.
     Outcome: Marcus logs in 30 seconds what used to take 10 minutes, so nothing falls through the cracks.
  9. Stalled-prospect rescue
     Trigger: AI detects a cultivation-stage prospect with no contact in 90+ days.
     Flow: Console surfaces a 'going cold' alert → shows last meaningful interaction and why they matter → recommends a re-engagement move → Marcus accepts and schedules.
     AI role: Monitors contact recency against stage norms and proactively flags relationships at risk of stalling.
     Outcome: High-value relationships don't quietly die from neglect.
  10. Event-driven cultivation prompts
     Trigger: A portfolio prospect RSVPs or attends a Northeastern event, reunion, or game.
     Flow: Engagement signal arrives → console raises a timely card ('Reconnect — attended Founders Day') → suggests a personalized follow-up within 48 hours → Marcus acts on the warm moment.
     AI role: Detects engagement spikes and converts them into time-sensitive cultivation actions.
     Outcome: Marcus strikes while donors are warm instead of missing the window.
  11. Portfolio health & coverage review
     Trigger: Weekly self-review or manager 1:1.
     Flow: Open coverage view → AI scores portfolio balance (too many stale, too few in solicitation) → flags neglected segments → recommends rebalancing or capacity-driven additions.
     AI role: Diagnoses portfolio composition and contact coverage gaps against best-practice benchmarks.
     Outcome: Marcus manages his whole book strategically, not just the loudest prospects.
  12. New-prospect assignment handoff
     Trigger: Annual Giving promotes a rising donor or research adds a discovered prospect.
     Flow: New prospect enters queue with a 'new to portfolio' badge → AI presents a qualification brief and recommended first move → Marcus accepts assignment and schedules discovery → prospect enters Qualification stage.
     AI role: Generates an onboarding qualification brief and suggested first-touch for newly assigned prospects.
     Outcome: Marcus ramps new prospects without a cold-start research scramble.
