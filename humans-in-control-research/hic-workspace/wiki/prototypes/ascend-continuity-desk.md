Frame: shifted-frame (probability 0.2)
Title: Ascend Continuity Desk
Thesis: A relationship-continuity workspace that protects donor trust as prospects move between Annual Giving, Major Gifts, and Gift Planning.
Why this frame: The riskiest advancement moment of 2026 isn't triage — it's the relationship transfer, where dropped context silently kills giving momentum. This console makes the handoff clean, audited, and seamless to the donor.

PRIMARY PERSONA:
  Name: Marcus Chen
  Role: Major Gift Officer, Northeastern University Advancement
  Context: Manages a 150-prospect portfolio across the College of Engineering and Khoury. Receives prospects handed up from Annual Giving and hands down to Gift Planning. Monday mornings he absorbs new inbound transfers while protecting the warmth of relationships he didn't build.
  Emotional state: Quietly anxious about inheriting a $2.5M relationship cold — afraid he'll repeat a question the prior officer already asked, or miss the personal thread that made the donor feel known.
  Pain points:
    - Inherited prospects arrive as a name and a capacity number with no narrative — he re-discovers history the donor expects him to already know
    - Field data is stale or unsourced; he can't tell which facts are verified versus inferred, so he hedges and the donor feels the seam
    - Handoffs happen over email and shared docs, leaving no audit trail when a gift later disputes who promised what
    - Cross-team capacity estimates conflict — Annual Giving's read and his read diverge, and nobody reconciles before the ask
  JTBD: When a high-value prospect is transferred to me, I want a verified, narrated continuity brief and a clean audit write-back, so I can absorb the relationship without the donor ever feeling handed off.
SECONDARY ACTORS:
  - Annual Giving Officer (Priya Raman): Originates upward handoffs, authors the initial relationship notes, confirms field provenance before transfer
  - Gift Planning Director (David Osei): Receives downward handoffs for planned/estate gifts, reviews continuity brief, accepts stewardship continuity schedule
  - Prospect Research Analyst (Lena Brooks): Resolves low-confidence field gaps routed during provenance verification, sources capacity and affiliation data
  - Advancement Operations / Compliance: Consumes the audit log write-back, validates that every accept event is timestamped and attributed

DEMONSTRATED MOMENT: A proposed handoff for Eleanor Whitfield lands in Marcus's review queue. He reads the AI-synthesized continuity brief, expands the field-provenance grid to verify capacity and affiliation, routes one stale field to research, then accepts — triggering a clean audit-log write-back and a stewardship continuity schedule that the donor never perceives.

USE CASES (11) - the prototype MUST demonstrate at least 5 of these as live flows:
  1. Inbound handoff review
     Trigger: A proposed handoff from Annual Giving appears in Marcus's Handoffs queue on Monday morning, badged 'Awaiting your review'.
     Flow: Marcus opens the Handoffs screen → sees three proposed transfers ranked by capacity and risk → clicks Eleanor Whitfield's proposal → a split view loads with the originating officer's notes on the left and the AI continuity brief generating on the right → he scans the summary before deciding to dig deeper or accept.
     AI role: continuity_brief_synthesis composes a narrated handoff summary from movement history, prior officer notes, and field data; weekly_digest_summary surfaces the handoff as a top focus item.
     Outcome: Marcus has, in under 90 seconds, a complete picture of who this donor is, why they're moving up, and what's verified versus uncertain.
  2. AI continuity brief generation
     Trigger: Marcus selects a proposed handoff and the brief panel renders, or he clicks 'Regenerate brief' after new field data resolves.
     Flow: Panel shows a loading shimmer with 'Synthesizing continuity brief…' → AI returns a three-part narrative (Who they are, Where the relationship stands, What not to repeat) → each claim is footnoted to a provenance source → Marcus can edit any sentence inline before saving the brief to the prospect record.
     AI role: continuity_brief_synthesis (Claude Opus 4.7) writes warm, executive-grade prose grounded only in sourced facts; if it fails, a deterministic templated brief assembles from seeded movement + notes data.
     Outcome: A donor-safe narrative Marcus can internalize and reference in the first call, so he never asks a question the prior officer already answered.
  3. Relationship history reconstruction
     Trigger: Marcus expands the Relationship Timeline tab inside the continuity brief or profile.
     Flow: Timeline renders every prior touch — gala attendance, prior gifts, email threads, stewardship moments — as a vertical ledger → AI annotates gaps ('No contact recorded Mar–Jul 2025') → Marcus hovers an entry for full detail → he can pin the three most relationship-defining moments to the brief.
     AI role: continuity_brief_synthesis identifies the narrative arc and flags suspicious silences; data_gap_inference labels timeline entries with confidence.
     Outcome: Marcus understands the emotional shape of the relationship, not just the transaction log — he can pick up the thread mid-sentence.
  4. Provenance verification before accept
     Trigger: Before accepting, Marcus opens the field-provenance grid to check what's trustworthy.
     Flow: Grid lists each field (capacity, affiliation, class year, last touch) with a confidence badge and source → fields ≥70 show green 'Verified'; <70 show gold 'Verify first' → Marcus clicks 'Verify first' on Eleanor's capacity estimate → a popover shows the inferred source and confidence reason → he either confirms or routes to research.
     AI role: data_gap_inference (Gemini 3.1 Pro) assigns confidence labels, names the inference source, and explains why each field is uncertain.
     Outcome: Marcus accepts the handoff knowing exactly which facts are solid — no silent inheritance of bad data into a $2.5M ask.
  5. Audit-log write-back
     Trigger: Marcus clicks 'Accept handoff'.
     Flow: A confirmation sheet summarizes what he's accepting and any fields still flagged → he confirms → the reducer writes an immutable audit entry (who, from, to, timestamp, fields verified, brief version) → the prospect's owner flips to Marcus → a toast confirms 'Handoff accepted · logged' → the Audit Log screen shows the new entry at the top.
     AI role: No model call — deterministic, compliance-grade. AI-authored brief version hash is recorded for traceability.
     Outcome: Compliance and future officers can reconstruct exactly who owned the relationship when, defending the institution if a gift is ever disputed.
  6. Cross-team capacity reconciliation
     Trigger: The continuity brief flags a divergence between Annual Giving's capacity read and the major-gift model's estimate.
     Flow: Reconciliation card shows both figures side by side ($2.1M originating vs $2.5M model) → AI proposes a reconciled capacity with a reason → Marcus reviews the working ask against it → he accepts the reconciled figure or overrides with a note.
     AI role: ask_reconciliation_advisor (Gemini 3.1 Pro) reconciles capacity, conversion likelihood, and stage into a recommended ask with an aligned/under/over/unset risk flag.
     Outcome: The two teams converge on one number before the ask, so the donor never hears conflicting expectations.
  7. Donor-trust risk flagging
     Trigger: AI detects a continuity risk during brief synthesis — e.g. a long contact gap, an unsourced personal detail, or a stalled stage.
     Flow: A gold risk ribbon appears atop the brief → Marcus expands it to read the hypothesis ('96-day silence may read as institutional neglect') → recommended mitigation appears ('Lead with a warm, non-ask re-introduction referencing the Gala') → Marcus acknowledges or dismisses with a reason.
     AI role: stall_detection (Gemini 3.1 Pro) generates a root-cause hypothesis distinguishing cold-contact from disinterest and proposes a re-engagement approach.
     Outcome: Marcus enters the relationship aware of the single thing most likely to damage trust, with a concrete first move to repair it.
  8. Stewardship continuity scheduling
     Trigger: On accept, the system proposes a continuity schedule so stewardship cadence doesn't reset.
     Flow: A schedule card shows the prior officer's cadence (quarterly touch, annual gala invite) → AI proposes the next three touches with dates and rationale → Marcus adjusts, then commits → tasks land in his Action Queue tagged 'Continuity'.
     AI role: weekly_digest_summary informs cadence priority; continuity_brief_synthesis proposes the timing rationale grounded in past pattern.
     Outcome: The donor experiences uninterrupted, consistent attention — the cadence they're used to continues seamlessly under a new name.
  9. Downward handoff to Gift Planning
     Trigger: Marcus identifies a prospect better served by planned/estate giving and initiates a handoff to Gift Planning.
     Flow: From the profile, Marcus clicks 'Propose handoff → Gift Planning' → he reviews the auto-drafted continuity brief → adds a personal note → sends → the proposal enters David Osei's review queue with full provenance attached.
     AI role: continuity_brief_synthesis drafts the outbound brief; data_gap_inference ensures every field carries a confidence badge before transfer.
     Outcome: Marcus hands a relationship down with the same care he wants when receiving — no degradation of trust as the donor moves to estate planning.
  10. Handoff queue triage on the Digest
     Trigger: Monday briefing surfaces 'two handoffs awaiting your review' as a top-three focus item.
     Flow: Marcus opens the Digest → reads the AI Monday briefing → sees handoffs called out with capacity totals → clicks through directly into the Handoffs review queue.
     AI role: weekly_digest_summary (Claude Opus 4.7) writes the briefing paragraph and ranks handoff review against other portfolio movement.
     Outcome: Marcus starts the week knowing the handoffs are the highest-leverage work, not buried under routine triage.
  11. Re-score after provenance resolution
     Trigger: A field Marcus routed to research returns resolved, and he wants the brief and ask updated.
     Flow: A 'Field resolved — re-synthesize?' prompt appears → Marcus accepts → continuity brief and ask reconciliation regenerate with the new verified data → the audit log notes the data change and brief revision.
     AI role: continuity_brief_synthesis and ask_reconciliation_advisor both re-run on the updated record; defensive validation falls back to seeded values if output shape is invalid.
     Outcome: The brief stays current and trustworthy; Marcus never acts on a stale narrative.
