Sam · 19:42
Why are you so sure I won't land a FAANG fall co-op? I had a 4.0 in HS and a CS Olympiad bronze.
Husky Compass · advisor
Your fundamentals are strong. The problem isn't you — it's the application math. Tier-1 brands receive 800–2,400 first-year applications for fall slots and prioritize sophomores+ NUworks·24. Of 47 NUworks alumni who applied 100% brand as first-years in your cohort tier between 2018–2024, only 5 secured fall placement (10.6%). The four highest-converting ones ALL had 3+ SME applications already in motion as a hedge by week 6 — they didn't replace the brand applications, they added to them NUworks Cohort Outcomes. The shift I'm recommending isn't "give up on Apple" — it's "stop relying only on Apple."
Sam · 19:45
If I add the four SMEs by Friday, what's my realistic best case?
Husky Compass · advisor
Best case based on the balanced archetype (38 alumni): one of the four SME applications becomes a first-round interview within 7 days, two within 14. Your FAANG portfolio keeps running in parallel — if any of those converts in late November, you have a real choice. Worst case: you have a co-op at Klaviyo by mid-November, which still nets you Hubspot-tier alumni network for sophomore-year applications NUworks·MarcusC·'21. Either outcome is better than the brand-only forecast.
