# University of Iowa Enterprise Leadership Fund | One Day for Iowa

Source: [University of Iowa Alumni](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aPffWwW9vAY)

Hi, I'm Courtney with Enterprise Leadership here at the University of Iowa. Enterprise Leadership is one of the fastest growing majors in the College of Liberal Arts and Sciences, with over 500 students preparing for careers in business, entrepreneurship, and leadership in a variety of different industries. But what makes Enterprise Leadership so special is that our students don't just learn about leadership and innovation, they experience it. Thanks to support from alumni and friends, Enterprise Leadership students gain real-world experience that prepares them to launch meaningful careers. Our students travel on career treks to companies across Iowa to meet entrepreneurs and industry leaders. They attend major professional events like the Roth Conference, where they see firsthand how companies raise capital and grow. Through our specialized academy programs, students work directly with real businesses on consulting projects, helping them solve problems and identify opportunities. They connect with alumni mentors, explore career paths, and build the confidence to lead teams and organizations. All of these experiences help our students graduate with strong professional networks, real experience, and the skills to make an impact from day one. On one day for Iowa, a gift of any size, $5, $50, $500, can open doors and create life-changing opportunities for our students. So we hope that you'll consider making a gift to Enterprise Leadership this year. After all, Hawkeyes help Hawkeyes.
