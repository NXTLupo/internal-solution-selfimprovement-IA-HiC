# Inside the University of Iowa’s Surgical Simulation Labs

Source: [University of Iowa Alumni](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AnybQKkcddk)

University of Iowa Health Care operates one of the most elite medical training centers in the world. The Center for Procedural Skills and Simulation is a state-of-the-art training facility for medical specialists to develop and advance their skills. The Center for Procedural Skills and Simulation provides training to pretty much everybody at UI Health Care. Every simulation center is different and we definitely stand out because we work with all of the groups. So for someone that does gall bladder removals all the time will come down here and practice not because they don't know how to do the gallbladder but what if they nick an artery where the anatomy is different than it is on a standard patient. We have intuitive da Vinci control console here so they have modules for each one of these things to give them practice. We actually built part of our space here to specifically accommodate having these da Vinci robots come so that we could train. The mannequins down here are also a really cool technology. We have the high fidelity simulator. It can mimic about everything that you need. It has pulses. It can turn blue if you want it to. Eyes blink. For me in pulmonary critical care we can practice intubations and hooking them up to a mechanical ventilator. Simulation really lends itself well to the high acuity low occurrence or halo events. Events that may not occur through everybody's training so this gives people a safe environment to practice. We like say this is a safe space down here. It's okay to make mistakes. It's okay to ask questions. It's an awesome space but really what makes the space all the people down here. We are there to make sure that everybody who is working directly with patients knows exactly what they're doing and can really have the best impact for the patient. You know training in health care is not just the stuff you do with your hands or your brain but it's how you communicate and how the team functions. I really think the majority of what we do focuses on teamwork and collaboration. We've got a lot of great technology. We've got a lot of great teaching tools. It really just comes down to you know letting people actually get their hands on things and learn through doing. We've had a few situations that we practice down here and then we've been able to see them happen on the floor. You see that and you experience that and you buy in and you understand the value and so it's been it's been really cool to be able to expand and just see the excitement of the things we can do down here. We are incredibly fortunate to have very generous donors who have seen the value of what we do and that has been really transformative for us to be able to meet the needs of our learners. One of our team members Jorge Salazar has built an amazing outreach program and on a regular basis every year we have a number of classes whether they're junior high or senior classes come for field trips to the Sim Center and they can get some experience about what does it mean to work and train in health care. We also work with the STEM program at the university and really try to partner to give the opportunity to see what it's like to be in a health care setting. I think it's a great program. I really get a lot out of it. I think the kids that come down through here also do and a lot of them become future Hawkeyes. The work we do is important and it makes a difference and we know it makes a difference and that's been driving all of us to keep going.
