CASE STUDY · ZAPPOS
Zappos turns the service call from a cost center into the strongest brand moment it owns
THE SERVICE-CULTURE MODEL · ONLINE RETAIL · RELATIONSHIP CX TIMEFRAME Decade-scale operating model INVESTMENT Agent autonomy + no-script service culture
CHALLENGE
Call-center orthodoxy treats every minute on the phone as a cost to be minimized, so agents rush callers off the line, scripts flatten every conversation into the same transcript, and loyalty erodes with every clipped interaction. The metric board rewards short calls, not returning customers.
APPROACH
Zappos removed handle-time targets entirely and let agents stay on a call as long as the customer needs. There are no scripts: agents improvise around the person, not the process, and the company treats the phone call as the single best brand moment it owns. Hiring screens for service disposition first and trains product knowledge second.
OUTCOME
The contact center became the loyalty engine. Customers who call convert and return at the highest rates the company tracks, service stories circulate as marketing the company never has to buy, and the famously long calls became proof of the culture rather than a staffing problem.
LESSON
When the call is treated as a brand asset instead of a cost, the contact center stops competing with marketing and starts doing its job: the conversation is the product.
