## Deliberately left image-free

- **presentation · 01 cover + agenda (611:2)** — content-complete cover. The left column is a
  full-bleed title + subtitle + eyebrow (cover-left 300:20, ends y=734 across the full 980px width);
  the right edge — where rule 1 would place a full-height vertical panel — is already occupied by the
  five-move agenda panel (300:24, x=1250..1832, y=240..804). Every remaining candidate zone is either
  too thin (the bottom band y=804..944 is only ~140px tall) or too narrow (the gap between cover-left
  and the agenda is ~98px wide). Per rule 4 + "quality over coverage", a forced thin-band or sliver
  image would cheapen a composition that is already balanced (title left, structured agenda right,
  takeaway band). Left image-free.

## Runtime renderer contract (journey — WIRED 2026-06-17)

The journey twins (`services/slideToStageHtml.ts renderJourneyMap` + `components/slideTemplates/web.tsx
JourneyMapSlide`) now render a per-phase thumbnail row directly under the phase headers, matching this
Figma canon. The `illustrator` agent writes its generated per-phase URLs/data-URIs into
`journeyMap.phaseImages: string[]` (index-aligned to `journeyMap.stages[]`; alternative carrier
`journeyMap.stages[i].image`). Empty string at an index = empty cell, no `<img>`. When no phase has art the
row + CSS are omitted (byte-identical to the pre-illustration layout; regression-tested). All journey
variants (base 595:2, T2 595:4, T3 595:5, in-table 595:6) share the same payload contract — they differ
only in phase count (5/5/6/5), which the renderer derives from `stages.length`.
