## Refresh protocol

1. Pull variable defs from 223:2 / 97:2 via Figma MCP.
2. Rewrite `hic-workspace/design-system/figma-tokens.json` (the `/api/design-tokens` source).
3. Keep the three token mirrors in sync: `components/slideTemplates/brands.ts`,
   `services/slideToStageHtml.ts` BrandTokens, `services/goldArtifact.ts` _NXT/_NU_TOKENS.
4. New GOLD frames get a gold twin under `wiki/design-system/gold/<base>-<brand>.html`
   and a row in this map.
