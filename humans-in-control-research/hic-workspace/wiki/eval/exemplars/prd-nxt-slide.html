NXT HUMANS

Product Requirements · User Stories

Five user stories that make the official path the one students choose

US-1
Must-have As a first-year with no resume experience, I want a guided first draft before I can get an advisor slot, so that I am not losing ground while I wait. The Blind Drafter
US-2
Must-have As a first-generation student, I want the matching rules explained before applications open, so that I rank employers knowing how the decision is made. The Rule-Blind Decider
US-3
Should-have As a student in a competitive major, I want roles ranked by fit to my major, so that I stop applying to everything just to get one reply. The Volume Applicant
US-4
Should-have As a student whose schedule conflicts with prep, I want on-demand interview practice, so that my first real interview is not my first rehearsal. The Unrehearsed Performer
US-5
Could-have As a co-op advisor, I want to receive a student's AI-prepared draft ahead of our meeting, so that our limited time goes to judgment, not formatting. The Blind Drafter
The problemFirst-years route around the official co-op channels because each one is too slow, too broad, or too opaque to use when it matters. The product's job is to make the official path the fastest, fitted, most candid one, so students stop improvising the highest-stakes search of their degree.
