Intelligence Augmentation Suite

THE CX RESEARCH-TO-DELIVERY SUITE

Listen to customers, find what matters, and ship board-ready work, in one place, grounded in evidence, on brand, with a human in control.

Intelligence Augmentation is a connected set of customer-experience tools that carry a team from raw customer voice all the way to a cited deck or a clickable prototype. The machine does the labor of collection, synthesis, design, and quality control. The person sets the direction and approves the work. This guide is the internal source of truth for how we position the suite, what each product promises, and the proof behind every claim.

Part I · Positioning

01The promise

One sentence we can all repeat:

Intelligence Augmentation turns scattered customer signal into work you can present, in a single guided session, with every claim tied to a named source and every visual already on brand.

The category is CX research and delivery. The wedge is that we collapse a chain of specialist tools into one workflow, and we never let the output drift into generic AI filler. What comes out is something a senior partner would put in front of a client without a rewrite.

02The problem we solve

A customer-experience team today runs a relay. One tool scrapes reviews. Another transcribes the interviews. A spreadsheet tags the themes. A slide tool builds the deck. A designer fixes the brand. An editor cuts the AI tells. Each handoff loses context, and the final artifact is only as good as the weakest baton pass. Worse, most AI writing tools produce confident text with no sources behind it, so the work cannot survive a sharp question in the room.

The cost shows up three ways: time lost to moving data between tools, trust lost when a claim has no source, and polish lost when output looks like a template instead of the brand. We attack all three at once.

03Why we win

Five messaging pillars. Lead with the one that fits the room.

01
The whole job, one workflow
Collect, analyze, map, write, prototype, and present without leaving the suite. The corpus built in the research step is the same corpus every deliverable cites. No re-keying, no lost context between tools.
02
Evidence you can defend
Every claim resolves to a named source. At least 60% of cited sources are external, not the client's own uploads, and the system rejects output that leans on a handful of internal documents. The work holds up when someone pushes back.
03
On brand the moment it is made
Colors, type, logos, spacing, and a 294-archetype component library are pulled live from the Figma design system at generation time. Output arrives as NXT Humans or Northeastern, with no manual styling pass.
04
Research becomes a working product
The same insight that fills a slide can render as a clickable, multi-screen prototype with real AI behavior wired inside it. You demo the idea, not a flat mockup.
05
A human stays in control
Checkpoints at every stage let the operator edit, approve, or reject. The AI proposes; the person decides. And the system learns each team's standards from those edits, so it gets sharper with use.

04Who it is for

CX and research leads
People who run discovery, synthesize voice of customer, and have to defend findings to a client or an exec. They feel the relay-of-tools pain most.

People who turn research into decks, blueprints, and recommendations on a deadline, and who get judged on rigor and polish.

People who want a clickable, on-brand prototype out of the research, not a static slide, to put a real experience in front of stakeholders.
Brand owners (NXT and Northeastern)
People who need every artifact to be correct to the design system every time, without policing it by hand.

10Humans in Control, in depth

HiC is where the suite earns the category. It runs the full arc of an engagement and keeps the person in the driver's seat the whole way.

How a session feels

State a goal
Write a research goal and, if you have them, drop in client documents and source links. The system checks whether a similar engagement ran before and loads what it learned.
Run research
The suite gathers evidence across a wide external base plus your uploads, deduplicates it, and splits it into cited, retrievable passages.
Approve the themes
A synthesis pass clusters the corpus into named themes with verbatim quotes. You edit, approve, or reject before anything is built on top.
Generate on demand
Type a hashtag in the Analyst Desk and get a deck, personas, a journey map, a blueprint, or a prototype, grounded in the same corpus and already on brand.
Export and revise
Push a deck to PPTX or PDF; run a prototype live and revise it by chatting. New work is written back into the knowledge base for next time.

12The end-to-end story

The demo that lands. Walk a prospect from a customer complaint to a board deck without changing tools.

  1. Listen. The Review Collector pulls what customers are saying across Reddit, TrustPilot, and the app store. The Video Analyzer transcribes the user interviews.
  2. Find the pattern. Social Research Analytics clusters the chatter into themes and tells you how people feel about each one.
  3. Map it. The Journey Mapper lays out the experience; the Miro Generator puts it on a board for the workshop.
  4. Build the work. Humans in Control turns the evidence into a cited strategy deck, a persona set, and a clickable prototype, all on brand.
  5. Present it. The Presentation Renderer sends a scrolling web story; the deck exports to PPTX for the room.
  6. Operationalize. The Copilot Recipe Generator stands up a customer-facing agent from the same work.

Every step reuses the evidence from the step before. The customer complaint that started it is still cited in the final deck.

13Hard questions, honest answers

"Is this just another AI deck tool?"

No. AI deck tools write confident text with nothing behind it. We ground every claim in a named source, weight external evidence over the client's own files, and run a quality layer that removes the AI tells those tools leave in. The output is meant to survive a sharp question, not just look finished.

"Will it stay on brand?"

The brand is not a theme applied after the fact. Colors, type, logos, and a 294-component library are read live from the design system at generation time, so the artifact is correct to NXT or Northeastern the moment it is made.

"Does the AI run unsupervised?"

No. There is a checkpoint at every stage where the person edits, approves, or rejects. The AI proposes and the human decides, and the system learns the team's standards from those decisions.

"What if a data source goes down?"

The suite tracks source and provider health and routes around what is failing, and the transcription and generation paths fall back across providers when one is throttled. The work keeps moving.

How HiC produces grounded, on-brand output (one paragraph)

A hashtag in the Analyst Desk routes to the canonical generation model, which is given a grounding context assembled from manifest chunk pointers (specific cited passages, not bulk text), the matching consulting framework, the live brand tokens, learned constraints from prior edits, and engagement memory. The model writes against that context. The output then passes through a runtime quality layer that resolves every citation to a named source, strips AI tells and process language, and holds the brand and content rules, before anything renders. Prototypes run the same grounding through a five-stage build that composes only from the 294-component design-system library and wires live, fault-tolerant AI calls into the result.
