Fragmentation risk: The PAI Vision research found that "there are dozens of commercially available AI tools to support the student journey, but they are disconnected and fragmented." The RACI assigns the PAI Platform Team as the single Accountable unit for integration, preventing that fragmentation from persisting into the new operating model.

Critical coordination interfaces

Interface
Co-op Office + PAI Platform Team
The highest-frequency coordination point. The Co-op Office owns placement accountability; the PAI Platform Team builds the AI tools that inform it. Without a formal handoff protocol, AI readiness signals risk being ignored in human advising workflows.
Interface
Employer Relations + Learner Profile Engine
Employer satisfaction signals and role requirement data must flow back into the Learner Profile Engine to improve matching quality over time. This feedback loop is the mechanism by which the AI model improves with each placement cycle.
Interface
Faculty Advisors + Learner Profile Engine
Faculty validate competency signals that the engine cannot infer from transactional data alone. Their input enriches the profile and closes the gap between academic achievement and workplace readiness assessment.
Interface
Steering Committee + All Units
The Steering Committee is Accountable for placement KPIs but depends on data flowing up from every operating unit. A regular cadence of outcome reporting from the Co-op Office and PAI Platform Team is the governance mechanism that keeps the north-star vision grounded in real performance.

Operating Context · Co-op AI at Northeastern

Five research themes define the terrain the operating model must navigate

Student and faculty interviews, market research, and technology research converged on five overarching themes that shaped the functional unit design above.

Research themes driving the operating model design

Theme 1
Connect Student Journey
The student digital ecosystem is fragmented and causing frustration. The Learner Profile Engine and PAI Platform Team exist specifically to address this.
Theme 2
Build Learner Profiles
Core to personalization is a rich, dynamic learner profile. ML clustering and dynamic cohorting are the technical mechanisms assigned to the PAI Platform Team.
Theme 3
Improve Co-op Placement
Improve co-op placement rates in competitive job climates and markets. This is the primary KPI owned by the Steering Committee and operationalized by the Co-op Office.
Theme 4
Multimodal AI Experiences
Multimodal and immersive AI experiences are better for education, learning, and AI reasoning. AI Vendor Partners supply these capabilities under PAI Platform Team governance.
Theme 5
Build Student Connections
Students feel more prepared when connecting with others who have done it before. Co-op Coordinators and Faculty Advisors are the human layer that help these connections.

Operating model design principles

  1. Agents as the next wave: The PAI Vision identifies AI agents as the next wave of the AI economy. The PAI Platform Team is structured to build and govern agent-based capabilities as they mature, rather than treating them as a separate initiative.
  2. Personalization requires dynamic cohorting: Personalized experiences succeed when using dynamic cohorting and ML clustering models. The Learner Profile Engine is the dedicated unit for this capability, not a feature bolted onto an existing system.
  3. Fragmentation is the enemy: Dozens of commercially available AI tools exist but are disconnected and fragmented. The operating model assigns a single Accountable unit (PAI Platform Team) for integration to prevent this pattern from recurring.
  4. Human advising remains essential: AI tools assess and recommend, but Co-op Coordinators and Faculty Advisors translate those signals into human coaching. The model does not replace advisors; it augments their capacity and precision.
  5. Employer feedback closes the loop: Employer satisfaction and role data must flow back into the AI layer. Employer Relations is positioned as a data-generating unit, not merely a relationship-management function.
  6. KPI accountability must be explicit: The stakeholder observation that placement metrics have dropped considerably demands that a single unit (the Steering Committee) holds unambiguous accountability for the outcome, even as responsibility is distributed across the operating model.

North-star framing

The objective stated in the roadmap prioritization is for Northeastern to be identified as the leader in AI-augmented experiential learning. The operating model above is the organizational expression of that ambition: a governance layer that owns the vision, a platform team that builds the AI infrastructure, a co-op operation that deploys it with students, and an employer network that validates it in the market.
