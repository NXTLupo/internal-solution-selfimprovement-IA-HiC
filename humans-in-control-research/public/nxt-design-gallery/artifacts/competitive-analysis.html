Where NU is behind — and who set the bar

three priority gaps · named leaders

Outcomes don't reach advancement.

Drexel converts a placement event into a donor-narrative artifact in 5 days; Northeastern's lag is 11 days. The donor relationship cools while the data sits.

Leader: Drexel (5 / 5) · NU: 2 / 5 22

No silence-detection in the application funnel.

Cincinnati flags students at day 5 of inactivity and routes them into a triage queue. Northeastern fires a single template email at day 7 with no follow-up logic.

Leader: Cincinnati (5 / 5) · NU: 1 / 5 21

Donor-narrative loop is brittle and slow.

Drexel publishes co-op stories every alumni-magazine cycle with a workflow that loops the student for verbatim approval; Northeastern's path is six handoffs and no verbatim approval at scale.

Leader: Drexel (5 / 5) · NU: 2 / 5 22
