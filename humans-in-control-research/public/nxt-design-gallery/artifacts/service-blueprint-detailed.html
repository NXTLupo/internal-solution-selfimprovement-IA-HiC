Service Blueprint · Future State

A skills-bench evaluator upstream of the ATS lifts qualified-candidate visibility from 6.7% to 38% and reclaims the Submission week from keyword gaming.

Each future-state step replaces a journey-stage fail point with a redesign tied to the corpus diagnosis. Customer Action and Frontstage live above the Line of Visibility; Backstage and Support Processes power the redesign below it.
