Reading the blueprint

Bitner 5-layer model · extended with fail points + technology
Above the line

What users see and do. Actors, customer action, customer needs, and emotion live here. Stages are named in user voice — every phase reads like something a first-year would actually say out loud.

Below the line

Frontstage (what staff visibly do), backstage (hidden operations), and the technology layer. Backstage is where fail points concentrate.

Fail points

Named failure modes with frequency and consequence. 1 per 2 stages minimum. These are the highest-leverage interventions in any service blueprint.

Moments of truth

Stage cells marked with a ★ are make-or-break interactions. Investment per moment-of-truth should be 2–3× the per-stage average.
