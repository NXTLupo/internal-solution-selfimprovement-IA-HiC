Heuristic UX Brief · NUworks v2 portal · Nielsen 10

Six of ten heuristics scored C or below on the student-application flow — three (visibility of system status, match between system and real world, error prevention) require redesign before bench-evaluator integration ships.

01
Visibility of system status Submission status reads "Submitted" for both ATS-accepted and ATS-rejected applications. Students cannot tell if a recruiter saw the resume. Observed in 8 of 12 student interviews; advisor support tickets cite this as the #1 confusion source.
Dseverity high
02
Match between system and real world Posting filters use vendor-jargon labels ("requisition class", "cycle window"); students search by major and term. Time-to-first-match averages 14 minutes on first session; peer benchmark is 3-4.
Cseverity high
03
User control and freedom No "withdraw application" affordance after submission. Students who change mind contact CECCA via email. ~120 withdrawal requests per cycle currently routed through advisors.
Cseverity med
04
Consistency and standards Filter UI patterns consistent within NUworks; mostly aligned with Workday norms on the employer side. Minor inconsistencies in date format (US vs ISO) across two screens.
Bseverity low
05
Error prevention Application form allows submission with required fields blank if user toggles "no preference" — silently fails ATS validation upstream. 11% of cycle-1 submissions fail this way; root cause of the recruiter "ghost application" pattern.
Dseverity high
06
Recognition rather than recall Saved searches and recently-viewed postings are visible on landing. Strong on this dimension. No student feedback flagging this heuristic in the interview set.
A-severity low
07
Flexibility and efficiency of use No keyboard shortcuts. No bulk-apply (students submit one at a time). Power users (returning co-op students) lose 4-6 minutes per session. Returning students rank this their #1 complaint (advisor pulse, 2025-09).
C+severity med
08
Aesthetic and minimalist design Landing page contains 14 sidebar links; 80% of students use 3. Cognitive load drag on first-time users. Heatmap data from 2025-Q4 (CECCA Data & Systems) confirms 9-link tail goes unclicked.
Cseverity med
09
Help users recognize, diagnose, recover from errors Error messages on form validation say "Field required" with no context. Students retry blind. 5-second test: 2 of 8 students in usability sessions could fix the error without help.
C-severity med
10
Help and documentation CECCA-authored help articles available inline; updated quarterly. Discoverable from each form section. Help-article click-through rate is healthy at 22%.
B+severity low
Methodology: Nielsen 10 applied across 8 student-flow tasks · n=12 student interviews · n=8 coordinator interviews · 2025-Q4 heatmap data
