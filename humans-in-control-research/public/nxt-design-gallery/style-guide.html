Case study

A team that governed its model garden

Problem

Ad-hoc AI tools caused brand drift across assets.

Approach

Centralized a prompt library and a governed model garden.

Result

Consistent output with a groundedness gate before ship.

Findings

Four forces reshape design work

  • Drafting collapses to minutes; judgment becomes the bottleneck.
  • Design systems become the contract for agentic AI.
  • Tool sprawl pushes teams toward governed platforms.
  • Accessibility shifts left into generation.

Platform

Production runs on a controlled stack

Model garden, prompt library, RAG, and a content-safety gate.
