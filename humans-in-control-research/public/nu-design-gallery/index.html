Density principles

What the templates fix, in order of priority.

01 · Kill the chrome

No methodology blurbs, no slide numbers on documents, no full source strips. Brand mark stays. Title stays. Everything else earns its space.

02 · Citations as chips, not bibliographies

Inline HiEd Dive '24 with a tooltip-grade hint. A consolidated source index lives once, at the foot of the document.

03 · Tabular numerals everywhere

Roboto with font-feature-settings: 'tnum' 1 applied globally. Numbers line up across rows; comparisons read at a glance.

04 · Multi-column over stacked

4-up persona roster, 12-tile theme grid, 3-column report inset. Side-by-side reads faster than scrolling.

05 · One section divider rule

A 1px line, an 18px display heading, optional counter on the right. No banner bars, no dark hero blocks (except deck section headers).

06 · Northeastern red is a scalpel

It marks critical state, target maturity, and one verbatim quote per page. It is never a background, never a divider, never decoration.
