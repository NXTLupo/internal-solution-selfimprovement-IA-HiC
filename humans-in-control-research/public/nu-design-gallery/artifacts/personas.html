I can spend forty minutes preparing for a single 9 a.m. coffee — pulling giving history out of Raiser's Edge, capacity from iWave, and student-affinity notes from a sticky on my desk. Maya R. · in-context interview, 2026-02-14 · transcript MGO-04 v

Cooper goal hierarchy 3 / 3

Life
Build relationships that survive job changes — donors who trust her, not the title.
Experience
Walk into every meeting feeling like the most-prepared person in the room.
Functional
Cut meeting prep from 40 minutes to under 10 without sacrificing nuance.

JTBD

When I'm preparing for a donor meeting, I want a single consolidated brief on capacity, history, and student affinity, so I can open with the right ask the first time.

Behaviors first 3 are behavioral, not demographic

Pre-meeting ritual40-min cross-referencing across 3 systems before each visit 1
Pipeline cadence34 active prospects · re-touches every 18 days on average
Trust signalVerifies any new data point against a second source before using it
Tool stackRaiser's Edge NXT · iWave · Outlook · paper notes

Pain points 3 of 4

  • Fragmented constituent data across 15+ departmental systems forces manual cross-referencing. "By the time I've stitched it together, the meeting's tomorrow." 1 3
  • No visibility into student-side affinity — when a donor's nephew is on co-op at the firm she's calling, she finds out after. "I missed an obvious bridge because the data lives in a different office." 5
  • AI scoring opacity — gift officers don't trust prospect scores they can't interrogate. "Tell me why this prospect is a 91, or I'll keep using my gut." 2

Motivations & opportunities 2 / 3

  • Recognition tied to portfolio outcomes, not activity counts — wants attribution for stewardship moves, not just closings. 4
  • Peer benchmarking against the other 28 gift officers in the unit — cohort-anonymous, but real. 6

I don't need another dashboard. I need to know which three things, this quarter, will move the campaign needle — and which two we've been doing out of habit. D. Chen · advisory board read-out, 2026-01-22 · memo VP-OPS-09 v

Cooper goal hierarchy 3 / 3

Life
Leave the advancement function structurally stronger than he found it.
Experience
Trust the operating cadence — fewer surprises in board meetings.
Functional
Hit the $1.6B campaign target two quarters ahead of plan.

JTBD

When I'm planning quarterly priorities, I want three high-confidence levers ranked by expected gift uplift, so I can concentrate gift officer capacity where it pays.

Behaviors

Decision cadenceBi-weekly portfolio reviews · monthly board-level signal scan
Data trust thresholdWon't quote a number that hasn't been counter-checked by ops
Cross-unit relationshipsReports to President · partners with Provost on student-success
ReadsCASE · Higher Ed Dive · The Chronicle of Philanthropy · Inside Higher Ed

Pain points

  • Generational giving cliff — young alumni giving declined 12% while overall fundraising grew 3%. 7
  • Tooling sprawl — 14 platforms across the division, 3 with overlapping CRM features. "We have two donor portals and neither one is the system of record." 8
  • No clear handoff between student-success signals and donor-relationship moves.

Motivations & opportunities

  • Re-allocate, don't add — fund this work from sunset tooling, not new budget. 8
  • Capacity-first metrics — measure gift officer time recovered, not seats deployed.
  • Codify what already works — peer-mentor handoffs the Boston region invented without a system.

First-years come in believing the system will assign them a co-op. I spend September walking them back from that assumption — and they still apply to four roles total. J. Liu · cohort retrospective, 2025-12-09 · field-note COORD-12 v

Cooper goal hierarchy

Life
Be the reason a student ended up in the right role for them, not just any role.
Experience
Stop spending office hours on the same five logistical questions.
Functional
Move every advisee from "applying to 4" to "applying to 12+" in first cycle.

JTBD

When a first-year is preparing for their first co-op cycle, I want a triage view of who's on pace and who isn't, so I can spend my hours on the students who need me.

Behaviors

Caseload180 active advisees · sees 22 each week on average
Triage signalReviews application count + last contact every Friday
TemplatesMaintains 12 personal resume / cover templates outside the system
Tool stackHandshake · Outlook · Google Sheets · Slack DMs

Pain points

  • No early-warning view for students who've stalled in the application funnel. "I find out a student didn't apply when the cycle's already closed." 9
  • Resume feedback bottleneck — 180 first drafts hit her inbox in a single week each fall. 10
  • Privacy guardrails are unclear when a donor's office calls about a specific student. 11

Motivations & opportunities

  • Peer-mentor scaffolding — first-years already trust juniors more than coordinators. 12
  • AI as triage, not authorship — wants help ranking who needs her, not writing resumes for them.
  • Outcome attribution — a way to show that her advisees' placement velocity tracks her interventions.

Cooper goal hierarchy

Life
Become the version of herself that has options — not the one who only had one.
Experience
Feel like she's part of a cohort, not staring at a system alone.
Functional
Land a research-lab co-op by end of sophomore spring.

JTBD

When I'm figuring out what to do this semester to be ready for co-op, I want a step-by-step plan and someone who's already done it, so I can stop guessing what counts.

Behaviors

Information sourceReddit r/Northeastern · upperclassmen DMs · official channels third
Engagement window9 p.m.–1 a.m. is when the prep work actually happens
Anxiety driverDoesn't know what "on pace" looks like vs. peers
Trust hierarchyPeer > coordinator > centralized portal

Pain points

  • No baseline for what "on track" means — pace is invisible until it's too late. 13
  • Generic advice from the system can't tell her whether her major requires a different cycle. "The dashboard told me to update my resume — I don't have one yet." 14
  • Discoverability — three campus resources exist for the question she's asking and she's used none. 15

Motivations & opportunities

  • Formalize peer mentorship — the trust is already there, the system isn't. 12
  • Pace dashboard — anonymized cohort-percentile so she knows where she stands. 16
  • Mobile-first — every prep moment happens on a phone in a dorm. 15
