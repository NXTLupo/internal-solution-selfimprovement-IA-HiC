Risk register · 4 tracked

FERPA exposure when bridging student-side data to advancement L:M I:H

Aggregated signals only. No GPA, no protected academic records. NACUBO guidance leaves gray zones around individual stewardship.

Mitigation: aggregated relationship-health buckets only; never individual academic records. General Counsel sign-off on data model in 30 days.
Officer rejection of prospect scores L:H I:M

Trust gap is the adoption ceiling. If "explain view" lags model deploy, the score itself loses credibility.

Mitigation: ship explain view first (G-04 quick win); model updates gated on explainability presence.
Sunset-replacement budget posture limits net-new platforms L:M I:M

VP Ops will not approve new SaaS without naming the platform that retires. Two satellite CRMs are the consolidation candidates.

Mitigation: name retirement targets in every roadmap initiative; track sunset $ alongside new $.
Cycle-aligned change windows L:H I:L

Co-op cycles run twice per year; deploys outside the change window cannot land. Plan dates inside Mar / Sep cohort breaks.

Mitigation: calendar-locked release plan; freeze 4 weeks pre-cycle.
