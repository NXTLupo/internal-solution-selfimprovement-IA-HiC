Stop condition

how the act-reflect loop exits

The loop halts when four of six sub-questions reach coverage, or when the budget cap is reached, whichever comes first. Each iteration writes a reflection trace to the engagement log, so the decision to stop is auditable, not silent.

The human-in-control gate
  1. No external calls during planning · the plan is composed, rendered, and edited before any connector runs
  2. Deterministic plan hash · the same goal and inputs produce the same plan, binding the run to what you approved
  3. Binding budget · the reflector halts when the cap is reached, never silently overspends
