Customer actionswhat the student does
Updates one resume against last year's examples and a single keyword set.
Scrolls two boards across two evenings, shortlists by gut, no semantic filter.
Submits a batch during midterms, then waits with no view of what happened.
Takes short remote interviews scheduled against the class timetable.
Accepts or declines inside the employer window, often without a comparison.
Frontstagevisible to the student · line of visibility
Prep workshop and a single resume-review slot, booked weeks ahead.
Posting boards with vendor-jargon filters; deadline reminder emails.
A confirmation that reads identical whether filtered or under review.
Interview scheduler; advisor mediates class-excuse conflicts.
Match confirmation and site-logistics prep, after the fact.
Backstagehidden from the student · line of internal interaction
Advisor caseload triage; resume templates maintained outside the system.
Employer configures ATS keyword thresholds tuned for senior roles.
ATS keyword filter ranks and removes applicants before any human review.
Recruiter reviews only the surfaced shortlist; the filtered pool is unseen.
Employer approvals drive a short acceptance window on its own timeline.
