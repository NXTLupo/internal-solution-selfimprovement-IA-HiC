Diagnosis

Northeastern's experiential-learning and advancement flywheels share a constituent but not a data spine. The placement-to-donor handoff lags 11 days against a 5-day peer benchmark, masking a generational giving cliff (−12% in young alumni) under a +3% aggregate line.

Guiding policy

We will instrument the connective tissue between the two flywheels, sequenced by fastest-recovered capacity. Triage before recommender accuracy. Sunset before purchase. Aggregated signals only between donor and student sides.

Coherent actions

Seven recommendations grouped into three MECE pillars. Three quick wins ship inside 90 days. Two strategic builds land FY27. Each names a sunset target and a quantified impact.

R-01
Pillar 03 · Peer-mentor scaffolding

Formalize and pay the peer-mentor pattern three Boston dorms already invented.

First-years escalate to upperclassmen 3.4× more often than to coordinators, and three Boston dorms have run informal peer-mentor pilots with measured +14-point engagement lift. Codify the trust pattern, pay mentors at student-worker rate, instrument the touchpoints, and run a one-cycle outcome test.

"He'd already been through it — and he answered me on a Tuesday at midnight." — A. Patel, first-year 3

Action items · 5

  1. Recruit 60 mentors across 6 dorms (Owner: Student Affairs · 4 wks)
  2. Pay rate at $18/hr, 4 hrs/week, capped at 16 hrs/term (Owner: Finance · 2 wks)
  3. Instrument touchpoints in Salesforce EDU with 5 event types (Owner: Data Eng · 3 wks)
  4. Outcome target: +12-pt cohort engagement vs. control dorms (Owner: Co-op Ops · cycle 1)
  5. Scale-or-retire review at end of cycle 1; decision criteria pre-registered

R-02
Pillar 01 · Connective tissue

Build a cross-record identity layer so officers stop reconstructing dossiers by hand.

28 major gift officers spend 40 min × 220 meetings per year on cross-system prep — ~$340K of officer time at fully-loaded rates. A shared identity layer between Raiser's Edge, Salesforce EDU, and the student-success warehouse cuts prep to under 10 min, with a single consolidated brief.

"By the time I've stitched it together, the meeting's tomorrow." — Maya R., MGO 1

Action items · 4

  1. Define canonical constituent ID across the 3 source systems (Owner: Data Architecture · 6 wks)
  2. Privacy review: aggregated signals only across the bridge, GC sign-off (Owner: GC · 4 wks parallel)
  3. Brief-builder service: one-pager per upcoming meeting (Owner: Adv Ops · 12 wks)
  4. Adoption target: 80% of MGOs use the brief for ≥70% of meetings by FY27 Q2

R-03
Pillar 02 · Triage as v1 AI surface

Detect silence at day five and route students to a coordinator triage queue.

Application-funnel attrition reaches 38% for students who go 14 days without a reply; Cincinnati's day-5 detection sets the benchmark. Replace the day-7 template email with a routed queue that ranks students by stalled-signal strength. Reuses existing Handshake webhooks.

"I find out a student didn't apply when the cycle's already closed." — J. Liu, coordinator 9

Action items · 4

  1. Define stall signal: 5 days no Handshake activity + open applications < 4 (Owner: Co-op Ops · 2 wks)
  2. Triage queue UI for coordinators ranked by signal strength (Owner: Co-op Ops Eng · 4 wks)
  3. Explain view: every queue entry shows why it was flagged (Owner: Data Eng · 2 wks)
  4. Outcome: cycle-on-cycle attrition cut from 38% → < 22% by end of FY27 Q1

R-04
Pillar 02 · Triage as v1 AI surface

Ship an "explain view" alongside every prospect & placement score before any new model deploys.

62% of officers report ignoring prospect scores they cannot interrogate; the trust gap, not accuracy, is the adoption ceiling. Surface top 5 contributing signals and a "why this score?" overlay on the existing model. Gate any future model release on explainability presence.

"Tell me why this prospect is a 91, or I'll keep using my gut." 8

Action items · 4

  1. SHAP-style feature attribution for the live prospect model (Owner: Data Sci · 3 wks)
  2. Officer UI: top 5 drivers + plain-English explanation per score (Owner: Adv Ops Eng · 3 wks)
  3. Governance: no new model ships without an explain view (Owner: Adv Ops · ongoing)
  4. Adoption metric: officer-cited-score uplift +30 pts in QoQ survey

R-05
Pillar 01 · Connective tissue

Compress the placement-to-donor handoff from 11 days to under 72 hours.

Drexel runs the placement-to-donor loop in 5 days at half NU's volume; the 11-day Northeastern lag costs ~$1.1M / yr in delayed stewardship moves at CASE-benchmarked elasticity. Daily digest of placement events tagged with donor-affinity bridges fires to MGOs while the full integration lands behind it.

"I missed an obvious bridge because the data lives in a different office." — Maya R. 1

Action items · 5

  1. Daily placement digest as a quick-win bridge (Owner: Adv Ops · 3 wks)
  2. Donor-affinity tag on placement events (Owner: Data Eng · 6 wks)

  4. Verbatim approval path: students approve their own quotes (Owner: Marketing Ops · 6 wks)
  5. Cycle target: 11 d → < 3 d median by FY27 Q4

Pillar 03 · Peer-mentor scaffolding

Bring the prep surface to where prep actually happens — mobile, 9 p.m.–1 a.m.
