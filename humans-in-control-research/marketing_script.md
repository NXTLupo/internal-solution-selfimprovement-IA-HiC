
# Video Script: Humans-In-Control (Deep Dive)
**Duration:** 2:00
**Tone:** Technical, Grounded, Precise.
**Visual Style:** Screen recording of the UI with close-ups on the cursor interactions.

---

**(0:00) [Visual: Split screen. Left side shows raw data files. Right side shows the "Thinking Stream" logging events.]**

**VO:**
Most AI research tools are black boxes. You upload data, wait two minutes, and hope the output makes sense. If it’s wrong, you’re stuck.
**NXT Humans** is different. We built a linear architecture with four distinct "Intervention Checkpoints." The AI does the heavy processing, but you approve every step of the logic chain.

**(0:18) [Visual: Cursor uploads files. The "Analysis Stream" logs: [PHASE] Data Ingestion. The Modal "Checkpoint: Ingestion" appears.]**

**VO:**
**Checkpoint One: Ingestion.**
The AI reads your documents and summarizes the context. Here’s the critical part: It assigns a confidence score.
*(Visual: Cursor hovers over the 'Confidence Score: 85%' and the 'Key Stakeholders' list)*
If the AI missed a key stakeholder group or misinterpreted the document type, you catch it here. You don't proceed until the foundation is correct.

**(0:35) [Visual: Transition to "Checkpoint: Strategic Themes". Cursor clicks the "Priority" toggle on a theme named "Cost of Living". Cursor clicks "Skip" on "Generic Satisfaction".]**

**VO:**
**Checkpoint Two: Strategic Weighting.**
The AI identifies patterns, but it doesn't know your business priorities. You do.
We allow you to weight the themes. Mark high-value insights as "Priority" and noise as "Skip."
*(Visual: User types into the feedback box: "Combine the financial themes into a single macro-trend.")*
You can also inject a "Lens." Tell the AI to combine themes or focus specifically on behavioral blockers. When you regenerate, the AI doesn't just guess again—it follows your strict constraints.

**(0:55) [Visual: Transition to "Checkpoint: Personas". The user is deleting a generic bullet point from "Pain Points" and typing a specific quote.]**

**VO:**
**Checkpoint Three: Persona Synthesis.**
Standard LLMs hallucinate generic personas. Here, you see the draft.
If a persona feels flat, you edit the "5 Ws" framework directly. You add specific "Pain Points" derived from your own knowledge.
*(Visual: Cursor clicks "Regenerate". The new persona appears, retaining the user's manual edits while expanding on them.)*
The system treats your manual edits as ground truth. It builds the rest of the profile *around* your interventions, ensuring the final output is factually accurate.

**(1:15) [Visual: Transition to "Checkpoint: Deck Structure". User clicks "+ Add Slide", types a title, and drags it to position 2.]**

**VO:**
**Checkpoint Four: Narrative Architecture.**
Before writing a single line of slide copy, we let you architect the storyline.
Review the slide list. Is the flow logical? If not, drag and drop to reorder. Add a missing slide. Edit the rationale.
You define the arc. The AI simply fills in the content.

**(1:32) [Visual: Results View. Left side: Video Script. User clicks "Edit" on Scene 3's Visual Description. They type: "Cinematic close up of hands holding a receipt, high contrast."]*

**VO:**
**Final Stage: Asset Generation.**
We produce a Video Script and a Strategy Deck. But even here, you have control.
Don't like an image? You don't just hit "retry." You edit the *Visual Prompt* itself.
*(Visual: User clicks "Redo Image". A new, more dramatic image generates in seconds.)*
You direct the visual style—minimalist, cinematic, or corporate—scene by scene, slide by slide.

**(1:50) [Visual: The final PDF is downloaded. It opens to show a perfectly formatted, 16:9 widescreen presentation.]**

**VO:**
This isn't automated research. It's augmented research. You get the speed of Gemini 3 Pro, but the insight and precision remains 100% yours.

**(2:00) [Visual: NXT Humans Logo. Text: "Humans-In-Control."]**
