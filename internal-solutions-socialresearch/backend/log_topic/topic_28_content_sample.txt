Content 1 (length: 334 chars):
A commitment scheme needs two properties. Binding means you cannot change your message after committing. Hiding means the commitment reveals nothing about your message until you choose to reveal it. Most naive approaches like just hashing a message fail the hiding property because anyone who guesses your message can verify it early.

Content 2 (length: 386 chars):
When you send a file through ShadowSign, every recipient gets their own uniquely encrypted copy. The file contents are identical — but each copy carries a cryptographic fingerprint derived from that recipient's public key using HMAC-SHA256. The sender's identity is never revealed. There's no account, no login, no server. Everything runs in the browser using the native Web Crypto API.
