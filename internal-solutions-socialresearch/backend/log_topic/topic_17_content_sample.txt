A level funded health plan is specifically how an employer funds paying for the benefits of the health plans they choose to offer their employees. They still might choose shitty options to reduce cost by shifting it to the employee.
Incorrect. Level funded only means that the positive difference between claims paid by UHC and premiums paid by employee/employer get split 50/50 between UHC and the employer. As for the deductible being high, yes it is. If I were you I’d put the savings in premium differential aside into an HSA and use it to offset any services you need before you meet the deductible. Although depending on your plan design PCP & SCP copays may not be subject to the deductible first.
