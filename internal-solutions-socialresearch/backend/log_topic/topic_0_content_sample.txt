Appreciate any honest advice. Being a business owner is mostly about running the business. If you're a window cleaner, it'd be fair to think that most of your time will be spent cleaning windows, but it won't. It will be spent running the business. It will be spent answering emails, making calls, picking out insurance policies, bookkeeping, internal admin, training employees, talking with customers, marketing, etc.. so make sure you like or at least can do these things day in and day out, because that's what really makes up most of the day to day. I always tell people that it's 80% running the business, 20% doing the thing that the business is about.
