What repetitive admin task still wastes the most time in your business? For small business owners: what repetitive admin task still wastes the most time in your business?

Content 2 (length: 247 chars):
Examples could be:
- copying customer info between tools
- following up with customers
- booking appointments
- updating spreadsheets
- chasing documents
- preparing quotes
- keeping CRM/customer records updated
- reporting what happened each week

Content 3 (length: 463 chars):
**Also, I wouldn't frame these things as a waste of time.** If they were truly waste of time, people wouldn't do it. Successful small business don't see themselves as time wasters.
For me the sneaky one is customer follow-up after the first contact. Not the big obvious stuff, but the half-finished handoffs: someone asked for a quote, someone needs a date confirmed, someone sent details in a text, someone is “probably interested” but nobody owns the next step.
