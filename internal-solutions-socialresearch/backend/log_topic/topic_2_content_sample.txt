Content 2 (length: 671 chars):
Thanks! For under 400 orders/month, I’d keep the accounting system boring and make the payout reconciliation clean. QBO or Xero can both work, but the part to decide early is how Stripe fees, shipping charges, sales tax, and inventory purchases land in the books so your CPA isn’t untangling mixed payout deposits later.
qbo is fine for this. set up the stripe integration from day one so fees and payouts auto-match instead of you manually splitting every deposit later. at my agency we've seen people try to reconcile stripe manually at first and it becomes a mess by month 3 when they realize gross sales don't match net deposits and the CPA has to rebuild everything.
