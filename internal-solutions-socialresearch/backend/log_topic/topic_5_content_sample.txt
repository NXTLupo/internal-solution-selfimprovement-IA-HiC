If you’re serious about building a long-term career in healthcare administration, you’ll eventually need to pursue a graduate degree. A Master of Healthcare Administration (MHA) is considered the gold standard in this field. If you’re looking for broader career flexibility, an MBA with a healthcare concentration can also be valuable, though in some markets it may not carry the same weight as an MHA for leadership roles in health systems.

I think the most important question to consider is what you ultimately hope to achieve in your career with an MHA. If the degree is pursued without a clear direction—simply with the hope that something will open up—it may be worth taking time to reflect on what you truly want to do. The MHA is a powerful and respected degree, but it is best suited for those who are committed to building a career in healthcare, particularly in leadership, administration, and operations. That said, I also know several individuals who have leveraged an MHA into high-level roles in IT and EMR strategy within health systems.
