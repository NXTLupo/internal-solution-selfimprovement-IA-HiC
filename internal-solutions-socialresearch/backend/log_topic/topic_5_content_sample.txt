If you withdraw your contributions from your Roth IRA there is no tax liability. Say you have contributed $21,000 over the years and it has grown to $26,000. You can withdraw $21,000, leave $5,000 -- no tax liability and no penalty. If you withdraw all of it, you should be able to have Vanguard withhold whatever percentage you want for both federal and state taxes (and the 10% penalty). You are responsible for keeping track of how much of your account is contribution vs gains.
