Back to the OP's question, if they have a trust already setup, they should be able to retitle their brokerage accounts without any issue. I am not sure offhand if Vanguard charges for this, but if even if they do it should not be much and there should be no additional taxes. Just like how you can move a house into a trust, though that usually has some costs with the doc stamps, etc.
For people where a trust makes sense in the first place, moving assets like a home, brokerage account, etc. usually is a good idea if you want to have the money there as part of the purpose of the trust. Brokerage accounts normally don't go through probate, so there isn't the same advantage there like there is for your house, but it still can simplify your estate planning, especially with young children (if applicable).
I can’t answer your question about the actual move itself. But trust are taxed at a much higher rate than individuals/couples. Plus Trusts have no standard deductions.
