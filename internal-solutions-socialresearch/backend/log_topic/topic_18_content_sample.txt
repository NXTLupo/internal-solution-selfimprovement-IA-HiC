If you have a w2 and look at the Medicare and social security withholding on your paystub, your employer is also paying that same amount. With the gig apps, you're responsible for both portions which is roughly 15.3% of profit. If you have w2 your federal income tax rate will be the highest you pay on your w2 earnings (if you pay 12% on the top end of your pay, your doordash profit will be taxed at 12%). Then there's also state tax if your state has it.
