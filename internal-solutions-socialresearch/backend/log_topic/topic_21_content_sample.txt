If you don't have insurance and you're operating as a sole proprietor, you have a personal risk: if you get sued, you'll have pay to defend yourself and if you have liability you'll be personally responsible for everything. If you don't have insurance and you're operating as an LLC or C/S-Corp or similar, you could just decide to close the business upon getting served regarding a lawsuit and let there be a default judgement against a non-entity.
