Customer Journey Analysis - E-commerce Experience

Background:
Sarah is a 34-year-old marketing professional looking to purchase a new laptop for work. She has been researching options for several weeks and has a budget of $1,500-$2,000.

Journey Timeline:

1. Awareness Stage (Week 1)
- Noticed her current laptop becoming slow during video calls
- Asked colleagues for recommendations
- Started researching laptop reviews online
- Visited tech blogs and comparison websites

2. Research Stage (Week 2-3)
- Compared specifications across different brands
- Read customer reviews on e-commerce sites
- Watched YouTube unboxing videos
- Checked price comparisons across multiple retailers

3. Consideration Stage (Week 4)
- Narrowed down to 3 laptop models
- Visited physical stores to test the laptops
- Asked sales representatives about warranties
- Compared prices and promotions

4. Purchase Stage (Week 5)
- Found the best deal online with free shipping
- Experienced difficulty during checkout process
- Had to contact customer service for payment issues
- Successfully completed purchase after 2 attempts

5. Post-Purchase Experience
- Received shipping notifications
- Laptop arrived on time and well-packaged
- Set up process was smooth
- Received follow-up email for product registration

Pain Points:
- Checkout process was confusing and had technical glitches
- Customer service wait time was 15 minutes
- Some product information was inconsistent across channels

Positive Moments:
- Helpful sales staff in physical stores
- Fast and reliable shipping
- Quality packaging and product setup experience
- Proactive follow-up communications

Recommendations:
- Improve checkout process reliability
- Reduce customer service response times
- Ensure consistent product information across all channels
- Consider implementing live chat for immediate support
