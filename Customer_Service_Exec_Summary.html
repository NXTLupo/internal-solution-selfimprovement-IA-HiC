Key Journey Insights

Our deep analysis of Northeastern University's current customer service journey reveals critical insights that inform our transformation strategy:

Operational Challenges

  • Staff members are managing over 10 disconnected systems, resulting in significant inefficiencies
  • Multiple uncoordinated service requests create confusion for both users and support staff
  • Inconsistent data across systems complicates processes and negatively impacts user experience
  • Support teams are overwhelmed by fragmented user demands across multiple channels
"Support teams overwhelmed by fragmented user demands." Source: ROI of Transformation Section, Page 2

Customer Experience Impact

  • Students face a complex support journey across multiple touchpoints and departments
  • Inconsistent information is often encountered when navigating disparate systems
  • The evolving expectations for seamless, on-demand support aren't being met
  • There's a lack of a consistent support system across the university
"A Northeastern student's customer support journey can involve multiple touchpoints and university departments – a complex process, with students often needing to navigate disparate systems and encountering inconsistent information." Source: Student Support Journey, Page 4

Transformation Opportunities

  • Potential for ~$700K+ annual savings through system consolidation
  • Up to 70% of inquiries could be automated through self-service solutions
  • AI integration could save 10-15 hours per employee per week
  • A unified data platform could provide 24/7 visibility and insights
  • Redirection of staff time to high-value interactions
"Integrating AI and automation to handle common inquiries." Source: ROI of Transformation Section, Page 2

Service Design Architecture

The future service model utilizes an intelligent double-agentic technology layer to enable and automate journey experiences:

Service Flow Architecture

  • Inquiry Channels: Email, Phone, In-Person, Chat
  • Inquiry Mode Selection: AI or Live Representative based on complexity
  • Agentic Inquiry Layer: Natural language processing and intent recognition
  • Routing Layer: Intelligent case assignment and prioritization
  • Agentic Support Layer: AI-assisted case handling and knowledge retrieval
  • Resolution Pathways: Automated or human-assisted resolution
  • Follow-up: Automated satisfaction surveys and next steps
  • Feedback Loop: Continuous improvement through analytics
