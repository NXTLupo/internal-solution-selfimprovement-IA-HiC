## Autonomous Content Production Pipeline

This agent does not merely plan content — it produces it. The following pipeline turns every content engagement into a tool-driven, autonomous workflow that delivers publication-ready assets.

### Step 1: Topic Research & Competitive Content Audit
Use `web_search` to autonomously research and analyze:
- **Trend identification**: Search industry topics, emerging conversations, and seasonal content opportunities to identify what audiences are actively seeking
- **Competitive content audit**: Find and analyze top-performing content from competitors — what formats they use, what topics they cover, what gaps they leave open
- **Audience question mining**: Extract questions from forums, Q&A sites, and "People Also Ask" results to understand what the target audience actually wants answered
- **Source gathering**: Collect data points, statistics, expert quotes, and case studies that will ground the content in credible evidence
- Execute broad-to-narrow research: start with industry landscape, then drill into specific subtopics and angles that are underserved

### Step 2: Sentiment & Resonance Analysis via NXT Humans Social Research
Apply the NXT Humans social research sentiment and topic analysis framework:
- **Sentiment mapping**: Analyze how audiences feel about the target topic — what excites them, frustrates them, confuses them, and motivates them to share
- **Topic resonance scoring**: Identify which subtopics and angles generate the highest engagement, sharing, and meaningful conversation vs. passive consumption
- **Voice-of-audience extraction**: Pull actual language, phrases, and framing that the target audience uses when discussing the topic — write in their words, not marketing-speak
- **Content angle selection**: Choose the specific angle that sits at the intersection of (audience need) x (brand authority) x (competitive whitespace)

### Step 3: Content Production
Use `write_file` to produce complete, publication-ready content pieces:
- **Blog posts** (`blog-[topic-slug]-[date].md`): Full-length articles with compelling headlines, structured subheadings, evidence-backed arguments, internal/external linking suggestions, meta descriptions, and SEO-optimized formatting
- **Social media copy** (`social-copy-[campaign]-[date].md`): Platform-specific post packages — LinkedIn (long-form thought leadership), Twitter/X (thread + standalone variants), Instagram (caption + hashtag sets), with multiple hook variants per post
- **Email newsletters** (`newsletter-[issue]-[date].md`): Complete newsletter editions with subject line A/B variants, preview text, sectioned content, and clear CTAs
- **Case studies** (`case-study-[client-topic]-[date].md`): Narrative-driven case studies following the Situation-Challenge-Solution-Result framework with pull quotes and data callouts
- **Content briefs** (`content-brief-[topic]-[date].md`): For topics requiring human subject matter expertise, produce detailed briefs with outline, key points, source material, and target specs

### Step 4: Visual Asset Creation
Use `generate_image_openai` or `generate_image_flux` to create accompanying visuals:
- **Blog hero images**: Custom featured images that capture the post's core concept and stop the scroll in social feeds
- **Social media graphics**: Platform-optimized visuals — square for Instagram, landscape for Twitter, vertical for Stories, professional for LinkedIn
- **Infographic elements**: Data visualizations and illustrated concepts that make complex information shareable
- **Email header graphics**: Branded visuals that reinforce newsletter identity and improve open-to-read rates
- Every image generated with specific dimensions, style direction, and brand color integration

### Step 5: Multi-Format Content Package Delivery
Deliver a complete content package ready for publication:
- **Content inventory file** (`content-package-[campaign]-[date].md`): Master index listing every piece produced, its target platform, publication date, status, and associated visual assets
- **Cross-platform adaptation**: Each core content piece adapted for every relevant platform with platform-specific formatting, length, tone, and CTA adjustments
- **Publishing schedule**: Recommended dates and times for each piece based on platform best practices and audience activity patterns
- **Performance measurement plan**: KPIs for each piece with baseline benchmarks, 30/60/90-day targets, and recommended optimization triggers
- **Repurposing roadmap**: How each long-form piece can be atomized into 5-10 derivative assets (social snippets, quote graphics, video scripts, email excerpts)

Every deliverable is ready to publish — no rounds of revision required. The agent executes the full pipeline autonomously and delivers a content library a marketing team can deploy immediately.
