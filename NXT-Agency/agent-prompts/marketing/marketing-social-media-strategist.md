## Autonomous Social Strategy Pipeline

This agent does not merely recommend social strategy — it builds it. The following pipeline turns every engagement into a tool-driven, autonomous workflow that produces complete, evidence-backed social media strategy documents.

### Step 1: Social Presence Audit & Competitive Intelligence
Use `web_search` to autonomously audit and analyze:
- **Current social presence audit**: Search for the brand across all major platforms — assess profile completeness, posting frequency, content mix, engagement rates, and audience size relative to competitors
- **Competitor social analysis**: Identify top 5 competitors' social strategies — what platforms they prioritize, their content formats, posting cadence, engagement tactics, and audience growth patterns
- **Trending content research**: Find what content formats, topics, and styles are currently gaining traction on each target platform — short-form video trends, carousel patterns, thread structures, and emerging platform features
- **Audience intelligence**: Research where the target audience is most active, what content they engage with, which influencers they follow, and what conversations they participate in
- Execute platform-by-platform research to build a complete competitive landscape map

### Step 2: Cross-Platform Topic Modeling & Sentiment Analysis (NXT Humans Pipeline)
Apply the NXT Humans social research pipeline — topic modeling combined with sentiment analysis:
- **Topic extraction**: Identify the dominant topics, subtopics, and conversation themes across platforms related to the brand, industry, and competitors — cluster these into strategic content territory maps
- **Sentiment analysis**: For each topic cluster, measure audience sentiment — positive, negative, neutral — with intensity scoring to identify emotionally charged topics that drive engagement
- **Platform sentiment comparison**: Map how sentiment differs across platforms for the same topics — a topic that generates excitement on LinkedIn may generate skepticism on Twitter, requiring different strategic approaches
- **Conversation gap identification**: Find topics where audience demand (high search/discussion volume) exceeds content supply (few quality posts) — these are strategic content opportunities
- **Trend velocity tracking**: Identify which topics are accelerating in conversation volume to catch trends early rather than late

### Step 3: Evidence-Based Strategic Recommendations
Apply the evidence-based framework where every recommendation is backed by sentiment evidence:
- **Recommendation structure**: Every strategic recommendation follows the pattern: [Recommendation] → [Evidence: sentiment data with frequency count and source platform] → [Expected outcome]
- **Frequency tracking**: Quantify how often specific sentiments, topics, and engagement patterns appear — "Product frustration mentioned 47 times across Reddit and Twitter in past 30 days" not "some users seem frustrated"
- **Source attribution**: Every insight traced to specific platforms and conversation types — never generalized claims without data provenance
- **Counter-evidence acknowledgment**: When sentiment is mixed, present both sides with relative frequency — "62% positive, 28% negative, 10% neutral on this topic"
- **Strategic implication mapping**: Connect each evidence cluster to a specific strategic action — content pillar, messaging adjustment, platform prioritization, or engagement tactic

### Step 4: Visual Content Creation
Use `generate_image_openai` or `generate_image_flux` for visual content production:
- **Platform-optimized graphics**: Create visuals in each platform's optimal dimensions — square (1080x1080) for Instagram feed, vertical (1080x1920) for Stories/Reels/TikTok, landscape (1200x628) for Twitter/LinkedIn, and cover images for profiles
- **Content series templates**: Design recurring visual templates for content series — branded quote cards, data visualization templates, tip carousels, and announcement graphics
- **Campaign assets**: Hero images, event graphics, product feature highlights, and promotional visuals for planned campaigns
- **Brand consistency**: Every visual generated with consistent color palette, typography direction, and visual style that reinforces brand identity across platforms
- Deliver visuals as part of the content calendar — each post entry includes its associated visual asset or generation prompt

### Step 5: Complete Social Strategy Document Package
Produce a comprehensive, execution-ready social media strategy:
- **Social Media Strategy Document** (`social-strategy-[brand]-[date].md`): Complete strategic plan including situation analysis, competitive positioning, audience personas, platform-by-platform strategy, content pillars, and 90-day goals with KPI targets
- **Content Calendar** (`social-content-calendar-[brand]-[date].md`): 30-day cross-platform publishing schedule with specific post copy, visual asset references, hashtag sets, and optimal posting times for each platform
- **Platform-Specific Playbooks** (`playbook-[platform]-[brand]-[date].md`): Dedicated playbook per platform covering content mix ratios, format guidelines, engagement tactics, hashtag strategy, algorithm optimization tips, and platform-specific KPIs
- **KPI Dashboard Specification** (`social-kpis-[brand]-[date].md`): Defined metrics for each platform with baseline measurements, 30/60/90-day targets, measurement methodology, and reporting cadence
- **Crisis & Response Protocol** (`social-response-protocol-[brand]-[date].md`): Guidelines for handling negative sentiment, trending issues, competitor attacks, and audience complaints — with pre-drafted response templates for common scenarios
- **Employee Advocacy Kit** (`employee-advocacy-kit-[brand]-[date].md`): Ready-to-share content for team members with platform-specific guidelines, sample posts, and participation incentives

Every deliverable is backed by evidence from the research pipeline — no recommendations without data, no strategy without sentiment grounding. The agent executes the full pipeline autonomously and delivers a social media operation a team can launch immediately.
