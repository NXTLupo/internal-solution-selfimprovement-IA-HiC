What changed, by the owner's five criteria

Currency

  • The period a request implies is decided in code: "today" is the calendar date in the owner's time zone, research with no stated period is the last 30 days (currency.py). The search planner is told the date and the period; the engines and news feeds are asked for that range.
  • Every page gets a published date from its metadata, its address or its byline. Older pages are left out; undated pages are capped so a run meets its own bar. Under four current sources, a run stops and says so.
  • Dates now ride on theme quotes, deck source lines and the report's source list. A hard check, sources_current, marks work built on stale sources as needing review.

Speed and the graphics card

  • Every call's token rate is recorded. Under 10 tokens a second the writer moves to the smaller model file for 15 minutes and the page says why. Figma is set to the Intel card on this laptop (measured).
  • The deck is planned in one short call and written three slides at a time, saved after each batch so a retry resumes: 7 to 9 minutes with pictures, against 55. The report is outlined, then written a section at a time: about 2.5 minutes, against 17.
  • One resident model plans searches, analyses and writes. A second model reviews only at deep depth. Plans queue for the card instead of fighting for it.
  • Plans on one engagement share one evidence run. A report on evidence already held does not collect again.

Breadth, depth and citations

  • Selected packs get a keyword query, their items stay unless plainly off the subject, and the summary counts what was kept after filtering. If every selected pack is empty the run stops with the reason; if one is, it is named.
  • Themes are clustered in two levels with every passage placed, each source's own centre removed so themes cross sources, a scope check that drops a theme far from the goal, and the house word list repaired in code.
  • A themes deck is laid out from the themes: one slide per group, a quote slide for the best-evidenced theme, then decisions.

The page and the three gaps

  • Gap 1: a Library panel over the local index, with filters and "add to this engagement".
  • Gap 2: a live feed on running research and themes cards (sources as read, findings as checked, themes as named); every step says what it is doing.
  • Gap 3: engagements are managed as projects (see the second pass above): settings, tags, pin, archive, several at once, merge and separate. Counts come from the table; a new conversation starts with no engagement and packs on Auto.
  • The false "Report ready, all 0 checks passed" card is gone: evidence has its own card, and zero checks reads "Not checked". Internal pointers left the audio transcript.
  • The audio panel defect the owner reported is fixed and checked in the page: the card's own button starts the voices at once (they cost nothing here, so no approval is raised elsewhere) and the card shows the player when they exist.
  • Every evidence-backed plan card now shows two choices before Start: the period its sources must come from, and the depth.

Data currency · owner priority

No case delivered current data, and the system cannot tell

The owner's rule: a request for today's headlines means today, and research means evidence current to today's date, within the month at least. Measured against that rule, every case failed, and the app has no way to notice.

Why it happens

  • No prompt contains today's date, so the local model writes queries from its training years (localsearch.QUERY_SYSTEM).
  • The SearXNG call has no time range (localsearch.search).
  • Web pages are stored without a published date (studio._fetch); wiki pages are archived with date: "" even when the feed gave one.
  • Second-brain reuse and the relevance filter rank by similarity alone; age is never a factor.
  • The scorecard has no date check, and the writer is free to call any item "today's".

The currency contract (workstream 0, before everything else)

  1. Every request gets a window, decided in code. "Today" means the calendar date. "Latest", "current", "right now", "new", "fresh", and any research request with no stated period mean the last 30 days. The window shows on the plan card as a chip the user can widen; it is never widened silently.
  2. The date goes into every prompt that plans a search or writes prose.
  3. Collection filters by date at the source. Web search uses the engine's day, week or month range and its news category. Pack items older than the window are not fetched into the run. Reuse from the second brain applies the same window.
  4. Every source must carry a published date. Read it from page metadata (article:published_time, JSON-LD datePublished, a time element, the URL, then the HTTP Last-Modified header). A page with no date is labelled undated, cannot support a "today" answer, and is capped at a small share of a research corpus.
  5. Older material is allowed only as labelled background (a definition, a baseline survey), in its own tier with a share cap, and the text states its year.
  6. Dates are visible. Each citation shows its date. The run's ledger and the card show the spread, for example "14 sources: 11 inside 30 days, 2 background, 1 undated".
  7. A hard gate. The scorecard check sources_current fails the work when the share inside the window is below the bar (100 percent for a "today" answer, 80 percent for research), and when the text calls an item today's that is not. Failed work is marked "Needs review (stale sources)".
  8. Short runs say so. When the window yields too little, the app reports what it found and asks before widening.
  9. The store keeps dates. Published date becomes a required field on every archived page, with a backfill for pages already written. The Library view filters and sorts by it.

The design that follows: three lanes and a scheduler

Graphics lane · one at a timeOne resident writer model with a fixed context and a long keep-alive. A priority queue: chat first, then the step the user is watching, then background work. Work yields between steps, never mid-call.
Processor lane · in parallel24 cores and 95 GB: the 2B and 4B models (29 and 18.6 tokens per second measured), reranking, clustering, speech, transcription. Zero graphics layers, so they never touch the 24 GB. Embeddings get a small fixed share of the card.
Network lane · in parallelFeed pulls, page fetches, date extraction, archiving. No model needed; today these run one after another inside the research step.
Picture engine · booked windowsImages and video get an exclusive window at the end of a task. The scheduler unloads the writer or waits; the two never share memory by accident.
  • Memory budget in code. Context size is part of the budget: the same model is 17.7 GB at 32K and 19 GB at 65K. Before a load: model size plus context cache plus a 1.5 GB margin must be under free dedicated memory. If not, use the smaller quantization in the seat chain, or wait, and say which on the card.
  • One writer, stable prefix. Same context size on every call so the model never reloads; evidence first in the prompt so the cache is reused across steps (prefix reuse measured today: 0.7 percent).
  • Two tasks at once. Today two tasks both start threads and fight for the card. With lanes, a second task collects and embeds while the first writes, then takes the graphics lane.
  • Chat stays responsive. A chat turn jumps the queue at the next step boundary; steps are kept short (under about 60 s) so the wait is bounded.
  • Watch the machine. Record tokens per second, dedicated and shared memory, and temperature per call. Under 10 tokens per second raises a banner with the cause. Use the Windows performance power mode on mains power.

Most of these sources are practitioner guides, not vendor documentation, and their benchmark figures conflict. Treat each action as a test to run on this laptop, with the token rate before and after recorded in LOCAL.md.

Known gaps

The three gaps the owner named, confirmed, with a design for each

Gap 1. The second brain cannot be browsed

Confirmed. The API has no route that lists wiki pages, chunks, themes or engagements' evidence. The only window is the Files tab of one engagement (a flat source list with on and off switches). HiC's manifest held 25,707 pages and 124,076 chunks today.

Design: a Library view. Three panes: facets on the left (pack, publisher, date, kind, engagement, theme), results in the middle, the passage with its citation on the right. Search answers from a local index (SQLite FTS5 plus the embedding index mirrored from the manifest), so a keystroke returns in under 200 ms; HiC's 25 s search is never on the typing path. Every row has "Add to engagement" and "Start from this" actions, and multi-select feeds a run directly. Saved themes from earlier runs are first-class rows with their passages.

Gap 2. Research shows nothing until it ends

Confirmed. Across about five hours of runs the card showed step names, a timer and at best a token count. No source, finding or theme appeared before a task finished.

Design: a live research stream. The Research Lead emits an event per source as it is fetched; the page lists them with publisher and date. The Analyst works per source and emits each finding with its quote as it is verified. Every 20 new findings, an incremental clustering pass (embeddings already computed, clustering on the processor in a few seconds) updates a theme board: cards appear, grow and merge with a short animation, each showing its count and newest quote. Naming runs on the 2B model per cluster and is refined at the end. The board is the loading state, and it stays as the result.

Gap 3. Engagements cannot be combined, renamed or managed

Confirmed. The API offers create and hide. A name can be typed at creation only. A run takes exactly one engagement.

Design: a workspace. Rename, archive, delete with an approval, duplicate, merge, tags, client and status fields, sort and search. Check boxes in the engagement list set a multi-engagement scope shown as chips in the composer; evidence keeps its engagement of origin in every citation. Runs across engagements read through the Library index, so no data is copied.
