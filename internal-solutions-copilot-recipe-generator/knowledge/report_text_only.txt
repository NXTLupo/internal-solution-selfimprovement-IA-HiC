Executive Narrative
🗺 The User's Journey
User enters site seeking Mandelic Acid suitability guidance. AI Chat is present but silent at entry. User selects 'Is Mandelic Acid right for me?' and receives ingredient education instead of suitability verdict. Across 20 screens, the core question—'Is this right for my skin?'—is never answered. AI recommends products, confirms compatibility, but never delivers personal suitability. Chat message order inverts, breaking trust. Blank inputs become dead ends. User rates AI 'No' and exits without buying.
Overall Readout
The AI Chat experience is structurally broken. Core user question—'Is Mandelic Acid right for me?'—goes unanswered across all 20 screens despite multiple opportunities. AI delivers education and compatibility verdicts but never confirms personal suitability. Message ordering inverts, placeholder chips visible, blank inputs trap users post-education. Entry points are silent. Conversion is blocked at highest-intent moment.
Strengths
Three pre-set option buttons ('Learn more', 'Find products', 'Is Mandelic Acid right for me?') successfully reduce cold-input abandonment
Inline 'Add to Cart' on product cards and dual-product regimen cards enable transaction without page redirect
Feedback mechanism ('Was this helpful? Yes / No') immediately surfaces dissatisfaction before user exits
Alignment to Brief
The unified personal shopper concept is present but non-functional. AI Chat entry points exist (homepage, PLP, search overlay) but offer zero value proposition or proactive guidance. Suitability assessment—core to the brief—is never completed. Regimen building is absent. Product discovery is fragmented across search results and chat. The experience increases friction, not reduces it. CSAT will suffer; repetitive support inquiries will persist.
🤖 AI Experience Assessment
AI responses are factually accurate (Mandelic Acid + Retinol compatibility, shipping info) but contextually incomplete. Suitability questions are ignored in favor of education or product recommendations. No skin type is collected systematically. No regimen sequencing is shown. Feedback mechanism surfaces dissatisfaction but offers no recovery. Trust is eroded by inverted message order and lorem ipsum placeholder text. The AI feels like a search engine, not a personal shopper.
