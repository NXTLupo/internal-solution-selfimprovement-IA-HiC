Online Shopping Experience Journey

This journey follows customers from initial product research through browsing, evaluation, purchase, delivery, and post-purchase support.

Pain Points

  • The checkout process is a major friction point with multiple issues that nearly cause abandonment
  • Product filtering and search functionality makes finding the right products unnecessarily difficult
  • Account creation is tedious and causes dropoff in conversion
  • Payment processing errors create frustration and lack of trust

Opportunities

  • Streamline the checkout process with guest checkout option and improved stability
  • Enhance product discovery with better filtering, search, and navigation
  • Implement one-click payment options to reduce friction
  • Add progress indicators to show customers where they are in the checkout flow
